(Iskandar) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Iskandar) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Iskandar) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Iskandar) Charisma — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 25 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Iskandar) Those Who Go Towards the Beyond — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Ignore Def for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Iskandar) Military Conquest Tactics — Rank: A  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Iskandar. All damage received from \[Anti-Army\] Noble Phantasms is reduced by 30%; all damage dealt by \[Anti-Army\] Noble Phantasms is increased by 30%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp (non-AA) for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt from Noble Phantasms **other** than \[Anti-Army\] is increased by 20%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Iskandar) Lightning Conqueror — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%; and applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Iskandar) Gordius Wheel: Wheel of Heaven’s Authority — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Iskandar performs a Riding Attack, damage dealt is increased by 50% and deals Lightning damage (half). Negated while inflicted with NP Seal.  
(Passive 2\) While inflicted with NP Seal, Passive 1, 2, and 3 of the Riding Skill are only active while the Active effect of the Riding Skill is in effect.

(Iskandar) Via Expugnatio: Distant Trampling Domination — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. This NP is used in the form of a Riding Attack, with a distance of 13 panels. Deals 4x damage plus 100\. Additionally, the DU rolls 10 six-sided dice. For each Failure, the DU takes 20 extra damage and has a 10% chance of being inflicted with Shock for 2◈ Turns. The conditions for Failure are as follows-  
END (Rank EX): 1 is rolled  
END (Rank A): 1 or 2 is rolled  
END (Rank B): 1, 2 or 3 is rolled...and so on.  
Example: DU with Rank B END rolls 10 six-sided dice. Four of the dice are 3 or less. Therefore, the DU takes 80 extra damage and has a 40% chance of being inflicted with Shock for 2◈ Turns.  
Note: The extra damage taken from dice rolls is not affected by any effects that modify damage taken **excluding** Invuln; with Invuln the extra damage is negated.  
This NP deals Lightning damage (half). Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
