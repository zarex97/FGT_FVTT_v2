(Mannanán) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Mannanán) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Mannanán) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
Fragarach Enbarr.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The Move Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Mannanán) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP.

(Mannanán) God’s Holder: Tradition Carrier — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) The maximum number of Fragarach Tokens Mannanán can have is 5\. Mannanán gains a Fragarach Token at the end of every Round.  
(Passive 2\) At the start of a Combat Phase, Mannanán can remove 1 Fragarach Token from herself, her Crit Chance is increased by 30% for that Combat Phase.  
(Passive 3\) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 5% for every Fragarach Token on Mannanán.  
(Passive 4\) The duration of buffs are extended by ⅓◈ extra Turns when applied to Mannanán.

(Mannanán) Toole Fragarach: Gouging Greatsword of the War God  
Attack Skill. Can only be used by removing 3 Fragarach Tokens from herself. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Deals normal damage three times; if the DU Evades, its Evade Roll is increased by 3 and if any Evade fails, the remaining hits cannot be Evaded; if the DU Blocks, damage is reduced from the Total Damage taken. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Mannanán) Fragarach Enbarr: Wildly Running Sword of the War God — Rank: EX (NP) \[Counter\]  
(Non-damaging) Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 5% for each Fragarach Counter on herself; the magnitude of which is halved for NP.  
2\. Applies Decoy for ⅓◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies the ‘Fragarach’ effect for ⅓◈ Turns to herself. The ‘Fragarach’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

The effects of ‘Fragarach’ are as follows-  
1\. When Mannanán is Attacked, she cannot perform a normal Counter.  
2\. When Mannanán is Attacked or inflicted with a debuff, at the end of the Combat Process (if Attacked), she automatically performs a Fragarach Counter on the DU, which deals 2.5x damage using her Base Attack (STR). A Fragarach Counter cannot be Blocked, and cannot be Evaded except with Dodge. Then, apply the following effects-  
a. The DU is inflicted with Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 10%; and Agility is reduced by 1 when damage is received.  
b. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Mannanán, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
c. Mannanán’s NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.  
d. Gain 1 Fragarach Token.  
3\. Fragarach Counters deal NP Damage; however they are not affected by NP Seal.  
4\. The Fragarach buff is Unremovable. 

(Mannanán) Fragarach: Gouging Sword of the War God — Rank: EX (NP) \[Counter\]  
Can only be used by removing 5 Fragarach Tokens from herself. Can be used when a Noble Phantasm is used against Mannanán. Cannot be used against (Passive) or (Non-damaging) Noble Phantasms. Has different effects depending on the NP used against her.  
1\. If the NP was the enemy Unit’s strongest NP (or its only damage-dealing NP), the NP is canceled and the Servant/Unit who used the NP is inflicted with Instakill.  
2\. If the Noble Phantasm was not the enemy Unit’s strongest, the Noble Phantasm is canceled and the equivalent amount of damage that NP would have dealt is dealt to the NP’s user instead (only affects the user if it was an AoE NP).  
Both versions of this NP are effective at any Range. Fragarach cannot be responded to (Block, Evade, Luck Check, Counter, etc).  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Mannanán) God’s Holder: Possession  
(Active) This Skill can only be used when Mannanán’s Health is less than 30% of its maximum value OR when she is defeated and while she has at least 1 Fragarach Token. Remove all Fragarach Counters from Mannanán and she enters Holder Mode, restoring her Health to 50% of its maximum value.  
(Passive) While Mannanán is in Holder Mode, she gains the following effects-  
1\. The maximum number of Fragarach Tokens is increased to 7\.  
2\. Mannanán now also gains 1 Fragarach Token at the end of every Turn she Acts.  
3\. Range is increased to 3 panels.  
4\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 to 2 use Base Attack (STR) and 30% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 150+75=225), not affected by Magic Resistance. Normal Attacks at a Range of 3 or higher use Base Attack (MAG).  
5\. Toole Fragarach is replaced with Hallowed Sea God’s Sword.

(Mannanán) Hallowed Sea God’s Sword  
Attack Skill. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Uses Base Attack (STR) and 50% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 150+125=275). Deals 30% extra damage; if the DU Evades, its Evade Roll is increased by 3\. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
