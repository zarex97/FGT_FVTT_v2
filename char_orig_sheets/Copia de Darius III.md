(Darius) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Unit’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 60% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Unit’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Unit can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Darius) Golden Rule — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever this Unit performs a Crit, it has an 80% chance of reducing its NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.

(Darius) The Divine Gate Shall Not be Sealed Off — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of itself, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.   
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Lawful) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of itself, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment is increased by 30%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Darius) Disengage — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used at the start of a Combat Phase when Darius is Attacked. The enemy Unit’s Attack is cancelled, Darius is allowed to Move 5 panels, then his Health is restored by 25% of its maximum value, and remove all debuffs from himself. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Darius) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Darius) Achaemenid Pride — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, applies Guts to Darius for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Darius is defeated he is revived with 30% of his Health. Then, hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Darius, inflicts Def Dwn (B) for 1◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units, all damage taken is increased by 20%; if NP, 30%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Darius) Athanaton Ten Thousand: Immortal Ten Thousand Soldiers — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Can be used in 2 different ways-  
1\. Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is Active. Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 3x5 or 5x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Darius for 4x damage plus 100; then inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%; and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%. After using this NP, roll an eight-sided die and add 4 to the number rolled, the number rolled equals the number of ‘Athanatoi’ summoned around Darius. Roll a six-sided die for each ‘Athanatoi’ summoned to determine which type is summoned (see below). Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is deactivated. When this NP is used in this way, the following effects are applied to Darius for a maximum of 2◈ Turns (can be ended earlier)-  
a. Darius is transformed into the Athanaton Elephant (size is increased from 1 panel to 2 panels.)  
b. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP.  
c. All damage received is reduced by 75%; if NP, 50%. Darius does not perform Injury Rolls when damaged by an Attack unless he was damaged by a NP.  
d. Darius’ Master is placed on top of him, and his Master cannot be targeted for an Attack; and receives no damage and effects from AoE Attacks if Darius is hit by one.  
e. Darius cannot Block; and when he performs Agility Checks, double the value of the roll.  
f. Darius can Move to any panel, when he Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Darius to stand on.  
g. Darius’ Range is reduced to 1, but his Normal Attacks now hit a 2x2 panel area (directly next to him), and knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
h. Darius has the ‘Large’ and ‘Undead’ Attributes when this NP is Active.  
i. At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Darius’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of each Turn if Darius Acted; and 10 Health if he did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.
