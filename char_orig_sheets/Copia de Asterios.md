(Asterios) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 60% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. This Servant’s Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns if its Master is defeated while ME is Active.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.

(Asterios) Chaos Labyrinthos: Eternally Unchanging Labyrinth  — Rank: EX (NP) \[Labyrinth\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects a 9x9 panel area around Asterios when used, this NP area will now be termed as the ‘Labyrinth’; if the Region is Greece, it affects an 11x11 panel area instead. Has the following effects-  
1\. Asterios’ MOV is increased by 4 while within the Labyrinth.  
2\. Upon Activation, inflicts Atk Dwn for 2◈ Turns on all enemy Units within the Labyrinth, all damage dealt is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%; and inflicts Def Dwn on those Units for 2◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%.  
3\. The MOV of all enemy Units within the Labyrinth is reduced by 2 (minimum MOV=2).  
4\. Units outside the Labyrinth are able to enter the Labyrinth.  
5\. In order to Escape the Labyrinth, an enemy Unit must first Move to the inner border of the Labyrinth. At this point, if that Unit does not have any MOV left, it is unable to Escape on the same Turn; but if the Unit is still able to Move at least 1 panel, it can attempt to Escape.  
The Base Success Chance of Escaping the Labyrinth is 20% (use a twenty-sided die for this). If the Unit Fails to Escape, it is Moved to a random panel within the Labyrinth. If the Unit Succeeds, it is able to Move out of the Labyrinth equivalent to the number of panels it can still Move. Every time a Unit fails an Escape, the success chance is increased by 5% for the next Escape attempt.   
6\. Lasts for 4◈ Turns. However, after the initial NP period, Asterios can extend the NP duration by 2◈ more Turns by reducing his Health by 200 (cannot be used if Asterios’s Health is less than 200), and can be repeatedly extended.  
7\. Every time the duration of the Labyrinth is extended, Asterios inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within the Labyrinth, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%; and inflicts Def Dwn on those Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%.  
8\. The Labyrinth is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Asterios is defeated.  
9\. If a(n enemy) Unit that has Successfully escaped the Labyrinth at least once reenters the Labyrinth, its Base Success Chance of Escaping is increased to 100%, and its Move is no longer halved within the Labyrinth. A Unit who has Successfully Escaped the Labyrinth before can also lead other allied Units out of the Labyrinth (i.e. all allied Units directly next to a Unit that has Successfully Escaped the Labyrinth before also has a Base Success Chance of Escaping of 100%, and its MOV is also no longer halved within the Labyrinth).  
10\. Units outside the Labyrinth cannot Attack or apply any effects to Units within the Labyrinth and vice versa.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns after the NP ends.
