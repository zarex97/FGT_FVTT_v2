(Zhang Jue) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Zhang Jue) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Zhang Jue) Philosophy Thaumaturgy  
(Passive) When Zhang Jue performs a Normal Attack, he can either use his Base Attack (STR) or use one of these listed Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG). Each of the listed Attacks can only be used once per Round.  
1\. Deals Fire damage with a 30% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns. Damage dealt is increased by 50% and Burn chance is increased by 30% if the DU is within a ‘Burning’ or ‘Sunlight’ area.  
2\. Deals Ice damage with a 30% chance of inflicting Disable for ⅓◈ Turns. Damage dealt is increased by 50% and Disable chance is increased by 30% if the DU is within a ‘Waterside’, ‘Snowfield’ or ‘Frozen’ area.  
3\. Deals Earth damage with a 30% chance of inflicting Immobilize for ⅓◈ Turns. Damage dealt is increased by 50% and Immobilize chance is increased by 30% if the DU is within a ‘Forest’ or ‘Cavern’ area.  
4\. Deals Lightning damage with a 30% chance of inflicting Shock for 1◈ Turns. Damage dealt is increased by 50% and Shock chance is increased by 30% if the DU is within a ‘Thunderstorm’ or ‘Magnetic’ area.

(Zhang Jue) General of Heaven — Rank: B-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Flip a Coin. If Heads, restore 2 Agility; if Tails, restore 1 Agility.  
2\. Flip a Coin. If Heads, restore 2 Luck; if Tails, restore 1 Luck.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns. 

(Zhang Jue) Great Teacher — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
3\. Removes all debuffs.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Zhang Jue) Way of the Taiping — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, reduce his NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns. Then, has the following effects on a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to himself-  
1\. Has an 80% chance of applying the Forest (Taiping) Terrain to that area.  
2\. Has an 80% chance of applying the Waterside (Taiping) Terrain to that area.  
3\. Has an 80% chance of applying the Burning (Taiping) Terrain to that area.  
4\. Has an 80% chance of applying the Sunlight (Taiping) Terrain to that area.  
5\. Has an 80% chance of applying the Thunderstorm (Taiping) Terrain to that area.  
6\. Has an 80% chance of applying the Snowfield (Taiping) Terrain to that area.  
All Taiping Terrain are categorized as the Terrain of the same name, but do not apply their additional effects as listed under ‘Terrain Effects’. (e.g. a ‘Forest (Taiping)’ area is only a ‘Forest’ area in name. Unit effects which make use of Forest areas can be applied, but the ‘Forest (Taiping)’ area has no effects of its own.)  
Taiping Terrain ignores Terrain Overlap of other Taiping Terrains.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Zhang Jue) Cāng Tiān Yǐ Sǐ, Huáng Tiān Dāng Lì — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
The Azure Sky is Already Dead, the Yellow Sky will Soon Rise. Base Attack (STR) is used. First, apply NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 5% for every different Terrain type present on the field (Note: Terrain with the same name such as Burning and Burning (Taiping) count as the same Terrain type). Then, hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. DUs standing on Sunlight Terrain or during a ‘Day’ Round are inflicted with Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
2\. DUs standing on Forest or Poison Swamp Terrain are inflicted with AGL Dwn for 1◈ Turns, the value of Agility Checks are increased by 2\.  
3\. DUs standing on Waterside or Cavern Terrain are inflicted with LUC Dwn for 1◈ Turns, the value of Luck Checks are increased by 2\.  
4\. DUs standing on Burning or Lava Terrain are inflicted with Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%.  
5\. DUs standing on Thunderstorm or Eldritch Terrain are inflicted with Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
6\. DUs standing on Snowfield or Frozen Terrain are inflicted with MOV Dwn for 1◈ Turns, MOV is reduced by 2\.  
Deals 4x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment is further increased by 150%. Then, applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
