(Jackie) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 5% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Jackie) Murderer of the Misty Night — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Jack is Attacked by an enemy Unit, and the AU is within Jack’s Range, Jack can Attack first instead of the opposing Unit. If it is a Day Round, the activation of this effect requires a Successful Luck Check. Luck Check is not required during Night Rounds.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Jack performs an Attack when both Jack and the DU are within ‘The Mist’ (see below); increase the DU’s Evade Roll by 4 if it Evades.  
(Active 1\) Used during your Turn. Restore 3 Agility, applies Dodge to Jack for ⅓◈ Turns; and applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. If used while within the Mist (see below), effect duration is increased to 1◈ Turns, and Atk Up boost is 50% instead of 30% (if NP, 30%). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns (4◈ Turns if used within the Mist).

(Jackie) Mental Pollution — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 15%.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by Mental Debuffs is reduced by 60%.

(Jackie) Information Erasure — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever Jack leaves the Detect area of an enemy Unit, erase all information recorded by that Player about Jack, if any had been recorded in the Player’s Notebook (let the GM do this).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Removes all buffs on the DU; then inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jackie) Surgical Procedure — Rank: E  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on Jack or an allied Unit directly next to her. Restore the Unit’s Health by 5% of its maximum value, and restores 1 Agility. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Jackie) The Mist: Darkened Misty Metropolis — Rank: C (NP) \[Barrier\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, a cloud of Mist forms around Jack which covers a maximum of 25 panels. This area (which will be termed as the ‘Mist’) can be in any shape, but cannot expand past a distance of 4 panels from Jack (including diagonal). The effects of this Mist are as follows-  
1\. Normal Humans immediately die if they are caught in the Mist (this counts as Jack killing the Human).  
2\. Enemy Masters and non-normal Humans are inflicted with Poison upon contact with the Mist; and are inflicted with Poison at the end of its Turn or at the end of a Turn they Act while still within the Mist. (Advanced Note: High Rank Masters are not inflicted with Poison upon contact with the Mist.)  
3\. Whenever an enemy Servant within the Mist Rolls for Evade, increase the value rolled by 3\.  
4\. The Move of all enemy Units within the Mist is halved.  
5\. The Detect of all enemy Units within the Mist is reduced to 1 panel.  
Note: A Servant ignores effects 3, 4 and 5 if they have the Instinct Skill of Rank B or higher. Effect 4 is also ignored by any allied Units of the Servant with Instinct (Rank B or higher) who are standing directly next to the aforementioned Servant. (Certain Skills are also counted as Instinct, which will be mentioned at the bottom of the page.)  
5\. During Jack’s Turn or at the end of any Turn Jack Acts, she can Move the Mist and/or change the shape of the Mist once, as long as it stays within the stated Range (4 panels, as above). Does not count as Moving a Unit and is not an Attack. Note: Jack does not need to be within the Mist.  
6\. This NP can be deactivated at any time. At the end of the Turn after every 1◈ Turns since this NP was activated, Jack’s Master loses 15 Health. This NP is forcefully deactivated if Jack’s Master has 15 Health or less and would lose Health due to this effect. Her Master does not lose Health on the same Turn this NP is deactivated. This NP is forcefully deactivated at the end of a Turn Jack is defeated. Jack can deactivate this NP during her Turn or at the start or end of any Turn or Round.  
7\. Jack is not affected by the Fog of War area within the Mist (i.e. she can see all Units within the Mist).  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after The Mist is deactivated. 

(Jackie) Maria the Ripper: The Holy Mother of Dismemberment — Rank: D\~B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Can be used in two different methods. Method 1 has no conditions while Method 2 has conditions that need to be met. They are as follows-  
1\. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 2.5x damage; if the DU is Female, damage dealt is further increased by 100% (doubled). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
2\. The three conditions required to use Method 2 of Maria the Ripper as as follows-  
i) If the Round is a Night Round (automatically fulfilled if playing without Day-Night cycle).  
ii) The target Unit is Female.  
iii) Both Jack and the target Unit are within the Mist (see above).  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=4.  
If used at a Range of 1 or 2, this NP deals 3.5x damage plus 100; if the DU is Female (which she is, of course), damage dealt is further increased by 100% (doubled). Then, has a 50% chance of inflicting Instakill.  
If used at a Range of 3 or 4, no damage is dealt, this NP just has a 50% chance of inflicting Instakill.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
