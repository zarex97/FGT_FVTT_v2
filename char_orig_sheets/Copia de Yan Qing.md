(Yan Qing) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 40% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Yan Qing) Doppelganger — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, Yan Qing can disguise himself as any other character (figurine), including Masters and Servants. However, it is only the appearance. All of Yan Qing’s Parameters and Stats remain the same. Skills and Noble Phantasms except Presence Concealment cannot be used while Doppelganger is Active. Can be deactivated at any time; and is automatically deactivated when Yan Qing takes damage equal to 10% of his Max Health or more in one Attack.   
When Doppelganger is manually deactivated by Yan Qing, apply the following effects to himself-  
1\. Restore 3 Agility.  
2\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30% after calculation; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Apply Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%.  
4\. Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30% after calculation.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns after deactivation.  
Note: As this NP effect would only be effective if Fog of War is implemented, if Fog of War is not implemented, it would have the same effect as Presence Concealment — Rank: B+, and the four above effects would be applied if PC was deactivated due to Yan Qing performing an Attack, or natural expiration.
