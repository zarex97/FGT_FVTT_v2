(ProtoGil) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C (E)  
Gilgamesh’s Magic Resistance is elevated from Rank E to Rank C due to his golden armor.  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage received is reduced by 3d20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(ProtoGil) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP.

(ProtoGil) Charisma — Rank: A+  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 27 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 27%; if NP, 12%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(ProtoGil) Golden Rule — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Gilgamesh performs a Crit, reduce his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn. If none of his NP are on Cooldown, reduce the Turn count of Enki by 1 Turn if it has been activated.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn.. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(ProtoGil) Treasury of Babylon — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Gilgamesh’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Gilgamesh) Enkidu: Chains of Heaven — Rank: ??? \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Divine\]  
Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals normal damage and has a 50% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. Damage dealt and chance of inflicting Stun increases the higher the Divinity Rank of the DU.  
Rank EX: Damage dealt increased by 150%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 100%.  
Rank A: Damage dealt increased by 100%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 50%.  
Rank B: Damage dealt increased by 80%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 40%.  
Rank C: Damage dealt increased by 60%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 30%.  
Rank D: Damage dealt increased by 40%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 20%.  
Rank E: Damage dealt increased by 20%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 10%.  
If the DU has the ‘Divine’ Attribute but no ‘Divinity’ Skill, treat it as if it has Rank A Divinity. Additionally, if the DU has the ‘Divine’ Attribute, Debuff Immune effects cannot prevent Stun from being inflicted, even those which specifically affect Stun (Debuff Resist can still reduce the chance\!).  
Does not decrease Master’s Health when used.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns
