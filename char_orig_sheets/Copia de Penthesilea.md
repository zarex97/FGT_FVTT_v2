(Penthesilea) Hatred of Achilles  
At any time, if there is a Greek Male Unit (regardless of enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area around Penthesilea, her Mad Enhancement is immediately activated regardless of Cooldown or any other factors. Mad Enhancement cannot be deactivated until there are no Greek Male Units within a 4 panel area of Penthesilea.  
During your Turn, if there are any Greek Male Units (regardless of enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area of Penthesilea, Penthesilea will ignore all orders/Player commands; she will constantly Move towards and Attack said Unit. This counts towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn.  
This effect can be disabled for 1◈ Turns by spending one Command Spell. Note: Does not deactivate Mad Enhancement\!

(Penthesilea) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, its Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. This Servant’s Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns if its Master is defeated while ME is Active.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).

(Penthesilea) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP.

(Penthesilea) Charisma — Rank: B  
Effects are negated and cannot be used when Mad Enhancement is activated.   
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 20 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Penthesilea) Golden Rule (Beauty) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, does not affect Instakill, Death, & Erase; and applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Penthesilea) Howl of the War God — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has several effects-  
1\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Penthesilea. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt which uses BA(STR) is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
2\. This effect is applied to herself. Applies Atk Up (GreekMale) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Male Units from the Greece region is increased by 100% including NP.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Penthesilea) Goddess of War: War God's Military Sash — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) The effect of this Noble Phantasm is only active when Mad Enhancement is deactivated. Grants the following effects to Penthesilea-  
1\. Whenever Penthesilea deals damage through a Normal Attack, roll a four-sided die. Damage dealt is increased by (Number rolled x 10)%.  
2\. Whenever Penthesilea receives damage, roll a four-sided die. Damage received is reduced by (Number rolled x 10)%; if NP, the magnitude of the effect is halved.  
3\. Evade rolls are reduced by 1d4.  
4\. Penthesilea’s Divinity Rank is increased from B to A.  
(Divinity — Rank: A)  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP.

(Penthesilea) Outrage Amazon: A Hero to Die on My Rage — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Only usable when Mad Enhancement is activated. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3.5x damage and inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
