(Gilles) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Gilles) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: \-  
See ‘Prelati’s Spellbook’.

(Gilles) Mental Pollution — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 25%.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by Mental Debuffs is reduced by 100%.

(Gilles) Appreciation of the Arts — Rank: E-  
(Passive 1\) When a NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute is used within a 3 panel area of Gilles, he has a 15% chance of learning the True Name of that NP (the information of that NP is copied to your Notebook).  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken from a NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute is reduced by 5%. 

Note: NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute would be any Noble Phantasm involving or related to artistic, literary, musical creations.

(Gilles) Blasphemy Aesthetic — Rank: C+++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Gilles. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 20%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (A) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 10%; and Luck is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn (B) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 10%; if NP, 20%.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Gilles) Evil Gaze of the Abyss — Rank: B+++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Gilles. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Terror for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Stun from Terror is 40% (40% Terror).  
2\. Inflicts Curse.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
4\. Applies Dmg Up (Curse) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with Curse is increased by 30%.  
5\. Applies the ‘Gazing’ buff for 1◈ Turns to himself, Normal Attacks inflict Curse; while Normal Attacks from all Horrors within a 2 panel area of himself have a 50% chance of inflicting Curse.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Gilles) Summoning: Horrors  
Spell. Can be used as a Counter. Roll a four-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Horrors summoned by Gilles (they will appear within a 5x5 panel area around Gilles). Counts as Gilles Attack for the Turn.  
Horrors have 100 Health, 10 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=1, MOV=3 and Base Attack (STR) is 100, Detect=2 panels. Horrors have the ‘Dark (Outsider)’ Attribute.  
Horrors do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Horror can only Move/Attack once per Turn.  
When a Horror is defeated, Gilles Flips a Coin. If Heads, the defeated Horror turns into two new Horrors. This effect does not activate if the Horror was defeated with Light damage or Attack that deals extra damage to ‘Dark’ or ‘Outsider’ Attribute Units.  
Enemy Units cannot Attack Gilles or his Master if there are any Horrors directly next to them.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Summoning: Elder Horror. Cannot be used if Prelati's Spellbook is on Cooldown.  
Cooldown: (Number of Horrors x ⅔◈) Turns

(Gilles) Summoning: Elder Horror  
Spell. Can be used as a Counter. Summons an Elder Horror on a panel next to Gilles. Counts as Gilles’ Attack for the Turn.  
The Elder Horror has 300 Health, 13 Agility, 7 Luck, Range=2, MOV=4 and Base Attack (STR) is 125, Detect=3 panels. Elder Horrors have the ‘Dark (Outsider)’ Attribute.  
Elder Horrors do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Elder Horror can only Move/Attack once per Turn.  
When an Elder Horror is defeated, Gilles rolls a four-sided die. If a 3 is rolled, the defeated Elder Horror turns into three new Horrors. This effect does not activate if the Elder Horror was defeated with Light damage or Attack that deals extra damage to ‘Dark’ or ‘Outsider’ Attribute Units.  
Enemy Units cannot Attack Gilles or his Master if there are any Elder Horrors directly next to them.  
Cannot be used if Prelati's Spellbook is on Cooldown. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Summoning: Horror.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Gilles) Prelati's Spellbook: Textbook of the Sunken Spiral Castle — Rank: EX (NP)  
(Passive) The basis for all of Gilles’ Spells. Whenever Gilles is Attacked, if the AU’s AGI Rank is Rank C or higher, it can instead target Prelati’s Spellbook. The options Gilles has are as follows  
1\. Evade: If Gilles fails to Evade, he has a 20% chance of taking the damage instead of Prelati’s Spellbook; otherwise Prelati’s Spellbook is destroyed.  
2\. Block: Gilles takes the damage instead of Prelati’s Spellbook.  
3\. Substitution: If Summoning: Horrors, Summoning: Elder Horrors, or Prelati’s Spellbook’s Active effect is not not on Cooldown, choose one of them, it enters Cooldown. If Gilles does this, instead of receiving the Attack, he can Move up to 4 panels; then roll a four-sided die, he summons that number of Horrors around the panel he was previously standing on, with at least one Horror standing on the panel he previously occupied, which is also the Horror that receives the AU’s Attack.  
4\. Luck Check: If successful, Prelati’s Spellbook is not destroyed. Can be used after a failed Evade (1).  
When Prelati’s Spellbook is destroyed, Gilles can no longer use Summoning: Horror, Summoning: Elder Horror, or Prelati’s Spellbook’s Active effect; and all Horrors on the board are immediately destroyed. However, when Prelati’s Spellbook is destroyed, Gilles has a 10% chance of transforming into the Gigantic Horror (see below).  
When Gilles is defeated and Prelati’s Spellbook is not destroyed, he has a 10% chance of transforming into the Gigantic Horror. Otherwise, X number of Elder Horrors appear on the panels around him, where X=number of Servants on the board.  
(Active) \[Anti-Army\] Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x4 or 4x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Gilles for 5x damage; then has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Foreigner-class Servants within a 3 panel area of himself by 1◈ Turns. Also affects non-Foreigner Class Servant Units with the ‘Existence outside the Domain’ Skill or ‘Outsider’ Attribute, but NP Cooldown is reduced by ½◈ Turns instead.   
2\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the DUs, all damage dealt is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the DUs, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Gilles) Prelati’s Spellbook: Gigantic Horror  
Only usable when Gilles’ Health is less than 25% of its maximum value. Has a 10% chance of automatically activating when Prelati’s Spellbook is destroyed, or when Gilles is defeated without Prelati’s Spellbook being destroyed. Transforms himself into a Gigantic Horror. Replace Gilles with the Gigantic Horror, and its new Stats are as follows-  
Health: 2000  
Agility: 0  
Luck: Same as Gilles  
MOV: 0  
Range: 3 panels, 3x3 panel area  
Base Attack (STR): 200 (Used when Range\<3)  
Base Attack (MAG): 200 (Used when Range\>2)  
Sustainability: None  
Attributes: Dark (Outsider), Large  
Detect: Same as Range  
When Gilles turns into the Gigantic Horror, its Stats are fully restored to its new values. Territory Creation and Mental Pollution become inactive; Blasphemy Aesthetic is unusable. When Summoning is used, the number of Horrors summoned is doubled. Evil Eye of the Abyss now hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to itself. The Gigantic Horror is no longer controlled by Gilles’ Master, and Command Spells have no effect; instead the Gigantic Horror takes its Turn during the GM’s Turn (i.e. the last Turn of the Round). When the Gigantic Horror Attacks, it always Attacks the nearest Unit. If there are no Units within its Range for 1 full Round, its Range will increase by 1 at the start of the next Round until it has Attacked a Unit, then it will reset.
