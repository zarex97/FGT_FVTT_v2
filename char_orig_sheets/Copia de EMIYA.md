(EMIYA) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B

(EMIYA) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

Then, apply Dmg Boost for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(EMIYA) Thaumaturgy: Projection Magic — Rank: A+  
(Passive) A Skill which is the basis of his Noble Phantasms. ‘Projection’ Skills and NP cannot be used and have no effects if EMIYA is inflicted with Silence. Note: ‘Projection’ Skills and NP are treated as Thaumaturgy Spells.

(EMIYA) Trace, On — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. If this is not the first time EMIYA has used this Skill in this game, reduce his Health by 5% of its maximum value (cannot drop below 1 in this way).  
2\. Apply one of the following effects of your choice to EMIYA- Activated Circuits (AC) or Blazing Circuits (BC), their effects are listed below.  
3\. Max and current Luck are increased by 5..  
4\. Restores 5 Agility.  
5\. Applies Atk Up (Trace) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%. If EMIYA uses a Thaumaturgy or Projection Skill/NP while he has the Atk Up (Trace) buff, its duration is extended by ⅓◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.  
If this Skill is used while EMIYA already has the Atk Up (Trace) buff, its duration is extended instead of stacking. Then, you can choose to swap from AC to BC or vice-versa.  
Note: EMIYA cannot have both the AC and BC effects at the same time.

Activated Circuits (AC)-  
1\. When EMIYA performs a Normal Attack while Unlimited Blade Works is Active, restore 1 Luck and reduce his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn at the end of the Combat Process.  
2\. The next time EMIYA uses an NP that deals damage, restore 2 Luck and for the rest of the Round, whenever EMIYA performs an Attack his NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of the Combat Process.  
3\. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable except through its own effect- When Atk Up (Trace) is removed from EMIYA, remove the AC effect from him as well.

Blazing Circuits (BC)-   
1\. When EMIYA performs a Normal Attack while Unlimited Blade Works is Active, damage dealt is increased by 50\.  
2\. The next time EMIYA uses an NP that deals damage, for the rest of the Round, all damage dealt by EMIYA is increased by 20% including NP.  
3\. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable except through its own effect- When Atk Up (Trace) is removed from EMIYA, remove the BC effect from him as well.
