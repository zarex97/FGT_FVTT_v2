(Kiritsugu) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 5% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Kiritsugu) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns if Summoned).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Kiritsugu) Magecraft — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Allows the use of Thaumaturgy. Only one Thaumaturgy Spell can be used per Turn.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever a Thaumaturgy Spell is used, extend the duration of the ‘Suppression’ buff by 1◈ Turns

(Kiritsugu) Thaumaturgy: Reinforcement  
Spell. Used at the start of a Combat Phase. During this Combat Phase, Normal Attack damage dealt is increased by 40%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Thaumaturgy: Penetration  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Penetration’ buff to Kiritsugu for ⅓◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- When Kiritsugu deals damage with an Attack, it has the Ignore Def effect and halves the effect of Invuln. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Thaumaturgy: Familiars  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Applies the Range Up buff for 1◈ Turns, Range is increased by 2 panels; and applies Crit Up (Familiar) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance for Attacks at a Range of 3 or higher is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Affection of the Holy Grail — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Kiritsugu’s Luck is increased from Rank E to Rank EX; the Luck Check rolls of all Units within a 2 panel area of Kiritsugu are increased by 4 except himself.  
If Kiritsugu is inflicted with Skill Seal, instead of reducing his Max Luck, all of his Luck Check rolls are increased by 20; and the original effect is negated. These effects only last for the duration of Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Pierce to himself for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp to himself for 1◈  Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. When this Skill is used, all Units within a 2 panel area of Kiritsugu except himself are inflicted with Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Scapegoat — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn or when an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Kiritsugu is Attacked. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Kiritsugu. Applies Decoy (Scapegoat) to that Unit for 1◈ Turns, then applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Kiritsugu or the target, Crit Chance is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Lethal Gunfire Suppression — Rank: B+  
(Passive) Whenever a Unit inflicted with Decoy (Scapegoat) is Attacked, if that AU is within Kiritsugu’s Range, Kiritsugu can instantly perform a Normal Attack on that AU which cannot be Reacted to unless the AU’s AGI Rank is higher than Kiritsugu’s. Additionally, Kiritsugu can use a Thaumaturgy Spell once before performing this Normal Attack (Note: This does not count towards the one Thaumaturgy Spell usage per Turn, but it will still enter Cooldown).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 4 Luck.  
2\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Suppression’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 5 times; its effects are as follows- Successful Normal Attacks remove 1 buff from the DU at the start of the Damage Step and if a buff was successfully removed, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to Kiritsugu, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Chronos Rose: Pick Ye Rosebuds While Ye May — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Time Alter. Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3.5x damage plus 100 that has the Ignore Def effect, then inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%, and increase the DU’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Kiritsugu) Phantasm Punishment: Mystery Bisection — Rank: C+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=1. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3x damage plus 100 with a 35% chance of inflicting Instakill if damage was dealt. Then, apply the ‘Kiritsugu’ debuff to the DU, the Unit’s Base Attack (both STR and MAG) are reduced by half of their original value; this effect ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects, and the ‘Kiritsugu’ debuff is Unremovable. The ‘Kiritsugu’ debuff does not stack. Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.
