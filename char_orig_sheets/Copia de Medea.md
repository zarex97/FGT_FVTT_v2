(Medea) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Medea) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Medea) High-Speed Divine Words — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Completely reduce the cooldown of all of Medea’s Spells. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
Note: This Skill cannot be used and its effects are negated while inflicted with Silence.

(Medea) Golden Fleece — Rank: \-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Recovers Health by Loving the Skin. Restores Medea’s Health by 30% of its maximum value, and restores 3 Agility. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Medea) Teachings of Circe — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an allied Unit within Range. Remove all debuffs from it, restores its Health by 10% of its maximum value, and apply NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Medea) Μαρδοξ

Trofa. Spell. Used when Attacked. Automatically Evades the Attack. If the Attack was a Noble Phantasm, Medea has a 50% chance of automatically Evading the NP. If Failed, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Κεραινο.

Atlas. Spell. Used during your Turn. Inflicts Stun on an enemy Unit within Range for 1◈ Turns. Chance of inflicting Stun is reduced by 25% on Units with a MAG Rank of B or higher; chance of inflicting Stun is reduced by 25% on Units with a Magic Resistance of Rank B or higher; this reduction does stack. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Medea) Dragon Tooth Warriors  
Spell. Roll a six-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Dragon Tooth Warriors conjured by Medea (they will appear within a 5x5 panel area around Medea). Then, roll a 4-sided die for each one. The number rolled represents which type of Dragon Tooth Warrior was conjured.  
1\. Dragon Tooth Warrior (Blade): 50 Health, 10 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=1, MOV=4 and Base Attack (STR)=100, Detect: 1 panel  
2\. Dragon Tooth Warrior (Bow): 50 Health, 10 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=3, MOV=4 and Base Attack (STR)=75, Detect: 3 panels  
3\. Dragon Tooth Warrior (Daggers): 50 Health, 12 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=1, MOV=5 and Base Attack (STR)=75, Detect: 2 panels  
4\. Your choice of Blade, Bow or Daggers  
Dragon Tooth Warriors do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Dragon Tooth Warrior can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Medea or her Master if any Dragon Tooth Warriors are directly next to them. Cooldown: (Number of Dragon Tooth Warriors x ⅔◈) Turns. 

(Medea) Rain of Light: Machia Hecatia Graea  
Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 3x damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

Rule Breaker: Destroyer of All Talismans. Range=1. Uses Base Attack (STR). Deals 1x damage plus 100 and removes all buffs from the DU. If the DU is a Servant (and it Failed to Evade the NP), Rule Breaker will cut the Servant’s Contract with its Master and remove the Master’s Command Spells; the Servant’s Contract, along with three Command Spells, will be given to Medea. Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.
