(Arjuna) Fated Rivals of the Mahabharata  
(Passive) If Karna is in the opposing Faction and within Range of Arjuna, they will only Attack each other. Karna’s Master can spend one Command Spell to negate this effect for 1◈ Turns, but it will only affect Arjuna.

(Arjuna) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Arjuna) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Arjuna) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Arjuna) Clairvoyance (Bowman) — Rank: B+  
(Passive) When Arjuna performs a Normal Attack at a Range of 3 or higher, the DU has a 90% chance of using Evade- when Evading. Otherwise, the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
2\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, does not affect Instakill/Death/Erase.   
3\. Applies Range Up (Clairvoyance) for 1◈ Turns, Range is increased by 1 panel, does not stack.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Arjuna) The Awarded Hero — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Regen for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Health is restored by 15% of its maximum value at the end of his Turn, at the end of any Turn he Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp (Awarded) for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10% for himself; and 5% for other allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
4\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Arjuna) Mana Burst (Flames) — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used when Arjuna performs a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, Arjuna’s Base Attack used is his BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (e.g. BA=325). Has a 50% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns on the DU. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Fire Damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Roar of the Flame God.

(Arjuna) Roar of the Flame God — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Agni’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, Normal Attacks deal Fire damage with a 30% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns; does not apply to & is not consumed by Attacks used with Mana Burst (Flames).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Mana Burst (Flames).

(Arjuna) Agni Gandiva: Flame God’s Yell — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Mana Burst (Flames), or if Mana Burst (Flames) is in Cooldown. Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. BA=325) is used, effect of Magic Resistance is halved. Range=7. Has the Aim effect. Deals 4x damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns on the DU. Fire damage. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns. When Agni Gandiva is used, Mana Burst (Flames) also enters Cooldown.

(Arjuna) Pashupata: Raised Arm of the Destruction God — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

Then, inflicts Def Dwn on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
