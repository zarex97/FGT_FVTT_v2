(Cú Chulainn) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Cú Chulainn) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Cú Chulainn) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Cú Chulainn) Shoal of Four Branches — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Cú is defeated he is revived with 25% of his Max Health.  
2\. Applies the Atk Up (Redbranch) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by (20+X)%; if NP, this value is halved. X is as follows- For every 10% of Health Cú is missing, X increases by 10 (e.g. Cú’s Health is 457/1000, so X=50).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Cú Chulainn) Disengage — Rank: C  
(Active) This Skill is used at the start of a Combat Phase when Cú is Attacked. The enemy Unit’s Attack is cancelled, Cú is allowed to Move 3 panels, then he restores his Health by 15% of its maximum value and removes 1 debuff from himself. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Cú Chulainn) Rune Magic — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Roll a four-sided die and apply the corresponding effect. Then, you can apply one more effect of your choice other than the one selected by the die.  
1\. Applies the ‘Tiwaz’ buff for 1◈ Turns, his Rank of Magic Resistance is increased by 1 Rank.  
2\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns  
Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.

Ath nGabla. Spell. Range=2. Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit; can only be used when there is at least one enemy Unit facing Cú Chulainn. When this Spell is used, the DU rolls a six-sided die, and performs that many Combat Phases with Cú Chulainn (The Unit with higher Agility goes first in each Combat Phase; if equal, Flip a Coin to decide). Both the DU and Cú Chulainn can Move between Combat Phases, but the maximum distance between the two Units must be 4 panels (clueless on how to make this work properly, but leaving it in anyway). When Ath nGabla is Active, the Rank of ‘Barbed Spear that Pierces with Death’ is increased from B to A; it deals 4x damage instead and Cooldown is increased to 7◈ Turns. Cooldown: X◈ Turns after Ath nGabla ends, X being number rolled on the six-sided die.

(Cú Chulainn) Protection from Arrows — Rank: B  
(Passive) Cú will automatically Evade any Attack from a Ranged weapon (arrows, firearms, thrown weapons, magic projectiles, etc) including Noble Phantasms from his Front and Sides; when he receives an aforementioned Attack from Behind (i.e. the 3 panels behind him) and he Evades, the Evade roll is reduced by 2\. Does not apply to Ranged AoE Attacks/Noble Phantasms. Has no effect if this Unit is affected by Skill Seal.

(Cú Chulainn) Gáe Bolg: Barbed Spear that Pierces with Death — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. Has the Aim effect. When this NP is used, the DU performs a Luck Check. If Failed, the DU is inflicted with Instakill and Curse (Bolg). If the Luck Check succeeded or Instakill was not inflicted, this NP deals 3.5x damage and inflicts Curse (Bolg). Curse (Bolg) is separate from normal Curse, does not stack, and is Unremovable; when Cú Chulainn is defeated, Curse (Bolg) is removed from all affected Units. Chance of this NP inflicting Instakill on Units with Invuln is reduced by 50%, while chance of it inflicting Instakill on Units with Anti-Purge is reduced by 100%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Cú Chulainn) Gáe Bolg: Soaring Spear that Strikes with Death — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Range=4. BA(STR) and half of BA(MAG) combined (e.g. BA=200) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage plus 100\. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns

Note: Barbed Spear that Pierces with Death cannot be used if Soaring Spear that Strikes with Death is on Cooldown and vice versa.
