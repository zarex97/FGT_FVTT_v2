# **Rules – Basic**

How to Play  
Each Player controls seven Servants and seven Masters. Victory is obtained by either defeating all enemy Units or obtaining the Holy Grail.  
OR  
Each Faction has 7 players cooperating with each other. Each player controls one Servant-Master pair. Victory is obtained by either defeating all enemy units or obtaining the Holy Grail.

Note: For the sake of convenience, this manual will have most of its game rules in the 3 Player format.

Note 2: If you like, one Faction can be controlled by multiple Players working together, so you could have 2 Players cooperating on each Faction.

Terminology  
A few terms will be used in this game and manual.  
AU: Attacking Unit  
DU: Defending Unit  
CS: Command Spell  
NP: Noble Phantasm  
MC: Magic Crest  
Stats: Health, Agility and Luck  
GM: Game Master; Overseer has the same meaning  
Board: The game board. Board and Field have the same meaning

Roll for Health (S): 10d20
Roll for Agility (S): 1d10+10
Roll for Luck: 1d20
Roll for Health (M): 5d20
Roll for Agility (M): 1d12

Turns and Rounds:  
A Player/Faction may only Move and initiate Attacks during its (Faction’s) Turn. When all Players’/Factions’ Turns are over, the Round ends. A Round is composed of 3 Turns (Both players’/Faction’s Turns, and the GM’s Turn).

Difficulty Levels  
This game can be played with varying methods/difficulty levels.  
Beginner: Damage modifiers & Luck Check removed.  
Intermediate: Luck Check removed.  
Expert: Nothing removed.  
Lunatic: Random Event rate up.

Note: To play without damage modifiers, just use Base Attack for damage calculation instead of Attack+/Attack-.

# **Rules – Stats**

Brief Explanation of Unit Stats  
1\. Base Health: Unit’s Base Health. Used to determine the Unit’s Max Health.  
2\. MOV: The maximum number of panels a Unit can Move during its Turn. Units are not allowed to Move diagonally.  
3\. Range: The Range and number of targets a Unit can attack during Combat.  
4\. Base Attack (STR). The Base Attack of a Unit, used by most Units. Used to determine damage dealt during Combat.  
5\. Base Attack (MAG). The Base Attack of a Unit, used by Casters and Masters. Used to determine damage dealt during Combat.  
6\. Sustainability. The number of Turns a Servant can remain on the field before disappearing after its Master is killed. For more information, see ‘Contracts and Sustainability’.  
7\. Noble Phantasm. The remaining number of times a Servant can use its Noble Phantasm(s).  
8\. Command Spells. The remaining number of Command Spells a Master has.  
9\. ZON. See ‘Rules \- Relationships’.

1\. Health: Unit's Health. The Unit is defeated when this drops below 0\. Max Health determines the maximum amount of Health the Unit has. (Current Health/Max Health)  
2\. Agility: Unit's Agility. Used for a variety of purposes. Max Agility determines the maximum amount of Agility the Unit has. (Current Agility/Max Agility)  
3\. Luck: Unit's Luck. Used for a variety of purposes. Max Luck determines the maximum amount of Luck the Unit has. (Current Luck/Max Luck)

# **Rules – Before Starting**

Players have a choice of playing with the regular board (13x13) or the large board (25x25). Gameplay is the same on both boards.

Day-Night Cycle  
When the game starts, Flip a Coin. If Heads, the first Round is ‘Day’. The next Round will be ‘Night’ and so on. If Tails, the first Round is ‘Night’. The next Round will be ‘Day’ and so on.  
During a Day Round, all damage received by Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute is increased by 25%, all damage dealt by Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute is reduced by 25%. Vice versa during a Night Round.

Before the Game  
Before the game begins, a number of procedures have to be carried out to determine certain values for all units.

(Servants)-  
Determining Max Health: For every Servant, Flip a Coin, then Roll for Health(S). If Heads, the Max Health is the Base Health plus the rolled value. If Tails, the Max Health is the Base Health minus the rolled value. Input the value for Max Health in the Servant's description and player's Notebook.

Determining Max Agility: For every Servant, Roll for Agility(S). This value is the Max Agility of each Servant. For the following Servants, add/deduct the following values to/from their Max Agility.  
Lancer: \+2  
Rider: \+1  
Caster: \-2  
Assassin: \+3  
Input the value for Max Agility in the Servant's description and player's Notebook.

Determining Max Luck: For every Servant, Roll for Luck. This value is the Max Luck of each Servant. For the following Servants, add/deduct the following values to/from their Max Luck.  
Lancer: \-2  
Assassin: \+2  
Input the value for Max Luck in the Servant's description and player's Notebook.

High Rank Masters: Base Attack (MAG)=125, ZON+1 panel, Servant’s Sustainability \+1◈ Turns  
Low Rank Masters: Base Attack (MAG)=100.

(Masters)-

Determining Max Agility: For every Master, Roll for Agility(M). This value is the Max Agility of each Master. Input the value for Max Agility in the Master's description and player's Notebook.

Determining Max Luck: For every Master, Roll for Luck. This value is the Max Luck of each Master. Input the value for Max Luck in the Master's description and player's Notebook.

# **Rules – Contracts & Sustainability**

All Servants are Contracted to their corresponding Masters. There are a few circumstances where a Servant would lose this Contract and they have different terms.  
Unbound Servant: A Servant whose Master has lost/used up his Command Seals. Servant is still controlled by the player, but is susceptible to Contracts from other parties.  
Free Servant: A Servant whose Master has been killed. Servant is still controlled by the player, but is susceptible to Contracts from other parties.

Masters and Caster are able to form a new Contract with both Unbound and Free Servants, but with different requirements.

Unbound Servant-  
Unbound Servants cannot be contracted by allied Masters/Caster, but can be contracted by enemy Masters/Caster. To form a Contract with an Unbound Servant, the Master/Caster must be on a panel next to the Unbound Servant. Use the ‘Contract Servant’ roll. If a 6 is rolled, the Contract is Successful. If not, then the Contract is Failed.

Free Servant-  
Free Servants can be contracted by both allied and enemy Masters/Caster. To form a Contract with a Free Servant, the Master/Caster must be on a panel next to the Free Servant. Allied Masters/Caster do not require a dice roll to form a Contract (always Successful). For enemy Masters/Caster, use the ‘Contract Servant’ roll. If a 5 or 6 is rolled, the Contract is Successful. If not, then the Contract is Failed.

Masters/Casters cannot attempt a Contract Servant roll if there is another enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of itself.

If the contracting of an Unbound or a Free Servant is Successful, the Master/Caster gains 3 Command Spells that can only be used on that Servant.

When a Master kills an enemy Master, or a Servant kills an enemy Master while within a 2 panel area of its own Master, or Caster kills a Master, the Master/Caster automatically Contracts the dead enemy Master’s Servant and gains all of the dead Master’s remaining Command Spells. The Command Spells obtained from the killed Master can only be used on the corresponding Servant.

If a Master has more than one Servant Contracted to it, at the end of its Turn, if more than one of its Servants Acted during that Turn, that Master loses 20 Health per additional Servant. If a Master has 20 Health or less, it cannot order more than one of its Servants to Act during its Turn.  
Note: Does not apply in the Grand Order Holy Grail War.

Sustainability-  
When a Servant’s Master is defeated, it becomes a Free Servant. After a set number of Turns, the Free Servant will disappear (be removed from the field). The number of Turns before a Free Servant disappears is determined by its Sustainability.

When a Free Servant uses its Noble Phantasm, it immediately disappears after use. If a Free Archer or Caster uses its Noble Phantasm (a), its Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns; while if it uses its Noble Phantasm (b), it immediately disappears after use.

# **Rules – Relationships**

When a Servant is too far from its Master, its strength will be reduced. All Masters have an ‘Effective Servant Zone’, otherwise known as ZON. When a Servant is outside its Master’s ZON, all damage dealt will be reduced.  
When a Servant deals damage with an Attack while outside of its Master’s ZON, damage dealt is reduced by 5d10 (i.e. the Damage Modifier roll).  
   
When a Master is standing directly next to its Servant, the Master’s MOV is increased by 1\.

When a Master becomes the target of an Attack while their Servant is within a 2 panel area of themselves, perform the following checks-

1\. If the Target Unit is a Master whose Servant is within the AU’s Range, the AU can only target the Servant.

2\. If the Target Unit is a Master whose Servant is not within the AU’s Range but standing on a panel directly next to their Master, the AU can target the Master, but the target is automatically changed to their Servant at the start of the Combat Phase.

3\. If the Target Unit is a Master whose Servant is not within the AU’s Range and also not standing on a panel directly next to their Master- At the start of the Combat Phase, the Master’s Servant performs an Agility Check (against the AU). If successful, the Servant Moves to the nearest panel which is directly next to their Master and the Attack target is changed to the Servant; if Failed, Combat proceeds as normal. Note: If the Servant has an effect which prevents it from Moving (e.g. Immobilize), then it cannot perform this Agility Check.

Units are not allowed to enter a 1 panel area of enemy Masters if that Master's Servant is within 2 panels of its Master.

When a Master that has its Servant within a 2 panel area of itself gets caught in an AoE Noble Phantasm and fails to Evade, the Servant performs an Agility Check/Agility Check-. If Successful, the Servant shoves (Moves) its Master out of the NP area (the Master is Moved to one panel outside of the NP area), and the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
If Failed, the Master receives no damage and effects, while the Total Damage the Servant takes from the AoE NP is increased by 100%; and in this situation, Servants cannot Evade the enemy Unit’s AoE NP if their Master is within a 2 panel range of them. If a Master in this situation has more than one Servant, and more than one of its Servants are within a 2 panel area of itself, the increase in Total Damage taken by the Servants are divided by the number of Servants Covering for their Master (Example: Master with two Servants within a 2 panel area of itself fails to Evade an AoE NP, so the Total Damage received by that Master’s Servants are increased by 50% respectively).

When a Master in the same conditions as the above paragraph gets caught in a non-NP AoE Attack, the above process can be performed, but it is optional. This Action will be termed as ‘Cover’.  
Note: If a Servant fails to Shove their Master out of an AoE NP, but that Servant is not within the NP area, that Servant cannot Cover for their Master.

An exception to the above three paragraphs is the ‘Presence Concealment’ Skill. For more information, read Assassin’s Bio and Info.  
While a Servant is affected by Charm, Confuse, Berserk, Stun, Stop, Petrify, Freeze, Sleep, or any other effect that prevents a Servant from Acting, the effects in the above three paragraphs are negated.

When CS: Teleport Servant is used by a Master when it is Attacked, the AU’s target is switched to the newly-teleported Servant. The newly-teleported Servant cannot use Evade or Block in this Combat Process.

# **The Holy Grail**

After a certain number of Servants are defeated (recommended number is nine, or at least seven), the Holy Grail will appear on a random panel on the field excluding Home Bases.  
In order to obtain the Holy Grail, a Unit must remain on a panel next to the Grail for 1 full Round, without any enemy Units within the Grail Area. (Grail Area=2 panels)  
The Grail cannot be obtained by a Unit if there are any enemy Units within the Grail Area.  
The game is won when one Faction has obtained the Grail.  
Note: A disappeared Servant counts towards the number of Servants needed for the Grail to materialize (but not if inflicted with Erase).  
If the Holy Grail is hit by an AoE NP that deals damage, it has a chance of being destroyed. The chance is X%, where X=the amount of damage dealt by the NP divided by 20\. If the Holy Grail is destroyed, there are no winners. Everyone loses. (The Overseer wins?)

Random Events  
The Overseer may introduce Random Events into the game at any time whatsoever.

Civilian-  
From time to time, a Civilian may appear on the field. If a Servant Attacks a Civilian, the Civilian is instantly killed. The Servant recovers 100 Health and 1 Agility. (On Lunatic, there should always be at least 2 Civilians on the board.)
