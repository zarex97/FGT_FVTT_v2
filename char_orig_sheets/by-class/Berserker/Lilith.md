(Kriemhild) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Passive) When Mashu Kyrielight is within a 2 panel area of herself, her Mad Enhancement is constantly Active and cannot be deactivated. In this situation, Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, its Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated. While this Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.

(Lilith) Divinity — Rank B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Lilith) Hatred — Rank C  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the 'Living Human' Attribute is increased by 10% including NP.

(Lilith) Like a Radiant Night — Rank EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 150% chance of inflicting Charm (Lilith) for 1◈ Turns (Has the same effect as normal Charm, does not stack with it).  
2\. Inflicts Crit DmDwn (Lilith) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is reduced by 50% if this Unit is also inflicted with Charm (Lilith), while it is reduced by 30% if it does not have Charm (Lilith).  
3\. Then, Lilith applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lilith) Like the Wings that Dance — Rank A  
(Passive) All Wind damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs from Attacks which deal Wind damage are reduced by 50%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Agility and 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
4\. Applies the Unremovable ‘Lilit’ effect for 2◈ Turns. If she already has it, then its duration is extended by 1◈ Turns instead.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

The effects of ‘Lilit’ are as follows-  
1\. Gains the ‘Levitating’ Attribute.  
2\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 to 2 deal 20% extra Wind damage.  
3\. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 3\.  
4\. Gains the use of the ‘Nightveil’ Attack Skill.

(Lilith) Like an Ugly Love — Rank C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Pierce for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Mental Debuff ResDwn for 2◈ Turns to herself, chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Lilith) Nightveil  
Attack Skill. Can only be used while Lilith has the ‘Lilit’ effect. Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, deal 50% damage to all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself; then select a DU which was successfully hit and deal normal Wind damage to it. If a DU Blocked the first hit, it must also Block the second hit; otherwise the DU cannot React to the second hit. Only the chosen DU of the second hit can Counter this Attack. Next, extend the duration of the ‘Lilit’ effect by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Lilith) Ishá Ra'á: Falsehood is the Daughter of Darkness — Rank B++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. First, applies Dmg Up (Lawful) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the 'Lawful' Alignment is increased by 30%; and applies Dmg Up (Good) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the 'Good' Alignment is increased by 30%. Deals 3.5x damage plus 200, damage dealt is further increased by 150% if the DU is inflicted with Charm; 200% if it was Charm (Lilith). Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Poison.  
2\. Inflicts Curse.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
If Mad Enhancement is Active, the chance of inflicting these debuffs are reduced by 20%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Ishá’ effect for 1◈ Turns, a Unit with this effect has both the ‘Chaotic’ and ‘Evil’ Attributes. This is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.  
   
(Lilith) For Lilim: O’ My Foul Beloved Children — Rank EX (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is not Active. During your Turn, this NP can be used to create up to 3 Lilim. The maximum number of Lilim on the field is 3\. Lilims have 18 Agility and 3 MOV. Each Lilim can Move once during your Turn and do not count towards the number of Units which Move during your Turn. When a Lilim is successfully Attacked, it is defeated. Whenever a Lilim is created using this NP, Lilith loses 30 Health for each Lilim created. Does not reduce her Master’s Health when used. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) If there is a Lilim within her Master's ZON, the area of that ZON is extended to a 2 panel area around that Lilim. Then, if a different Lilim is within the extended ZON of the first Lilim, that ZON is extended again and so on.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Lilith performs a successful non-NP Attack, roll 1 ten-sided die for every Lilim within a 2 panel area of the DU at the start of the Damage Step and deal that much Fixed STR damage to the DU.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever an enemy Unit would perform an Agility Check, their roll is increased by 1 for every Lilim within a 2 panel area of that Unit.  
(Active) Range+2 for the Combat Process. First, Lilith loses 30 Health (cannot drop below 1 in this way, cannot be used below 30 Health). Deals 300 MAG damage to a 3x3 panel area within Range, Total damage dealt to Units with the ‘Good’ Alignment is tripled and ignores Magic Resistance; this MAG damage is unaffected by any damage modifying effects on Lilith. Then, apply the following effects to herself-  
1\. The maximum number of Lilim on the field is permanently reduced by 1\. If the maximum number of Lilims are currently on the field when this NP is used, choose one to be deleted at the start of the Combat Phase.  
2\. Max Luck is permanently reduced by 1, then lose 1 Luck.  
3\. Base Attack (MAG) is permanently reduced by 20\.  
Does not reduce her Master’s Health when used. Can only be used once per Round.
