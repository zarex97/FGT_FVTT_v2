(Atalante) Beastly Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
(Active) This Skill is categorized as Mad Enhancement — Rank: A. Used during your Turn. When Beastly Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Unit’s Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 25 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 80% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Unit’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While Beastly Enhancement is Active, this Unit can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Beastly Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while it is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
(Passive 2\) When Beastly Enhancement is Active, Atalante’s Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Curse.

(Atalante) Self Evolution — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 25%.

(Atalante) Demonic Habituation — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt at a Range of 1 is increased by 25%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up to herself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself except herself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp (Habituation) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt at a Range of 2 or lower is increased by 50%.   
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Atalante) Crossing Arcadia — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Moving, Atalanta can ignore all obstacles in her path (but still cannot enter the area around an enemy Master if…see Master-Servant Relationships). Also, Atalanta can Move, Attack, then Move again, as long as the total number of panels Moved does not exceed Atalanta’s MOV. (Same effect as Riding Skill’s Double Move.)  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel range of Atalanta. Has different effects on Atalanta and on other allied Units-  
1\. Atalanta: Restore her Agility by 25% of its maximum value; then applies Atk Up (Arcadia) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Other affected Units: Restore all affected Units’ Agility by 25% of their maximum value; then applies Atk Up (Arcadia) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%. Also applies MOV Up for 1 time to all affected Units, MOV is increased by 3\.  
Note: ~~The Atk Up (Arcadia) buff is removed from a Unit at the end of the Turn if that Unit has Moved during that Turn.~~ The effect of Atk Up (Arcadia) is the same as normal Atk Up; however it has no effect until after an affected Unit has Moved once, and also the Atk Up (Arcadia) buff is removed at the end of the Turn if an affected Unit has Moved during that Turn.  
While Beastly Enhancement is Active, the effects of Crossing Arcadia only apply to herself (i.e. Effect 2 does not activate).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Atalante) Feral Logic — Rank: B  
(Passive) Effect 6 of Beastly Enhancement is negated.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. If Beastly Enhancement is Active, this Skill automatically activates at the start of a Combat Phase when Atalante is Attacked if it is not on Cooldown. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns and apply Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. 

(Atalante) Tauropolos Skia Thermokrasia: The Arrow that Eclipsed The Somber Sky — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) and 40% of Atalante’s Base Attack (MAG) is used (120+70=190), not affected by Magic Resistance. Range=Atalante’s MOV. When this NP is used, Atalante first Moves to a panel next to the DU, then deals 4x damage and inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns, and also inflicts Curse. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
