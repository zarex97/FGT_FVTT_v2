(Morgan) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 60% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Morgan) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Morgan) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: EX  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 75%; this effect is halved for Instakill (40%), and further halved for Death (20%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 75%; this effect is halved for Instakill (40%), and further halved for Death (20%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Morgan) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Morgan) Glam Sight: Fairy Eyes — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Morgan is Attacked, Crit Chance of the AU is reduced by 25%.

(Morgan) Charisma of Desire — Rank: B

2\. Reduces Morgan’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns to all affected enemy Units, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Morgan) Beyond the Furthest Ends of the World — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Morgan is defeated she is revived with 25% of her Max Health.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Saihate’ buff for 1◈ Turns.    
5\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns  
The effects of the ‘Saihate’ buff are as follows-  
1\. At the end of Morgan’s Turn and at the end of any Turn she Acts, inflict Atk Dwn for ⅓◈ Turns to all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. At the end of Morgan’s Turn and at the end of any Turn she Acts, inflict Crit Dwn for ⅓◈ Turns to all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%.

(Morgan) Roadless Camelot: The Now Unreachable Utopia — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Fortress\]

Then, hits a 3x6 or 6x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Morgan for 5x damage and inflicts Curse; damage dealt to Units with the \[Man\] Attribute is further increased by 200%.  
After that, applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 40%.
