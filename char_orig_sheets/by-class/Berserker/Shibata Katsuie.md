(Shibata) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Cannot be deactivated while Kakare Shibata is Active (Note: Cooldown still progresses while Kakare Shibata is Active). Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated. If Kakare Shibata is Active, his Master’s Health cannot drop below 10 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 60% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated. If Shibata is inflicted with Skill Seal while Kakare Shibata is Active, Mad Enhancement is deactivated for the duration of Skill Seal, but Kakare Shibata is not deactivated.

(Shibata) Kakare Shibata: The Advancing Shibata — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging/Passive/Activation) This NP can only be activated when Shibata is within his Home Base. When this NP is Active, Mad Enhancement is automatically Activated; and Shibata can only Move 1 panel per Turn, must Move during his Turn, and will automatically Move at the end of any Turn he Acts. However, every time he Moves, apply the following effects to him at the end of the Move-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (Shibata) to himself, all damage dealt is increased by 5% including NP. Atk Up (Shibata) is Unremovable.  
2\. Applies Def Up (Shibata) to himself, all damage taken is reduced by 5% including NP. Def Up (Shibata) is Unremovable.  
3\. His Health is restored by 2% of its maximum value.  
The maximum magnitude of both Atk Up (Shibata) and Def Up (Shibata) are 75%.  
While this NP is Active, ZON does not apply to Shibata and his Master. While this NP is Active, Shibata’s Master’s Health loss from Shibata’s activated Mad Enhancement is halved.  
When Shibata takes his 15th step (Moves for the 15th time) while this NP is Active, he is teleported back to his Home Base at the end of that Turn, and all Atk Up (Shibata) & Def Up (Shibata) buffs are removed from him.  
Note: The first step (panel) that is counted is the first step **outside** Shibata’s Home Base. Additionally, when he is teleported back, he is teleported to the border within his Home Base, and not in the middle.  
Shibata can deactivate Kakare Shibata during his Turn, at the end of any Turn he Acts, or at the end of the Round. When Kakare Shibata is deactivated, all Atk Up (Shibata) and Def Up (Shibata) buffs are removed from him. Mad Enhancement has to be deactivated separately.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after Kakare Shibata is deactivated.
