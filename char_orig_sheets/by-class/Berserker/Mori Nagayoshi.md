(Mori) Type A Spirit Limiter Device  
When Mori is summoned, he has a 25% chance of being summoned with Type A Spirit Limiter Device (also known as ‘Power Armor’).  
(Active) Type A Spirit Limiter Device can be deactivated and activated during Mori’s Turn. When it is activated/deactivated, 2◈ Turns must pass before it can be deactivated/reactivated. (Cooldown: 2◈ Turns). Has the following effects while Activated-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 25% including NP.  
2\. All damage received is reduced by 25% including NP.  
3\. Agility Check rolls are increased by 2\.  
4\. When Mori is defeated, he is revived with 10% of his Max Health.  
5\. Mori’s Master’s Health loss from Mori’s activated Mad Enhancement and usage of his NP is halved.  
6\. ZON does not apply to Mori and his Master.  
7\. At the end of a Turn Mori Acts, the 3x3 panel area around him (only the panel he was standing on) becomes ‘Dead’ panels (mark those panels with a black scribble or something). When Mori is standing on a ‘Dead’ panel, Effects 1, 4, 5, and 6 of Type A Spirit Limiter Device are negated, and all damage dealt is reduced by 50% including NP (Note: overwrites the original damage reducing effect of ‘Dead Zone’ Terrain).  
8\. When Mori is damaged by an Anti-Army Noble Phantasm or higher, his Type A Spirit Limiter Device is broken and he loses its effects.  
Note: Type A Spirit Limiter Device cannot be Activated if Mori was summoned without it.

(Mori) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 10 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 10 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 20% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.
