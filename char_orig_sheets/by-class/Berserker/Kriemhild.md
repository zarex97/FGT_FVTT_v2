(Kriemhild) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Passive) When Siegfried is within a 2 panel area of herself, her Mad Enhancement is constantly Active and cannot be deactivated. In this situation, Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, its Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated. While this Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.

(Kriemhild) Revenge Plan (Rampaging) — Rank: B++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Has the following effects-

2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
3\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kriemhild) Affection of Hohe Minne — Rank: EX
