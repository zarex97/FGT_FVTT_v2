(Lü Bu) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: A  
Lü Bu’s Mad Enhancement is constantly Active and cannot be deactivated unless an allied Chen Gong or Red Hare is within a 2 panel area of himself.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; its Master’s Health cannot drop below 25 in this way while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 80% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.  
Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).

(Lü Bu) Bravery — Rank: B

(Lü Bu) Nature of a Rebellious Spirit — Rank: B

(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%.

(Lü Bu) God Force: The Five Soldiers of the War God — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Army\]  
Houtengageki. Lü Bu’s weapon has six different forms (known as ‘Forces’). When Lü Bu is summoned, Houtengageki starts in the Halberd Force. Once per Turn, Lü Bu can switch his Houtengageki into one of its other Forces except the Cannon Force. The Forces of Houtengageki are as follows-  
1\. Slash \- Blade Force. Range=1 panel, 1 target. Normal Attacks have the Ignore Def effect and ignore Block.  
2\. Thrust \- Halberd Force. Range=2 panels, 1 target. Normal Attacks have a 20% chance of inflicting Stun for ⅓◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. Blunt \- Bracer Force. Range=1 panel, 1 target. While in this Force, all damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
4\. Sweep \- Scythe Force. Range=2x3 or 3x2 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Lü Bu, all targets.  
5\. Parry \- Whip Force. Range=2 panels, 1 target. While in this Force, Evade rolls are reduced by 2, and Counter damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
6\. Shoot \- Cannon Force. Cannot be used for Normal Attacks.  
Note: When Mad Enhancement is Active, Lü Bu can only use the Halberd Force and the Cannon Force.

(Lü Bu) God Force \- Cannon Force: The Five Soldiers of the War God — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=5. Hits a 3x3 panel area for 4x damage that has the Ignore Def effect, then has a 50% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. The NP area of God Force can be increased to a 5x5 panel area if Lü Bu’s Master’s Health is further reduced by 25 when using the NP (in addition to the regular cost); the NP is also upgraded to \[Anti-Fortress\] in this case. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
