(Jack) Thousand Faces — Rank: A  
(Passive/Activation) When Jack is summoned, he does not have a figurine, and shares the same figurine as his Master. During your Turn, Jack can activate this Skill, if he does, he takes the form of a regular Civilian; as a Civilian, all his Parameters are Rank E.  
Whenever Jack encounters a new Unit (i.e. enters a 2-panel area of that Unit), record that encounter in his Notebook page. Jack can use this Skill to transform into any recorded Unit. When transformed into a Unit other than a normal Civilian, his Parameters are the same as that Unit, but reduced by 2 levels (e.g. from Rank A to Rank C) with a minimum of Rank E; and he can use all of that Unit’s Skills, but reduced to Rank E. Changes to Stats and Skill effects due to these changes are as below-  
STR: Base Attack (STR) increased by 25 for each Rank, increased/decreased by 10 for each \+/- (Note: BA (STR) starts at 100 at Rank E if a Saber-class Servant is copied, while it starts at 50 at Rank E for all other Servants).  
END: Max (& current) Health is increased by 500 for each Rank, increased/decreased by 100 for each \+/-.  
AGI: Max (& current) Agility increased by 2 for each Rank.  
MAG: Base Attack (MAG) increased by 25 for each Rank, increased/decreased by 10 for each \+/-.  
LUC: Max (& current) Luck increased by 4 for each Rank, increased/decreased by 1 for each \+/-.  
Skills: For every decrease in Rank, the magnitude of the Skill’s effect(s) is reduced by 5% (minimum effect magnitude is 5%). For Skills which were originally Rank EX to B that drops to Rank E, their required Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns. For Skills which are listed in ‘**Advanced: Common Skills**’, just use the stated effects at Rank E.  
Jack can only transform once per Round.  
(Passive 2\) Jack’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.

If Jack is Discovered while this Skill’s effects are Active, this Skill’s effects are negated for 1◈ Turns.

(Jack) From Hell: The Evil Mist Will Perish With London's Daybreak — Rank: A+\~E- (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive/Activation) When Jack is summoned, measure the size of the game board. Mark an XxX panel area in the centre of the board, where X=the number of panels on one side of the board, divided by 2 and rounded up (down?) to the closest odd number. The marked area will be designated as ‘Urban’ while the area outside the marked area will be designated as ‘Rural’. The effects of this NP differ depending on whether Jack is in an ‘Urban’ or ‘Rural’ area.  
Note: If the game board has natural ‘City’ Terrain, then instead of measuring the size of the game board, all natural ‘City’ Terrain plus the panels directly next to a ‘City’ area are designated as ‘Urban’; all other areas are designated as ‘Rural’.  
When this NP is used, Jack transforms into the ‘Hell Demon’. Its Stats are as follows-

Hell Demon (Urban)  
Health: 2000  
Agility: 18  
Luck: 14  
MOV: 7  
Range: 3 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 200  
Base Attack (MAG): 150  
Attributes: Large, Demonic, Servant  
All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute is increased by 100%.  
All damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Jack’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of each Turn if Jack Acted; and 10 Health if he did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 20 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.

Hell Demon (Rural)  
Health: 1000  
Agility: 14  
Luck: 10  
MOV: 5  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 125  
Base Attack (MAG): 100  
Attributes: Demonic, Servant  
All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute is increased by 100%.  
All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Jack’s Master loses 10 Health at the end of each Turn if Jack Acted; and 5 Health if he did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 10 or 5, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.

Jack can end From Hell during its Turn, and at the start or end of any Turn or Round.  
If Jack Moves from a Rural area to an Urban Area and vice-versa, the effects and Stat changes of From Hell are changed accordingly. When this NP is Activated, Jack’s Master loses Health equivalent to if a C-Rank Noble Phantasm was used. Wanderer of the Misty Night is not Active while From Hell is Active. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after From Hell ends.

Note: ‘City’ Terrain (created by effects, if any) are always categorized as ‘Urban’ areas.

(Jack) Natural Born Killers: Thou Art Unworthy of a Tragic Demise — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, roll a six-sided die and add 3 to the number rolled. Jack summons X copies of himself within a 2 panel area of himself; however, his Master can decide the number of Jack copies summoned, which has to be less than X with a minimum of 2 Jacks. All copies of Jack are treated as the ‘real’ Jack and have their own Stats, and can use all of his Skills and NP. However, Natural Born Killers cannot be used when there are more than 3 Jacks on the board. While this NP is Active (i.e. there is more than 1 Jack on the board), Jack’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2\.  
At the end of the Round, Jack’s Master loses X Health depending on the number of additional Jacks on the board, as follows-  
Human Jack: 5 Health  
Transformed Jack: 10 Health  
From Hell Jack: 20 Health  
If Jack’s Master’s Health is equal to or less than the amount needed to be deducted due to this NP, this NP is forcefully deactivated, leaving only the Jack nearest to its Master on the board; Jack’s Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
Jack copies do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn (Moving all Jacks count as Moving only one Unit). The same Jack can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Jack’s Master if any Jacks are directly next to him/her.  
Can be deactivated during Jack’s Turn and at the start or end of any Turn or Round, leaving only the Jack nearest to its Master on the board.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after the NP is deactivated.
