## (Beni) Swimsuit\!

(Passive) All Total Water Damage received is reduced by 50% including NP. Only active while in Summer Mode and Hell Mode.

## (Beni) Summer Proprietress

(Passive) At the start of the Round, roll an eight-sided die. Benienma changes into the corresponding Mode as follows-  
1 to 5: Work Mode (At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Water damage.)  
6 or 7: Summer Mode (At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).)  
8: Your choice of Work Mode or Summer Mode  
(Active) Used at the start of the Round, after the dice roll from this Skill’s Passive effect. The Player can choose which Form is used by Benienma for this Round. Cooldown: 3 full Rounds.

## (Beni) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B

(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

## (Beni) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A

(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

## (Beni) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: C

(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 40% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

## (Beni) Ventriloquism — Rank: EX

(Passive) Benienma is Immune to the Skill Seal and Silence debuffs.

## (Beni) Friend of Mayoiga — Rank: EX

(Passive 1\) Chance of having buffs removed from herself is reduced by 20%.  
(Passive 2\) When Benienma is summoned, all allied Units on the field gain a Fullness Gauge. The maximum value of the Fullness Gauge is 4, while it is 8 for Units with the ‘Large’ Attribute, and it is 10 for Units named ‘Arturia’.  
(Passive 3\) All allied Units gain the following effect- After Moving 3 panels or more, lose 1 Fullness Token at the end of the Move (does not apply when forcefully Moved); after performing a successful Attack or Evade, lose 1 Fullness Token at the end of the Damage Step.  
(This Skill is categorized as Territory Creation?)

### (Beni) Kurakura’s Meals — Rank: A

(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself (3 panels if used within her Home Base). First, all affected Units are applied with S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 5%. Then, activate the corresponding Meal effect depending on how many times this Skill has been used-  
1\. Yakisoba: Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Curry: Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max & current Health are increased by 200\.  
3\. Ramen: NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
4\. Frankfurt: Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
5\. Skewers: Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
6\. Parfait: Health is restored by X, where X=(10 minus the number of Fullness Tokens on the Unit’s Fullness Gauge) times 50\.  
After the Meal effect is applied, all affected Units gain 2 Fullness Tokens. Meal effects will not be applied to Units whose Fullness Gauge are at maximum when this Skill is used\!   
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.  
Additionally, whenever Benienma uses this Skill, roll a twenty-sided die. If a 20 is rolled, activate the ‘Legendary Onigiri’ Meal effect instead, which applies all six effects and also fills all affected Units’ Fullness Gauge to the maximum. Then, this Skill enters Cooldown for 3◈ Turns instead of 1◈.

### (Beni) Beheading Old Man’s Needle-Mountain Sword — Rank: EX

(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Beheading’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Noble Phantasm has a 100% chance of inflicting Instakill at the end of the Damage Step; this Instakill debuff is affected by Debuff Resist on the DU but not Debuff Immune.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns; if Benienma has 2 or less Fullness Tokens, it is reduced by another 1◈ Turns.  
4\. If Benienma had 1 or less Fullness Tokens when this Skill was used, apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.

### (Beni) Cocytus Kakigori

Attack Skill. Can only be used in Work Mode. Range=2. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 20% extra damage with a 10% chance of inflicting Freeze for 1◈ Turns; if Freeze was not inflicted, then inflict Disable for 1◈ Turns. Ice damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

### (Beni) Beer Jet Cutter

Attack Skill. Can only be used in Work Mode. Range=4. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals normal damage with a 25% increased Crit Chance and has a 50% chance of inflicting Berserk for 1◈ Turns. Water damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

## (Beni) Hell’s Tormenting Devils in Summer — Rank: EX

(Passive) Activates at the end of the Turn when there are three or more allied Units with a Fullness Gauge at half or higher. Benienma changes into Hell Mode and gains the following effects-  
1\. At a Range of 1 to 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Fire damage with a 10% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. When Benienma performs a Normal Attack using Base Attack (STR) while she has any Fullness Tokens, that Attack deals X0 bonus Fire damage; X equals the number rolled on a die, and the die rolled depends on the maximum value of Benienma’s Fullness Gauge (either a d4 or d8).  
3\. The effects of Summer Proprietress are negated.  
4\. At the start of a Round where there are less than three allied Units with a Fullness Gauge at half or higher, Benienma exits Hell Mode, then use the ‘Summer Proprietress’ Skill to determine which Mode she reverts to.  
(This Skill is categorized as ‘Mad Enhancement’.)

### (Beni) Hell’s Judgment

Attack Skill. Can only be used in Hell Mode. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 50% extra damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns. Benienma cannot be within the Skill area. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

#### (Beni) Jyuuou Manpuku・Tsuzura no Enkai — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

Base Attack (STR) is used. First, applies Dmg Up (Undead) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Undead’ Attribute is increased by 50%. Hits all enemy Units within a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself for 5x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment is further increased by 150%. Then, inflict Soaked for 2◈ Turns if Benienma was in Summer Mode, Slow for 2◈ Turns if Benienma was in Work Mode, or Burn for 2◈ Turns if Benienma was in Hell Mode. Next, apply the following effects to herself and all allied Units within the NP area-  
1\. Applies the ‘Hearty’ buff for 2◈ Turns, the maximum value of the Fullness Gauge is increased by 4\.  
2\. Removes all Volatile Debuffs and Defensive Debuffs.  
3\. Applies Max HpUp for 2◈ Turns, Max & current Health is increased by 200\.  
4\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ½◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
