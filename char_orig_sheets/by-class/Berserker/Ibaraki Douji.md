(Ibaraki) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 60% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Ibaraki) Kishu no Ma — Rank: A  
Oni-type Demon.  
(Passive 1\) Ibaraki’s Normal Attacks have a 30% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns on the DU, and deal Fire damage (half); if ME is Active, chance of inflicting Burn is 60% instead.  
(Passive 2\) Total Fire Damage taken is reduced by 30%.  
(Passive 3\) Chance of being inflicted by Burn is reduced by 30%, if ME is Active, the chance of being inflicted with Burn is reduced by 60% instead.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Ibaraki. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%; and applies NP DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 35%..   
Can be used when performing a Normal Attack. In this case, also applies the ‘Mana Burst’ effect to Ibaraki for that Combat Process, Ibaraki’s Base Attack used is her Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 125+150=275), and Burn chance is 100% (instead of 30% or 60%); not affected by Magic Resistance.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ibaraki) Disengage — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used at the start of a Combat Phase when Ibaraki is Attacked. The enemy Unit’s Attack is cancelled, Ibaraki is allowed to Move 5 panels, then her Health is restored by 25% of its maximum value, and remove all debuffs from herself. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Rampage of Mt. Ooe’s Oni. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(Ibaraki) Rampage of Mt. Ooe’s Oni — Rank: A+  
(Active) This Skill is used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Ibaraki except herself; and is used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when that allied Unit is Attacked. The enemy Unit’s Attack is cancelled, then apply the following effects-  
1\. Ibaraki Moves the affected Unit up to 5 panels from its current position.  
2\. The affected Unit’s Health is restored by 25% of its maximum value.  
3\. Remove all debuffs from the affected Unit.  
4\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
5\. If there is an allied Shuten Douji within a 3 panel area of Ibaraki when this Skill is used, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to that allied Shuten Douji, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
6\. If there is an allied Shuten Douji on the field, reduce Ibaraki’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Disengage. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Ibaraki) Shapeshift — Rank: A  
(Passive) Reduces all damage received by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Process when Ibaraki is Attacked. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is further reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ibaraki) Rashōmon Daiengi: Great Grudge of Rashōmon — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is Active. Range=4. Deals 3.5x damage; then removes all buffs on the DU and inflicts Def Dwn, all damage received is increased by 30%. Fire damage (half).   
Additionally, all Units within a 2 panel area of the DU (excluding the DU) receive 1.5x damage; these Units can Block/Evade, but cannot Counter. Fire damage.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Ibaraki) Ōeyama Daiengi: Great Flame of Mount Ooe — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is Deactivated. Range=4. When this NP is used, Ibaraki conjures 10 Sōgenbi. Then, she can command each Sōgenbi to respectively Attack an enemy Unit within Range. A total of three different Units can be targeted across all 10 Sōgenbi. (Example: 3 Sōgenbi on Archer, 4 Sōgenbi on Lancer, 3 Sōgenbi on Rider.) Of course, enemy Masters cannot be targeted if their Servant is within a 2 panel Range of them, the Servant will take the hit.  
Each Sōgenbi deals 0.35x damage. Add up the total multiplier of the Sōgenbi then add 100 to the damage dealt (Example: 6 Sōgenbi on Lancer=6(0.35x)+100=2.1x damage plus 100\).  
After damage is dealt to all targeted Units, all DUs are inflicted with Burn for 2◈ Turns; and inflicted with Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%. This NP deals Fire damage.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
