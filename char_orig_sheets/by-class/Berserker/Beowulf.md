(Beowulf) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: E-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Unit’s Master loses 5 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 5 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 5%; has no effect on NP.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 5% including NP; has no effect on Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Unit’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. ~~While ME is Active, this Unit can only Evade with Evade-.~~ This effect is negated.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Beowulf) Berserk — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%; and NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%. Then, has a 25% chance of inflicting Berserk on himself for ⅔◈ Turns; not affected by effects which modify chance of being inflicted with debuffs on himself. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Beowulf) Instinct — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is Attacked, it has a 60% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If Instinct Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is 30% instead.  
(Passive 2\) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
This Skill is negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Beowulf) Inspired Hero — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Apply Crit Up for 1◈ Turns to Beowulf, Crit Chance is increased by 30%  
2\. Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to Beowulf, Crit Damage is increased by 50%.   
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Beowulf) Battle Continuation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+15; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 4d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

When Beowulf performs a Normal Attack, the Player controlling Beowulf has to state whether he uses Hrunting or Naegling to Attack. If Beowulf has an effect which causes the Player to lose control of him, then the choice is random.

(Beowulf) Hrunting: Hound of the Red Plains — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Attacks performed with Hrunting have the ‘Aim’ and ‘Guaranteed Crit’ effects.

(Beowulf) Naegling: Iron Hammer Snake Smash — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Attacks performed with Naegling have the ‘Pierce’ effect and deal 50% extra damage. After damage is dealt with Naegling for the first time, Naegling has a 10% chance of breaking. The chance of Naegling breaking increases by 5% every time Naegling is used in a successful Attack (i.e. not Evaded), with a maximum break chance of 90%. When Naegling breaks, it is no longer usable.

(Beowulf) Grendel Buster: Primal Conflict — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
In order to use this Noble Phantasm, Hrunting and Naegling must be discarded. They will both be no longer usable after Grendel Buster is used.  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Cannot be Evaded with automatic Evade effects (e.g. Dodge buffs), normal Evasion is still possible. Deals 4x damage plus 100; then inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
