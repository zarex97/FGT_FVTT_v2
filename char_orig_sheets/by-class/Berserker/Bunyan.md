(Bunyan) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 10 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 10 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 20% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Bunyan) Happy Comrades — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Bunyan. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%; and Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Bunyan) Bean Soup Lake — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Bunyan. Restores the Health of all affected Units by 20% of their maximum value. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Bunyan) Popcorn Blizzard — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Bunyan, or all enemy Units in a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Bunyan. Inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%, and inflicts the Heal ChDwn debuff for 1◈ Turns- when an affected Unit receives healing, the chance of being healed is reduced by 50%; does not affect healing due to Home Base effects. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Bunyan) Wisconsin Death Trip — Rank: ??? (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Activation/Passive) Only usable when Mad Enhancement is Active. When this NP is used, the following effects are applied to Bunyan for a maximum of 2◈ Turns (can be ended earlier)-  
1\. All damage dealt is further increased by 50%. If the DU’s Region is North America OR the current Grail War's Region is North America, damage dealt is further increased by 100% instead.  
2\. Normal Attacks have a 50% chance of inflicting Bleed on the DU for 1◈ Turns; if the DU already had Bleed, then its duration is reset.  
3\. Range is reduced to 1\.  
4\. Mad Enhancement cannot be deactivated while this NP is Active.  
5\. At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Bunyan’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of each Turn if Bunyan Acted; and 10 Health if she did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 20 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens. This effect overwrites the Health loss from active Mad Enhancement.  
6\. Bunyan cannot use Skills while Wisconsin Death Trip is Active.  
Bunyan can end Wisconsin Death Trip at the end of any Turn.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after Wisconsin Death Trip ends.

(Bunyan) Marvelous Exploits: Exploits to be Astonished By — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging/Passive) Only usable when Mad Enhancement is not Active. When this NP is used, the following effects are applied to Bunyan for a maximum of 2◈ Turns (can be ended earlier)-  
1\. Bunyan’s size is increased from 1 panel to 3 panels (Note: She has a custom figurine for this, use that.)  
2\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP.  
3\. All damage received is reduced by 75%; if NP, 50%. Bunyan does not perform Injury Rolls when damaged by an Attack unless she was damaged by a NP.  
4\. Bunyan cannot Block, and when she performs an Agility Check, double the value of the roll.  
5\. Bunyan can Move to any panel, when she Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Bunyan to stand on.  
6\. Bunyan’s Range is reduced to 1, her Normal Attacks now hit a 3x3 panel area (directly next to her) and knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
7\. Bunyan has the ‘Giant’ Attribute when this NP is Active.  
8\. At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Bunyan’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn if Bunyan Acted; and 10 Health if she did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.   
9\. Bunyan cannot use Skills while Marvelous Exploits is Active.  
Bunyan can end Marvelous Exploits at the end of any Turn.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after Marvelous Exploits ends.

Note: Wisconsin Death Trip cannot be used if Marvelous Exploits is on Cooldown and vice versa.
