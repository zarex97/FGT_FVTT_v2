(Nightingale) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Nightingale’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; its Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Nightingale’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Nightingale can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated/deactivated for its duration if Mad Enhancement does not meet the condition to be deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
(Passive) If there is an ‘Injured’ (Health is less than 80% of its maximum value) allied Unit within a 3 panel area of Nightingale, her Mad Enhancement is activated (regardless of Cooldown) and cannot be deactivated until there are no Injured allied Units within a 3 panel area of Nightingale, her Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this situation. Additionally, if Nightingale’s Mad Enhancement is Active, she will constantly Move towards the nearest Injured allied Unit until she is directly next to it.  
In this situation, Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).

(Nightingale) Nurse of Steel — Rank: A+  
(Passive) When Nightingale is standing directly next to an allied Unit, at the end of a Turn Nightingale and/or that Unit Acted, restore that Unit’s Health by 5% of its maximum value. Does not take effect if Nightingale Attacked this Turn or used the Active effect of Nurse of Steel this Turn.  
If there is more than one allied Unit who Acted that Turn and standing directly next to Nightingale and the end of that Turn, only one of those Units’ Health is restored (choose which one).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Nightingale. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores that Unit’s Health and Agility by 30% of their maximum values. However, if that allied Unit is within a 1 panel area of Nightingale, Health and Agility is restored by 40% instead. 2\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 3 times; does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
3\. Applies Instakill Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time. Does not affect Death.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Nightingale) Human Anatomy Understanding — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute is increased by 50\. Negated while Nightingale has Dmg Up (Humanoid).  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken from Units with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute is reduced by 25 including NP. Negated while Nightingale has Ward (Humanoid).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Apply Dmg Up (Humanoid) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute is increased by 50%; and applies Ward (Humanoid) for 1◈Turns, all damage taken from Units with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute is reduced by 25%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nightingale) Angel’s Cry — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Select an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to that Unit, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%. Then, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all Units within a 2 panel area of herself except that Unit and herself, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Apply Crit DmUp (STR) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Damage of Attacks which deal STR Damage is increased by 30%.  
3\. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Nightingale) Nightingale Pledge: I Will Abstain From Whatever Is Deleterious And Mischievous — Rank: C+ (NP)  
(Non-damaging) Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Nightingale. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within that area, NP Damage dealt is reduced by 75%; and Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. Then, the Health and Agility of all allied Units within a 5x5 panel area of Nightingale is restored by 50% of their maximum values, and all debuffs are removed from those Units. Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.
