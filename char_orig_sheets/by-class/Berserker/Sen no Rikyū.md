(Rikyū) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement (Sabi) — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Rikyū’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated. If Rikyū is in a ‘Japan’ region, this Health cost is 15 instead of 30\.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 50%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Rikyū’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Rikyū can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. Chance of being inflicted with Offensive and Defensive Debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
8\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Rikyū) Class Skill: Territory Creation (Wabi) — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
(Passive 3\) Mental Debuff Resist of allied Units within Rikyū’s Home Base is increased by 20%.  
(Passive 4\) Rikyū’s Home Base is treated as a ‘Japan’ Region, but does not apply Parameter boosts to Servants from that region.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Rikyū) Appreciation of the Arts (Tea) — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When a NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute is used within a 3 panel area of Rikyū, she has a 50% chance of learning the True Name of that NP (the information of that NP is copied to your Notebook). If the NP was used by a Unit from the ‘Japan’ Region, the chance of learning its True Name is 100% instead of 50%.  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken from a NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute is reduced by 25%; if the NP was used by a Unit from the ‘Japan’ Region, damage taken is reduced by 50% instead.  
(Passive 3\) Chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 10%.

(Rikyū) Unfettered and Flexible — Rank: B  
(Passive) When a Normal Attack Crit is performed, reduce NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.

(Rikyū) Extremity of Wabi — Rank: A-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
4\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.  

(Rikyū) Ichirin no Hana — Rank: B  
(Active) A Single Flower. Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 20%.  
3\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Rikyū) Subtle Blackness — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Counter) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt by Counters is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Blackness’ buff for 1◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- When a successful Attack is performed, inflict Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns at the start of the Damage Step, all damage taken is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Komahime) Mita no Tsurugi  
(Passive) While Mad Enhancement is not Active, Normal Attacks which deal STR damage deal 5d20 extra damage against Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment.

(Rikyū) Ichigo Ichie: One Moment, One Meeting — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area if Mad Enhancement is Active (5x5 panel area if it is not) in any non-diagonal direction next to herself for 3x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the ‘Man’ Attribute. Then, inflicts NP Seal for ⅓◈ Turns (1◈ Turns) and inflicts (Stage 2\) Curse.  
However, if there is only one DU within the NP area, Total Damage dealt is increased by 50% and the effects of this NP change to the effects in this colour.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
