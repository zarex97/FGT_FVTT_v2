(Cú Alter) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: C (EX)  
Mad Enhancement Rank has degraded from EX to C because of the incorrect Berserker Class of Cú Chulainn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 40% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Cú Alter) Class Skill: Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Cú Alter) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Cú Alter) Rune Magic — Rank: B  
(Passive) Has the same effect as Magic Resistance — Rank: B.

Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Cú Alter) Protection from Arrows — Rank: C  
(Passive) Cú Alter has a 75% chance of automatically Evading any Attack from a Ranged weapon (arrows, firearms, thrown weapons, etc) which does not deal MAG damage including Noble Phantasms from his Front and Sides (with a few possible exceptions); when he receives an aforementioned Attack from Behind (i.e. the 3 panels behind him) and he Evades, the Evade roll is reduced by 2\. If Protection from Arrows fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. Does not apply to Ranged AoE Attacks/Noble Phantasms. Has no effect if this Unit is affected by Skill Seal.

(Cú Alter) Fairies’ Maddening — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range; affects all enemy Units within the Skill area. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Inflicts AGL Dwn for 1◈ Turns, the value of Agility Check rolls are increased by 1d4.  
3\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Cú Alter) The Mad King — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts (Mad) to Cú Alter for 2◈ Turns, when Cú Alter is defeated he is revived with 20% of his Max Health. This Guts buff stacks with other normal Guts buffs.  
2\. If ME is not Active, the Cooldown of Gáe Bolg is reduced by 1◈ Turns. If ME is Active, the Cooldown of Curruid Coinchenn is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
4\. Applies Dmg Up (Lawful) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Cú Alter) Gáe Bolg: Gouging Piercing Spear of Carnage — Rank: B++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is deactivated. Range+3 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range, Cú Alter cannot be within the NP area. First, all DU perform a Luck Check. If Failed, the DU is inflicted with Instakill and Curse. If Successful, the DU receives 3.5x damage plus 200 and is inflicted with Curse. Then, has a 500% chance of inflicting Atk Dwn on Cú Alter for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%. Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.

(Cú Alter) Curruid Coinchenn: Beast of Crunching Deathtusk — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Activation/Passive) Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is activated. Cú Alter puts on the Armor of Curruid (enters Curruid Mode), and gains the following effects-  
1\. Range is reduced to 1 panel.  
2\. STR Rank is raised to EX, Base Attack (STR) is increased by 50\.  
3\. All damage taken is (further) reduced by 25% including Noble Phantasm.  
4\. Mad Enhancement cannot be deactivated while Curruid Coinchenn is in effect.  
5\. Normal Attacks remove a random Defensive Buff from the DU at the start of the Damage Step.  
At the end of each Turn Cú Alter Acts, the Health loss incurred by his Master due to Active Mad Enhancement is doubled. Curruid Coinchenn can be deactivated during Cú Alter’s Turn, and at the start or end of any Turn or Round. If Mad Enhancement is deactivated through any other method or effect, Curruid Coinchenn is also deactivated. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after deactivation.
