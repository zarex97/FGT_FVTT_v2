7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated. However, if Huyan has 5 Praise Tokens, Mad Enhancement will not be deactivated even while inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Huyan) Phantasmal Beast Possession — Rank: C  
(Passive) During Huyan’s Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase involving herself, if her Master is within a 2 panel area of her, her Master can use the ‘Praise’ Action on Huyan Zhuo, which gives her 1 Praise Token (max. 5). ‘Praise’ can only be used once per Turn.  
For every Praise Token on herself, Crit Chance increases by 10% & Crit Damage dealt increases by 5%.  
Whenever Huyan Zhuo takes damage from an Attack, she loses 1 Praise Token.  
If Huyan Zhuo takes damage while she has no Praise Tokens, she has a 60% chance of being inflicted with the Unremovable ‘Downcast’ Mental Debuff for 2◈ Turns (does not stack, ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune), its effects are as follows-  
1\. All damage dealt is reduced by 30% including NP.  
2\. All damage taken is increased by 20% including NP.  
3\. Crit Chance is reduced by 10%.  
4\. Mad Enhancement cannot be Activated, and it is instantly deactivated if it was Active upon being inflicted with ‘Downcast’.  
When her Master uses the ‘Praise’ Action while Huyan Zhuo is Downcast, she does not gain Praise Tokens, but reduce the duration of the ‘Downcast’ debuff by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is increased by 30%.
