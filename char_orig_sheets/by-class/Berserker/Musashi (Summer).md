(Musashi) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Musashi) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Musashi) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Musashi) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 2 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Musashi) Divinity — Rank: D  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 20 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Musashi) Accel Turn — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Dodge for 1 time and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%. Can be used at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked if this Unit’s AGI Rank is higher than the AU’s. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Musashi) Fifth Serving — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the ‘Daigozei’ buff to Musashi for ⅓◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
a. Whenever Musashi performs a Normal Attack, she performs two Attacks instead of one; calculate them as two separate, consecutive Normal Attacks. Damage dealt (for both Attacks) is reduced by 20%.  
b. When Musashi performs a Normal Attack with ‘Daigozei’, if both Attacks of ‘Daigozei’ are Crits, the duration of ‘Daigozei’ is extended by ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the ‘Udonhai’ buff for 1◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
a. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
b. When Musashi performs the second consecutive Normal Attack due to the effects of ‘Fifth Serving’, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn.  
c. Whenever Musashi performs a Crit Normal Attack, it has a 20% chance of reducing the Cooldown of this Skill by 1 Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Both ‘Daigozei’ and ‘Udonhai’ do not stack.

(Musashi) Tenma Gōgan — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, reduce Musashi’s Health by 40% of its maximum value; Musashi’s Health cannot drop below 1 in this way; but this Skill cannot be used if her Health is equal to or below 20% of its maximum value. Then, applies the following effects to Musashi-  
1\. Applies Pierce for 2◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies Guts for 3◈ Turns, when Musashi is defeated she is revived with 1 Health.  
4\. Applies the ‘Gōgan’ buff to Musashi for 2◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- Whenever Musashi enters Combat, Def Dwn (Gōgan) is inflicted on the enemy Unit for 1◈ Turns at the start of Combat Phase, all damage taken is increased by 30%. Def Dwn (Gōgan) does not stack, but its duration is reset if reapplied.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Musashi) Ganryū-jima: I Was Told There Would be Magic Sword-Busting\! — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=4. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 5x damage, damage dealt is increased by 200% against Saber-class Servants, while it is increased by 100% against non-Saber-class Servants who use a ‘Sword’; then inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%. Additionally, NP Seal is inflicted for 2◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units who are either a Saber-class Servant or uses a ‘Sword’. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
