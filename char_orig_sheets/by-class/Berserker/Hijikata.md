(Hijikata) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: D+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 10 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 10 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 25%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 25% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Hijikata) Demon of the Battlefield — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Hijikata. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%; and Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Hijikata) Disengage — Rank: C  
(Active) This Skill is used at the start of a Combat Phase when Hijikata is Attacked. The enemy Unit’s Attack is cancelled, Hijikata is allowed to Move 3 panels, then he restores his Health by 15% of its maximum value and remove 1 debuff from himself. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Disengage is used, Hijikata gains a Guts buff until the end of the Round, when Hijikata is defeated he is revived with 1 Health.

(Hijikata) Furious Journey  — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Remove all debuffs from himself.  
2\. Restore his Health by 10% of its maximum value.  
3\. Applies Guts (Furious) for 1◈ Turns, when Hijikata is defeated he is revived with 1 Health. This Guts (Furious) buff stacks with other Guts buffs, and is consumed first before normal Guts buffs.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Hijikata) Law of the Shinsengumi — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. This Skill can only be used once per Round, and can only be used 5 times throughout the entire game. Has the following effects when used, in order-  
1\. Reduce Hijikata’s Health by 20% of its maximum value.  
2\. Increase Base Attack (STR and MAG) by 25\. Unremovable.  
3\. Applies Crit Up (Law) until the end of the Turn where Effect 4 of this Skill is used, Crit Chance is increased by 40%. Otherwise, Crit Up (Law) is Unremovable.  
4\. Applies Crit DmUp (Law) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by X%, where X is 20 plus the percentage of Health Hijikata is missing (e.g. Hijikata’s Health is 700/1000, so Crit Damage is increased by 20%+30%=50%). When Crit DmUp (Law) is removed from Hijikata, apply the Crit DmUp (Shinsengumi) buff to himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by X, where X \= the percentage of Health Hijikata is missing (e.g. Hijikata’s Health is 700/1000, so Crit Damage is increased by 30); this Crit DmUp (Shinsengumi) buff is Unremovable.  
5\. Used on an enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of Hijikata. Applies the ‘Boshin’ effect to that Unit, all damage dealt by Hijikata to that Unit is increased by 100%, and all damage dealt by that Unit to Hijikata is increased by 100%. Also affects Noble Phantasm. The ‘Boshin’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
(Passive) Whenever this Skill is used, reduce the Cooldown of ‘Furious Journey’ and ‘Shinsengumi: Fumetsu no Makoto’ by 1◈ Turns.

(Hijikata Toshizo) Military Tactics — Rank: D  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Hijikata. All damage received from \[Anti-Army\] Noble Phantasms is reduced by 20%; all damage dealt by \[Anti-Army\] Noble Phantasms is increased by 20%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies ‘NP DmUp (nonAA)’ for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Hijikata, NP damage dealt by Noble Phantasms **other** than \[Anti-Army\] is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Hijikata) Shinsengumi: Fumetsu no Makoto — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Shinsengumi: Undying Sincerity. Deals 3x damage plus 200 plus the value of Health Hijikata is missing (e.g. Hijikata’s Health is 467/1193, so 726 extra damage is dealt). Then, reduce Hijikata’s Health by half of the damage dealt to the DU. Hijikata’s Health cannot fall below 1 in this way. Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns.
