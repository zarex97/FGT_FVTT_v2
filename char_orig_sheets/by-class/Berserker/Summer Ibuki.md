(S.Ibuki) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(S.Ibuki) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: E  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Ibuki’s Master loses 5 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 5 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Ibuki’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Ibuki can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

3\. Applies NP Regen (Midsummer) for 1◈ Turns to all Units with the ‘Swimsuit’ Skill, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of the Damage Step whenever a successful non-NP Attack is performed.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(S.Ibuki) Goddess of Summer  
(Passive 1\) At the start of the game (or Stage), Ibuki gains a Surge Token. Whenever Ibuki uses a non-Attack Skill, she gains a Surge Token (max. 3).  
(Passive 2\) At the start of a Combat Phase, if Mad Enhancement is not Active, Ibuki can remove one Surge Token from herself to apply the ‘Enchant Water’ buff to herself until the end of that Combat Phase. The ‘Enchant Water’ buff has the following effects-  
1\. Normal Attacks use Ibuki’s Base Attack (STR) and half of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 135+125=260), the effect of Magic Resistance is halved.  
2\. Normal Attacks deal Water damage (half).  
3\. Normal Attacks have a 50% chance of inflicting Soaked for 1◈ Turns.
