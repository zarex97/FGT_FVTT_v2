(Heracles) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B  
(Passive) Constantly Active. Cannot be deactivated normally, ME can only be deactivated if there is an allied Unit with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute within a 2 panel area of himself. Whenever Heracles is successfully Attacked by Unit with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute, his Mad Enhancement has a 10% chance of deactivating for 1◈Turns at the end of the Damage Step.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; its Master’s Health cannot drop below 20 in this way while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 60% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.  
Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).

Note: Units named Ilyasviel have the ‘Ilya’ Attribute.

(Heracles) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Heracles) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Heracles) Indomitable — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the Guts (Undying) for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Herc is defeated he is revived with 25% of his Max Health. Guts (Undying) stacks with other Guts buffs. Guts (Undying) is consumed first before normal Guts buffs.  
2\. Applies the ‘Indomitable’ buff to Herc for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns. Its effects are as follows- Whenever Herc is defeated and revived through any effect, Atk Up is applied to him for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  

(Heracles) Bravery — Rank: A+  
Note: Bravery cannot be used and has no effects when Mad Enhancement is Active.  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, STR damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Heracles) Eye of the Mind (False) — Rank: B  
(Passive) Evade Rolls are reduced by 2, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns, and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 35%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Heracles) God Hand: Twelve Labors — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Heracles’ Health is reduced to 0, roll ten twenty-sided dice and restore his Health by the number rolled, then remove 1 Labor Token from himself. If the damage of the Attack that defeated Heracles exceeds his current Health, the excess damage is reduced from his newly restored Health, and so on. This effect cannot activate if Heracles has no Labor Tokens.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever an Attack reduces Heracle’s Health to 0 for the first time, record that Attack under this NP. These recorded Attacks can no longer defeat Heracles- whenever a recorded Attack would reduce his Health to 0, his Health will remain at 1 instead.  
(Passive 3\) All damage taken is reduced by 60% from Attacks which meet one of the following conditions-  
1\. An Attack from a Unit whose STR Rank is B or lower. If the AU has no Parameters, then this condition is met if the Unit’s BA (STR) is 125 or lower.  
2\. An Attack from a Unit whose MAG Rank is B or lower. If the AU has no Parameters, then this condition is met if the Unit’s BA (MAG) is 175 or lower.  
3\. A NP with a Rank of B or lower. Does not apply to ‘*Attacks categorized as NP*’.  
Passive 3 is ignored by Units or Attacks which have the ‘Pierce’ buff/effect.

(Heracles) Nine Lives: Shooting the Hundred Heads — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 4x damage plus 100 and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
