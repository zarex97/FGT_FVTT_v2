(Zerkerlot) Grudge Towards the King  
(Passive) During your Turn, if there are any Units with the ‘Saberface’ Attribute within a 4 panel area of Lancelot, his Mad Enhancement is immediately activated regardless of its Cooldown or any other effects/conditions, and he gains the ‘Berserk (Saberface)’ effect.  
Mad Enhancement cannot be deactivated as long as there are any Units with the ‘Saberface’ Attribute (enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area of Lancelot.  
At the end of the Turn, if there are no Units with the ‘Saberface’ Attribute (enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area of herself, remove the ‘Berserk (Saberface)’ effect from himself.  
His Master can disable/negate the effect of this Skill for 1◈ Turns by spending one Command Spell. If he had the Berserk (Saberface) effect, it is removed from him for the duration that this Skill is disabled with the CS. ~~Note: Does not deactivate Mad Enhancement\!~~ If Mad Enhancement was Active while this Skill is disabled with the CS, the Player can choose to deactivate Mad Enhancement as well.

Berserk (Saberface) has the following effects on the Unit-  
1\. Will only Move towards and Attack the nearest Units with the ‘Saberface’ Attribute (both enemy and allied).  
2\. This Unit has to Move and Attack on its Turn if able (if there are multiple Units on the board capable of Attacking in the same Turn and the Player performs any Attacks, this Unit must be one of the Attackers).  
3\. Immune to (normal) Berserk, Confuse, and Charm.  
Note: Berserk (Saberface) is an Unremovable Mental Debuff that ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects.

(Zerkerlot) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: C  
Lancelot starts the game with Active Mad Enhancement.   
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated (see ‘Grudge Towards the King’), its Master’s Health cannot drop below 15 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 40% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated. While this Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
After Lancelot uses Arondight, Mad Enhancement’s Rank is reduced to Rank E for the rest of the game.  
(Mad Enhancement — Rank: E)  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 5 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 5 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Zerkerlot) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to E is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 10%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Zerkerlot) Eternal Arms Mastership — Rank: A+  
(Passive) When Mad Enhancement is Active, Effect 6 of Mad Enhancement is negated. When Mad Enhancement is not Active, Lancelot never uses Evade- when he performs Evade rolls. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks.  
(Active) Applies Crit Up (Mastership) to Lancelot for 1◈ Turns, if Mad Enhancement is Active, Crit Chance is increased by 30%; if Mad Enhancement is not Active, Crit Chance is increased by 60%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Zerkerlot) Protection of the Fairies — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Lancelot performs a Luck Check in Combat, reduce the value rolled by 4\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores 4 Luck. Then, if ‘Arondight’ is Active, Flip a Coin. Gain 2 Arondight Tokens on Heads, while only 1 is gained on Tails. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Zerkerlot) Reverse Flow of Mana — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen (Mana) for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns whenever Lancelot is successful hit by an Attack which deals MAG damage.   
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen (Reversal) for 1◈ Turns, whenever Lancelot is successfully hit by an Attack that deals STR damage at a Range of 2 or lower, the AU’s NP Cooldown is increased by ⅓◈ Turns; then if Lancelot successfully lands a Counter on that Unit, his NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.    
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

First applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%; then hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 4x damage plus 200\. Cooldown: 7◈+⅔◈ Turns.

(Zerkerlot) Arondight: The Unfading Light of the Lake — Rank: A++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging/Activation) Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is deactivated. When Arondight is drawn (activated), the Passive effects of this NP become activated for the rest of the game. Using this NP does not count as an Attack, and does not decrease his Master’s Health as a cost.  
(Passive) The Passive effects of this NP are normally inactive, and only become active after Arondight is drawn. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. Grudge Towards the King, For Someone’s Glory, & Knight of Owner are permanently disabled/deactivated/unusable for the rest of the game; and Mad Enhancement Rank is reduced to Rank E for the rest of the game.  
2\. Increase Lancelot’s Max & current Health by 100, Max & current Agility by 2, Max & current Luck by 2, Base Attack (STR) & (MAG) by 25, but decrease his Range to 1\.  
3\. Normal Attacks deal 100 extra damage to Units with the ‘Dragon’ Attribute.  
4\. At the start of every Round, Lancelot gains 3 Arondight Tokens (max. 3). Whenever Lancelot performs a Normal Attack while he has any Arondight Tokens, the Normal Attack uses his Base Attack (STR) and (X+2)0% of his Base Attack (MAG) combined (not affected by Magic Resistance), X=the number of Arondight Tokens on Lancelot; then he loses 1 Arondight Token at the end of the Damage Step.
