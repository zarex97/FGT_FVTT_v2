If Vlad III is summoned, the player who summoned him Flips a Coin. If Heads, Vlad III (Lancer) is summoned. If Tails, Vlad III (Berserker) is summoned. (When playing on Normal HGW or Ainsworth HGW, do not Flip a Coin if Lancer has already been summoned.) Note: Does not apply to certain scenarios (e.g. Grand Order Campaign, choose between Lancer or Berserker class before deploying).

(Vlad) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Vlad) Bloodsucking — Rank: A  
(Active) Range=1. Used on an enemy Unit. Increase its NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, and reduce Vlad’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. If Mad Enhancement is Active, the Range of this Skill is increased by 1, but the chance of increasing the DU’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Vlad) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Vlad) Fearsome Immortal — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts (Fearsome) to Vlad for ⅓◈ Turns, when Vlad is defeated he is revived with 20% of his Max Health. This Guts buff stacks with other normal Guts buffs.  
2\. Applies Guts to Vlad for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Vlad is defeated he is revived with 20% of his Max Health.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns   

(Vlad) Shapeshift — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Reduces all damage received by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Process when Vlad is Attacked. Applies Def Up (Shapeshift) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is further reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%. Def Up (Shapeshift) does not stack. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) If this Skill is used while Vlad has any Def Up (Shapeshift) and/or Atk Up (Shapeshift) buffs, their duration is reset to 1◈ Turns.

Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Vlad) Legend of Dracula: The Succession of Blood — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Applies the following effects to Vlad-  
1\. Applies the ‘Dark (Vampire)’ Attribute to Vlad.  
2\. Vlad restores 100 Health and 1 Agility at the end of any Turn he Acts during a Night Round.  
3\. Every time Vlad deals damage to a Unit, he heals 100 Health and 1 Agility. Each time he kills a non-Civilian Unit, his Health and Agility is restored by 25% of their maximum values.  
(Active/Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-

The ‘Dracula’ effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable (see the next line for the exception).  
3\. If this Skill is used during a ‘Day’ Round, Vlad does not gain the ‘Dracula’ effect. If the Round changes from Night to Day while Vlad has the ‘Dracula’ effect, it is removed from him at the start of the Day Round.  
4\. Applies Atk Up (Shapeshift) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Does not consume his Master’s Health as a cost when used.
