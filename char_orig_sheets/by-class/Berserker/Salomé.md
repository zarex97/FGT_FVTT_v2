(Salomé) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: C-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 25%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 35% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) (15%).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Salomé) Blood-Soaked Silver Plate — Rank: B+  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful Good’ Alignment is increased by 25 including NP. This effect is negated while Salome has the Dmg Up (Jokanaan) buff.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Jokanaan) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful Good’ Alignment is increased by 50% including.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Salomé) Natural Body (Body) — Rank: C

2\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 1 times.  
3\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.

(Salomé) Dance of the Seven Veils — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Seven Veils’ effect to Salomé, and she gains seven ‘Veils’. The ‘Seven Veils’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Has the following effects-  
1\. After every ⅓◈ Turns, at the end of the Turn, remove 1 Veil from Salomé.  
2\. Whenever a Veil is removed from Salomé, remove one random Defensive Buff from herself.  
3\. Whenever a Veil is removed from Salomé, her Health is restored by 7% of its maximum value.  
4\. When the last Veil is removed from Salomé, reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Salomé by 5◈ Turns; then remove the ‘Seven Veils’ effect from herself.  
Note: If Salomé does not have the ‘Seven Veils’ effect, removing Veils from herself has no effect. The maximum number of Veils Salomé can have is seven.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns after the ‘Seven Veils’ effect ends.

(Salomé) Femme Fatale Baiser: Kissing the Severed Head — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 3.5x damage. Then, inflicts Curse on the DU; and inflicts Evil Curse on the DU for 1◈ Turns, Curse Damage received is doubled.  
If Mad Enhancement was Active when this NP was used, Curse Damage received is tripled instead of doubled for Evil Curse; then inflict Skill Seal on Salomé for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
