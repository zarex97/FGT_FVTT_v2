(Penthesilea) Hatred of Achilles  
(Passive) At any time, if there is a Greek Male Unit (regardless of enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area around Penthesilea, her Mad Enhancement is immediately activated regardless of its Cooldown or any other effects/conditions, and she gains the ‘Berserk (Priam)’ effect. ~~(Note: If Mad Enhancement on Cooldown is activated in this way, the Cooldown will continue to reduce at the end of every Turn)~~. If an allied Hippolyta is within a 2 panel area of Penthesilea, whenever this effect would activate, the chance of it activating is reduced by 50%, and if the effect did not activate, then it will no longer activate for the remainder of this Round.  
Mad Enhancement cannot be deactivated as long as there are any Greek Male Units (enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area of Penthesilea.  
At the end of the Turn, if there are no Greek Male Units (enemy or ally) within a 4 panel area of herself, remove the ‘Berserk (Priam)’ effect from herself.  
Her Master can disable/negate the effect of this Skill for 1◈ Turns by spending one Command Spell. If she had the Berserk (Priam) effect, it is removed from her for the duration that this Skill is disabled with the CS. ~~Note: Does not deactivate Mad Enhancement\!~~ If Mad Enhancement was Active while this Skill is disabled with the CS, the Player can choose to deactivate Mad Enhancement as well.

Berserk (Priam) has the following effects on the Unit-  
1\. Will only Move towards and Attack the nearest Greek Male Unit (both enemy and allied).  
2\. This Unit has to Move and Attack on its Turn if able (if there are multiple Units on the board capable of Attacking in the same Turn and the Player performs any Attacks, this Unit must be one of the Attackers).  
3\. Immune to (normal) Berserk, Confuse, and Charm.  
Note: Berserk (Priam) is an Unremovable Mental Debuff that ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects.

(Penthesilea) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated (see ‘Hatred of Achilles’), its Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated. While this Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.

(Penthesilea) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Penthesilea) Charisma — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 20 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma), is inflicted with Skill Seal, or if Mad Enhancement is active.

(Penthesilea) Amazoness Queen — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Ignore Def for 1◈ Turns to herself.  
3\. Penthesilea’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns plus ⅓◈ Turns for every other Female Unit within a 2 panel area of herself.  
4\. Applies the ‘Amazoness’ buff for 2◈ Turns to herself.    
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Penthesilea gains the following effects while she has the ‘Amazoness’ buff.  
1\. ‘Charisma’ and Goddess of War are not negated while Mad Enhancement is Active.  
2\. Outrage Amazon can be used while Mad Enhancement is not Active.  
3\. The effects of ‘Hatred of Achilles’ are disabled/negated.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Penthesilea) Golden Rule (Beauty) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, does not affect Instakill, Death, & Erase; and applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Penthesilea) Howl of the War God — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Penthesilea. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, all STR damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
2\. This effect is applied to herself. Applies Dmg Up (Priam) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Greek Male Units is increased by 100%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Penthesilea) Goddess of War: War God's Military Sash — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) The effect of this Noble Phantasm is only active when Mad Enhancement is deactivated. Has the following effects (Note: X=the number rolled on a four-sided die)\-  
1\. Normal Attack damage dealt is increased by X0%  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by X0%; if NP, the magnitude is halved.  
3\. Evade rolls are reduced by X. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks.  
4\. Penthesilea’s Divinity Rank is increased from B to A.  
(Divinity — Rank: A)

(Penthesilea) Outrage Amazon: A Hero to Die on My Rage — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Only usable when Mad Enhancement is activated. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3.5x damage and inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
