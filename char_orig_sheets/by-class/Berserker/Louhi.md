(Louhi) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 40% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Louhi) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Louhi) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Louhi) Celestial Orbit Tuning — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
Then, the 5x5 panel area (7x7 panel area when used in Pohjantähti Mode) around the panel Louhi was standing on becomes ‘Darkness’ Terrain for 2◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Louhi) Calamity of the North — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Stun for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Removes all Defensive Buffs.  
3\. Inflicts Poison.  
4\. Inflicts Curse.  
5\. Inflicts Heal Dwn for 1◈ Turns, when this Unit’s Health is restored, the amount restored is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Louhi) Conundrum of Ruin — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 4◈ Turns.  
2\. NP Cooldown of all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
3\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Crit Chance is reduced by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Louhi) Polaris Circulation: North Star Celestial Sphere Rotation — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Pohjantähti Levikki. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+3 for the Combat Process. When used, Louhi transforms into Pohjantähti Mode for 3◈ Turns (if used while already in as Pohjantähti Mode, extend its duration by 3◈ Turns). First, has the following effect-  
1\. If Mad Enhancement is not Active: Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn, and applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
2\. If Mad Enhancement is Active: Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn, and applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns (does not affect herself). Next, apply NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn, and apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to those Units, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Louhi gains the following effects in Pohjantähti Mode-  
1\. Gains the Wild Beast, Demonic Beast, Animalistic, and Levitating Attributes.  
2\. Mad Enhancement is permanently Active and its Effects 1 & 6 are negated.  
3\. Range is increased by 1\. At a Range of 1 to 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and deal 40 bonus Fire damage. At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. Calamity of the North now affects all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area around Louhi.  
5\. Liekehtivä Kokko cannot be used. Jäätävän Tonttu now inflicts Disable instead of Freeze. Finn Curse: Gandr now hits a 3x3 panel area within Range.  
6\. Turisas Murska can be used.  
7\. If Louhi is inflicted with NP Seal while in Pohjantähti Mode, it immediately ends.
