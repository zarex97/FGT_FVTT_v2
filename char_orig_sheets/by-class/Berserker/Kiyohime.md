(Kiyohime) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Kiyohime) Shapeshift: Fire Dragon — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%. Then, inflicts Severe Burn for 2◈ Turns to all other Units within a 2 panel area around herself, Burn Damage received is doubled. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Kiyohime) Stalking — Rank: B  
(Passive) While Kiyohime has the ‘Stalker’ buff, a Unit inflicted with Def Dwn (Stalking) always counts as a ‘Unit inflicted with Burn or Severe Burn’.

(Kiyohime) Super Positive Stalker — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn (Stalking) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
Then, apply the following effects to herself-  
1\. Applies Dmg Up (Burn) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with Burn or Severe Burn is increased by 30%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Stalker’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 5 times, when Kiyohime performs a successful Attack, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step for every Unit hit that already had a Burn/Severe Burn debuff at the start of the Damage Step.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Kiyohime) Flame-Coloured Kiss — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Remove all debuffs from Kiyohime; then apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%; and apply Burn ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting Burn is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Kiyohime) Tenshin Kashou Zanmai — Rank: EX (NP)  
Only usable when Mad Enhancement is Active. ~~Base Attack used is Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined~~ Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Kiyohime for 5x damage. Then, has a 50% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns on all affected Units, and inflicts Burn for 4◈ Turns. Fire damage. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns

(Kiyohime) Samadhi Through Transforming Flames — Rank: EX (NP)  
(Non-damaging/Passive) Only usable when Mad Enhancement is not Active. When this NP is used, the following effects are applied to Kiyohime for a maximum of 2◈ Turns (can be ended earlier)-  
1\. Kiyohime’s size is increased from 1 panel to 2 panels (Note: She has a custom figurine for this, use that).  
2\. All damage dealt is increased by 50% including NP.  
3\. All damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. Kiyohime does not perform Injury Rolls when damaged by an Attack unless she was damaged by a NP.  
5\. Kiyohime cannot Block; and when she Evades, increase the value rolled by 4\.  
6\. Kiyohime can Move to any panel, when she Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Kiyohime to stand on.  
7\. Kiyohime has the ‘Large’ Attribute when this NP is Active.  
8\. Kiyohime can perform 2 different types of Normal Attacks-  
a) Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 2x2 panel area within Range and deals normal damage. Fire damage. Inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns.  
b) Range=1. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 50% extra damage to a single Unit. Inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns.  
9\. At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Kiyohime’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn if Kiyohime Acted; and 10 Health if she did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
10\. Kiyohime cannot use any Skills while this NP is Active.  
These effects are Unremovable. Kiyohime can end Samadhi Through Transforming Flames during her Turn, and at the start or end of any Turn or Round.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns after Samadhi Through Transforming Flames ends.

Note: Tenshin Kashou Zanmae cannot be used if Samadhi Through Transforming Flames is on Cooldown and vice versa.
