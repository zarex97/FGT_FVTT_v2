(Kouyou) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 25 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 80% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Kouyou) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kouyou) Shapeshift: Dinosaur — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Passive 2\) When in Dinosaur Form, MOV is increased by 1 and Agility Check rolls are increased by 1\. When in Human Form, Agility Check rolls are reduced by 1\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used once per Turn. When this Skill is used, Kouyou transforms from her Dinosaur Form to her Human Form, and vice versa. Has different effects depending on which form she transforms into-  
1\. Human Form into Dinosaur Form: Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is further reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%; and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%.  
2\. Dinosaur Form into Human Form: Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is further reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%; and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. ~~1◈ Turns after using this Skill, it can be used again (even while in Cooldown)~~ This Skill can be used while in Cooldown, but Def Up and Crit Up are not applied to Kouyou, she only changes forms; then the Cooldown for this Skill is increased by 1◈ Turns.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever she changes forms through this Skill, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns and apply Crit DmUp (Dinosaur) for 1 time, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%. Crit DmUp (Dinosaur) does not stack (can only stack up to 3 times?).

(Kouyou) Nine-Headed Dragon’s Fire — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, select an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Kouyou. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores Kouyou’s Agility by 3, and applies Atk Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to the selected allied Unit, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 25%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kouyou) Rope of Life — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Kouyou. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores the Unit’s Health by 20% of its maximum value.  
2\. Removes one debuff from the affected Unit.  
3\. Applies Instakill Immune for 1◈ Turns, the affected Unit is Immune to Instakill; while chance of being inflicted with Death is reduced by 75%. Does not affect Erase.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kouyou) Momijigari: Autumn Leaf Hunting — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. When this NP is used, Kouyou is first inflicted with Burn (Momiji) for 2◈ Turns at a 500% chance, and apply Atk Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X%; if NP, (half of X)%; where X=(15 \+ 5 \* the number of debuffs on Kouyou). Then, deal 3x damage to the DU. After that, Kouyou transforms into her Human Form at the end of the Combat Process and reduces the Cooldown of ‘Shapeshift: Dinosaur’ by half its current Cooldown. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

Burn (Momiji) has the same effect as normal Burn, but Base Attack reduction does not affect NP. If inflicted on a Unit with normal Burn, then it will be removed and replaced with Burn (Momiji).
