(Spartacus) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Passive) When Spartacus enters Combat, he will not leave Combat and will continue Moving towards and Attacking enemy Units who had previously engaged in Combat with Spartacus until all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of himself are defeated, or all enemy Units that previously engaged Spartacus in Combat are at least 6 panels (5 panels?) away.   
Spartacus can be forced to not Move towards an enemy Unit who previously engaged Spartacus in Combat, who would be within Range after Spartacus Moves by expending 1 Command Spell.  
Spartacus can be forced to retreat from Combat (i.e. Move away from an enemy Unit who previously engaged Spartacus in Combat) by expending 2 Command Spells.  
Whenever a Command Spell(s) is used on Spartacus, Spartacus’ Mad Enhancement is Activated (regardless of Cooldown) and cannot be deactivated for 2◈ Turns for each Command Spell used.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated; while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated, its Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated unless it is Active due to Command Spells being used on Spartacus.

(Spartacus) Honor of the Battered — Rank: B+  
(Passive) Spartacus’ Health is restored by 50 at the end of his Turn and at the end of every Turn he Acts. Negated while he has the Regen (Battered) buff.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Regen (Battered) to himself for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Spartacus’ Health is restored by 15% of its maximum value at the end of his Turn, at the end of any Turn he Acts, and at the end of the Round. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Spartacus) Unyielding Will — Rank: A  
(Passive) Spartacus does not perform Injury Rolls when damaged by non-NP Attacks.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Guts to Spartacus for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Spartacus is defeated he is revived with 1 Health; and reduce his NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Spartacus) Triumphant Return of the Sword — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores Spartacus’ Health by 40% of its maximum value; and applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Spartacus) Crying Warmonger: The Howl of the Wounded Beast — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive) Whenever Spartacus receives damage, the amount of damage received is added to Spartacus’ ‘Crying Warmonger’ Counter. Whenever Spartacus performs a Normal Attack, his Attack deals additional damage equal to (half of?) his ‘Crying Warmonger’ Counter. Whenever Spartacus’ Health is restored, his ‘Crying Warmonger’ Counter is reduced by half of the amount restored.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has different effects depending on the amount of ‘Crying Warmonger’ Spartacus has-  
1\. Crying Warmonger’s value is equal or less than 50% of Spartacus’ Max Health: Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any direction next to Spartacus for 4x damage plus 100 plus Crying Warmonger’s current value; then Spartacus’ Health is restored by 20% of its maximum value. \[Anti-Army\]  
2\. Crying Warmonger’s value equals 51% to 100% of Spartacus’ Max Health: Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any direction next to Spartacus for 4x damage plus 100 plus Crying Warmonger’s current value; then Spartacus’ Health is restored by 40% of its maximum value. \[Anti-Army\]  
3\. Crying Warmonger’s value equals 101% to 200% of Spartacus’ Max Health: Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area in any direction next to Spartacus for 4x damage plus 100 plus Crying Warmonger’s current value; then Spartacus’ Health is restored by 60% of its maximum value. \[Anti-Fortress\]  
4\. Crying Warmonger’s value is higher than 200% of Spartacus’ Max Health: Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 9x9 panel area in any direction next to Spartacus for 4x damage plus 100 plus Crying Warmonger’s current value; then Spartacus’ Health is restored by 80% of its maximum value. \[Anti-Fortress\]  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Spartacus) Warcry Overload: Extreme Adversity Wounded Beast's Howl — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)/Anti-Fortress\]  
Can only be used by expending 1 Command Spell. Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, Spartacus can Move to a panel of his choice within his MOV (even if he already has Moved in this Turn, this Move does not count towards the number of Units who Move in a Turn). Hits a 11x11 panel area around Spartacus for 4x damage plus 100 plus Crying Warmonger’s current value, Total damage dealt is further increased by 100%. Then, Spartacus is inflicted with Erase. Can only be used once per game.
