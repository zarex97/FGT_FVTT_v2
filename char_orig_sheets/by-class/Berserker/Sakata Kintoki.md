(Kintoki) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: E  
(Passive) Cannot normally be Activated. Whenever Kintoki receives damage from an Attack, he performs a Luck Check. If the Luck Check Fails, Mad Enhancement is Activated for 2◈ Turns, it cannot be manually deactivated and is automatically deactivated after its duration ends. His Luck is not reduced (by 1\) when a Luck Check is used in this manner  
These effects are applied to Kintoki while Mad Enhancement is Active.  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 5 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 5 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Kintoki) Divinity — Rank: D  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 20 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Kintoki) Monstrous Strength — Rank: A+

(Active) Used during your Turn. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Kintoki) Natural Body — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores his Health by 30% of its maximum value, then applies Off.Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by Offensive Debuffs is reduced by 120%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kintoki) Golden Eater: Golden Eating — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Whenever Kintoki performs a Normal Attack while he has any Cartridges, it uses up 1 Cartridge and deals 10% extra damage. Whenever Kintoki performs a Normal Attack, Golden Eater has a 5% chance of being Jammed, chance of getting Jammed is increased by 5% for each successful Normal Attack, and Jammed chance is reset whenever it occurs. When Jammed occurs, Kintoki’s Normal Attack is negated, but it does not consume a Cartridge; however, the enemy Unit can still Counter. Note: When Jammed occurs, it still counts as Kintoki has performed an Attack for that Turn.  
Kintoki’s Normal Attacks deal Lightning damage (half) with a 20% chance of inflicting Shock for 1◈ Turns.  
After every 2 full Rounds, Kintoki gains 1 Cartridge at the end of the Round.  
During your Turn, if Kintoki is standing directly next to an allied Unit with the ‘Item Construction’ Skill, that allied Unit can use that Skill to create 1 Cartridge for every Rank in Item Construction that Unit has. Cooldown for this Active effect on that Unit: 2◈ Turns.  
If Kintoki performs a Normal Attack without any Cartridges, Total Damage dealt is reduced by 20%.  
(Active/Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Can be used from the start of the game. Kintoki gains 1d6 Cartridges. Counts as Kintoki’s Attack for the Turn. Does not cost his Master’s Health when used. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kintoki) Golden Spark: Golden Shock — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=3. Consumes 3 Cartridges, cannot be used if Kintoki has less than 3 Cartridges. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. This NP has the Ignore Def effect. Deals damage as follows-  
1\. 3x3 panel area in the middle: Deals 3x damage, then inflicts Shock for 2◈ Turns and has a 75% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. Deals Lightning damage (half). Then, inflicts Def Dwn (B) for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%.   
2\. Outer panels that are not the 3x3 panel area in the middle: Deals 1.5x damage, then inflicts Shock for 2◈ Turns and has a 50% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. Lightning damage. Then, inflicts Def Dwn (B) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%. DUs on these panels can Block & Evade, but cannot Counter.  
When this NP is used, Kintoki can consume additional Cartridges to increase its damage; for each Cartridge consumed in this way, this NP deals 25 bonus Lightning damage.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
