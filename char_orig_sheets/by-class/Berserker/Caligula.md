(Caligula) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: A  
(Passive) Constantly Active. Can only be deactivated if there is another allied Unit with the ‘Roma’ Attribute within a 2 panel area of Caligula.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; its Master’s Health cannot drop below 25 in this way while the Skill does not meet the condition to be deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 55%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 85% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) (40%).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is deactivated for its duration.  
Mad Enhancement can be deactivated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell (it will reactivate if the aforementioned conditions are still met after those 1◈ Turns).

(Caligula) Imperial Privilege — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores his Health by 30% of its maximum value.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Caligula) Sadistic Constitution — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken is increased by 10% including NP.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever Caligula takes damage from an Attack, apply Atk Up (Sadist) for 1◈ Turns to himself at the end of the Damage Step, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Atk Up (Sadist) does not stack, but its duration is reset if reapplied.
