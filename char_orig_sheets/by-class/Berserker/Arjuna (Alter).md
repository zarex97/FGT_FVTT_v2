(Junao) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Junao) Divinity — Rank: EX  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 60 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Junao) Anti-Evil (Unique) — Rank: EX  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Debuffed) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with debuffs is increased by 30%; does not apply to Unremovable debuffs (if the Unit has other debuffs that are not Unremovable then this effect still applies).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Junao) Clairvoyance (Transcendental) — Rank: EX  
(Passive) When a Unit attempts to Evade Arjuna’s Attack, the Unit always rolls for Evade-; if the DU has the ‘Divine’ Attribute, its Evade roll is increased by 2 instead.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%; and reduce Arjuna’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Junao) Lamplight of Souls — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Arjuna is defeated he is revived with 15% of his Health, Agility and Luck. Also applies Regen (Souls) for 1◈ Turns, Arjuna’s Health is restored by 15% of its maximum value and his Agility & Luck are restored by 2 at the end of his Turn and at the end of every Turn he Acts. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Junao) Mahāpralaya: The Revolving Sword that Adjudicates Reoccurring Destruction — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Range=6. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area within Range. First inflicts Def Dwn (B) on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%; if NP, 60%. Then, deals 5x damage. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
