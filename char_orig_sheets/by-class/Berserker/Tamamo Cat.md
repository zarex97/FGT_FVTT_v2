(Tamamo Cat) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 25% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 25% including NP. 

(Tamamo Cat) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 40% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Tamamo Cat) Monstrous Strength — Rank: B  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 80%; if NP, 40%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Note: This effect is not treated as applying a buff. 

(Tamamo Cat) Witchcraft — Rank: E  
(Passive) Tamamo Cat’s Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) have a 20% chance of ignoring Magic Resistance. Negated while affected by Skill Seal or Silence.

(Tamamo Cat) Jusō: Byōnisshō — Rank: B  
Spell. Mantra: Feline Sunshine. Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit. Has a 60% chance to increase the enemy Unit’s NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns. Then, applies Invuln to herself for 1 time. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Tamamo Cat) Shapeshift (Lunch) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects when used-  
1\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%.   
2\. Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 20% of its original value.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for ⅓◈ Turns. Does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tamamo Cat) Jusō: Shōnisshō  
Damage Spell. Mantra: Shining Sunshine. Range=3. Hits a 3x3 panel area for normal damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Tamamo Cat) Sansan Nikkō Hiruyasumi Shuchi Nikurin — Rank: D+ (NP)  
Opulence of Sun and Shine and Catnap. Only usable when Mad Enhancement is deactivated. Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Tamamo Cat for 2.5x damage plus 100\. Then, inflicts Sleep on Tamamo Cat for 1◈ Turns; and applies Regen for 2◈ Turns, Tamamo Cat restores her Health by 50% of its maximum value at the end of her Turn, at the end of every Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round. Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Tamamo Cat) Sansan Nikkō Hiruyasumi Shuchi Nikurin: Bakeneko Henge — Rank: ??? (NP)  
(Non-damaging/Passive) Monster Cat Transformation. Only usable when Mad Enhancement is Active. When this NP is used, the following effects are applied to Tamamo Cat for a maximum of 2◈ Turns (can be ended earlier)-  
1\. Tamamo Cat’s size is increased from 1 panel to 2 panels (Note: She has a custom figurine for this, use that).  
2\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP. This effect overwrites the damage increasing effect of Mad Enhancement (Effect 3).  
3\. When this NP is used/activated, Tamamo Cat’s Max Health is increased by 50% of its maximum value for the duration of the NP, and she also heals her current Health by that amount.  
4\. All damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. This effect overwrites the damage reduction effect of Mad Enhancement.  
5\. The amount of damage taken that is required to trigger an Injury Roll is doubled (i.e. 200\).  
6\. Tamamo Cat cannot Block; and when she performs an Agility Check, double the value of the roll.  
7\. Tamamo Cat can Move to any panel, when she Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Tamamo Cat to stand on.  
8\. Tamamo Cat’s Normal Attacks now hit a 2x2 panel area directly next to her, and knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
9\. Tamamo Cat has the ‘Large’ Attribute when this NP is Active.  
10\. At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Tamamo Cat’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of each Turn if Tamamo Cat Acted; and 10 Health if she did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens. This effect overwrites the Health loss from active Mad Enhancement.  
11\. Tamamo Cat cannot use any Skills while this NP is Active.  
Tamamo Cat can end Bakeneko Henge at the end of any Turn.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after Bakeneko Henge ends.

Note: Sansan Nikkō Hiruyasumi Shuchi Nikurin cannot be used if Bakeneko Henge is on Cooldown and vice versa.
