(Shinpachi) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: D+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 10 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Master’s Health is 10 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 25%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 25% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Unit can only Evade with Evade-. Negated while ‘Chikai no Haori’ is Active.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Shinpachi) Gamushin — Rank: A  
(Active) Reckless Shinpachi. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Gamushin’ buff for 1◈ Turns, whenever he is successfully Attacked, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
3\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Shinpachi is defeated, he is revived with 30% of his Max Health.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Shinpachi) Eye of the Mind (True) — Rank: B  
(Passive) Evade rolls are reduced by 2, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Def Up for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Shinpachi) Kenbatsu Dochou — Rank: C  
(Passive) Whenever Shinpachi performs a successful Normal Attack, it deals an extra (5\*X) Fire MAG damage, where X=the number rolled on a twelve-sided die. Additionally, if the number rolled was 7 or greater, the DU is also knocked back by 1 panel. If Mad Enhancement is Active, X is increased by 3\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up (Kenbatsu) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Kenbatsu) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Shinpachi) Ryūhiken — Rank: \- (NP) \[Anti-Unit Mystic Sword\]  
Dragonflight Blade. Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. First, has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
3\. Remove all Dodge buffs from the DU.  
Deals 3x damage and knocks back the DU by 2 panels. Fire damage (half). Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Shinpachi) Chikai no Haori — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Coat of Oaths. When this NP is used, it applies the following effects on Shinpachi-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 30% including NP.  
2\. All damage received is decreased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. MOV is increased by 1\.   
4\. Range is increased by 1\.  
5\. For Kenbatsu Dochou, X is increased by 3\.  
6\. Crit DmUp applied by Ryūhiken becomes Crit DmUp (Kenbatsu).  
These effects lasts for 2◈ Turns, or after Shinpachi has performed 5 Crit Attacks, or the Player cancels it. After 2◈ Turns, Shinpachi can extend the duration of Chikai no Haori by 1◈ additional Turns by reducing his Health by 100 (cannot be extended if his Health is equal or below that), but it will still be forcefully ended after he performs a Crit for the fifth time for the duration of that activation. Shinpachi can end the effect of Chikai no Haori during his Turn and at the end of any Turn he Acts. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after Chikai no Haori ends.    
Note: Crit Attacks include NP Crits.
