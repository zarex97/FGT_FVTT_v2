#### (Daedalus) Naucrate Alloy Heart: Bygone Unfading Smile — Rank: B \[Anti-Unit\]

(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Can only be used while an allied Naucrate Unit does not exist on the field. When used, consume half of Daedalus’ Build Stacks to construct a Naucrate Unit, then replace Daedalus’ figurine with the Naucrate figurine. When Daedalus is summoned, he creates a Naucrate Unit for free. The Naucrate Unit has the same base Stats as Daedalus, but Max Health is halved. Can be used while inflicted with NP Seal. Does not consume his Master’s Health as a Cost when used. For the terminology of this Servant, in situations where Daedalus does not have a Naucrate Unit, it will be written as ‘while Naucrate Alloy Heart is not Active’ and vice-versa.  
(Passive 1\) While Naucrate Alloy Heart is not Active, Daedalus gains the following effects-  
1\. Loses the ‘Female’ Attribute.  
2\. Total STR damage dealt is reduced by 40% including NP.  
(Passive 2\) For every 20 Build Stacks on Daedalus, Naucrate’s Build Level increases by 1 (0\~20: Level 1, 21\~40: Level 2, 41\~60: Level 3\). For every Build Level of Naucrate after Level 1, gain the following effects-  
1\. Naucrate’s Max & current Health is increased by 100\.  
2\. Naucrate’s MOV is increased by 1\.  
3\. Naucrate’s Base Attack (both) is increased by 10\.  
4\. At Build Level 3, gain the ‘Levitating’ Attribute and Agility Check rolls are reduced by 3\.  
(Passive 3\) All buffs, debuffs, and other effects on Daedalus are shared with Naucrate and vice-versa.  
(Passive 4\) When Naucrate is defeated, her figurine is replaced with the Daedalus figurine; then Daedalus takes damage equal to the amount exceeding Naucrate’s Health when she was defeated.  
(Passive 5\) While Naucrate Alloy Heart is Active and Build Level is Level 2 or lower, at the start of a Damage Step where Naucrate is successfully Attacked, Daedalus can choose to take all damage from that Attack instead of Naucrate (damage is dealt to the Daedalus Unit which is not on field). If Daedalus is defeated before Naucrate, Naucrate remains on the field, but she gains a STN of 2◈ Turns plus 1◈ Turns for every Build Level on herself. Naucrate in this situation cannot be Contracted by other Players, while the Player (Master) controlling her cannot use Command Spells on Naucrate, also she loses the ‘Male’ Attribute.

## (Daedalus) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement (Modification) — Rank: B-

(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Daedalus’ Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn he Acts; when its Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.   
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 35%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 55%; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Daedalus’ Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Daedalus can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and cannot be removed except by its own effect/duration; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

## (Daedalus) Class Skill: Item Construction (Physical) — Rank: EX

(Passive 1\) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself; if he is within his Home Base, then affects all allied Units within his Home Base instead- Chance of having buffs applied to this Unit is increased by 10%; further increased by 10% if Daedalus is within his Home Base; further increased by 10% if Mad Enhancement (Modification) is Active.  
(Passive 2\) Daedalus gains Build Stacks whenever he performs the following Actions-  
1\. Uses Mad Enhancement (Modification): \+1d6  
2\. Uses a Skill other than ME (Modification): \+3  
3\. Performs a successful non-NP Attack: \+1, \+2 instead if Crit.  
4\. Uses ‘Icarus Ascends Again’: \+5 for every Unit hit by the NP.  
5\. Uses ‘Crucible of Cyclops’: \+3 for every allied Unit within the NP area when used.  
6\. Once per Turn, during your Turn, consume 1 Design Token to gain 1d8 Build Stacks.  
While Mad Enhancement (Modification) is Active, all Build Stack gain is increased by 1\.

## (Daedalus) Class Skill: Territory Creation (Physical) — Rank: EX

(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 3d20 including NP; number of dice rolled is doubled while Mad Enhancement (Modification) is not Active.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP; while Mad Enhancement (Modification) is not Active, reduced by (3d10+50) instead.

### (Daedalus) Cultural Hero — Rank: B

(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP is increased by 10%.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
3\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 2 times to himself.  
4\. Applies the ‘Crete’ buff for 2◈ Turns, 9 times to himself. Does not stack.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

‘Crete’ buff-  
When performing a non-NP Normal Attack, roll a six-sided die and apply the corresponding effect to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself at the end of the Damage Step, each effect also corresponds to an Attack Skill, do not roll a die for those. Each effect can only be applied up to twice per Turn-  
1, 2, Steel Rain, Icarus Feather: Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP is increased by 10%.  
3, 4, Melting Crucible, Thruster Overburn: Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
5, 6, Sen’etsu Buster, Cyclopean Smelter: Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
When Daedalus is affected by a buff removing effect while he has the ‘Crete’ buff, if that effect would only remove one buff and the ‘Crete’ buff was selected by it, it is reduced by 3 stacks (times) instead of being fully removed.

### (Daedalus) Steel Rain / Icarus Feather

Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
1\. Steel Rain. Attack Skill. Cannot be used at Build Level 3\. Range=3. Roll a four-sided die for every 2 Build Stacks on Daedalus and deal that much STR damage on the DU, not affected by any damage-modifying effects on Daedalus. For every Design Token on Daedalus when this Skill was used, the grade of die rolled is increased by one grade (i.e. 1 Design Token \= d6, 2 Design Tokens \= d8, 3 Design Tokens \= d10).  
2\. Icarus Feather. Attack Skill. Replaces ‘Steel Rain’ while at Build Level 3\. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals 30% damage X times, X increases by 1 for every 5 Build Stacks on Daedalus. If the Attack is Blocked, damage is reduced from the Total damage taken; if the DU Evades, it has to Evade each hit separately, and can no longer Evade after failing any Evades.  
Then, if Naucrates or Daedalus’s Health is less than 50% of its maximum value, apply Regen (Repair) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, at the end of this Unit’s Turn, at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round, Health is restored by 5% of its maximum value; affected Units with the ‘Mechanical’ Attribute have their Health restored by 10% instead. Otherwise, apply the ‘Refit’ buff for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself.

‘Refit’ buff-   
1\. Crit Chance of non-NP Attacks have an 80% chance of being increased by 10%; if this effect activated, Crit Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 20%.  
2\. This buff is categorized as both Crit Up and Crit DmUp.  
3\. Chance of this buff being removed is reduced by 50%.

#### (Daedalus) Crucible of Cyclops: Blacksmith God’s Crucible — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Can only be used while ME (Modification) is not Active. Roll an eight-sided die and create that many Mechataurus (they will appear within a 5x5 panel area around him).  
Mechataurus have 300 Health, 9 Agility, 9 Luck, Range=1, MOV=3, Base Attack (STR)=90, Detect=3 panels. Mechataurus have the \[Sky\] & ‘Mechanical’ Attributes.  
Mechataurus do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Mechataurus can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Daedalus or his Master if there is a Mechataurus directly next to them.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns plus ½◈ Turns for every Mechataurus created on this usage.

#### (Daedalus) Icarus Ascends Again: Soar Once More, Indestructible Wings — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]

Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+3 for the Combat Process. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. First, apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 15% plus 5% for every Build Level of Naucrate. Deals 4x damage plus 100 and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%; this damage and debuff does not affect all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Daedalus. Then, removes all debuffs from all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Daedalus and restore their Health by 200\. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

#### (Daedalus) Aplόs Chaos Labyrinthos: Simple Eternally Unchanging Labyrinth — Rank: B (NP) \[Labyrinth\]

(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Can only be used while ME (Modification) is Active. Affects a 7x7 panel area around Daedalus when used, this NP area will now be termed as the ‘Labyrinth’. Has the following effects-  
1\. When this NP is used, three Mechataurus are created and placed inside the Labyrinth. At the end of every Round, one Mechataurus is created and placed inside the Labyrinth.  
2\. The MOV of all enemy Units within the Labyrinth is reduced by 2 (minimum MOV=2).  
3\. Units outside the Labyrinth are able to enter the Labyrinth.  
4\. In order to Escape the Labyrinth, an enemy Unit must first Move to the inner border of the Labyrinth. At this point, if that Unit does not have any MOV left, it is unable to Escape on the same Turn; but if the Unit is still able to Move at least 1 panel, it can attempt to Escape.  
The Base Success Chance of Escaping the Labyrinth is 40% (use a twenty-sided die for this). If the Unit Fails to Escape, it is Moved to a random panel within the Labyrinth. If the Unit Succeeds, it is able to Move out of the Labyrinth equivalent to the number of panels it can still Move. Every time a Unit fails an Escape, the success chance is increased by 10% for the next Escape attempt.   
5\. Lasts for 3◈ Turns (2◈ Turns?).  
6\. The Labyrinth is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Daedalus is defeated.  
7\. If a(n enemy) Unit that has Successfully escaped the Labyrinth at least once reenters the Labyrinth, its Base Success Chance of Escaping is increased to 100%, and its MOV is no longer reduced within the Labyrinth. A Unit who has Successfully Escaped the Labyrinth before can also lead other allied Units out of the Labyrinth (i.e. all allied Units directly next to a Unit that has Successfully Escaped the Labyrinth before also has a Base Success Chance of Escaping of 100%, and its MOV is also no longer reduced within the Labyrinth).  
8\. Units outside the Labyrinth cannot Attack or apply any effects to Units within the Labyrinth and vice versa.  
9\. The inside of the Labyrinth is ‘Indoors’ Terrain.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after the NP ends.
