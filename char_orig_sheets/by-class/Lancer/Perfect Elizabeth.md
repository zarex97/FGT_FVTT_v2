(Liz) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Liz) Successor of Planet P. — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up (Eliza) for 1◈ Turns to herself, STR and NP damage dealt are increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Elizaburn’ buff for 1◈ Turns to herself, all Attacks inflict Burn (Liz) for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: Burn (Liz) has the same effect as normal Burn, but its duration can be extended by inflicting it again. Burn (Liz) does not stack with normal Burn, and will overwrite normal Burn if applied to a Unit with it.

(Liz) Eliza Particles — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 (3) panel area of herself by ⅔◈ Turns.   
3\. Applies the ‘Eliza’ effect to all other Units within a 2 (3) panel area of herself for 1◈ Turns. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff, and also is not applied on Units whose True Name is Elizabeth Báthory. However, when applying this effect to enemy Units, it is affected by half the magnitude of their Mental Debuff Resist. Not affected by Debuff Immune.  
4a. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Mental Debuff ResDwn to all other allied Units within a 2 (3) panel area of herself for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by Mental Debuffs is increased by 50%.  
4b. Has a 50% chance of inflicting Mental Debuff ResDwn to all enemy Units within a 2 (3) panel area of herself for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by Mental Debuffs is increased by 50%. However, if an affected enemy Unit has the ‘Eliza’ effect, the chance of inflicting Mental Debuff ResDwn becomes 500%.  
5\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 5% (10%).  
6\. Gain 1 Particles for every other Unit within the Skill area.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Elizabeth performs the following Actions, she gains the stated number of Particles as follows-  
1\. Performing a successful non-NP Attack: \+1, \+2 if Crit  
2\. Performing or receiving a successful Attack on/from a Unit with the ‘Elizabeth’ Attribute: \+3, \+4 if Crit. (Note: Overwrites Effect 1.)  
3\. Using a non-Attack Skill other than Eliza Particles: \+2  
4\. Receiving an NP or Skill Cooldown reducing effect from another Unit: \+2  
5\. Performing a successful NP: \+5  
6\. A Command Spell is used on her: \+7
