(Ieyasu) Tokugawa System: Kagemusha — Rank: A (NP) \[Self\]  
(Passive) When Ieyasu is summoned, roll an eight-sided die. The number rolled determines the Servant Class he is summoned as.  
1\. Saber  
2\. Lancer  
3\. Archer  
4\. Rider  
5\. Caster  
6\. Assassin  
7\. Berserker  
8\. Your choice of the above seven.  
(Non-damaging) Can be used after 2 full Rounds have passed. Can be used during your Turn, at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked, and at the start of the Round. When used, Ieyasu can swap to one of the other six Servant Classes; each Class having its own Stats, Parameters, and Class Skills, all listed further down. When Ieyasu swaps his Servant Class, his Stats are transferred accordingly to their percentage; remember to roll Stats for every version of Ieyasu when summoned (including the Ieyasu who has lost all Items, see below). Whenever Ieyasu swaps his Servant Class, his Master loses 20 Health (instead of the normal Health loss from Servant’s NP). Cooldown: 2 full Rounds.

(Ieyasu) Tokugawa System: Seven Weapons  
(Passive) Ieyasu owns seven Items which are the Noble Phantasms that allow him to swap to each of the seven Servant Classes. If Ieyasu loses any one of those Items, he must immediately swap to a different Servant Class; and as long as does not regain that Item, he is unable to swap to the corresponding Servant Class. If Ieyasu loses all of those Items, he remains in the last Servant Class he swapped to; while his Stats are changed to those as reflected on this page (above). The Items are as follows-  
1\. \[Daitengu Masaie\] \- Saber  
2\. \[Meisou Tonbogiri\] \- Lancer  
3\. \[Kuni Kuzushi\] \- Archer  
4\. \[Iishi no Aka Sonae\] \- Rider  
5\. \[Tenrin Shippou no Ichi\] \- Caster  
6\. \[Mumyou Hikunai\] \- Assassin  
7\. \[Muramasa BLADE\] \- Berserker  
Ieyasu Equips the corresponding Item depending on which Servant Class he is currently in, but he does not receive any effects from said Item (the effects are already applied to him in his Parameter/Stat/Skill/NP changes).

**Items-**

\[Daitengu Masaie\]  
*A sword said to have been used by Yagyuu Tajima-no-Kami Munenori.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Base Attack (STR) by 50 and Max Agility by 1, and Range is changed to 1 panel. Also, Crit Chance is increased by 10% and Evade rolls are reduced by 1\.

\[Meisou Tonbogiri\]  
*One of Japan’s Three Great Spears, used by Takadatsu Honda.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Base Attack (STR) by 25 and Max Agility by 2, and Range is changed to 2 panels. Also, Crit Chance is increased by 25% and Crit Damage dealt is increased by 10%.

\[Kuni Kuzushi\]  
*A breech-loading swivel gun, also known as a Frankish gun.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Base Attack (STR) by 30, and Range is increased by 2 panels (max Range=4 panels). Also, Normal Attack damage dealt is reduced by 20%; and the Unit has a 40% chance of being able to perform a 2nd Normal Attack after performing a Normal Attack.

\[Ii no Aka Sonae\]  
*Armor as red as blood, said to have a psychological impact on the enemy.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Max Health by 100 and MOV is increased by 1\. Also, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%; and whenever this Unit initiates Combat (Attacks first in a Combat Phase), damage dealt is increased by 15%.

\[Tenrin Shippou no Ichi\]  
*One of the seven treasures of Chakravarti.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Base Attack (MAG) by 50 and Max Luck by 1, and Range is increased by 2 panels (max Range=3 panels). Also, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG), Normal Attack damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute is increased by 25%, and Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns.

\[Mumyou Hikunai\]  
*A nameless kunai, used by the nameless assassins who lurked in the shadows.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Base Attack (STR) by 10, Max Agility by 2, and Move by 1, and Range is increased by 2 panels (max Range=3 panels). Also, Normal Attacks have a 25% chance of inflicting Bleed for 1◈ Turns, and a 10% chance of inflicting Evade Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Evade rolls are increased by 1\.

\[Muramasa BLADE\]  
*A cursed katana which drives its wielder mad with bloodlust.*  
When Equipped, the Unit increases its Base Attack (STR) by 50, and Range is increased by 1 panel. Also, Normal Attacks have a 25% chance of inflicting Curse, and a 10% chance of inflicting Luck Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Luck Check rolls are increased by 1\.
