(Caenis) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Caenis) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Caenis is damaged by an Attack while Mad Enhancement is inactive, he has a 10% chance of activating Mad Enhancement for 2◈ Turns. This chance is increased by 10% for each time Caenis is damaged and Mad Enhancement is not activated. This chance is reset whenever Mad Enhancement is activated, then deactivated.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Caenis is damaged while Mad Enhancement is active, he has a 10% chance of being affected with Berserk for 1◈ Turns. This chance is increased by 10% for each time Caenis is damaged and Berserk is not activated. This chance is reset whenever Mad Enhancement is deactivated.  
(Passive 3\) While Caenis is affected by Berserk, Caenis treats all Units as enemy Units. While Caenis is affected by Berserk, he has a 30% chance of ignoring Command Spell commands that do not involve healing, increasing damage dealt or reducing damage received.  
(Passive 4\) When Mad Enhancement is Active, Caenis’ Master can spend one Command Spell to deactivate it. When Caenis is affected by Berserk, his Master can spend one Command Spell to remove Berserk from Caenis. Berserk on Caenis cannot be removed with the Command Spell: Cure Servant command.   
While Mad Enhancement is Active, Caenis gains the following effects-  
1\. Caenis’ Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn he Acts; his Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Caenis’ Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Caenis can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Caenis) Sea God’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Caenis performs a successful Normal Attack, he has a 10% chance of restoring 1 Luck at the end of the Damage Step.  
(Passive 3\) MOV is doubled and Agility Check rolls are halved when Caenis is within a ‘Waterside’ area. (Halve the roll value before any additions/subtractions to the value.)  
(Passive 4\) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% if Caenis is not within a ‘Waterside’ area; while all damage and effects from Water damage are negated if Caenis is within a ‘Waterside’ area. Also applies to NP.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Caenis) Monstrous Strength — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 90%; if NP, 45%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns. Note: This effect is not treated as applying a buff.

(Caenis) Halberd of Pillaging — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the ‘Halberd’ buff to Caenis for 1◈ Turns, 3 times. Whenever Caenis performs a Normal Attack, apply Atk Up to himself for ⅓◈ Turns at the start of the Combat Process, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Pillaging’ buff to Caenis for 1◈ Turns, 3 times. Whenever Caenis performs a successful Normal Attack, inflict Atk Dwn for ⅓◈ Turns on the DU, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

Whenever Caenis uses Halberd of Pillaging, it has a chance of being upgraded to ‘Halberd of Usurpation’ after being used, this chance is increased by 30% every time Halberd of Pillaging is used.

(Caenis) Halberd of Usurpation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the ‘Halberd’ buff to Caenis for 1◈ Turns, 3 times. Whenever Caenis performs a Normal Attack, apply Atk Up to himself for ⅓◈ Turns at the start of the Combat Process, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Pillaging’ buff to Caenis for 1◈ Turns, 3 times. Whenever Caenis performs a successful Normal Attack, inflict Atk Dwn for ⅓◈ Turns on the DU, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit is increased by 10%.  
4\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Caenis) Affection of the Sea God — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has different effects depending on whether Caenis was in a ‘Waterside’ area or not when this Skill is used. Has the following effects-  
1\. When used outside a ‘Waterside’ area: Applies Dmg Cut to himself for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage received is reduced by 200; and applies Guts to himself for 1◈+½◈ Turns, when Caenis is defeated he is revived with 15% of his Max Health.  
2\. When used within a ‘Waterside’ area: Applies Dmg Cut to himself for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage received is reduced by 300; and applies Guts to himself for 1◈+½◈ Turns, when Caenis is defeated he is revived with 25% of his Max Health.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Caenis) Lapithai Caeneus: Soar, My Great Golden Wings — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
{M} Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=6. First, applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%; and applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%. Then, hits a 5x5(3x3?) panel area within Range for 4x damage. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(Caenis) Poseidon Maelstrom: God of the Sea, Rampaging Tidal Wave — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Fortress/Anti-Ocean\]  
{S} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=4. Hits a 5x5(3x3?) panel area within Range for 5x damage and inflicts Slow for 1◈ Turns. Water damage.  
However, when used within a ‘Waterside’ area, Range=6; hits a 7x7(5x5?) panel area within Range for 5x damage and has a 60% chance of inflicting Drowning for 1◈ Turns; if Drowning fails to be inflicted, then inflicts Slow for 2◈ Turns. Water damage.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

Note: Lapithai Caeneus cannot be used on the same Round as Poseidon Maelstrom and vice versa.
