(Percival) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B++  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B++ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Percival) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: C+  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 3 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Percival) Protection of the Holy Grail — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Percival) Guardian Knight (Holy Lance) — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself other than himself is Attacked while Percival is within Range of the AU. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Decoy to himself for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Dmged NP Regen (Guardian) to himself for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by X Turns at the end of the Damage Step whenever Percival receives damage from an Attack. (X=1d4 if ◈ is 6 or less; X=1d6 if ◈ is greater than 6.)  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Percival) Light of Salvation — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns and restores the Unit’s Health by 200\. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Percival) Longinus Count Zero: The Illuminating Lance of Destiny — Rank: C+\~A+ (NP) \[Anti-Fortress\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. When this NP is used, first check the 5x5 panel area around Percival for the allied Unit with the greatest percentage difference in Max/current Health; this Unit will be termed as the ‘Illuminated’.  
Apply Pierce to Percival for ⅓◈ Turns and apply NP DmUp to Percival for this Combat Phase, NP damage dealt is increased by X%, where X is the percentage difference of the Illuminated Unit’s Max/current Health (minimum=20%).  
Deals 3.5x damage plus 100; then restore the Illuminated Unit’s Health by 30% of its maximum value.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns. When this NP is used, it is treated as being a Rank B+ NP.
