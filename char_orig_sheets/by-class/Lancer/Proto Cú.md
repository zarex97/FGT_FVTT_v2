(ProtoCú) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(ProtoCú) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(ProtoCú) Rune Magic — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. This Skill can be used in several different methods. Only one effect of Rune Magic can be active at a time.  
1\. Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals normal damage and has a 45% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 45%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
3\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 45%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies LUC Up for 1◈ Turns, Luck Check rolls are reduced by 2\. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
This Skill cannot be used while inflicted with Silence.

(ProtoCú) Protection from Arrows — Rank: B  
(Passive) Cú will automatically Evade any Attack from a Ranged weapon (arrows, firearms, thrown weapons, magic projectiles, etc) including Noble Phantasms from his Front and Sides (with a few possible exceptions?); when he receives an aforementioned Attack from Behind (i.e. the 3 panels behind him) and he Evades, the Evade roll is reduced by 2\. Does not apply to Ranged AoE Attacks/Noble Phantasms. Has no effect if this Unit is affected by Skill Seal.

(ProtoCú) Beast Slayer — Rank: B++  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute is increased by 40% including NP.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Dmg Up (Wild) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute is further increased by 60%; and applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(ProtoCú) Green Spear of Reminiscence  
When Proto Cú is summoned, he first uses his Green Spear of Reminiscence in Combat. When he is using the Green Spear of Reminiscence, Total STR damage dealt is reduced by 10%.  
Whenever Proto Cú Attacks, Blocks, or Evades, the Spear has a 10% chance of breaking at the end of the Damage Step. If the Attack was a Noble Phantasm, the chance of breaking is increased by 30%. When the Spear breaks, he swaps to Gáe Bolg, and his STR damage dealt is no longer reduced.  
Proto Cú’s Master can use a Command Spell to make Proto Cú use his Gáe Bolg instead of his Green Spear, this lasts for the rest of the game.

(ProtoCú) Gáe Bolg: Piercing Crimson Lance — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. Has different effects depending on whether Proto Cú is still using his Green Spear or not-  
Green Spear  
First, restore 2 Agility and apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Deals 3.5x damage plus 100 with a 50% chance of inflicting Instakill and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%. This NP has the Ignore Def effect.  
Not using Green Spear  
First, restore 3 Agility and apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. This NP has the Aim & Ignore Def effects. When this NP is used, the DU performs a Luck Check. If Failed, the DU is inflicted with Instakill and Def Crk (Bolg).  
If the Luck Check succeeded or Instakill was not inflicted, this NP deals 3.5x damage plus 100\. Then, inflict Def Dwn on the DU for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%; and inflict Def Crk (Bolg), all damage taken is increased by 30 including NP. Def Crk (Bolg) does not stack and is Unremovable; when Proto Cú is defeated, Def Crk (Bolg) is removed from all affected Units.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: Chance of this NP inflicting Instakill on Units with Invuln is reduced by 50%, while chance of it inflicting Instakill on Units with Anti-Purge is reduced by 100%.
