(Valkyrie) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Valkyrie) Divinity — Rank: A

6\. Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
7\. Debuff ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 25%.  
8\. Your choice of any of the above effect(s).  
On an enemy Unit, Range=2: Roll 2 eight-sided die. Apply the effects as follows-  
1\. Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 25%, if NP; 15%.  
2\. Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 25%.  
3\. Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%.  
4\. Crit DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage is reduced by 25%.  
5\. NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is reduced by 30%.

7\. Debuff ChDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
8\. Your choice of any of the above effect(s).  
If a duplicate number is rolled, apply the effect twice. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns
