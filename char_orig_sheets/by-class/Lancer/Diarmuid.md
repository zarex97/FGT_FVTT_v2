(Diarmuid) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Diarmuid) Eye of the Mind (True) — Rank: B  
(Passive) Evade rolls are reduced by 2, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Def Up for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage received by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(Diarmuid) Love Spot — Rank: C  
(Passive) At the start of any Combat Phase against a Female Unit facing him, Diarmuid has a chance to inflict Charm on the DU for ⅔◈ Turns depending on the DU’s Magic Resistance. The percentage chance is as follows-  
1\. Magic Resistance Rank D or lower: 75% chance  
2\. Magic Resistance Rank C: 50% chance  
3\. Magic Resistance Rank B or higher: Unaffected  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Phase against a Female Unit. Inflicts Atk Dwn on the DU for that Combat Phase, all damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Diarmuid) Knight Tactics — Rank: B  
(Passive) When Diarmuid successfully Evades an Attack then Counters, if the DU attempts to Evade Diarmuid’s Counter, the DU can only use the Evade- roll.

(Diarmuid) Blighted Love Flows Profusely — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. For every 2 Female Units on the field, randomly activate one of the following effects, each effect can only activate once per usage of this Skill-  
1\. Restore 2 Agility and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Restore 2 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
4\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Diarmuid) Fianna Knight’s Twin Spears  
(Passive) When Diarmuid performs a Normal Attack, the player controlling Diarmuid has to state whether he uses Gae Buidhe or Gae Dearg to Attack (at the start of the Combat Process). After he declares his Attack and the DU gives a response, Diarmuid can perform an Agility Check. If Successful, he can swap to his other spear for the Attack instead.

(Diarmuid) Gáe Dearg: Crimson Rose of Exorcism — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Diarmuid receives an Attack which deals MAG damage and he performs Block, he has an 80% of automatically negating the Attack, Diarmuid receives no damage and effects from it. Otherwise, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If it was an AoE Attack, all Units in the straight line area behind Diarmuid also receive no damage and effects.  
(Passive 2\) When Diarmuid successfully Attacks with Gae Dearg, it has a 40% chance of removing all buffs from the DU at the start of the Damage Step. Attacks with Gae Dearg have the Ignore Def effect.  
(Active/Non-damaging) When a Noble Phantasm that deals MAG damage is used on Diarmuid or an allied Unit within a 2 panel area, Gáe Dearg can be used. If this NP was used to protect an allied Unit, Diarmuid Moves in front of that allied Unit first; if the panel in front of that allied Unit is occupied, Diarmuid knocks that allied Unit back by 1 panel and Moves onto the panel it was previously standing on.  
Diarmuid then rolls a ten-sided die. If a 1 to 4 is rolled, Total Damage taken from that NP is reduced by 50%. If a 5 to 10 is rolled, no damage and effects are received from that NP. If it was an AoE NP, all Units in the straight line area behind Diarmuid also receive this effect of Gáe Dearg. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: If the received Attack deals both STR and MAG damage, and the effect of Gáe Dearg activates, it only affects the MAG damage.

(Diarmuid) Gáe Buidhe: Yellow Rose of Mortality — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Whenever Diarmuid successfully Attacks with Gae Buidhe, the damage dealt is permanently deducted from both the DU’s Max Health and current Health through the ‘Buidhe’ debuff. The ‘Buidhe’ debuff ignores Debuff Immune & Resist effects and is Unremovable. This debuff is removed from all Units affected by it when Diarmuid is defeated.  
Additionally, Diarmuid can choose to remove the ‘Buidhe’ debuff from all affected Units by breaking Gáe Buidhe during his Turn. If he does so, Gáe Buidhe can no longer be used.

To keep track of the amount of Health deducted from other Units due to the ‘Buidhe’ debuff, note the value next to the debuff (e.g. Buidhe (128)).
