(Ana) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ana) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 100 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 30%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Ana) Ephemeral Sisters — Rank: A  
(Active) Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. If the Unit is Male, inflicts Charm for 1◈ Turns. If the DU is Female, the chance of inflicting Charm is reduced by 50%. However, if used on Stheno or Euryale, chance of inflicting Charm is increased by 100% instead of being reduced.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%, and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ana) Monstrous Strength — Rank: C  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 60%; if NP, 30%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. Note: This effect is not treated as applying a buff.

(Ana) Affections towards the Beyond — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Medusa Lily is defeated and revived due to an effect, apply Dmg Cut for 1 time, all damage taken is reduced by 100 including NP, and reduce her NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns for every time this effect was activated. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Ana) White Garland — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Apply Guts for 2◈ Turns, when Medusa Lily is defeated she is revived with 20% of her Max Health.  
2\. Apply Invuln for 1 time.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ana) Harpe: Immortal Slaying Scythe — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute is increased by 100%. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 50%. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Serpent’ Attribute but without the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute is increased by 25%. If a Unit has two or more of the listed Attributes, only the effect with the strongest magnitude takes effect. Also affects Caress of the Medusa.  
2\. All Attacks including Caress of the Medusa have a 25% chance of inflicting Instakill on Units with the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. Refraction of Longevity- All Attacks including Caress of the Medusa inflict the ‘Harpe (X)’ debuff on the DU, X=the total amount of damage dealt by Medusa Lily to this Unit (X increases whenever Medusa Lily deals damage to a Unit with the ‘Harpe’ debuff). Whenever a Unit with the ‘Harpe’ debuff has its Health restored, the amount of Health restored is reduced by the value of the ‘Harpe’ debuff. However, the ‘Harpe’ debuff **does not** affect the Health restoration effect of Home Bases. The ‘Harpe’ debuff is Unremovable and is only removed when Medusa Lily is defeated. The ‘Harpe’ debuff ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects.

(Ana) Caress of the Medusa: Embrace of the Goddess — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3.5x damage plus 100, then inflicts Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%, and Agility is reduced by 1 when damage is received; and inflicts Stun for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
