(Anning) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to E is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 10%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Anning) Charisma of Sea Lilies — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied ‘Female’ and ‘Gender Unknown’ Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Female: Restore 2 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Also applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Gender Unknown: Restore 1 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 10%. Also applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of the Turn every 2 Turns.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Anning) Fossil Lady’s Great Discovery — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Mary performs a ‘Search’ Action, the chance of it succeeding is increased by 30%. Additionally, the size of Searchable Areas are increased by 1 panel for Mary (e.g. 3x3 becomes 5x5). (Searchable Area: Sometimes, a Player will be notified when they enter an area with a hidden object which can be ‘Searched’ for. This area is increased for Mary.)  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 3 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%; if this Skill was used while Mary is within a ‘Searchable Area’, apply as ‘Crit Chance is increased by 15%’ instead. Then, Mary’s Health is reduced by 7% of its maximum value (cannot drop below 1 in this way). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
