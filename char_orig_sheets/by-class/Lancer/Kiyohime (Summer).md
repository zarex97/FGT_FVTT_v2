(Passive) All Total Water Damage received is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Kiyolancer) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Kiyohime’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 30 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 50%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Kiyohime’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Kiyohime can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Kiyolancer) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kiyolancer) Blazing Summer Passion — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Kiyohime. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%.  
2\. Inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns.  
3\. Kiyohime’s NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns for every enemy Unit inflicted with Burn.  
4\. Applies the ‘Passion’ buff for 1◈ Turns to herself, Normal Attacks inflict Severe Burn for 2◈ Turns.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Passive) While Kiyohime has the Atk Up (Bathing) buff, damage dealt by ‘Breath of Love’ is increased by 20% and Burn is applied by an additional 1◈ Turns.
