(JaguarMan) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(JaguarMan) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: E  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Jaguarman’s Master loses 5 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 5 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Jaguarman’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Jaguarman can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and cannot be removed except by its own effect/duration; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(JaguarMan) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the Passive effect of Jaguar Punch is halved (35% chance of Evading the NP). This effect is negated if Jaguarman is inflicted with Skill Seal.

4\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Instinct’.

(JaguarMan) Jaguar in the Black: In the Forest’s Darkness Lurks the Jaguar — Rank: B (NP) \[Barrier\]  
(Non-damaging) This NP can only be used during a Night Round. When this NP is used, Jaguar Man gains the ‘Blackness’ effect for 2◈ Turns or until the Night Round changes to a Day Round- the 5x5 panel area around Jaguar Man becomes a ‘Blackness’ area.  
As long as Jaguar Man is within a Forest area, its effects remain even if the Night Round changes to a Day Round.  
If Jaguar Man used this NP while she is within a ‘Forest’ area, then the size of the ‘Black’ area created from the ‘Blackness’ effect is 7x7 panels instead of 5x5.  
All allied Units gain the following effects while within a ‘Blackness’ area-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP.  
2\. Evade rolls are reduced by 2\. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks.  
3\. Evade rolls of enemy Units Attacked by Jaguar Man or her allied Units while affected by this NP are increased by 2\.  
4\. Applies the effects of ‘Presence Concealment — Rank: B’ until an allied Unit within the ‘Blackness’ area either Attacks or is Discovered by an enemy Unit; when this happens, the Presence Concealment effect on all affected allied Units is no longer in effect towards that enemy Unit.   
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
(Presence Concealment — Rank: B)  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current Agility is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt ~~to Servants~~ is increased by 100% (doubled); if NP, 50%. Does not apply against Masters.  
~~5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.~~  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Note: The ‘Blackness’ area is treated as ‘Darkness’ Terrain but does not have effect (iii).
