(Fionn) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Fionn) Divinity — Rank: D  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 20 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Fionn) Clairvoyance (Beauty) — Rank: B++  
(Passive) Has two effects-  
1\. When Fionn performs a Normal Attack, the DU rolls a ten-sided die. The DU has an 80% chance of using Evade- when Evading. Otherwise, the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
2\. When an allied Unit within a 3x3 panel area of Fionn uses a Normal Attack, the DU rolls a ten-sided die. The DU has a 40% chance of using Evade- when Evading. Otherwise, the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Crit Up to himself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 40%; and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all other allied Units directly next to Fionn, Crit Chance is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.   

(Fionn) Beauty of Trouble with Women — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Fionn enters Combat with a Female Unit, he has an 80% chance of being inflicted with Charm for 1◈ Turns at the end of Combat Phase.

(Fionn) Magnificent Free Spirit — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has several effects when used-  
1\. Applies Dodge (Cumhaill) for 1◈ Turns, Dodge (Cumhaill) only has an 80% chance of activating when Attacked, otherwise Combat proceeds as normal. Note: If Dodge (Cumhaill) fails to activate, Fionn can still perform a normal Evade roll.  
2\. Applies Decoy for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Reduce NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

Note: Dodge (Cumhaill) is separate from the normal Dodge buff. If Fionn has both Dodge (Cumhaill) and the normal Dodge buff, then the normal Dodge buff takes priority.

(Fionn) Magecraft — Rank: B  
(Active) Cannot be used while inflicted with Silence. Can be used in 2 different methods-  
1\. Used at the start of a Combat Phase when performing a Normal Attack- the Attack deals 40% extra damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
2\. Used together with Mac an Luin. Total damage dealt by Mac an Luin in this Combat Process is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Method 1 cannot be used if Method 2 is on Cooldown and vice versa.  
(Passive) Whenever this Skill is used, Fionn restores 1d4 Luck before the Method effect activates.

(Fionn) Mac an Luin: Undefeated Violet Flower — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive) Fionn’s chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
(Active) {R} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x5 or 5x1 panel area in any direction (if diagonal, 1x4 or 4x1 panel area) for 4x damage plus 100\. Water damage. Then, applies Atk Dwn on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%; and applies nvDebuff Immune to himself for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Fionn) Fintan Finegas: Biting the Thumb Swelling with Wisdom — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Wisdom\]  
(Non-damaging) Fionn’s thumb which has become swollen from the drop of boiling oil from the fat of the Salmon of Knowledge. Allows the use of Uisce Beatha: Life Scooped with These Hands. (Or you could, you know, slap an enemy with the Salmon I guess).

(Fionn) Uisce Beatha: Life Scooped with These Hands — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used on himself or on an allied Unit directly next to Fionn. Restores their Health by 30% of its maximum value, restores 3 Agility, and removes all volatile debuffs from the Unit. This NP does not reduce his Master’s Health as a cost when used. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
