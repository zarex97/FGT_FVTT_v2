(Inshun) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Inshun) Knowledge of Respect and Harmony — Rank: B+  
(Passive) Inshun’s Normal Attacks have the Aim effect. This effect is negated if Inshun is affected with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Process where Inshun is the AU. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15% and applies Over Crit for ⅓◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Inshun) Seeking the Truth of Martial Arts — Rank: B

(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with non-volatile debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever a Passive effect of Sen no Sen is successfully activated, restore 1 Agility.

(Inshun) The Art of the Circle of Transmigration — Rank: A

(Inshun) Sen no Sen — Rank: B+

(Passive 2\) During a Combat Phase, if Inshun performs a Block after performing a successful Normal Attack during the previous Combat Process, he rolls 2 extra dice for his Block.
