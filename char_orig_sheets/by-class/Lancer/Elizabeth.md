(Elizabeth) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Elizabeth) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Elizabeth) Battle Continuation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+15; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 4d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Elizabeth) Hot-Blooded Encore — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈+⅓◈ Turns, when this Unit is defeated it is revived with 20% of its Max Health.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Atk Up (Encore) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. If Elizabeth is defeated and revived while she has Atk Up (Encore), its magnitude is doubled; this effect can only activate once per instance of Atk Up (Encore).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. 

(Elizabeth) Sadistic Charisma — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other Female allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 25 including NP; this effect is negated if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal. This Passive has no effect on Units with Atk Up (Sadistic).   
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Sadistic) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%. This effect is applied twice to all affected Female allied Units excluding herself. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Elizabeth) Torture Techniques — Rank: A  
(Passive) All STR damage dealt to Female Units is increased by 40\.

(Elizabeth) Night at Čachtice — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits all enemy Units in a 3x3 panel area directly next to herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
2\. Remove 1 buff.  
3\. Inflicts Skill Seal for ⅓◈ Turns.  
4\. If used on a Female Unit, the Cooldown of this Skill is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Elizabeth) Kilenc Sárkány: Dragon Cry Thundering Voice — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{R} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=4. Deals 2.5x damage, then removes 2 random buffs from the DU, and inflicts Stun for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used if inflicted with Silence.

(Elizabeth) Báthory Erzsébet: Fresh Blood Demoness — Rank: E+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
{R} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x4 or 4x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Elizabeth for 2x damage plus 100 that has the Ignore Def effect. Then, inflicts Curse on all affected Units and inflicts Buff ChDown for 1◈ Turns, chance of applying buffs is reduced by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈+⅓◈ Turns. Cannot be used if inflicted with Silence.

Note: Kilenc Sárkány cannot be used if Báthory Erzsébet is on Cooldown and vice versa.

(Elizabeth) Idol’s Grand Encore  
(Trigger) When Elizabeth is defeated, she has a 0% chance of reviving and transforming into Perfect Elizabeth, in this case her Health & Agility will be restored to half of their new values, while Luck is fully restored to its new value. Also, she gains Particles equal to the success chance of this Skill. The chance of this Skill activating increases as follows-  
1\. For every 5 full Rounds (3 full Rounds?) which passes: \+1%  
2\. For every non-Civilian/Master/mob Unit she successfully defeats: \+1%  
3\. For every time she is defeated and revived with a Skill: \+1%  
Note: This Skill has the lowest priority out of all revival effects. Basically, only perform the trigger check for this Skill when Elizabeth is about to be truly defeated.  
Note 2: If both normal Elizabeth and Perfect Elizabeth are summoned on the field, then this Skill has no effect.
