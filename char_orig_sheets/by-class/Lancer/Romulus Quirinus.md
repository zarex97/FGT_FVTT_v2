(RomulusQ) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all Units within a 2 panel area of Romulus. Has the following effects-

3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all affected allied Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(RomulusQ) Per Aspera Ad Astra: Our Arms Cut a Path Across the Universe — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Star\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits all enemy Units within a 7x7 panel area around Romulus. If the region of the Holy Grail War is Rome, the NP area of this NP is increased from 7x7 panels to 9x9 panels. This NP has the following effects-  
1\. Deals 5x damage.  
2\. Damage dealt is further increased by 100% to Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute.  
3\. Damage dealt is **further** increased by 20% for every Roma Stack on the DU.  
4\. Inflicts a Roma Stack for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns to all affected enemy units.  
5\. Applies a Roma Stack for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns to all allied Units within the NP area.  
6\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within the NP area, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
