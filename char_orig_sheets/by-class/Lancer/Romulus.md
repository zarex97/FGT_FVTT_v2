(Romulus) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Romulus) Natural Body — Rank: C  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted with Offensive Debuffs is reduced by 40%.

(Romulus) Wind of Romance — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Off.Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Offensive Debuffs is (further) reduced by 40%.  
2\. Restores Romulus’ Health by 40% of its maximum value.  
3\. Reduces his NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Romulus) Imperial Privilege — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores his Health by 30% of its maximum value.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 45%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 45%; if NP, 25%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Romulus) Septem Colles — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Romulus. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, the Unit is revived with 10% of its Max Health when defeated. If the Unit has the ‘Roma’ Attribute, Guts is instead applied for 2◈ Turns, and the Unit is instead revived with 20% of its Max Health and Agility when defeated.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. If the Unit has the ‘Roma’ Attribute, Atk Up is instead applied for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target Unit, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
4\. Applies Buff ChUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target Unit, chance of applying buffs is increased by 40%.  
5\. Applies Heal Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target Unit, when an affected Unit has its Health restored, the amount restored is increased by 80%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Romulus) Magna Voluisse Magnum: All Things Lead to My Spear — Rank: A++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=4. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 4x damage plus 200; then applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%. If the region of the Holy Grail War is Rome, the NP area of this NP is increased from 3x3 panels to 5x5 panels. Cooldown: 7◈+⅔◈ Turns.

(Romulus) Moles Necessrie: My Love Reaches All — Rank: B (NP) \[Bounded Field/Barrier\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects the 7x7 panel area around Romulus upon Activation. This NP Area is now termed as the ‘Ramparts’. Has the following effects-  
1\. All damage received by allied Units within the Ramparts is reduced by 25% including NP.  
2\. All damage dealt by allied Units within the Ramparts is increased by 25% including NP.  
3\. Enemy Units outside the Ramparts cannot enter it. Enemy Units inside the Ramparts can attempt to Escape when standing on a panel at the border of the Ramparts if their STR Rank is B or higher; roll a ten-sided die. If an 8 to 10 is rolled, the Unit has Successfully Escaped.  
4\. Units within the Ramparts cannot be targeted by Attacks from outside the Ramparts (but Units within the Ramparts can Attack Units outside the Ramparts). However, the Ramparts can be Attacked with an AoE Noble Phantasm. In this case, all Servants within the Ramparts receive damage from the NP, but its Total Damage is reduced by 75%; while Masters are undamaged and unaffected by other effects.  
Note: If the NP used on the Ramparts was an \[Anti-Fortress\] or higher Noble Phantasm, Moles Necessrie is forcefully ended after the Units within receive damage and effects.  
5\. When this NP is activated, all enemy Units standing on a panel directly next to the border of the NP area receive 1.5x damage (Romulus’ Base Attack (STR) is used) and are knocked back by 1 panel.  
6\. If the region of the Holy Grail War is Rome, the NP area of this NP is increased from 7x7 panels to 9x9 panels. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
Note: Affected by effects that affect \[Anti-Fortress\] Noble Phantasms.

Note: All Units from the Region of ‘Rome’ have the ‘Roma’ Attribute. All Units inflicted/affected/who have Roma Stacks have the ‘Roma’ Attribute. Roma Stacks are categorized as a debuff when applied to enemy Units and are categorized as a buff when applied to allied Units.
