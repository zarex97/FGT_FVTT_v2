(Santa Lily) Holy Night  
(Passive) All damage dealt during a Night Round as dictated by the game state is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.

(Santa Lily) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Santa Lily) Saint’s Gift — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Heals its Health by 30% of its maximum value, then applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(Santa Lily) Self-Upheaval — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; then applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Santa Lily) Ephemeral Dream — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Santa Lily’s Health by 100 (cannot fall below 1 in this way). Then, applies Invuln for ⅔◈ Turns; and applies Atk Up for 1 time, all damage dealt is increased by 65%; if NP, 45%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(Santa Lily) La Grâce Fille Noël: Sing Elegantly, For The Christmas — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Santa Lily for 5x damage and inflicts Debuff ResDown for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 10%. The NP area becomes a ‘Snowfield’ area for 3◈ Turns. Then, the following effects are applied to all allied Units within a 2 panel range of Santa Lily-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Heal Up for 1◈ Turns; for the next 1◈ Turns, whenever affected Units have their Health restored, the amount of Health restored is increased by 60% of the amount restored.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
