(Leonidas) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Leonidas) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Leonidas) Defiance of Three Hundred — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the Guts (Defiance) buff for 2◈ Turns, 1 time, Leonidas is revived with 45% of his Health when he is defeated (this Guts buff stacks with other normal Guts buffs); and apply the ‘Sparta’ buff for 1◈ Turns- Whenever Leonidas is revived with an effect, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Leonidas) Pride of the Rearguard — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Decoy to self for 1◈ Turns  
2\. Applies NP Regen (Rearguard) for 1◈ Turns to himself, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn, and is also reduced by 1 Turn whenever Leonidas is hit by an Attack.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
4\. Applies Atk Up (Rearguard) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself-  
a. If Health is greater than 60% of its maximum value, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
b. If Health is between 30% to 60% of its maximum value, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
c. If Health is less than 30% of its maximum value, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
5\. Applies Crit DmUp (Rearguard) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by either a) 30%, b) 40%, or c) 50%, following the same conditions as Atk Up (Rearguard).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Leonidas) Warrior’s Warcry — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Leonidas. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 35%; if NP, 25%. Also applies the ‘Warcry’ buff to himself for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 40 including NP. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Leonidas) Thermopylae Enomotia: Guardian of the Flame Gates — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Can be used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase where Leonidas or an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Leonidas is Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Decoy to himself for 3◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Def Up to himself for 3◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself for ⅓◈ Turns. Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
If this NP is used when Leonidas or an allied Unit within the aforementioned area is Attacked, that Attack’s target is changed to Leonidas even if he is out of Range (~~the AU is forced to Move until Leonidas is within Range~~ Leonidas is forcibly Moved until he is within Range of the Attack). If this NP is used in response to an AoE Attack/NP, only Leonidas receives damage from that Attack.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
