(Parvati) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Parvati) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Parvati) Imaginary Around — Rank: EX  
(Passive) At the end of each Round, Parvati applies one of the two effects below to herself, as follows-  
1\. Restores 3 Agility.  
2\. Reduce NP Cooldown by ~~1◈~~ ⅓◈ Turns (in addition to normal Cooldown).  
This effect is negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. The Passive effect of this Skill does not activate at the end of the Round this Skill is used in this way. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Parvati) Ashes of Kama — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
4\. Applies Debuff ResUp for ⅔◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 100%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Parvati) Blessing of the Goddess — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area. First, increase Parvati’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Then, apply the following effects-  
1\. Restore the Unit’s Health by 25% of its maximum value.  
2\. Reduce the Unit’s NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Parvati) Trishula Shakti: Knowing What is Love, and Refusing to Love; That is Antipathy — Rank: EX \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=2. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 5x damage with a 60% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns; then inflicts Shock for 2◈ Turns. Lightning damage. After that, reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Parvati by 1◈ Turns. Does not damage or Charm herself if she is within the NP area. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
