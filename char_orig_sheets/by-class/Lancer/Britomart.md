(Britomart) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Britomart) Trial of the Faerie Knight  
(Trigger) Britomart can attempt to complete the Trial of the Faerie Knight. To complete this Trial, Britomart has to enter an enemy Home Base, defeat a non-Master/non-mob enemy Unit while both of them are within that enemy Unit’s Home Base, then return to her own Home Base and end her Turn. When the Trial is completed, Britomart automatically transforms into Faerie Knight Mode. She gains the following effects while in Faerie Knight Mode-  
1\. Britomart can no longer change into Mail Mode.  
2\. Negate Effect 3 of not being in Mail Mode.  
3\. The effects of Fortress Angela are treated as if she was in Mail Mode.  
4\. The magnitude of some effects are upgraded to the effects in this colour.  
5\. Windrai and Wind Wyrst can be used.  
6\. Penetrate Bladud inflicts both Def Dwn (B) and Def Dwn (C).  
(Note: This is completely optional)
