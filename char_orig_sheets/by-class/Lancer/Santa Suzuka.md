(Santa Gozen) Holy Night  
(Passive) All damage dealt during a Night Round as dictated by the game state is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.

(Santa Gozen) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Santa Gozen) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Santa Gozen) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Santa Gozen) Jintsūriki — Rank: B  
(Active) Supernatural Power. Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%; and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 35%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Santa Gozen) Love Confiserie — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Suzuka. Inflicts MOV Dwn for 1◈ Turns, MOV is decreased by 2; and inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Santa Gozen) Holy Night’s Blessings — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen (Seiya) for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Applies Aim (Seiya) for ⅓◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp (Seiya) for ⅓◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 10%.  
The duration of ‘Seiya’ buffs are not reduced during a ‘Night’ Round as dictated by the game state (not from ‘Night’ created by effects such as ‘Darkness’ Terrain).   
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Santa Gozen) Sanzenrensensekai — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Three Thousand Worlds Dyed in Love. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=3. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 2◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units, Crit Chance is reduced by 15%.  
2\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 2◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Increases the NP Cooldown on all affected enemy Units by 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Crit Up for 2◈ Turns to all affected allied Units, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
5\. Applies MOV Up for 2◈ Turns to all affected allied Units, MOV is increased by 1\.  
6\. Applies Atk Up for 2◈ Turns on all affected allied Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
7\. Reduces the NP Cooldown of all affected allied Units by 2◈ Turns, does not affect Sanzenrensensekai?.  
If this NP is used during a ‘Night’ Round as dictated by the game state (not from ‘Night’ created by effects such as ‘Darkness’ Terrain), then all buffs & debuffs applied by this NP become ‘Seiya’ buffs & debuffs (refer to ‘Holy Night’s Blessings’).  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
