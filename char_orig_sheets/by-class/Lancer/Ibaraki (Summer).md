(Ibaraki) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Ibaraki’s Master loses 10 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 10 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 20% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Ibaraki’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Ibaraki can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and cannot be removed except by its own effect/duration; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.
