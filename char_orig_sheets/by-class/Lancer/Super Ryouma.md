(Ryouma) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ryouma) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Ryouma) Dragon of the Reformation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of themselves. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Oryou) White Orochi of Takachiho — Rank: A-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns (2◈ Turns)  
2\. Applies Debuff Removal ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of having buffs removed from themselves is reduced by 75% (100%)  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns (also affects Instakill).  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Ryouma) Amasakahoko (Dual) — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies Divine DmgUp for 1◈ Turns, all Divine damage dealt is increased by 20% including NP.  
4\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of themself is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ryouma) Divine Sealing Spear  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x3 or 3x1 panel area in any direction next to himself (if diagonal, 1x2 or 2x1). Deals Divine damage. Then, all DUs with the ‘Divine’ Attribute Flip a Coin. If Heads, it is inflicted with Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns; if Tails, it is inflicted with NP Seal for 1◈ Turns. Not affected by Debuff Resist effects. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns (2◈ Turns if no debuffs were inflicted).

(Oryou) Breath of Takachiho  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 50% extra damage and inflicts Poison. Fire damage. Can be used immediately after Ryouma performs a successful Normal Attack, but this Attack Skill deals normal damage (instead of 50% extra) if used in this way; also the DU cannot Evade Breath of Takachiho if used in this way, even with a Luck Check, while the DU also has to Block Breath of Takachiho if it Blocked the previous Normal Attack. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Ryouma) Ryuu yo, Wadatsumi no Hara wo Yuke: My Dear, Conquer the Fields of Wadatsumi — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Range+4 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (STR) is used. First, applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. Also applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30% (40% if Ryouma is within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area). Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 5x damage and increase their NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Then, Ryouma enters Wadatsumi Mode. If Ryouma or any of the DUs are within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area, this NP deals Water damage (half). Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Ryouma) Ama-no-Sakahoko: Heavenly Reversed Spear — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Ryouma gains the following effects when in Wadatsumi Mode-  
1\. Range is increased by 1\.  
2\. At a Range of 1 to 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and 50% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 125+75=200), not affected by Magic Resistance. Deals Divine damage (half) and have a 25% chance of inflicting Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns on Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute.  
3\.  Normal Attacks at a Range of 3 or higher use Base Attack (MAG), deals Divine damage and have a 25% chance of inflicting NP Seal for 1◈ Turns on Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute.  
4\. All damage taken is reduced by 50 including NP.  
5\. The effect of ‘White Orochi of Takachiho’ is upgraded to the effects in this colour.  
6\. While Ryouma is in Wadatsumi Mode, their Master loses 20 Health at the end of their Turn and at the end of any Turn they Acts; if their Master’s Health is less than 20, Wadatsumi Mode is forcefully ended. Ryouma can choose to end Wadatsumi Mode during their Turn, at the end of any Turn they Act (after their Master pays the Health cost), or at the end of a Round.
