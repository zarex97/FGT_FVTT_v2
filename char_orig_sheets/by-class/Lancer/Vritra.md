(Vritra) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Vritra) Dragonkind — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP.  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken is reduced by 25; if NP, 50\.

(Vritra) The All-Penetrating Vajra — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies N.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Normal Attacks is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Vritra) Destined Enemy of the Gods — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is increased by 30\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Dmg Up (Divine) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is increased by 30%; and reduce Vritra’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Vritra) Eternal Immortal Dragon — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts to herself for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Vritra is defeated she is revived with 20% of her Max Health.  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, 3 times, to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
4\. Applies Dmg Up \[Sky\] for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Vritra) Confined Boundary  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Can be used on any Unit. The ‘Interference’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
