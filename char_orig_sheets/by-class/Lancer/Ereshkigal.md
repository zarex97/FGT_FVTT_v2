(Eresh) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Eresh) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (5d12+5) including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+22) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Eresh) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Eresh) Hidden Great Crown — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn and at the start of a Combat Phase. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Has an 80% chance of applying Debuff Immune for ⅓◈ Turns, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
3\. Has an 80% chance of applying Instakill Immune (Eresh) for ⅓◈ Turns, this effect is halved for Death (i.e. 40% chance of Immunity if inflicted with Death).  
4\. Has an 80% chance of applying Buff Removal ResUp for ⅓◈ Turns, chance of having buffs removed from the Unit is reduced by 100%.  
5\. Skill Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.   
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive) If Ereshkigal has the ‘Kur’ buff, the chances of applying Effect 2, 3, and 4 are 100% instead of 80%.

(Eresh) Mana Burst (Cage) — Rank: A+  
(Passive) At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Active) This Skill is used when Ereshkigal performs a Normal Attack. First, reduce Eresh’s NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns. For that Combat Process, Eresh’s Base Attack used is her BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=325). Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Eresh) Protection of the Underworld — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Kur’ buff for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Ereshkigal. The effects of the ‘Kur’ buff are as follows- When ‘Kur Kigal Igalla’ is used, the ‘Kur’ buff is removed from all Units within a 3 panel area of Ereshkigal, then the following effects are applied to them-  
1\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 20% of its value.  
If the ‘Kur’ buff naturally expires on an affected Unit (i.e. its duration runs out), it has a 50% chance of applying each respective effect to the affected Unit.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Eresh) Kur Kigal Irkalla: The Bellows of Kur that Tramples upon Ekur — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Mountain\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Eresh, this area becomes an ‘Underworld’ area for 3◈ Turns when this NP is used. First, applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%. Deals 4x damage plus 100, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the \[Earth\] Attribute.  
After that, apply the following effects to all Units with the ‘Kur’ buff on the field-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit Guard for 1◈ Turns, when this Unit is Attacked, the Crit Chance of the AU is reduced by 20%.  
3\. Applies Instakill Immune (Eresh) for 1◈ Turns, this effect is halved for Death (i.e. 50% chance of Immunity if inflicted with Death).  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

Note: The effect of the ‘Kur’ buff triggers after Kur Kigal Irkalla applies its effects to those Units.
