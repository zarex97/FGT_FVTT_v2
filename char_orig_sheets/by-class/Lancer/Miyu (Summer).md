(Miyu) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Miyu) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Miyu) Territory Creation (Inshū) — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) The 3 panel area around Miyu is known as the Milk Zone.  
(Passive 2\) All damage dealt is increased by 5% while Miyu is in the Milk Zone.  
(Passive 3\) For every Unit with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute within the Milk Zone, Normal Attacks use an additional 10% (5%?) of Base Attack (MAG) for damage calculation, up to 100% (70%?) (e.g. 3 Units with ‘Ilya’ Attribute within the Milk Zone, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and 30% of Base Attack (MAG) combined); not affected by Magic Resistance.  
(Passive 4\) If there is a Unit with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute within the Milk Zone, Crit Damage dealt increases by 10%.

(Miyu) Infinite Mana Supply — Rank: A  
(Passive) At the end of every Turn Miyu Acts, her NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns. Has no effect if Miyu is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Miyu) Playing Alone — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Agility and 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 3 times; chance of being inflicted with Instakill is reduced by 50%, while chance of being inflicted with Death is reduced by 25%. Does not affect Erase.  
4\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value at the end of this Unit’s Turn, at the end of any Turn it Acts or at the end of the Round.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Miyu) Inshūmura no Miko — Rank: A+  
(Active) Shrine Maiden of the Ancient Customs Village. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. NP Cooldown is further reduced by ⅓◈ Turns for every Unit with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute within the Milk Zone.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within the Milk Zone, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
4\. Applies Buff Removal ResUp for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within the Milk Zone, chance of having buffs removed from this Unit is reduced by 100%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive) When there are 5 or more Units with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute within the Milk Zone, Miyu enters Milk Mode, gaining the following effects-  
1\. The size of the Milk Zone increases by 1 panel.  
2\. The Milk Zone is now a ‘Waterside’ area.  
3\. Normal Attacks deal 10d10 bonus Water damage.  
4\. The Range of Milky Beam increases by 1 and now hits a 3x3 panel area within Range.  
5\. Milk-Coloured Dream with You now hits a 5x5 panel area instead.  
6\. Miyu’s Alignment changes to Lawful Evil.  
At the end of the Turn, if there are less than 5 Units with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute within the Milk Zone, Miyu leaves Milk Mode.

(Miyu) Ilyaism — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
2\. Applies the ‘Ilyalove’ buff for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step when performing a successful Attack against a Unit with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute.  
3\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Ilyafied’ effect for 2◈ Turns to all other Units within the Milk Zone. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. If the ‘Ilyafied’ is applied to a Unit who already has it, its duration is extended instead (does not stack). A Unit with the ‘Ilyafied’ effect has the ‘Ilya’ Attribute.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

Note: Units named Ilyasviel have the ‘Ilya’ Attribute.

(Miyu) Fairy Whisper  
Damage Spell. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Deals 50% damage and has a 50% chance of applying the ‘Ilyafied’ effect for 1◈ Turns. If Evaded, the DU’s Evade Roll is increased by 3\. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.

(Miyu) Sakatsuki Shine  
Damage Spell. Hits all enemy Units directly next to Miyu for 70% damage. Cannot be Countered. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.

(Miyu) Milky Beam  
Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals normal damage and has the following effects-  
1\. The DU Flips a Coin. If Heads, inflict Slow for 1◈ Turns. If Tails, inflict Silence for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the ‘Ilyafied’ effect for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Miyu) Milk-Coloured Dream with You: Sakatsuki of the Milky Way which Pierces the Sun — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
{S} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+3 for the Combat Process. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. First, applies Dmg Up (Evil) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment is increased by 50%. Deals 5x damage; but does not deal damage to herself or allied Units within the NP area who have the ‘Ilya’ Attribute. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within the Milk Zone by ½◈ Turns.  
2\. Further reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within the Milk Zone with the ‘Ilya’ Attribute by ½◈ Turns.  
3\. Miyu gains the ‘Inshū’ effect for 2◈ Turns. While Miyu has the ‘Inshū’ effect, she enters Milk Mode even if the conditions are unmet. The duration of the ‘Inshū’ effect is paused as long as Miyu meets the original conditions for being in Milk Mode. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. If this effect is applied to her while she already has it, reset its duration instead.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
