(Melusine) Fairy Knight  
(Passive) When Melusine is summoned, she is summoned as Fairy Knight Lancelot and is named as such. As long as she is named ‘Fairy Knight Lancelot’, she gains the ‘Round Table’ Attribute, the ‘Eternal Arms Mastership’ Skill, and the following effects-  
1\. MOV is increased by 3\.  
2\. Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, maximum distance that can be Moved on both times combined is equal to the Unit’s MOV before being increased by Effect 1.  
Note: Basically, Fairy Knight Lancelot’s MOV is increased by 3 on Turns where she does not use Double Move. So if she has already Moved more than 6 panels during her Turn, she cannot use Double Move.  
3\. Gains the ‘Levitating’ Attribute. When Fairy Knight Lancelot Moves, she can ignore all obstacles in her path (but still cannot enter the area around an enemy Master if…see Master-Servant Relationships).  
4\. Evade rolls of Units Attacked by Fairy Knight Lancelot are increased by 1d6, while her Evade rolls are reduced by 1d6 when Attacked. This effect is negated if the opposing Unit has the ‘Levitating’ Attribute.

(Melusine) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Melusine) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Melusine) Dragon Heart — Rank: B

1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. ~~5◈ Turns (6◈ Turns?) if used with its ‘Mana Burst’ effect.~~

(Melusine) Ray Horizon — Rank: A  
(Active 1\) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used while Melusine is ‘Fairy Knight Lancelot’, she loses the ‘Fairy Knight Lancelot’ name and transforms into her ‘Albion Mode’. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 6◈ Turns. (Note: The remaining Cooldown of Innocence Arondight is transferred to Hollow Heart Albion when Melusine transforms, then reduced.)  
2\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active 2\) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects when Melusine is already in Albion Mode-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
2\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. This Skill can no longer be used after Albion Mode is deactivated.

(Melusine) Corpse of the White Dragon  
(Passive) Melusine gains the following effects while in Albion Mode-  
1\. Range is increased by 1\.  
2\. Her Master’s ZON is increased by 2\.  
3\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 2 or higher use Base Attack (STR) and 50% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 100+105=205), the effect of Magic Resistance is halved.  
4\. At the end of any Turn Melusine Acts while in Albion Mode, her Master loses 20 Health. When this effect is triggered while her Master has 20 Health or less, Albion Mode is forcefully ended instead; while her Master does not lose Health from this effect.  
5\. Albion Mode can be deactivated during her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round. When deactivated, Melusine does not return to being Fairy Knight Lancelot, she returns to just normal Melusine.  
6\. Has the ‘Spaceflight’ Attribute.  
7\. While Melusine is just normal Melusine and not Fairy Knight Lancelot, her Master can spend one Command Spell to activate Melusine’s ‘Ray Horizon’ Skill with its first effect and transform her into Albion Mode.

Then, deals 5x damage and inflicts Burn for 3◈ Turns. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
