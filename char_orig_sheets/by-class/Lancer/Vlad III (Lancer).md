If Vlad III is summoned, the player who summoned him Flips a Coin. If Heads, Vlad III (Lancer) is summoned. If Tails, Vlad III (Berserker) is summoned. (When playing on Normal HGW or Ainsworth HGW, do not Flip a Coin if Berserker has already been summoned.) Note: Does not apply to certain scenarios (e.g. Grand Order Campaign, choose between Lancer or Berserker class before deploying).

(Vlad) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Vlad) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Vlad) Shapeshift — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Vlad is Attacked. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is further reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Vlad) Demonic Defender of the State — Rank: EX  
(Passive) When Vlad III (Lancer) is summoned, the player with Vlad marks out a 9x9 panel area on the half of the board nearer to or around Vlad’s Home Base. This area cannot intersect the Home Base of other Players. This area will be known as ‘Wallachia’. Has the following effects-  
1\. As long as Vlad is within Wallachia, all damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.   
2\. As long as Vlad is within Wallachia, all STR damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; if MAG damage, 50%.  
3\. Vlad’s Sustainability does not decrease as long as he is within Wallachia, and his Master’s ZON is ignored.  
4\. Wallachia functions as a second Home Base for Vlad only. On panels where his actual Home Base and Wallachia overlap, the end-of-the-Round healing does not stack.  
Note: If Wallachia would intersect the Home Base of another Player no matter where it is placed, then the shape of the Wallachia area is reduced to exclude panels which would intersect the Home Base of another Player.

(Vlad) Kazıklı Bey: The Lord of Execution — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive) Whenever Vlad successfully deals damage to a Unit with a Normal Attack, apply one ‘Kazıklı’ stack to the DU at the end of the Damage Step. ‘Kazıklı’ stacks are categorized as a debuff, the chance of a ‘Kazıklı’ stack being removed is reduced by 50%.  
(Active 1\) Used during your Turn. This Noble Phantasm can only be used within Wallachia. When this Noble Phantasm is used, its becomes Activated for ⅔◈ Turns and has the following effects-  
While Kazıklı Bey is Activated, at the end of every Turn Vlad Acts and at the end of the Round, all enemy Units within the NP area roll a six-sided die respectively. They will then respectively receive X STR damage, X being the number rolled \* 100, if the NP is not Evaded. Damaged Units will then be inflicted with Curse. This NP is able to damage Masters even if their respective Servant is within a 2 panel area of them. Damage from this NP has the Ignore Def effect and also ignores Block, but Units with the Invuln and Anti-Purge buffs will not take damage.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
(Active 2\) Used during your Turn, or at the start or end of a Combat Process involving Vlad. Affects all Units with ‘Kazıklı’ stacks within a 3 panel area of himself. Those Units receive Y damage, where Y being 50 \* the number of ‘Kazıklı’ stacks on each Unit respectively; then inflict Curse to the affected Units, and remove all ‘Kazıklı’ stacks from the affected Units. This damage has the Pierce and Ignore Def effects. Counts as Vlad’s Attack for the Turn if used during his Turn.  
If this NP is used within the Wallachia, Y is 100 \* the number of ‘Kazıklı’ stacks on the respective affected Units who are also within Wallachia (those outside of it use the original calculation instead, if any).  
If this NP is used at the start of a Combat Process where Vlad is receiving the Attack; the AU’s Attack is cancelled, then Vlad can Counter if the enemy Unit is within Range.  
If this NP is used at the end of a Combat Process where Vlad performed the Attack; the DU cannot Counter Vlad’s Attack.  
Can only be used once per Round (Turn?). Does not reduce his Master’s Health as a cost when used.  
Note: Damage from both (Active 1\) and (Active 2\) of this NP are not affected by any damage-modifying effects on Vlad.

(Vlad) Legend of Dracula: The Succession of Blood — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit(Self)\]  
(Activation/Passive) In order to use this Noble Phantasm, Vlad’s Master has to use a Command Spell. However, if the Master is within Wallachia when it is activated, Vlad will immediately kill his Master after this NP is activated. Cannot be deactivated after it is used. Permanently applies the following effects to Vlad-  
1\. Vlad gains the ‘Dark (Vampire)’ Attribute.  
2\. During a Night Round, Vlad heals 100 Health and 1 Agility at the end of his Turn, at the end of any Turn he Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Each time Vlad deals damage to a Unit, he heals 100 Health and 1 Agility (no effect if no damage is dealt). Each time he defeats a Unit, his Health and Agility is restored by 25% of their maximum values. However, if he defeats a Civilian, he only heals 100 Health and 1 Agility.  
4\. The magnitude of Shapeshift is doubled.  
5\. Kazıklı Bey is unusable and has no effects. Magic Resistance is disabled.  
6\. Vlad will always Attack (and Move towards) the closest Unit, including allied Units.  
7\. Range is increased by 1 panel, and number of targets is increased by 1\. Vlad can Move to any unoccupied panel while Moving, regardless of obstacles (but still cannot enter the area around an enemy Master if…see Master-Servant Relationships).  
8\. Base Attack (STR) is increased by 75\.  
9\. Whenever Vlad performs an Evade roll, reduce the value rolled by 4\. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks.  
10\. Total Fire damage taken is increased by 25%.
