(Lapse) Ten Thousand Forms of Avarice — Rank: EX  
(Passive) All NP damage taken from NP which are lower than \[Anti-World\] is reduced by 25%.

(Lapse) Nega-Desire — Rank: A  
Constantly affects all Units on the board.  
(Passive 1\) Chance of inflicting Charm is increased by 30%.  
(Passive 2\) All damage dealt by Units inflicted by Charm is increased by 30% including NP; all damage taken by Units inflicted with Charm is reduced by 30% including NP.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever an effect that removes Charm affects a Unit inflicted with Charm, the chance of Charm being removed from that Unit is reduced by 30%.  
(Passive 4\) As long as there is a Unit inflicted with Charm on the board, Lapse’s Sustainability is increased by 3◈ Turns.

(Lapse) Overflowing Affection  
(Passive) NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of the Turn after every ⅓◈ Turns.

(Lapse) Scorn of Māra-Pāpman  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. Inflicts Def Dwn on all affected enemy Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%. Then, reduce Lapse’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lapse) Extending Arms towards Maen  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. First apply Crit DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%; and inflict Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns on the enemy Unit, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lapse) Seductive Sringara  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Lapse. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 30% chance of inflicting Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units.  
2\. Has a 30% chance of inflicting NP Seal for 1◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units.  
3\. Has a 30% chance of inflicting Charm for 2◈ Turns on all affected Units (if an allied Unit is affected by Charm from this Skill, control of it is given to Lapse).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lapse) Demon Lord’s Caress

(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Pierce for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lapse) Samsara Kama/Mara Avaruddha: Realm of Love, Burning Cosmos — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]
