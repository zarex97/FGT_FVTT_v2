(Camazotz) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, Double Move, Riding Attack, and Passenger Seat can be used during this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Camazotz) Vukub-Came  
(Passive) Seventh Death. When an Active Skill is used for the third time during the same Turn by any enemy Unit on the board (except Attack Skills and Damage Spells), this Skill automatically activates. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Death ChUp (Vukub) for 1◈ Turns to Camazotz, chance of inflicting Instakill and Death with Attacks is increased by 70%. If he already had the buff, its duration is extended.  
2\. Inflicts Buff ChDwn (Vukub) for 1◈ Turns on the three nearest enemy Units, chance of applying buffs is reduced by 70%. If an affected Unit already had this debuff, its duration is extended instead.  
The activation of this Skill cannot be prevented through any effects or methods. This Skill can only activate once per Turn.

Note: If there are more than three enemy Units which are nearest and equally far from Camazotz, the targets of the Skill are randomly chosen (e.g. four enemy Units who are all 2 panels away from Camazotz), the same Unit can only be chosen once.
