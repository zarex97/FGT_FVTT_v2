(Goetia) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: C  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Goetia) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 after calculation including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Goetia) Class Skill: Independent Manifestation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive 4\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs, Instakill, Death, and Erase is reduced by 80%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Independent Action — Rank: A’.

(Goetia) Nega Summon — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When used, activate the Passive effects of this Skill for 3◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns after the effect of this Skill ends.  
(Passive) The Passive effects of this Skill are not applied until Goetia uses its Active effect. Applies the following effects to all other Servant Units within a 3 panel area of himself-  
1\. Noble Phantasms cannot be used, even through Command Spells or any other method. The only exception to this effect is Ars Nova.  
2\. The Passive effects of all Noble Phantasms are negated.  
3\. Remove all buffs, debuffs, and other effects from all Units within the Skill area which were applied by Noble Phantasms including Unremovable effects. This also affects Goetia.  
If Goetia is inflicted with Skill Seal while this Skill is activated, it is immediately deactivated.

(Goetia) Clairvoyance — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever a Unit attempts to Evade Goetia’s Attack, the DU can only use Evade-. When Goetia Rolls for Evade, Goetia never uses Evade-, and the value of his Evade roll is reduced by 3\. The effects of this Skill is negated if Goetia is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Goetia) High Speed Incantation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, and gain 3 Goetia Tokens. Cannot be used if inflicted with Silence. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Goetia) Summoning — Rank: EX  
Enables the use of the Ars Goetia (see below). All Ars Goetia Spells have a Cooldown of 4◈ Turns. If an Ars Goetia Spell is on Cooldown, the other 3 Ars Goetia Spells cannot be used.  
(Passive) If Goetia uses an Ars Goetia Spell while he has any Goetia Tokens, remove one Goetia Token from himself, while the Ars Goetia Spell that he used does not enter Cooldown. The maximum number of Goetia Tokens Goetia can have is 3\.

(Goetia) Ars Goetia: Flauros  
Damage Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Goetia for 3x damage, then increase the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by 1◈ Turns.

(Goetia) Ars Goetia: Forneus  
Damage Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Goetia for 3x damage, then inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 50%.

(Goetia) Ars Goetia: Barbatos  
Damage Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Goetia for 3x damage, then inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 25%; if NP. 15%; and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 25%.

(Goetia) Ars Goetia: Halphas  
Damage Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Goetia for 3x damage, then inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns.

(Goetia) Ars Almadel Salomonis: The Time of Birth has Come, He is the One who Masters All — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Humanity/Anti-Unit/Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Goetia for 5x damage. Then, inflicts the following effects on the DUs-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Reduces Agility by 25% of its maximum value.  
3\. Reduces Luck by 25% of its maximum value.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
