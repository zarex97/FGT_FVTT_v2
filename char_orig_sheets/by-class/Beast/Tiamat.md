(Tiamat) Class Skill: Authority of the Beast — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Heroic Spirits (Servants) and Divine Spirits is increased by 100% including NP.

(Tiamat) Class Skill: Independent Manifestation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive 4\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs, Instakill, Death, and Erase is reduced by 70%.

(Tiamat) Self-Modification — Rank: EX  
(Passive) All damage received is reduced by 25% including Noble Phantasm.

(Tiamat) Sea of Life — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restore Tiamat’s Health by 25% of its maximum value, and reduce Tiamat’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Tiamat) Monstrous Strength — Rank: A++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, all STR damage dealt is increased by 110% after calculation; if NP, 55%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns. 

(Tiamat) Nega Genesis — Rank: A  
(Active). Affects all Units in a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Tiamat. Inflicts NP Seal on all affected Units with the \[Man\] or \[Star\] Attribute for 3◈ Turns that ignores all other buffs, debuffs, and effects on those Units. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Tiamat) Sing  
Spell. Affects all Units within a 2 panel area around Tiamat (except herself). When used, roll a four-sided die. The number rolled determines which Song Tiamat Sings (see below). If a 4 is rolled, you can choose which Song is used.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Tiamat) Sing: First Song  
1\. Inflicts Buff Dwn for 2◈ Turns, chance of having buffs applied to itself is reduced by 20%.  
2\. Inflicts Buff ChDwn for 2◈ Turns, chance of applying Buffs to Units is decreased by 20%.  
3\. Inflicts Heal Down for 2◈ Turns, healing received is reduced by 20% of the amount healed.

(Tiamat) Sing: Second Song  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 2◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 15%.  
3\. Inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns.

(Tiamat) Sing: Third Song  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 2◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 10%.  
2\. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 2◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is is reduced by 20%.  
3\. Inflicts Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns.
