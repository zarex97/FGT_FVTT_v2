(Rapture) Class Skill: Authority of the Beast — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Heroic Spirits/Servants and Divine Spirits is increased by 50% including NP.

(Rapture) Independent Manifestation — Rank: ???  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive 4\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs, Instakill, Death, and Erase is reduced by 70%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Independent Action — Rank: B’.

(Rapture) Logos Eater — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Rapture’s Attacks deal 10% extra damage for every Rank in MAG the DU has. If the DU has no MAG Rank, damage dealt is further increased by 5% for every 25 points in the Unit’s Base Attack (MAG); has no effect if the DU also has no Base Attack (MAG).  
(Passive 2\) All damage received from ‘Humanoid’ Units is reduced by 10% including NP.

(Rapture) Nega-Saviour — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All Skills (including Passive Skills) of Ruler-class and Saver-class Servant are unusable and negated within a 2 panel area of Rapture. If a Unit with buffs received from a Ruler-class or Saver-class Servant’s Skill enters a 2 panel area of Rapture, those buffs are removed.  
(Passive 2\) All damage dealt to Ruler-class and Saver-class Servants is increased by 25% including NP.

(Rapture) Ten Thousand Coloured Stagnation — Rank: EX  
(Active) Range=3. Can be used both on an allied or enemy Unit; but has no effect on BB, the Sakura Five, or Saver-class Servants. Inflicts Charm (TTCS) which ignores Debuff Resist or Debuff Immune on the Unit. Charm (TTCS) is not removed when the Unit is damaged by an Attack, it is only removed when the Unit loses 30% of its current Health from Attack damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.   
Note: Charm (TTCS) is affected by Resist or Immune effects that specify Charm. If an allied Unit is inflicted with Charm (TTCS), control of that Unit is given to Rapture.

(Rapture) Karma Phage — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Automatically used on a Unit when it is affected by Charm (TTCS). Grants the following effects to the Unit as long as it has the Charm (TTCS) debuff-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 50% including NP.  
2\. All damage received is reduced by 50% including NP.  
When a Unit is affected by Karma Phage, it is granted one of the following titles- Body, Sight, Mouth, Spice or Organ. There can only be a maximum of five Karma Phages, one of each title. Whenever a new Karma Phage is created, the magnitude of Karma Phage’s effects are reduced by 10%. Whenever a Karma Phage is defeated, the magnitude is increased back by 10%, unless it was the only remaining Karma Phage.

(Rapture) Giver of Fearlessness  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Rapture. Inflicts the ‘Fearless’ debuff for 2◈ Turns, its effects are as follows-  
When a Unit is inflicted with Fearless, the Fearless debuff starts at Stage 1, and it increases by 1 level at the end of the Turn after every ⅓◈ Turns. For each Stage of Fearless a Unit has, its chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 15%.  
When a Unit uses its NP while affected by Fearless, NP damage dealt is increased by 15% for each Stage of Fearless the Unit has; this effect only occurs the first time the Unit uses its NP while affected by an instance of Fearless. (This means the first time NP is used each time the Unit is affected by Fearless).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

When Rapture is summoned, roll a four-sided die. The number rolled determines which of the below Skills Rapture is able to use. If a 4 is rolled, your choice.  
1\. Three Roots of Kleshas  
2\. Matter is Eternal  
3\. Hisage Makyou Sessyoin  
4\. Your choice

(Rapture) Three Roots of Kleshas  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. All affected enemy Units roll a four-sided die, and are respectively inflicted with the corresponding debuff for 1◈ Turns, as follows-  
1\. First Root: All damage dealt is reduced by 50% including NP.  
2\. Second Root: The value of Evade rolls are doubled for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Third Root: The value of Luck Check rolls are doubled for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. No effect. (Roll again.)  
This Skill counts as inflicting a debuff, and is affected by Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Rapture) Matter is Eternal  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores her Health by 20% of its maximum value, then applies Heal Up to herself for 2◈ Turns, all healing received is increased by 100%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Rapture) Hisage Makyou Sessyoin  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the following effects to Rapture. However, for each Karma Phage on the field, roll a six-sided die. The number rolled determines which effect is not applied to Rapture (if a duplicate number is rolled, roll the die again).  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
4\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
5\. Applies the ‘Hisage’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of Rapture is reduced by 10%.  
6\. Applies the ‘Makyou’ buff for 1◈ Turns, natural NP Cooldown of all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of Rapture is negated.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Rapture) Utate Hana no Bishō: Beautiful Flower’s Smile — Rank: ??? (NP) \[Anti-Humanity\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Rapture. First, remove all buffs on all affected enemy Units, then deals 5x damage to all Units in the NP area with the ‘Human’ or ‘Humanoid’ Attributes; and deals 3x damage to all Units in the NP area without the ‘Human’ or ‘Humanoid’ Attributes. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
