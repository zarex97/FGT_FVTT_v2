(Gilgamesh) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Sustainability does not apply.  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.

(Gilgamesh) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to E is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 10%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Gilgamesh) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Gilgamesh) Gate of Babylon: Golden Armor  
(Passive 1\) All damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
(Passive 2\) Gilgamesh is Immune to Petrify.  
(Passive 3\) The Rank of Magic Resistance is increased from E to C.  
Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.

(Gilgamesh) Charisma — Rank: A+  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 27 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Gilgamesh) The One Who Saw It All — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allies within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Gilgamesh) Golden Rule — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Gilgamesh performs a Crit, reduce his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn. If none of his NP are on Cooldown, reduce the Cooldown of ‘Treasury of Babylon’ by 1 Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Gilgamesh) Treasury of Babylon — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Gilgamesh’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Gilgamesh) Enkidu: Chains of Heaven — Rank: ??? \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Divine\]  
Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals normal damage and has a 50% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. Damage dealt and chance of inflicting Stun increases the higher the Divinity Rank of the DU.  
Rank EX: Damage dealt increased by 150%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 100%.  
Rank A: Damage dealt increased by 100%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 50%.  
Rank B: Damage dealt increased by 80%, chance of inflicting Stun increased by 40%.
