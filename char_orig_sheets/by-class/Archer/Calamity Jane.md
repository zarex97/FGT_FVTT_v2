(Jane) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Jane) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Jane) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt ~~to Servants~~ is increased by 100% (doubled); if NP, 50%. Does not apply against Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When this Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Damage Spells.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Jane) Subversive Activities — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Jane. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%; and Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Can be used when Presence Concealment is Active. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Goddess’ Chaperone.

(Jane) Goddess’ Chaperone — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%; and inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%. Then, apply the following effects-  
1\. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
2\. Has a (3+X)0% chance of applying S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 5%; X=the number of enemy Units who were successfully debuffed by this Skill. This effect activates four times (roll separately for each activation).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Subversive Activities.

(Jane) Galaxy Messenger — Rank: EX

1\. Increase the NP Cooldown of all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Jane by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Jane by ⅓◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to Jane, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
4\. Has an 80% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jane) Show Me the Way, Polar Star — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Jane. First, apply Buff ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of applying buffs is increased by 40%. Then, the following effects are applied depending on the level of Crit Up already applied to the Unit from buffs-  
1\. Crit Up 10% to 19%: Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
2\. Crit Up 20% to 29%: Apply Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 150% (Note: Does not apply to the requirements for this Skill, this Skill only uses the total Crit Up buffs that were already on the Unit before this Skill was used).  
3\. Crit Up 30% to 39%: Apply Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns.  
4\. Crit Up 40% to 49%: Apply Pierce for 1◈ Turns.  
5\. Crit Up 50% or higher: Reduce NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jane) Space Dead Man's Hand: Black Hand that Invites Death — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=4. Base Attack (STR) is used. This NP always hits from behind the DU regardless of where Jane and/or the DU are facing. This NP has the Aim effect. First, applies the following effects to the DU-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (C) for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%, and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack.  
This NP then deals 2.5x damage. Any Luck Checks the DU performs against this NP will always fail. Then, the DU is inflicted with LUC Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Luck Check rolls are increased by 1d4. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jane) Sentinel Stellaris: Polar Star, Illuminate My Enemies — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Divine\]  
(Non-damaging) Used on a 9x9 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Jane. Affects all enemy Units in that area. Has the following effects-  
1\. Remove all Defensive Buffs from the affected Units.  
2\. All affected Units are inflicted with the ‘No Block’ debuff for 3◈ Turns, affected Units cannot use the Block action.  
3\. All affected Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute are inflicted with the ‘Polar Star’ debuff for 3◈ Turns, the effects of ‘Polar Star’ are as follows-  
a. Negates all Passive damage-reducing and Defensive effects, Skills, and NP on the Unit. If said effect/Skill/NP combines Defensive effects with other effects, only the Defensive effects are negated.  
b. Defensive Buffs cannot be applied to the Unit.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.  
Note: Defensive effects would be effects that generally have the same effect as Defensive Buffs (e.g. damage reduction).
