(Napoleon) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Napoleon) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Napoleon) Triumphant Charisma — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; this effect is applied twice to himself. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Napoleon) Fire Support (Cannon) — Rank: B+  
(Passive) Normal Attack damage dealt at a Range of 4 or higher is increased by 50%.

(Napoleon) Grande Armée — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Napoleon. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
2\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP is increased by 20%.  
3\. Applies Crit Up (Armée) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%; if a Unit other than himself with this buff is within a 2 panel area of Napoleon, Crit Chance is increased by 50% instead.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Napoleon) Light of Possibilities — Rank: B  
(Passive) Once per Turn, when Napoleon Fails any type of roll (e.g. Flip a Coin, Evade, Luck Check, etc), he can reroll that roll; if he does he must use the new result.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Pierce to himself for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for himself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Napoleon) Imperial Privilege — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores his Health by 15% of its maximum value.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 35%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 35%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Napoleon) Arc de Triomphe de l'Étoile: The Iridescent (Rain)Bow that Signals His Triumphal Return Sonorously — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x5 or 5x3 panel area directly next to Napoleon for 4x damage plus 100 that has the Ignore Def effect, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is further increased by 150%. Then applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Napoleon, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
