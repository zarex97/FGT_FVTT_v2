(Durga) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Durga) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Durga) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Durga) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Durga) Divine-Bestowed Arms — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) STR damage dealt by Normal Attacks is increased by 10%.  
(Passive 2\) Normal Attack Crits which use Base Attack (STR) deal 10% extra damage.  
(Passive 3\) Normal Attacks which use Base Attack (STR) deal 50 (100?) extra damage to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute.

(Durga) Mahayama — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. NP Cooldown is reduced by 4◈ Turns. Then, apply Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP are increased by 25%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Durga) The Unapproachable One — Rank: A  
(Passive) All Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute are unable to Move onto a panel directly next to Durga.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 3 times. Then, apply the ‘Durga’ buff for 8 times, its effects are as follows- The first Normal Attack Durga performs while she has this buff deals 10% extra damage, the next Normal Attack will deal 20% extra damage, then 30%, and so on. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.   
If Durga uses this Skill while she already has the ‘Durga’ buff, it is reset to 8 times and the damage bonus is reset to 10%.

(Durga) The Third Eye  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
2\. Apply the ‘Ajna’ buff for 1◈ Turns, at the end of a Damage Step after performing a Normal Attack, restore 1 Luck and NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn.  
3\. Apply Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns.  
4\. Apply Range Up for 1◈ Turns, Range is increased by 1 panel.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Durga) Divy Jaanavar  
Attack Skill. Can be used in two different methods as follows-  
1\. Sinha Vahini. Range=2. Deals normal damage and inflicts Disable for 1◈ Turns. Ice damage (half).  
2\. Vasuki. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=3. Deals normal damage and inflicts Silence for 1◈ Turns. Water damage.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Durga) Pāvaka Agni  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area around herself for normal damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Durga) Mahisasuramardini: O Fury, O Prayers, O Goddess Who Exterminates the Devils — Rank: EX (NP) \[Extermination\]  
Range \+2 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, apply Dmg Up \[Sky\] for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute is increased by 50%. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 5x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is further increased by 150%. Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the affected Units, all damage taken is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Durga) Alter-Ego of Affection: Kali  
(Trigger) At the end of Durga’s Turn, she has a chance of transforming into Kali. The chance of this occurring is as follows-  
1\. For every 2 full Rounds which passes: \+5%   
2\. For every Servant that has been defeated: \+10%  
3\. For every Command Spell her Master is missing: \+10%  
4\. If the Region is India: \+10%  
5\. While she has the ‘Ajna’ buff: \+10%  
When Durga transforms into Kali, her Master’s current Health is halved, the Master loses 1 Command Spell, and Kali’s Stats are restored to their maximum values, but Range is reduced by 1\. Additionally, Kali is unable to use Divy Jaanavar, Pāvaka Agni and Mahisasuramardini, but instead gains the Skills and NP listed below.  
By consuming a Command Spell, Durga’s Master can force Kali to transform back into Durga (Master does not lose Health again). In this case, Durga cannot transform into Kali again.

(Kali) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dark (Outsider)’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 25% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Dark (Outsider)’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 25% including NP.

(Kali) Samhara Tandava  
(Passive) At the end of Kali’s Turn or at the end of any Turn she Acts, she has a 10% chance of being inflicted with Berserk (Tandava) for 1◈ Turns (ignores Debuff Resist and Immune). Chance of being inflicted with Berserk increases depending on the following conditions-  
1\. If she was damaged by an Attack during that Turn: \+10%  
2\. If she successfully damaged a Unit with her Attack during that Turn: \+30%  
3\. If she defeats a Unit during that Turn: \+50% (overwrites effect 2\)  
4\. If she defeats a Unit with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute during that Turn: \+70% (overwrites effect 2 and 3\)  
Berserk (Tandava) has the same effects as normal Berserk. Although it does not stack, she can be inflicted with it again, which extends the duration of Berserk (Tandava).

(Kali) Mahakala

If a DU has the ‘Dark’ Attribute, it receives 50% extra damage and is also inflicted with Severe Burn for 1◈ Turns. Kali cannot be within the Skill area. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kali) Devi Mahatmya  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 25% extra damage and inflicts Seal for 1◈ Turns. Kali cannot be within the Skill area. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kali) Samhara Kali: World Shattering Victory Dance — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. First, apply Dmg Up \[Sky\] for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute is increased by 50%. Deals 5x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is further increased by 200%. At this point, if Devi Mahatmya is not on Cooldown, Kali uses that Attack Skill on all Units within the NP area (size of the Skill area increases accordingly). Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the affected Units, all damage taken is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
