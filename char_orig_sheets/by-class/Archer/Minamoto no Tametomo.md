(Tametomo) Leyline Artillery Anchor  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used when Tametomo is within his Home Base. When used, Tametomo enters Artillery Mode if he was not in that mode; and deactivates Artillery Mode if he was. Tametomo gains the following effects while in Artillery Mode-  
1\. Cannot Move, ~~use Skills (except this one),~~ Block, Evade, or Counter.  
2\. Range is increased by 4 panels and Normal Attacks hit a 3x3 panel area. However, Tametomo cannot Attack Units who are directly next to him.  
3\. If a DU Evades Tametomo’s Attack, its Evade roll is increased by X, where X=the number of panels between the DU and Tametomo.  
4\. His first Normal Attack every Turn (Round?) deals 25% extra damage.  
5\. If Tametomo is Attacked and receives damage from that Attack, Artillery Mode is forcefully ended at the end of that Combat Process and this Skill enters Cooldown.  
6\. As long as Tametomo is in Artillery Mode, his Sustainability does not reduce at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
(Passive) At the end of any Turn Tametomo Acts when not in Artillery Mode, his Master loses 15 Health (cannot drop below 1 in this way).
