(Orion) Blessings from Poseidon — Rank: B  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 40\. If Orion is within a ‘Waterside’ area, all damage dealt is increased by 80 instead; however if it is a Waterside (Rain) area, all damage dealt is increased by 60\. Also affects NP.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 40\. If Orion is within a ‘Waterside’ area, all damage taken is reduced by 80 instead; however if it is a Waterside (Rain) area, all damage taken is reduced by 60\. Also affects NP.  
3\. When Orion is within a Waterside area, MOV is increased by 1 and Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2; however if it is a Waterside (Rain) area, MOV is not increased and Agility Check rolls are only reduced by 1\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, the 7x7 panel area around Orion becomes a ‘Waterside (Rain)’ area for 1◈ Turns; this area does not move with Orion. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Divinity’.

(Orion) Curse of Scorpio — Rank: D  
(Passive) Poison Damage taken is increased by 20%.

(Orion) Stout Arm of Brutality — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (Brutality) for ⅓◈ Turns, at a Range of 1 to 2, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%; at a Range of 3 or higher, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Brutality) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ or ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute is increased by 50%..  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Orion) Pressure of the Moon Goddess — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies LUC Up for 1◈ Turns, Luck Check rolls are reduced by 3\.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Orion is defeated he is revived with 20% of his Max Health.  
4\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Orion, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Orion) Bowman of Three Stars — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Whenever Orion performs a Normal Attack, he has a 10% chance of applying Crit DmUp to himself for ⅓◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.   
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Orion’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, whenever Orion performs a Normal Attack, Crit DmUp is applied to him for ⅓◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Orion) Artemis Hagnós: Moon Goddess' Innocent Affections — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Pierce for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns to himself; with chance of being inflicted with Instakill is reduced by 30%, while chance of being inflicted with Death is reduced by 15%. Does not affect Erase.  
4\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%.   
5\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, roll a six-sided die. If a 1 or 6 is rolled, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 200%; otherwise, apply as ‘Crit Damage dealt is increased by 150%’ instead.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Orion) Orion Hórkos: No Beast is Beyond My Arrow's Reach — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive) When Orion is Summoned, roll a six-sided die. Orion only gains the Passive effect of this NP if a 1 or 6 is rolled. The effect is as follows- Whenever an enemy Unit with the ‘Demonic’ or ‘Beast’ Attribute enters Orion’s Range, he automatically performs a Normal Attack on that Unit.  
(Active) Range+3 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (STR) is used. If the DU has the ‘Demonic’ or ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute, inflict the ‘Hórkos’ debuff on that Unit for 2◈ Turns at the start of the Damage Step; its effects are as follows- Whenever Orion Attacks a Unit with the ‘Hórkos’ debuff, that Attack has the Ignore Def effect and also ignores all other damage reducing effects on that Unit.  
Deals 3.5x damage plus 100; if the DU does not have the ‘Demonic’ or ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute, Total Damage dealt is reduced by ⅓. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
