(Ptolemy) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Ptolemy) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ptolemy) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (5d12+5) including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+22) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Ptolemy) Saint Graph Data Preservation — Rank: A  
(Passive) Chance of having buffs removed from himself is reduced by 20%.

(Ptolemy) Charisma of Salvation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. If used as Ptolemy, apply as ‘all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%’ instead.  
2\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 100 at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 5%. If used as Ptolemy, apply as ‘Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 10%’ instead.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ptolemy) Thought Partition (King) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Crit Guard for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of a Unit’s Attack is reduced by 20% against a Unit with this buff. If used as Ptolemaios, apply as ‘Crit Chance of a Unit’s Attack is reduced by 20% against a Unit with this buff’ instead.  
4\. Ptolemy’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns. If used as Ptolemaios, 1◈+⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ptolemy) Contacting Wisdom — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Servant is summoned, Flip a Coin. If Heads, he is summoned as Ptolemy; if Tails, he is summoned as Ptolemaios.  
(Passive 2\) As Ptolemy, Normal Attacks use BA (STR) at a Range of 1 to 2 while they use BA (MAG) at a Range of 3 or higher. As Ptolemaios, Normal Attacks use BA (STR) at a Range of 1 while they use BA (MAG) at a Range of 2 or higher.  
(Passive 3\) Skill effects in this colour can only activate once per Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when initiating an Attack. Has different effects depending on whether he is currently Ptolemy or Ptolemaios.  
1\. As Ptolemy: Changes into Ptolemaios, then NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns and apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
2\. As Ptolemaios: Changes into Ptolemy, then apply the following effects-  
i) Apply NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
ii) Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
iii) Apply Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
iv) Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
