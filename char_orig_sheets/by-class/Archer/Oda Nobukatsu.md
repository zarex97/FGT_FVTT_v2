(Nobukatsu) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Nobukatsu) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Nobukatsu) Artless Stratagem — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Disorder for 1◈ Turns. At the start of every Turn, a Unit inflicted with Disorder has a 30% chance of being inflicted with Skill Seal for that Turn. (30% Disorder)  
2\. Inflicts Def Down (B) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Nobukatsu) Sacrifice to the Heavens — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Then, apply the following effects to any ‘Nobunaga’ allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself,  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 80%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 300%.  
3\. Applies Over Crit for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nobukatsu) War-torn Flower — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Decoy to himself for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Dmged NP Regen to himself for ⅓◈ Turns, whenever Nobukatsu receives damage from an Attack, his NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies the ‘Adabana’ buff to himself for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, 1 time, when Nobukatsu is defeated, restore the Health of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself by 200 excluding himself. This effect triggers even if Nobukatsu is revived with a revival effect (e.g. Guts).  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Nobukatsu) Chibi Nobbu Army  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Roll a four-sided die and add 2 to the number rolled. Nobukatsu summons that number of Chibi Nobbus within a 2 panel area of himself. Roll a six-sided die for each Chibi Nobbu summoned to determine which type is summoned, as follows-  
1\. Nobbu  
2\. Nobu-sengumi  
3\. Nobbutank  
4\. Nobbu UFO  
5\. Kabuki Nobbu  
6\. Your choice  
The Stats of each Chibi Nobbu are at the bottom of the page. Whenever a Chibi Nobbu is defeated, it has a 50% chance of exploding, dealing 50 Fixed damage to all Units within a 3x3 panel area around it. This explosion can be Evaded, but cannot be Blocked.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(Nobukatsu) Maou Kaiten**・**Higanbana — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Demon King Revolution: Red Spider Lily. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself by ⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all ‘Nobunaga’ allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
4\. Inflicts Sacrifice on himself, his Health is reduced to 0; Sacrifice cannot be prevented by any Resist or Immunity effects.  
Then, after the effects of this NP have resolved, Nobukatsu has a 5% chance of summoning a random ‘Nobunaga’ allied Unit on a panel directly next to him, this Nobunaga is automatically contracted to Nobukatsu’s Master. If Nobukatsu was **not** revived after using his NP, the chance of summoning a Nobunaga is 25% instead of 5%.  
Note: If a version of Maou Nobunaga was summoned through this effect, it cannot use the ‘Nobunaga Collective’ Skill.
