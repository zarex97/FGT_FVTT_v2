(Fujino) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Sustainability does not apply.  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.

(Fujino) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Fujino) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is further increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Fujino) Mystic Eyes of Distortion — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Fujino’s Normal Attacks have the Ignore Def effect.  
(Passive 2\) Fujino cannot perform Normal Attacks with her Base Attack (MAG) if she is inflicted with Blind.  
(Active) Applies Pierce (Distortion) for 1◈ Turns; and applies Atk Up (Distortion) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 45%; if NP, 35%. ‘Distortion’ buffs have no effects while inflicted with Blind. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Fujino) Clairvoyance (Darkness) — Rank: C++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Aim for 1◈ Turns  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.   
4\. Applies the ‘Waikyoku’ effect for 1◈ Turns to herself.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.

‘Waikyoku’ effect-  
1\. Fujino is able to Attack Units to her sides and behind her without changing Direction  
2\. Fujino can perform Normal Attacks with Base Attack (MAG) while inflicted with Blind.  
3\. ‘Distortion’ buffs are unaffected by Blind.  
4\. ‘Waikyoku’ is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.

(Fujino) Residual Sense of Pain — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the end of the Damage Step where Fujino receives damage from an Attack. Inflict Max HpDwn on Fujino for 1◈ Turns, Max and current Health is reduced by ⅙ of its maximum value, then apply the following effects on Fujino-  
1\. Applies Dmg Cut for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 200\.  
2\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Fujino is defeated, she is revived with 1 Health.  
3\. Apply the ‘Unfeeling’ buff for 1◈ Turns, when performing an Injury Roll, the number rolled is reduced by 2\.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Fujino) Yuishiki・Waikyoku no Magan — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Vijñaptimātratā: Mystic Eyes of Distortion. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 5x damage; then inflicts No Buff for 1 time; and inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Ignores Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns. 

(Fujino) Rainy Bridge: Collapse — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used, but deals STR damage. Hits a 3x5 or 5x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Fujino for 3.5x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Large’ Attribute is further increased by 100%. Then, inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
*“Bend, bend\! BENNNNNDDDD\!”*

Note: Yuishiki・Waikyoku no Magan cannot be used if Rainy Bridge Collapse is on Cooldown and vice versa.
