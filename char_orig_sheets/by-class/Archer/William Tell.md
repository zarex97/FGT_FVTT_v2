(William) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(William) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(William) Hunter of Uri — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has a 75% chance of inflicting Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns. Then, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of William, Crit Chance is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
If the Region of the Grail War is Switzerland, this Skill is elevated from Rank B to Rank EX. Its effects are as follows-  
(William) Hunter of Uri — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Chance of being inflicted by Offensive Debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
(Passive 2\) MOV is increased by 1 and Range is increased by 1 panel.  
(Passive 3\) Evade rolls of Units Attacked by William are increased by 1d4.  
(Passive 4\) Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Inflicts Skill Seal for 2◈ Turns. Then, apply S.Crit Up for 1◈+⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(William) Unwavering Patience — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up (Patience) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune (Patience) for 1◈ Turns, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
4\. Applies the ‘Patience’ effect to William, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff, and is Unremovable; but is removed from William after he Moves or Attacks.  
If William loses the ‘Patience’ effect due to Moving, he also loses Debuff Immune (Patience), while the magnitude of Atk Up (Patience) is reduced by half.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(William) Aiming — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up (Aim) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%, also affects NP; while William has the ‘Patience’ effect, Crit Chance is instead increased by 100%.  
2\.  Applies Crit DmUp (Aim) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%; affects NP as well if William has the ‘Patience’ effect.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(William) Opportunistic Patience — Rank: B  
(Passive) William’s Normal Attacks deal 40 extra damage to Units with the ‘Marked’ debuff.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any direction next to William. Has different effects depending on whether an affected Unit has a removable Dodge buff or not.  
1\. Unit with removable Dodge: Has a 500% chance of inflicting the ‘Marked’ debuff for 1◈ Turns. Units with the ‘Marked’ debuff are treated as ‘has a removable Dodge buff’ for the purposes of Apfel Schiessen.  
2\. Unit without removable Dodge: Has a 500% chance of inflicting the ‘Opportunistic’ debuff for 2◈ Turns. When a Unit with the ‘Opportunistic’ debuff gains a removable Dodge buff or performs an Evade, inflict the ‘Marked’ debuff for 1◈ Turns at a 500% chance on that Unit, then remove the ‘Opportunistic’ debuff from it.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(William) Apfel Schiessen: Released First Arrow of Faith — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process.. This NP has the Aim effect. Deals 3x damage. If the DU has a removable ‘Dodge’ buff (i.e. not Unremovable), damage dealt is further increased by 200%. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(William) Zweite Schiessen: Released Second Best Second Arrow — Rank: C+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) When Apfel Schiessen is used, William chooses an enemy Unit within the same Range of Apfel Schiessen (Note: Can be the same target as Apfel Schiessen’s target). If Apfel Schiessen fails to hit its target through any effect whatsoever, Zweite Schiessen is automatically activated, dealing 3x damage plus 100 to that second targeted Unit; Zweite Schiessen cannot be prevented through any method whatsoever.
