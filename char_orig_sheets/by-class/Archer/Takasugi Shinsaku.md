(Takasugi) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Takasugi) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Takasugi) Hero of the Reformation (Eccentric) — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 5%.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Takasugi performs a successful Crit Attack, restore 1 Luck at the end of the Damage Step. This effect cannot be activated if the Crit was caused by a successful Luck Check.

(Takasugi) Proto-Arahabaki  
(Passive) Once per Round, Takasugi can perform a Normal Attack using Proto-Arahabaki, Range=4. Takasugi must have at least 1 Industry Stock. When used, roll a six-sided die and remove that many Industry Stocks from Takasugi, that Normal Attack deals X0% extra damage.

(Takasugi) Unconventional Child Prodigy — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Agility.  
2\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Apply Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP are increased by 20%.  
4\. If any affected Units have a ‘Kihei’ buff, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to those Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
5\. Gain 2 Industry Stocks for every affected Unit with a ‘Kihei’ buff.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Takasugi) Innovate: Armaments Reformation — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within Range. Has different effects depending on whether the Unit has a ‘Kihei’ buff or not.  
1\. Without a ‘Kihei’ buff: Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%; and reduce its Skill Cooldowns by ⅓◈ Turns. Then, Takasugi gains 1d4 Industry Stocks.  
2\. With a ‘Kihei’ buff: Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%; and reduce its Skill Cooldowns by 1◈ Turns. Then, Takasugi gains 2d4 Industry Stocks.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Takasugi) My Japanese Spirit Will Live Forever — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. If Takasugi is in Sonjuku Mode, this Skill can also be used at the start of a Combat Phase when Takasugi is Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Takasugi is defeated he is revived with 25% of its Max Health.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Gain 1d6 Industry Stocks.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Takasugi is defeated and revived with an effect, gain 1d6 Industry Stocks.

(Takasugi) Kiheitai・Irregular Militia — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Can be used from the start of the game. Used during your Turn. Can only be used once per Round. Range=2. Used on an allied Unit within Range other than himself. First, roll a six-sided die; then roll X six-sided dice, where X=the number rolled on the first die. The first die determines how many ‘Kihei’ buffs are applied, and the second roll determines which ‘Kihei’ buffs are applied-  
1\. Kihei (STR): STR is increased by 1 Rank, Base Attack (STR) is increased by 25\.  
2\. Kihei (END): END is increased by 1 Rank, Max & current Health are increased by 250   
3\. Kihei (AGI): AGI is increased by 1 Rank, Max & current Agility are increased by 2\.  
4\. Kihei (MAG): MAG is increased by 1 Rank, Base Attack (MAG) is increased by 25\.  
5\. Kihei (LUC): LUC is increased by 1 Rank, Max & current Luck are increased by 2\.  
6\. Your choice.  
‘Kihei’ buffs are Unremovable and last for 3◈ Turns when applied. The same ‘Kihei’ buff can be applied more than once in a single usage of this NP. ‘Kihei’ buffs cannot enhance Parameters past Rank A, if a Parameter would be enhanced past Rank A by a ‘Kihei’ buff, a \+ is added to it instead (Note: Effects of \+ on Parameters can be checked in **Rules – Before Starting** sections).  
Takasugi loses 2 Industry Stocks for every ‘Kihei’ buff applied in this way. If Takasugi has insufficient Industry Stocks, the number of ‘Kihei’ buffs applied are reduced to a valid amount.  
Masters with a ‘Kihei (END)’ buff cannot be Overpowered.  
Masters with a ‘Kihei (STR)’ buff cannot be Underpowered when performing an Attack which uses Base Attack (STR).  
Masters with a ‘Kihei (MAG)’ buff cannot be Underpowered when performing an Attack which uses Base Attack (MAG).

(Takasugi) Innovate: Takasugi Heavy Industries  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Counts as Takasugi’s Attack for the Turn. Roll an eight-sided die. The number rolled determines how many Kiheitai are produced by Takasugi. Takasugi loses 3 Industry Stocks for every Kiheitai produced in this way. If Takasugi has insufficient Industry Stocks, the number of Kiheitai produced are reduced to a valid number.  
Roll a six-sided die for each one, the number rolled represents which type of Kiheitai are produced-  
1, 2\. Kiheitai: 500 Health, 5 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=2, MOV=3 and Base Attack (STR)=100.  
3, 4\. Heavy Kiheitai: 750 Health, 3 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=2, MOV=2 and Base Attack (STR)=100.  
5, 6\. Heretical Kiheitai: 500 Health, 5 Agility, 3 Luck, Range=2, MOV=3 and Base Attack (STR)=125.  
Kiheitai do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Kiheitai can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Takasugi or his Master if any Kiheitai are directly next to them. All Kiheitai have the \[Earth\], ‘Humanoid’, and ‘Mechanical’ Attributes. Cooldown: (Number of Kiheitai x ½◈) Turns. 

(Takasugi) Teachings of Yoshida Shoin  
(Passive) When Takasugi has 10 or less Industry Stocks, he forcibly changes into Sonjuku Mode. While in Sonjuku Mode, Takasugi has the following effects-  
1\. Irregular Militia and Takasugi Heavy Industries cannot be used.  
2\. Agility and Luck Check rolls are reduced by 2\.  
3\. Range is increased by 1\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 3 or higher use Base Attack (STR) and half of his Base Attack (MAG) combined, not affected by Magic Resistance.  
4\. At the end of Takasugi’s Turn and whenever he or an allied Unit within 2 panel area of himself performs a successful non-NP Attack, gain 1 Industry Stock at the end of the Damage Step.  
5\. At the end of a Turn, if Takasugi has more than 10 Industry Stocks, Sonjuku Mode ends.

(Takasugi) Innovate: Burst Ampules  
(Passive) Can only be used in Sonjuku Mode. Can only be used once per Round. Used when performing a Normal Attack at a Range of 2 or lower. Roll a four-sided die and apply the corresponding effect to Takasugi’s Attack-  
1\. Rekka: Deals 40% extra damage with a 60% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage (half).  
2\. Rekkō: Deals 40% extra damage with a 60% chance of inflicting Shock for 2◈ Turns. Lightning damage (half).  
3\. Reppu: Deals 40% extra damage with a 60% chance of inflicting Confuse for 1◈ Turns. Wind damage (half).  
4\. Rekkan: Deals 40% extra damage with a 60% chance of inflicting Disable for 1◈ Turns. Ice damage (half).

Then roll an eight-sided die and remove that many Industry Stocks from himself (if he had less than the number rolled, remove all of them). Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Takasugi) Choukyuu Ishin・Kiheitai — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Ultra Advanced Reformation: Kiheitai. Base Attack (STR) is used. When this NP is used, first perform the following steps-  
1\. Roll a four-sided die. Remove that number of Industry Stocks from himself and restore his Agility by the number rolled.  
2\. Roll a four-sided die. Remove that number of Industry Stocks from himself and restore his Luck by the number rolled.  
3\. Roll a six-sided die. Remove that number of Industry Stocks from himself and apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X0%; if NP, (X-1)0%. X=the number rolled.  
4\. Roll a ten-sided die. Remove that number of Industry Stocks from himself and apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by (Y\*5)%, Y=the number rolled.  
If Takasugi runs out of Industry Stocks when performing the above steps, treat all remaining unrolled dice as if a 1 was rolled. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage plus 100\. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
