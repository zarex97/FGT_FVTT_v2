(Billy) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Billy) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 3 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Billy) Marksmanship — Rank: A++  
(Passive 1\) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60%.  
(Passive 2\) Noble Phantasm is affected by effects that affect Crit Damage.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is further increased by 60%; and applies Range Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Range is increased by 1 panel. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Billy) Quick Draw — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Billy’s NP Cooldown by 1◈+⅔◈ Turns. Can be used at the start of a Combat Phase when Billy is Attacked by an enemy Unit; if used in this way, Billy can Attack first instead of the enemy Unit (NP Cooldown reduction is still applied and applied first). Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Billy) Eye of the Mind (False) — Rank: C  
(Passive) Evade rolls are reduced by 1, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns, and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Billy) Boy Bandit King — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Roll 6 six-sided dice and apply effects which correspond to the numbers rolled (do not reapply duplicate numbers). However, if any dice rolls a six, apply all effects-  
1\. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Restores 3 Agility and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
4\. Applies Crit Up (Billy) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
5\. Applies S.Crit Up (Billy) for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
6\. Applies the ‘Billy’ effect to himself, when Billy uses ‘Thunderer’, he can use Effect 2 of it without being Attacked by an enemy Unit; then remove the ‘Billy’ buff from himself regardless of whether Effect 2 of his NP was used in this method or not. The ‘Billy’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Billy) Thunderer: Thunderbolt of Broken Sound — Rank: C+++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. This NP has the Aim effect. Can be used in 2 methods, as follows-  
1\. Performs 3 separate, consecutive Normal Attacks on the DU (in a single Combat Process); then inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns at the end of the Combat Process X times, Crit Chance is reduced by 10% (X=the number of Attacks which successfully hit the DU). If the DU Evades, use the normal Evade rolls instead of Evade NP rolls; if Evaded the Unit has to perform the same number of Evade rolls and will be hit once for each Failure. If Blocked, it reduces damage from each hit separately. Use the Total Damage taken from those three Attacks when determining whether to perform an Injury Roll. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
2\. Used when Billy is Attacked by an enemy Unit; if used in this way, Billy Attacks/uses this NP first instead of the enemy Unit. Deals 3x damage plus 300 and cancels the enemy Unit’s Attack. Then, inflicts Crit Dwn on the DU for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 60%. This method cannot be used if the Unit who is Attacking Billy has the ‘Aim’ buff, or that Unit’s Attack has the ‘Aim’ effect. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
Note: When this NP is used, Billy’s Master loses Health equivalent to as if an E Rank Noble Phantasm was used.
