(Tomoe) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Tomoe) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Tomoe) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: E  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Tomoe’s Master loses 5 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; when its Health is 5 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Tomoe’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Tomoe can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Tomoe) Kishu no Ma — Rank: B  
Oni-type Demon.  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Tomoe’s Normal Attacks have a 25% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns to the DU and deal Fire damage (half).  
2\. Tomoe’s chance of being inflicted by Burn is reduced by 25%, and Total Fire Damage received is reduced by 25% including NP. If Mad Enhancement is Active, the chance of being inflicted by Burn is reduced by 50% instead.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Tomoe. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and applies NP DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 25%.   
Can be used when performing a Normal Attack. In this case, also applies the ‘Mana Burst’ effect to Tomoe, for that Combat Process, the Base Attack used is her Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 100+175=275), not affected by Magic Resistance; Burn chance is increased by 75% & is inflicted for 2◈ Turns instead.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tomoe) Knowledge of Chaotic Battle — Rank: B++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%  
2\. If there are more than three other Units within a 2 panel area of Tomoe and there is at least one enemy Unit among them, applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
3\. Tomoe’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Crit Up to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of Tomoe for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.   
5\. Applies the ‘Ransen’ buff for 1◈ Turns to herself, its effects are as follows- Tomoe’s Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2, and if Mad Enhancement is Active, the effects of Passive 6 are negated. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Tomoe) Blood Excitation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts to herself for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Tomoe is defeated she is revived with 20% of her Max Health.  
2\. Applies Max HpUp (Excitation) for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 30% of its original value. When the effects of Max HpUp (Excitation) ends (regardless of natural expiration or removal effects), reduce Tomoe’s Health by 10% of its maximum value.  
3\. Applies the ‘Excitation’ buff to herself for 1 time, her next Normal Attack deals 100% extra damage.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

However, when Kishu no Ma is used at the start of a Combat Process, Tomoe can use this NP instead of a Normal Attack; and this NP would use her Base Attack (STR) and 40% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined instead (BA=170), not affected by Magic Resistance.  
Deals 3.5x damage plus 200 and inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%. Then, inflicts Burn for 5◈ Turns; and inflicts Severe Burn for 2◈+½◈ Turns, inflicts for 5◈ Turns instead if Kishu no Ma was used, Burn Damage received is doubled.   
If Mad Enhancement is Active when this NP is used, the 5x5 panel area around Tomoe when this NP was used becomes a ‘Burning’ area for 5◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.
