(David) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(David) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(David) Divinity — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) All damage received from Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute and sources that are related to ‘Christ’ is reduced by 20 including NP.

(David) Divine Protection — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage taken is reduced by 50 including NP.

(David) Warning Bell for the Great Arrogant One — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ½◈ Turns; if there is an enemy Unit with the ‘Giant’ Attribute within a 3 panel area of himself, it is reduced by 1◈ Turns instead.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%; if there is an enemy Unit with the ‘Giant’ Attribute within a 3 panel area of himself, apply as ‘all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%’ instead.  
3\. Applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. If there is an enemy Unit with the ‘Giant’ Attribute within a 3 panel area of himself, it is applied for 1◈ Turns instead.  
4\. David’s Health is restored by 15% of its maximum value; if there is an enemy Unit with the ‘Giant’ Attribute within a 3 panel area of himself, it is restored by 25% instead.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(David) Harp of Healing — Rank: B  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of David. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores their Health by 10% of their maximum values.  
2\. Applies Dodge for 1 time.  
3\. Removes all non-volatile debuffs.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(David) Charisma — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 20 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=5. This NP has the Aim effect. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(David) Ark: The Ark of the Covenant — Rank: EX (NP) \[Covenant\]  
(Passive/Non-damaging) When David is summoned, the GM makes a secret mark on any panel on the board. The Ark will be hidden in that panel. Whenever a Servant gets within a 3 panel area of the panel where the Ark is hidden, they can use the Search action. If the Servant uses Search while standing on the Ark’s panel or on a panel directly next to it, the Servant obtains the Item \[Ark of the Covenant\]. A Servant who uses the Search action cannot Attack on the same Turn (but it does not count as that Servant Unit Attacking on that Turn).

Items held:

Item- \[Ark of the Covenant\]  
*A wooden box that contains the original engravings of the Ten Commandments issued to Moses by God.*  
In order to use this Item, the Servant with the \[Ark of the Covenant\] throws it at a Unit, the Range of this Attack is as follows (counts as the Servant’s Attack for the Turn)-  
STR Rank EX, A: 3 panels  
STR Rank B, C: 2 panels  
STR Rank D, E: 1 panels  
The targeted Unit then has to perform an Evade roll. If the Evade fails, the DU is inflicted with Death. If the Evade succeeds, the Attack fails and the DU is allowed to Move up to 2 panels.  
Regardless of success or failure, the \[Ark of the Covenant\] will land on the panel it was thrown at (represented by its figurine). In order to use the \[Ark of the Covenant\] again, its owner has to Retrieve the \[Ark\] while it is standing directly next to it. Any Unit other than the \[Ark\]’s current owner will be inflicted with Death if they touch/Retrieve the \[Ark\].  
When the \[Ark of the Covenant\]’s current owner is defeated, the \[Ark\] is dropped on the panel it was standing on, and it can be safely Retrieved by a different Servant, who becomes the \[Ark\]’s new owner. The \[Ark\] can also be safely Retrieved if its owner is defeated while it is on another panel.  
A Servant who uses the Retrieve action cannot Attack on the same Turn.
