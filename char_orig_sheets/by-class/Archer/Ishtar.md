(Ishtar) Jewel Formation of Absolute Fortune — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Gain 1d10 Jewels  
2\. Apply the ‘Formation’ effect to herself and gain three Formation Counters.  
3\. Apply Buff Up for 1◈ Turns, chance of having buffs applied to this Unit is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. This Skill cannot be used while Ishtar has the ‘Formation’ effect.

‘Formation’ effect-  
1\. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable except by its own effect.  
2\. Ishtar cannot gain Jewels while she has the ‘Formation’ effect.  
3\. Whenever Ishtar performs an Action while she has any Formation Counters, lose 1 Formation Counter.  
4\. When Ishtar loses her last Formation Counter, she loses the ‘Formation’ effect.  
5\. When ‘Jewel Formation of Absolute Fortune’ is used, until the start of her next Turn, if Ishtar did not Act, remove the ‘Formation’ effect and all Formation Counters from her, then apply the ‘Fortune’ buff for 1 time.  
6\. Whenever Ishtar loses a Formation Counter, from then until the start of her next Turn, if Ishtar did not Act, remove the ‘Formation’ effect and all Formation Counters from her, then apply the ‘Fortune’ buff for 1 time.

‘Fortune’ buff-  
1\. Chance of this buff being removed through effects is reduced by 80%.  
2\. All damage dealt is increased by X0%; if NP, the value is halved. X=the number of Jewels on Ishtar when the ‘Fortune’ buff was applied.  
3\. Range is increased by 2\.  
4\. Normal Attacks hit a 3x3 panel area within Range.  
5\. After Ishtar Moves, at the end of the Round, she will lose the ‘Fortune’ buff.  
6\. Jewels cannot be consumed to increase the damage of Normal Attacks.  
7\. After performing an Attack which consumes the ‘Fortune’ buff, Ishtar loses 1d10 Jewels, then reduce the Cooldown of ‘Jewel Formation of Absolute Fortune’ by 1◈ Turns.
