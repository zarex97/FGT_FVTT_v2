(Tesla) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Tesla) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Tesla) Galvanism — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All Total Lightning Damage taken is reduced by ⅔ including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever MAG damage is taken, restore Tesla’s Health by 25% of the damage dealt to him and reduce his NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever Lightning damage is taken, apply MOV Up (Galvanism) for 1◈ Turns, MOV is increased by 2\. MOV Up (Galvanism) does not stack, but its duration is reset if reapplied.

(Tesla) Tesla Coil — Rank: A++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Tesla. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen to himself for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Applies NP Regen to all other affected Units for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies Debuff ChUp to himself for ⅓◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%.  
4\. Applies Buff ChUp to all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, chance of applying buffs is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Tesla) Natural Born Genius — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Tesla is defeated, he is revived with 20% of his Max Health.  
2\. Has an 80% chance of applying NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Has an 80% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tesla) Pioneer of the Stars — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces Tesla’s NP Cooldown by 1◈+⅔◈ Turns; then applies Pierce for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns; and applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

If the Unit has the \[Earth\] or \[Sky\] Attribute, damage dealt is further increased by 200%. Lightning damage. Then, Tesla’s Health is reduced by 5% (10%?) of its maximum value.
