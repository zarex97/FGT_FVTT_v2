(Atalanta) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Atalanta) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Atalanta) Crossing Arcadia — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Moving, Atalanta can ignore all obstacles in her path (but still cannot enter the area around an enemy Master if…see Master-Servant Relationships). Also, Atalanta can Move, Attack, then Move again, as long as the total number of panels Moved does not exceed Atalanta’s MOV. (Same effect as Riding Skill’s Double Move.)  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel range of Atalanta. Has different effects on Atalanta and on other allied Units-  
1\. Atalanta: Restore her Agility by 25% of its maximum value; then applies Atk Up (Arcadia) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Other affected Units: Restore all affected Units’ Agility by 25% of their maximum value; then applies Atk Up (Arcadia) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%. Also applies MOV Up for 1 time to all affected Units, MOV is increased by 3\.  
Note: ~~The Atk Up (Arcadia) buff is removed from a Unit at the end of the Turn if that Unit has Moved during that Turn.~~ The effect of Atk Up (Arcadia) is the same as normal Atk Up; however it has no effect until after an affected Unit has Moved once, and also the Atk Up (Arcadia) buff is removed at the end of the Turn if an affected Unit has Moved during that Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Atalanta) Aesthetics of the Last Spurt — Rank: C  
(Passive) When Atalanta Attacks a Unit that is able to Counter her Attack (i.e. if Atlanta is within Range of any of the DU’s usable non-NP Attacks), Atalanta has a choice to reverse the combat order, letting the enemy Unit go first followed by herself (this Skill cannot be denied by the enemy Unit, they must perform an Attack on Atalanta). If she does, she has a 75% chance of automatically Evading the enemy Unit’s Attack. If Failed, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. Then if Atalanta Counters the Attack, the enemy Unit is unable to respond to the Attack and Crit Chance of this Attack is increased by 75%.

(Atalanta) Calydonian Hunt — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the ‘Calydon’ buff for 1◈ Turns, successful Normal Attacks at a Range of 2 or higher reduce NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns; if at a Range of 4 or higher, reduce by an additional ⅓◈ Turns; if the DU has the ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute, reduce by an additional ⅓◈ Turns    
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
5\. Applies Dmg Up (Wild) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Atalanta) Phoebus Catastrophe: Complaint Message on the Arrow — Rank: B+ (NP)  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Can be used in 2 different methods-  
1\. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage plus 100\.  
2\. Hits an enemy Unit within Range for 3.5x damage plus 100, Total Damage dealt is further increased by 40%.  
Regardless of which is used. S.Crit Up is applied to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Atalanta for ⅓◈ Turns after damage is dealt, Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Atalanta) Agrius Metamorphosis: Boar of Divine Punishment — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging/Passive/Activation) When this Noble Phantasm is activated, it has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Mad Enhancement (Agrius) — Rank: A to Atalanta that is permanently activated.  
2\. Range is reduced by 1\.  
3\. Phoebe Catastrophe is unusable.  
4\. Atalanta will always Attack (and Move towards) the closest Unit, including Allied Units.  
5\. When Crossing Arcadia is used, the effects no longer apply to allied Units (Atalanta only).  
Agrius Metamorphosis cannot be deactivated after it is used.  
(Atalanta) Mad Enhancement (Agrius) — Rank: A  
1\. Atalanta’s Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn she Acts; its Health cannot drop below 25 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 80% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2 and Atalanta’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Atalanta can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and cannot be removed except by its own effect/duration; if this Unit is inflicted with NP Seal, the effects of Mad Enhancement are negated for the duration of NP Seal.
