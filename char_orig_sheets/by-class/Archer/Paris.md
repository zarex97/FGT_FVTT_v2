Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Paris) Eyes of Apollo (False) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. First, has the following effects on Paris-  
1\. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns, 1 time.  
2\. Restores 2 Agility.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Apollo’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Normal Attacks inflict the VSI debuff for 1◈ Turns.   
Then, applies the following effects on the targeted enemy Unit-  
1\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%.  
2\. Removes all buffs from the Unit.  
3\. If this Skill successfully removed buffs from the targeted Unit, inflict the ~~Vital Spot~~ ‘Identified’ ~~(VSI)~~ debuff for 2◈ Turns.  
Finally, if Crit Dwn was successfully applied on the DU, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Paris, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Paris) Affections of the Male God — Rank: B

3\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is reduced by 50%. Does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Paris) Golden Apple — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores Paris’ Health and Agility by 25% of their maximum values, and reduces his NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Paris) Troia Velos: Shining Arrow of a Promised Conclusion — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

Note: Units with the ‘Vital Spot’ Attribute- Siegfried, Achilles, Roland, Gilles, Prelati (with the Spellbook), any other Unit who have a specific effect tied to a specific weak spot

(Paris) Dystychía Milo: The Golden Apple that Invites Dissention — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Paris, all affected Units must perform a Luck Check, increase the Luck Check roll by 1 for every Rank in MAG the Unit has lower than EX (e.g. Rank A MAG=increased by 1, Rank C MAG=increased by 3). If the Luck Check fails, the Unit is inflicted with Confuse (Milo) for 2◈ Turns, Confuse (Milo) is only removed by Attack damage if the damage received exceeds 200 or more in one hit. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
