(Euryale) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Sustainability does not apply.  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.

(Euryale) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Euryale) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 120 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Euryale) Bloodsucking — Rank: C   
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has a 60% chance of increasing its NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, 80% if used at a Range of 1\. Then, reduce Euryale’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; if it failed to increase the DU’s NP Cooldown, ⅓◈ Turns. The magnitude of this Skill is doubled if the DU is (any version of) Medusa & success rate is fixed to 100%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Euryale) Siren Song — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has a 100% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns. If the DU is Female, the chance of being Charmed is reduced by 50%. For every Rank in Magic Resistance the DU has, the chance of being Charmed is reduced by 10% (e.g. Rank D=2 Ranks=20% reduction). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Euryale) Goddess’ Whim — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, Euryale restores 3 Luck, and applies Atk Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Then, roll 2 eight-sided die and apply the effects as rolled (duplicate die are rerolled)-  
1\. Applies the ‘Glitter’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and half of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 50+125=175), effect of Magic Resistance is halved.  
2\. Applies MOV Up for 1◈ Turns, MOV is increased by 4\.  
3\. Inflict Stun for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units in a 2 panel area around Euryale.  
4\. Applies Def Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
5\. Applies Invuln (Whim) to her Master for 1◈ Turns; MOV Up (Whim) for 1◈ Turns, MOV is doubled; and apply Atk Up (Whim) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%; these effects are applied regardless of Range. As long as the Master has at least one ‘Whim’ buff applied to it, the Master cannot be Overpowered or Underpowered by Servants.  
6\. Inflicts Confuse on her Master for 1◈ Turns, regardless of Range.  
7\. Cancel the Luck recovery and Atk Up buff effects of this Skill.  
8\. Inflicts Sleep on herself until the end of this Turn that ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects, and cannot be removed by debuff removal effects (can be removed by taking damage).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Euryale) Eye of the Euryale: Goddess’ Gaze — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 3.5x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 200% if the DU is Male. Then, has a 150% chance of inflicting Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%. Next, has a 200% chance of inflicting Charm on the DU for 1◈ Turns if the DU is Male. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
