(Barghest) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP. Has no effect if Barghest is in Maid Mode.

(Barghest) Head Maid Chief  
(Trigger) As long as the Health of Barghest’s Master is less than 50% of its maximum value, Barghest automatically transforms into Maid Mode, gaining the following effects-  
1\. The Range at which she can Cover for her Master is increased by 1 panel.  
2\. When Covering for her Master, Total Damage taken from the Attack is increased by 50% instead of 100%.  
3\. All damage taken is reduced by 10% including NP.  
4\. If her Master’s Health has been restored back to above 50% of its maximum value, Maid Mode ends and she returns to normal.

(Barghest) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Barghest) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 40% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. This Servant’s Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns if its Master is defeated while ME is Active.  
6\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
8\. If Barghest enters Maid Mode or Fairy Knight Mode, Mad Enhancement is automatically deactivated (even if it was on Cooldown, its Cooldown is reset in this case) and cannot be used.

5\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5%.

(Barghest) Watersave Galatine: Horn of the Sun that Cleanses Calamities — Rank: B+\~A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
First, Barghest enters Fairy Knight Mode for 2◈ Turns (if she was already in Fairy Knight Mode, its duration is extended). Base Attack (MAG) is used. Can be used in two different methods-  
1\. Rank B+: Restore 2 Luck and apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage plus 100 and removes all Offensive Buffs from the affected Units. Water damage. Then, remove all Burn and Severe Burn buffs from the affected Units. Additionally, if the NP area was a ‘Burning’ or ‘Lava’ area, those panels are returned to normal (if it is a permanently ‘Burning’ or ‘Lava’ area, then those panels become normal for 2◈ Turns). Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Rank A: Restore 4 Luck and apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. Deals 4x damage and removes all Offensive Buffs from the DU. Then, remove all Burn and Severe Burn buffs from the DU. This NP is affected by Crit DmUp effects, but their magnitude is halved. Water damage. Additionally, if the 3x3 panel area around the DU was a ‘Burning’ or ‘Lava’ area, those panels are returned to normal (if it is a permanently ‘Burning’ or ‘Lava’ area, then those panels become normal for 2◈ Turns). Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.  
Note: Method 2 cannot be used if Method 1 is on Cooldown and vice-versa.
