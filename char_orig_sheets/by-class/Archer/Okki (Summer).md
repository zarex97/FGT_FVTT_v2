(SummerOkki) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(SummerOkki) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SummerOkki) Class Skill: Fortification Construction — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, the 5x5 panel area around Okki becomes a ‘Fort’. The ‘Fort’ lasts for 2◈ Turns, and has the following effects-  
1\. All damage dealt by Okki while she is within the ‘Fort’ is increased by 5d10.  
2\. All damage taken by allied Units within the ‘Fort’ is reduced by 3d10+15.  
The ‘Fort’ is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Okki is defeated, or if the area of an AoE Noble Phantasm intersects with the ‘Fort’ area.  
3\. Enemy Units can only enter the ‘Fort’ if their STR Rank is C or higher.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Territory Creation’.

(SummerOkki) Great Commander of Chiyogami — Rank: EX

(SummerOkki) Great Commander of Chiyogami: Yadokari Turret  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, Flip a Coin. If Heads, Okki creates and places two Yadokari Turrets within a 2 panel area of herself; if Tails, only one Yadokari Turret is produced. Yadokari Turrets have a Base Attack (STR) of 75, a MOV of 3, and a Range of 2 panels, 1 target. Whenever an enemy Unit enters the Range of a Yadokari Turret, the Yadokari Turret automatically performs an Attack on that Unit, this Attack cannot be Countered. A Yadokari Turret is instantly destroyed when Attacked.  
Yadokari Turrets do not count towards the number of Units who Move/Attack during your Turn. The same Yadokari Turret can only Move and Attack once during its Turn. Using this Skill counts as Okki’s Attack for the Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(SummerOkki) Hakuro Jyou・Senshiki Milimili Night Fever — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
White Egret Castle's Thousand Forms Milimili Night Fever. Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Hits a 7x7 panel area within Range for 5x damage plus 100 damage for every Yadokari Turret within the NP area. If Okki has built a ‘Fort’, the ‘Fort’ cannot be within the NP area. Then, applies the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Okki, or to all allied Units within Okki’s ‘Fort’ if she is inside it-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
