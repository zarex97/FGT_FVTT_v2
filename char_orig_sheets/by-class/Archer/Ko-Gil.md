(Ko-Gil) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Ko-Gil) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to E is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 10%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ko-Gil) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Ko-Gil) Charisma — Rank: A+  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 27 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Ko-Gil) Golden Rule — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Ko-Gil performs a Crit, reduce his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn.. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ko-Gil) Burgeon of the Sage King — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has an 80% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns if the DU has the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute; then applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ko-Gil) Arrow Protection Amulet — Rank: D- (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive/Non-damaging) All Ranged Attacks targeted at Ko-Gil with an STR or MAG Rank of D or lower have all their damage and effects negated. This NP has a maximum of 10 uses. After the 10th usage, the Amulet breaks and is no longer usable.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for X0 damage, X being the number of usages the Amulet has remaining and inflicts Blind for 1◈ Turns. The Amulet breaks and is no longer usable after this usage. Damage dealt is not affected by any damage modifying effects on Ko-Gil. This NP does not consume Ko-Gil’s Master’s Health when used in this way.

(Ko-Gil) Hades's Cap of Invisibility: Cloth of Concealment — Rank: E (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Gilgamesh. Applies the same effects as Presence Concealment — Rank: E. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. This NP does not consume Ko-Gil’s Master’s Health when used in this way.  
(Presence Concealment — Rank: E)  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current Agility is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has an 80% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with NP Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.
