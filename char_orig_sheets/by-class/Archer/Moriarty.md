(Moriarty) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Moriarty) Der Freischütz — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Moriarty performs a Normal Attack at a Range of 3 or higher, roll an eight-sided die at the start of the Combat Process. If a 1 to 6 is rolled, the Attack has the Aim effect. If a 7 is rolled, the Attack hits Moriarty instead. If an 8 is rolled, roll again. Note: When a 7 is rolled and the Attack hits himself, the Combat Phase ends after this Combat Process, the intended DU cannot Counter.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Pierce and Ignore Def for ⅓◈ Turns to himself.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance is increased by 60%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself except himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Whenever a 7 is rolled on the Passive effect of this Skill, the Cooldown for this Skill’s Active effect is immediately fully reduced.

(Moriarty) The End of the Spider’s Thread — Rank: A+++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Crit Dwn to Moriarty for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 10%. Ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects on himself.  
2\. Reduce Moriarty’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Apply NP DmUp to Moriarty for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
4\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Moriarty except himself, applies the ‘Malignant’ effect for 1◈ Turns, their Alignment is changed from ‘Good’ or ‘Neutral’ to ‘Evil’; if the Unit had no Alignment it is instead granted the ‘Evil’ Alignment. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Moriarty) Charisma of an Evil Genius — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself with the ‘Evil’ Alignment is increased by 25 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%; this effect is applied twice to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment excluding himself. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Moriarty) The Dynamics of an Asteroid: The Ultimate Crime — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. First inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 40%; then deals 4x damage plus 100\. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Moriarty) Catastrophe Crime: Ultimate Crime — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-City\]  
Can only be used when Moriarty is in his Home Base. Roll a six-sided die. After X◈ Turns (X being the number rolled on the die), this NP drops an Asteroid that hits a 9x9 panel area anywhere on the board at the start of the Turn, the NP area cannot intersect with Moriarty’s Home Base.  
Base Attack (MAG) is used, this NP (asteroid) deals 4x damage plus (X\*100, X being the number previously rolled on the six-sided die); deals STR damage. Additionally, if X is 4 or greater, the NP area is increased to a 11x11 panel area instead of 9x9. Moriarty cannot leave his Home Base until the Asteroid has fallen.   
If Moriarty is defeated at least 1◈ Turns before the Asteroid falls, this NP is cancelled; otherwise the Asteroid will still fall. If both Moriarty and his Master are defeated, the GM decides where the Asteroid will fall.   
Can only be used once during the entire game.
