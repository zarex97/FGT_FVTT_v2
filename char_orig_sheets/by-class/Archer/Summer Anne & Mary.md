**Summer Anne & Mary**

Summer Anne and Mary are summoned as two separate Servants as one. Anne and Mary have their individual Stats; however, the maximum distance between the two is 5 panels between them (4 if diagonal); (If Large Board, max distance between them is 8 panels; if diagonal, 7\). If either one is defeated, the other one is also defeated as well regardless of remaining Health. Both Anne and Mary are respectively allowed to Move and Attack once during their Turn; each one counts as 0.5 Units.

When calculating whether the damage dealt by either one of them needs to be reduced if she is too far from their Master, as long as the other counterpart is within their Master’s ZON, damage dealt is not reduced.

When either Anne or Mary uses a Skill, the Skill enters Cooldown for both of them.

(SummerAnne) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(SummerAnne) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(SummerAnne) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SummerAnne) Beach Flower — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Anne. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Then, applies Crit Up to all Male allied Units within that area for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 40%.  
If Mary is outside this Skill’s area when this Skill is used, the Atk Up effect is also applied to her, but 10% instead; if NP, 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(SummerAnne) Treasure Hunt (Ocean) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up to herself and Mary for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(SummerAnne) Pirate’s Honor — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Guts, Anne revives with 1 Health the next time she is defeated.  
3\. Applies Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 50%.  
If Mary is within a 3 panel area of Anne when this Skill is used, the effects of this Skill apply to her as well. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(SummerAnne\&Mary) Caribbean Free Bird Act 2: Wings Abreast As If Trees With Entwined Branches — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Uses Anne and Mary’s Base Attack (STR) combined (i.e. BA=125). Can only be used when the maximum distance between Anne and Mary is 3 panels (if diagonal, 2 panels). Range=3 panels from the NP initiator. Deals 3x damage plus 200; for every enemy Unit within a 3 panel area of both Anne and Mary and for every 200 Health that both Anne and Mary are missing, damage dealt is further increased by 10%. Note: The damage increasing effect does not stack if the same enemy Unit is within a 3 panel area of both Anne and Mary.  
Example: Anne’s Max Health is 956, Mary’s Max Health is 893\. 2 enemy Units within 3 panel area of Mary, 1 enemy Unit within a 3 panel area of Anne, Anne’s Health is 723, Mary’s Health is 402\. Therefore, damage dealt is further increased by 60%.  
Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the DU, all damage received is increased by 30%. Counts as both Anne and Mary’s Attack for the Turn. The effects of all Skills, buffs and debuffs on both Anne and Mary are combined when calculating damage for this NP. Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns.
