(Artemis/Orion) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A+  
1\. Sustainability does not apply.  
2\. Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
3\. Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.

(Artemis/Orion) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Artemis/Orion) Affections of the Goddess — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Artemis/Orion) Whimsical Bond — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Normal Attack damage dealt to Male Units is increased by 5d20. This effect is negated while Artemis has the ‘Whimsy’ effect.  
(Passive 2\) Only in effect while Artemis has the ‘Whimsy’ effect. All damage dealt to Male Units is increased by 100%; if NP, 80%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Apply the ‘Whimsy’ effect for ⅓◈ Turns. It is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Artemis/Orion) Eye of the Mind (False) — Rank: B-

(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns, and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 25%. Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.
