(Urd) Urðarþráður Forn: Thread that Weaves Bygone Pasts — Rank: EX (NP) \[Fate (Past)\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits all enemy Units within a 7x7 panel area around herself or a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. First, apply NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 5% for every Unit with a Past Counter on the field. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is increased by ⅓◈ Turns for every 3 Past Counters on the Unit (minimum increase=⅓◈ Turns)  
2\. Give them 1 Past Counter.  
Deals 5x damage, damage dealt is increased by 20% for every Past Counter on the affected Unit.  
Now, at this point, Urd returns the state of the game board to the start of the Round from three Rounds ago (includes this Round), this means the positions, Stats, status effects, Cooldowns of all Units are returned to that state as well. However, there are several things which will not be reversed by this NP, as follows-  
1\. Any Units defeated by this NP will not be returned to the board after the time reversal effect.  
2\. All damage dealt by this NP (including Injury Roll & NP Cooldown increase) will not be reversed.  
3\. The number/duration of Past Counters on Units are not affected.  
4\. Urd can choose not to be affected by this NP’s time reversal effect. However, if she chooses to be time reversed as well, the Cooldown of this NP will not be affected by the time reversal (i.e. will be reset to maximum value of Cooldown after time reversal).  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns. This NP cannot be Countered.

Note: Yggdrasil Urðarbrunnr cannot be used on the same Turn as Urðarþráður Forn and vice-versa.
