(Tristan) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B

(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Tristan) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Tristan) Harp of Healing — Rank: C  
(Passive) Whenever Tristan performs an Attack, apply the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself at the end of the Combat Process-  
1\. Has a 50% chance of restoring 50 Health to the affected Units.  
2\. Has a 10% chance of removing one random non-volatile debuff from the affected Units,  
This Skill has no effect if Tristan is affected by Skill Seal.

(Tristan) In Sonorous Praise of Love — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores the Unit’s Health by 10% of its maximum value.  
2\. Removes all debuffs.  
3\. Applies Dodge for 1 time.  
4\. Restores 2 Agility, then applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tristan) Unblessed Birth — Rank: B  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, then applies NP Seal for 1◈~~\+⅔◈~~ Turns (ignores Debuff Resist & Debuff Immune effects).  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Tristan) Admonishment to the King of Knights — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit. Removes all buffs from the Unit, and applies Crit Down for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%.   
If this Skill is used on an ‘Arturia’ Unit, Crit Chance is reduced by 30% instead of 20%; and also inflicts No Buff for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tristan) Weakness: Poison — Rank: D
