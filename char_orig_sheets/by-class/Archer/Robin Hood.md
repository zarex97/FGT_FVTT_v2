(Robin) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Robin) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Robin) Subversive Activities — Rank: A  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Robin. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 15%; if NP, 5%; and inflicts Poison. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Robin) Golden Rule — Rank: E  
(Passive) Whenever Robin performs a Crit, it has a 20% chance of reducing his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.

(Robin) Sage of the Forest — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ½◈ Turns; if Robin is within a ‘Forest’ area, it is reduced by an additional ½◈ Turns.   
2\. Applies NP Regen (Forest) for 1◈ Turns, as long as Robin is within a ‘Forest’ area, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Restores 2 Luck.  
4\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Robin) May King — Rank: B  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Applies Dodge for ⅔◈ Turns and Aim for ⅔◈ Turns; then applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Robin) Yew Bow: The Bow of Prayer — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Whenever Robin successfully performs a Normal Attack on a Poisoned Unit, the DU is inflicted with Poison (Poison increases by 1 Stage. No effect on un-Poisoned Units).   
(Active) Can only be used when conditions regarding No Face May King are met (see below) Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 2.5x damage. For every Stage of Poison the DU has, damage dealt is further increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Robin) No Face May King: Faceless King — Rank: ??? (NP) \[N/A\]  
(Non-damaging/Passive) Robin is constantly under the effect of Presence Concealment — Rank: B except when Combat is initiated (deactivated at the immediate start of the Combat Phase and during it, reactivates at the end of the Combat Phase). Can be activated/deactivated during your Turn, or at the start or end of any Turn or Round without Cooldown. However, No Face May King must be deactivated for at least 1◈ Turns (1 full Round?) before Yew Bow can be used, and can only be reactivated 2◈ Turns after Yew Bow was used. If Robin was Discovered by an enemy Unit, No Face May King can only be reactivated 1◈ Turns (1 full Round?) after being Discovered.  
No Face May King has no effect while Robin is affected by NP Seal.  
Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
~~5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.~~ Not in effect.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.
