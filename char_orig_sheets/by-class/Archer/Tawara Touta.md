(Touta) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
   
(Touta) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.  
   
(Touta) Protection from the Divine Dragon — Rank: C  
(Passive) All Elemental damage taken is reduced by 25% including NP.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores his Health by 20% of its maximum value, 2 Agility, & 2 Luck. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
   
(Touta) Protection from Arrows — Rank: C  
(Passive) Touta has a 75% chance of automatically Evading any ‘arrow’ based Ranged Attack including Noble Phantasms from his Front and Sides (with a few possible exceptions); when he receives an aforementioned Attack from Behind (i.e. the 3 panels behind him) and he Evades, the Evade roll is reduced by 2\. If Protection from Arrows fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. Does not apply to Ranged AoE Attacks/Noble Phantasms. Has no effect if this Unit is affected by Skill Seal.  
   
(Touta) Mujin Tawara — Rank: EX  
Bale of Inexhaustibility. Can be used in two different methods-  
(Active 1\) Used on your Turn. Used on an other(?) allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Touta. Restores its Health by 30% of its maximum value, restores 3 Agility, and removes a random debuff from them. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Active 2\) Attack Skill. Range=2, Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 50% extra damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
   
(Touta) Namu Hachiman Daibosatsu・Kono Ya ni Kago wo — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
   
(Touta) Mujin Tawara: Bale of Inexhaustibility — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Banquet\]

3\. Applies Atk Up for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
4\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns except for Mujin Tawara.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
