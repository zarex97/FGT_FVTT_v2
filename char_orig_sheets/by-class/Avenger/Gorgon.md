(Gorgon) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank B  
(Passive) Increases all damage taken by 80 including NP. If Gorgon Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 80 including NP.

(Gorgon) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: C  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 45\.

(Gorgon) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: A  
(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 50 and NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns. 

(Gorgon) Monstrous Strength — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 105%; if NP, 55%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Note: This effect is not treated as applying a buff.   
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Great Monster’s Trampling.

(Gorgon) Great Monster’s Trampling — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (Outrage) for 2◈ Turns, 4 times, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%. When Gorgon performs a Normal Attack using Monstrous Strength, the effect of Atk Up (Outrage) does not apply to that Attack (and it is not consumed by that Attack either).  
2\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Monstrous Strength.

(Gorgon) Demonic Mutation — Rank: B++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts to self for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Gorgon is defeated she is revived with 30% of her Max Health.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Bind) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with Bind effects is increased by 50%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Mutation’ buff for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns when performing a successful STR-damage Normal Attack Crit.    
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Gorgon) Scream of Awe — Rank: A++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all other Units within a 3x3 panel area of herself. Inflicts Curse; then inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Gorgon) Mystic Eyes — Rank: A++  
(Active) Mystic Eyes of Petrification: Cybele. Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit. Cannot be used on a Unit if there is an obstacle/obstruction between Gorgon and the target Unit, and can only be used if Gorgon and the targeted Unit are facing each other.   
(Example: Unit \[Cannot be targeted\]-Unit \[Can be targeted\]-Gorgon) Has different effects depending on who it is used on.  
Humans (including Masters): Perform an Agility Check. If Successful, the DU is Stunned for 2◈ Turns. If Failed, the DU is Petrified.  
Non-humans, Servants with MAG of Rank C or lower: Perform an Agility Check. If Successful, reduce the DU’s Agility by 3\. If Failed, roll again. On the second time, if Successful, the DU is Stunned for 1◈ Turns. If Failed, the DU is Petrified.  
Servants with MAG of Rank B or higher: Perform an Agility Check. If Successful, reduce the DU’s Agility by 3\. If Failed, the DU is Stunned for 1◈ Turns.  
Debuffs inflicted by this Skill ignore the DU’s debuff resistance due to Magic Resistance.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Gorgon) Pandemonium Cetus: Forced Seal, Pandemonic Temple — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area around Gorgon. Units 2 panels away from Gorgon receive 4x damage plus 100, while Units on the edge/outer ring receive 3x damage plus 100\. Then, inflicts Stage 2 Curse on all affected Units. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
