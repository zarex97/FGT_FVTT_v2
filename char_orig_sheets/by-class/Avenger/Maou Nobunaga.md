(MaouNobu) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank: B-  
(Passive) (Passive) Increases all damage taken by 100 including NP. If Nobunaga Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 100 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Nobunaga takes Burn Damage, the Burn Damage taken is added to the Burning Avenger counter. Whenever Nobunaga deals damage with an Attack, the Attack deals bonus damage equal to the value of Burning Avenger; then reduce Burning Avenger to 0 after damage is dealt.  
(Passive 3\) Base Attack reduction when Nobunaga is inflicted with Burn is reduced by half. If Nobunaga **is in** Oda Nobunaga form, her Base Attack is not reduced due to Burn.  
(Passive 4\) When Nobunaga is inflicted with Burn, she receives damage at the end of every Turn she Acts as well as at the end of the Round.

(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 30 and NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns. 

(MaouNobu) Nobunaga Collective  
Maou Nobunaga has three possible forms. During your Turn, or at the end of any Turn Maou Nobunaga Acts, she can transform into two of her other forms. This Skill can only be used once per Round. Her forms are as follows-  
1\. Oda Nobunaga  
2\. Oda Kippoushi  
3\. Maou Nobunaga

(MaouNobu) Zehi mo Nashi\! — Rank: A-  
(Active) It’s Inevitable\! Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (Zehi) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%. After every ⅓◈ Turns, the magnitude of Atk Up (Zehi) is further increased by 20% (i.e. after ⅓◈ Turns=50%; NP, 25%; after ⅔◈ Turns=70%; NP, 35%).  
If Nobunaga is **not** in Oda Nobunaga form, the magnitude of Atk Up (Zehi) is reduced by 10% (don’t reduce for NP, just halve it).  
2\. The 5x5 panel area around Nobunaga becomes ‘Burning (Zehi)’ for 2◈ Turns. Nobunaga is not affected by the ‘Burning (Zehi)’ area.  
3\. Nobunaga is inflicted with Burn for 2◈ Turns. Ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects on herself.  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(MaouNobu) Yugen no Gotoku — Rank: B++  
(Passive) While Nobunaga is within a ‘Burning’ area, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.  
(Active) As If It Was A Dream. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Buff Removal ResUp (Yugen) for 1◈ Turn, chance of having buffs removed from Maou Nobunaga is reduced by 75%. If Nobunaga **is in** Oda Kippoushi form, chance of having buffs removed is reduced by 100% instead.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Yugen) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute while Nobunaga is within a ‘Burning’ area is (further) increased by 50% including NP.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns; If used when Nobunaga **is in** Oda Kippoushi form, Cooldown is 3◈-⅔◈ Turns instead.

(MaouNobu) Dairokuten Maou — Rank: EX  
(Active) Demon King of the Sixth Heaven. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. If used when Nobunaga **is in** Maou Nobunaga form, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns instead.  
2\. Applies Area CritUp (Maou) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%. If used when Nobunaga **is in** Maou Nobunaga form, Area Crit Up (Maou) is applied for 2◈ Turns instead, and Crit Chance is increased by 20% instead of 10% (Note: Reverts to 10% if she leaves Maou Nobunaga form, but duration is unaffected).  
3\. Applies Atk Up (Maou) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt while Nobunaga is within a ‘Burning’ area is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(MaouNobu) Hajun Henjou・Sanzen Daisenten Maou — Rank: A- (NP) \[Anti-Divine\]  
Papiyas Metamorphosis: Demon King of a Billion Worlds. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=4. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. First, remove all Defensive Buffs from all affected Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute.  
2\. Deals 4x damage minus 100\. Deals extra damage to Units with the ‘Divinity’ Skill. Amount of extra damage dealt is dependant on the DU’s Rank in Divinity-  
Rank EX: Damage dealt is further increased by 300%.  
Rank A: Damage dealt is further increased by 250%.  
Rank B: Damage dealt is further increased by 200%.  
Rank C: Damage dealt is further increased by 150%.  
Rank D: Damage dealt is further increased by 100%.  
Rank E: Damage dealt is further increased by 50%.  
For every \+ in the DU’s Divinity Rank, damage dealt is increased by 100; for every \- in the DU’s Divinity Rank, damage dealt is decreased by 100\.  
If the DU only has the ‘Divine’ Attribute but not the ‘Divinity’ Skill, treat it as having Rank B Divinity.  
However, if the DU does not have the ‘Divinity’ Skill or ‘Divine’ Attribute, Total Damage dealt to the DU is reduced by 25%.  
Deals Fire damage; ignores effects that resist Fire damage on Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute.  
3\. Inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns; ignores effects that resist Burn on Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute.  
Cooldown: 7◈-⅓◈ Turns.
