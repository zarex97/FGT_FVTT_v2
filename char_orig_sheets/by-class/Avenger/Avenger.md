(Avenger) The End of the Four Nights — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 6◈ Turns, when Avenger is defeated he is revived with 50% of his Max Health.  
2\. Applies the ‘Yotsuya’ effect for 5◈ Turns, has the following effect- Every 1◈ Turns, at the end of the Turn, Avenger’s Base Attack (both) is increased by 25\. However, if Avenger is defeated before the ‘Yotsuya’ effect naturally expires, then the ‘Yotsuya’ effect is removed from him without resetting his Base Attack. The ‘Yotsuya’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff, and is Unremovable.
