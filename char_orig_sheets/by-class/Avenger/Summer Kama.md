(Kama) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Kama) The One Without Body  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Kama can switch between her ‘Child Form’ and ‘Adult Form’. Can only be used once per Turn. She gains different effects depending on which Form she is in, as follows-  
1\. Child Form: Has the ‘Child’ Attribute. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2\.  
2\. Adult Form: All damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
When Kama is summoned, Flip a Coin to determine which Form she is summoned in (Heads=Child, Tails=Adult).

(Kama) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kama) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can be used in two different ways-  
1\. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.   
2\. Only usable when Kama is directly next to her Master. Restores the Health of both Kama and her Master by 25% of their respective maximum values, and both their Luck by 2\. Neither Kama or her Master can Attack on the Turn this effect is used; and cannot Move after this effect is used. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. 

(Kama) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank: B  
(Passive) Increases all damage taken by 80 including NP. If Kama Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 80 including NP.

(Kama) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: B  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60\.

(Kama) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: A  
(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 50 and NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.

(Kama) Demon King’s Divine Core (?) — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive) Kama is Immune to Charm.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Kama) Class Skill: Independent Manifestation — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (4◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 1 panel.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs, Instakill, Death, and Erase is reduced by 40%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Independent Action — Rank: E’.

(Kama) Makara Floating — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kama) Hollow Demon — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%. Then, hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself, has a 100% chance of inflicting Charm on all affected enemy Units. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kama) Mara in Midsummer Sea — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈+½◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the ‘Climaxing’ buff for 1◈ Turns- Normal Attacks inflict the ‘Blaze’ debuff for 1◈ Turns on the DU.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Blaze) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Blaze’ debuff is increased by 10% including NP for every ‘Blaze’ debuff on it.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: The maximum number of ‘Blaze’ debuffs a Unit can have is 10\. If ‘Blaze’ is inflicted on a Unit already at max stacks, then the ‘Blaze’ with the shortest remaining duration is removed and replaced with a new ‘Blaze’ debuff.

(Kama) Kama Rūpāstra: A Climaxing Love is Passion in Summer — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage, damage dealt to Units inflicted with Charm is further increased by 150%. Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all affected Units, all damage taken is increased by 20%. Does not affect herself. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Kama) Heartfelt Blaze  
(Passive 1\) Kama gains Heartfelt Tokens when performing the following Actions-  
1\. Performing a successful Normal Attack, Evade, or Luck Check: \+1  
2\. Using a Skill except Class Skills & ‘The One Without Body’: \+2  
3\. Performing a successful NP: \+3  
4\. Performing any of the above Actions while within a 2 panel area of her Master: \+1  
5\. Successfully inflicting Charm: \+3 per Unit inflicted  
6\. Using Effect 2 of Riding: \+5  
7\. Master uses a Skill on her: \+2  
8\. Master uses a Command Spell on her: \+3  
9\. Kama Shoves her Master out of a NP area or performs a Cover for her Master: \+4  
The maximum number of Heartfelt Tokens is 30\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used while Kama has 30 Heartfelt Tokens. When used, Kama enters Sunyata Form.  
(Passive 2\) Gains the following effects while in Sunyata Form-  
1\. Gains the ‘Levitating’ and ‘Spaceflight’ Attributes.  
2\. ‘The One Without Body’ can no longer be used and has no effects.  
3\. The maximum stack of ‘Blaze’ is increased to 20\.  
4\. Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting ‘Blaze’ for 1◈ Turns.  
5\. The ‘Blaze’ debuff gains the following effects- At the end of the Round, take 5 Fixed Fire damage for every stack of Blaze on this Unit.  
6\. Kama Rūpāstra can no longer be used and is replaced with Mara Śūnyatā.

(Kama) Summer’s Embrace  
Attack Skill. Can only be used in Sunyata Form. Range=1 panel. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals normal Fire damage. Then, if the DU had any ‘Blaze’ debuffs, double the number of ‘Blaze’ debuffs on the DU and reset their duration to 1◈ Turns; if the DU had no ‘Blaze’ debuffs, then inflict two ‘Blaze’ debuffs on the DU for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. If this Attack Skill was successfully Evaded, it does not enter Cooldown, but it cannot be used again on the same Turn.

(Mara) Mara Śūnyatā: To Descend into Passion is the End of Love — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Can only be used in Sunyata Form. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself for 3.5x damage, damage dealt to Units inflicted with Charm is further increased by 200%. Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all affected Units, all damage taken is increased by 25%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
