(Dantès) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank A  
(Passive) Increases all damage taken by 100 including NP. If Dantès Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 100 including NP.

(Dantès) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: B  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60\.

(Dantès) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: D  
(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 20 and NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.   
   
(Dantès) Determination of Steel — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Dantès has to perform an Injury Roll, roll a four-sided die. Dantès will only have to perform an Injury Roll if a 1 is rolled.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns, and applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
   
(Dantès) Golden Rule — Rank A  
(Passive) Whenever this Unit performs a Crit, reduce its NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.

(Dantès) The Treasure of Monte Cristo — Rank A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, reduce Dantès’ NP Cooldown by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Applies the ‘Monte’ buff for 1◈ Turns, a successful activation of ‘Golden Rule’ reduces NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns instead of 1 Turn.  
3\. Applies the ‘Cristo’ buff for 1◈ Turns, successful non-NP Crit Attacks will remove 1 buff from the DU at the end of the Damage Step.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Dantès) Wisdom of Predicament — Rank A  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Dantès performs a Luck Check, he will always succeed. If any other Unit would perform a Luck Check involving Dantès, roll a twelve-sided die instead. The Luck Check will only succeed if a 12 is rolled. For any effects involving comparing LUC Ranks, Dantès’ will always be higher/highest.  
Note on Luck Checks-  
i) Dantes can only use Lucky Evasion and Lucky Hit if his current Agility is higher than the opposing Unit’s.  
ii) Strengthen Block, Reduced Damage, and Light Wound can only be used if his END Rank is higher than the AU’s STR or MAG Rank, depending on which type of damage the Attack used.  
iii) Prevention can only be used once per Turn.  
(Passive 2\) The chance of inflicting debuffs of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Dantès is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 3\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs of all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of Dantès is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%). Does not affect himself.  
(Active 1\) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Increase its NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns. Then, remove all debuffs from Dantès and apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Active 2\) Used during your Turn. Has the same effect as the Active effect of ‘Item Construction — Rank B’. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Note: Active 1 of this Skill cannot be used on the same Turn as Active 2 and vice-versa. Passive 2 and Passive 3 of this Skill are negated while any Active effect of this Skill is on Cooldown.  
   
(Dantès) Monte Cristo Mythologie: The King of the Cavern — Rank C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) ~~Dantes is Immune to Poison, Curse, and Burn.~~ Whenever Dantes performs a successful Normal Attack, the DU rolls a four-sided die. The following will occur at a 30% chance depending on the number rolled-  
1\. The DU is inflicted with Poison.  
2\. The DU is inflicted with Curse.  
3\. The DU is inflicted with Burn for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Nothing happens.  
(Active) Used when performing a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, the Base Attack used is Dantès’ BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (e.g. BA=300); then the DU has a 70% chance of being inflicted with Burn for 1◈ Turns, Curse, and Poison respectively. Not affected by Magic Resistance. *Categorized as Noble Phantasm*. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.  
Note: The debuff applying chances of this NP are not affected by any effects which modify this Servant’s chance of inflicting debuffs.  
   
(Dantès) Attendre, Espérer: Wait, and Hope — Rank B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Can be used in two different methods-  
1\. Used during your Turn or at the end of a Combat Process. When used, Dantes restores his Health and Agility by 50% of their maximum values.  
2\. If a(n allied) Unit is defeated on a panel next to Dantes, Dantes can revive that Unit, restoring its Health, Agility and Luck by half of their maximum values.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
   
(Dantès) Enfer Château d'If: Tyger, Tyger, Burning Bright — Rank A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Dantes’ Range for 4x damage plus 100\. Then, inflicts Stage 2 Curse on all affected Units and Evil Curse for 1◈ Turns, Curse Damage received is doubled. Also inflicts Def Dwn on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓ ◈ Turns.

(Dantès) Paradis Château d'If: O’ You, Conquer the Brilliant Road — Rank B+ (NP) \[Barrier/Trial\]  
(Non-damaging) After Dantès’ Health has been reduced to 5% or lower once since the start of the game- at the end of his Turn, at the end of every Turn he Acts, and at the end of the Round, he has a 40% chance of disappearing from the board (Note: This does not count as being defeated). Then, his Master gains a Trial Counter which starts at 0\. The following Actions of his Master can increase the Trial Counter as follows-  
1\. Involved in a Combat Phase: \+5  
2\. Defeats a non-mob, Master, or Servant Unit: \+5  
3\. Defeats a Master: \+10  
4\. Defeats a Servant: \+20  
While his Master has a Trial Counter, its chance of being Overpowered is reduced by 20%.  
At the start of every Combat Phase his Master participates in, this NP has a X% chance of summoning a Count of Monte Cristo next to his Master (Note: this Count of Monte Cristo is treated as the same Servant as the originally summoned Edmond Dantès), X=the value of the Trial Counter. Then, remove the Trial Counter from his Master.
