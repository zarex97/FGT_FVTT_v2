(Kagekiyo) Genji, Shi ni Sourae — Rank: A++  
Genji Must Die. Has the following effects-  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Genji’ Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dmg Up (Genji) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Genji’ Attribute is further increased by 100%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Grudge) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60% plus 10% for every Grudge stack on herself.  
3\. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is reduced by 10%. If an affected allied Unit has the ‘Genji’ Attribute, they are instead inflicted with No Crit for 1◈ Turns, 3 times.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Kagekiyo) Kagekiyo wa Shinazu — Rank: EX  
(Active) Kagekiyo Never Dies. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts (Shinazu) for 1◈ Turns, when Kagekiyo is defeated she is revived with 25% of her Max Health. If Kagekiyo is inflicted with Death while she has the Guts (Shinazu) buff, it has a 75% chance of reviving her. Note: This Guts (Shinazu) stacks with normal Guts buffs.  
2\. Applies Death ResUp (Shinazu) for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Instakill is reduced by 100%; if Death, 50%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Kagekiyo’ buff for 1◈ Turns, whenever Kagekiyo is defeated and revived, she gains a Grudge stack for 3◈+⅓◈ Turns. The maximum number of Grudge stacks Kagekiyo can have is 4\. Each Grudge stack is categorized as a separate buff and they can be removed by buff removal effects.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Kagekiyo is defeated and revived while she does not have the ‘Kagekiyo’ buff, she has a 25% chance of gaining a Grudge stack for 3◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) While Kagekiyo has more than 1 Grudge stack, Range and NP Range is increased by 1\. While Kagekiyo has more than 2 Grudge stacks, Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Curse. While Kagekiyo has more than 3 Grudge stacks, Normal Attacks use her Base Attack (STR) and 40% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 75+70=145) and have a 20% chance of inflicting Curse, not affected by Magic Resistance.  
(Passive 3\) If Kagekiyo’s Health is less than 25% of its maximum value, the durations of all Grudge stacks on herself are paused and Grudge stacks are Unremovable.

(Kagekiyo) Hateful Ambush  
Attack Skill. Range=2 to 3\. Kagekiyo teleports (Moves) to the panel directly behind the DU, performs a Normal Attack on the DU, then Moves to the panel in front of the DU at the end of the Combat Process. If the Attack was Successful, Kagekiyo has a 5% chance of gaining a Grudge stack for 1◈ Turns; if it Failed, chance of gaining a Grudge stack is 10% instead. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

Then, inflict Curse on the DU; for every 2 Grudge stacks Kagekiyo has, inflict an extra stage of Curse on the DU. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
