(SIshtar) Space Goddess of Origin  
When Space Ishtar is summoned, roll a four-sided die. The number rolled determines which form of Space Ishtar is summoned, as follows-  
1\. Astarte  
2\. Ishtarin  
3\. Ishtar Ashtoret  
4\. Your choice  
Each form has different Normal Attack Ranges, as follows-  
Astarte: Range=2 panels, 1 target; Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR).  
Ishtarin: Range=3 panels, 1 target; Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR).  
Ishtar Ashtoret: Range=3 panels, 1 target; Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
Note: Astarte can still be summoned even if Ishtarin has already been summoned and vice versa, but neither can be summoned if Ishtar Ashtoret has already been summoned and vice versa.  
Note 2: Ishtar Ashtoret has the ‘Levitating’ Attribute.  
Note 3: If Astarte is defeated on a panel next to Ishtarin or vice versa, the undefeated Astarte/Ishtarin is able to transform into Ishtar Ashtoret; and if she chooses to, the transformation success chance is 60%. If the transformation succeeds, her Stats are restored by 30% of their maximum values. If the transformation fails, nothing happens (the defeated Unit is still defeated). This effect can be used for both allied and enemy Unit defeats. Can also be used if the defeated Unit is 2 panels away, but success chance is 30% instead.

(SIshtar) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SIshtar) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(SIshtar) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: A++  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 110 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 30%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(SIshtar) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank EX  
(Passive) Increases all damage taken by 125 including NP. If Space Ishtar Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 125 including NP.

(SIshtar) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: A  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 75\.

(SIshtar) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: B  
(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 40 and NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns. 

(SIshtar) Devil’s Sugar — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. If Space Ishtar is Astarte, apply as ‘damage dealt is 30%; if NP, 15%’ instead.  
3\. Applies Charm Immune to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(SIshtar) Venus Driver — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
2\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 1 time. If Space Ishtar is Ishtarin, Invuln is applied for 1◈ Turns, 2 times instead.  
3\. Applies one of the following buffs of your choice to Space Ishtar for 1◈ Turns- Venus A, Venus B or Venus C, whose effects are as follows-  
Venus A: At the end of the Combat Process after Space Ishtar uses her NP, she restores 2 Luck and applies NP Regen (Venusia) to herself until the end of the Round- whenever Space Ishtar performs a successful Attack, her NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of the Damage Step. Note: NP Regen (Venusia) replaces Venus A when applied through this effect.  
Venus B: At the end of the Combat Process after Space Ishtar uses her NP, apply Atk Up (Venusia) to herself until the end of the Round, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%. Note: Atk Up (Venusia replaces Venus B when applied through this effect.  
Venus C: At the end of the Combat Process after Space Ishtar uses her NP, she restores 2 Agility and applies Crit Up (Venusia) until the end of the Round, Crit Chance is increased by 20%. Note: Crit Up (Venusia) replaces Venus C when applied through this effect.  
‘Venusia’ buffs are Unremovable and ignore effects which prevent/reduce the chance of buffs from being applied to herself.  
Space Ishtar can only have one ‘Venus’ buff on her at any time, if a different ‘Venus’ buff is applied to her while she already has one, the previous ‘Venus’ buff is removed.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

5\. If either Effect 3 or 4 is triggered (or both) are triggered and Effect 2 is not triggered, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%
