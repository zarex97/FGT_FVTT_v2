(Kuroe) How’s The Progress? — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Mangaka’ buff for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, 5 times, its effects are as follows- When a Unit with this buff performs an Attack, gain 1 Manuscript Page for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step for every Unit hit by that Attack. Manuscripts are categorized as a buff, and are each categorized as a separate buff. Note: The maximum number of Manuscript Pages a Unit can have is 10\.  
4\. Apply the ‘Editor’ buff for 1◈ Turns to herself, whenever she performs an Attack, apply Crit DmUp (STR) for 1◈ Turns to herself, STR Crit Damage dealt is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kuroe) There’s Enough Time for a Revision — Rank: D++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when the Unit is defeated it is revived with 150 Health. Then, apply the ‘Revision’ buff for ⅓◈ Turns to herself; when this buff naturally expires, Chloe swaps places with the furthest allied Unit from her within a 3 panel area of herself, that is not the allied Unit that this Skill applied Guts to unless that Unit is the only one available. If there are no applicable Units, this effect does not activate. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Kuroe) I’ve Received Your Final Manuscript\! — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%; then remove all Manuscripts from the affected Units and reduce their NP Cooldown by ½◈ Turn for each Manuscript removed in this way. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. 

(Kuroe) Deadline Chaser  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=3. Deals normal damage, then the DU Flips a Coin. If Heads, it is inflicted with Berserk for 1◈ Turns; if Tails, it is inflicted with Disorder (30%) for 1◈ Turns. If this Skill successfully inflicted the debuff, Chloe gains 2 Manuscript Pages for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kuroe) Monochrome Impulse: Delivering the Vibrant Black and White to You — Rank: B++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. First, restore 1 Agility for every 3 Manuscript Pages on herself and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%. Deals 3.5x damage plus 200, damage dealt is further increased by 10% (20%?) for every Manuscript Page on herself. Then, increase the DU’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; while Chloe enters Dangerous Mode for ½◈ Turns for every Manuscript Page on herself. The effects of Dangerous Mode are as follows-  
1\. Chloe gains the ‘Animalistic’ Attribute.  
2\. STR is increased by one Rank, which increases Base Attack (STR) by 25\.  
3\. Guts buff from ‘There’s Enough Time for a Revision’ revives the Unit with 300 Health instead of 150\.  
4\. All effects which grant Manuscript will grant 1 extra Manuscript.  
5\. Chloe’s Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of giving her 1 Final Manuscript at the end of the Damage Step; the effects of a Final Manuscript are the same as normal Manuscripts, except they have no duration and will not removed by ‘I’ve Received Your Final Manuscript\!’ but still count towards the number of Manuscript Pages for its second effect. Not affected by Effect 4 of Dangerous Mode.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.
