(Nito) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank D++  
(Passive) Increases all damage taken by 50 including NP. If Nitocris Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 50 including NP.

(Nito) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: A  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 75\.

(Nito) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: B  
(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 40 and NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.

(Nito) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) Nitocris’ Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 2\) At the end of any Turn she Acts, Nitocris loses 10 Health while her Master loses 5 Health, Health cannot drop below those listed values in this way.  
(Passive 3\) All damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Passive 4\) All STR damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP.

(Nito) Divinity — Rank: EX  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 60 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Nito) Funeral Records — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 15% of its maximum value at the end of her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nito) Arbitration of the Underworld Goddess — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Nitocris’ NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns for every enemy Unit within the Skill area.  
2\. Applies the ‘Malignant’ effect for 1◈ Turns to all enemy Units within the Skill area, their Alignment is changed from ‘Good’ or ‘Neutral’ to ‘Evil’; if the Unit had no Alignment it is instead granted the ‘Evil’ Alignment. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
3\. Inflicts Buff Down for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within the Skill area with the ‘Evil’ Alignment, chance of having buffs applied to them is reduced by 20%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Nefer’ effect for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself,  their Alignment is changed from ‘Evil’ or ‘Neutral’ to ‘Good’; if the Unit had no Alignment it is instead granted the ‘Good’ Alignment. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
5\. Applies Buff Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself with the ‘Good’ Alignment, chance of having buffs applied to them is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nito) Night Breeze in the Desert — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Has a 60% (80%) chance of applying Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time (1◈ Turns, 3 times) to Units with the ‘Good’ Alignment.  
3\. Has a 60% (80%) chance of applying Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time (1◈ Turns, 3 times) to Units with the ‘Good’ Alignment.  
4\. Has a 60% (80%) chance of applying Death Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time (1◈ Turns, 3 times) to Units with the ‘Good’ Alignment, the Unit is Immune to Instakill and Death.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nitocris) Neferu Suen Anubis: My Beautiful Netherworld, Open Thy Passage — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. First, apply NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 5% for every enemy Unit with the ‘Evil’ Alignment within the NP area. Deals 4x damage to all affected enemy Units, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the \[Man\] Attribute. Then, has a 50% chance of inflicting Death on the DU, this chance is 100% instead of 50% if the DU has the ‘Evil’ Alignment. Then, Nitocris enters Anubis Form. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(Nitocris) A Heart Sacrificed to Anubis  
(Passive) Nitocris gains the following effects while in Anubis Form-  
1\. Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. The effects of ‘Night Breeze in the Desert’ are upgraded to the values in this colour.  
3\. Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Death, 20% if the DU has the ‘Evil’ Alignment.  
4\. Nitocris is Immune to Instakill and Death.  
5\. While Nitocris is in Anubis Form, at the end of her Turn and at the end of any Turn she Acts, Nitocris loses 60 Health while their Master loses 35 Health. If her Master’s Health is less than 35, Anubis Form is forcefully ended. Nitocris can be defeated if her Health drops below 60 in this way. Nitocris can choose to end Anubis Form during their Turn, at the end of any Turn they Act (after their Master pays the Health cost), or at the end of a Round.
