(Ranmaru) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: D  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs including Instakill is reduced by 10%, this effect is halved for Death (5%), does not affect Erase.  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 10% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 10% including NP.

4\. Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Ranmaru) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Passive 6\) When Ranmaru receives healing due to being in her Home Base at the end of the Round, she also restores 3 Ranmanium.  
(Passive 7\) When Ranmaru receives a healing effect from a source other than her Home Base, she restores 2+1d6 Ranmanium.  
(Passive 8\) Ranmaru’s Master can spend 1 Command Spell to restore 10 Ranmanium to Ranmaru.
