(Salieri) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank: C  
(Passive) Increases all damage taken by 60 including NP. If Salieri Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 60 including NP.

(Salieri) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: B  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60\.

(Salieri) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: C  
(Passive) At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 30 and NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns. 

(Salieri) Innocent Monster — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Crit DmUp to Salieri for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, 3 times, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Salieri) Wailing Facade — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) This Skill has 2 Modes, Armored and Unarmored. During your Turn, you can swap from one mode to the other, but the Cooldown for swapping between modes is 2◈ Turns.  
Unarmored: All damage dealt is decreased by 15% including NP. Range is reduced by 1\. Innocent Monster cannot be used and has no effect.  
Armored: All damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. At the end of every Turn Salieri Acts while Armored, his Master loses 15 Health. Gains the ‘Levitating’ Attribute.  
(Passive 2\) If Salieri is Armored and Mozart is on the field, Salieri will always Move towards Mozart and Attack him during his Turn; all damage dealt to Mozart is further increased by 30% including NP. 

(Salieri) Horrible Wildfire — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Salieri. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage taken is increased by 30%. Then, has a 30% chance of inflicting Sacrifice on Units without the ‘Magic Resistance’ Skill. After that, apply the following effects to Salieri-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 200%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Salieri) Dio Santissimo Misericordia de mi: To The Great One Up In The Heavens, Please Take Pity On Me — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x5 or 5x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Salieri for 3x damage plus 200, damage dealt is further increased by 200% against Units with the \[Star\] Attribute. Then, has then following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn (A) to all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%; and Luck is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack.   
2\. All allied Units within a 3 panel area of Salieri are inflicted with Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%. For every Rank in LUC an affected Unit has, chance of being inflicted with Crit Dwn is reduced by 20%.  
3\. All allied Units who were not inflicted with Crit Dwn from Effect 2 are applied with S.Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns
