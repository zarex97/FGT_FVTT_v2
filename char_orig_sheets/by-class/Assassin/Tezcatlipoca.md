(Tezcatlipoca) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Tezcatlipoca) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Tezcatlipoca) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Tezcatlipoca) Almighty Intelligence — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) At the end of any Combat Phase Tezcatlipoca is involved in, note the enemy Unit(s) who fought him under this Skill.  
(Passive 2\) All damage dealt to Units listed under this Skill is increased by 10% including NP.  
(Passive 3\) When a Unit listed under this Skill tries to inflict a debuff on Tezcatlipoca, the chance of being inflicted by those debuffs is reduced by 10%.

(Tezcatlipoca) Titlacauan: Warrior Chief — Rank: A  
(Passive) As long as Tezcatlipoca has a Master, apply these effects to his Master-  
1\. Magnitude of all effects applied is increased by 10%.  
2\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP.  
3\. All damage taken is reduced by 10% including NP.  
4\. Chance of being Overpowered or Underpowered is reduced by 20%.

(Tezcatlipoca) Smoking Mirror  
(Passive 1\) At the start of a Combat Phase involving this Unit, this Skill has a 30% chance of applying Invuln (Nahuatl) to himself for 1 time. Not affected by effects which modify the chance of having buffs applied to him, but is affected by No Buff. This Skill can only apply Invuln (Nahuatl) once per Turn in this way. Negated if Tezcatlipoca is affected by Skill Seal.  
Invuln (Nahuatl) has the same effects as normal Invuln, but is a separate buff, and is consumed first before normal Invuln if he is hit by an Attack while he has it. Does not stack. Invuln (Nahuatl) is removed from all Units who have it at the end of every Turn.  
(Passive 2\) While in Xibalba Mode, Passive 1 of this Skill also affects allied Units within a 2 panel area around himself. Additionally, it can also successfully activate up to 3 times per Turn; but can only apply Invuln (Nahuatl) to the same Unit once per Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Nahuatl’ buff for 1◈ Turns, chance of this Skill’s Passive effect activating is increased to 100%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tezcatlipoca) Charisma of Conflict — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all Units with the ‘Guts’ buff or any other revival effects (including Passive revival effects), all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tezcatlipoca) Black Sun — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce his NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns.  
2\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 2 times to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself.  
4\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting No Buff (Itztli) for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself- When Invuln is applied to this Unit, it fails to be applied; Invuln (Nahuatl) is not affected.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tezcatlipoca) Tepeyollotl: Heart of the Mountains — Rank: A

2\. Restore 3 Luck.

(Tezcatlipoca) Ce acatl  
Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals 50% damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Confuse for 1◈ Turns. Wind damage. Then, if this Spell was not Evaded, Tezcatlipoca can Move to a panel directly next to the DU (within his MOV) and perform a Normal Attack on it; damage from this Attack will not remove Confuse inflicted by this Spell. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns; 3◈ Turns if a Normal Attack was used after the Spell.  
Note: This Spell can only inflict ‘Obsidized’ once per usage (see below). If the Spell inflicted it, then the follow-up Normal Attack cannot inflict it; if the Spell did not inflict it, then the follow-up Normal Attack can inflict it.

(Tezcatlipoca) Toxcatl  
Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for normal damage and inflicts Blind for 1◈ Turns. Dark damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Tezcatlipoca) Xipe Totec  
Attack Skill. Deals normal damage and inflicts Bleed for 1◈ Turns. If Bleed was successfully inflicted, then has a 50% chance of inflicting Immobilize/Disable for 1◈ Turns (Flip a Coin, Heads=Disable, Tails=Immobilize). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Tezcatlipoca) The First Sun: Xibalba — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Tezcatlipoca enters Xibalba Mode for 3◈ Turns (if he is already in Xibalba Mode, its duration is extended instead). First, apply Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns and apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, magnitude of NP DmUp is as follows-  
1\. Increased by 5% for every Unit listed under ‘Almighty Intelligence’ (max increase \= 40%).  
2\. Increased by 10% if used during a ‘Night’ Round or when within a ‘Darkness’ area.  
Hits all enemy Units within a 9x9 panel area (11x11 on Large Board?) around himself for 3.5x damage. Then, reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within the NP area by 1 Turn (⅓◈ Turns?) for every enemy Unit affected by this NP.   
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Tezcatlipoca gains the following effects while in Xibalba Mode-  
1\. Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and 20% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 125+40=165), not affected by Magic Resistance.  
2\. Activation chance of ‘Smoking Mirror’s Passive effect is increased by 40% and Passive 2 of ‘Smoking Mirror’ becomes active.  
3\. Maximum Cooldown of all Attack Skills & Damage Spells are reduced by 1◈ Turns; upon transforming into Xibalba Mode, reduce the Cooldown of all Attack Skills & Damage Spells by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Upon performing a successful Normal Attack, Tezcatlipoca has a 40% chance of inflicting the ‘Obsidized’ Volatile debuff on the DU; upon performing a successful Attack Skill or Damage Spell, he has an 80% chance of inflicting it instead.  
5\. Presence Concealment cannot be used.

The ‘Obsidized’ Volatile debuff has 3 Stages and its effects for each Stage of it are as follows-  
Stage 1: MOV is reduced by 1\.  
Stage 2: All damage dealt is reduced by 15% including NP. (Note: The effect of Stage 1 Obsidized is still present\!)  
Stage 3: When Stage 3 Obsidized is inflicted, Obsidized is removed from the affected Unit, who is then inflicted with Crystallize for 1◈ Turns. Crystallize inflicted in this way ignores Debuff Resist & Debuff Immune effects.  
A Unit with Crystallize is Immune to Obsidized.
