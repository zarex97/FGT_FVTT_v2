(Locusta) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Locusta) Mental Pollution (Poison) — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) Chance of inflicting Poison and Deadly Poison is increased by 20%.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 10%.

(Locusta) Poison Compounding — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Inflicts Poison on all affected enemy Units, with a 30% chance of inflicting an additional Stage of Poison. Then, has the following effects on herself-  
1\. Restore 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Locusta’ buff for 1◈ Turns, when Locusta performs a successful Attack on a Unit inflicted with Poison, her NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.    
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Locusta) Robust Health (Poison) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Locusta is defeated she is revived with 20% of her Max Health.  
2\. Applies Dmg Cut for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 50 including NP.  
3\. Applies PoisHeal for 2◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Locusta) Cooking (Mushrooms) — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Reduce their NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns. Then, has a 20% chance of inflicting Poison on these affected Units; Units who are inflicted with Poison by this effect have their NP Cooldown further reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. Has a 20% chance of applying PoisHeal for 1◈ Turns to all other allied Units inflicted with Poison due to Effect 1\.  
3\. Applies the ‘Kinoko’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 3 times to herself, Normal Attacks inflict Poison.  
4\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Poison on herself.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Locusta) Finis Fungus: When It Comes to Sacred Offerings, it’s Mushrooms — Rank: C+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=3. First, apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt is increased by X%; if NP, (X-10)%, X starts at 10 and increases by 5 for every Stage of Poison on Locusta (max increase=30). Then, inflict Poison on an enemy Unit within Range and deal 3x damage plus 100, damage dealt is further increased by 15% for every Stage of Poison on the DU. Next, inflict Deadly Poison for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Poison Damage taken is doubled. Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.  

(Locusta) Finis Venenum Nero: Farewell, My Beloved Emperor — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Range=1. Can only be used on Units named ‘Nero Claudius’. If this NP is not successfully Evaded, inflict Sleep (Farewell) on the DU until the end of the Round and Poison (Venenum). Both debuffs ignore Debuff Resist/Debuff Immune effects and are Unremovable. When this NP is used, her Master’s Health is reduced as if a Rank E NP was used. Once this NP is used successfully, it can no longer be used for the rest of the game. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Sleep (Farewell) has the same effects as normal Sleep debuff, but replaces it if the affected Unit already has Sleep, Sleep (Farewell) is not removed even from taking damage.  
Poison (Venemum) is separate from the normal Poison debuff, Poison (Venenum) is treated as Stage 20 Poison.
