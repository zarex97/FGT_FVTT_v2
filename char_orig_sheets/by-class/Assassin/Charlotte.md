(Charlotte) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 40% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Damage Spells.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Charlotte) Angelic Egg  
Charlotte is accompanied by the ‘Angelic Egg’ Unit. The Angelic Egg has 15 Agility and a MOV of 5\. During Charlotte’s Turn or at the end of any Turn she Acts, she can Move the Angelic Egg.  
Charlotte’s usual Range is 1 panel, 1 target. However, when the Angelic Egg is on a panel directly next to Charlotte, her Range is increased by 2 panels.  
During Charlotte’s Turn, she can teleport/instantly Move to the panel occupied by her Angelic Egg and perform her Normal Attack for the Turn. This teleport can be used even if Charlotte has already Moved normally during her Turn.  
An enemy Unit can Attack the Angelic Egg; if the Attack was successful, the Angelic Egg immediately returns to Charlotte’s side (a panel directly next to her); and cannot Move on its own for 1◈ Turns (only Moves together with Charlotte, remaining in the same orientation).

(Charlotte) Angel of Assassination — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Death ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting Instakill and Death is increased by 75%; and applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 2 times. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Charlotte) Determination of Steel — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Charlotte has to perform an Injury Roll from receiving damage, roll a twenty-sided die. Charlotte only has to roll for Injury if a 1 to 9 is rolled (45% chance).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Pierce for 1◈ Turns, and Atk Up for 1 time, damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Charlotte) Ludicrous Planning — Rank: D+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Roll a ten-sided die. Apply the following effect(s) to Charlotte depending on the number rolled-  
1 to 3: Restore 3 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
4 to 6: Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
7 to 9: Apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
10: Apply all three of the above effects.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Charlotte) Divine Providence (Assassination) — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Charlotte performs a successful Normal Attack Crit which deals STR Damage, gain 1 Providence Token (max. 3).  
(Passive 2\) When Charlotte uses Ludicrous Planning, instead of rolling a die, she can instead remove 1 to 3 Providence Tokens from herself and choose that number of effects to activate (the same effect can only be chosen once).  
(Passive 3\) When Ludicrous Planning is used by removing Providence Tokens, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 5% plus 5% for every Providence Token removed in this way. 

(Charlotte) Angel Ray  
Attack Skill. Can only be used when Angelic Egg is directly next to Charlotte. Range=3. Deals 25% extra damage that uses Base Attack (MAG). Unaffected by effects that modify Crit Chance and Crit Damage dealt. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.

(Charlotte) Le Rêve Ensoleillé: Love to the Homeland, Like Drowning in a Dream — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=1. This NP has the Pierce effect. Deals 3x damage that has the Ignore Def effect and has a 75% chance of inflicting Instakill at the end of the Damage Step. Additionally, this NP has the Aim effect when used on Units with the ‘Instinct’ or ‘Eye of the Mind’ Skills (regardless of whether the Skill’s effects are Active or not). Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
