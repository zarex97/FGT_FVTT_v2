(Cleopatra) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Damage Spells.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Cleopatra) Divinity — Rank: D  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 20 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Cleopatra) Imperial Privilege — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores her Health by 30% of its maximum value.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Cleopatra) Golden Rule (Wealth and Body) — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of the Round (2 Turns if ◈ is greater than 4). Negated if this Unit has the NP Regen (Isis) buff.  
(Passive 2\) Cleopatra’s Health is restored by 50 at the end of her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, & at the end of the Round. Negated if this Unit has the Regen (Isis) buff.

(Cleopatra) Akin to Isis — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn, has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the ‘Isis’ buff for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn when performing a successful Normal Attack, reduced by 1 additional Turn if the Attack dealt damage, and reduced by 1 more additional Turn if the Attack was a Crit. If ◈ is greater than 4, 1 Turn is replaced with 2 Turns.  
2\. Applies NP Regen (Isis) for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of each Turn.  
3\. Applies Regen (Isis) for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value at the end of her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
4\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
5\. Applies Buff ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of applying buffs is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Cleopatra) Protection of the Goddess — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns.   
2\. Removes all debuffs from Cleopatra.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

After that, reduce Cleopatra’s Health by 10% of its maximum value (Cleopatra’s Health cannot drop below 1 in this way). Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns
