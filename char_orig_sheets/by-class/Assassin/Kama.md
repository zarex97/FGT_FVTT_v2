(Kama) The One Without Body  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Kama can switch between her ‘Child Form’ and ‘Adult Form’. Can only be used once per Turn. She gains different effects depending on which Form she is in, as follows-  
1\. Child Form: Has the ‘Child’ Attribute. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2\.  
2\. Adult Form: All damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Trigger) When Kama is summoned, Flip a Coin to determine which Form she is summoned in (Heads=Child, Tails=Adult).

(Kama) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kama) Class Skill: Independent Manifestation — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive 4\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs, Instakill, Death, and Erase is reduced by 60%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Independent Action — Rank: C’.

(Kama) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can be used in two different ways-  
1\. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.   
2\. Only usable when Kama is directly next to her Master. Restores the Health of both Kama and her Master by 25% of their respective maximum values, and both their Luck by 2\. Neither Kama or her Master can Attack on the Turn this effect is used; and cannot Move after this effect is used. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. 

(Kama) God of Love’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive) Kama is Immune to Charm.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Kama) Blessing of the Goddess — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Kama. Has the following effects-  
1\. Permanently reduce the affected Unit’s Max Health by 100 (cannot drop below 100 in this way). If used on herself, Max Health is only reduced by 50 (cannot drop below 50 in this way).  
2\. Applies Overcharge (Kama) for 1◈ Turns, 1 times, the magnitude & duration of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 50%.  
3\. Kama’s Health is restored by 25% of its maximum value.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kama) Ananga Ranga — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Guts to herself, when Kama is defeated she is revived with ⅙ of her Max Health; and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) If this Skill is not on Cooldown, it has a 10% chance of automatically activating when Kama is defeated. If this Skill is on Cooldown, has a 5% chance of automatically activating when Kama is defeated, then the Skill’s Cooldown is reset.

(Kama) Mara-Papiyas — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈+½◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the ‘Papiyas’ buff for 1◈ Turns; all damage dealt to Alter-Ego Class Servants and Units with the Alter-Ego Skill is increased by 50% including NP; and all damage taken from Alter-Ego Class Servants and Units with the Alter-Ego Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.  
3\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
4\. Inflicts Charm ResDwn for 1◈ Turns to all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of Kama, chance of being inflicted with Charm is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kama) Aag Shiva  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals normal damage to an enemy Unit within Range and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. Cannot be used in Mara Form.

(Kama) Kama Sammohana: A Withering Affection is not Love — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Restores Kama’s Agility by 3 and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Then, deals 3x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 100% against Units inflicted with Charm. Finally, has an 80% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns on the DU, or extends the duration of Charm if the DU is already affected by it. Damage dealt by this NP will not remove Charm from DUs. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Kama) Burning Love  
(Passive 1\) Kama gains Love Counters when performing the following Actions-  
1\. Performing a successful Normal Attack, Evade, or Luck Check: \+1  
2\. Using a Skill except Class Skills & ‘The One Without Body’: \+2  
3\. Performing a successful NP: \+3  
4\. Performing any of the above Actions while within a 2 panel area of her Master: \+1  
5\. Successfully inflicting Charm: \+3 per Unit inflicted  
6\. Using Effect 2 of Riding: \+5  
7\. Master uses a Skill on her: \+2  
8\. Master uses a Command Spell on her: \+3  
9\. Kama performs Shoves her Master out of a NP area or performs a Cover for her Master: \+4  
The maximum number of Love Counters is 30\.  
(Passive 2\) For every 10 Love Counters on Kama, Normal Attacks deal 30% extra damage using her Base Attack (MAG). Has no effect while in Mara Form.  
(Trigger) When Kama uses her NP while she has 30 Love Counters, she automatically changes into Mara Form at the start of the Combat Phase and uses her new NP instead.  
(Passive 3\) Kama gains the following effects while in Mara Form-  
1\. Range is reduced by 1\.  
2\. At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and half of Base Attack (MAG) combined, not affected by Magic Resistance, and have a 50% chance of inflicting Burn (Shiva). Deals Fire damage (half).  
3\. At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG), the effect of Magic Resistance is halved, and have a 50% chance of inflicting Burn (Shiva). Deals Fire damage.  
4\. Kama Sammohana can no longer be used, and changes into Saṃsāra Kama instead.  
Note: Burn (Shiva) is the same as regular Burn, but is Unremovable. It does not stack with regular Burn.

(Kama) Saṃsāra Kama: To Incinerate Affection in the Name of Love — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range+3 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, has an 80% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns on the DU, or extends the duration of Charm if the DU is already affected by it. Next, restores Kama’s Agility by 4 and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. Then, deals 3.5x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units inflicted with Charm. Damage dealt by this NP will not remove Charm from DUs. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
