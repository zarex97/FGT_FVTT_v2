(OldManLi) Veteran — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 10% including Noble Phantasm.

(OldManLi) Chinese Martial Art: Bajiquan — Rank: A+++  
(Passive) Li’s Normal Attacks have a 50% chance of having the Pierce effect.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Damage Step where Li is the AU. Applies Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(OldManLi) Sphere Boundary \- Zenith — Rank: EX  
(Active) Can be used in two ways. Only one effect of Sphere Boundary can be active at a time-  
1\. Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Sphere (Zenith)’ effect for 2◈ Turns, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff and has the same effect as Presence Concealment — Rank: A-. This effect cannot be used if Li is in Yin Mode (see Intersection of Yin-Yang). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns after the ‘Sphere (Zenith)’ effect expires. Cannot be used while Li has the Dodge (Zenith) or Crit Up (Zenith) buffs.  
2\. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Applies Dodge (Zenith) for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Crit Up (Zenith) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Cannot be used while Li has the ‘Sphere (Zenith)’ effect.  
(Passive 1\) Whenever the Active effect of this Skill is used, apply the following effects-  
1\. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
2\. Li’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up \[Man\] for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Man\] Attribute is increased by 30%.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Li performs a successful Normal Attack while he has a ‘Zenith’ buff or effect, reduce the Cooldown of ‘Sphere Boundary \- Zenith’ by ½◈ Turns.

(Presence Concealment — Rank: A-)  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current Agility is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt ~~to Servants~~ is increased by 100% (doubled); if NP, 50%. Does not apply against Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 15% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(OldManLi) Intersection of Yin-Yang — Rank: B  
(Passive) When Li is summoned, he starts the game in Yang Mode. When this Skill is used (see Active), he switches from Yang Mode to Yin Mode (and vice versa). In Yin Mode, all damage dealt is increased by 20% including NP and all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When used, Li switches between his Yang Mode and Yin Mode. After switching between either Mode, Atk Up is applied for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%; and Debuff Immune (YinYang) is applied for ⅓◈ Turns (also affects Instakill, but does not affect Death & Erase). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

Then deals 3x (5x?) damage with a 100% chance of inflicting Instakill (150% if Li is in Yin Mode). Cooldown: 5◈ Turns. When used, Li’s Master’s Health is reduced as if a Rank C (EX?) NP was used.

Deals 4x damage that has the Ignore Def effect and Break (+30%dm) effect, then has a 50% chance of inflicting Instakill. After that, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns. When used, Li’s Master’s Health is reduced as if a Rank A NP was used.
