(Erik) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Erik) The Masque of the Red Death — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Erik gains 7 Masque Tokens. This effect is not activated if Erik has any Masque Tokens.  
2\. If this Skill is used while Erik is within an ‘Indoors’ area, this Skill gains the following additional effect- That ‘Indoors’ area becomes a ‘Darkness (Prospero)’ area. Darkness (Prospero) Terrain has the same effect as normal Indoors and Darkness Terrain, but enemy Units cannot leave a ‘Darkness (Prospero)’ area. Darkness (Prospero) Terrain is Unremovable; if Erik is defeated or has no Masque Tokens remaining, all Darkness (Prospero) areas are immediately removed from the board.  
3\. Restore 2 Luck to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to those Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. If Erik is within a ‘Darkness’ area, apply NP DmUp (Masque) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%; if an affected Unit is within a ‘Darkness’ area, NP Damage dealt is increased by 40% instead.  
5\. Apply Debuff ChUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 25%.  
6\. Apply Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Erik gains the following effects as long as he has any Masque Tokens-  
1\. Whenever Erik is Attacked, at the end of the Combat Process, inflict Bleed (Masque) for 1◈ Turns on the AU. Bleed (Masque) has the same effects as normal Bleed, but it is a Mental Debuff and if reapplied, extend its duration. Note: It is treated as a separate debuff from normal Bleed.  
2\. Evade Rolls are reduced by X, X=the number of Masque Tokens on Erik.  
3\. If a buff removing effect is applied to Erik, remove half of his Masque Tokens, rounded down.
