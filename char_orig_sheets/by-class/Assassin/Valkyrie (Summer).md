(S.Valkyrie) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(S.Valkyrie) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(S.Valkyrie) Divinity — Rank: A

(S.Valkyrie) Valkyrie Style Team Combat — Rank: B+  
(Passive) For every other ‘Valkyrie’ Unit within a 2 panel area of herself, Normal Attacks use 10% (20%?) of Base Attack (MAG) combined with Base Attack (STR), not affected by Magic Resistance. Note: Brynhildr is categorized as a ‘Valkyrie’ Unit.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-

2\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all affected ‘Valkyrie’ Units, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(S.Valkyrie) Warmaiden’s Troth — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. Health is restored by 30% of its maximum value,  
3\. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
If this Skill was used on an allied Valkyrie Unit other than herself, apply Effects 1 and 2 to herself as well.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(S.Valkyrie) Fylgja Valkyrie: Last Assault \- Heavenly Spears Halo — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Range+2 for the Combat Process. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. First, roll a six-sided die for each DU within the NP area, each DU takes 15% damage, X times, Base Attack (STR) is used. X=1 \+ the number rolled on the six-sided die \+ 1 more for every other allied ‘Valkyrie (Summer)’ within a 2 panel area of herself.   
Then, deal 3.5x damage using Base Attack (MAG), damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the \[Earth\] Attribute. Has a 40% chance of inflicting Instakill on Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute.  
If the DU Blocks the first part of the NP, damage is reduced from the Total damage received in the first part and the DU also has to Block the second part. If the DU Evades, both parts of the NP have to be Evaded separately; and if the DU fails to Evade the first part, they are unable to Evade the second part.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
