(Carmilla) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 60% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Damage Spells.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Carmilla) Bloodstained Countess  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Carmilla successfully deals damage to a Unit with a Normal Attack, restore her Health by 15% of the damage dealt.  
(Passive 2\) All damage dealt to Units named ‘Elizabeth Bathory’ is increased by 10% including NP.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever ‘Blood Bath’ is used, gain 3 Bloodbath Counters. When performing a successful Attack, inflict Bleed for 1◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step, then remove 1 Bloodbath Counter from herself if Bleed was successfully inflicted.

(Carmilla) Torture Technique — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts Def Dwn (Torture) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Carmilla Attacks a Unit inflicted with Def Dwn (Torture), damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.

(Carmilla) Bloodsucking — Rank: C  
(Active) Range=1. Used on an enemy Unit. Has a 60% chance to increasing its NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Then, reduce Carmilla’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; if it failed to increase the DU’s NP Cooldown, ⅓◈ Turns instead. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Carmilla) Blood Bath — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, reduces Crit Chance by 25%. Then, applies Area CritUp to Carmilla for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Carmilla) Phantom Maiden: Phantasmal Iron Maiden — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3x damage. If the DU is Female, damage dealt is further increased by 200%. Then, restores Carmilla’s Health by 20% of its maximum value and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.   
1\. If the DU was inflicted with Def Dwn (Torture), reduce the Cooldown of ‘Torture Technique’ by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. If the DU was inflicted with Bleed, extend its duration of Bleed by 1◈ Turns and reduce the Cooldown of Carmilla’s ‘Blood Bath’ Skill by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
