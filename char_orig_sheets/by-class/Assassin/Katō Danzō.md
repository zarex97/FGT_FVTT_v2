(Danzō) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Danzō) Karakuri Genpō: Kawarimi  
(Active) Mechanical Illusion: Substitution. Used at the start of a Combat Phase when Danzō or an other allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Danzō is Attacked.  
1\. If Danzō is the one being Attacked, she can swap herself with any other Unit within a 2 panel area of herself except the AU. The Combat Process continues as normal with the swapped in Unit as the new DU.  
2\. If the other allied Unit is the one being Attacked, she can swap herself with that allied Unit. The Combat Process continues as normal with Danzō as the new DU.  
Cannot be used if Fubatsu and/or Kirihanasu is on Cooldown. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Danzō) Karakuri Genpō: Kirihanasu  
(Active) Mechanical Illusion: Detachment. Used when Danzō is Attacked and receives fatal damage without being revived (i.e. Health would be reduced to 0). First, the Player privately informs the GM that it wants to use this Skill. Instead of taking damage, the opposing Player is shown and informed that Danzō has been defeated; meanwhile Danzō’s Presence Concealment is Activated (regardless of its Cooldown) and is Moved to another panel on the board as per her MOV.  
From this point on, an invisible (Presence Concealed) Danzō is being secretly controlled by her Player by informing the GM of her Moves; she does not apply to the number of Units that Move during a Turn while the effect of this Skill is Active (Note: Her Move is still performed during her Turn, not the GM’s Turn). The Presence Concealment activated by Kirihanasu is Active until Danzō Attacks, and her survived existence is revealed to all other Players at this point.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns, and Danzō’s Health must have been restored back to above 75% of its maximum value since using Kirihanasu.
