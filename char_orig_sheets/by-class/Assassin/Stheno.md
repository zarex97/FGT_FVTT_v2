(Stheno) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 5% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Stheno) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Stheno) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 120 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Stheno) Bloodsucking — Rank: C   
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has a 60% chance of increasing its NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, 80% if used at a Range of 1\. Then, reduce Stheno’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; if it failed to increase the DU’s NP Cooldown, ⅓◈ Turns. The magnitude of this Skill is doubled if the DU is (any version of) Medusa & success rate is fixed to 100%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Stheno) Alluring Euphony — Rank: A  
(Passive) Stheno can choose whether to use Base Attack (STR) or Base Attack (MAG) when performing Normal Attacks. For Normal Attacks which use Base Attack (STR), Crit Chance is increased by 25%, and Crit Damage dealt is increased by 25%. This effect is negated if Stheno is inflicted with Silence.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has a 100% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns. If the DU is Female, the chance of being Charmed is reduced by 50%. For every Rank in Magic Resistance the DU has, the chance of being Charmed is reduced by 10% (e.g. Rank D=2 Ranks=20% reduction). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Like a Silver Bell.

(Stheno) Like a Silver Bell — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Can be used on either an enemy or an allied Unit within Range. If used on an enemy Unit-  
1\. Inflicts Charm for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Agility Boost for 1◈ Turns, 2 times to a random other allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself.   
3\. Applies Luck Boost for 1◈ Turns, 2 times to a random other allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself.  
4\. Applies Dmg Boost for 1◈ Turns, 2 times to a random other allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP.  
If used on an allied Unit, the allied Unit cannot be Euryale or have any effects which specifically resist Charm; and cannot be used if there is a Unit on the field already inflicted with Charm (Stheno). Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Charm (Stheno). Has the same effect as Normal Charm, but ignores Debuff Resist and Immune effects and is Unremovable except by its own effect. As long as the Unit with Charm (Stheno) is within a 2 panel area of the Stheno, they are treated as having a ‘Servant-Master relationship’ in Combat situations (see **Rules – Relationships** in the Rulebook). (Servant=Unit with Charm (Stheno), Master=Stheno). A Unit inflicted with Charm (Stheno) is Immune to other forms of Charm.  
2\. Applies an Unremovable Agility Boost (Stheno) to the Unit inflicted with Charm (Stheno). Agility Boost (Stheno) is removed when Charm (Stheno) is removed from the affected Unit.  
3\. Applies an Unremovable Luck Boost (Stheno) to the Unit inflicted with Charm (Stheno). Luck Boost (Stheno) is removed when Charm (Stheno) is removed from the affected Unit.  
4\. Applies an Unremovable Damage Boost (Stheno) to the Unit inflicted with Charm (Stheno), all damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. Damage Boost (Stheno) is removed when Charm (Stheno) is removed from the affected Unit.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Alluring Euphony. 

(Stheno) Goddess’ Whim — Rank: A  
Used during your Turn. First, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Stheno, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. This effect’s magnitude is doubled on Units with Divinity. Then, roll 2 eight-sided dice and apply the effects as rolled (duplicate die are rerolled)-  
1\. Applies Def Up to all Units affected by this Skill, all damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Reduce Stheno’s Skill and NP Cooldowns by 1◈ Turns  
3\. Applies Regen to herself for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value at the end of her Turn, at the end of every Turn Stheno Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
4\. Applies Dodge (Whim) to her Master for 1◈ Turns; MOV Up (Whim) for 1◈ Turns, MOV is doubled; and apply Atk Up (Whim) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%; these effects are applied regardless of distance between Master and herself. As long as the Master has a ‘Whim’ buff applied to it, it cannot be Overpowered or Underpowered.  
5\. Applies Debuff ChUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%.  
6\. Inflict Decoy on her Master for 1◈ Turns, regardless of distance between Master and herself.  
7\. Atk Dwn is inflicted on all affected allied Units instead of the original Atk Up (same magnitude and duration) excluding herself(?).  
8\. Inflicts Sleep on herself until the end of this Turn that ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects, and cannot be removed by debuff removal effects (can be removed by taking damage).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Stheno) Smile of the Stheno: Goddess’ Smile — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 150% chance of inflicting Instakill if the DU is Male.  
2\. Has a 150% chance of inflicting Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
3\. Removes all buffs from the DU.   
4\. Has a 200% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns if the DU is Male (and he survived the Instakill debuff).  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
