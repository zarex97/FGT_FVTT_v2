(1stFBIDir) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2 (4).  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 60% (10%) chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Damage Spells.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated. (2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.)

(1stFBIDir) Espionage — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When used, gain the ‘Espionage’ effect for 1◈ Turns- 1st FBI Director’s Presence Concealment Rank is increased from D to A (modified effects are blue text in parentheses). Also applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. The ‘Espionage’ effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable.

(1stFBIDir) Filing — Rank: A  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Whenever 1st FBI Director engages in Combat with another Servant for the first time, that DU is Filed under this Skill at the end of the Combat Phase after Passive Effect 2 of this Skill activates (i.e. make a note).  
2\. Every time 1st FBI Director engages in Combat with another Servant, increase his Max & current Luck by 1 at the end of the Combat Phase. His Max Luck cannot be increased by more than 8 points in this way. If the DU is a ‘Filed’ Servant, then chance of this effect activating is reduced by 50%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%; if the DU is a ‘Filed’ Servant, apply as ‘all damage received is increased by 40%’ instead. Then, if used at the start of a Combat Phase, apply N.Atk Up to himself for that Combat Phase, Normal Attack damage dealt is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(1stFBIDir) Coercion — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Skill is used, 1st FBI Director is able to force the DU to give full information of one of its Skills other than Class Skills.  
2\. Then inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%; if the DU is a ‘Filed’ Servant, apply as ‘all damage dealt is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%’ instead.  
3\. Inflicts Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. If this Skill is used on a Servant that is not ‘Filed’ yet, then that Servant becomes ‘Filed’.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

1\. When the Unit is first inflicted with the ‘Classified’ debuff, all of its information (from its Notebook page) is immediately shared to all Units within a 2 panel area of itself, those Units are then applied with the ‘Confidential: (insert name of Servant inflicted with Classified here)’ Unremovable buff.

3\. When a Unit with the ‘Confidential’ (X) buff is Attacked by ‘X’ Servant, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns if ‘Classfied’ was successfully inflicted; 3◈ Turns if it was not.
