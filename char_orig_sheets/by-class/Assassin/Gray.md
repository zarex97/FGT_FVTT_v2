2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current Agility is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt ~~to Servants~~ is increased by 100% (doubled); if NP, 50%. Does not apply against Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has an 80% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Note: This Skill is not affected by Skill Seal.

(Gray) Rhongomyniad: The Spear That Shines To The Ends Of The World — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Fortress\]  
Can only be used while Gray has the ‘Logos’ effect. Applies Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns to herself. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x6 or 6x1 panel area in any direction next to Gray (if diagonal, 1x5 or 5x1). Damage dealt depends on whether how many of the following criteria are met, as follows-  
1\. At least two DUs have at least 3 Parameters respectively that are equal to or higher than Gray’s. *“The enemy must be more powerful than oneself.”*  
2\. There is only one DU in the NP area. *“The battle must be one-on-one.”*  
3\. At least two DUs have the ‘Evil’ Alignment. *“The battle must be one against evil.”*  
4\. Gray’s Health is less than at least two of the DUs. *“The battle must be to live.”*  
5\. None of the DUs have at least 3 Parameters respectively that are two Ranks below Gray’s. *“The battle must be an honorable one.”*  
6\. None of the DUs have the ‘Elemental’ Attribute. *“The enemy must not be an Elemental.”*  
If none of the criteria are met, Rhongomyniad deals 3x damage plus 100\. For every criteria that is met, damage dealt is increased by 0.2x (e.g. 4 criteria met, damage=3.8x).  
Note: Criteria 1, 3, 4, and 5 are also accounted for when Criteria 2 is fulfilled, in this case the single DU needs to meet the conditions of the Criteria for them to be fulfilled.  
When Rhongomyniad is used and none of the criteria are met, Gray’s Master loses 30 Health as the cost for this NP. For every criteria that is met, his Master loses an additional 10 Health.  
Then, all affected enemy Units are inflicted with ~~Def Dwn (B)~~ NP Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all NP damage received is increased by ~~20%; if NP,~~ 30%; and inflicted with Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%, and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack. Then, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn for each Criteria that is met and gain 2 Mythos Counters for every Unit hit by this NP.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns if no criteria are met, increases by ½◈ Turns for every criteria that is met.

4\. This debuff is Unremovable except by an effect from a Rank EX Skill or NP.
