(Sanson) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 60% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Sanson) Executioner — Rank: A++  
(Passive) All Attacks including NP deal 60 extra damage to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment. This effect is negated while Sanson has a Dmg Up (Evil) buff.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Dmg Up (Evil) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment is increased by 60%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Sanson) Medical Techniques — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 1 panel area of Sanson. Restores its Health by 30% of its maximum value, then removes 3 debuffs from it. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Sanson) Human Anatomy Study — Rank: B  
(Passive) All Attacks including NP deal 40 extra damage to Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute. This effect is negated while Sanson has a Dmg Up (Human) buff.

(Sanson) Lifeline Severance — Rank: A-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Instakill ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting Instakill is increased by 15%.  
2\. Applies Instakill ChUp (Human) for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting Instakill on Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute is increased by 15%  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Human) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute is increased by 60%.   
4\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
5\. If there is an enemy Unit with the ‘Human’ Attribute within a 2 panel area of Sanson, his NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.

If the DU, as part of its history or legend, has been executed/gone through an execution before, the chance of Instakill being inflicted is 80% instead. If Instakill fails to be inflicted, this NP deals 4x damage plus 100, then Def Down is inflicted for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
