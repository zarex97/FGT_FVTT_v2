(Kashin) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Kashin) Class Skill: Karakuri Construction — Rank: EX  
This Skill affects all allied Units with the ‘Mechanical’ Attribute within a 2 panel area of this Unit. The magnitude of this Skill is reduced for herself (use the values in this colour).  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 100% (50%); this effect is halved for Instakill (50% | N/A), and further halved for Death (25% | N/A).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 100% (50%); this effect is halved for Instakill (50% | 30%), and further halved for Death (25% | 10%).  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Item Construction’.

(Kashin) Saikikei Kashin Koji・Jorougumo  
(Passive) Kashin Koji Final Model: Jorougumo. Has the following effects-

4\. Minimum damage required for Kashin to perform an Injury Roll is 150\.
