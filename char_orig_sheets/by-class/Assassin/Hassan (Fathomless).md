(Hassan) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 75% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 6\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 0% chance of being discovered. Additionally, any other types of effects which can Detect/Discover a Unit are negated against Hassan.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.  
(Passive) As long as Hassan is within a ‘Darkness’ area or his Home Base, Presence Concealment is constantly Active (even if it was on Cooldown, in this case the Cooldown is paused). If Hassan performs an Attack or is hit by an AoE Attack, Presence Concealment is only deactivated until the end of this Turn. Using an Active Skill which targets/affects an enemy Unit will not deactivate Presence Concealment. While Hassan’s Presence Concealment is deactivated, its Rank is reduced to A+.

(Passive 2\) While Hassan is within a ‘Darkness’ area, when performing a Normal Attack, Hassan can Attack the DU from any direction (Note: The DU does not need to be within a ‘Darkness’ area for this effect).

The affected area becomes ‘Darkness’ Terrain for 1◈ Turns. Then, applies the ‘Zilaal’ buff for 1◈ Turns to himself, his Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) for damage calculation. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Hassan) Zabaniya: Meditative Sensitivity — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Can only be used when one of the following conditions are fulfilled-  
1\. When Hassan is defeated (without being revived), this NP is automatically used at the end of the Combat Process and targets the Unit who defeated him.  
2\. After Hassan’s Master has been defeated, Hassan can use this NP normally (Range=3, used on an enemy Unit within Range); this condition becomes unfulfilled once more if he gets a new Master. Note: If Hassan does not have a Master to begin with, this condition is not fulfilled (unless he gets a Master later).  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. This NP ignores all damage-reducing effects on the DU except Invuln, Shield, Anti-Purge, and effects which specifically reduce MAG damage taken/prevent MAG damage. First, inflict Death on the targeted Unit, then hits a 7x7 panel area around the target for 5x damage. Dark damage. Then, inflict the ‘Fathoms’ debuff for 3◈ Turns on all affected Units and the NP area becomes ‘~~Fathomless~~ Abyss’ Terrain for 3◈ Turns. After using this NP, Hassan is defeated and cannot be revived through any method. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.  
The ‘Fathoms’ debuff has the following effects-  
1\. All Light damage dealt by this Unit is reduced to 0 including NP.  
2\. If this Unit has the ‘Divine’ Attribute, negate all Passive effects of this Unit’s Skills & NP.

Items held:

‘Abyss’ Terrain has the following effects-  
1\. ‘Abyss’ Terrain is treated as ‘Darkness’ Terrain.  
2\. All Light damage taken by Units within ‘Fathomless’ Terrain is reduced to 0; while all Dark damage taken is increased by 50%. Also affects NP.  
3\. All damage taken by Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is increased by 50%, and all damage dealt by those Units is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.
