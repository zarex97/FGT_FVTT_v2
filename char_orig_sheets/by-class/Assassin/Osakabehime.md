(Osakabehime) Class Skill: Presence Concealment (Shade) — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.  
(Passive) Osakabehime’s Normal Attacks when Presence Concealment is Active use Base Attack (STR), are Guaranteed Crits, and have a Range of 1 panel.

(Osakabehime) Territory Creation — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (5d10+5) including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+22) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Osakabehime) Divinity  — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Osakabehime) Shapeshift — Rank: A+  
(Passive) All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.

(Osakabehime) Four Gods Divination (Hakuro) — Rank: A

2\. Applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%.  
4\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Osakabehime, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Osakabehime) Chiyogami Manipulation — Rank: EX  
Only one of the below Passive effects can be active at any time. During your Turn, you can swap from one Passive effect to the other, but the Cooldown for swapping said Passive effects is 2◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Damage dealt by Normal Attacks is increased by 20%.  
(Passive 2\) All damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
3\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP are increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Osakabehime) Chiyogami Manipulation: Hozo  
Spell. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase where Osakabehime is Attacked. Places a ‘Dovetail Figurine’ within a 4 panel area of herself. The Dovetail Figurine cannot Move or Attack, and has 10 Health with a permanent Decoy debuff.  
If used when Osakabehime is Attacked, she is replaced with a Dovetail Figurine, then she is able to Move by a distance up to her MOV. The AU’s Attack target is changed to a newly spawned Dovetail Figurine.  
The maximum number of Dovetail Figurines on the field at any time is 5\.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Osakabehime) Chiyogami Manipulation: Koumori  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Increases Osakabehime’s MOV by 3 for the Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
This MOV Up effect is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Osakabehime) Chiyogami Manipulation: Tsuru  
Damage Spell. Deals normal damage to the DU, the effect of Magic Resistance is halved. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Osakabehime) Chiyogami Manipulation: Uma  
Damage Spell. Deals 50% extra damage and knocks back the DU by 1 panel, the effect of Magic Resistance is halved. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Osakabehime) Castle Monster — Rank: A++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%; and remove all buffs from the affected Unit. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Osakabehime) Hakuro Jyou no Hyakki Hachitendou-sama — Rank: EX (NP) \[Fortress/Anti-Fortress\]  
(Non-damaging) Lord Hachitendo of the White Egret Castle's Hundred Demons. Can only be used after 5 full Rounds have passed. Affects a 7x7 panel area around Osakabehime; if the Region is ‘Japan’, the NP area is 9x9 instead. This NP area is now termed as ‘Himeji Castle’. Osakabehime cannot leave Himeji Castle. Has the following effects-  
1\. All allied Units within Himeji Castle upon activation have their Agility restored by 40% of their maximum value. Also applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within Himeji Castle upon activation, Max Health is increased by 20% of its original value.  
2\. Applies Atk Up to all allied Units within Himeji Castle upon activation for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies Def Up to all allied Units within Himeji Castle upon activation for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is decreased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Himeji Castle functions as a second Home Base for Osakabehime’s Faction. The effect of Osakabehime’s Territory Creation extends one panel away from Himeji Castle.  
5\. Osakabehime’s Sustainability does not decrease while she is within Himeji Castle. Her Master’s ZON is ignored when she Attacks.  
6\. Osakabehime’s Normal Attacks now use Base Attack (STR) and 60% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined instead (e.g. 45+126=171), not affected by Magic Resistance.  
7\. Units within Himeji Castle cannot be targeted by Attacks from outside Himeji Castle (but Units within Himeji Castle can Attack Units outside it). However, Himeji Castle can be Attacked with an AoE Noble Phantasm. In this case, all Servants within Himeji Castle receive damage from the NP, but Total Damage received is reduced by 50%; while Masters receive no damage and effects from it.  
Note: If the NP used on Himeji Castle was an \[Anti-Fortress\] or higher Noble Phantasm, Hakuro Jyou no Hyakki Hachitendou-sama is forcefully ended after the Units within receive the damage and effects.  
8\. Himeji Castle is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Osakabehime is defeated.  
This Noble Phantasm lasts for 2◈ Turns (3◈ Turns if the Region is Japan). Cooldown: 8◈ Turns after the NP ends.
