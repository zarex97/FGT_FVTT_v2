Note: If Jekyll is summoned, the player has to roll Stats for both Jekyll AND Hyde before starting.

(Jekyll) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.  
Cannot be used by Hyde. If it is Active when Jekyll uses Dangerous Game, it is forcibly deactivated.

(Jekyll/Hyde) Monstrous Strength — Rank: B  
(Active/Hyde) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing a Normal Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 80%; if NP, 40%. If used as Hyde, the magnitude of this Skill is doubled. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Note: This effect is not treated as applying a buff.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as ‘Double Entities’.

(Jekyll/Hyde) Double Entities — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has different effects depending on whether used as Jekyll or as Hyde-  
As Jekyll  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
4\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP is increased by 10%.  
As Hyde  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 35%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up \[Man\] for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Man\] Attribute is increased by 30%.  
4\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when this Unit is defeated he is revived with 20% of his Max Health.  
5\. Applies Buff Removal ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of having buffs removed from this Unit is reduced by 100%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as ‘Monstrous Strength’. 

(Jekyll/Hyde) Voice of Panic — Rank: A  
(Active/Hyde) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has a 25% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns on the DU. If this Skill is used as Hyde, the chance of inflicting Stun is 145% instead. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jekyll/Hyde) Self-Modification — Rank: D  
(Passive) Crit Attacks deal 15 extra damage. As Hyde, Crit Attacks deal 35 extra damage instead.

(Jekyll/Hyde) Self-Alteration — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 15%.  
2\. If used as Hyde, apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 35%.  
3\. Apply the ‘Opiate’ buff for 2◈ Turns, 1 time, its effects are as follows- As long as this Unit has this buff, it can use its Noble Phantasm regardless of its Cooldown. If NP is used through this buff, its current Cooldown is unaffected.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns 

(Jekyll/Hyde) Dangerous Game: The Secret Game of Sin — Rank: C+ \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used as Jekyll, Jekyll turns into Hyde; and if this NP is used as Hyde, Hyde turns into Jekyll. Replace Jekyll’s Stats and Parameters (or character sheet) with Hyde’s when Jekyll transforms into Hyde and vice versa. Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns. Note: When Hyde transforms back into Jekyll, Jekyll’s Stats are not restored (they are the same as they were before transforming into Hyde) and the same goes for Jekyll’s subsequent transformations into Hyde.

(Hyde) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: A  
Cannot be used by Jekyll. Constantly Active as Hyde. It is forcibly deactivated when Hyde uses Dangerous Game.   
While Mad Enhancement is Active, Hyde gains the following effects-  
1\. Hyde’ Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn he Acts; his Master’s Health cannot drop below 25 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 50% after calculation; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 80% after calculation including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Hyde can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while ME is Active, its effects are negated for the duration of Skill Seal.
