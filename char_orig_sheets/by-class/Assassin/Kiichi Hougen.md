(Kiichi) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kiichi) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 40% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Kiichi) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B+

(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+17) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Kiichi) Rikutou Hijutsu — Rank: EX  
(Active) Six Secret Arts of War. Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies the ‘Rikutou’ effect to all affected Units except herself and Units with the ‘Hijutsu’ effect. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff, is Unremovable, and does not stack. When a Unit with the ‘Rikutou’ effect performs its sixth Attack since being applied with ‘Rikutou’, remove the ‘Rikutou’ effect from it and reduce its NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns, then apply the ‘Hijutsu’ effect to that Unit.

The ‘Hijutsu’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff, is Unremovable, and does not stack. When a Unit with the ‘Hijutsu’ effect performs its sixth successful Attack since being applied with ‘Hijutsu’, reduce its NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns and reset the number of Attacks needed to trigger the ‘Hijutsu’ effect; then if Kiichi is within a 2 panel area of the AU, she gains 1 Onmyōdō Token.
