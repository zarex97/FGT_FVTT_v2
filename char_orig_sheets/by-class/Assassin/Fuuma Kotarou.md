(Kotarou) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 6 Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Kotarou) Subversive Activities — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Kotarou. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%; and Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. Can be used when Presence Concealment is Active; if used while Presence Concealment is Active, Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.

(Kotarou) Ninjutsu: Makibishichiri  
(Active) Scattered Caltrops. Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 50% chance of inflicting Immobilize for 1◈+⅓◈ Turns. If Immobilize failed to be inflicted, has a 100% chance of inflicting Slow for 1◈+⅓◈ Turns.   
2\. If the DU was inflicted with a debuff by Effect 1, it is inflicted Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 10%.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Kazegakushi. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Can be used when Presence Concealment is Active, but has a 50% chance of deactivating Presence Concealment when used; if used while Presence Concealment is Active without deactivating it, Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.

(Kotarou) Ogre Trance: Turning into the Endless Rakshasa — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Only usable when Kotarou’s Health is less than 25% of its maximum value. Has the same effect as Mad Enhancement — Rank: C. When Ogre Trance is Active, Kotarou is unable to use any Skills (including Presence Concealment, which would be deactivated upon Ogre Trance activating) and Immortal Chaos Brigade. Also, Fuuma gains the ‘Demonic (Oni)’ Attribute. Ogre Trance lasts for 4◈ Turns. When Ogre Trance is deactivated, Kotarou’s Health is reduced to 5% of its maximum value if it was above that. Ogre Trance can only be used once during the entire game.  
Mad Enhancement — Rank: C  
1\. Kotarou’s Master loses 15 Health at the end of every Turn he Acts; when its Health is 15 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 40% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Fuuma’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. Kotarou’s Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns if his Master is defeated while ME is Active.  
6\. While ME is Active, Kotarou can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.
