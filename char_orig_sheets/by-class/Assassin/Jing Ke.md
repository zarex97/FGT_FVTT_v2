(Jing Ke) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Jing Ke) Suppression — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt while Presence Concealment is Active is increased by 100\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Suppression’ buff to herself for 1◈ Turns. Its effects are different depending on whether Jing Ke’s Presence Concealment is Active or not, as follows-  
1\. Presence Concealment not Active: Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
2\. Presence Concealment is Active: Crit Chance is increased by 200% and the chance of being ‘Discovered’ is halved.  
If used while Jing Ke has the ‘Audacious’ effect, remove the ‘Audacious’ effect from herself. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jing Ke) Planning — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Planning’ buff to Jing Ke for 1◈ Turns. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. Crit Chance is increased by 30%, its magnitude is reduced by 10% at the end of the Turn after every ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. The chance of Nonreturnable Dagger inflicting Instakill is increased by 25%, and it gains the Aim effect.  
3\. 1◈ Turns after Presence Concealment is used, damage dealt while Presence Concealment is Active is increased by 200% instead of 100%; if NP, 100% instead of 50%.  
If an effect is used to remove buffs from Jing Ke, the chance of ‘Planning’ being removed is reduced by 80%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jing Ke) Kill a Man in Ten Steps — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dmg Up (King) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘King’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies the ‘Ten Steps’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 5 times; whenever Jing Ke performs an Attack, she inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the DU at the end of the Damage Step, all damage taken is increased by 10%; if the Attack was a Crit, Def Dwn is applied once more for free.  
3\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Jing Ke) Like There's No One Watching — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Cannot be used when Presence Concealment is Active. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Agility, then applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Audacious’ effect to Jing Ke for 1◈ Turns, The ‘Audacious’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable except through its own duration and the effect of ‘Suppression’. The effects of the ‘Audacious’ effect are as follows-  
a. Presence Concealment cannot be used when Jing Ke has the ‘Audacious’ effect.   
b. Chance of Nonreturnable Dagger inflicting Instakill is reduced by 50%.  
c. When Nonreturnable Dagger is used while Jing Ke has the ‘Planning’ effect, the Aim effect from ‘Planning’ is lost/removed  
d. If the DU Evades the NP, reduce the Evade roll by 4\.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jing Ke) Only For Killing: Nonreturnable Dagger — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=2. Deals 3.5x damage plus 100, then has a 75% chance of inflicting Instakill; if Instakill is not inflicted, the DU is inflicted with Poison, and Jing Ke’s Health is reduced by 10% of its maximum value. Units who are Immune to Poison cannot be inflicted with Instakill or Poison with this NP.   
If the DU is Qin Shi Huang, the chance of Instakill is fixed at 90% ignoring other effects except ‘Audacious’; if Instakill fails to be inflicted, Jing Ke is inflicted with Instakill at the end of the Combat Process.  
Regardless of which effect occurs, at the end of the Damage Step, S.Crit Up is applied to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Jing Ke for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%.   
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
