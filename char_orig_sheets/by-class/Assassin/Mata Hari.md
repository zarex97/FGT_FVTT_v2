(Mata Hari) Espionage — Rank: A++  
(Passive) Whenever Mata Hari successfully performs a Normal Attack on an enemy Unit, she has a 50% chance of inflicting Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the DU, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%. Then, if Crit Dwn was successfully inflicted, she gains 1 Espionage Token (max. 4\)  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When used, Mata Hari gains an Espionage Token, and applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) While Mata Hari has an Espionage Token, she can remove it from herself during her Turn or at the start or end of any Turn or Round to activate the effects of Presence Concealment — Rank: A++ on herself for 2◈ Turns, this effect cannot be used if Presence Concealment is already Active on herself.  
Presence Concealment — Rank: A++  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 0% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Mata Hari) The Sun Sets on Vincennes  — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. When this Skill is used, she can choose to remove 1 Espionage Token from herself to improve its effects, as follows-  
1\. Without Espionage Token: Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 35%; and applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased 35%.  
2\. With Espionage Token: Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%; and applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
(Passive) During Mata Hari’s Turn, she can remove 1 Espionage Token from herself to reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by 1◈ Turns. Can only be used once per Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mata Hari) Pheromone — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever Mata Hari successfully performs a Normal Attack on an enemy Unit, she has a 50% chance of inflicting Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns on the DU, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.

(Mata Hari) The Sweet Fragrance of Indulgence — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 5x5 panel area around Mata Hari or a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Mata Hari. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Charm ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, chance of being inflicted with Charm is increased by 60%.  
2\. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Charm on all affected enemy Male Units for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all affected Units, all damage received is increased by 20%.  
4\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all affected Units with the Charm debuff, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
5\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mata Hari) Double Cross — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Mata Hari successfully performs a Normal Attack on a Unit inflicted with Charm, she has a 50% chance of inflicting NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 40%.

(Mata Hari) Mata Hari: The Girl Who Has Sunny Eyes — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, it affects all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of Mata Hari. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Charm, does not affect Units with Active Mad Enhancement.  
2\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%.  
4\. If the Unit is inflicted with Charm by this NP, apply the ‘Matahari’ effect to that Unit, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
When this NP is used on a Unit with the ‘Matahari’ effect, chance of inflicting Charm is 100% instead of 60%, and the duration of Atk Dwn & Def Dwn lasts for 2◈ Turns instead of 1◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever the game state changes from a Day Round to a Night Round, the Cooldown of this NP is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
