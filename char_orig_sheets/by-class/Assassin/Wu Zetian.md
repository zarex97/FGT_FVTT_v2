(Wu Zetian) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: D  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 60% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Wu Zetian) Torture Technique — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Inflicts Def Dwn (Torture) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: Torture Technique and Bone-Charred Peony cannot be used on the same Turn.  
(Passive) When Zetian Attacks a Unit inflicted with Def Dwn (Torture), damage dealt is increased by ~~50%; if NP, 25%~~ 30%; if NP, 15%.

(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
2\. Inflicts No Buff for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
3\. Inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Wu Zetian) Wise Empress — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 35%; if NP, 20%.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 35%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Restores her Health by 20% of its maximum value.  
4\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
5\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself by ⅓◈ Turns.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Wu Zetian) Charisma of the Empress — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Zetian. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Zetian, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Restores 2 Agility all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of Zetian and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to those Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Wu Zetian) Summoning: Kù Lì  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Counts as Zetian’s Attack for the Turn. Roll a four-sided die. Zetian summons that number of Atrocious Officers on the panels directly around her. The Stats of an Atrocious Officer are as follows-  
Health: 250  
Agility: 10  
Luck: Half of Zetian’s Max Luck  
MOV: 4  
Range: 1 panel, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 100  
Attributes: Female, Humanoid  
Detect: 2 panels  
Atrocious Officer’s Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Bleed for 1◈ Turns.  
Damage dealt by Atrocious Officers to Units inflicted with Def Dwn (Torture) is increased by 25%, and chance of inflicting Bleed is increased by 10%.   
Atrocious Officers do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack per Turn. The same Atrocious Officer can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Zetian or her Master if any Atrocious Officers are within a 2 panel area of them.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Wu Zetian) Gàomì Luózhī Jīng: Manual of Accusation — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 3.5x damage plus 100, then removes all buffs from the DU and inflicts Poison. Next, applies Crit DmUp to Zetian for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 75%; if the DU had Def Dwn (Torture), the magnitude of Crit DmUp is 100% instead of 75%. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.
