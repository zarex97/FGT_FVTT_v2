(BBPele) Swimsuit\!

(BBPele) MoonCancer  
(Passive) All damage dealt including NP is increased by 5% for every different enemy Servant Class on the field other than MoonCancer. If this Servant was summoned in their Region, the magnitudes of this Skill are doubled.

(BBPele) Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(BBPele) One Who Drinks The Earth — Rank: EX  
(Passive) BB is Immune to Fire damage and the Burn debuff. BB’s Normal Attacks have a 30% chance of dealing Fire damage and inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns.

(BBPele) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: B  
(Passive) Has the following effects on BB-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs including Instakill is reduced by 20%, this effect is halved for Death (10%) and further halved for Erase (5%).  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 30% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 30% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Dark (Outsider) Attribute’ is increased by 40% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(BBPele) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: A

(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 30%.

(BBPele) Faceless Moon — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, apply the ‘Faceless Moon’ buff to BB for 3◈ Turns. Its effects constantly affect all Units within a 7x7 panel area of BB, all damage dealt by an affected Unit’s Attack will be exactly the same amount as its first Attack dealt since entering this Skill’s Area of Effect.  
Example:  
Lancer of Red performs its first Normal Attack since entering Faceless Moon’s area and deals damage.  
During the next Round, Lancer of Red performs a Normal Attack. The damage dealt will be the exact same amount/value as the previous Normal Attack, regardless of any new or removed damage-modifying effects on Lancer of Red.  
This effect is applied separately to all Attack Skills, Damage Spells, and NP of Units within Faceless Moon’s area. This effect is lost when a Unit leaves the area, and is applied to any Units entering the area.  
The ‘Faceless Moon’ buff also has the Area CritUp effect on BB, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns after the Faceless Moon buff naturally expires/is removed.

(BBPele) Cursed Cutting Crater: C.C.C. — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 (5x5?) panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to BB. Deals 5x damage; then increases the DUs’ NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; then has a 50% chance of further increasing the DUs’ NP Cooldown by another 1◈ Turns. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
