(Gyokuto) Jade Rabbit  
(Passive 1\) Gyokuto automatically switches between two Forms in Day and Night Rounds. At the start of a Day Round, she switches to her Swimsuit Form. At the start of a Night Round, she switches to her Yukata Form.  
(Passive 2\) While Gyokuto has the ‘Nichirin’ effect, she will remain in Swimsuit Form, regardless of Day or Night. While Gyokuto has the ‘Gekka’ effect, she will remain in Yukata Form regardless of Day or Night.  
Note: Passive 1 and 2 will not activate while in Kaguya Form.  
(Passive 3\) Having the ‘Nichirin’ effect will treat Gyokuto as being in a Day Round (even if it is Night), while having the ‘Gekka’ effect will treat her as being in a Night Round (even if it is Day).

(Gyokuto) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Gyokuto) MoonCancer  
(Passive) All damage dealt including NP is increased by 5% for every different enemy Servant Class on the field other than MoonCancer. All damage taken including NP is reduced by 5% for every different enemy Servant Class on the field other than MoonCancer.

(Gyokuto) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Sustainability does not apply.  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.

(Gyokuto) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Gyokuto) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 2\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 60% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

1\. Remove the ‘Nichirin’ effect if present, then apply the ‘Gekka’ effect to herself for 2◈ Turns.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 4◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns.  
(Active 2\) Nichirin. Has the following effects-

(Gyokuto) Vijñaptimātratā

(Gyokuto) Manashiki・Izayoizanka — Rank: EX  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=4. First, applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%; 40% if Gyokuto has a ‘Nichirin’ or ‘Gekka’ effect. Then, hits differently depending on whether used during the Day/with the ‘Nichirin’ effect or during the Night/with the ‘Gekka’ effect-  
1\. Nichirin (Day): Deals 5x damage.  
2\. Gekka (Night): Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 5x damage, Total Damage dealt is reduced by 10% (15%?) for every Unit within the NP area.  
Damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute. Then, has a 60% chance of inflicting Death (chance of inflicting Death is reduced by 5% for every Unit within the NP area if used during Night/with the ‘Gekka’ effect). Finally, reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself by ⅓◈ Turns, and Gyokuto enters Kaguya Form for 4◈ Turns (3◈ Turns?). Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
