(BB) MoonCancer  
(Passive) All damage dealt including NP is increased by 5% for every different enemy Servant Class on the field other than MoonCancer. All damage taken including NP is reduced by 5% for every different enemy Servant Class on the field other than MoonCancer. If this Servant was summoned in their Region, the magnitudes of this Skill are doubled.

(BB) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(BB) Class Skill: Item Construction  — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(BB) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(BB) Ten Crowns — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of BB. Removes 2 debuffs from the Unit and restores its Health by 20% of its maximum value. Then applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time; does not affect Instakill, Death, & Erase. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(BB) Aurea Boura — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of BB. Removes all Invuln and Dodge buffs on the DU, then inflicts Stun for 1◈ Turns. Does not affect Anti-Purge or Substitution. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(BB) Self-Modification (Affection) — Rank: EX

(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-

Applies NP Regen (Domina) for 1◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- Whenever BB performs a successful Normal Attack and the damage dealt has a ‘1’ or ‘0’ in its value, reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself by ⅓◈ Turns; if the damage has both ‘1’ and ‘0’ in its value, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns instead.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
