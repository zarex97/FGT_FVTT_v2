(Hakuno) Checkmate Interceptor — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Hakuno performs a Normal Attack, at the start of the Damage Step, pass the three Interceptor cards (ATTACK, GUARD, BREAK) to Hakuno and the (nearest) DU. Then, both Players choose 1 card to play and reveal it to each other. Depending on the result of the game, different effects are applied to Hakuno.  
(ATTACK beats BREAK, GUARD beats ATTACK, BREAK beats GUARD.)  
If Hakuno loses the game, Total Damage dealt by his Attack is reduced by 30%.  
If the game was a draw, no effect is applied.  
If Hakuno wins the game and his Attack was successful, apply the following effect depending on which card was played by Hakuno-  
1\. ATTACK: Hakuno’s NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step.  
2\. GUARD:  Apply Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns to Hakuno at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. BREAK: Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to Hakuno at the start of the Damage Step, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Each effect can only be applied once per Round (Note: Hakuno can still play Interceptor cards which have already applied their effect, but the effect won’t be applied again if he wins). However, if Hakuno loses three Interceptor card games in the same Round, immediately apply the ‘Zabiko’ effect to Hakuno until the end of the next Round- The Passive effect of Checkmate Interceptor will not activate, and all Total Damage dealt is reduced by 30% including NP.
