(Jinako) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

Giant Statue God.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’ and ‘Riding Attack’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Jinako) Giant Statue God  
When Jinako is summoned, she is summoned inside her Statue. While Jinako is within the Statue, apply the following effects-  
1\. All damage taken is reduced by 25% including NP.  
2\. Whenever Jinako performs an Attack and the DU Evades, reduce the DU’s Evade roll by 2\.  
Whenever Jinako receives damage from an Attack, the Statue has a 5% chance of breaking. If the Statue does not break, its break chance is increased by 5% each time she is damaged by an Attack and the Statue does not break. When the Statue breaks, Jinako loses the above effects.  
3\. Whenever Jinako uses her Riding Skill while the Statue is broken, she receives the effects 1 & 2 of this Skill for that Turn ‘MOV Up’ is applied for from Riding.
