## (A:E) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A+

(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

## (A:E) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A

(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

## (A:E) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX

(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 6d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
(Passive 3\) ‘Inner Sea of the Planet’ and ‘Millennium Castle’ Terrain count as a Home Base for Archetype:Earth and her Master.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

## (A:E) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A

This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

## (A:E) Ultimate One — Rank: D

Has different effects depending on the Terrain type Archetype:Earth is within as follows-  
(Passive 1\) If Archetype:Earth is on Planet Earth, restore 1 Agility and 1 Luck at the end of her Turn. All damage dealt is increased by 10% while all damage taken is reduced by 10%, also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) If Archetype:Earth is on the Moon or within an ‘Inner Sea of the Planet’ Terrain, restore 2 Agility and 2 Luck at the end of her Turn, and all damage dealt is increased by 20% while all damage taken is reduced by 20%, also affects NP.  
(Passive 3\) If Archetype:Earth is within a ‘Millennium Castle’ Terrain, restore 3 Agility and 3 Luck at the end of her Turn, and all damage dealt is increased by 30% while all damage taken is reduced by 30%, also affects NP.
