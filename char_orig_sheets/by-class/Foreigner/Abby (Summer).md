(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units in a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Abby. Has the following effects-

3\. Applies Atk Up (Midsummer) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. Against Units inflicted with Terror or Stun (Terror), the magnitude of this buff is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.
