(Yang) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%, this effect is halved for Death (15%) and further halved for Erase (5%).  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Outsider’ Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Yang) Embodiment of Three Thousand Affections — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Whenever a Male enemy Unit performs an Attack within a 2 panel area of Yang Guifei, it has a 25% chance of changing its target to Yang Guifei instead at the start of the Damage Step.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-

2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.

4\. Applies Decoy to herself for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.  

(Yang) Beloved and Adored Consort — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Yang Guifei. Has the following effects-  
1\. Increase the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Reduce Yang Guifei’s Cooldown by X \* ⅓◈ Turns, where X=the number of Units whose NP Cooldown was successfully increased by Effect 1 of this Skill.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Yang) Sun of a Calamitous Star — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the ‘Living Flame’ buff for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Restores 3 Luck and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The effects of the Living Flame buff are as follows- Whenever this Unit is successfully Attacked, at the end of the Damage Step, inflict Burn (LF) on the AU for 2◈ Turns and inflict Def Dwn on the AU for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 10%.  
The Burn (LF) is the same as the normal Burn debuff, but it can be stacked; stacking Burn (LF) does not affect the Base Attack decrease, but the affected Unit takes 50 Burn Damage at the end of every Round for every Burn (LF) debuff it has (if the Unit with LF Burn is also inflicted with regular Burn, the Unit also receives Burn Damage from the regular Burn in addition to the LF Burn, but the Unit’s Base Attack decrease is not doubled if it has both Burn (LF) and normal Burn).  
(Passive) Whenever Burn (LF) is successfully inflicted on a Unit through the ‘Living Flame’ buff, Flip a Coin. If Heads, extend the duration of ‘Living Flame’ by ⅓◈ Turns. If Tails, reduce Yang Guifei’s NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns.
