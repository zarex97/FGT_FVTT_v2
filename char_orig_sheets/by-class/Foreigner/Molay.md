(Molay) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: A  
(Passive) Has the following effects on Molay-

2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 40% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 40% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Outsider’ Attribute is increased by 40% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.
