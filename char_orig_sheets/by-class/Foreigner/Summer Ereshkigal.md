(S.Eresh) Underworld Space Goddess  
When Space Ereshkigal is summoned, flip a coin. If Heads, she is summoned as Summer Ereshkigal. If Tails, she is summoned as Anki Ereshkigal. The differences between them are as follows-  
1\. Summer Ereshkigal: Range=3 panels, 1 target. At a Range of 1 to 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and 50% of Base Attack (MAG) combined, not affected by Magic Resistance. At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. Anki Ereshkigal:  Range=4 panels, 1 target. Has the Levitating, Large, and Non-Hominidae Attributes. Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Swaps from Summer Ereshkigal to Anki Ereshkigal or vice-versa. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Passive) Only in effect as Summer Ereshkigal. All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(S.Eresh) Anti-Thaumaturgy — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When Ereshkigal would be inflicted with a debuff, it has no effect; this effect can only activate three times.  
(Passive 2\) Ereshkigal is Immune to MAG damage and any effects from Spells & Attacks which deal MAG damage.

(S.Eresh) Spatial Storage — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When Ereshkigal performs a successful non-NP Attack, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn.  
(Passive 2\) During Ereshkigal’s Turn, she can place any number of Items she is holding inside her Spatial Storage; and she can retrieve any number of Items from inside the Spatial Storage and hold them. Items inside the Spatial Storage cannot be Stolen, but have no effect and cannot be Equipped. An allied Unit who is standing directly next to Ereshkigal can also perform this Action during their Turn.
