(Hokusai) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Has the following effects on Hokusai-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs including Instakill is reduced by 30%, this effect is halved for Death (15%) and further halved for Erase (5%).  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Outsider’ Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Hokusai) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Hokusai) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d6 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+5) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).
