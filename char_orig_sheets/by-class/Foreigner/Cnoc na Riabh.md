(Cnoc) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Cnoc) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Cnoc) Melty Heart — Rank: A  
(Passive) Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 3 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.

(Cnoc) Southern Star — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. During a ‘Day’ Round, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
2\. During a ‘Night’ Round, Natural NP regen is 2 Turns instead of 1 Turn.  
3\. All damage dealt to and received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Outsider’ Attribute is increased by 25% including NP.  
4\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Cnoc) Queen’s Tomb — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
2\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value at the end of this Unit’s Turn, the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
4\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself with the MFS debuff by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Cnoc) Queen’s Covenant — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
2\. Inflicts the ‘My Fair Soldier’ (MFS)’ debuff for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units except herself, this debuff has the same effect as Skill Seal.  
3\. Applies Atk Up (MFS) for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units except herself, as long as an affected Unit has the MFS debuff, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
4\. Applies Def Up (MFS) for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units except herself, as long as an affected Unit has the MFS debuff, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Cnoc) VVV — Rank: A

2\. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Stun to all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself; this chance is reduced by 30% against Units inflicted with Deafen.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns to all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
4\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Silence for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself except Castoria and herself.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

Damage Spell. Range=3. Deals normal damage and inflicts Charm (Melty) for 1◈ Turns. Remove the ‘Mab’ buff from an affected Unit if it is not inflicted with Charm (Melty). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
