(Aoko) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: E  
(Passive) Has the following effects on Aoko-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs including Instakill is reduced by 5%, does not affect Death and Erase.  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 5% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 5% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Dark (Outsider) Attribute’ is increased by 5% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Aoko) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d4 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by 3d10 including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Aoko) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: E  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 10%; this effect is halved for Instakill (5%), and does not affect Death.  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%; this effect is halved for Instakill (5%), and does not affect Death.  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).
