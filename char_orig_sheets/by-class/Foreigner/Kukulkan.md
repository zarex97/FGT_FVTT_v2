(Kukulkan) Archetype ORT  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When Invuln is applied to herself, it is converted to Anti-Purge instead.  
2\. Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.  
3\. Natural NP regen is 2 Turns instead of 1 Turn.  
4\. All damage dealt to and received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Outsider’ Attribute is increased by 25% including NP.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Existence Outside The Domain’.

4\. Whenever an effect that reduces Crit Chance is applied to Kukulkan, she loses half of her Mictlan Tokens, rounded down.

(Kukulkan) Heart of the Ultimate One  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used when the following conditions are met-  
1\. 9 full Rounds have passed.  
2\. Her Master has used Command Spells at least once.  
3\. Kukulkan has Covered her Master from an NP or Shoved her Master out of an AoE NP at least once.  
4\. Kukulkan has at least 1 Mictlan Token.  
When this Skill is used, remove all Mictlan Tokens from Kukulkan and she transforms into ORT Mode, then restore her Health by 10% of its maximum value for every Mictlan Token removed from her in this way.  
(Passive) Kukulkan gains the following effects while in ORT Mode-  
1\. STR is increased by one Rank (Base Attack (STR) increased by 25).  
2\. END is increased by one Rank (Max Health increased by 250).  
3\. At the end of her Turn, gain 2 Mictlan Tokens instead of 1\. At the end of any Turn she Acts, she gains 1 Mictlan Token.  
4\. The effects of some Skills are upgraded to the effects in this colour.  
5\. At the start of the Round, apply Invuln for 1 time to herself.  
6\. Gain the use of the Skill ‘Xibalba Supernova’. Her NP ‘Kinich Ahau Impact’ is replaced with ‘Last Sun Xibalba’.
