(Wandjina) Great Cosmic Elemental  
(Passive) Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG). When she performs a Normal Attack, roll a six-sided die to determine the effect-  
1\. Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
2\. Deals Water damage with a 20% chance of inflicting Slow for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Deals Lightning damage with a 20% chance of inflicting Shock for 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Deals Wind damage with a 20% chance of inflicting Confuse for 1◈ Turns.  
5\. Deals Nature damage with a 20% chance of inflicting Poison.  
6\. Your choice

(Wandjina) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: B  
(Passive) Has the following effects on Wandjina-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs including Instakill is reduced by 20%, this effect is halved for Death (10%) and further halved for Erase (5%).  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 30% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 30% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Dark (Outsider) Attribute’ is increased by 30% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Wandjina) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Wandjina) Cradle of the Stars — Rank: C  
(Passive) When within an ‘Airspace’ area, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used in Mamaragan Form. Remove 10 Mamaragan Counters from herself and gain the ‘Atmosphere’ effect for 2◈ Turns, the 3 panel area around herself becomes an ‘Airspace’ area. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.

(Wandjina) Droplets of Nature — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Droplets’ buff for 1◈ Turns, when a successful Normal Attack is performed, apply the ‘Elemental Wrath’ buff (max. 10\) for 2◈ Turns to herself at the end of the Damage Step.  
4\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Elemental Wrath: When a Unit other than Wandjina with this buff performs a non-NP Attack which deals Elemental damage, remove all Elemental Wrath from that Unit and increase Elemental damage dealt by 10% for every Elemental Wrath removed in this way. For Wandjina, this effect is optional, and if she uses it, she can choose how many Elemental Wrath to remove from herself (for her Normal Attack, decide whether to use them after the d6 roll, but before the DU chooses its Reaction).

(Wandjina) Blessing of Life — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units with the \[Man\] Attribute, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Munurru’ buff for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units with the \[Man\] Attribute, when this Unit is Attacked, apply the ‘Elemental Wrath’ buff (max. 10\) for 2◈ Turns to itself at the end of the Damage Step.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Wandjina) The One who Gazes at the Aquatic Star — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value.

1\. Whenever she gains or consumes ‘Elemental Wrath’: \+2  
2\. Whenever any other allied Unit gains ‘Elemental Wrath’: \+1 for every Unit who gained it  
3\. When she successfully inflicts a debuff through the effect of ‘Great Cosmic Elemental’: \+2  
4\. When NP is used: \+7  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Wandjina has 30 Mamaragan Tokens, remove all of them from herself and change into Mamaragan Form, gaining the following effects-  
1\. Range is increased by 1\.  
2\. Gains the ‘Levitating’ Attribute.  
3\. At the start of her Turn or at the end of any Turn she Acts, apply 1 Elemental Wrath to herself (no duration).  
4\. Whenever she performs a successful Normal Attack, apply 1 Elemental Wrath (no duration) to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself.  
5\. The effects of some Skills/NP are changed to the effect in this colour.  
6\. Debuff inflict chance of Great Cosmic Elemental is increased to 40%.
