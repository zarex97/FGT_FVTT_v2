(Sitonai) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Sitonai) Familiar: Shiro  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 3 for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from this Skill’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Sitonai) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Sitonai) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Sitonai) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Sitonai) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Sitonai) Snow Fairy — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln to herself for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Dmg Cut to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Sitonai for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage received is reduced by 50 including NP.  
3\. Applies the ‘Sitonai’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Sitonai’s Normal Attacks have a 25% chance of inflicting Disable for 1◈ Turns on the DU.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Sitonai) Emotional Freezing — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores Luck by 3; then applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%; and applies Debuff Immune for 1 time, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Sitonai) Kamuy Yukar — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has an 80% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Has an 80% chance of applying Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%.  
3\. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Has an 80% chance of applying Dmg Up (Dragon) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dragon’ Attribute is increased by 30%.  
5\. Has an 80% chance of applying Dmg Up (Demonic) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive) Whenever an effect fails to be applied by Kamuy Yukar, Sitonai can remove 1 Kamuy Token from herself to change that failure into a success instead (1 Kamuy Token per effect).

(Sitonai) Crimson Gold — Rank: B++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit standing next to Sitonai. Sitonai’s Health is reduced by 10% of its maximum value; then restore the targeted allied Unit’s Health and Luck by 10% of their maximum values. Then, Sitonai gains 2 Kamuy Tokens. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.
