(Meltryllis) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Meltryllis) Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Meltryllis) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Meltryllis) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Meltryllis) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Meltryllis) Crime Ballet — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Albrecht’ buff for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns whenever this Unit performs a successful Evade.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Meltryllis) Sadistic Constitution — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn on herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 10%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Sadistic’ buff for 1◈ Turns, whenever Meltryllis performs a Normal Attack, Crit DmUp is applied to herself for 1◈ Turns at the start of the Damage Step, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Meltryllis) Melt Virus — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns on all Units within a 2 panel area of Meltryllis except herself, NP Damage dealt is reduced by 50%.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage is increased by 30% plus 5% for every non-Master/Civilian Unit successfully inflicted with NP DmDwn from Effect 1\.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Meltryllis) Saraswati Meltout: Benzaiten's Five-String Biwa — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. First restores Meltryllis’s Agility by 3 and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Deals 5x damage and removes all buffs from the DU. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
