4\. The maximum number of Mainyu Tokens Rasputin can have is 18\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used when Rasputin has 18 Mainyu Tokens. Remove all Mainyu Tokens from himself to enter Angra Form. While in Angra Form, Rasputin gains the following effects-

2\. Rasputin loses the Executor, Baptism Sacrament, and all Black Key Skills.

4\. Normal Attacks have a 50% chance of inflicting Curse.  
5\. All Skill effects are upgraded to the values in this color.  
6\. Rasputin no longer gains Mainyu Tokens while in Angra Form.
