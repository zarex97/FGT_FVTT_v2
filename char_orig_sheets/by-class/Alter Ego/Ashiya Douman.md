(Douman) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Douman) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Douman) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Douman) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Douman) Dark Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of inflicting Instakill and Death is increased by 20%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Douman) Hedonism — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Douman receives damage from an Attack, reduce his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn. If that Attack was a Crit, reduce his NP Cooldown by 2 Turns instead.

(Douman) Ridicule Cat — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased 30%.  
3\. Inflicts Terror for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Stun from Terror is 40% (40% Terror).  
4\. Inflicts Disorder for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Skill Seal from Disorder is 30% (30% Disorder).  
5\. Inflicts Curse.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Douman) Dark Karma — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts to himself for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, 2 times, when Douman is defeated he is revived with 20% of his Health.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units with the ‘Chaotic’ Alignment within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Douman) Curse of Douman — Rank: A++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduces his NP Cooldown by 1◈+½◈ Turns; for every Unit that is inflicted with Curse within a 3 panel area of himself, his NP Cooldown is further reduced by ⅓◈ Turns. (his NP Cooldown is further reduced by ⅓◈ Turns for each Stage of Curse inflicted on all Units within a 3 panel area of himself.)  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units with the ‘Chaotic’ Alignment within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
4\. Inflicts Curse on all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of himself.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Douman) Kyouran Dotou・Akuryousafu — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-City\]

Also inflicts Evil Curse for 2◈ Turns to the affected Units, Curse Damage received is doubled. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
