(Passionlip) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Passionlip) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 5% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Passionlip) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

4\. Applies the NP Regen (Valley) buff for 1◈ Turns, at the end of a Combat Phase where Passionlip and the AU/DU are directly next to each other, reduce Passionlip’s NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns.

(Passionlip) Masochistic Constitution — Rank: A+

(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Decoy for 1◈ Turns; and Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Additionally, if Passionlip’s Health is below 50%, applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X%, X being the percentage amount of Health Passionlip has lost when the Skill was used (preferably rounded down to a 0 or a 5); if NP, (X/2)%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Passionlip) Trash and Crush — Rank: EX

3\. Applies Def Up for 2◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.

5\. Applies Pierce for 2◈ Turns.
