(Kazuradrop) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP. This effect is negated if Kazuradrop is not the ‘Alter-Ego’ Servant Class.

(Kazuradrop) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kazuradrop) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 6d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Kazuradrop) Repulsion for One’s Kin — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt to BB and ‘Sakura Five’ Units is increased by 30% including NP.  
(Passive 2\) All damage dealt to Units with the same Servant Class as herself is increased by 70% including NP. Has no effect against the Shielder and Beast Servant Classes.

(Kazuradrop) Pixie Finger — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area directly next to herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is increased by ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (C) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30% and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever this Unit is successfully Attacked.  
3\. Inflicts Crit ResDwn for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage taken is increased by 50%.  
4\. Applies Debuff ChUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%.  
5\. Applies the ‘Pixie’ buff for 2 times to herself, has the following effects- At the start of this Unit’s Turn (a Turn this Unit Acts?), inflicts Effect 2 and 3 of this Skill on all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself (all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area directly next to herself?).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kazuradrop) Sakura Eater — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
2\. For every BB and other ‘Sakura Five’ Unit within a 3 panel area of herself, NP Cooldown is further reduced by 2◈ Turns (1◈ Turns?).  
3\. If there are no BB or other ‘Sakura Five’ Units within a 3 panel area of herself, Skill Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns instead.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Kazuradrop successfully defeats a BB or Sakura Five Unit, she can choose one of the non-Class Skills on the defeated Unit and obtain it for her own usage permanently (for the rest of the game). Skills obtained in this way do not count towards the Skill limit of Insect Eater.

(Kazuradrop) Moon’s Pupa — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Removes all debuffs from herself.  
2\. Restores her Health and Agility by 35% of their maximum values.  
3\. Choose an enemy Servant within Range. Kazuradrop changes her Servant Class to the selected Servant’s for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns on that selected enemy Servant, all damage taken is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kazuradrop) id\_es: Insect Eater  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Kazuradrop performs a successful Attack, she has a 15% chance of ‘stealing’ one of the DU’s Skills at the end of the Damage Step. A ‘stolen’ Skill cannot be used by its original owner and has no effects for that Unit, while Kazuradrop can now use that Skill and gain its effects as if it was her own. The chance of this Skill triggering is affected by the following-  
1\. If the Region is the Moon Cell: \+30%  
2\. If Kazuradrop has Debuff ChUp: \+X%  
3\. If the target is BB or a Sakura Five Unit: \+30%  
4\. For every Rank in Magic Resistance on the DU: \-5%  
5\. If Kazuradrop has Debuff ChDwn: \-Y%  
Kazuradrop can only hold a maximum of three ‘stolen’ Skills at any time. If she obtains a fourth Skill, she must choose which ‘stolen’ Skill to discard, she loses the discarded Skill and its owner can use it again.  
Note: Whenever a Skill is ‘stolen’ by Insect Eater, its Cooldown is also carried over.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Kazuradrop successfully ‘steals’ a Skill with Insect Eater, gain 1 Eater Token (max. 7).

(Kazuradrop) Gully Trap Funny Muryan: Expanding World — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Can be used in two different methods as follows-  
1\. (Non-damaging) Counts as Kazuradrop’s Attack for the Turn. Used on a Unit directly next to herself. If this NP was not successfully Evaded, Kazuradrop captures the targeted Unit inside her Bug Space (i.e. obtains the targeted Unit as a Special Item, cannot be Stolen). All effects, durations, and Cooldowns are paused & have no effect on Units within Bug Space. If an allied Unit within a 2 panel area within herself is about to receive an Attack, at the start of the Damage Step, Kazuradrop can use this NP to capture that allied Unit into Bug Space. The maximum number of Units that can be held inside Bug Space is three. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Effect 2 or Atomicros Muryan Foot. Does not consume Master’s Health to use.  
During Kazuradrop’s Turn, she can release any Units caught inside Bug Space onto any panel within a 2 panel area of herself. This Action does not have a Cooldown.  
2\. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel directly next to herself, deals 3.5x damage to all Units within the NP area and Units currently within Bug Space when the NP was used. Then, inflicts the following effects on all affected Units-  
a. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
b. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
c. Has a 40% chance of inflicting Stun for ⅓◈ Turns.  
Then, if there are any Units inside Bug Space, they are all forcefully released onto random panels within a 2 panel area of Kazuradrop. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Effect 1\.

(Kazuradrop) Atomicros Muryan Foot: Shrinking World — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive 1\) This NP cannot be normally used. It can only be used while in Atomicros Form, which can be achieved by meeting one of the two conditions-  
1\. During your Turn, remove 7 Eater Tokens from herself and discard all Skills ‘stolen’ with Insect Eater to enter Atomicros Form.  
2\. When Kazuradrop has defeated a total of two BB and/or Sakura Five Units, she automatically changes into Atomicros Form at the end of the Combat Phase.  
(Passive 2\) Kazuradrop gains the following effects while in Atomicros Form-  
1\. MOV is increased by 1\.  
2\. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 3\.  
3\. The base chance of Insect Eater succeeding is increased by 15%.  
4\. Bug Space can now hold a maximum of five Units instead of three.  
(Active) Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel directly next to herself, deals 5x damage to all Units within the NP area and Units currently within Bug Space when the NP was used. Then, inflicts the following effects on all affected Units-  
a. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
b. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 40%.  
c. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns.  
Then, if there are any Units inside Bug Space, they are all forcefully released onto random panels within a 2 panel area of Kazuradrop. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Effect 1 of Gully Trap Funny Muryan.
