(Mecha Eli-chan) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Mecha Eli-chan) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Mecha Eli-chan) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 after calculation including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Mecha Eli-chan) Innocent Kaiju — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5%; and Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mecha Eli-chan) Revised Overload — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces Mecha Eli’s Health by 5% of its maximum value (cannot fall below 1 in this way) and reduce her NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns. Then, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Mecha-Eli chan) Final Eli-chan — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 500% chance of removing all Defensive Buffs from Mecha Eli.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Final Eli-chan is used, it has a 30% chance of being upgraded from Rank C to Rank A before its effects are applied, this chance increases by 30% for every upgrade failure.

(Mecha-Eli chan) Final Eli-chan — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 500% chance of removing all Defensive Buffs from Mecha Eli.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 60%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Eli-chan’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Normal Attacks inflict Burn (Eli-chan) for 1◈ Turns and when receiving a successful Attack which uses Base Attack (STR) at a Range of 1, inflict Burn (Eli-chan) on the DU for 1◈ Turns.  
5\. Reduce the Cooldown of a random ‘Mecha Classic’ Skill by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mecha Eli-chan) Mecha Classic: Rocket Punch  
Attack Skill. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals 2x damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Mecha Eli-Chan) Mecha Classic: Thunder Break  
Attack Skill. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 2x damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Shock for 2◈ Turns. Lightning damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Mecha Eli-Chan) Mecha Classic: Breast Fire  
Attack Skill. Can only be used after Final Eli-chan has been upgraded to Rank A. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 2x damage and inflicts Burn (Eli-chan) for 2◈ Turns. If Eli-chan has the ‘Eli-chan’ buff, also inflict Severe Burn for 2◈ Turns, Burn damage taken is doubled. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

Note: Burn (Eli-chan) has the same effect as normal Burn, but its duration can be extended by inflicting it again. Burn (Eli-chan) does not stack with normal Burn, and will overwrite normal Burn if applied to a Unit with it.
