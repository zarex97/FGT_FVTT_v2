(Flora) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Flora) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Flora) Divine Core of Flowers — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 105 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
(Passive 3\) Chance of having buffs removed from herself is reduced by 20%.  
(Passive 4\) Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Nature damage. However, if Flora is not within or directly next to a ‘Meadow’ area, Total damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if Flora is within or directly next to a ‘Forest’ area but without a ‘Meadow’ area, then Total damage dealt is only reduced by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Flora) Avatar of Spring — Rank: A  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself- Whenever Health is restored, the amount restored is increased by 20%.

(Flora) Never-Ending Bud — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Flora is defeated and revived through an effect, NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area around herself is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.

(Flora) Floralia, for You — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 20%. Does not affect herself.  
2\. Upon performing a successful Attack, NP Cooldown has a 60% chance of being reduced by 1 Turn.  
3\. The effects of this Skill are negated while Flora is inflicted with a Mental Debuff.

2\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP is increased by 10%.  
3\. NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies the ‘Flower’ buff for 1◈ Turns to herself- At the end of her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round, apply Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
