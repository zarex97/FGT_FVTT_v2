3\. Applies Dmg Cut for (Dice 3\) times, all damage taken is reduced by 80 (100) including NP.

(Draco) Aurea Poculum — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Skill Seal for ⅓◈ Turns (1◈ Turns).  
2\. Inflicts Burn (Draco) for 2◈ Turns.  
3\. Reduce their NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.

Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Draco) Applause for the Evils of Humanity  
(Passive) When Draco has defeated at least 3 ~~(N?)~~ Units other than Masters and Mob Units (e.g. Civilians, Dragon Tooth Warriors, Horrors, basically most Units which are summoned through Skills/Spells/NP and are not Masters or Servants) or she defeats a non-Master/non-Mob Unit while her Health is 10% or less of its maximum value, she automatically transforms into Applause Form at the start of her next Turn, at the start of the next Turn she Acts, or at the start of the next Round. ~~N=70% of the number of Servants present on the board at the start of the game, rounded down.~~ Draco gains the following effects while in Applause Form-  
1\. The effects of certain Skills and NP are upgraded to the effects in this colour.  
2\. When using Number of the Beast, the dice will always roll the maximum number (i.e. 6).  
3\. When using Aurea Poculum, the coin flip will always be Heads.  
4a. Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 to 2 use Base Attack (STR) and half of Base Attack (MAG) combined, not affected by Magic Resistance. Deals Fire damage (half) and inflicts Burn (Draco) for 1◈ Turns.  
4b. Normal Attacks at a Range of 3 or higher use Base Attack (MAG), deal Fire damage and inflict Burn (Draco) for 2◈ Turns.  
5\. Draco gains the use of the Skill ‘Incarnadine Blaze’. The following effect is added to Aurea Poculum- ‘4. Reduce the Cooldown of Incarnadine Blaze by 1◈ Turns’.  
6\. Draco loses the ‘Child’ Attribute.
