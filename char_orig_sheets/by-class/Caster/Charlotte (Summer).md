(Corday) Class Skill: Magic Show Field — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (1d20\*5) including NP. When it is not in its Home Base, all damage dealt is increased by (1d10\*5) instead.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, Crit Damage dealt by Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) is increased by 10%; while it is increased by 5% while she is not within it.  
(Passive 3\) While this Unit is on the field, Crit Damage dealt by Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) of all allied Units who are in their Home Base is increased by 5% except herself.

(Corday) Illusionist (False) — Rank: C+  
(Active) Cannot be used if inflicted with Silence. Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, it has a 30% chance of failing; if Failed, it does not enter Cooldown but it cannot be used again this Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself- Inflicts Disorder for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units. At the start of every Turn, a Unit inflicted with Disorder has a 30% chance of being inflicted with Skill Seal for that Turn (30% Disorder).  
4\. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns, 1 time, to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  

(Corday) Ludicrous Show Planning — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within a 3 panel area of herself. Has a 70% chance of inflicting Skill Seal for ⅓◈ Turns. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within 2 panel area of herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Corday) Angel of Tricks — Rank: EX  
(Passive) All of Charlotte’s Attacks are categorized as ‘Attacks from behind’. When a Unit inflicted with a ‘Sealing’ effect other than NP Seal Evades an Attack from Charlotte, its Evade roll is increased by 2\.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Charlotte’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then choose an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself and reduce its NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Then, Charlotte can swap places with that selected allied Unit (unless it was herself) and if she does, inflict Addle for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of her previous position. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
