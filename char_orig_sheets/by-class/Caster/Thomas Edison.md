(Edison) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX (D)  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP; but the total value of the roll is ~~doubled~~ halved.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP; but the total value of this effect is halved.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Edison) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: EX (D)  
(Passive) This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
1\. The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
2\. The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit directly next to Edison who uses a ‘Weapon’. Has a 100% chance of applying the ‘Invention’ effect on the DU; this chance is reduced by 20% for every Rank in Divinity the Unit has; if the Unit has the ‘Divine’ Attribute but not the ‘Divinity’ Skill, chance is reduced by 50% instead. For every stack of ‘Invention’ on a Unit (max. 3 stacks), its Base Attack (STR) is permanently increased 10, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Edison) Shapeshift — Rank: C  
(Passive) Reduces all damage received by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Process when Edison is Attacked. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Edison) The Wizard of Menlo Park — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Edison. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%; and reduces its Skill Cooldown by 1◈ Turns (does not affect Spell Cooldown). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Edison) Mass Production — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp to himself for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Edison is increased by 10%.  
If this Skill is used when Edison is within his Home Base, the duration of NP Regen, NP DmUp, and Area CritUp is reduced to 1◈ Turns, then roll a 4-sided die. The number rolled determines the number of Mechanized Infantry created by Edison, Edison cannot Attack on the same Turn as this Skill was used if used in this way. The Stats of Mechanized Infantry are as follows-  
Health: 500  
Agility: 12  
Luck: 3  
MOV: 3  
Range: 3 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 75  
Attributes: Mechanical  
Detect: 3 panels  
When counting the number of Units who Move/Attack during your Turn, one Mechanized Infantry counts as 0.25 of a Servant (i.e. 4 Mechanized Infantry are equal to 1 Servant). The same Mechanized Infantry can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Edison or his Master if any Mechanized Infantry are within a 2 panel area of them.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Edison) Conceptual Improvement — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Edison. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Overcharge for ⅓◈ Turns, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are ~~increased by 100%~~ doubled.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.   
3\. Applies the ‘Improvement’ effect for 1 time, the success chance of Edison’s next usage of the Active effect of his ‘Item Construction’ Skill is increased by 30%. The ‘Improvement’ effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Edison) Single-Mindedness — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies NP Regen (Singleminded) for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turns at the end of every Turn; and applies Crit Up (Singleminded) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Attacks which use Base Attack (STR) is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) While Edison has a ‘Singleminded’ buff, when Edison creates Mechanized Infantry with his ‘Mass Production’ Skill, he always creates 4 Mechanized Infantry, the dice roll is unneeded.

(Edison) World Faith Domination: W・F・D — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Populace\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits the 7x7 panel area around Edison for 5x damage, then inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns and Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns. Also inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 50%. Does not affect himself.  
Then, has an X% chance of applying the ‘WFD’ effect on the DU, X being 10 for each of Rank in MAG the Unit has plus each Rank in Divinity the Unit has (e.g. Rank B MAG and Rank D Divinity, 40%+20%=60% chance). If the Unit has the ‘Divine’ Attribute but not the ‘Divinity’ Skill, X is increased by 30 instead. For every stack of ‘WFD’ effect on a Unit, its Base Attack (MAG) is reduced by 25; this effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable.  
If the ‘WFD’ effect was not applied to a DU, then Unit is inflicted with Atk Dwn (MAG) for 1◈ Turns instead, all MAG damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
