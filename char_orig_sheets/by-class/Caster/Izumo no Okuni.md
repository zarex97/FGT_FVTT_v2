When Izumo no Okuni is summoned, she is summoned along with Zanzaburou. Both Okuni and Zanzaburou have their individual Stats, however Zanzaburou cannot Move beyond 3 panels of Okuni. Note: Moving Zanzaburou does not count towards the number of Units Moved during a Turn.

Okuni cannot be Attacked if Zanzaburou is in between Okuni and the AU.

(Okuni) Class Skill: Territory Creation (Kagura) — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d8 after calculation including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+10) including NP.  
(Passive 3\) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 5%; 10% if within her Home Base.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Okuni) Class Skill: Item Construction (Karakuri) — Rank: C  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 3\) Crit Damage dealt by Zanzaburou is increased by 5%; 10% if within her Home Base.  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Okuni) New Okuni Kabuki — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When Okuni & Zanzaburou perform a Normal Attack during their Turn, they can perform it in the following methods-  
1\. Both Okuni & Zanzaburou perform a separate Normal Attack, in this case Okuni’s Attack does not count towards the number of Units that Attack during a Turn, but only if Zanzaburou has already Attacked.  
2\. Both Okuni & Zanzaburou Attack the same target simultaneously. In this case, calculate damage separately for both of them (Zanzaburou first), but the DU can only respond to Zanzaburou’s Attack.  
(Passive 2\) If Zanzaburou is defeated, he can revive with 50% of his Max Health at the start of the Round after 4 full Rounds have passed. But if Okuni is defeated, Zanzaburou will disappear from the field at the end of the Round after 2 full Rounds have passed; Okuni’s Master can extend the duration of Zanzaburou’s existence by 1 full Round with a Command Spell.
