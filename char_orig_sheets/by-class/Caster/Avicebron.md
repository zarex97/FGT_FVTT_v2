(Avicebron) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B+  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 45%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 45%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Avicebron) Item Construction: Golems  
Avicebron has incredibly low combat abilities. His main form of combat is by creating Golems and having them fight.  
(Active) When this Skill is used, Avicebron summons a number of Golems. The number summoned is dependant on the situation-  
1\. If Avicebron is within his Home Base, roll a ten-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Golems summoned.  
2\. If Avicebron is not within his Home Base, roll a six-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Golems summoned.  
A Golem’s Stats are as follows-  
Health: 500  
Agility: 12  
Luck: 6  
MOV: 3  
Range: 1 panel, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 100  
Attributes: \[Sky\], Mechanical  
Detect: 3 panels  
When counting the number of Units who Move/Attack during your Turn, one Golem counts as 0.2 of a Servant (i.e. 5 Golems are equal to 1 Servant). The same Golem can only Move/Attack once per Turn. There can only be a maximum of 30 Golems on the field. An allied Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack if there is a Golem within a 2 panel area of the Unit.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Avicebron) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Avicebron) Numerology — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When used, all Golems on the field restore 3 Luck. Then, Atk Up is applied to all Golems on the field for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Avicebron) High-Speed Incantation — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce the Cooldown of all Skills by 1◈ Turns, then gain 5 Construction Points. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill cannot be used and its effects are negated while inflicted with Silence.

(Avicebron) Fig Tree of Tranquility — Rank: EX  
(Passive) This Skill is automatically activated when Avicebron is defeated and not revived. Applies Invuln to all allied Units within a 7x7 panel area of himself for 3 times, and restores the Health of those affected allied Units by 20% of their maximum value. Can only be used once.

(Avicebron) Golem Keter Malkuth: Royal Crown, the Light of Wisdom — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Golem Keter Malkuth (GKM) can only be used when Construction Points reach 50\. There are several ways to gain Construction Points-  
1\. Increased by 1 at the end of every Round.  
2\. When Avicebron uses Item Construction: Golems, Construction is increased by 1 for every 3 Golems created.  
3\. When Avicebron uses Numerology, Construction is increased by 1 for every 2 Golems on the field (Max increase is 10).  
4\. For every 5 Golems that are destroyed/defeated by enemy Units, Construction is increased by 1\.  
5\. During your Turn, Avicebron and/or his Master can perform ‘Gather’, which increases Construction by 2 per Unit. Using ‘Gather’ counts as a Unit’s ‘Move’ for that Turn. A Unit cannot Attack on the same Turn it uses ‘Gather’.  
When Construction Points reaches 50, GKM can be used; and when it is, it is summoned directly next to Avicebron. Avicebron then moves onto the top of GKM. Avicebron cannot be targeted by Attacks while GKM is active. GKM has the following Stats-  
Health: 2000 (4000)  
Agility: 6 (12)  
Luck: Shared with Avicebron  
MOV: 3 (6)  
Range: 1 panel, 2x2 panel area (3x3 panel area)  
Base Attack (STR): 150 (300)  
Attributes: Giant, ~~Mechanical~~  
Detect: 5 panels  
GKM can Move to any panel, when it Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for GKM to stand on.  
GKM’s Normal Attacks knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
At the start of every Round ~~(1◈ Turns)~~ since GKM is summoned, GKM’s Max and current Health will increase by 200, until a maximum of 4000 is reached.  
At the start of the Round every 3 full Rounds ~~(3◈ Turns)~~ since GKM is summoned, its Max and current Agility will increase by 2 until a maximum of 12 is reached; and its MOV will increase by 1 until a maximum of 6 is reached.  
At the start of the Round, 5 full Rounds (5◈ Turns) since GKM is summoned, its Range increases from a 2x2 panel area to a 3x3 panel area.  
At the start of every Round ~~(1◈ Turns)~~ since GKM is summoned, its BA (STR) will increase by 10\. From the sixth activation of this effect onwards, its BA (STR) will increase by 20 instead of 10 instead, until a maximum of 300 is reached.  
At the start of its Turn, GKM’s Health is restored by 500 and Agility is restored by 1\.  
All allied Units within a 2 panel area of GKM gain the following effect- At the end of this Unit’s Turn and at the end of any Turn this Unit Acts, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn; 2 Turns if ◈ is greater than 4\.  
Avicebron is unable to create Golems while GKM is Active. When GKM is defeated, Avicebron is returned to a panel within the area GKM was last standing on. After GKM is defeated, Avicebron can attempt to rebuild GKM from scratch (its Construction is reduced to 0).  
Note: For Avicebron’s ‘Numerology’ Skill, GKM counts as a Golem.
