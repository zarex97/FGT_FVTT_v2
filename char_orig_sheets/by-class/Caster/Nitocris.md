(Nitocris) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d12 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Nitocris) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B+  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 45%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 45%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Nitocris) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Nitocris) High-Speed Divine Words — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Completely reduce the cooldown of two of Nitocris’ Spells, and reduce her NP Cooldown by 3◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
Note: Cannot be used while inflicted with Silence.

(Nitocris) Affection of Horus — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Removes all debuffs from Nitocris; then applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Nitocris is defeated she is revived with 15% of her Max Health. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nitocris) Egyptian Magecraft — Rank: A  
Nitocris’ thaumaturgic ability.

(Nitocris) Summoning: Mummy  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Roll a four-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Mummies conjured by Nitocris (they will appear within a 5x5 panel area around Nitocris).  
Each Mummy has 50 Health, 8 Agility, 4 Luck, Range=1, MOV=3 and Base Attack (STR) is 100, Detect=2 panels; their Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Curse.  
Mummies do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Mummy can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Nitocris or her Master if any Mummies are directly next to them.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Summoning: Medjed.  
Cooldown: (Number of Mummies summoned x ⅔◈) Turns

(Nitocris) Summoning: Medjed  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Summons a Medjed next to Nitocris.  
A Medjed has 200 Health, 10 Agility, 7 Luck, Range=3, MOV=4 and Base Attack (MAG) is 125, Detect=3 panels.  
Medjeds do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Medjed can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Nitocris or her Master if any Medjeds are directly next to them.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Summoning: Mummy.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns
