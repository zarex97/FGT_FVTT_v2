(Mephisto) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Mephisto) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: C+  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (5d8+5) including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+12) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Mephisto) Witchcraft — Rank: A  
(Passive) Mephisto’s Attacks have an 80% chance to ignore Magic Resistance. Negated while affected by Skill Seal or Silence.

(Mephisto) A Curse Just For You — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit. Has a 90% chance of increasing the Unit’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; if NP Cooldown was successfully increased, Mephisto’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns. Then, apply Dmg Up (Curse) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with Curse is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Mephisto) Innocent Monster — Rank: B  
(Passive) Mephisto’s Crit Chance is increased by 10%.

(Mephisto) Impotent Rage — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 5%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Inflicts Heal Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within the Skill area, when Health is restored, the amount restored is reduced by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Mephisto) Clown’s Laughter — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts No Buff for 3 times and inflicts Curse. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Mephisto) Mephisto Bomb  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals damage to the DU with a 5% chance of inflicting Curse. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Mephisto) Trick Bomb  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals damage to the DU, then roll a twelve-sided die. The DU is inflicted with the debuff indicated by the number.  
1\. Curse  
2\. Burn (2◈ Turns)  
3\. Shock (2◈ Turns)  
4\. Poison  
5\. Immobilize (1◈ Turns)  
6\. Slow (2◈ Turns)  
7\. Bleed (1◈ Turns)  
8\. Charm (1◈ Turns)  
9\. Decoy (1◈ Turns)  
10\. Berserk (1◈ Turns)  
11\. Disable (1◈ Turns)  
12\. Sleep (1◈ Turns)  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Mephisto) Sticky Bomb  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. When successfully used on the DU, the DU gains the ‘Sticko’ effect for X◈ Turns, where X= (the number rolled on a six-sided die x ⅓◈) Turns. When the ‘Sticko’ effect naturally expires, the bomb explodes, dealing 2x damage to the Unit the bomb is stuck to, and 1x damage is dealt to all Units within a 1 panel area of the ‘Sticko’ Unit. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Mephisto) Bomb Juggling.  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Target a random enemy Unit within Range of Mephisto, deal 1.5x damage to that Unit; repeat this 3 times. Each hit has a 5% chance of inflicting Curse. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Note: The same Unit can be targeted repeatedly.

(Mephisto) Ticktock Bomb: Dozing Bomb — Rank: A+ (NP)  
(Activation) Base Attack (MAG) is used. This NP is used on an enemy Unit within Range. If this NP was not Evaded, the DU receives the ‘Ticktock’ effect. Mephisto can cause the Unit with the ‘Ticktock’ effect to explode at any time during the game.  
When the affected Unit explodes, it deals 4x damage plus 100 to the Unit with the ‘Ticktock’ effect; and deals 2x damage plus 100 to all other Units within a 3x3 panel area of the Unit with the ‘Ticktock’ effect. Then, Curse is inflicted to all affected Units.  
When the Unit with the ‘Ticktock’ effect explodes, the Unit cannot Evade it, and it ignores Dodge on that Unit.   
The ‘Ticktock’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff, and is Unremovable; however it is removed if Mephisto is defeated. Mephisto cannot activate the explosion of the ‘Ticktock’ effect on the same Turn he is defeated.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈  Turns.
