(Tamamo) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d8 including NP.

(Tamamo) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) Only in effect while ‘Yata no Kagami’ is activated, negated and has no effects while it is not. All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Tamamo) Witchcraft — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Tamamo’s Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) ignore Magic Resistance. Negated while affected by Skill Seal or Silence.  
(Passive 2\) When Tamamo uses a Mantra Spell other than Jusō: Kōnisshō, Jusō・Kokutendō, and Jusō・Kūretsu Daimitten while she has any Mantra Tokens, remove one Mantra Token from herself and the Mantra Spell that she used does not enter Cooldown. The maximum number of Mantra Tokens Tamamo can have is 3\.

(Tamamo) Jusō・Kokutendō

1\. Gains the ‘Decoy’ effect.  
2\. Cannot Evade and Dodge/Substitution has no effect.  
3\. Tamamo automatically Blocks every Attack received.  
4\. All damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
5\. Whenever Tamamo is Attacked while in the ‘Aphotic’ State, at the end of the Damage Step, reduce her Spell Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns (including this Spell\!). If all Spells are not in Cooldown, then NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns. If all Spells and NP are not in Cooldown, then gain the Dmg Up (Aphotic) buff for 1 time, damage dealt by the next Attack which deals MAG damage is increased by 15% including NP. Dmg Up (Aphotic) can stack and is Unremovable.  
This Spell cannot be used if Tamamo is already in the ‘Aphotic’ state. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tamamo) Suiten Nikkou Amaterasu Yanoshisu Ishi・Yata no Kagami — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Eightfold Blessings of Amaterasu on the Weight Stone under the Sunlit Watery Heavens. Affects a 5x5 panel area around Tamamo, this area will be termed as the ‘Amaterasu Area’. Tamamo cannot leave the Amaterasu Area. Increases Tamamo’s Range by 1 for the NP duration. If the Region is Japan, the Amaterasu Area is 7x7 instead, and Tamamo’s Range is increased by 2\.   
This NP has the following effects-  
1\. All allied Units within the Amaterasu Area upon activation have their Health and Agility and Luck restored by 30% of their maximum values.  
2\. Reduces Skill, Spell, and NP Cooldown of all allied Units within the Amaterasu Area upon activation by 1◈ Turns (excluding this NP’s own Cooldown).  
3\. Increases damage dealt by all allied Units within the Amaterasu Area. ~~Damage dealt which uses BA(MAG)~~ MAG damage dealt is increased by 100%, while ~~damage dealt which uses BA(STR)~~ STR damage dealt is increased by 50%. Applies to Noble Phantasm as well.  
4\. Allied Masters’ ZON do not apply to their Servants within the Amaterasu Area.  
5\. The Sustainability of allied Free Servants do not decrease when they are within the Amaterasu Area.  
6\. All effects of Servants that would reduce its Master’s Health when used/it takes effect is halved including Noble Phantasm usage.  
7\. The maximum number of Mantra Tokens she can hold is increased to 6, then she gains Mantra Tokens up to the maximum amount.  
8\. Tamamo’s ‘Divinity’ Skill becomes activated.  
9\. If Tamamo is defeated, this NP is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn.  
This Noble Phantasm lasts for 2◈ Turns (3◈ Turns if the Region is Japan). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns after it ends.

4\. Hits a random enemy Unit within the Spell area for 50% damage with a 25% chance of inflicting Shock for 1◈ Turns, Lightning damage.  
5\. Hits a random enemy Unit within the Spell area for 50% damage with a 25% chance of inflicting Slow for 1◈ Turns, Water damage.

9\. Then, deals 2x damage to all enemy Units within the Spell area and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns, Fire damage.  
When this Spell is used, Suiten Nikkou Amaterasu Yanoshisu Ishi・Yata no Kagami is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, and its Cooldown is increased by an additional 1◈ Turns.
