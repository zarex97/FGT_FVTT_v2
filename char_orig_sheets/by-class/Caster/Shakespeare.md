(Shakespeare) Non-combatant  
(Passive) All Total Damage dealt is reduced by 75% including NP.

(Shakespeare) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d8 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+10) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Shakespeare) Enchant — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself except himself. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
If used on an allied Master, apply the ‘Enchant’ buff on that Unit for 2◈ Turns instead. The effects of the ‘Enchant’ buff are as follows-  
1\. The Master cannot be Overpowered by Servants.  
2\. The Master cannot be Underpowered by Servants.  
3\. MOV is increased by 2\.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Shakespeare) Self-Preservation — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever Shakespeare is Attacked and receives damage, he can Move 3 panels after receiving damage and if he does so, his Health cannot drop below 1 from that Attack. This Skill cannot be used in this way if Shakespeare’s Master would become able to be Attacked by enemy Servants after Shakespeare Moves and his Master does not have the ‘Enchant’ buff. (See ‘**Rules – Relationships**’ for details.)

(Shakespeare) Iambic Pentameter — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns  
2\. Restore Shakespeare’s Health by 20% of its maximum value and restore 2 Agility.  
3\. Restore the Health of all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself by 10% of their maximum values and restore 1 Agility.  
4\. NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.  
5\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 10%; if his Master is within the Skill area, apply as ‘NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%’ instead.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Shakespeare) The King’s Men — Rank: C  
Enables the use of Shakespeare’s ‘The King’s Men’ Spells. Shakespeare can only use one ‘The King’s Men’ Spell per Turn.

(Shakespeare) The King’s Men: Encouragement  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Shakespeare OR used on an allied Unit that is standing directly next to a ‘Stagehand’. Reduce its NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns and apply Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 100%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Shakespeare) The King’s Men: Dissuasion  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of Shakespeare OR used on an enemy Unit that is standing directly next to a ‘Stagehand’. Increase its NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then roll a six-sided die and apply the stated effect, as follows-  
1\. Inflict NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is reduced by 40%.  
2\. Inflict Berserk for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Inflict Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 40%.  
4\. Inflict Confuse for 1◈ Turns.  
5\. Inflict Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns.  
6\. Inflict Sleep for 1◈ Turns.  
If the affected Unit’s END and MAG Ranks are B- or higher (both are required), chance of being affected by the dice roll’s debuff is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Shakespeare) The King’s Men: Stagehands  
Spell. Used during your Turn. Roll a six-sided die and add 2 to that number, that is the number of ‘Stagehands’ summoned within a 2 panel area of Shakespeare. Stagehands have a MOV of 4 and have the Decoy effect. A Stagehand can Move once on every Turn. Stagehands disappear from the field 1◈ Turns after being Summoned, at the end of the Turn. When a Stagehand is Attacked, it immediately disappears, and has a 10% chance of inflicting Blind for 1◈ Turns on the AU if the AU was within a 2 panel area of the Stagehand. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Shakespeare) The Globe: The King’s Men — Rank: ??? (NP) \[???\]  
(Non-damaging) Hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Shakespeare. All affected enemy Units have a 75% chance of being inflicted with a debuff. If the Unit is successfully inflicted with the debuff, roll a four-sided die, and apply the corresponding effect-  
1\. Inflicted with Blind for 2 ◈Turns.  
2\. Inflicted with Sleep for 2◈ Turns.  
3\. Inflicted with Berserk for 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Shakespeare’s choice of 1, 2 or 3\.  
Then, summon 12 Stagehands within a 3 panel area of Shakespeare, and fully reduce the Cooldown for all of Shakespeare’s ‘The King’s Men’ spells. Stagehands summoned with this Noble Phantasm disappear after 2◈ Turns instead of 1◈ Turns.  
If an affected Unit’s END and MAG Ranks are B- or higher (both are required), it only has a 25% chance of being affected by the dice roll’s effect.  
Then, Shakespeare gains the ‘Globe’ effect for 2◈ Turns- when Dissuasion is used, roll two six-sided die and apply both debuffs (if the same number is rolled or a combination of Berserk, Confuse and/or Sleep is rolled, roll again). The ‘Globe’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
