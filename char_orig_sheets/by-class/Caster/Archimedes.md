(Archimedes) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A+  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 55%; this effect is halved for Instakill (30%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 55%; this effect is halved for Instakill (30%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Archimedes) High Speed Incantation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns and gain 3 Slaughter Tokens (max. 3). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive) If Archimedes uses a ‘Machine of Slaughter’ Spell while he has any Slaughter Tokens, remove one Slaughter Token from himself, while the Spell that he used does not enter Cooldown.

(Archimedes) Technique Elucidation — Rank: A  
(Passive) Activates when Archimedes is Attacked with an Attack that ~~uses Base Attack (MAG)~~ deals MAG damage. At the end of the Combat Process, note down the AU and the Attack that was used against Archimedes (e.g. if it was a Normal Attack, note it like ‘Unit’s name: Normal Attack’). The next time Archimedes receives that Attack again, reduce the Total Damage received by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Restore the Unit’s Health by 15% of its maximum value and remove all Offensive Debuffs on that Unit. Then, undo/remove all effects that are temporarily and/or permanently reducing the Unit’s Base Attack (STR & MAG) if any except Volatile debuffs, even if those effects are Unremovable. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Archimedes) Aptitude for Slaughter (Tools) — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute is increased by 50%. Does not apply to Noble Phantasm.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Dmg Up (Slaughter) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Archimedes performs a successful Normal Attack on a Unit with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute, reduce the Cooldown of ‘Aptitude for Slaughter (Tools)’ by 1 Turn. Does not activate on the same Turn he used the Skill.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever Archimedes uses a ‘Machines of Slaughter’ Spell, extend the duration of Dmg Up (Slaughter) on himself by ½◈ Turns. 

(Archimedes) Machines of Slaughter  
The inventions of Archimedes. All Machine of Slaughter Spells share a Cooldown of 2◈ Turns. When one Spell is on Cooldown, the others cannot be used until Cooldown ends for that Spell.

(Archimedes) Machines of Slaughter: Syrakousón Méthodos  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Deals 50% extra damage. After successfully hitting the targeted Unit, this Spell can hit an additional enemy Unit that is within a 3 panel area of the targeted Unit; this additional enemy Unit cannot Counter. Water damage.

(Archimedes) Machines of Slaughter: Archimedean Reflection  
Damage Spell. Hits a 1x4 or 4x1 panel area in any direction next to Archimedes for 25% extra damage (if diagonal, 1x3 or 3x1), and inflicts Blind for 1◈ Turns.

(Archimedes) Machines of Slaughter: Wizard of Balance  
Spell. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Archimedes or the 5x5 panel area around him. Inflicts Slow on all affected enemy Units for 2◈ Turns. Counts as Archimedes’ Attack for the Turn.

(Archimedes) Machines of Slaughter: Syrakousón Harpage  
Damage Spell. Range=3. Deals 50% extra damage, then Archimedes can Move the DU up to 3 panels in any direction at the end of the Damage Step.

(Archimedes) Κατόπτρων Κατω φλέγον: Clustered Straws, Burn Out like the Moon — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Range=5. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage. If this NP is used on a ‘Ship’, all Units onboard the ‘Ship’ receive full damage and are inflicted with Burn for 4◈ Turns, then the ‘Ship’ is immediately destroyed at the end of the Damage Step. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
