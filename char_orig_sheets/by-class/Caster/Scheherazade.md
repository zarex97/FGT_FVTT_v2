(Scheherazade) Storyteller — Rank: EX

(Scheherazade) Bedchambers of Survival — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on a 3x3 panel area in any direction next to Scheherazade. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Sleep on all affected Male enemy Units for 1◈ Turns. If Sleep is not inflicted, this Skill then has a 60% chance of inflicting Charm on all affected Male enemy Units for 1◈ Turns. Then, apply Def Up for 1◈ Turns to Scheherazade, all damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Scheherazade) Counter-Hero (Speech) — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of Scheherazade. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns on that enemy Unit if it has the ‘King’ Attribute, all damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Applies Guts to Scheherazade for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when she is defeated she is revived with 20% of her Max Health.  
3\. Applies Guts to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of Scheherazade for ⅔◈ Turns, when the Unit is defeated it is revived with 5% of its Max Health.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
