(Alexandre) Sublimation  
(Passive) All Total Damage dealt is reduced by 80% including NP.

(Alexandre) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d4 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by 3d10 including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Alexandre) Class Skill: Item Construction (Revision) — Rank: EX  
See ‘Grand Dictionnaire de Cuisine: The Long Unended Epicurean Saga’.

(Alexandre) Era Observer — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Chance of being inflicted by Mental Debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
(Passive 2\) When affected by Offensive Debuffs, the magnitude of the debuff is reduced by half.

(Alexandre) Gourmand — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Health and Agility restored from being in his Home Base at the end of the Round is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Alexandre uses a Consumable Item on himself, any Stats restored have the restored amount doubled, the magnitude & duration of any buffs applied are doubled, and the magnitude & duration of any debuffs or negative effects are halved.

(Alexandre) Innocent Monster — Rank: E  
(Passive) Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 5%; NP is not affected. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Alexandre) Grand Dictionnaire de Cuisine: The Long Unended Epicurean Saga — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Object\]  
(Non-damaging) Can be used 2 full Rounds after the game has begun. Used during your Turn. Alexandre is able to Construct an Item from either the Weapon, Treasure, or Consumable category. The Player informs the GM roughly what kind of Item he wants (e.g. a sword, protection, magic enhancer, etc), and the GM will randomly select an Item that meets that criteria and give it to Alexandre.  
However, Alexandre and other Servants cannot use/Equip Weapon or Treasure Items crafted by Alexandre, and neither can he Construct Items that are Servants only. When Alexandre is standing directly next to an allied Master or allied non-Servant Unit, he can pass them as many Items as he wishes. Additionally, Alexandre cannot Construct Rare Items, which have their names marked with a \* (or will have its name written in this colour on the Item Categories list/page).  
Note: Alexandre can construct an Item from the Composite category, but that Item cannot have the ‘Special’ category included.  
When Alexandre is defeated, all Items Constructed by him can no longer be used and are discarded.  
Cooldown: (X+1)◈ Turns, X being the number rolled on a six-sided die. Roll the die after each time Alexandre Constructs an Item. However, Cooldown after Constructing a Consumable Item is always 2◈ Turns. When this NP is used, Alexandre’s Master loses X0 Health instead of the regular NP Health loss, X being the number rolled on the six-sided die to determine the Cooldown for the NP.  
Note: When an Item Constructed by Alexandre and its original version of the Item are in existence/on the field at the same time, the Item Constructed by Alexandre can no longer be used and discarded.  
(Passive) Consumable Items created by Alexandre gain the following effects-  
1\. Stat restoration will have the restored amount doubled.  
2\. The magnitude & duration of any buffs applied are doubled.  
3\. The magnitude & duration of any debuffs or negative effects are halved.  
4\. The above three effects do not stack with Passive 2 of his ‘Gourmand’ Skill.

(Alexandre) Musketeers' Masquerade: Musketeers, Challenge the Windmills — Rank: E\~A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) When Alexandre is summoned, roll a six-sided die. The number rolled determines the number of times this NP can be used.  
Range=3. Used on Alexandre’s Master. Applies the ‘Musketeer’ effect to his Master, it has the following effects-  
1\. Roll a ten-sided die. All damage dealt by Alexandre’s Master is increased by X0% including NP.  
2\. Roll a six-sided die. All damage taken by Alexandre’s Master is reduced by X0% including NP.  
3\. Roll an eight-sided die. Alexandre’s Master’s Max & current Health is increased by 50\*X.  
4\. Roll a four-sided die and add 2 to the number. Alexandre’s Master’s Max & current Agility is increased by X; while its MOV is increased by the number originally rolled on the four-sided die.  
5\. Alexandre’s Master cannot be Overpowered and Underpowered by Servants.  
The ‘Musketeer’ effect is neither a buff nor a debuff and is Unremovable. The ‘Musketeer’ effect lasts for 2◈ Turns if the number of times Musketeers' Masquerade can be used is more than 3; while it lasts for 3◈ Turns instead if the number of times Musketeers' Masquerade can be used is 3 or less.  
Cooldown: (X+1)◈+⅓◈ Turns, X being the number rolled on a six-sided die. Roll the die after each time Alexandre uses Musketeers' Masquerade. When this NP is used, Alexandre’s Master loses (X+1)0 Health instead of the regular NP Health loss, X being the number rolled on the six-sided die to determine the Cooldown for the NP.  
Once per game(?), Alexandre's Master can use a Command Spell with (the normal usage of) this NP to activate all its effects at the maximum value.  
Note: For Effects 1 to 4, roll the dice every time the NP is used to determine the value for that usage.
