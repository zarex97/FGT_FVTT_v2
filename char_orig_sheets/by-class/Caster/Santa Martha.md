(Martha) Holy Night  
(Passive) All damage dealt during a Night Round as dictated by the game state is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.

(Martha) Let Onee-chan Cook For You\!  
(Passive 1\) At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Passive 2\) All Total Damage dealt is reduced by 50%.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever Martha performs a Normal Attack, she creates a \[Christmas Food\] Item at the end of the Combat Process. When this Item is created, it can be given to any allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself, then the Item can be used immediately or held for later. Martha can only create up to three ~~(four?)~~ \[Christmas Food\] Items per Round in this way.  
Whenever a \[Christmas Food\] Item is created, roll a six-sided die to determine which Item is created, the list of \[Christmas Food\] Items is at the bottom of the character sheet.

(Martha) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Martha) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: D  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Martha) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: EX  
Tarasque.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Martha) Martha’s Home Cooking — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 15% of its original value.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Then, if Martha has not already created 3 \[Christmas Food\] Items this Round, she creates 3 \[Christmas Food\] Items which are given to random allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Martha) Elder Sister’s Meddling — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Inflicts NP Seal for ⅓◈ Turns and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Martha) Santa Claus’s Invitation — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
3\. Reduces Martha’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Martha) Happy Merry Christmas: May Everyone Enjoy this Christmas Feast — Rank: C+ (NP) \[Cooking\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of herself. However, if Martha is within her Home Base when this NP is used, it affects all allied Units within her Home Base instead. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Martha) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’, ‘Divine’, or ‘Undead’ Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
4\. Restores 300 Health.  
Then, all affected Units are given one random \[Christmas Food\] Item.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.
