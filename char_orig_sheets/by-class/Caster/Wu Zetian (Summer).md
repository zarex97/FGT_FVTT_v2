(Zetian) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Zetian) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Zetian) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Zetian) Ambition for Imperial Ascension — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Zetian is Immune to Mental Debuffs.

(Zetian) Consort’s Charisma (Summer) — Rank: EX  
(Passive) At the start of the Round, all Male enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Zetian roll a six-sided die. A Unit which rolls a 1 is inflicted with Charm for the rest of the Round. Ignores Debuff Resist effects (but not Debuff Immune effects). Negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panels of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. If there are 2 (3?) or more ‘Summer’ Servants within this Skill’s area, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Zetian) Inauspicious Wu Princess — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Increase the NP Cooldown of all affected enemy Units by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Applies Dmg Up (Undead) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Undead’ Attribute is increased by 30%. Then, reduce their NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Zetian) Empress’ Taoist Arts — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Damage dealt by Normal Attacks which use Base Attack (STR) is increased by 60 against Units with the ‘Undead’ Attribute.   
(Passive 2\) When performing a successful Normal Attack on a Unit with the ‘Jiangshi’ effect, extend the duration of the ‘Jiangshi’ effect on the DU by ½◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Inflicts Stun for ⅓◈ Turns to all affected enemy Units with the ‘Undead’ Attribute. This Stun debuff is unaffected by effects that modify the chance of inflicting debuffs, Debuff Immune, or Debuff Resist. This includes effects that specifically resist Stun.  
2\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Restore their Health and Agility by 20% of their maximum values and remove all debuffs from the affected Units.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Zetian) Summoning: Summer Jiangshi  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Counts as Zetian’s Attack for the Turn. Roll a six-sided die. Zetian summons that number of Summer Jiangshi on the panels directly around her. The Stats of a Summer Jiangshi are as follows-  
Health: 300  
Agility: 8  
Luck: Half of Zetian’s Max Luck  
MOV: 3  
Range: 1 panel, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 125  
Attributes: Female, Humanoid, Undead  
Detect: 2 panels  
Normal Attacks of a Summer Jiangshi have a 30% chance of applying the ‘Jiangshi’ effect for 1◈ Turns to the DU at the end of the Damage Step.  
Summer Jiangshi do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack per Turn. The same Summer Jiangshi can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Zetian or her Master if any Summer Jiangshi are within a 2 panel area of them.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Zetian) Láilái, Kù Lì Jiāngshī-mén — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Come Hither, Atrocious Jiangshi Corps. Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, apply NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns to herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 10% plus 5% more for every Summer Jiangshi on the field (max. 30%). Range+2 for the Combat Process. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. First, apply the ‘Jiangshi’ effect for 2◈ Turns on all affected Units. Deal 3.5x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the \[Man\] Attribute. Then, inflict Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

Note: The effects of the ‘Jiangshi’ effect are as follows- A Unit with the ‘Jiangshi’ effect has the ‘Undead’ Attribute and when performing an Agility Roll, treat the affected Unit’s Current Agility as half of its actual value. The ‘Jiangshi’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Does not stack.
