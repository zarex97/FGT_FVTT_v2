(Morgan) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Morgan) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Morgan) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: C  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Morgan) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Morgan) Glam Sight: Fairy Eyes — Rank: C

(Morgan) Charisma of Blessing — Rank: A

1\. Atk Up  
2\. Atk Up (**≥** 80%)  
3\. Atk Up (**≥** 60%)  
4\. Atk Up (**≥** 40%)  
5\. Atk Up (**≥** 20%)

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Morgan) Last Resort — Rank: A  
This Skill cannot be used until the start of a Round where Morgan is able to use her NP.  
(Trigger) At the start of a Round where Morgan is able to use her NP, the Cooldown of this Skill is increased by 3◈ Turns. Only activates once.  
(Passive 1\) Whenever an enemy Unit is defeated, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill. 1◈ Turns if it was a Servant or a ‘Strong’ Unit, ⅔◈ Turns if it was a Master or a ‘Average’ Unit, and ⅓◈ Turns if it was a Mob Unit (e.g. Civilians, Dragon Tooth Warriors, Horrors, basically most Units which are summoned through Skills/Spells/NP and are not Masters or Servants) or a ‘Weak’ Unit.    
(Passive 2\) Whenever Morgan performs a non-NP Attack which causes an Injury Roll, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by half of their respective maximum NP Cooldowns.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when an affected Unit is defeated it is revived with 300 Health.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
