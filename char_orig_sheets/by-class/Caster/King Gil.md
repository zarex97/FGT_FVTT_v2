(KingGil) Class Skill: Item Construction (False) — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 3\) Negated while inflicted with NP Seal.  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(KingGil) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d12 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(KingGil) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(KingGil) Golden Rule — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever this Unit performs a Crit, reduce its NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.

(KingGil) King’s Return — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce King Gil’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then apply the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.   
2\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by ⅓◈ Turns for each allied Unit within the Skill area.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(KingGil) Charisma — Rank: A+

3\. Evade Rolls are reduced by 2; for King Gil, 4\. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks.

5\. The Range of Melammu Dingir is increased to 7 panels.  
6\. Crit Chance is increased by 30%; for King Gil, 50%.  
Cannot be deactivated. While King Gil is inflicted with NP Seal, the effects of this NP are negated.
