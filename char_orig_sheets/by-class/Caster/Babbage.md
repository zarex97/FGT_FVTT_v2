(Babbage) Single-Mindedness — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies NP Regen for ⅔◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn; and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 100%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Babbage) Mechanized Armor — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Improves Babbage’s Parameters. All damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%; and applies Invuln for ⅔◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Passive) Overload — Rank: D  
(Passive) Whenever Babbage uses a Skill, he has a 20% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns on himself after the effects resolve. When Babbage uses a NP, he has a 60% chance of inflicting Burn for 3◈ Turns on himself after the effects resolve.

(Babbage) Steam Engine Output Rising — Rank: B  
(Passive) While Babbage is inflicted with Burn, gain the following effects-  
1\. Base Attack is not reduced  
2\. MOV is increased by 1\.  
3\. Attacks which use Base Attack (STR) deal 40 extra damage.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 25%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
3\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Burn to himself for 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.     
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Babbage) Dimension of Steam: Difference Engine  
Used during your Turn. Babbage creates a Mechanical Golem on a panel next to him. Counts as Babbage’s Attack for the Turn. The Mechanical Golem has the following stats-  
Health: 500  
Agility: 12  
Luck: 4  
MOV: 3  
Range: 1 panel, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 100  
Attributes: Mechanical  
Detect: 2 panels  
When counting the number of Units who Move/Attack during your Turn, one Mechanical Golem counts as 0.33 of a Servant (i.e. 3 Mechanical Golems are equal to 1 Servant). The same Mechanical Golem can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Babbage or his Master if any Mechanical Golems are within a 2 panel area of them. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Analytical Engine. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns (1◈ Turns if used within Babbage’s Home Base)

(Babbage) Dimension of Steam: Analytical Engine  
Used during your Turn. Babbage’s Health is reduced by 200 (100 if Analytical Engine was used within his Home Base) and creates 3 Mechanical Golems within a 2 panel area of him. Counts as Babbage’s Attack for the Turn. When counting the number of Units who Move/Attack during your Turn, one Mechanical Golem counts as 0.33 of a Servant (i.e. 3 Mechanical Golems are equal to 1 Servant). The same Mechanical Golem can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Babbage or his Master if any Mechanical Golems are within a 2 panel area of them. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Difference Engine. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Babbage) Dimension of Steam: Gorgeous World of Ashes — Rank: EX (NP)  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Babbage for 5x damage; then inflicts Def Dwn on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.  
Note: If Babbage is inflicted with NP Seal, he also cannot use any ‘Dimension of Steam’ Skills.
