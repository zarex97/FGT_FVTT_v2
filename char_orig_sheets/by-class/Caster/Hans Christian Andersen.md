(Andersen) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: C  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Andersen) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d6 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+5) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Andersen) Human Observation — Rank: A++  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 10%, if the DU has the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20% instead.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns to affected Units with the ‘Good’ Alignment, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to affected Units with the ‘Neutral’ Alignment (but without ‘Good’ or ‘Evil’), NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Also affects Units with no Alignment.  
4\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to affected Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
5\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to affected Units whose Alignment is neither ‘Good’, ‘Neutral’, or ‘Evil’ (e.g. Summer, Insane, Bride), Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%. Also affects Units with no Alignment.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns-⅔◈ Turns.

(Andersen) Märchen Meines Lebens: A Story Just For You — Rank: C+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used on an allied Unit within Range. This NP gets stronger the higher the Märchen counter is. The methods to increase the Märchen counter are as follows-  
1\. At the end of every Round, the Märchen counter is increased by 1\.  
2\. Whenever Andersen uses a Spell, the Märchen counter is increased by 2\.  
3\. Whenever Andersen uses a Skill (excluding Spells and High Speed Incantation), the Märchen counter is increased by 1\.  
4\. During your Turn, Andersen can perform the ‘Write’ Action, which increases the Märchen counter by 1d8 (1d12 if performed while in his Home Base). Andersen cannot Move or Attack on the same Turn he uses ‘Write’.

When this NP is used, it applies the Märchen buff for X◈ Turns, for every 3 Märchen counters, X is increased by 1 (minimum of X is 1). The Märchen buff has the following effects-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by (3\*number of Märchen counters)% including Magic Crest; if NP, the magnitude is halved.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by (3\*number of Märchen counters)%; if NP, the magnitude is halved.  
3\. Crit Chance is increased by 5% for every 3 Märchen counters.  
4\. Has the Over Crit effect.  
5\. Health is restored by (10\*number of Märchen counters) at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of every Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
6\. If the affected unit is a Master, it has the following additional effects-  
a. The Master cannot be Overpowered or Underpowered by Servants.  
b. The Master’s Max and current Agility is increased by 1 for every 3 Märchen counters.  
c. The Master’s MOV is increased by 1 for every 3 Märchen counters (maximum increase is 4).  
When Märchen Meines Lebens is used, reduce the number of Märchen counters to 0, and the number of Märchen counters cannot increase until the effects of this NP on the affected Unit ends. The Märchen buff is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.
