(Helena) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Helena) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d12 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Helena) Mana Tuning — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Reduces NP Cooldown by ½◈ Turns. However, if the affected Unit has the same MAG Rank as herself, NP Cooldown is instead reduced by 1◈ Turns; and additionally, her own NP Cooldown would also be reduced by 1◈ Turns instead of ½◈ Turns if there is at least one affected Unit with the same MAG Rank as herself. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Helena) Mahatma — Rank: A++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, roll an eight-sided die. The number rolled indicates the effect that occurs. However, instead of rolling a die, Helena can perform a Luck Check instead. If Successful, she can choose the effect of her choice. Can only be used once per Turn.  
1\. Summons a ‘Blavatsky Familiar’ on a panel directly next to Helena. It has 400 Health, 10 Agility, 8 Luck, MOV=4, Range=2 panels, 1 target, and Base Attack (MAG)=100. It has the ‘Levitating’ and ‘Mechanical’ Attributes. The Blavatsky Familiar does not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn; but can only Move and Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Helena or her Master if the Blavatsky Familiar is directly next to one of them. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp to herself for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 2◈ Turns to herself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 5%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns  
4\. Applies Atk Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns  
5\. Gives Helena the ‘Magic Resistance — Rank: C’ Skill for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns. (‘Mahatma 5’ buff is applied for 1◈ Turns.)  
6\. Helena’s next Normal Attack becomes a Damage Spell that deals 2x damage and inflicts Curse, with an additional 50% chance to inflict an extra Stage of Curse. Dark damage. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns (‘Mahatma 6’ buff is applied for 1 time.)  
7\. Inflicts Confuse for 1◈ Turns; LUC Down for 1◈ Turns, Luck Check rolls are increased by 3; and No Buff for 1 time on all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area round Helena. Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns  
8\. Helena’s next Normal Attack becomes a Damage Spell that deals 50% extra damage and has a 50% chance of increasing the DU’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. If the DU has the  ‘Servant’, ‘Dark’ or ‘Demonic’ Attributes, it deals 2x damage instead and the chance of increasing NP Cooldown is 100% instead of 50%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns (‘Mahatma 8’ buff is applied for 1 time.)  
Mahatma cannot be used while any one of its effects is on Cooldown. Mahatma cannot be used if Helena is inflicted with Silence.  
Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Helena) Pursuit of the Unknown — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Helena. Restores 2 Agility, 2 Luck, and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Helena) Sanat Kumara: Venusian God, Heavenly Lord of the Flame — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=5. Hits a 3x3 panel area for 4x damage plus 100 that has the Ignore Def effect; if a Blavatsky Familiar is on the field, hits a 5x5 panel area instead. Then, has the following effects on all affected Units-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
2\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%.  
3\. Inflicts Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
