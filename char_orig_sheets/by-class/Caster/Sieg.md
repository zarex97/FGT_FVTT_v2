(Sieg) Dead Count Shapeshifter: Dragon Revelation Command Spells — Rank: EX  
(Active) When Sieg is summoned, he has three Dragon Revelation Command Spells (DRCS) Can be used in 2 different ways, as follows-  
1\. Used during your Turn. Reduces Sieg’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns and applies Dmg Up (Dragon) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dragon’ Attribute is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Then, this Skill has a 25% chance of consuming one of Sieg’s DRCS. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
This usage of this Skill can be used while Sieg has no DRCS left, but NP Cooldown is only reduced by ½◈ Turns, and Dmg Up (Dragon) increases the damage dealt to ‘Dragon’ Attribute Units by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Requires 1 DRCS to use. When this Skill is used, reduce Sieg’s DRCS by 1, then Sieg is transformed into Siegfried, with all Skills and NP available and usable (although NP can only be used if the number of Rounds passed meets the game conditions). Roll a four-sided die. Sieg’s transformation into Siegfried lasts for X Turns, where X=(1◈+\[½◈\*Number rolled on the four-sided die\]).  
When Sieg transforms into ‘Siegfried’, he keeps all buffs, debuffs, and other effects that were affecting him before he transformed. When the transformation ends, ‘Siegfried’ transforms back into Sieg with his Stats and Skill/Spell/NP Cooldowns the same as they were before he transformed, then his Health is reduced by 100 (Sieg’s Health cannot drop below 1 in this way). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns plus the number of Turns Sieg was transformed for, after the transformation ends.  
Note: New Stats have to be rolled for the transformed Sieg. Any \+ or \- to Sieg’s Parameters are carried over to the transformed Sieg.

This Skill is in effect even while Sieg is transformed into Siegfried. This effect is negated while affected with NP Seal.

Can be used in 2 different methods, as follows-

e. Sieg (Fafnir) can Move to any panel, when he Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for him to stand on.  
f. Sieg (Fafnir) gains the ‘Large’ Attribute.  
g. If the Master’s Health is less than 40 or 20, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.

j. Sieg can end Akafiloga Agrise during his Turn, or at the start or end of any Turn or Round.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns after Akafiloga Argrise ends.
