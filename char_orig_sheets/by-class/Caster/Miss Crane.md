(MissCrane) Class Skill: Territory Creation (Atelier) — Rank: C+  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is further increased by (5d8+5) including NP.  
2\. While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+12) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).  
3\. Miss Crane’s Crit Chance is increased by 30% while she is within her Home Base.

(MissCrane) Class Skill: Item Construction (Clothes) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit directly next to her (cannot be used on herself), that allied Unit gains 3 Costume Tokens. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. If this Skill was used while both Miss Crane and the targeted Unit are in Miss Crane’s Home Base, the Cooldown for this Skill is 1◈ Turns instead.  
A Unit with Costume Tokens has the ‘Costumed’ Attribute. Whenever a Unit with Costume Tokens performs an Injury Roll, it loses 1 Costume Token.  
(Passive 1\) The success rate of inflicting debuffs of all allied Units with the ‘Costumed’ Attribute within 3 panels of Miss Crane is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs of all allied Units with the ‘Costumed’ Attribute within 3 panels of Miss Crane is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Some Units have the ‘Undressed’ Attribute (listed below), these Units count as having the ‘Costumed’ Attribute, but when they receive the Passive effects of this Skill, its magnitudes are reduced by 20% (cannot go into negatives).  
When a Unit with the ‘Undressed’ Attribute has Costume Tokens, the magnitudes of the Passive effects of this Skill are no longer reduced and instead increased by 10% for those Units.  
Note: A Unit affected by the effects of Item Construction (Clothes) cannot receive the effects of other ‘Item Construction’ Skills.

(MissCrane) A Lady’s Affection for Garments — Rank: EX
