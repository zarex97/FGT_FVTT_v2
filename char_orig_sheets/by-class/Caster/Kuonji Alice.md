(Alice) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Alice) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d12 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Alice) Folklore Defense — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage taken is reduced by 40 excluding NP.  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken from Volatile Debuffs is reduced by 40\.

(Alice) Yumina — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When Alice is defeated from being inflicted with Instakill or Death, she is revived with 5% of her maximum Health. This effect can only activate a maximum of five times per game.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Alice is revived through an effect, her Health is additionally restored by 15% of its maximum value.  
(Passive 3\) Alice starts the game with 13 Robin Counters.

(Alice) Diddle Diddle: Banquet of the Night — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Luck and 2 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%. If this Skill is used at Night or within a ‘Darkness’ area, this buff is applied one more time.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns; 2◈ Turns instead if used at Night or within a ‘Darkness’ area.  
4\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself (3 panel area if used at Night or within a ‘Darkness’ area), NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Alice) Thames Troll: Giant of the Bridge — Rank: C  
(Passive) Whenever Alice performs a Normal Attack, she can roll a six-sided die. If a 1 or 6 is rolled, the Attack deals STR damage instead of MAG damage (but still uses her Base Attack (MAG)). However, if Alice is within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area, she can directly choose to activate this effect.  
This effect is disabled while in Witch Form.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
Then, if Alice is within or directly next to a Forest, City, or Darkness (but not Night) area, apply the following effects-  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
4\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.

(Alice) Lost Robin Rondo: Cock Robin’s Murder — Rank: E

2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
3\. Alice’s Robin Counters are reduced by 1\.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.

(Alice) Great Three, Flat Snark: Fairy Tale Monster, Oil of the Moon — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. This NP has the Aim effect. If this NP is used during the Day, the game state becomes Night until the end of the Round (next Round will still be Night as usual). First, apply Dmg Up (Human) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Human’ Attribute is increased by 30%. Hits a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself for 4x damage plus 100, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the \[Man\] Attribute. Then, has a 500% chance of inflicting Terror for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Stun by Terror is 60%; and inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Alice) Hunting Hound of the Red Roses

1\. For every Robin Counter she has lost: \+5%  
2\. For every time she has been defeated and revived: \+5%  
(Passive) Alice gains the following effects while in Witch Form-  
1\. MOV is reduced by 1\.  
2\. Range is increased by 2 (3?).  
3\. When Diddle Diddle is used, it is treated as if it were always Night.  
4\. Normal Attacks ignore Magic Resistance.  
5\. When a Normal Attack is used against an enemy Unit, it has a 50% chance of hitting all other enemy Units who are directly next to the target for 50% damage, which can be Evaded or Blocked, those other Units cannot Counter. (Note: Check the hit chance separately for all affected Units.)  
6\. Great Three, Flat Snark can no longer be used. Great Three, Wandersnatch can now be used.
