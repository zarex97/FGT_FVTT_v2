(Mozart) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 after calculation including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Mozart) Protection of the Music God (False) — Rank: EX  
The basis of Mozart’s Attacks.  
(Passive 1\) Mozart can only use one ‘Composition’ Spell per Turn.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Mozart performs a successful Normal Attack, reduce the Cooldown of a random ‘Composition’ Spell by 1 Turn.  
(Passive 3\) Total damage of ‘Composition’ Spells against Units inflicted with Deafen is reduced by 50%, and apply no additional effects.

(Mozart) Amadeus — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 4 Luck  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 45%; if NP, 35%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ½◈ Turns for every ‘Composition’ Spell which is not on Cooldown.  
4\. Applies Debuff ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mozart) Appreciation of the Arts — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When a NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute is used within a 3 panel area of Mozart, he has a 80% chance of learning the True Name of that NP (the information of that NP is copied to your Notebook)  
(Passive 2\) All damage taken from a NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute is reduced by 20%.

Note: NP with the ‘Artistic’ Attribute would be any Noble Phantasm involving or related to artistic, literary, musical creations.

(Mozart) Angel’s Melody — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 20%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (A) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%; and Luck is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack.  
3\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mozart) Eine Kleine Nachtmusik — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Nachtmusik’ effect to Mozart. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
2\. If Mozart Acts while he has this effect, it is removed from him at the end of the Turn.  
3\. Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Mozart is increased by 50%. During a Night Round as dictated by the game state, it affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of Mozart instead. Note: If Day-Night cycle is not in use and the game is permanently set to ‘Day’, then the Range of this effect has a 50% chance to be a 3 panel area instead of 2 when applied.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns after the ‘Nachtmusik’ effect is removed.

(Mozart) Composition: Ave verum corpus  
Damage Spell. Hail, True Body. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 50% extra damage; deals 100 extra damage to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Mozart) Composition: Die Zauberflöte  
Damage Spell. The Magic Flute. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 50% extra damage and inflicts Skill Seal or Silence for 1◈ Turns (DU Flips a Coin, Heads=Skill Seal, Tails=Silence). Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Mozart) Composition: Don Giovanni  
Damage Spell. The Libertine Punished. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 50% extra damage and increases their NP Cooldown by ½◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Mozart) Composition: Leck mich im Arsch  
Damage Spell. Lick me in the Arse. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 50% extra damage and inflicts Poison. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Mozart) Requiem for Death: A Funeral March For the Death God — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Mozart. All Units affected by this NP have to perform 3 separate Luck Checks, in this order; and if their MAG Rank is lower than Rank B, the value of the Luck Roll is increased by 3\.  
1\. Atk Dwn is inflicted for 1◈ Turns. If the 1st Luck Check failed, all damage dealt is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%. If it succeeded, apply as ‘all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%’ instead.  
2\. Def Dwn is inflicted for 1◈ Turns. If the 2nd Luck Check failed, all damage received is increased by 40%. If it succeeded, apply as ‘all damage received is increased by 20%’ instead.  
3\. If the 3rd Luck Check failed, Stage 2 Curse is inflicted. If it succeeded, Stage 1 Curse is inflicted instead.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
