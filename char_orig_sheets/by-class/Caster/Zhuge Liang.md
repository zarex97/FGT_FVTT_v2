(Zhuge Liang) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Zhuge Liang) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A

(Zhuge Liang) Shí Bīng Bā Zhèn — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Unreturning Army: Stone Sentinel Maze. Affects a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Zhuge Liang. This area is now known as the ‘Maze’. Has the following effects-  
1\. The following effects are applied on all enemy Units within the NP area upon activation-  
i) Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%.  
ii) Increases NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
iii) Inflicts Curse (Maze).  
iv) 25% chance to inflict Stun for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. At the end of every full Round (1◈ Turns since activation), inflict Curse (Maze) on all enemy Units within the Maze.  
3\. Enemy Units outside the Maze are unable to enter the Maze. Whenever an enemy Unit within the Maze attempts to leave, roll a twenty-sided die. The Unit only successfully escapes the Maze if a 17 or higher is rolled. Otherwise, escape fails (but the Unit can still move within the Maze). If Successful, the Unit is Moved to a panel outside the Maze, and cannot Act for the rest of its Turn.  
4\. Lasts for 3◈ Turns. However, after the initial NP period, Zhuge can extend the NP duration by 1◈ more Turns by reducing his Health by 100 (cannot be used if Zhuge’s Health is less than 100), and can be repeatedly extended. Curse (Maze) is removed from all enemy Units within the NP area when it ends.  
5\. The Maze is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Zhuge is defeated.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after the NP ends  
Note: Curse (Maze) has the same effect as regular Curse, but is applied separately from it.

(Zhuge Liang) Chū Shī Biăo — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Army (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) This Noble Phantasm is activated when Zhuge Liang is defeated. Roll a six-sided die. If a 1 to 5 is rolled, this NP has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Unremovable Atk Up to all allied Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.   
2\. Applies Unremovable Def Up to all allied Units, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Unremovable Crit Up to all allied Units, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Suishi’ effect to all allied Masters, chance of being Overpowered is reduced by 25%. This effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable.  
If a 6 is rolled, Zhuge Liang summons a new Servant to replace him. That Servant is now Zhuge Liang's Master's new Servant. Roll a twenty-sided die to determine the category from which the new Servant is selected-  
1 to 7: A Servant who is related to Waver Velvet/Lord El-Melloi I  
8 to 14: A Servant from the Three Kingdoms period  
15 to 17: A Servant from the Region of China  
18 to 20: Random
