(Asclepius) Aesculapius: Rod of Asclepius  
At a Range of 1, Asclepius’ Normal Attacks use his Base Attack (STR) and 25% of his Base Attack (MAG) combined (75+50=125), not affected by Magic Resistance.  
At a Range of 2 or higher, Asclepius’ Normal Attacks use his Base Attack (STR) and half of his Base Attack (MAG) combined (75+100=175), effect of Magic Resistance is reduced by half.

(Asclepius) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: EX  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 75%; this effect is halved for Instakill (40%), and further halved for Death (20%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 75%; this effect is halved for Instakill (40%), and further halved for Death (20%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Asclepius) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d12 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Asclepius) Divinity— Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Asclepius) Aesculapius: Serpent Breath  
Damage Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range and deals normal damage. Asclepius cannot be within the Spell area. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.

(Asclepius) God of Medicine — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an allied Unit. Restores their Health by 50% of its maximum value and Agility by 25% of its maximum value; then applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Asclepius) Son of Apollo — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns; then applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Asclepius, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Asclepius) Ophiuchus — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Asclepius. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce their NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. Removes all debuffs.  
3\. Restore 2 Luck and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Applies Heal Up for 1◈ Turns, when an affected Unit’s Health is restored, increase the amount restored by 80%.  
5\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns to himself, when this Unit is defeated it is revived with 30% of its Max Health.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Asclepius) Resurrection Fraught Hades: Emulated Medicine・The Obsolete Underworld's Grief — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. When Resurrection Fraught Hades is used, it affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of Asclepius. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts to all affected Units except Asclepius for 3◈ Turns, the Unit is revived with 30% of its Max Health when defeated.  
2\. Applies Debuff Immune (Asclepius) for 3◈ Turns, 3 times; chance of being inflicted with Instakill is reduced by 50%, while chance of being inflicted with Death is reduced by 25%. Does not affect Erase. Does not stack and does not extend duration when reapplied, but the number of stacks (times) is reset when reapplied.  
3\. Applies Regen for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, the Unit’s Health is restored by 30% of its maximum value at the end of its Turn, at the end of any Turn it Acts, or the end of the Round.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Asclepius) Resurrection Fraught Hades: True Medicine・The Obsolete Underworld's Grief — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Can only be used when the following conditions are met, in the listed order  
1a. Asclepius successfully Attacks and damages a Unit with the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute; he will receive the \[Gorgon’s Blood\] Item at the end of the Damage Step.  
OR  
1b. Asclepius receives the \[Gorgon Blood\] Item from an allied Unit with the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute by standing directly next to her, Asclepius cannot perform any other Action other than Move on this Turn.  
2\. Asclepius, with the \[Gorgon’s Blood\] Item, receives damage from an Attack from a Unit with the ‘Divinity’ Skill or ‘Divine’ Attribute; or an Attack which deals Divine damage.  
3\. Depending on the AU’s Divinity Rank, the \[Gorgon’s Blood\] Item has a chance of transmuting into the \[Resurrection Fraught Hades\] Item. For every Rank in Divinity the AU has, the chance of the \[Gorgon’s Blood\] transmuting is increased by 10% (e.g. Divinity Rank B=40% chance); if ‘Divine’ Attribute or Divine damage without the ‘Divinity’ Skill, roll a six-sided die, and the transmute chance will be X0%.  
Usage of \[Resurrection Fraught Hades\]: Used at any time. This Item can be used to revive any Unit that has been defeated, regardless of how much time has passed since that Unit was defeated; the Unit is revived with full Stats. If it was a Servant, Asclepius and his Master have a choice of either returning the Servant to its original Master; or taking the revived Servant as Asclepius’ or his Master’s own Servant. \[Resurrection Fraught Hades\] is consumed after being used to revive the defeated Unit.  
\[Resurrection Fraught Hades\] can only be created once per game.
