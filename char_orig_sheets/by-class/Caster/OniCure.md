(OniCure) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 40%; this effect is halved for Instakill (20%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(OniCure) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(OniCure) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(OniCure) Heart Break: Dharmapala of Nalanda Oni \- Heart Ripper — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflict NP Seal for 1◈ Turns. Ignores Debuff Resist effects (but not Debuff Immune\!) if the DU has the ‘Oni’ Attribute.  
2\. Inflict Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%; if the affected Unit has the ‘Oni’ Attribute, apply as ‘Crit Chance is reduced by 50%’ instead.  
The next three effects are applied to herself-  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Applies Dmg Up (Oni) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Oni’ Attribute is increased by 30%.  
5\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns. If the DU has the ‘Oni’ Attribute, it is reduced by 1◈ Turns instead.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(OniCure) Break Rod: Dharmapala of Nalanda Oni \- Bludgeoning Club — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Process where OniCure is the AU. Restore 3 Luck; then applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%; and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(OniCure) Kishu no Ma (Gou) — Rank: A  
Oni-type Demon (Protection)  
Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all Units within a 2 panel area of OniCure, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; then applies Dmg Up (Demonic) for 1◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 40%.  
Can be used when performing a Normal Attack. In this case, she also gains the ‘Mana Burst’ effect, for that Combat Process, OniCure’s Base Attack used is her Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 320); not affected by Magic Resistance.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(OniCure) Gohou Shoujo \- Kuzuryu Ousatsu: Dharmapala Maiden \- Nine-Headed Dragon Extermination — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=3. This NP has the Aim effect. Deals 3x damage plus 200; then inflicts Poison on the DU, and has a 50% chance of inflicting an extra Stage of Poison on the DU. Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns.

(OniCure) Dharmapala Maiden: Banana Explosion  
(Passive) Whenever Gohou Shoujo \- Kuzuryu Ousatsu is used, roll a ten-sided die. If a 10 is rolled, all other Units within a 3x3 panel area around the DU receive 1.5x damage and are inflicted with Poison. This version of the NP does not have the Aim effect. If an allied Ibaraki Douji is within a 2 panel area of OniCure when Gohou Shoujo \- Kuzuryu Ousatsu is used, Banana Explosion is activated if a 8 to 10 is rolled.
