(Tonelico) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Tonelico) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: C  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Tonelico) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Tonelico) Glam Sight: Fairy Eyes — Rank: C  
(Passive) When Tonelico is Attacked, Crit Chance of the AU is reduced by 15%.

(Tonelico) Sprite Knocker  
(Passive) Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 use Base Attack (STR) and deal 50 (70) bonus Fixed MAG damage; this Fixed MAG damage is affected by the DU’s Magic Resistance (reduced by 10 for every Rank of Magic Resistance the DU has).

2\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.  
3\. Reduce her NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.

1\. Atk Up  
2\. Atk Up (**≤** 99%)  
3\. Atk Up (**≤** 75%)  
4\. Atk Up (**≤** 50%)  
5\. Atk Up (**≤** 25%)

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Tonelico) Last Resort — Rank: A  
This Skill cannot be used until the start of a Round where Tonelico is able to use her NP.  
(Trigger) At the start of a Round where Tonelico is able to use her NP, the Cooldown of this Skill is increased by 3◈ Turns. Only activates once.  
(Passive 1\) Whenever an allied Unit is defeated, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by 1◈ Turns. Allied Mob Units (e.g. Civilians, Dragon Tooth Warriors, Horrors, basically most Units which are summoned through Skills/Spells/NP and are not Masters or Servants) do not count towards this effect.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Tonelico performs a non-NP Attack which causes an Injury Roll, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by half of their respective maximum NP Cooldowns.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns (Guts has no duration), when an affected Unit is defeated it is revived with 300 Health.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns (5◈ Turns)

(Tonelico) Despair Event Horizon: Tonelico the Saviour  
(Trigger) After 3 allied Units have been defeated, **Tonelico the Rain Witch** turns into **Tonelico the Saviour**. Allied mob Units (e.g. Dragon Tooth Warriors, Horrors, basically most Units which are summoned through Skills/Spells/NP and are not Masters or Servants) do not count towards this effect.  
(Passive) As Tonelico the Saviour, she gains the following effects-  
1\. Her Alignment changes to Lawful Neutral.  
2\. All Attacks which deal Water damage ignore effects that specifically reduce Water damage taken.  
3\. Accolon Cluster and all Attacks which deal Water damage also deal bonus Dark damage equal to 25% of the damage dealt by that Attack; this bonus Dark damage is not affected by effects which modify damage dealt on Tonelico.  
4\. The effects of certain Skills and NP are upgraded to the effects in this colour.  
5\. When Tonelico turns into Tonelico the Saviour, the Cooldown of all Spells are fully reduced, then reduce the Cooldown of Last Resort by 2◈+½◈ Turns.
