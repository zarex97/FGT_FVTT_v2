(Castoria) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Castoria) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Castoria) Independent Thaumaturgy — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has the same effects as Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 2\) Crit Damage of Castoria’s Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) only is increased by 10%.  
Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s Servant Range is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Castoria) Glam Sight: Fairy Eyes — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Castoria is Attacked, Crit Chance of the AU is reduced by 25%.

(Castoria) Charisma of Hope — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and reduce the NP Cooldown of the affected Units by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Castoria) ~~Protection of the Lake~~ Avalon le Fae — Rank: A

(Castoria) Vivian Shine  
Damage Spell. Range=4. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 2x damage, does not damage herself. Then, restores the Health of all allied Units within a 3x3 panel area around Arturia by 100\. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Castoria) Evasion Detonation  
Damage Spell. Used when Arturia performs an Evade roll (does not activate on non-Evade Agility Checks). First, her Evade roll is reduced by 2\. If the Evade is Successful, Arturia performs an Attack that deals 50% extra damage to the AU (this counts as a Counter for that Combat Phase). To Block this Attack, the AU’s AGI Rank has to be the same as Arturia’s or higher; if the AU Evades, increase the AU’s Evade roll by 3\. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Castoria) Around Caliburn: Hope’s Radiant Embrace — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Castoria is Attacked. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Arturia. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turn, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Removes all debuffs.  
3\. Applies Anti-Purge or 1◈ Turns, 1 time. For every 2 allied Units affected by this NP excluding Artoria Caster, the number of times ‘Anti-Purge’ activates is increased by 1 (e.g. 4 other allied Units are affected by this NP, Anti-Purge lasts for 1◈ Turns, 3 times; maximum times is 5). Note: For every Overcharge buff on Castoria, increase the number of times of Anti-Purge by 1\.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(Castoria) Fairy of the Inner Sea: Artoria Avalon  
(Trigger) When a total of 3 allied Units have been defeated within a 3 panel area of Arturia, she transforms into her Avalon Form immediately after the Combat Phase where that third allied Unit was defeated. Allied mob Units (e.g. Dragon Tooth Warriors, Horrors, basically most Units which are summoned through Skills/Spells/NP and are not Masters or Servants) do not count towards this effect. If her Master is defeated, she immediately transforms into her Avalon Form.  
(Passive 1\) In Avalon Form, her ‘Sword of Selection’ Skill is upgraded to ‘Holy Sword Creation’, NP is upgraded from Around Caliburn to Round of Avalon, and her Normal Attacks change as follows-  
At a Range of 1, Castoria’s Normal Attacks use her Base Attack (STR) and 25% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 125+50=175), not affected by Magic Resistance; at a Range of 2 or higher, Castoria’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Passive 2\) In Avalon Form, Vivian Shine and Evasion Detonation are no longer usable.

(Casturia) Carnwennan: Shadow Treader  
Damage Spell. Can only be used in Avalon Form. Range=4. Deals 2x damage, then applies the effects of ‘Presence Concealment — Rank: B’ to herself for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.
