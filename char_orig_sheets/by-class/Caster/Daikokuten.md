(Daikokuten) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d4 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by 3d10 including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Daikokuten) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: D  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Daikokuten) Divinity — Rank: E-  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 5 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Daikokuten) The Mice of Daikokuten  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Daikokuten is defeated and not revived, roll a six-sided die. Reduce the number of Mice Counters by that number, then create a new Daikokuten within a 2 panel area of their Master (Skill and NP Cooldowns however are carried over from the old Daikokuten). Daikokuten is only completely defeated when their Mice Counters reach 0\.  
(Passive 2\) At a Range of 1, Daikokuten’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR), Crit Chance is increased by 15%, and hit the DU from behind, regardless of the position Daikokuten and the DU are facing.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can only be used when Daikokuten is directly next to her Master. Restores the Health of both Daikokuten and her Master by 10% of their respective maximum values. Neither Daikokuten or her Master can Attack on the Turn this effect is used; and cannot Move after this effect is used. Then, Daikokuten gains the ‘Breeded’ effect for 1◈ Turns; when this effect naturally expires, increase the number of Mice Counter by 1d8. The ‘Breeded’ effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable; but whenever Daikokuten performs an Injury Roll while she has the ‘Breeded’ effect, she has a X0% chance of losing the ‘Breeded’ effect, X equals the amount of Agility lost from the Injury Roll.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Daikokuten) Uchide no Kozuchi: The Tap-Appear Mallet — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive) At a Range of 2 or higher, Daikokuten’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).

(Daikokuten) Meritorious Service — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to themselves, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to themselves, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Daikokuten is increased by 10%.  
4\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of themselves, at the end of a Unit’s Turn, at the end of a Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round, the Unit restores 100 Health and 1 Agility.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Daikokuten) To Benefit All Sapient Beings — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Daikokuten’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, and apply NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of themselves, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Daikokuten) Kingdom of Children — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of themselves. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
2\. Applies the ‘Nezumi’ buff for 1◈ Turns, whenever an affected Unit performs a successful Attack or Evade, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Daikokuten) Window Cleaning  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals Water damage, then remove 1 random debuff from the DU, but inflict Soaked for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
   
(Daikokuten) Nezumi Joudo: Pure Land of the Mice — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Hits all enemy Units within a 7x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Daikokuten. First, inflict Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns on all affected Units, all damage received is increased by 20%; and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever successfully Attacked. Deal 5 Fixed STR Damage to all affected Units X times, where X=the number of Mice Counters Daikokuten has (Note: This Fixed Damage does not trigger the Agility reduction effect of Def Dwn (C)), then deals 4x damage to all affected Units using Base Attack (MAG). After dealing damage, apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Daikokuten, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
