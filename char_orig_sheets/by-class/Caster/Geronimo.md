(Geronimo) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: C  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Geronimo) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Geronimo) Bloodstained Demon — Rank: B  
(Passive) Geronimo’s Normal Attacks deal 50 extra STR damage. Whenever Geronimo successfully deals damage with a Normal Attack, reduce the Cooldown of Shamanism by ⅓◈ Turns.   
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies N.Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, Normal Attack damage dealt is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Geronimo) Shamanism — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. This Skill can be used in several different methods.   
1\. Damage Spell. Deals damage to an enemy Unit and has a 50% chance of inflicting Shock for 2◈ Turns. Lightning damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
2\. Range=2, used on an allied Unit. Restores the Unit’s Health by 10% of its maximum value. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
3\. Applies Dodge (Shamanism) to himself for 3 times. Does not stack, if reapplied, reset its count (times). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Atk Up to his Guardian Beast (see below) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns  
5\. Applies Crit Up to himself and his Guardian Beast for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns  
Cannot be used if any of its effects are on Cooldown. Cannot be used if Geronimo is inflicted with Silence.

The Spirit Coyote’s Health is restored by 50 at the end of every Turn it Acts.
