(ProtoMerlin) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: D  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is further increased by 5d6 including NP.  
2\. While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+5) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(ProtoMerlin) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A+  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 55%; this effect is halved for Instakill (30%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 55%; this effect is halved for Instakill (30%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(ProtoMerlin) Hybrid — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Proto Merlin’s NP Cooldown is reduced, whether through natural reduction (i.e. at the end of the Turn) or an effect, it is reduced by 1 extra Turn.

(ProtoMerlin) Hero Creation — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Merlin. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 20% of its original value.  
3\. Applies Crit DmBoost for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20\.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(ProtoMerlin) Dreamlike Charisma — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; does not affect NP.  
2\. Reduces NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(ProtoMerlin) Succubus’ Vicinity — Rank: EX  
(Passive) At the end of a Unit’s Turn, if that Unit is within a 2 panel area of Proto Merlin, its NP Cooldown has a 50% chance of not naturally reducing (Note: Only affects natural NP regen, not NP regen from external effects such as buffs). If this effect activates, Proto Merlin’s NP Cooldown is instead reduced by 1 Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln to all allied Units within 2 panels of herself for 1 time.  
2\. Affects all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of herself, increase their NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then reduce Proto Merlin’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns for every enemy Unit affected by this effect.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies the ‘Unsealed’ buff for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- Skill & Spell Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn; and all MAG damage dealt is increased by 30% including NP.
