(Iri) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Iri) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 after calculation including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Iri) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 60 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 20%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Iri) Resolute Devotion — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Total Damage dealt is reduced by 50% including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Irisviel restores a Unit’s Health, increase the amount restored by 50\.  
(Passive 3\) The magnitude of Defensive Buffs applied by Irisviel is increased by 25%.

(Iri) Heaven’s Feel — Rank: E  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Heal Boost (Tenhai) buff for 1◈ Turns, when Irisviel performs healing, the amount healed is increased by 100%; has a different effect on ‘Song of Grail’.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 3◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Iri) Child of Nature — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns, and applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Iri) Healing Magecraft — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores the Health of an allied Unit within Range by 25% of its maximum value. Cannot be used if Irisviel is affected by Silence. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Iri) Einzbern Alchemy: Doves  
Damage Spell. Deals 50% extra damage with a 25% chance of inflicting either Immobilize, Disable, or Stun for 1◈ Turns (roll a six-sided die to determine: 1, 2=Immobilize, 3, 4=Disable, 5, 6=Stun). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Iri) Einzbern Alchemy: Wires  
Spell. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when an allied Unit within Range is Attacked. Used on an allied Unit within Iri’s Range. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Iri) Goddess of the Drive  
Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘~~Haphazard~~ Driver’ effect to Irisviel. This effect has the following effects-  
1\. Irisviel’s MOV is increased by 6\.  
2\. Irisviel’s Normal Attack becomes a Riding Attack that deals 2x damage, Base Attack (STR) is used; this damage is not affected by Resolute Devotion. Then, remove the ~~Haphazard~~ Driver effect from Irisviel at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. Irisviel gains the ‘Passenger Seat’ ability as long as she has the ‘~~Haphazard~~ Driver’ effect; this Passenger Seat ability can bring up to 4 allied Units with her when she Moves.  
4\. Irisviel’s Evade Rolls are reduced by 2\. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks.  
5\. Total Damage received from the next Attack is reduced by 50%, then remove the ~~Haphazard~~ Driver effect from Irisviel at the end of the Damage Step.  
6\. Irisviel cannot use Skills, Spells, and NP as long as she has the ‘~~Haphazard~~ Driver’ effect.  
The ‘~~Haphazard~~ Driver’ effect is neither a buff nor a debuff, and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns after the ‘Driver’ effect is removed from Irisviel.  
Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.

(Iri) Song of Grail: Sing Out, O’ White Grail — Rank: B (NP) \[Thaumaturgy\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn or when an other allied Unit within a 3 panel area is defeated. Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of Irisviel. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores the Health and Agility of all affected Units by 25% of their maximum values; if Irisviel has Heal Boost (Tenhai), the magnitude of this effect is 50% instead of 25%.  
2\. Removes 2 random debuffs from all affected Units, with volatile debuffs taking priority over other debuffs.  
3\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when an affected Unit is defeated it is revived with 25% of its Health.  
4\. If used when an allied Unit within a 3 panel area is defeated, revives all allied Units who were defeated at that same time with 25% of their Health; but does not apply effects 1 and 3 to said Units.  
This NP cannot be used if Irisviel is affected by Silence.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
