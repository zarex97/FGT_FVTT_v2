(Komachi) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: D  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%; this effect is halved for Instakill (10%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Komachi) Bodhisattva Incarnate — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When Komachi enters Bodhisattva Mode, remove all Decay Counters from herself, then her Health is restored by X0% of its maximum value, X=the number of Decay Counters on herself.  
(Passive 2\) Gains the following effects while in Bodhisattva Mode-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP.  
2\. Immune to Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
3\. Normal Attacks deal Light damage. All Total damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute is increased by 20%. Additionally, Normal Attacks have a 20% chance of inflicting Terror (20%) for 1◈ Turns on Units with the ‘Undead’ or ‘Demonic’ Attributes or ‘Evil’ Alignment.  
4\. Gains the Levitating, non-Hominidae, and Spaceflight Attributes.  
5\. Komachi cannot be targeted by Attacks which use Base Attack (STR) and also takes no damage or effects from them.  
6\. Cannot gain Decay Tokens.  
7\. After 3◈ Turns have passed since transforming into Bodhisattva Mode, at the end of any Turn she Acts, her Master loses 40 Health. If her Master’s Health is 40 or less when this activates, Komachi automatically exits Bodhisattva Mode; her Master will not lose Health due to this effect when this activates. During this period after said 3◈ Turns have passed, her Master can spend 1 Command Spell to make Komachi exit Bodhisattva Mode. From the second time onwards of entering Bodhisattva Mode, after 3◈ Turns have passed, Komachi can exit Bodhisattva Mode at the end of her Turn or any Turn she Acts, her Master will not lose Health due to this effect in this case.  
8\. When Bodhisattva Mode ends after the first time it is Activated, Komachi retains the ‘Levitating’ Attribute as well as Effect 1 of this Skill.  
9\. When Bodhisattva Mode ends after the first time it is Activated, Komachi retains Effect 2 of this Skill, but its effects become ‘Chance of being inflicted with Instakill, Death, & Erase is reduced by 20% while not in Bodhisattva Mode. When in Bodhisattva Mode, Komachi is immune to Instakill, Death, and Erase’.  
Note: This Skill is treated as Divinity, and even while it is not Active (i.e. Komachi is not in Bodhisattva Mode), Komachi is treated as having it.
