(Anastasia) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Anastasia) Fae Contract — Rank: A  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%. Chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 10%. Also affects Instakill, Death, and Erase.

(Anastasia) Mystic Eyes of Clairvoyance — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2, used on an enemy Unit. Cannot be used if there is an obstacle between Anastasia and the intended target; and Anastasia and the target have to be facing each other. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Pierce to Anastasia for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up to herself for ⅓◈ Turns, damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Inflicts Debuff ResDwn on the targeted Unit for ⅓◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is increased by 100%.  
4\. Inflicts the ‘Samovilas’ debuff for ⅓◈ Turns, 1 time, the affected Unit cannot Evade Anastasia’s Attacks and negates the effect of Dodge buffs on it when Attacked by Anastasia.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Anastasia) Glacial Charisma — Rank: B  
(Passive) All Total Ice damage taken is reduced by 25% including NP.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all Units within a 2 panel area of Anastasia. Has two different effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns to all enemy Units, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Disable Atk for 1◈ Turns to herself, Normal Attacks have a 10% chance of inflicting Disable for ⅓◈ Turns.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Anastasia) Shvibzik — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the NP Cooldown of ‘Viy Viy Viy’ by 1◈+⅔◈ Turns.   
2\. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn (A) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%; and Luck is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Anastasia) Viy Viy Viy: Gallop \- Spirit Eyeballs — Rank: EX (NP) \[Spiritual\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Anastasia for 5x damage. Then, inflicts Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns; and inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%. Ice damage (half). Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Anastasia) Sumerki Kremlin: Afterglow, Abominable Fortress of Blood — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Fortress\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects the 7x7 panel area around Anastasia upon activation. If the Grail War region is Russia, affects a 9x9 panel area instead. This NP area is now termed as the ‘Kremlin’. Has the following effects-  
1\. All enemy Units within the Kremlin upon activation are forced out of the Kremlin.  
2\. The Kremlin functions as a second Home Base for Anastasia’s Faction. The effect of Anastasia’s Territory Creation extends one panel away from the Kremlin.  
3\. Anastasia’s STN does not decrease while she is within the Kremlin. Her Master’s ZON is ignored when she Attacks (no damage reduction) while within the Kremlin, or when directly outside it (1 panel).  
4\. Enemy Units can attempt to enter the Kremlin. Roll a 20-sided die. If the Unit’s STR or MAG is B Rank or higher, they can enter if they roll a 17 or higher. Otherwise, a roll of 19 or higher is required. The same process is required if they attempt to leave the Kremlin (excluding the initial defenestration upon activation).  
5\. All damage received by allied Units within the Kremlin is reduced by 25% including NP.  
6\. Units within the Kremlin cannot be targeted by Attacks from outside the Kremlin (But Units within the Kremlin can Attack Units outside it). However, the Kremlin can be Attacked with an AoE Noble Phantasm. In this case, all Servants within the Kremlin receive damage from the NP, but Total Damage received from it is reduced by 75%; while Masters receive no damage and effects from it.  
Note: If the NP used on the Kremlin was an \[Anti-Fortress\] or higher Noble Phantasm, Sumerki Kremlin is forcefully ended at the end of the Combat Process.  
7\. All damage dealt by allied Units within the Kremlin is increased by 25%, does not affect NP.  
Sumerki Kremlin initially lasts for 2◈ Turns (If the Grail War region is Russia, 3◈ Turns). After the initial NP period, Anastasia can extend the duration of Sumerki Kremlin by 1◈ more Turns by reducing her Health by 150 (cannot be used if Anastasia’s Health is less than 150), and can be repeatedly extended.  
8\. The Kremlin is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Anastasia is defeated.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns after the NP ends
