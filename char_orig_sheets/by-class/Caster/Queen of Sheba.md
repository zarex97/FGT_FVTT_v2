(Sheba) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d8 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+10) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Sheba) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Sheba) Queen’s Spiritual Fragrance — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Ward (Spirit) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage taken from Units with the ‘Spirit’ Attribute is reduced by 20%.  
3\. Sheba’s NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns for every Unit with the ‘Spirit’ Attribute within a 2 panel area around herself.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Sheba) Faerie Eye — Rank: A  
(Passive) Sheba’s chance of Discovering Units with Active Presence Concealment or similar effects is increased by 20%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 75%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Sheba) Demon God’s Wisdom — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Sheba) The First Riddle  
Damage Spell. Deals 50% extra damage and inflicts Bleed for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
*“An enclosure with ten doors: When one is open nine are shut, when nine are open one is shut.”*

(Sheba) The Second Riddle  
Damage Spell. Deals 50% extra damage and inflicts Immobilize for 1◈ Turns. Nature damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
*“Living, moves not; yet when its head is cut off it moves.”*

(Sheba) The Third Riddle  
Damage Spell. Hits a 1x3 or 3x1 panel area in any direction next to Sheba (if diagonal, 1x2 or 2x1). Deals normal damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
*“Produced from the ground, man produces it, while its food is the fruit of the ground.”*

(Sheba) Three Enigmas: Three Riddle Tellings — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=4. Deals 3.5x damage. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
3\. Inflicts Heal Dwn for 1◈ Turns, when healing is received, the amount of Health healed is reduced by 70%.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns
