(Cú) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Cú) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Cú) Primordial Rune — Rank: \-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Gain 2 PRS Tokens. Then, the Skill can be used in two different methods, as follows-  
On an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself: Roll 2 eight-sided die. Apply the effects as follows-  
1\. Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
4\. Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage is increased by 25%.  
5\. NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
6\. Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
7\. Debuff ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 25%.  
8\. Your choice of any of the above effect(s).  
On an enemy Unit, Range=2: Roll 2 eight-sided die. Apply the effects as follows-  
1\. Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 25%, if NP; 15%.  
2\. Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 25%.  
3\. Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%.  
4\. Crit DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage is reduced by 25%.  
5\. NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is reduced by 30%.  
6\. Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 25%.  
7\. Debuff ChDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of inflicting debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
8\. Your choice of any of the above effect(s).  
If a duplicate number is rolled, apply the effect twice. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Cú) Rune Magic — Rank: A  
(Passive) If Cú uses a Primordial Rune Spell while he has any PRS Tokens, remove one PRS Token from himself, while the Primordial Rune Spell that he used does not enter Cooldown. The maximum number of PRS Tokens Cú can have is 3\.

(Cú) Týr  
Primordial Rune Spell. Damage Spell. Deals 2x damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Cú) Yr  
Primordial Rune Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range, inflicts Poison on the affected Units. Cú can still perform a Normal Attack on the same Turn this Spell was used (but not another Damage Spell or NP). Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Cú) Kaun  
Primordial Rune Spell. Damage Spell. Deals 2x damage and inflicts Blind for 1◈ Turns. Light damage. If the DU has the ‘Dark’ Attribute, this Spell does an additional 100 damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Cú) Maðr  
Primordial Rune Spell. Used on an allied Unit within Range. Restores the Unit’s Health by 20% of its maximum value. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. Can only be used once per Turn.

(Cú) Protection from Arrows — Rank: A  
(Passive) Cú will automatically Evade any Attack from a non-AoE Ranged weapon (arrows, firearms, thrown weapons, magic projectiles, etc) including Noble Phantasms. When he Evades ranged AoE weapon Attacks/Noble Phantasms, ignore the Evade roll penalty due to the AoE Attack (i.e. Evade Roll reduced by 3). Has no effect if this Unit is affected by Skill Seal.

(Cú) Disengage — Rank: C  
(Active) This Skill is used at the start of a Combat Phase when Cú is Attacked. The enemy Unit’s Attack is cancelled, Cú is allowed to Move 3 panels, then he restores his Health by 15% of its maximum value and removes 1 debuff from himself. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Cú) At The Well — Rank: EX  
(Active) Mímisbrunnr. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Removes all debuffs from himself.  
2\. Restores Cú’s Health by 15% of its maximum value.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Fount’ buff for ⅓◈ Turns. Its effects are as follows-

1. When this buff naturally expires, apply Guts to himself for ⅓◈ Turns, when defeated he is revived with ⅓ of his Max Health.  
2. When this buff naturally expires, apply the ‘Mímisbrunnr’ buff to himself for ⅓◈ Turns, 1 time- When the Unit with the ‘Fount’ buff is defeated and revived, its NP Cooldown is reduced by 4◈ Turns.  
3. When this buff naturally expires, inflicts Sacrifice on himself.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Cú) Wicker Man: Cage of Scorching, Consuming Flames — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]

7\. Inflicts NP Seal (Passive) for 2◈ Turns, the ~~effect of all Passive~~ Passive effects of all Noble Phantasms are negated.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
