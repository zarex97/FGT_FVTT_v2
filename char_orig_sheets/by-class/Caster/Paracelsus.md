(Paracelsus) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: EX  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 75%; this effect is halved for Instakill (40%), and further halved for Death (20%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 75%; this effect is halved for Instakill (40%), and further halved for Death (20%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Paracelsus) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d12 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Paracelsus) High Speed Incantation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces NP Cooldown by 3◈ Turns, then Paracelsus gains 3 Elemental Tokens. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive) If Paracelsus uses an Elemental Spell while he has any Elemental Tokens, remove one Elemental Token from himself, while the Elemental Spell that he used does not enter Cooldown; then his NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns. The maximum number of Elemental Tokens Paracelsus can have is 3\.  
Note: Cannot be used while inflicted with Silence.

(Paracelsus) Philosopher’s Stone — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Restores Paracelsus’ Health by 100 at the end of every Turn he Acts.   
(Passive 2\) When Paracelsus is defeated, he is revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. When this effect is on Cooldown, the effect of Passive 1 Philosopher’s Stone is negated.

(Paracelsus) Elemental — Rank: A+  
The ability to make use of artificial spirits corresponding to the five elements.  
(Passive) Whenever Paracelsus uses an Elemental Spell, apply Atk Up (Elementalist) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself at the start of the Damage Step, all Elemental damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%. Atk Up (Elementalist) does not stack, but its duration is extended if reapplied.

(Paracelsus) Elemental \- Water

Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals 50% extra damage and has a 50% chance of inflicting Silence on the DU for 1◈+⅓◈ Turns.

All of Paracelsus’ Elemental Spells have a shared Cooldown of 3◈-⅓◈ Turns  (i.e. if one of them is used, the other four cannot be used until the Cooldown ends).

(Paracelsus) Sword of Paracelsus: Magic Sword of the Element User — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x5 or 5x3 area in any non-diagonal direction next to Paracelsus for 4x damage plus 100; then inflict Def Down for 1◈ Turns on the affected Units, all damage received is increased by 30%. Increase the NP Cooldown of all DUs by ⅓◈ Turns, then reduce the Cooldown of this NP by ⅓◈ Turns for every Unit whose NP Cooldown was successfully increased by this effect. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Paracelsus) Sword of Paracelsus: Azoth Origin — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Mystery\]  
(Non-damaging) Cannot be used if ‘Sword of Paracelsus: Magic Sword of the Element User’ is on Cooldown. Can be used when the following conditions are met-  
1\. When an enemy Unit within a 3 panel area of Paracelsus performs a non-NP Attack which uses Base Attack (MAG), this NP can be used at the start of the Combat Process. Cancel that enemy Unit’s Attack, then inflicts Silence (Azoth) for 3◈ Turns on that Unit. Silence (Azoth) has the same effects as normal Silence, but also affects Attack Skills; as well as ignores Debuff Resist & Debuff Immune effects on the DU. Then, the Cooldown of ‘Sword of Paracelsus: Magic Sword of the Element User’ is increased by ½◈ Turns for every Rank of MAG on the enemy Unit. Note: If the DU has no MAG Rank, refer to ‘**Extra Note \- Class Skills & NP**’. When used in this way, the Master’s Health cost for using this NP is halved.   
2\. When an enemy Unit within a 3 panel area of Paracelsus uses a NP, this NP can be activated. Cancel that enemy Unit’s NP, then inflicts NP Seal (Azoth) for 3◈ Turns on that Unit. NP Seal (Azoth) has the same effects as normal NP Seal, but also affects all Passive NP effects; as well as ignores Debuff Resist & Debuff Immune effects on the DU. Then, the Cooldown of ‘Sword of Paracelsus: Magic Sword of the Element User’ is increased by 1◈ Turns for every Rank of the NP used by that enemy Unit. When used in this way, the Master’s Health cost for using this NP is equal to the Rank of NP used by that enemy Unit.  
3\. When Paracelsus is within the area of an enemy Unit’s NP that is constantly Active (e.g. Ramesseum Tentyris, Papiyas, Piedra del Sol, Sikera Usum, etc), this NP can be used during his Turn. When used, apply the ‘Azoth’ effect for 3◈ Turns to himself- Negate all effects of an activated/constantly active NP area within a 3 panel area of himself. Then, the Cooldown of ‘Sword of Paracelsus: Magic Sword of the Element User’ is increased by 5◈ Turns. When used in this way, the Master’s Health cost for using this NP is halved.
