(George) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(George) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
Mount: Bayard.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(George) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(George) Instinct — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) At the start of a Combat Phase when this Unit is Attacked, it has a 50% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If Instinct Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is 25% instead.  
(Passive 2\) Crit Chance for Counters is increased by 5%.  
This Skill is negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn, or at the start of a Damage Step when Countering. Applies Crit Up (Counter) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance for Counters is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(George) Guardian Knight — Rank: A+  
(Passive) If an allied Unit within a 3x3 panel area around George is Attacked, George can receive the Attack instead of the originally targeted allied Unit. If he does, damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. The effect of Instinct does not trigger in this situation.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when an other allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself is Attacked. Applies Decoy for 1◈ Turns; and applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(George) Soul of a Martyr — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Restores his Health by 200 and Agility by 20% of its maximum value; then applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is reduced by 100%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(George) Bayard: The Phantom War Horse — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Can only be used once during the entire game. Used when either George or his Master is Attacked (or both of them, if the Master is within 2 panels of George, if it was an AoE Attack). Negates all damage and effects of one Attack, including Noble Phantasms, used on either George or his Master (or both of them, if the Master is within 2 panels of George, if it was an AoE Attack).

(George) Ascalon: The Blessed Sword By Which Force is Slain — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) All damage dealt by George when he Counters is increased by 50% including NP.

(George) Abyssus Draconis: Thou Shalt a Serpent Become — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Hits a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to George. George applies ‘Draconis’ effect’ to all affected Units for 2◈ Turns; this effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. This NP cannot be Blocked or Evaded. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Interfectum Dracones. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.  
A Unit with the ‘Draconis’ effect has the ‘Dragon’ Attribute.

(George) Interfectum Dracones: Dragon Slayer — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Damage dealt by Normal Attacks to Units with the ‘Dragon’ Attribute is increased by 25%.  
(Active) Can be used in 2 different methods-  
1\. Range=1. Uses Base Attack (STR) and 60% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e.75+75=150). Deals 3x damage. If the DU has the ‘Dragon’ Attribute, damage dealt is increased by 100%. Not affected by Magic Resistance.  
2\. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 3x damage. If the DU has the ‘Dragon’ Attribute, damage dealt is increased by 100%.   
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Abyssus Draconis. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
