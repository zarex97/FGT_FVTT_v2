(Elizabeth) Solo Live in the Storm  
(Passive 1\) Elizabeth JAPAN is Immune to Curse and Burn.  
(Passive 2\) NP Damage received is reduced by 30%.

(Elizabeth) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 3 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Elizabeth) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Elizabeth) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d8 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+10) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Elizabeth) Battle Continuation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+15; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 4d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Elizabeth) Thanks for the Encore\!  
(Passive) Whenever Elizabeth has to perform an Injury Roll, apply the following effects to herself after the Injury Roll-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 10%.

(Elizabeth) Crimson Charisma — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%. Then, applies the following additional effects to herself-  
1\. Applies Ward (Saber) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken from Saber-class Servants is reduced by 40% including NP.  
2\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 2 times. Does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Elizabeth) Silent Torture  
(Active) Hits all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. All affected Units Flip a Coin. If Heads, it is inflicted with Silence for 1◈ Turns; if Tails, it is inflicted with Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Inflicts NP Degen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is increased by 1 Turns at the end of every Turn, and natural NP cooldown reduction is stopped.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Elizabeth) Wild Metal  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Inflicts NP Seal for ⅓◈ Turns and inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Elizabeth) Demonic Magic — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 5x5 panel area around Elizabeth. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%; and applies NP DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%.   
Can be used when performing a Normal Attack. In this case, also applies the ‘Mana Burst’ effect to Elizabeth for that Combat Process, Elizabeth’s Base Attack used is her Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 125+200=325), deals Dark damage (half) and has a 50% chance of inflicting Curse; not affected by Magic Resistance.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Elizabeth) Ryūmei Raisei: Dragon Cry Thundering Voice — Rank: D++ \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=4. Deals 2.5x damage plus 200, then has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Deafen (Ryumei) for 2◈ Turns. Deafen (Ryumei) has the same effects as normal Deafen, but starts at a magnitude of 8 (i.e. Deafen (8)). After every ⅓◈ Turns passes, the magnitude of Deafen (Ryumei) is reduced by 1 at the end of the Turn.  
2\. Inflicts Mental Debuff ResDwn (Ryumei) for 2◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is increased by 80%. After every ⅓◈ Turns passes, the magnitude of Mental Debuff ResDwn (Ryumei) is reduced by 10% at the end of the Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅔◈ Turns. Note: This NP cannot be used if inflicted with Silence.  
‘Ryumei’ debuffs do not stack. Deafen (Ryumei) does not stack with normal Deafen, and will replace the normal Deafen debuff if the DU has it.
