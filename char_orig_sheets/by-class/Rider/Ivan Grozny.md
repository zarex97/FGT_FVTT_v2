(Ivan) Thunderous Emperor  
(Passive) At a Range of 1, Ivan’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Ivan’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Lightning damage with a 10% chance of inflicting Shock for 1◈ Turns.

(Ivan) Riding — Rank: EX  
Mammoth Yaga.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat. Treat it as a Normal Attack performed at a Range of 1\.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Ivan) Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ivan) Contradictory Mind — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Ivan gains the ‘Chaotic’ Alignment (in addition to his Lawful Evil), negated while Ivan is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Reduces his NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Lawful) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Chaotic) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Chaotic’ Alignment is increased by 30%.  
5\. Removes all debuffs from himself.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Passive) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 5%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ivan) Almighty Supreme Authority — Rank: A  
(Passive) The effects of Charisma of Rank: B or lower are negated on all Units who are within a 2 panel area of Ivan. Also affects Atk Up (Charisma) buffs from Charisma — Rank: B or lower.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Ivan. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln to Ivan for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Remove all buffs from the affected enemy Units.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ivan) Chornyi Oprichnik: The Black Dogs Seeping Out of My Dreams — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Trigger) When Ivan is summoned, Flip a Coin. If Heads, he is summoned with Chornyi Oprichnik inactive. If Tails, he is summoned with Chornyi Oprichnik active.  
(Passive/Non-damaging) When this NP is Active, Ivan is constantly inflicted with Sleep. This NP can only be activated (used) during your Turn when Ivan is in his Home Base.  
When this NP is Activated, roll a six-sided die and add 4 to that. The number rolled is the number of Oprichniki summoned around Ivan. The Stats of an Oprichniki are as follows-  
Oprichniki  
Health: 500 (if Grail War Region is Russia, 750\)  
Agility: 18  
Luck: 10  
MOV: 6  
Range: 3 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 100 (if Grail War Region is Russia, 125\)  
Attributes: Evil, Humanoid, Male  
Oprichniki do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Oprichniki can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Ivan or his Master if any Oprichniki are next to them. The maximum number of Oprichniki on the board is 50\.  
Oprichniki cannot Move beyond 9 panels of Ivan’s Home Base; however if the Region of the Grail War is Russia, that limitation is removed.  
At the start of Ivan’s Turn, if this NP is Active, roll a six-sided die and summon that number of Oprichniki around him.  
While this NP is Active, if Ivan takes damage equal to or more than 10% of his Max Health in one Attack, Sleep is removed from him and this NP is deactivated. Otherwise, Ivan gains 1 Awaken Counter for every 10 damage received from that Attack.  
When this NP is deactivated, Sleep is removed from Ivan and all Oprichniki on the field immediately disappear. All Awaken Counters are also removed from Ivan.  
Ivan cannot deactivate this NP on his own. In order to deactivate it, his Master has to use the ‘Awaken’ Action during its Turn on Ivan. Roll a six-sided die, and Ivan gains that many Awaken Counters.  
When Ivan has 10◈ Awaken Counters, this NP is deactivated immediately.  
If Sleep is inflicted on Ivan when he is outside his Home Base, Chornyi Oprichnik is immediately activated (regardless of Cooldown) and the Oprichniki are summoned.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after this NP is deactivated.

(Ivan) Zveri・Krestnyy Khod: The Beast that Accompanied me On My Journeys — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive) All effects that affect Units with the ‘Giant’ Attribute will affect Ivan, but their magnitudes are halved. This effect is negated if Method 2 of this NP is Active on Ivan (see below).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can be used in 2 different methods-  
1\. Range=6. Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, has an X0% chance of applying NP DmUp for the turn this NP is used, NP damage dealt is increased by 50%; where X is the number of full Rounds since Chornyi Oprichnik was last deactivated. Then, hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 5x damage and inflicts Def Down (B) on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%. Also inflicts Shock for 4◈ Turns. Lightning damage. Does not hit himself. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.  
2\. (Non-damaging) When this effect is used, it transforms Ivan into his Mammoth Yaga form. Has the following effects for a maximum of 2◈ Turns (can be ended earlier)-  
1\. Ivan’s size is increased from 1 panel to 3 panels.  
2\. Ivan gains the ‘Giant’ Attribute.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
4\. All damage received is reduced by 75%; if NP, 50%.  
5\. Ivan cannot Block; and when he Evades, double the Total value of the Evade roll.  
6\. Ivan can Move to any panel, when he Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Ivan to stand on.  
7\. Ivan can perform 2 different types of Normal Attacks-  
a) Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=1. Hits a 3x3 panel area, and knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
b) Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=4. Hits a 2x2 panel area within Range and has a 50% chance of inflicting Shock for 2◈ Turns. Lightning damage.  
8\. The Range of Almighty Supreme Authority’s Passive effect is 3 panels while this NP is Active, and its Active effect hits a 5x5 panel area instead.  
9\. At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Ivan’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of each Turn if Ivan Acted; and 10 Health if he did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
Ivan can end Zveri・Krestnyy Khod during his Turn, at the end of any Turn, and at the end of the Round.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns (after the NP ends).
