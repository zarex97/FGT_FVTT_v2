(MistressC) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(MistressC) Testarossa Maiden  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Riding’.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(MistressC) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(MistressC) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(MistressC) Spieldorf & Reinfeldt  
Mistress C is accompanied by her two dogs, Spieldorf and Reinfeldt. Their Stats are as follows-  
Spieldorf  
Health: 400  
Agility: 20  
Luck: 10  
MOV: 5 \[Must always be within a 2 panel area of Mistress C or her Master\]  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 75  
Attributes: Beast, Mechanical  
Detect: 3 panels

Reinfeldt  
Health: 300  
Agility: 20  
Luck: 10  
MOV: 5 \[Must always be within a 2 panel area of Mistress C or her Master\]  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 100  
Attributes: Beast, Mechanical  
Detect: 3 panels  
Whenever Mistress C Moves, Spieldorf and Reinfeldt automatically Move together with her (like Passenger Seat). They do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn; but can respectively only Move and/or Attack once per Turn.  
Enemy Units cannot target Mistress C or her Master for an Attack if either Spieldorf or Reinfeldt is directly next to them. Aside from that, Mistress C and her dogs are treated as having a ‘Servant-Master relationship’ in Combat situations (see **Rules – Relationships** in the Rulebook). (Servant=Dogs, Master=Mistress C).  
During your Turn, Mistress C can send one of her dogs to Guard her Master, the dog uses its Move action to Move towards her Master, and remains within a 2 panel area of her Master until Mistress C Calls her dog back.  
During your Turn, Mistress C can use the Call action to call one of her dogs, which is Guarding her Master, back to her side. Mistress C can only either order her dog to Guard or Call it back once per Turn.  
When Spieldorf or Reinfeldt is defeated, it will reappear on a panel directly next to Mistress C after 3◈ Turns.

(MistressC) Femme Fatale (False) — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Skill Seal for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Inflicts Crit Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all Crit Damage taken is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(MistressC) Phantom Thief’s Calling Card — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 30%.  
2\. Inflicts the ‘Kaitou’ debuff for ⅓◈ Turns. When the ‘Kaitou’ debuff naturally expires, increase the Unit’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; then reduce the NP Cooldown of Mistress C and all of Mistress C’s allied Units that are within a 2 panel area of the Unit inflicted with the ‘Kaitou’ debuff by 1◈ Turns. (Note: Mistress C does not need to be within a 2 panel area of the affected Unit to have her NP Cooldown reduced.)  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(MistressC) Mistress・C — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Mistress C. Has the following effects on all enemy Units within that area-  
1\. Reduce the Health of each Unit by 10% (5%?) of their maximum (current?) values, then restore Mistress C’s Health by the total amount drained.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
