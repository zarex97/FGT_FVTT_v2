**Ryouma & Oryou**

Ryouma and Oryou are summoned as two separate Servants as one. Ryouma and Oryou have their individual stats; however, Oryou cannot Move beyond 2 panels of Ryouma. If either one is defeated, the other one is also defeated as well regardless of remaining Health. Note: Moving Oryou does not count towards the number of Units Moved during a Turn.

Both Ryouma and Oryou are allowed a separate Attack during their Turn. Oryou’s Attack does not count towards the number of Units that Attack during a Turn, but she cannot Attack if Ryouma does not Move or Attack during their Turn. 

Ryouma cannot be Attacked if Oryou is in between Ryouma and the AU.

(Ryouma) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Ryouma) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ryouma) Charisma — Rank: C+  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 15 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Ryouma) Eight Proposals while Shipboard — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Ryouma. Reduce their NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns; then apply Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ryouma) Hero of the Reformation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Ryouma. Restores 2 Luck and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%; then apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
