(SimaYi) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SimaYi) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
Trimmau.  
(Passive) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(SimaYi) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(SimaYi) Advice of the Strategist — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Sima Yi. Reduce their NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns; and applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 35%; if NP, 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(SimaYi) Emperor Xuan of Jin’s Command — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Sima Yi. Has the following effects-

2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.
