(Blackbeard) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage taken is reduced by 10% including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Blackbeard) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: \-  
See ‘Voyager of the Storm’.

(Blackbeard) Voyager of the Storm — Rank: A  
(Passive) Sails on the ‘Queen Anne’s Revenge’ (see below).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel range of Blackbeard; or if the Queen Anne’s Revenge is boarded, affects all allied Units upon the Queen Anne’s Revenge. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Blackbeard) Pirate’s Honor — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Guts for 1 time, Blackbeard revives with 1 Health the next time he is defeated.  
3\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. 

(Blackbeard) Gentleman’s Love — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel range of Blackbeard; or if the Queen Anne’s Revenge is boarded, affects all allied Units upon the Queen Anne’s Revenge. Restores their Health by 20% of its maximum value. If an affected Unit is Female or has the ‘Feminine’ Attribute, Health is restored by 40% of its maximum value instead. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Blackbeard) Queen Anne’s Revenge — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging/Passive/Activation) When the Queen Anne’s Revenge is activated, place the ‘Queen Anne’s Revenge’ token on the field, then place Blackbeard upon it. The QAR’s token size is 12 panels (4x3 panels). The QAR’s Stats are as follows-  
Health: 2000  
Agility: 12 (Only performs Injury Roll when damaged by NP)  
Luck: Shared with Blackbeard  
MOV: 7  
Range: 4 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 150  
Attributes: Large, Mechanical  
Detect: 4 panels  
The Queen Anne’s Revenge can move onto occupied panels (when it does, place it on top of the figurines). Other allied Units can board and unboard the QAR by Moving onto it normally. Including Blackbeard, it can hold a maximum of nine Units. Blackbeard cannot unboard the QAR.  
At the end of every ~~Round/1◈ Turns~~ full Round QAR is Active, Blackbeard’s Master loses 30 Health. The QAR is forcefully deactivated at the end of the Round if Blackbeard’s Master has 30 Health or less; his Master does not lose Health at the end of the Round in this situation. This effect overwrites the normal Master Health loss when a Servant uses its NP.  
Enemy Units on the ground cannot target Units onboard the QAR for Attacks/Skills/Spells/Noble Phantasms. AoE Attacks and Noble Phantasms deal full damage to the QAR, and 50% Total Damage to all Units upon it; while Masters onboard the QAR take no damage and effects from those.  
Enemy Units on the ground can only Attack the QAR with ranged Attacks, and allied Units on the QAR can only Attack enemy Units on the ground with ranged Attacks. Units onboard the QAR cannot Attack Units directly below the QAR with ranged Attacks and vice versa.  
Blackbeard’s Normal Attacks are replaced with Attacks from the QAR. Damage dealt by the QAR’s Normal Attacks is increased by 20% for every allied Servant onboard it excluding Blackbeard (e.g. 2 other allied Servants onboard=40% increase).  
If an enemy Unit attempts to board the QAR, it rolls an eight-sided die. The enemy unit successfully boards the QAR if an 8 is rolled. When the QAR is destroyed/deactivated, all Units onboard it are randomly scattered below it. The QAR cannot be affected by buffs and/or debuffs.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns after the Queen Anne’s Revenge is destroyed/deactivated. Blackbeard can deactivate the QAR during his Turn, or at the start or end of any Turn or Round.

(Blackbeard) Queen Anne's Revenge: Revenge of the Queen Anne — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
The QAR’s Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 5x3 or 3x5 panel area on either the left or the right side (either of the wider sides) of the QAR for 3x damage plus 200\. Damage dealt by this NP is increased by 20% for every allied Servant onboard the QAR excluding Blackbeard (e.g. 2 other allied Servants onboard=40% increase).  
Note: Can be used even if the QAR isn't present/activated; in this case the Range is still the same, just applied to Blackbeard.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns.
