(Astolfo) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A (D)  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also see ‘Casseur de Logistille’. If Casseur de Logistille is not selected/on Cooldown, Astolfo’s Magic Resistance is Rank D.  
Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Astolfo) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
Mount: Hippogriff. Passive 1, 2, and 3 are only in effect if Hippogriff: Otherworldly Phantom Horse is selected/not on Cooldown.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, if Astolfo does not have Hippogriff: Otherworldly Phantom Horse selected/usable, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Astolfo) Independent Action — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Astolfo) Monstrous Strength — Rank: C-  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns. Note: This effect is not treated as applying a buff.  
(Passive) At the end of every Combat Phase where Astolfo uses this Skill, Astolfo’s Health is reduced by 30 (cannot drop below 1 in this way).

(Astolfo) Evaporation of Sanity — Rank: D+  
When Astolfo is summoned, roll a four-sided die. The Noble Phantasm corresponding to the number rolled is unusable.  
(Passive) When Astolfo is Attacked, he has a 45% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If Evaporation of Sanity Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is 25% instead. This effect is negated if Astolfo is affected by Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, reduce Astolfo’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Then, applies the ‘Evaporation’ buff to Astolfo for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns. At the start of every Turn, the ‘Evaporation’ buff causes the following effects-  
1\. Has a 65% chance of applying Crit Up to Astolfo for that Turn, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
2\. Has a 65% chance of applying Crit DmUp to Astolfo for that Turn, Crit Damage is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Astolfo) La Black Luna: Magic Flute That Calls Panic — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Army\]                1   
Base Attack (MAG) is used. This NP cannot be Evaded (Note: This is different from having the Aim effect). Hits a 7x7 panel area around Astolfo for 3x damage, then has the following effects-  
1\. Removes Dodge from all affected Units  
2\. Inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%.  
3\. Inflicts Deafen (3) for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns. Has no effect on Units inflicted with Deafen.

(Astolfo) Casseur de Logistille: Destruction Declaration — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]      2  
(Passive) Increases Astolfo’s Magic Resistance Rank from D to A.  
(Trigger) When Astolfo receives damage from a Noble Phantasm that uses Base Attack (MAG), the effect of Magic Resistance is not applied; instead the Total Damage received from that Noble Phantasm is reduced by 50%. Additionally, Astolfo’s Master is not affected by that Noble Phantasm if the Master is directly next to Astolfo. When this effect is triggered, this NP enters Cooldown. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Astolfo) Trap of Argalia: Down with a Touch\! — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]                               3  
(Passive) Whenever Astolfo successfully hits an enemy Servant with a Normal Attack, the DU is inflicted with the ‘Argalia’ debuff for 1◈ Turns- its Max & current Agility and MOV is halved. The ‘Argalia’ debuff does not stack, and is not reset to 1◈ Turns if the DU is hit again.  
When the ‘Argalia’ debuff is removed from a Unit, Max and current Agility are restored back to what it was originally, excluding any Agility lost from Injury Rolls.

(Astolfo) Hippogriff: Otherworldly Phantom Horse — Rank: B++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]                 4  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x11 or 11x1 panel area in a straight line in any direction (including diagonal) next to Astolfo for 3.5x damage plus 200, this NP has the Ignore Def effect; not affected by Magic Resistance. Then, apply Dodge to Astolfo for 3 times and apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 20% . Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.  
Note: Hits in both directions (Front and back); but only hits in one direction (Front) if playing on the Large Board.
