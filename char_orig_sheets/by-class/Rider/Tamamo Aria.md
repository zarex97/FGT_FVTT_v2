(Aria) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: C  
Mount: Kitsune/Master.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Can be used in two different ways-  
1\. Increases MOV by 3 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.   
2\. Only usable when Aria is directly next to her Master. Restores the Health of both Aria and her Master by 15% of their respective maximum values, and both their Luck by 3\. Neither Aria or her Master can Attack on the Turn this effect is used; and cannot Move after this effect is used. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

Charm (Aria) has the same effects as normal Charm, except that it will not be removed when damaged by an Attack as long as that Unit has any Aria Tokens. Whenever a Unit with Aria Tokens receives damage from an Attack, they lose 1 Aria Token at the end of the Combat Phase. When a Unit inflicted with Charm (Aria) loses all Aria Tokens, Charm (Aria) is removed from them. All Aria Tokens will be removed from a Unit if Charm (Aria) is removed from them with any other effect.

(Aria) Leyline Severance  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Cannot be used if there are any enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Aria. Used on an enemy Servant, Aria must be in between the target and the target’s Home Base. When this Skill is successfully used, apply the ‘Disconnected’ effect to the targeted enemy Servant, its’ effects are as follows-  
1\. All damage dealt is reduced by 30% including NP.  
2\. All damage taken is increased by 30% including NP.  
3\. Natural NP Cooldown reduction only occurs once every 2 Turns. (i.e. NP Lag).  
This effect cannot be prevented by any method and is Unremovable. The only way to remove the ‘Disconnected’ effect is for the affected Servant to be in their Home Base at the end of the Round and there are no enemy Units within a 2 panel area of the affected Servant. Only one Servant per Faction can have the ‘Disconnected’ effect at any time.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
