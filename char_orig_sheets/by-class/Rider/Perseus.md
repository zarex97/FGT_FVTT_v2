(Perseus) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Perseus) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Perseus) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP.

(Perseus) Winged Sandals of Hermes — Rank: E (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Applies MOV Up for 1◈ Turns, MOV is increased by 6; and the ‘Hermes’ buff for 1◈ Turns, the rolled value of Agility Check rolls are reduced by half (the dice value, not the final value). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Perseus) Bronze Mirror Shield of Athena — Rank: D (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Perseus’s Evade Rolls are not reduced due to being Attacked from the sides or behind.  
2\. Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
3\. When an enemy Unit Evades Perseus’ Attack, its Evade roll is increased by 2\.

(Perseus) Kibisis: Bag of Mirror Inversion — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Sealing\]  
(Non-damaging) Range=3. Used on an enemy Unit within Range when it is about to use a non-damaging Skill, Spell, or Noble Phantasm that would inflict a debuff(s) on Perseus or an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Perseus; the targeted enemy Unit’s Skill/Spell/NP is instead targeted onto itself only. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Perseus) Harpe: Immortal Slaying Scythe — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Normal Attack damage dealt to Units with the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute is increased by 100%. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 50%. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Serpent’ Attribute but without the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute is increased by 25%. If a Unit has two or more of the listed Attributes, only the effect with the strongest magnitude takes effect.  
2\. Normal Attacks have a 25% chance of inflicting Instakill on Units with the ‘Gorgon’ Attribute at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. Refraction of Longevity- Normal Attacks inflict the ‘Harpe (X)’ debuff on the DU, X=the total amount of damage dealt by Perseus to this Unit (X increases whenever Perseus deals damage to a Unit with the ‘Harpe’ debuff). Whenever a Unit with the ‘Harpe’ debuff has its Health restored, the amount of Health restored is reduced by the value of the ‘Harpe’ debuff. However, the ‘Harpe’ debuff **does not** affect the Health restoration effect of Home Bases. The ‘Harpe’ debuff is Unremovable and is only removed when Perseus is defeated. The ‘Harpe’ debuff ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects.

(Perseus) Bellerophon: Bridle of Chivalry \- Pegasus — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x13 or 13x1 panel area in a straight line in any direction (including diagonal) next to Perseus for 4x damage plus 100\. Then, applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: Hits in both directions (Front and back); but only hits in one direction (Front) if playing on the Large Board.
