(Quetz) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: EX  
Quetzalcoatl.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Quetz) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Quetz) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 120 including NP. While within a ‘Burning (Piedra)’ area, all damage dealt is increased by 180 instead including NP; if a ‘Burning (Piedra)’ area exists on the field but Quetz is outside it, all damage dealt is increased by 150 instead including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 50%.  
(Passive 3\) While within a ‘Burning (Piedra)’ area, all damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP; if a ‘Burning (Piedra)’ area exists on the field but Quetz is outside it, all damage taken is reduced by 25% instead including NP.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Quetz) Charisma of the Sun — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.

3\. Applies the ‘Sol’ buff to herself for 1◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- The 5x5 panel area around Quetz is ‘Sunlight’ Terrain.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Quetz) Xiuhcoatl: O Flame, Burn the Gods Themselves — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\~Anti-Fortress\]  
Base Attack (STR) and half of Base Attack (MAG) is used (BA=250), not affected by Magic Resistance. Range+1 for the Combat Process; if the MOV Up effect of ‘Riding’ is Active, Range \+2 for the Combat Process instead. Deals 4x damage and inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns, and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage (half).   
Then (regardless of whether the NP successfully hits the DU or not), deals normal damage to all Units within a 2 panel area of Quetzalcoatl except herself and the previously targeted Unit (Base Attack (MAG) is used) with a 25% chance of inflicting NP Seal for 1◈ Turns and inflicts Burn for 1◈ Turns. These Units can Block/Evade, but cannot Counter. Fire damage.  
Xiuhcoatl cannot be used if Quetz is Riding the Quetzalcoatlus.  
If this NP is used within or directly next to a \[Fortress\] NP (regardless of ally’s or enemy’s), that NP area and the panels directly outside/next to the NP area are now ‘Burning’ until the Fortress NP is deactivated.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(Quetz) Quetzalcoatl: Winged Serpent — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\~Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, Quetz summons a Quetzalcoatlus at her position, and she is Moved onto the Quetzalcoatlus together with her Master if her Master is next to her/the Quetzalcoatlus (otherwise her Master can get on the Quetzalcoatlus when they are directly next to it during their Turn or at the end of any Turn they or the Quetzalcoatlus Acts). The Quetzalcoatlus’ Stats are as follows-  
Health: 1000  
Agility: 16  
Luck: Shared with Quetz’s  
MOV: 7  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 150  
Attributes: Large, Wild Beast, \[Earth\], Levitating  
While Quetz is Riding the Quetzalcoatlus, gain the following effects-  
1\. Quetz’s Move and Normal Attack is replaced with Quetzalcoatlus’.   
2\. Quetz or her Master cannot be targeted for an Attack while they are Riding the Quetzalcoatlus. If they are hit with an AoE Attack, the Quetzalcoatlus receives full damage, Quetz receives 50% Total Damage while her Master receives no damage and effects.  
3\. The Quetzalcoatlus ignores obstacles while Moving, and can Move onto occupied panels (place the Quetzalcoatlus on top of anything occupying said panels). Note: While the Quetzalcoatlus is on top of occupied panels, Range is treated as 1 panel for the other Unit(s) on those panels  
4\. While Quetz is Riding the Quetzalcoatlus, she is able to use the three Quetzal Spells listed below.   
5\. Passive 1 of Goddess’ Divine Core has no effect.  
6\. After every 1◈ Turns (At the end of every Full Round), Quetz’s Master’s Health is reduced by 25 at the end of the Turn (Round) if this NP is Active unless the NP was deactivated during this Round. If her Master’s Health is 25 or less, this NP is forcefully deactivated at the end of the Round. This NP can be deactivated during Quetz’s Turn or at the start or end of any Round or Turn, but cannot be deactivated for 2◈ Turns after it was activated.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after Quetzalcoatlus is defeated.
