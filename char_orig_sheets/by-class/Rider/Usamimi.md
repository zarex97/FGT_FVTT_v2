(Usamimi) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Usamimi) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 2 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

4\. SFX(4): MOV is increased by 2 while the effect of Presence Concealment (SFX) is Active. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2 while the effect of Presence Concealment (SFX) is Active and until the end of the Turn it is deactivated.

5\. SFX(5): While the effect of Presence Concealment (SFX) is Active, Health is restored by 5% of its maximum value at the end of the Turn after every ⅓◈ Turns.

6\. SFX(6): When the Unit has the SFX(6) effect, it gains the following Attack Skill- Hits the 3x3 panel area behind the Unit for 2x damage (Usamimi’s Base Attack (MAG) is used), inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns and knocks back all affected Units by 1 panel, then remove the SFX(6) effect from the Unit. Fire damage. Damage dealt by this Attack is not affected by Presence Concealment (SFX).

When a Unit with an SFX effect receives damage from an Attack while the effect of Presence Concealment (SFX) is Active, remove the SFX effect from the Unit. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Class Skill: Presence Concealment (SFX) — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 20% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.

(Usamimi) Illusion — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or when herself or an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself is Attacked. Creates an Usamimi clone within a 3 panel area of herself that has the Decoy effect. The clone cannot Attack but can Move; it can Move once per Turn during every Turn. When that clone is Attacked, roll a six-sided die and activate the corresponding effect-  
1\. The clone explodes in a shower of confetti. Has a 25% chance of inflicting Confuse for 1◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step on the Unit who Attacked it.  
2\. The clone melts into a puddle. Inflict Slow on the Unit who Attacked it for 1◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. The clone blows a raspberry and disappears. The Unit who Attack it is inflicted with LUC Dwn for 1◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step, all Luck Check rolls are increased by 3\.  
4\. The clone swears before disappearing. All enemy Units within a 2 panel area of the clone are inflicted with Curse at the end of the Damage Step.  
5\. The clone’s clothes explode before disappearing. All Male Units within a 2 panel area of the clone are inflicted with Charm for 1◈ Turns at the end of the Damage Step, affected Units are controlled by Usamimi.  
6\. The clone explodes like a nuke. Deals 3x damage to all Units (use Usamimi’s Base Attack (MAG)) within a 3 panel area of the clone and inflict Burn for 3◈ Turns (activates at the end of the Damage Step). Fire damage.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

The Stats of Selenites are as follows-

Agility: 13
