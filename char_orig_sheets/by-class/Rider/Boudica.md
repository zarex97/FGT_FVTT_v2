(Boudica) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Boudica) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
Chariot Of Boudica.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Boudica) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Boudica) Oath to the Goddess — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute is increased by 50% including Noble Phantasm.

(Boudica) Queen of Victory — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Boudica. Applies Dmg Up (Rome) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute is increased by 50%. Also applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Boudica) Battlefield Provisions — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Applies Guts for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when the Unit is defeated it is revived with 200 Health; and applies Max HpUp for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Max & current Health is increased by 200\. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Boudica) Protection from Andraste — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Boudica; but has no effect on Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. Applies Block Up for 1◈ Turns, double the value of the Unit’s Block rolls.  
4\. Applies Agility Boost and Luck Boost for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Boudica) Chariot of Boudica: Chariot Without Promised Protection — Rank: B++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn or when an allied Unit within a 3 panel area of Boudica is Attacked. Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of Boudica. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Further applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.

(Boudica) Sword of Boudica: Sword Without Promised Protection — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Can be used in 2 different methods-  
1\. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals normal damage. Categorized as a Normal Attack; does not consume her Master’s Health when used. Counts as Boudica’s Attack for the Turn.  
2\. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 3.5x damage. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns. Method 1 of Sword of Boudica cannot be used for 2◈ Turns after using Method 2\.

Note: All Units from the Region of ‘Rome’ have the ‘Roma’ Attribute. All Units inflicted/affected/who have Roma Stacks have the ‘Roma’ Attribute. Roma Stacks are categorized as a debuff when applied to enemy Units and are categorized as a buff when applied to allied Units.
