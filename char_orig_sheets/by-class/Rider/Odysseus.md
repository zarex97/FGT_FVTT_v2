(Odysseus) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Odysseus) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B+  
Aigis, Troia Hippos.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Odysseus) Troia Hippos: Full Summoning  
Used during your Turn. When used in this way, Odysseus summons Troia Hippos on the panel he was standing on, and he moves onto the top of it. Troia Hippos has the following Stats-

Troia Hippos  
Health: 2000  
Agility: 14  
Luck: Shared with Odysseus’s  
MOV: 6 (Trojan Mode), 5 (Warrior Mode)  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target (Trojan Mode), 3 panels, 1 target (Warrior Mode)  
Base Attack (STR): 150  
Base Attack (MAG): 235  
Attributes: Giant, Mechanical  
At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
During Odysseus’ Turn, before he Acts, Troia Hippos can transform from Trojan Mode into Warrior Mode, and vice versa. When Troia Hippos is summoned, it is summoned in Trojan Mode.  
Troia Hippos can Move to any panel, when it moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Troia Hippos to stand on.  
Troia Hippos’s Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
Odysseus cannot be Attacked while he is on Troia Hippos.  
Troia Hippos is affected by the Passive effect of Aigis. Odysseus cannot use Skills while he is within Troia Hippos excluding the Riding Skill; all effects of the Riding Skill are active/can be used while Troia Hippos is summoned.  
At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Odysseus’ Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn if Trois Hippos Acted; and 10 Health if it did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 25 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
Troia Hippos can use Colossal Trojan Horse of Obliteration, if he does the Range is a 3x6 or 6x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to himself and uses Troia Hippos’ Base Attack (MAG). Then, Health loss incurred by Odysseus’s Master due to NP usage is increased by 50% plus 30; and this NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn.  
Odysseus can end Troia Hippos: Full Summoning during his Turn, at the end of any Turn he Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
If Troia Hippos is defeated, Troia Hippos is forcefully ended at the end of the Combat Process; and its Cooldown is doubled. If Odysseus is inflicted with NP Seal, Troia Hippos is immediately ended.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns after it ends.
