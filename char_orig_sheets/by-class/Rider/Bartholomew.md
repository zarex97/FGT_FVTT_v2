(Bartholomew) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to E is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 10%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Bartholomew) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 2 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Bartholomew) Voyager of the Storm — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Bart. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Bartholomew) Pirate Gentleman — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns on Bart, Crit Chance is reduced by 100%; then apply the following effects to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage is increased by 40%. For Units with the ‘Mekakure’ Attribute, apply as ‘Crit Damage is increased by 60%’ instead.  
2\. Reduce their NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns; affected Units with the ‘Mekakure’ Attribute have their NP Cooldown reduced by 1◈ Turns instead.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns

(Bartholomew) Plundering Gale — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Bart. Has the following effects-  
1\. Bart restores 2 Agility and applies Atk Up to himself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
3\. Inflicts Crit Dwn (Plundered) for 1◈ Turns on the affected enemy Units, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%.  
4\. Apply the ‘Plunderer’ buff to Bart for 1◈ Turns- Normal Attacks have a 20% chance of Stealing a random Item from the enemy Unit; and Steal chance is further increased by 20% on Units with a ‘Plundered’ debuff for both Normal Attack Steals and the actual Steal action.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
