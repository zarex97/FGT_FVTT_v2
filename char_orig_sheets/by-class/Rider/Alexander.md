(Alexander) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
Mount: Bucephalus.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Alexander) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Alexander) Divinity — Rank: E  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 10 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Alexander) Charisma — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 15 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Alexander) Road to Iskandar — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Alexander’s NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn for every Unit affected by this Skill. If ◈ is greater than 4, 1 Turn is replaced with 2 Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Alexander) Rosy-cheeked Adonis — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute. Has an 80% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Alexander) Signs of a King — Rank: A  
Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Agility Boost & Luck Boost to Alexander for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area around Alexander. Restore 2 Agility and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Alexander) Bucephalus: The Beginning of Trampling Conquest — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. When this NP is used, Alexander can Move to an unoccupied panel, then hit a 3x3 panel area around him for 3.5x damage plus 100\. Lightning damage (half). Has a 50% chance of inflicting Shock for 2◈ Turns. Then, applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Alexander) Zeus’ Thunder: God's Blessing — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Can be used after 3 full Rounds have passed since the start of the game. When this NP is used, increase Alexander’s STR by one Rank (Base Attack (STR) is increased by 25\), and decrease his Skill ‘Rosy-cheeked Adonis’ by one Rank (e.g. B to C). For every decrease in Rank, Rosy-cheeked Adonis’ chance of inflicting Charm is reduced by 20%.   
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns. Can only be used a maximum of 3 times.  
(Passive) After the first usage of this NP, Alexander's Normal Attacks deal Lightning damage (half) with a 20% chance of inflicting Shock for 1◈ Turns.

(Alexander) Rosy-cheeked Adonis (Lightning) — Rank: A  
(Trigger) After Zeus’ Thunder: God’s Blessing has been used a total of 3 times, the next time Alexander uses the ‘Signs of a King’ Skill, Alexander’s Rosy-cheeked Adonis Skill turns into Rosy-cheeked Adonis (Lightning).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range with the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute. Inflicts Charm for 1◈ Turns and removes all buffs from that Unit. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
