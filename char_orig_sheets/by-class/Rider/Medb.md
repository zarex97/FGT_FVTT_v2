(Medb) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Medb) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
Chariot My Love.   
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Can be used in two different ways-  
1\. Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.  
2\. Used during your Turn. Only usable when Medb is directly next to her Master. Restores the Health of both Medb and her Master by 25% of their respective maximum values, and both their Luck by 2\. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Neither Medb or her Master can Attack on the Turn this Active Skill effect is used; and cannot Move after this effect is used.

(Medb) Golden Rule (Body) — Rank: A  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted with debuffs is reduced by 20%; if Instakill, 10%; if Death, 5%. Does not affect Erase.  
2\. Medb’s Health is restored by 25 at the end of her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round. Negated while Medb has the Regen (Queen) buff.  
3\. Whenever Medb performs or receives a successful Attack, she has a 50% chance of reducing her NP Cooldown by 1 Turn at the end of the Damage Step. Negated while Medb has the NP Regen (Queen) buff.

(Medb) Queen’s Body — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used on your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns; with chance of being inflicted with Instakill is reduced by 50%, while chance of being inflicted with Death is reduced by 25%. Does not affect Erase.  
2\. Applies Regen (Queen) for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value at the end of Medb’s Turn and at the end of any Turn Medb Acts.  
3\. Applies NP Regen (Queen) for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of each Turn.  
4\. Reduces the NP Cooldown of all Male allied Units and allied Units with the ‘Fae’ Attribute within a 2 panel area of herself by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Medb) Queen’s Discipline — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; this effect is applied twice on all affected Male Units. Then, Medb’s Health is restored by 15% of its maximum value. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Medb) Alluring Nightingale — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has an 80% chance of inflicting Charm on the DU for 1◈ Turns. For every Rank in Magic Resistance the DU has, the chance of being Charmed is reduced by 10% (e.g. Rank D=2 Ranks=20% reduction). If the DU is Female, the chance of being Charmed is halved (after the Magic Resistance reduction effect). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Medb) Blood Soldier Manufactory  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Roll a six-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Celtic Blood Soldiers created by Medb (they will appear within a 5x5 panel area around Medb). Then, roll a six-sided die for each one. The number rolled represents which type of Celtic Blood Soldier is created. All Celtic Blood Soldiers have the ‘Male’ Attribute. Counts as Medb’s Attack for the Turn.  
1, 2\. Celtic Blood Soldier (Rogue): 100 Health, 12 Agility, 6 Luck, Range=1, MOV=5 and Base Attack (STR)=75, Detect=2 panels  
3, 4\. Celtic Blood Soldier (Archer): 100 Health, 10 Agility, 6 Luck, Range=3, MOV=4 and Base Attack (STR)=75, Detect=3 panels  
5, 6\. Celtic Blood Soldier (Spearman): 125 Health, 10 Agility, 6 Luck, Range=2, MOV=4 and Base Attack (STR)=75, Detect=2 panels  
Celtic Blood Soldiers do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Celtic Blood Soldier can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot target Medb or her Master for Attacks if any Celtic Blood Soldiers are directly next to them. Cooldown: (Number of Celtic Blood Soldiers created x ⅔◈) Turns.

(Medb) Chariot My Love: My Dear Iron Chariot — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army/Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=7. Deals 3.5x damage plus 100, damage dealt is further increased by 200% on Male Units. Then, inflicts nvDebuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with non-volatile debuffs is increased by 60%.  
After that, has a 50% chance of inflicting Terror on all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of the DU for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Stun from Terror is 25% (25% Terror).  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Medb) My Red Mead: My Dear Honey Alcohol — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects all Male Units within a 7x7 panel area around Medb. Inflicts Charm (Mead) on them for 3◈ Turns, if damage is received from an Attack, Charm (Mead) is not removed unless the damage received in one hit is more than 5% of the affected Unit’s Max Health. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

Cooldown: 7◈ Turns

(Medb) Fergus My Love: My Dear Rainbow Sword — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x4 or 4x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Medb for 4x damage plus 100\. Has a 50% chance of inflicting Instakill on (any version of) Cú Chulainn. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

Note: Fergus My Love cannot be used while Medb has the ‘Conchobar’ buff.
