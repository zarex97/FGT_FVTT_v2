(Mandricardo) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Mandricardo) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
Mount: Brigliadoro.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Mandricardo) Serment de Durandal: Oath of the Sword — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Mandricardo cannot equip any Weapon Items aside from \[Wooden Sword\] and \[Durandal\]. When Mandricardo is summoned, he is equipped with the \[Wooden Sword\]. The \[Wooden Sword\] has a Durability of 100\. Whenever Mandricardo performs a Normal Attack with the \[Wooden Sword\] equipped, its Durability is reduced by 10; if NP, 50\. When Durability drops to 0 or lower, the Wooden Sword breaks after performing the Attack. Additionally, whenever Mandricardo performs an Attack with a \[Wooden Sword\] equipped, it has a X% chance of breaking after performing the Attack, where X=(100 \- current Durability).  
When the \[Wooden Sword\] breaks, Mandricardo’s Base Attack (STR) is reduced to 50\. When Mandricardo does not have a \[Wooden Sword\] (regardless of how it was lost), he obtains a new one after 1◈ Turns (1 full Round?) have passed since his previous \[Wooden Sword\] broke.  
(Active) Base Attack (STR) and half of Base Attack (MAG) combined is used; effect of Magic Resistance is halved. Range=half of Mandricardo’s Move. Can only be used if \[Wooden Sword\] or \[Durandal\] is Equipped. First, his Agility and Luck are restored by X, where X depends on how much Durability Mandricardo’s Wooden Sword has. X is as follows-  
Durability higher than 75: 4  
Durability 25 to 75: 3  
Durability lower than 25: 2  
Then apply Atk Up to Mandricardo for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by Y0%n, where Y depends on how much Durability Mandricardo’s Wooden Sword has. Y is as follows-  
Durability higher than 75: 50% (NP, 30%)  
Durability 25 to 75: 40% (NP, 30%)  
Durability lower than 25: 30% (NP, 20%)  
Deals 4x damage. At the end of the Damage Step, Mandricardo Moves to a panel directly next to the DU, and Mandricardo is inflicted with Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
If used while \[Durandal\] is Equipped, all effects are activated at their maximum values.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Rêve de Durandal is on Cooldown.
