(Europa) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Europa) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
White Bull: Taurus.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Europa) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Europa) Princess of Purity — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects.  
1\. Applies Pierce (Purity) to herself for 3 times.  
2\. Applies Debuff ResUp (Purity) to herself for 3 times, when Europa receives an effect that applies debuff(s) to her, the chance of being inflicted by all debuffs in that effect is reduced by 100%, this effect is reduced by 10% each time a debuff is received (1st time=100%, 2nd time=90%, 3rd time=80%).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Whenever a ‘Purity’ buff is applied while she already has the same ‘Purity’ buff, the number of times it can activate is reset to 3 times.

If Tails, apply Dmg Boost (Charm) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with Charm is increased by 30%.

(Europa) Bronze Giant Talos: Full Summoning  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. When used in this way, Europa summons Talos on the panel she was standing on, and she moves onto the top of it. Talos has the following Stats-

Bronze Giant Talos  
Health: 3000  
Agility: 8  
Luck: Shared with Europa’s  
MOV: 3  
Range: 4 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 200  
Base Attack (MAG): 200  
Attributes: Giant, Mechanical  
At a Range of 1 to 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
Talos can Move to any panel, when it Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Talos to stand on.  
Talos’s Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 to 2 knocks back the DU by one panel.  
Europa cannot be Attacked while she is on Talos. Europa cannot use Skills while she is on Talos.  
Talos can use Sudden Impact and Chrome Buster, damage dealt is increased by 50% if used in this way.  
At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Europa’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn if Talos Acted; and 10 Health if it did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
Talos can use Sfyrí tou Tálos, if he does the Range is calculated using his figurine, but Range=2, and uses Talos’ Base Attack (STR). Then, Health loss incurred by Europa’s Master due to NP usage is increased by 50% plus 30; and this NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn.  
Europa can end Bronze Giant Talos: Full Summoning during her Turn, at the end of any Turn, and at the end of the Round.  
If Talos is defeated, ‘Bronze Giant Talos: Full Summoning’ is forcefully ended at the end of the Combat Process; and its Cooldown is doubled. In this case, while ‘Bronze Giant Talos: Full Summoning’ is on Cooldown, both Talos Skills cannot be used.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after it ends. If Europa is inflicted with NP Seal, ‘Bronze Giant Talos: Full Summoning’ is immediately ended.

(Europa) Sfyrí tou Tálos: Bronze Giant's Ultra-Heavy Mallet — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Range=4. Uses Talos’ Base Attack (STR)=200. Has the following effects, in this order-  
1\. First applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%; this Def Up buff is extended by ⅓◈ Turns if only one of Talos’ Skills is on Cooldown, and is extended by ⅔◈ Turns if none of Talos’ Skills are on Cooldown.  
2\. Then applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, depending on how many Talos Skills are on Cooldown except Full Summoning-  
a. Both Talos Skills are on Cooldown: All damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
b. Only one Talos Skill is on Cooldown: All damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
c. No Talos Skills are on Cooldown: All damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 40%.  
3\. Then, hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 4x damage and inflicts Burn for 3◈ Turns. Fire damage (half). Also hits the panels directly around the NP area for 2x damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈+½◈ Turns. Fire damage.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

Note: Bronze Giant Talos: Full Summoning cannot be used if Sfyrí tou Tálos is on Cooldown and vice versa.
