**Anne Bonny & Mary Read**

Anne and Mary are summoned as two separate Servants as one. Anne and Mary have their individual stats; however, the maximum distance between the two is 5 panels between them (4 if diagonal); (If Large Board, max distance between them is 8 panels; if diagonal, 7\). If either one is defeated, the other one is also defeated as well regardless of remaining Health. Both Anne and Mary are respectively allowed to Move and Attack once during their Turn; each one counts as 0.5 Units.

When calculating whether the damage dealt by either one of them needs to be reduced if she is too far from their Master, as long as the other counterpart is within their Master’s Servant Range, damage dealt is not reduced.

When either Anne or Mary uses a Skill, the Skill enters Cooldown for both of them.

(Anne) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Anne) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: \-  
See ‘Voyage’.

(Anne) Voyage — Rank: A  
When Anne is within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area, they can use the effects of this Skill.  
(Passive) Passenger Seat: When Anne Moves, up to 5 other allied Units that are within a 2 panel area of her can Move along with her; after Moving, all other Units that Moved together with Anne must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Voyage’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.  
This Skill is treated as ‘Riding’.

(Anne) Docking Assault — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, Anne is revived with 1 Health when defeated.  
2\. Applies Decoy for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
4\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
5\. Apply MOV Up for 1◈ Turns, MOV is increased by 2\.  
If Mary is within a 3 panel area of Anne when this Skill is used, the effects of this Skill are applied to her as well.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Anne) Marksmanship — Rank: B  
(Passive) Crit Damage dealt at a Range of 3 or higher is increased by 50%.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Phase when performing a Normal Attack, Range is increased by 2 for that Normal Attack. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Anne) Dazzling Pirate Princesses — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns to herself and Mary, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns. 

(Anne) Combination — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has different effects depending on the distance between Anne and Mary.  
1\. When Mary is within a 3 panel area of Anne, this Skill applies Atk Up (Combination) to both of them for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%; and applies Crit Up (Combination) to both of them for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
2\. When Mary is further than 3 panels away from Anne, this Skill applies Atk Up (Combination) to both of them for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and applies Crit Up (Combination) to both of them for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Anne\&Mary) Caribbean Free Bird: Wings Abreast As If Trees With Entwined Branches — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Uses Anne and Mary’s Base Attack (STR) combined (i.e. BA=175). Can only be used when the maximum distance between Anne and Mary is 3 panels (if diagonal, 2 panels). Range=3 panels from the NP initiator. Deals 3x damage plus 200; for every enemy Unit within a 3 panel area of both Anne and Mary and for every 200 Health that both Anne and Mary are missing, damage dealt is further increased by 10%. Note: The damage increasing effect does not stack if the same enemy Unit is within a 3 panel area of both Anne and Mary.  
Example: Anne’s Max Health is 956, Mary’s Max Health is 893\. 2 enemy Units within 3 panel area of Mary, 1 enemy Unit within a 2 panel area of Anne, Anne’s Health is 723, Mary’s Health is 402\. Therefore, damage dealt is further increased by 60%.  
If both Anne & Mary have at least one ‘Combination’ buff on each of them, this NP is affected by Crit Up and Crit DmUp buffs. Counts as both Anne and Mary’s Attack for the Turn. The effects of all Skills, buffs and debuffs on both Anne and Mary are combined when calculating damage for this NP.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns.
