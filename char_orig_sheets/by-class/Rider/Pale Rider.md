(Pale Rider) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. Pale Rider cannot take damage.  
2\. Pale Rider’s Master’s ZON is increased by X panels, X=Pale Rider’s MOV.  
3\. Pale Rider cannot perform Normal Attacks.  
4\. Pale Rider cannot Evade, Block, or Counter.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Pale Rider) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Pale Rider) Contagion — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) The 2 panel area around Pale Rider is the Contagion area. The effects of Contagion trigger whenever the following occurs-  
1\. At the end of Pale Rider’s Turn: Affects all enemy Units within the Contagion area.  
2\. If an enemy Unit ended its Turn within the Contagion area, or at the end of a Turn an enemy Unit Acted and ended that Turn within the Contagion area: Only affects that enemy Unit.  
When triggered, the effects of Contagion are as follows-  
1\. Health is reduced by 100\. Not affected by effects that modify damage taken (does not count as ‘damage’).  
2\. Has a 50% chance of being inflicted with Poison.  
3\. Has a 10% chance of being inflicted with Charm for 1◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Contagious’ effect for 1◈ Turns, this effect increases the Contagion area by a Range of 2 panels (i.e. from 5x5 panel area around Pale Rider to a 9x9 panel area). The ‘Contagious’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) While ‘Doomsday Come’ is Active, Contagion constantly affects all enemy Units within the NP area instead of its usual Range; and the chance of being inflicted with Poison is 75% while chance of being inflicted with Charm is 25%; and if the enemy Unit is within a 3 panel area of Pale Rider’s Master, Health is reduced by 150 instead of 100\.

(Pale Rider) Innocent World — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Constantly affects all enemy Units within ‘Doomsday Come’. Applies the following effects depending on which of the Unit’s Parameters are highest, as follows-  
1\. STR highest: All damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. END highest: All damage received is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. AGI highest: The value of Agility Check rolls are increased by 4\.  
4\. MAG highest: Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 50% and Total Debuff Damage taken is increased by 50%.  
5\. LUC highest: The value of Luck Check rolls are increased by 4\.  
6\. If the Unit has any NP whose Rank is higher than all its Parameters: The Servant is affected with NP Seal.  
If a Unit has no Parameters, roll a six-sided die and apply the effect corresponding to the number rolled; that Unit will receive the same effect every time it is affected by Innocent World. If the Unit has two or more Parameters of the same Rank, it is affected by all related effects.  
The effects of Innocent World cannot be prevented or removed as long as a Unit is within ‘Doomsday Come’.

(Pale Rider) Guidance of the Netherworld — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of itself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value at the end of the Unit’s Turn, the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Applies Dmg Cut for 1◈ Turns, 3 times; all damage taken is reduced by 100\.  
Then, applies the ‘Guidance’ effect to all affected Units excluding itself. The ‘Guidance’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. When a Unit with the ‘Guidance’ effect enters the area of ‘Doomsday Come’, apply Effects 1 to 3 of ‘Guidance of the Netherworld’ to that Unit, then remove the ‘Guidance’ effect from that Unit (Note: Does not trigger if applied while that Unit is within the area of ‘Doomsday Come’, that Unit needs to leave and reenter the ‘Doomsday Come’ area to trigger the ‘Guidance’ effect).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Pale Rider) Doomsday Come: Come to Me, Realm of the Dead, Come to Me — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, an X panel area around Pale Rider’s Master becomes the ‘Doomsday Come’ area, where X=(2+number rolled on a four-sided die) for 2◈ Turns; this ‘Doomsday Come’ area Moves together with Pale Rider’s Master.  
Enemy Units within the Doomsday Come area cannot leave said area, but enemy Units outside the Doomsday Come area can enter it; while allied Units can freely Move in and out of the Doomsday Come area. If an enemy Unit is at the inner border of the Doomsday Come area and Pale Rider would Move in a way that would cause that Unit to leave the area, then that Unit is forced to Move along with the Doomsday Come area.  
Units outside the Doomsday Come area cannot Attack Units within it and vice versa.  
After the initial NP period, Pale Rider’s Master can extend the NP duration by 1◈ more Turns by reducing its Health by 100 (cannot be used if the Master’s Health is less than 100), and can be repeatedly extended.  
During Pale Rider’s Turn, if there are any enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Doomsday Come’s outer border, Pale Rider can target an enemy Unit within this Range, that target has to perform an Evade roll. If the Evade succeeded, nothing happens. If the Evade failed, the DU is forcibly dragged into the Doomsday Come area and placed on a random panel within. Can only be used once per Turn.  
A Noble Phantasm of \[Anti-World\] or higher can be used on Doomsday Come (from outside) or within Doomsday Come (Note: The entire Doomsday Come area is treated as one Unit for this purpose, Range is calculated from its nearest border. Additionally, (Non-damaging) Noble Phantasms can also be used for this purpose). If used in this way, Doomsday Come is forcibly ended at the end of that Combat Process, and all Units within it receive the damage from that NP, but its Total Damage is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns after Doomsday Come ends.

The following Servant-Master Relationship Rules have no effect between Pale Rider and its Master; but apply between Kagome Spirits and Pale Rider’s Master (replace ‘Servant’ with ‘Kagome Spirit’)-

Master-Servant Relationship in Combat situations  
When a Master becomes the target of an Attack while their Servant is within a 2 panel area of themselves, perform the following checks-

1\. If the Target Unit is a Master whose Servant is within the AU’s Range, the AU can only target the Servant.

2\. If the Target Unit is a Master whose Servant is not within the AU’s Range but standing on a panel directly next to their Master, the AU can target the Master, but the target is automatically changed to their Servant at the start of the Combat Phase.

3\. If the Target Unit is a Master whose Servant is not within the AU’s Range and also not standing on a panel directly next to their Master- At the start of the Combat Phase, the Master’s Servant performs an Agility Check (against the AU). If successful, the Servant Moves to the nearest panel which is directly next to their Master and the Attack target is changed to the Servant; if Failed, Combat proceeds as normal. Note: If the Servant has an effect which prevents it from Moving (e.g. Immobilize), then it cannot perform this Agility Check.  
   
Units are not allowed to enter a 1 panel area of enemy Masters if that Master's Servant is within 2 panels of its Master. However, Masters can stop on a panel directly next to an enemy Unit (this is because I cannot think of a way to stop this happening in reverse).  
   
When a Master that has its Servant within a 2 panel area of itself gets caught in an AoE Noble Phantasm and fails to Evade, the Servant performs an Agility Check/Agility Check-. If Successful, the Servant shoves (Moves) its Master out of the NP area (the Master is Moved to one panel outside of the NP area), and the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
If Failed, the Master receives no damage and effects, while the Total Damage the Servant takes from the AoE NP is increased by 100%; and in this situation, Servants cannot Evade the enemy Unit’s AoE NP if their Master is within a 2 panel range of them.  
If a Master in this situation has more than one Servant, and more than one of its Servants are within a 2 panel area of itself, the increase in Total Damage taken by the Servants are divided by the number of Servants Covering for their Master (Example: Master with two Servants within a 2 panel area of itself fails to Evade an AoE NP, so the Total Damage received by that Master’s Servants are increased by 50% respectively).

When a Master in the same conditions as the above paragraph gets caught in a non-NP AoE Attack, the above process can be performed, but it is optional. This Action in general will be termed as ‘Cover’.  
Note: If a Servant fails to Shove their Master out of an AoE NP, but that Servant is not within the NP area, that Servant cannot Cover for their Master.

Items held: Pale Rider cannot hold Items, all Items that would be obtained by Pale Rider are instead obtained by his Master if he/she is within a 2 panel area of Pale Rider; otherwise the Item will be left on the panel where the Unit who dropped the Item was standing on.
