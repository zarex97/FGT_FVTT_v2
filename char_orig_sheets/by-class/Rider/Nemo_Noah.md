(Nemo/Noah) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
Noah’s Ark.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Nemo/Noah) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Nemo/Noah) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Sustainability does not apply.  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.

(Nemo/Noah) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Nemo) 20000 Leagues Under the Sea  
This Skill is only in effect while not in Zohar Mode.  
(Passive) At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Water damage. At a Range of 3, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Active) Attack Skill. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. Deals normal damage with a 20% chance of inflicting Drowning for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Nemo/Noah) Voyager of Salvation — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever Noah uses the Active effect of his Riding Skill, apply Shield (200) for 1◈ Turns to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for 1 time, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 10%.  
4\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to affected ‘Animal’ Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
5\. Removes all debuffs from affected ‘Animal’ Units.  
6\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns to all affected ‘Animal’ Units, Health is restored by 200 at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

‘Animal’ Units: Units with either the ‘Wild Beast’, ‘Demonic Beast’, or ‘Animalistic’ Attribute

6\. NP Cooldown of all allied ‘Animal’ Units within a 2 panel area of himself is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nemo/Noah) Zohar’s Glowing Stone — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Zohar’ buff for 1◈ Turns, when Noah performs a successful Normal Attack, apply Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
4\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.

(Nemo/Noah) Noah’s Ark: The Arkship of Noah — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Cannot be used while in Zohar Mode. First, count the number of other allied Units within a 3 panel area of Noah. This NP has different effects depending on whether that number is an odd or an even number.  
Odd number: Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%. Then, hits all enemy Units within a 7x7 panel area next to himself.  
Even number: Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself, all damage taken is reduced by 60%; if NP, 30%. Then, hits all enemy Units within a 9x9 panel area next to himself.  
Inflicts Def Dwn (A) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 10%; and Luck is reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack. Deals 4x damage plus 100\. Water damage. Then, the NP area becomes a ‘Waterside’ area for 3◈ Turns. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Nemo/Noah) Great Ram, Noah’s Ark: Re-Genesis Tribulations — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Giant\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Can only be used while in Zohar Mode. First, applies Dmg Up (Giant) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Giant’ Attribute is increased by 50%; and applies Dmg Up (Large) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Large’ Attribute is increased by 50%. Also applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 10% for every Zohar Stone on himself. Hits a 7x7 panel area within Range for 4x damage, Noah cannot be within the NP area. Then, reduce the NP Cooldown of all other allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself by ½◈ Turns, and reduce the NP Cooldown of all allied ‘Animal’ Units within a 3 panel area of himself by ½◈ Turns. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

Note: Noah’s Ark cannot be used if Great Ram, Noah’s Ark is on Cooldown and vice-versa.
