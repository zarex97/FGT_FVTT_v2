(Martha) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Martha) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A++  
Tarasque.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Martha) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Martha) Protection of the Faith — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Chance of being inflicted with debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Health is restored, the amount of Health restored is increased by 25%.

(Martha) Saint of Tarascon  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores Martha’s Health by 20% of its maximum value.  
2\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 100%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Dmg Up (Dragon) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dragon’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Martha) Miracle — Rank: D+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Martha. Restores the Health of all affected Units by 10% of their maximum values and removes all debuffs. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Martha) Vow of the Saintess — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy unit. Removes all buffs on the DU and inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Martha) Tarasque: O’ Dragon’s Shield that Shall Not Let a Blade Pass — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging/Trigger) Can only be used once per Round. Automatically used when Martha or an allied Unit within a 1 panel area of Martha receives damage from an Attack. Damage received is reduced by 50% including NP. If the Attack was an AoE Attack, damage is reduced from all allied Units within a 1 panel area of Martha. All Masters cannot be Overpowered if damaged while receiving the effects of this NP.

(Martha) Tarasque: O' Tragic Drake Who Knew Naught of Love — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (STR) and 50% of Base Attack (MAG) combined is used (e.g. BA=175). Range=4. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 5x damage, then inflicts Def Down on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 40%. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
