(Constantine) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Constantine) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Constantine) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: C++  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈+⅔◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Constantine) Theodosios Constantinos: By These Threefold Walls, I Swear — Rank: B++ (NP) \[Anti-Army (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. If used while Constantine is within his Home Base and his Home Base is larger than 21 panels, his whole Home Base is changed into the ‘Constantinos’ area. Otherwise, the 7x7 panel area around Constantine becomes the ‘Constantinos’ area. The ‘Constantinos’ area has the following effects on all allied Units within-  
1\. The ‘Constantinos’ area is treated as a Home Base for Constantine’s Faction.  
2\. All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP.  
3\. All damage taken is reduced by 250\. This value is reduced by 50 (25?) for every 2 allied Units within the ‘Constantinos’ area.  
Additionally, when this NP is activated, apply the following effects to all allied Units within the ‘Constantinos’ area-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. On Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute, apply as ‘all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%’ instead.  
2\. Applies Invuln (Constantinos) for 1◈ Turns, 1 time (apply for 1◈ Turns instead for ‘Roma’ Units). Invuln (Constantinos) stacks with normal Invuln, and is consumed first before normal Invuln.  
3\. Applies Dmg Cut for ⅓◈ Turns to ‘Roma’ Units, all damage taken is reduced by 500 including NP.  
4\. Applies Def Up for ⅓◈ Turns ~~to all ‘non-Roma’ Units~~, all damage taken is reduced by 80%; if NP, 60%.  
5\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units except himself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%. On Units with the ‘Roma’ Attribute, apply as ‘NP damage dealt is increased by 40%’ instead.  
Theodosios Constantinos initially lasts for 2◈ Turns (if the Grail War region is Rome or the Middle East, 3◈ Turns). After the initial NP period, Constantine can extend the duration of Theodosios Constantinos by 1◈ more Turns by reducing his Health by 150 (cannot be used if Constantine’s Health is less than 150), and can be repeatedly extended.  
8\. Theodosios Constantinos is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Constantine is defeated.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns after the NP ends.
