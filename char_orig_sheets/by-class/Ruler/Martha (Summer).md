(MarthaRuler) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(MarthaRuler) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(MarthaRuler) Class Skill: True Name Discernment — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When you meet an enemy Servant for the first time, record all information about it from its Notebook page to your Notebook page. If that enemy Servant has Skills or effects that hide its information, a successful Luck Check is required for this effect to activate.  
(Passive 2\) All NP damage taken is reduced by 15%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 25%. For every NP the DU has, the chance of inflicting NP DmDwn is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(MarthaRuler) Waterfront Saintess — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces Martha’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. If Martha is within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
If Martha is not within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area, Flip a Coin. If Heads, applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; if Tails, nothing happens. Note: This coin flip is not a chance of applying buff, so it is not affected by effects which modify it.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns (3◈-⅓◈ Turns if Atk Up was not applied).

(MarthaRuler) Natural Body (Ocean) — Rank: A  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%. Does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores her Health by 25% of its maximum value; and applies Debuff Immune (Ocean) for 1 time, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
If Debuff Immune (Ocean) is triggered while Martha is within a ‘Waterside’ area, then its stack (times) is not consumed. Debuff Immune (Ocean) stacks with regular Debuff Immune, and is consumed/activated first before the regular version.

(MarthaRuler) Jacob’s Limbs — Rank: B+  
(Passive) Damage dealt by Normal Attacks to Units with the ‘Demonic’, ‘Dark’, or ‘Divine’ Attribute is increased by 100%. Whenever Martha performs a successful Attack against a Unit with the aforementioned Attribute(s), the Cooldown of this Skill is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.   
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Applies N.Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, damage dealt by Normal Attacks is increased by 30%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(MarthaRuler) Tarasque: O' Tragic Drake Rage — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-Dragon\]  
Range=2. Base Attack (STR) is used. First, inflict Def Dwn for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%. Then, deals 4x damage plus 100\. If the DU has the ‘Dragon’ Attribute, the magnitude of Def Dwn is 60% instead of 30%; and this NP deals 4x damage plus 200 instead. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
