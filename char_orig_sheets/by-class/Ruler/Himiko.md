(Himiko) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Himiko) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Himiko) Body of Nature — Rank: A  
(Passive) Himiko is Immune to Curse and Poison.

(Himiko) Shamaness’ Charisma — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Himiko) Kidō — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Demonic) for 1◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Invuln to herself for ⅓◈ Turns.  
4\. NP Cooldown of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
5\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
6\. Distribute 24 Kidō Counters to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) All Units with Kidō Counters gain the following effects-  
1\. When performing an Attack which deals STR damage, Crit Chance of the Attack is increased by 25%; then remove one Kidō Counter from the Unit at the end of the Damage Step.  
2\. When performing an Attack which deals MAG damage, damage dealt is increased by 25% including NP, then remove one Kidō Counter from the Unit at the end of the Damage Step.  
3\. Chance of being Overpowered and Underpowered is reduced by 25%.  
4\. At the end of the Round the ‘Kidō’ Skill was used, lose 1 Kidō Counter. For every additional Round that passes since the ‘Kidō’ Skill was used, lose 1 additional Kidō Counter (i.e. end of 1st Round, lose 1; end of 2nd Round, lose 2; and so on). This effect is reset every time the ‘Kidō’ Skill is used.  
Note: There can only be a maximum of 24 Kidō Counters on the field at any time. Kidō Counters are treated as a buff and will be removed if a Unit with them is affected by a buff removing effect.

(Himiko) Oracle’s Brilliance — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduces Himiko’s NP Cooldown by half of its current Cooldown.  
2\. Applies Crit Up (STR) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Attacks which deal STR damage is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp (STR) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt by Attacks which deal STR damage is increased by 75%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Himiko) Seishin Katadoru Kyūenkyō: Eternal Mirror that Models the Celestial Bodies — Rank: B (NP) \[Barrier\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-

2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by (3+X)0%; if NP, the value is reduced by 20% (e.g. 60%-20%=40%). X=The number of Mirror Tokens Himiko has.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by (3+2\*X)0%.  
4\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 times, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by (4+X)0%.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Himiko) Mirror Reflector Himiko Beam  
Damage Spell. Hits a 5x5 panel area around Himiko. Deals 0.5x damage to all enemy Units within the Spell area X times, X=the number of Mirror Tokens Himiko has.  
If a DU Blocks, damage reduction is reduced from the Total Damage received. Other damage reduction effects reduce the damage taken per hit.  
If a DU Evades, it has to Evade X times. If it fails any of its Evade rolls, it cannot Evade the rest of the hits of this Spell.  
Then, Himiko has a 25% chance of gaining 1 Mirror Token.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.
