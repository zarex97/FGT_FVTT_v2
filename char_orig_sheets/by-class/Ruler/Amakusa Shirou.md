(Amakusa) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
(Passive 3\) MAG damage from a source of Christianity ignores Amakusa’s Magic Resistance.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Amakusa) Class Skill: True Name Discernment — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When you meet an enemy Servant for the first time, record all information about it from its Notebook page to your Notebook page. If that enemy Servant has Skills or effects that hide its information, a successful Luck Check is required for this effect to activate.  
(Passive 2\) All NP damage taken is reduced by 20%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 30%. For every NP the DU has, the chance of inflicting NP DmDwn is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Amakusa) Class Skill: God’s Resolution (False) — Rank: C++

(Amakusa) Revelation — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Area CritUp (Revelation) for 2◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20% for himself; and 10% for other allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as ‘Pilgrimage’.  
(Passive) When Amakusa has a ‘Revelation’ buff and he is Attacked, Amakusa has a 70% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If the effect Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is halved (35%). If Amakusa does not have a ‘Revelation’ buff, the chance of automatically Evading the Attack is 40% instead; if NP, 20%. This effect is negated while Amakusa is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Amakusa) Baptism Rite — Rank: B+  
Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals normal damage with a 50% chance of increasing the DU’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. If the DU has the ‘Dark’, ‘Demonic’, or ‘Undead’ Attributes, this Spell deals 2x damage instead, and the chance of increasing NP Cooldown is 100% instead of 50%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Amakusa uses any Active Skill other than Grail of Fiery Heavens, he has a 50% chance of gaining 1 Baptism Token.  
(Passive 2\) When Amakusa uses Baptism Rite while he has any Baptism Tokens, he can remove any number of Baptism Tokens from himself to apply the following effects to Baptism Rite-  
1\. Damage dealt is increased by 20% for every Baptism Token removed in this way.  
2\. Spell Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns for every Baptism Token removed in this way.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
