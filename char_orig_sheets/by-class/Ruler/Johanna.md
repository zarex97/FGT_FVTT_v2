(Johanna) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Johanna) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Johanna) Imaginary Saint — Rank: A  
(Passive) Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG), deal Light damage and deal 50(%?) extra damage to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up (Johanna) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Johanna) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt by Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Johanna) The Shunned Papal Procession — Rank: C  
(Passive) Enemy Units with the ‘Anti-Hero’ Attribute are unable to enter the 2 panel area around Johanna. This effect is negated if Johanna is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself without the ‘Anti-Hero’ Attribute. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (AH) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Anti-Hero’ Attribute is increased by 20% including NP.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time. Does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
4\. Applies Dmg Cut (AH) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken from Units with the ‘Anti-Hero’ Attribute is reduced by 100 including NP.  
5\. Reduce Johanna’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Johanna) Papal Discourse — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of every Turn; and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Johanna) Legendum Papal Thronus: Devote Prayers to the Radiant Throne of Glory — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. First, apply Atk Up (Evil) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Evil’ Attribute is increased by 50% including NP. Deals 4x damage and inflicts No Buff for 1◈ Turns, 1 time; damage dealt to Units with the \[Man\] Attribute is further increased by 100%. Ignores Magic Resistance on DUs with the ‘Anti-Hero’ Attribute. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
