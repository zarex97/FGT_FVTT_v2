(MetaJeanne) Throne Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5%.  
(Passive 2\) NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn she Acts and a Sloth Check does not succeed.

(MetaJeanne) Archangel’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) NP Damage dealt is increased by 10%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(MetaJeanne) Seven Sins Bestowal (Sloth) — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with Sleep is increased by 50%.  
(Passive 3\) At the start of every Turn, perform a Sloth Check for Metatron Jeanne; this Sloth Check has a X0% of succeeding (X=number of Sloth Counters on herself). If the Sloth Check succeeded, Metatron Jeanne cannot perform any Actions this Turn other than Move (MOV is halved for this Turn) & Reactions, then remove 1 Sloth Counter from herself. If the Sloth Check fails, nothing happens. This effect does not activate while in Metatron Form.  
When a Sloth Check is successful, her Master can perform a Luck Check in order to change it to a failure (changes if the Master’s Luck Check is successful). Additionally, during Turns where a Sloth Check has succeeded, her Master can spend one Command Spell to negate the effect of that Sloth Check for this Turn and the next two Sloth Check failures.  
(Passive 4\) Upon performing a successful non-Counter Attack, she has a 30% chance of removing 1 Sloth Counter from herself; does not trigger effects that reduce Skill Cooldowns when a Sloth Counter is removed in this way. Can only be successfully triggered once per Turn.

(MetaJeanne) Sloth Beam  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Can also be used on Turns where a Sloth Check succeeded. Cannot be used while in Metatron Form. Inflicts the ‘Procrastinate’ debuff on the DU until the end of the Round, its effects are as follows- the affected Unit cannot perform any Actions other than Move (MOV is halved for this Turn) & Reactions. The ‘Procrastinate ’ debuff ignores Debuff Resist effects and the chance of removing it with an effect is reduced by 50%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. Whenever a Sloth Counter is removed from herself, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
In a situation where the DU attempts to use ‘Luck Check: Prevention’ to negate the effect of Sloth Beam, if this Luck Check fails, it is inflicted with Sleep (Sloth) until the end of the Round instead. Sleep (Sloth) ignores Debuff Resist & Immune effects and is Unremovable even after taking damage from an Attack.

(Passive 1\) True Name Discernment has no effect on Metatron Jeanne.  
(Passive 2\) Evade rolls against Attacks which deal MAG damage only are reduced by 7 including NP.  
(Passive 3\) Aim has no effect against Metatron Jeanne.  
(Passive 4\) If Curse or Evil Curse would be inflicted on Jeanne, the debuff is instead inflicted on its user.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Buff Removal ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of having buffs removed from herself is reduced by 100%.  
2\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 3 times.  
3\. Applies Dodge (72) for 1◈ Turns, 3 times. Attacks which deal MAG damage only will not reduce the stacks (number of times) of Dodge (72).  
4\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
