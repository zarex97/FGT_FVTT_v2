(Iyo) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Iyo) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Iyo) Natural Limbs — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Iyo is Immune to Poison, Burn, and Shock.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with Slow, Disable, and Immobilize is reduced by 50%.

(Iyo) Kuna’s Mantra — Rank: C  
At the start of the game, roll a six-sided die. Iyo starts the game with that number of Magatama Tokens (max. 6).  
(Passive 1\) Iyo has the ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute.  
(Passive 2\) When a Normal Attack Crit is performed, restore 1 Agility.  
(Passive 3\) When a Normal Attack which only uses Base Attack (MAG) is performed, gain 1 Magatama Token.  
(Passive 4\) When performing a Normal Attack which uses Base Attack (STR), Iyo can remove one Magatama Token from herself to deal 60% extra damage using Base Attack (MAG). This damage is rolled separately and is affected by Magic Resistance.  
The effects of this Skill are negated if Iyo is affected by Skill Seal or Silence.

(Iyo) Kidō (Destruction) — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Divine) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
4\. Then, hits a 3x3 panel area within Range, inflicts Curse on all affected enemy Units.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Iyo) Dark Oracle — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Apply Crit Up (MAG) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Attacks which deal MAG Damage is increased by 50%.  
3\. Apply Crit DmUp (MAG) for ⅓◈ Turns, MAG Crit Damage is increased by 100%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Iyo) Shrine Maiden of the End — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all affected Units with the ‘Wild Beast’ Attribute, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Iyo) Yoiyami Kageru Tsuki-no-Mitama: Divine Pearl of Dusk-Shadowed Moon — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Can only be used if Iyo has at least 1 Magatama Token. First, remove all Magatama Tokens from herself and apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 10% for every Magatama Token removed from herself.  
Hits a 7x7 panel area within Range for 3x damage and inflicts Curse. Then, inflict Evil Curse for 1◈ Turns, all Curse Damage taken is doubled. Does not hit herself.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
