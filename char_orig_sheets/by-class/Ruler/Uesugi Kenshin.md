(Kenshin) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Kenshin) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
Mount: Houshoutsukige  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Kenshin) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Kenshin) Treasure is in the Heart — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Chance of having buffs removed from herself is reduced by 10%.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted with Instakill and Death is reduced by 10%.

(Kenshin) Fate is in the Heavens, Armor is on the Chest, Accomplishment is in the Feet — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Dodge (Kenshin) for 1◈ Turns, 2 times. Attacks from Ranged weapons will not consume a usage/stack of Dodge (Kenshin), and at the end of a Combat Process after being Attacked by a Ranged weapon, increase the duration of Dodge (Kenshin) by ⅓◈ Turns. Note: Excludes MAG damage/damage that is affected by Magic Resistance and AoE Attacks.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kenshin) Shiranui・White Flame — Rank: A  
(Passive) Only one ‘Shiranui’ Skill can be used per Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP. 20%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kenshin) Bishamonten’s Pagoda — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damaging effects of NP are increased by 30%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kenshin) Shiranui・Homura  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Applies the ‘Enchant Fire’ buff to herself for ⅓◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. Normal Attacks use Kenshin’s Base Attack (STR) and 60% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 125+105=230), the effect of Magic Resistance is halved.  
2\. Normal Attacks deal Fire damage (half).  
3\. Normal Attacks have a 20% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Kenshin) Shiranui・Biten Artillery  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Deals MAG damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Kenshin) Biten Hassou・Shiranui — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Range+2 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Restore 3 Luck and apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt is increased by 35%; if NP, 25%. Remove all buffs from the DU and deal 4x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 150% if the DU has the \[Man\] Attribute. Then, Kenshin enters Bishamonten Mode for 3◈ Turns. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(Kenshin) Wargod of Echigo  
(Passive) Gains the following effects while in Bishamonten Mode-  
1\. Range is increased by 1\.  
2\. Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Divine damage.  
3\. Biten Artillery now hits a 3x3 panel area within Range and damage dealt is increased by 30%. Deals Divine damage.  
4\. While Kenshin has the Enchant Fire buff, Normal Attacks deal Fire damage and have a 60% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns.  
5\. When Bishamonten Mode naturally expires, Kenshin can instead reduce her Health by 100 to extend its duration by 1◈ Turns. Cannot be used if her Health would drop below 100 in this way.  
6\. If Biten Hassou・Shiranui is used while Kenshin is in Bishamonten Mode, the duration of Bishamonten Mode is reset to 3◈ Turns.
