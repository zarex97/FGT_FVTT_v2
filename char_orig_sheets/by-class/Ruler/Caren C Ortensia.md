(Caren) Item Construction: God’s Cartridge  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Instead of Moving/Attacking during this Turn, Caren can begin crafting a \[God’s Cartridge\], when this Skill is used, Caren gains 1 \[God’s Cartridge\] at the start of her next Turn. When Caren gains the \[God’s Cartridge\] through this effect, she can perform a successful Luck Check to gain 1 more.  
While Caren is crafting a \[God’s Cartridge\], she cannot respond to enemy Attacks. If Caren uses this Skill, she does not count towards the number of Units who Move/Attack during your Turn.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. As long as Item Construction: God Cartridge’s Cooldown is 2◈ Turns or higher, the Passive effects of Item Construction are negated.  
*Just one shot of the God Bazooka costs an exorbitant sum of money.*

Note: If the optional Active effect of Item Construction is being used, that effect becomes Active 1 of Item Construction while ‘Item Construction: God’s Cartridge’ is treated as Active 2\. Active 1 cannot be used if Active 2 is on Cooldown and vice-versa.
