Note: At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Water damage.

(Summer Skaði) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Summer Skaði) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Summer Skaði) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Summer Skaði) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 100 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 20%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Summer Skaði) Primordial Rune (Midsummer) — Rank: \-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 4 Agility. If the Unit is a ‘Summer’ Servant (has the ‘Swimsuit’ Skill), also apply the ‘Fasta’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Max & current Agility is increased by 4\. When the ‘Fasta’ buff is removed, Max Agility is reduced by 4 but current Agility is not reduced unless it exceeds the Unit’s Max Agility.  
2\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. If the Unit is a ‘Summer’ Servant (has the ‘Swimsuit’ Skill), increase both magnitudes by 10% when applied.  
3\. Apply Crit DmUp (Sumarr) for 1◈ Turns, STR Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100% while MAG Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Summer Skaði) Midsummer Ice Cream — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Restore 1 Agility.  
3\. Apply Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to herself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5%.  
4\. Has a 30% chance of applying the ‘Brainfreeze’ debuff until the end of this Turn, an affected Unit cannot Attack, use Skills, Spells, NP, MOV is reduced by 2, and Agility Check rolls are increased by 2\. Ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects.  
5\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all Units who were inflicted with Brainfreeze, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Summer Skaði) Dreaming of Late Summer Nights — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce NP Cooldown by 3◈ Turns.  
2\. Apply N.Atk Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Normal Attacks is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  

(Summer Skaði) S’Winter Magic  
Damage Spell. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals normal damage and inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%. Ice damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Summer Skaði) Gate of Aegir: Gate towards The Bountiful Ocean — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Summer\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, apply NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns if Skaði is within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%. Hits all enemy Units within a 7x7 panel area around herself for 4x damage, damage dealt to Units with the “Lawful’ Alignment is further increased by 150%. Then, increase the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by 1◈ Turns. Water damage. The NP area becomes a ‘Waterside’ area for 3◈ Turns and Skaði enters Aegir Mode. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(Summer Skaði) Aegir’s Protection  
(Passive) Skaði gains the following effects while in Aegir Mode-  
1\. MOV is increased by 1\. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2\.  
2\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 use Base Attack (STR) and 50% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 50+125=175). Not affected by Magic Resistance. Deals Ice damage.  
3\. Skaði is Immune to Water damage and all debuffs from Attacks which deal Water damage.  
4\. While Skaði is in Aegir Mode, her Master loses 15 Health at the end of her Turn and at the end of any Turn she Acts; if her Master’s Health is less than 15, Aegir Mode is forcefully ended. Skaði can choose to end Aegir Mode during her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts (after her Master pays the Health cost), or at the end of a Round.

(Summer Skaði) Clione Gleam  
Damage Spell. Can only be used in Aegir Mode. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for normal damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.
