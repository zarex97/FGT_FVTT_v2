(SambaQuetz) LUCHA LIBRE  
(Trigger) When Samba Quetz is summoned, roll a four-sided die.  
If a 1 is rolled, Samba Quetz is summoned.  
If a 2 is rolled, Técnico Samba Quetz is summoned. (STR A-, BA (STR) 140; END B++)  
If a 3 is rolled, Rudos Samba Quetz is summoned. (STR A+, BA (STR) 160; END B)  
If a 4 is rolled, your choice of normal, Técnico, or Rudos Samba Quetz is summoned.  
If a Técnico Samba Quetz has been summoned, a Rudos Samba Quetz can still be summoned in the same game, but not by the same Faction and vice-versa (while a normal Samba Quetz can no longer be summoned). If a normal Samba Quetz has been summoned, then neither a ‘Técnico’ or ‘Rudos’ Samba Quetz can be summoned after that.  
(Passive) Use the effects with a colour to the corresponding version of Samba Quetz.  
(Active) Can only be used by normal Samba Quetz. Choose to apply either the ‘Técnico’ or ‘Rudos’ effect for 1◈ Turns, Samba Quetz is treated as that version of herself for the duration of the effect; this effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(SambaQuetz) Holy Lucha Night  
(Passive) All damage dealt during a Night Round as dictated by the game state is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.

(SambaQuetz) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SambaQuetz) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 100 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(SambaQuetz) Sambista — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up to all Units within a 2 panel area of Samba Quetz for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20% (15%/25%); if NP, 10% (5%/15%). Then, applies Area CritUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10% (15%/5%). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(SambaQuetz) Goddess’ Gift — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Samba Quetz. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%; and then applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%. Then, the Cooldown of this Skill is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns for every other allied Unit with the ‘Divine’ or ‘Santa’ Attribute within a 2 panel area of herself. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(SambaQuetz) Christmas Killing Method — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up to Samba Quetz for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40% (30%/50%); if NP, 30% (20%/40%); and applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60% (80%/40%). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(SambaQuetz) Yucatán Regalo de Navidad: Delivering Love to Children on the Holy Night — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=3. First, if this NP is used by normal Samba Quetz, she can choose one of the following effects to activate, depending on which effect she currently has on herself-  
1\. If she has the Técnico effect, extend its duration by 1◈ Turns and apply Regen for 1◈ Turns, at the end of her Turn, at the end of any Turn she Acts, and at the end of the Round, her Health is restored by 5% of its maximum value.  
2\. If she has the Técnico effect, she removes it from herself and gains the Rudos effect for 1◈ Turns, then inflict Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns to all other Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%.  
3\. If she has the Rudos effect, extend its duration by 1◈ Turns and apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 25%.  
4\. If she has the Rudos effect, she removes it from herself and gains the Técnico effect for 1◈ Turns, then apply Guard Counter for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
Then, applies Crit Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 100%, and deals 4x damage. Next, applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Samba Quetz, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
