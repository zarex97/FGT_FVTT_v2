(Holmes) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 6d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Holmes) Natural Insight — Rank: A+++  
(Passive) When Holmes is Attacked, he has a 25% chance of automatically Evading the Attack including NP. Otherwise, the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (A) for 1◈ Turns on all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of around the Unit targeted by Effect 1, all damage taken is increased by 20%, and Luck is reduced by 1 whenever this Unit is successfully Attacked.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Holmes) Deductive Reasoning — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Crit Chance is increased by 15%.

(Holmes) The One Who Reveals the Truth — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
2\. Applies NP Regen (Deductive) for 1◈ Turns, whenever this Unit performs a successful Crit, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Holmes) Baritsu — Rank: B++  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Phase. Applies Evade to Holmes for that Turn, and applies Atk Up (STR) for that Turn, all STR damage dealt is increased by 50% including NP. Cooldown: 3◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Holmes) Elementary, My Dear: It's Elementary, My Friend — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit/Anti-World\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects all Units within a 7x7 panel area of Holmes. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Def Dwn on all enemy Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies Pierce to all allied Units for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Ignore Def to all allied Units for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Crit DmUp to all allied Units for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Holmes) Empty House: The Adventure at the Empty House — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. When this NP is used, Holmes can disguise himself as any other character (figurine), including Masters and Servants. However, it is only the appearance. All of Holmes’ Parameters and Stats remain the same. Skills and Noble Phantasms cannot be used while Empty House is Active. Can be deactivated during your Turn, at the end of a Turn this Unit Acts, or at the end of the Round; and is automatically deactivated when Holmes takes damage equal to 10% of his Max Health or more in one Attack.  Cooldown: 6◈  
Note: As this NP effect would only be effective if Fog of War is implemented, if Fog of War is not implemented, it would have the same effect as Presence Concealment — Rank: C. (Cooldown: 3◈ Turns after Presence Concealment is deactivated)  
(Presence Concealment — Rank: C)  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 40% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with NP Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.
