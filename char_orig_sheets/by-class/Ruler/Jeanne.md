(Jeanne) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
(Passive 3\) MAG damage from a source of Christianity (i.e. from a Unit with the ‘Christian’ Attribute) ignores Jeanne’s Magic Resistance.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Jeanne) Class Skill: True Name Discernment — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When you meet an enemy Servant for the first time, record all information about it from its Notebook page to your Notebook page. If that enemy Servant has Skills or effects that hide its information, a successful Luck Check is required for this effect to activate.  
(Passive 2\) All NP damage taken is reduced by 20%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 30%. For every NP the DU has, the chance of inflicting NP DmDwn is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. 

(Jeanne) Class Skill: God’s Resolution — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts Stun for 1◈ Turns. Ignores Stun Resist, Stun Immune, and any other effects that specifically reduce the chance of being inflicted with Stun. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jeanne) Restoration of the Brilliant Holy Light — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 30%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jeanne) Revelation — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Jeanne has a ‘Revelation’ buff and she is Attacked, Jeanne has a 70% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If the effect Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is halved (35%). If Jeanne does not have a ‘Revelation’ buff, the chance of automatically Evading the Attack is 40% instead; if NP, 20%. This effect is negated while Jeanne is inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Jeanne) At the End of the Pure Prayers — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Area CritUp (Revelation) for 2◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20% for herself; and 10% for other allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. 

(Jeanne) Charisma — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 15 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Jeanne) Saint — Rank: B  
(Passive) Jeanne restores 100 Health, 1 Agility, and 1 Luck at the end of her Turn. If her Health is full, her NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns instead (in addition to natural reduction).

(Jeanne) Luminosité Eternelle: God is Here With Me — Rank: A (NP) \[Barrier\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn, or at the start of a Combat Phase when Jeanne or an allied Unit within a 2 panel area or herself is Attacked. When used, applies the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Jeanne-  
1\. Removes all debuffs.  
2\. Applies Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 20% of its maximum value at the end of its Turn; at the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Def Up (Luminosité) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 25% including NP. A Unit with Def Up (Luminosité) cannot be Overpowered.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
