(BunnyKing) Swimsuit Swordmaster of Las Vegas  
(Passive) Arturia automatically switches between two Forms in Day and Night Rounds. At the start of a Day Round, she switches to her Swimsuit Form. At the start of a Night Round, she switches to her Bunny Form. The differences between both Forms are as follows-  
Swimsuit Form-  
1\. At a Range of 1 to 2, Arturia’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and half of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. BA=150), effect of Magic Resistance is halved. At a Range of 3 or higher, Arturia’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. When Arturia Blocks, roll 2 additional ten-sided dice and add that value to the Block roll.  
Bunny Form-  
1\. At a Range of 1 to 2, Arturia’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and 25% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. BA=125), not affected by Magic Resistance. At a Range of 3 or higher, Arturia’s Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. Crit Chance is increased by 10%; Agility Check rolls are reduced by 1\.  
Note: If Day-Night Cycle is not in effect, this Skill becomes an Active Skill which changes her current Form to the other Form when used during her Turn, Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
Note 2: If Arturia enters an area which is not affected by Day or Night (e.g. Cavern, Indoors, etc), she remains in whatever Form she was in until she leaves that area.  
Note 3: Swapping between Forms due to Day-Night cycle is dictated by the game state’s time of day.  
(Active) Used when the form switching Passive effect of this Skill is about to activate. When used, Arturia remains in her current form without switching for the rest of this Round. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Passive) ~~Only Active in Swimsuit Form.~~ All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(BunnyKing) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(BunnyKing) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(BunnyKing) Royal Card — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all Units within a 2 panel area around Arturia. Has the following effects-  
1\. Remove all buffs and debuffs from every affected Unit; then each buff and debuff is respectively applied to a random affected Unit, the remaining durations of the buffs and debuffs are unchanged. This Skill cannot shuffle Unremovable buffs/debuffs/effects. This Skill ignores Buff Removal ResUp when removing effects; and effects that prevent buffs and debuffs from being applied/inflicted to Units when reassigning effects.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Arturia, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen (Royal) for 1 time to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Arturia, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns upon performing a successful Attack.    
4\. Select one of the allied Units affected by this Skill, that Unit is inflicted with the Royal Seal debuff for ⅓◈ Turns, the affected Unit cannot Attack or use any Attack Skills, Damage Spells, and NP that deals damage. Royal Seal ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.
