(SummerVinci) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.

(SummerVinci) Riding — Rank: EX  
Mecha Dinosaurs.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(SummerVinci) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(SummerVinci) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SummerVinci) Dream Machine — Rank: A  
(Passive) When da Vinci performs an Attack while she has a Dream Machine (DM) buff on herself, roll a four-sided die at the start of the Combat Process, the effect of the corresponding buff is activated for herself for that Combat Process. Note: If da Vinci uses her NP while she has the DM(R) buff, its effects will always be activated, do not roll a four-sided die.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the following buffs to herself for 1◈ Turns-  
1\. DM(R): All damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20% if a 1 is rolled.  
2\. DM(G): Restores 1 Agility, and damage dealt by Normal Attacks is increased by 20% if a 2 is rolled.  
3\. DM(B): Restores 1 Luck, and damage dealt by Normal Attacks is increased by 20% if a 3 is rolled.  
If a 4 is rolled, you can choose the buff effect to be activated. Note: All DM buffs are categorized as ‘Atk Up’ buffs.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns  
Note 2: The corresponding effect can only be activated if da Vinci has the correct buff applied to herself. Example: If da Vinci has the DM(R) and DM(B) buffs but does not have the DM(G) buff, and she rolls a 2 at the start of the Combat Phase, there is no effect as da Vinci does not have the correct buff on herself.

(SummerVinci) Dinosaur Summer Goodbye: Unforgettable Summer Vacation, Cretaceous Dream — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

Whenever da Vinci uses the ‘Dream Machine’ Skill while she has more than 1 Dinosaur Token, remove 1 Dinosaur Token from herself and the duration of DM buffs applied become 2◈ Turns instead of 1◈ Turns.  
(Active) Range+2 for the Combat Process. Base Attack (STR) and 50% of Base Attack (MAG) is used (e.g. BA=175), the effect of Magic Resistance is halved. First, apply NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns to herself, NP Damage dealt is increased by (20+X)%, X increases by 5 for every Dinosaur Token da Vinci has. Deals 4x damage. Then, roll a four-sided die and apply the corresponding effect to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%.  
3\. Reduces NP Cooldown by ⅕ of each NP’s maximum Cooldown.  
4\. Your choice.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
