Then, inflicts Skill Seal for ⅓◈ Turns and applies the ‘Malignant’ effect for 1◈ Turns, their Alignment is changed from ‘Good’ or ‘Neutral’ to ‘Evil’; if the Unit had no Alignment it is instead granted the ‘Evil’ Alignment. If James is inflicted by an effect that removes buffs, remove half of his Mathematical Tokens, rounded up.

1\. Alignment is changed to Chaotic Evil.  
2\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 use Base Attack (STR). Normal Attacks at a Range of 2 or higher use Base Attack (MAG).  
3\. The maximum number of Mathematical Tokens is increased to 12\.  
4\. The area of Mathematical Malignant Annihilate is increased to a 9x9 (7x7?) panel area.  
These changes are Unremovable.
