(QSH) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(QSH) All Books Must Be Burned — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to QSH. Has the following effects-  
1\. Increase their NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%.  
3\. Inflicts MAG Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Base Attack (MAG) is reduced by 30\.  
If an affected Unit has the ‘Scholar’ Attribute, the magnitudes of effects applied by this Skill are doubled.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(QSH) All Scholars Must Be Buried — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to QSH. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 70% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. For every Rank in STR the DU has, the chance of being inflicted with Stun is increased by 5%.

3\. Inflicts Debuff ResDwn (Buried) for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs from Attacks which deal Earth damage is increased by 50%.  
4\. Apply Atk Up to QSH for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
