(Karl) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Karl) Class Skill: True Name Discernment — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) When you meet an enemy Servant for the first time, record all information about it from its Notebook page to your Notebook page. If that enemy Servant has Skills or effects that hide its information, a successful Luck Check is required for this effect to activate.  
(Passive 2\) All NP damage taken is reduced by 15%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts NP DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is reduced by 20%. For every NP the DU has, the chance of inflicting NP DmDwn is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Karl) Class Skill: God’s Resolution — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Has an 80% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns. Ignores Stun Resist, Stun Immune, and any other effects that specifically reduce the chance of being inflicted with Stun. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Karl) Charisma — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 20 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Karl) Ceremonial Reformation — Rank: A  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units other than himself as long as he is on the board. Has the following effects-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 10% including NP.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 10% including NP.  
3\. Max Health is increased by 100\.  
4\. Max Agility is increased by 1\.  
5\. Max Luck is increased by 1\.  
6\. At the end of every Turn an allied Unit Acts, its Health is restored by 5% of its maximum value.  
The magnitudes of Effects 1, 2, and 6 are doubled when the affected Unit is in its Home Base.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Karl) Joyeuse: Peerless Sword — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

(Karl) Carolus Patricius — Rank: A (NP) \[Fortress\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, place the ‘Carolus Patricius’ token on the board, with the middle panel being the panel Karl was standing on. The token has an area of 9x9 panels. All Units who were within a 9x9 panel area of Karl are moved onto the ‘Patricius’ token. Has the following effects-  
1\. The Carolus functions as a second Home Base for Karl’s faction.  
2\. Karl’s Master’s ZON is ignored when he Attacks (no damage reduction) while within the Patricius.  
3\. In order to Escape the Carolus, enemy Servants have to Succeed a Luck Check against Karl (can be performed anywhere within the Carolus). If Successful, the Unit is Moved to the nearest panel immediately outside the Carolus.  
4\. Enemy Units can attempt to enter the Carolus. Either the Unit’s STR or AGI Parameter has to be Rank A, and the nearest panel of the Carolus must be within the Unit’s MOV minus 2 (when attempting to enter the Patricius, the Unit’s MOV is reduced by 2\).  
5\. All damage received by allied Units within the Patricius is reduced by 15% including NP.  
6\. Units within the Carolus cannot be targeted by Attacks from outside the Carolus and vice versa. However, the Patricius can be Attacked with an AoE Noble Phantasm. In this case, all Servants within the Carolus receive damage from the NP, but Total Damage received is reduced by 50%; while Masters are undamaged and unaffected by other effects.  
Note: If the NP used on the Carolus was an \[Anti-Fortress\] or higher Noble Phantasm, Carolus Patricius is forcefully ended at the end of the Combat Process.  
7\. All damage dealt by allied Units within the Carolus is increased by 15% except Noble Phantasms.  
8\. Units can Move under the Carolus (i.e. onto panels occupied by the Patricius, place the Carolus token on top of those Units).  
9\. Carolus Patricius initially lasts for 2◈ Turns. After the initial NP period, Karl can extend the duration of Carolus Patricius by 1◈ more Turns by reducing his Health by 200 (cannot be used if Karl’s Health is less than 200), and can be repeatedly extended.  
10\. The Carolus is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Karl or his Master is defeated.  
Cannot be used if Carolus Patricius Auctoritas is on Cooldown.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after the NP ends.

(Karl) Carolus Patricius: Karl Cannons  
Attack Skill. Range=5. Only usable when Carolus Patricius is Active. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 2x damage. Range is calculated from the edge of Carolus, along with the area directly under the Carolus. *Categorized as Noble Phantasm.* Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Karl) Carolus Patricius Auctoritas: Sanctus, Now the Authority is Replenishing the Earth — Rank: A++ (NP) \[Anti-Army/Anti-Fortress/Fortress\]  
Can be used in 2 different methods, as follows-  
1\. Range=7. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. First deals 2x damage plus 100 using Base Attack (STR); then deals 2x damage plus 100 using Base Attack (MAG). Both hits of this NP can be separately Blocked and/or Evaded. Cannot be used if Carolus Patricius is Active. Cooldown: 7◈+⅔◈ Turns.   
2\. Used during your Turn. When used in this way, Karl summons the Carolus Patricius Auctoritas on the panel he was standing on, and he moves onto the top of it. Can be activated while Carolus Patricius is Active, if used in this way, all Units on the Carolus Patricius excluding his Master are scattered below the Carolus, while his Master is placed together with Karl. The Carolus Patricius Auctoritas has the following Stats-

Carolus Patricius Auctoritas (CPA)  
Health: 3000  
Agility: 8  
Luck: Shared with Karl’s  
MOV: 3  
Range: 4 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR/MAG): 200  
Attributes: Giant, Mechanical  
At a Range of 1 to 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
CPA can Move to any panel, when it Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for CPA to stand on.  
CPA’s Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 to 2 knocks back the DU(s) by one panel.  
Karl and his Master cannot be Attacked while they are on the CPA.  
At the end of every Turn this NP is Active, Karl’s Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn if CPA Acted; and 10 Health if it did not Act (instead of the normal Master Health loss that occurs when an NP is used). If the Master’s Health is equal to or less than 30 or 10, the NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn, the Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
While Usage 2 of this NP is Active, Karl can use Usage 1 of this NP, if he does the Range is calculated from the edge of CPA, and uses CPA’s Base Attack. Then, Health loss incurred by his Master due to NP usage is increased by 50% plus 30; and this NP is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅔◈ Turns after it ends.  
Note: Usage 1 cannot be used if Usage 2 is on Cooldown and vice versa.

Note: Carolus Patricius cannot be used if Carolus Patricius Auctoritas is on Cooldown and vice versa.
