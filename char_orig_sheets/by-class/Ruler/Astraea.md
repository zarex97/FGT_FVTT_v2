(Astraea) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Astraea) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Astraea) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Astraea) Protection of the Libra — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Astraea. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every 2 Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Astraea) Mana Burst (Star) — Rank: A+  
(Active) This Skill is used when Astraea performs a Normal Attack at a Range of ~~3~~ 2 or lower. First, restore 3 Luck. Then, for that Combat Process, the Base Attack used is Astraea’s BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (e.g. BA=410). Additionally, Crit Chance for that Attack is increased by 30%.  
Not affected by Magic Resistance. ~~at a Range of 1 to 2; while at a Range of 3, effect of Magic Resistance is halved.~~  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Astraea) Judgement of the Stars — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. First, inflict Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns on all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance is reduced by 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by X%, X being 20 plus the (number of allied Units inflicted with Crit Dwn\*5); maximum value of X \= 50%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by X%, X being 20 plus the (number of allied Units inflicted with Crit Dwn\*5); maximum value of X \= 50%.  
4\. Reduces Astraea’s NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Astraea) Custos Morum: The Hour of Judgement Is Here. State Thy Name — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Evil\]  
Range=4. This NP has the Pierce effect. First deals 2.5x damage using Base Attack (MAG); then deals 2.5x damage using Base Attack (STR); if the DU has the ‘Evil’ Alignment, both steps of damage deal an additional 150 damage. Then, apply S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Astraea for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Astraea) Iam Redit et Virgo: Now Justice Returns — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Evil\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 3 panel area of Astraea with the ‘Evil’ Alignment. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 400% chance of inflicting Stun for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Has a 400% chance of inflicting the ‘Iam’ debuff for 1◈ Turns, when this debuff naturally expires, the affected Unit is inflicted with NP Seal for 2◈ Turns.  
3\. Has a 400% chance of inflicting the ‘Redit’ debuff for 1◈ Turns, when this debuff naturally expires, the affected Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal for 2◈ Turns.  
4\. Has a 400% chance of inflicting the ‘etVirgo’ debuff for 1◈ Turns, when the debuff naturally expires, the affected Unit has a 50% chance of being inflicted with Atk Dwn (etVirgo) for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%. Atk Dwn (etVirgo) ignores effects which modify the chance of being inflicted with debuffs other than Debuff Immune.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

Note: Custos Morum cannot be used on the same Turn as Iam Redit et Virgo and vice-versa.
