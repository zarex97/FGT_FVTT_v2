(Solomon) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Solomon uses an Ars Goetia Spell, roll an eight-sided die at the end of the Damage Step and gain the corresponding Unremovable effect-  
1\. Lemegeton: Anti-Saber  
2\. Lemegeton: Anti-Lancer  
3\. Lemegeton: Anti-Archer  
4\. Lemegeton: Anti-Rider  
5\. Lemegeton: Anti-Caster  
6\. Lemegeton: Anti-Assassin  
7\. Lemegeton: Anti-Berserker  
8\. Your choice  
For every ‘Lemegeton’ effect on Solomon, all damage dealt to Servants of the corresponding Class is increased by 10 including NP, and all damage taken from those Servants are reduced by 10 including NP (e.g. Solomon has Anti-Saber & Anti-Rider, so all damage dealt to Sabers & Riders are increased by 20, all damage taken from them reduced by 20\).  
If the number rolled corresponds to a ‘Lemegeton’ effect already on Solomon, do not roll again, instead reduce the Cooldown of the Ars Goetia Spell just used by ⅓◈ Turns.   
Note: If Solomon already has all seven Lemegeton effects, the eight-sided die is no longer rolled.
