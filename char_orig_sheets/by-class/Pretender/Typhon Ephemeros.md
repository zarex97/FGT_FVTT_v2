(Ephemeros) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, Ephemeros chooses a standard Servant Class. Apply the ‘Pretender (\>X)’ effect to herself for 2◈ Turns, where X is the chosen Servant Class; its effects are as follows- All damage dealt to the selected Servant Class is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. While Ephemeros has any ‘Pretender’ effect, the Passive effects of this Skill are negated.  
The ‘Pretender’effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable unless Ephemeros is inflicted with Skill Seal, then the ‘Pretender’ effect is removed from her.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Ephemeros already has a ‘Pretender’ effect.  
(Passive 2\) While the ‘Pretender’ buff is Active, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks deal Lightning damage with a 25% chance of inflicting Shock for 1◈ Turns. Additionally, Etna Eruzione cannot be used and the effect of the ‘Etna’ buff is negated.
