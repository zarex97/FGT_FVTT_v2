## (Phantasmoon) Holy Night

(Passive) Only in effect as Santasmoon. All damage dealt during a Night Round as dictated by the game state is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.

## (Phantasmoon) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role

(Passive 1\) Has different effects depending on Phantasmoon’s current form, as follows-  
1\. Phantasmoon: All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
2\. Space Phantasmoon V: All damage dealt to Cavalry-class Servants is increased by 25% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 25%; applies to NP as well.  
3\. Santasmoon: All damage dealt to Knight-class Servants is increased by 25% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 25%; applies to NP as well. Loses the ‘Levitating’ and ‘Spaceflight’ Attributes but gains the ‘Santa’ Attribute.  
(Passive 2\) When Phantasmoon is outside her Home Base, she becomes Santasmoon. When Phantasmoon is inside her Home Base (or an ‘Indoors’ area?), she becomes Space Phantasmoon V. These changes are applied when she finishes her Move.  
(Active) By spending one Command Spell, her Master can command Phantasmoon to change to any of her forms for 3 full Rounds, Phantasmoon cannot change form due to her Passive effects while the form change caused by the Command Spell is Active. This effect cannot be used again while a form change caused by the Command Spell is Active. 

## (Phantasmoon) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B

(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

## (Phantasmoon) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A

This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

## (Phantasmoon) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A

(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

## (Phantasmoon) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: \-

1\. Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. Range is increased by 2\.  
3\. MOV is reduced by 2\.  
Note: Phantasmoon is treated as NOT having the ‘Territory Creation’ Skill.

## (Phantasmoon) White Moon Princess

(Passive) Only in effect as Phantasmoon. Has the following effects-  
1\. At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2, Normal Attacks can use either Base Attack (STR) or (MAG). At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. When entering Combat against a Unit with the ‘Evil’ Alignment, the duration of Phantasmoon is extended by 1◈ Turns.

## (Phantasmoon) Incarnation of the Holy Night — EX

(Passive) Only in effect as Santasmoon. Has the following effects-  
1\. At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
2\. Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5%.  
3\. During a Night Round or when on Darkness Terrain, all damage dealt is increased by 20% including NP.  
4\. During a Night Round or when on Darkness Terrain, at the end of her Turn and at the end of the Round, restore 1 Luck and 1 Agility.
