(Faker) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role

All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, Faker chooses a standard Servant Class. Apply the ‘Pretender (\>X)’ effect to herself for 2◈ Turns, where X is the chosen Servant Class; its effects are as follows- All damage dealt to the selected Servant Class is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. While Faker has any ‘Pretender’ effect, the Passive effects of this Skill are negated.
