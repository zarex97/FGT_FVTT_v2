(Lady) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, Lady chooses a standard Servant Class. Apply the ‘Pretender (\>X)’ effect to herself for 2◈ Turns, where X is the chosen Servant Class; its effects are as follows- All damage dealt to the selected Servant Class is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. While Lady has any ‘Pretender’ effect, the Passive effects of this Skill are negated.  
The ‘Pretender’effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable unless Lady is inflicted with Skill Seal, then the ‘Pretender’ effect is removed from her.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Lady already has a ‘Pretender’ effect.  
(Passive 2\) While Lady Avalon has the ‘Pretender’ buff, Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 use Base Attack (STR) and half of Base Attack (MAG) combined (i.e. 50+110=160), the effect of Magic Resistance is halved. At a Range of 2 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).

(Lady) Swimsuit\!  
(Passive) All Total Water Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP. Only in effect when Lady Avalon does not have the ‘Pretender’ buff.

(Lady) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: D  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is further increased by 5d6 including NP.  
2\. While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+5) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Lady) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A+  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 55%; this effect is halved for Instakill (30%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 55%; this effect is halved for Instakill (30%), and further halved for Death (15%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Lady) Hybrid — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Lady Avalon’s NP Cooldown is reduced, whether through natural reduction (i.e. at the end of the Turn) or an effect, it is reduced by 1 extra Turn.

(Lady) High Speed Divine Words (Bewitching) — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Lady Avalon uses a Skill or performs an Attack which only uses Base Attack (MAG), she has a 30% chance of inflicting Charm (Bewitched) for ⅓◈ Turns on all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of herself. If Charm (Bewitched) is inflicted on a Unit already affected by it, its duration is extended. Not affected by effects which modify the chance of inflicting debuffs.

(Lady) Class Skill: Independent Manifestation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive 4\) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs, Instakill, Death, and Erase is reduced by 100%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Independent Action — Rank: A’.

(Lady) Light Unto Thy Hands — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 4 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 20% of its original value.  
4\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lady) Flowers Blooming on a Summer Night — Rank: D  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel are of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; has no effect on NP.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ½◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Lady) Succubus’ Ridge — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Affects all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Lady Avalon. At the end of the Unit’s Turn, its NP Cooldown has a 50% chance of not naturally reducing by 1 Turn (or X Turns, if that Unit has an effect that increases natural NP Cooldown reduction). If this occurs, Proto Merlin’s NP Cooldown is instead reduced by 1 Turn (or X Turns)  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself.  
2\. Hits all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself. Inflicts NP Degen for 1◈ Turns, natural NP Cooldown is stopped and is increased by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Lady) Winds of Avalon: The Utopia which Beckons from a Distance — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 3 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Max HpUp for 1◈ Turns, Max Health is increased by 30% of its original value (40% if Lady Avalon is affected by Overcharge).  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn (2 Turns if Lady Avalon is affected by Overcharge).  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns to herself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 15%.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
