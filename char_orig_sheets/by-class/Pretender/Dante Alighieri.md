(Dante) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Apply the ‘Pretender (Alighieri)’ effect to himself for 2◈ Turns plus ⅓◈ more Turns for every Unit on the field with a ‘Sin’ effect; but if there are seven Units on the field with a ‘Sin’ effect, the effect is applied for 5◈ Turns. (Note: Dante does not apply for Units with the ‘Sin’ effect.)  
Its effects are as follows- All damage dealt to Standard Servant Classes with a ‘Sin’ effect is increased by 70 and all damage taken from them is reduced by 70; applies to NP as well. While Dante has any ‘Pretender’ effect, the Passive effects of this Skill are negated. (Example: If a Saber-class Servant on the field has a ‘Sin’ effect, then all damage dealt to/taken from all Saber-class Servants is increased/reduced by 70.)  
The ‘Pretender’effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable unless Dante is inflicted with Skill Seal, then the ‘Pretender’ effect is removed from him.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Dante already has a ‘Pretender’ effect.

(Dante) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B+  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈+⅓◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Dante) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: D++  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (5d6+10) including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+9) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Dante) Wandering the Three Realms — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Dante is not affected by effects which reduce his MOV.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Agility and 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies MOV Up for 1◈ Turns, MOV is increased by 2\.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
4\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Dante is defeated he is revived with 100 Health.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Dante) Stars of a Hundred Cantos — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Then, choose either to apply the ‘Heaven’ Terrain Effect or the ‘Hell’ Terrain Effect to the 9x9 panel area around himself for 2◈ Turns. Next, apply the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself depending on whether Dante is standing within ‘Heaven’ or ‘Hell’ terrain-  
1\. If Heaven,

1. Restore 2 Luck.  
2. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
3. Apply Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
4. Apply Regen for 1◈ Turns, Health is restored by 100 at the end of every Turn, at the end of every Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.

2\. If Hell,

1. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
2. Apply NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3. Apply NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.

If the panel Dante is standing on is both Heaven and Hell terrain when this Skill is used, apply all the above effects. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: ‘Heaven’ Terrain is also treated as ‘Airspace’ Terrain; and ‘Hell’ Terrain is also treated as ‘Underworld’ Terrain without the ‘Darkness’ effect.

(Dante) Brands of the Seven Sins — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
2\. Gain 7 Sin Brands for 1◈ Turns. For each Sin Brand on Dante, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 10%.  
3\. Applies the ‘Spada’ buff for 1◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- Whenever Dante performs a successful Attack, remove one buff from the DU at the start of the Damage Step. If a buff was successfully removed by this effect, remove 1 Sin Brand from Dante & apply Atk Up (Sin) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%; for every buff removed by this effect (e.g. if NP hits 3 Units and successfully removes 1 buff from each DU, remove 3 Sin Brands and apply Atk Up (Sin) three times).  
4\. Applies the ‘Pecatti’ buff for 1◈ Turns, its effects are as follows- Whenever Dante is defeated and revived by an effect, remove 1 Sin Brand from Dante & apply Atk Up (Sin) for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Whenever Dante successfully removes 7 Sin Brands from a single usage of this Skill without any Sin Brands naturally expiring, he gains the Unremovable ‘Purgatorio’ effect, its effects are as follows-  
1\. Base Attack (STR) is increased by 70\.  
2\. Successful Normal Attack Crits remove 1 Sin Brand from himself at the start of the Damage Step & apply Atk Up (Sin) for 1◈ Turns if a Sin Brand was removed.  
3\. The effects of Stars of a Hundred Cantos are now applied to all allied Units within the Heaven/Hell area.  
(Passive 2\) Dante is treated as having a ‘Sin’ effect as long as he has any Sin Brands.

(Dante) Pecatti Spada: Crestsword of Seven Sins — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive 1\) Normal Attacks at a Range of 1 use Base Attack (STR). Whenever Dante performs a successful Normal Attack at a Range of 1, he has a 70% chance of applying a ‘Sin’ effect on the DU at the end of the Damage Step; if the DU has the ‘Saint’ Attribute, this chance is 20%; while if the DU has the ‘Dark’ Attribute, this chance is 100%. Roll an eight-sided die to determine which ‘Sin’ effect is applied. Their effects are as follows-  
1\. Sin: Pride. Has the ‘Decoy’ effect towards all other Units with a ‘Sin’ effect (except Dante). All damage dealt to Units with a ‘Sin’ effect is increased by 70% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 70% including NP. If there are any other Units on the field with a ‘Sin’ effect, the Unit with ‘Sin: Pride’ cannot target Units without a ‘Sin’ effect for Attacks.

2\. Sin: Wrath. This Unit will only Move towards and Attack the nearest enemy Unit. All damage dealt is increased by 70% including NP, Crit Damage dealt is reduced by 70%, all damage taken is reduced by 70 including NP. This Unit **has** to Move and Attack on its Turn if able (if there are multiple Units on the board capable of Attacking in the same Turn and the player performs any Attacks, the Unit with ‘Sin:Wrath’ must be one of the Attackers). 

3\. Sin: Envy. All damage dealt to Units with at least two Parameters higher than themselves is increased by 70% including NP. All damage dealt to Units with at least two Parameters lower than themselves is reduced by 70% including NP. Additionally, when Sin: Envy is applied, choose one enemy Unit, all damage dealt to that Unit is increased by 70 and all damage taken from that Unit is reduced by 70 including NP.

4\. Sin: Greed. All damage dealt to Units with more Health than this Unit is increased by 70% including NP. All damage dealt to Units with less Health than this Unit is reduced by 70% including NP. For every X% of Health this Unit is missing, all damage dealt is increased by X and all damage taken is reduced by X including NP.

5\. Sin: Sloth. At the start of every Turn, perform a Sloth Check for this Unit; this Sloth Check has a 70% of succeeding. If the Sloth Check succeeded, this Unit cannot perform any Actions this Turn other than Move (MOV is halved for this Turn) & Reactions. If the Sloth Check fails, for the rest of this Turn, all damage dealt is increased by 70% and all damage taken is reduced by 70% including NP, Agility Checks are reduced by 7, and MOV is doubled.

6\. Sin: Gluttony. If there are no enemy Units within a 4 panel area of this Unit, MOV is reduced by 2\. If there are any enemy Units within a 4 panel area of this Unit, MOV is increased by 2\. After performing a successful non-NP Attack, at the end of the Damage Step, Health is restored by 70% of the damage dealt and transfer one of the DU’s buffs to this Unit, two buffs if the Attack was a Crit. (Note: Does not affect Unremovable buffs.)

7\. Sin: Lust. Has the following effects-

1. Chance of being inflicted with Charm is increased by 700%.  
2. At the end of every Turn, has a 70% chance of inflicting Charm (Lust) for 1◈ Turns to all Units within a 3 panel area of themselves of the opposite gender (does not stack, but duration is refreshed if re-applied).   
3. At the start of their Turn, a Unit with Charm (Lust) performs a Lust Check with a 70% chance of succeeding, if succeeded, the Unit with the ‘Sin: Lust’ effect gains control of that Unit for this Turn, and the controlled Unit will not count towards the number of Units who Move/Attack this Turn. If the Lust Check failed, the Unit who failed the Lust Check can only Move towards and Attack the Unit with the ‘Sin: Lust’ effect; in this case, their Range becomes 1 and can only perform Attacks using Base Attack (STR).  
4. When a Unit affected by Charm (Lust) Attacks the Unit with the ‘Sin: Lust’ effect, it restores their Health by the amount of damage dealt and restores 1 Agility. Additionally, instead of taking damage, the Unit with the ‘Sin: Lust’ effect restores Health equal to the amount of damage which would have been dealt and also restores 1 Luck.  
5. Units who already have a different ‘Sin’ effect are Immune to Charm (Lust).

8\. Your choice.  
‘Sin’ effects are neither a buff or a debuff and are Unremovable except through the following methods- Staying in their Home Base for a full Round without engaging in Combat, by their Master spending a Command Spell, or by Dante, who during his Turn, can choose to remove any number of ‘Sin’ effects from any Unit on the field, but cannot remove a specific ‘Sin’ effect on the same Turn it is applied.  
The same ‘Sin’ effect cannot be applied to more than one Unit.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Dante successfully applies a ‘Sin’ effect on another Unit, the durations of the following effects on himself are extended by 1◈ Turns if he has them- Sin Brands, Spada, Pecatti.

(Passive) At a Range of 2, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Active) Base Attack (MAG) is used. First, has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20% plus 5% for every Unit on the field inflicted with a ‘Sin’ effect (Note: Dante himself does not count).  
2\. If Dante is within Heaven terrain, applies Atk Up (\[Sky\]) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Sky\] Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.  
3\. If Dante is within Hell terrain, applies Atk Up (\[Earth)\] for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the \[Earth\] Attribute is increased by 50% including NP.    
Hits a 7x5 or 5x7 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to himself. Deals 5x damage, inflicts Curse, and increase the NP Cooldown of affected Units by 1◈ Turns. Additionally, deal an extra 70% damage using Base Attack (MAG) to Units with a ‘Sin’ effect, Fire damage. (Note: This still counts as NP Damage. However, it is calculated separately from the actual NP damage.) Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.
