(Oberon) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, Oberon chooses a standard Servant Class. Apply the ‘Pretender (\>X)’ effect to himself for 2◈ Turns, where X is the chosen Servant Class; its effects are as follows- All damage dealt to the selected Servant Class is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. While Oberon has any ‘Pretender’ effect, the Passive 1 effects of this Skill are negated, and his Alignment is changed to Lawful Good.  
The ‘Pretender’effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable unless Oberon is inflicted with Skill Seal, then the ‘Pretender’ effect is removed from him.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Oberon already has a ‘Pretender’ effect.  
(Passive 2\) When Oberon is summoned, he is first summoned with the Pretender (Oberon) effect on him, its effects are as follows- All damage taken from standard Servant Classes is reduced by 25% and all damage dealt to them is increased by 25%, applies to NP as well. The Pretender (Oberon) buff has an infinite duration. Pretender (Oberon) is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable unless Oberon is inflicted with Skill Seal, then the ‘Pretender’ effect is removed from him.  
(Trigger) If the Active effect of this Skill is used while Oberon has the Pretender (Oberon) buff, the Active effect is instead changed to ‘Remove the Pretender (Oberon) buff from himself’.

(Oberon) Anti-Human Order — Rank: D  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 10% including Instakill, Death, and Erase. Does not affect Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill.  
2\. Only affects Units with the name ‘Merlin’: Chance of applying buffs is reduced by 20%.  
3\. Oberon cannot be applied with buffs from Unit with the name ‘Merlin’.

(Oberon) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: E-  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by (5d4-5) including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10-2) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Oberon) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: A  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%; this effect is halved for Instakill (25%), and further halved for Death (10%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Oberon) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The Move Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Oberon) A Midsummer Night’s Dream — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Oberon is Immune to Curse.  
(Passive 2\) Oberon is Immune to Mental Debuffs.  
(Passive 3\) Oberon is Immune to Sleep debuffs except Eternal Sleep.

(Oberon) Glam Sight: Fairy Eyes — Rank: \-

(Passive 2\) The value of Luck Checks rolls against Oberon are increased by 1d6.

(Oberon) Curtain of the Night — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 30%; and reduce their NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Oberon) Morning Lark — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Reduce the Unit’s NP Cooldown by 5◈ Turns.  
2\. Apply the ‘Lark’ buff for 1◈ Turns- When this buff naturally expires, increase the Unit’s NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns. The ‘Lark’ effect cannot increase a Unit’s NP Cooldown past their maximum values.  
3\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the targeted Unit, Crit Chance is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Oberon) End of the Dream — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%.  
2\. Applies NP DmUp Boost for 1◈ Turns, the magnitude of all effects that increase NP Damage dealt is doubled.  
3\. Applies the Unremovable ‘Oberon’ buff for 1◈ Turns. When this buff naturally expires, it has the following effects-  
a. Has a 1000% chance of removing the affected Unit’s buffs.  
b. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Eternal Sleep for 3◈ Turns, an affected Unit is unable to Act. Ignores Debuff Resist, Debuff Immunity, is Unremovable even through receiving Attack damage, and the affected Unit is Immune to Sacrifice. Eternal Sleep is categorized as a ‘Sleep’ debuff.  
c. Has a 500% chance of applying Decoy for 3◈ Turns.  
d. Has a 500% chance of inflicting the ‘Dormant (Eternal)’ effect for 3◈ Turns- The affected Unit cannot be Moved from its current position on the board through any method whatsoever except knockback.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Oberon) Wry Rhyme Goodfellow: Tales of Dreams Told Over Yonder — Rank: E (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Can only be used while Oberon has any ‘Pretender’ buff. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Oberon. Deals 2x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment is increased by 150%. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Removes all Offensive Buffs.  
2\. Inflicts Sleep for ⅓◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns (demerit).  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Vortigern) Lie Like Vortigern: Eye of Dreams that Fall to the Distance — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]  
Can only be used while Oberon does not have any ‘Pretender’ buff. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range+6 for the Combat Process. Hits a 9x9 panel area within Range for 5x damage, damage dealt to Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment is increased by 150%; Vortigern cannot be within the NP area. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Has a (35-X)% chance of inflicting Erase, X is increased by 5 for every Rank in AGI the DU has.  
2\. Removes all Offensive Buffs.  
3\. Applies Sleep for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns (demerit).  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

Note: Wry Rhyme Goodfellow cannot be used if Lie Like Vortigern is on Cooldown and vice versa.
