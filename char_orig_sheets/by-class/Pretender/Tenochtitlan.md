## (Tenochtitlan) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role

(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Apply one of the following effects of your choice to Tenochtitlan for 2◈ Turns-  
1\. Pretender (Tlaloc): All damage dealt to Cavalry-class Servants is increased by 25% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 25%; applies to NP as well.  
2\. Pretender (Huitzilopochtli): All damage dealt to Knight-class Servants is increased by 25% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 25%; applies to NP as well.  
While Tenochtitlan has any ‘Pretender’ effect, the Passive effects of this Skill are negated.  
The ‘Pretender’effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable unless Tenochtitlan is inflicted with Skill Seal, then the ‘Pretender’ effect is removed from her.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Tenochtitlan already has a ‘Pretender’ effect.

(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 6d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.

## (Tenochtitlan) Waterside’s Lifework — Rank: A+

(Passive 1\) As long as Tenochtitlan is on the field, her Home Base is treated as a ‘Waterside’ area (but does not have the effects of Waterside terrain).

## (Tenochtitlan) City-State Alliance — Rank: A

(Passive) Affects all other allied Units within a 3 panel area of herself. They gain the following effects-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 10; if NP, 20\. These values are doubled if the AU is within or directly next to a ‘Waterside’ area.  
2\. When a successful Normal Attack is performed, the AU has a 30% chance of restoring 1 Luck at the end of the Damage Step.
