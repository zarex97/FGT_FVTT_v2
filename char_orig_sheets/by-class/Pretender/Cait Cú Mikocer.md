## (Mikocer) Pretender: The One who Wears a Role

(Passive 1\) Has different effects depending on Baobhan’s current form, as follows-  
1\. Priestess (Mikocer): All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Alter-Ego’ Skill is increased by 50% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 50%; applies to NP as well. All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% and all damage taken from them is increased by 50%; applies to NP as well.  
2\. Rain Witch (Baobhan): All damage dealt to Cavalry-class Servants is increased by 25% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 25%; applies to NP as well.  
3\. Fairy Knight (Tristan): All damage dealt to Knight-class Servants is increased by 25% and all damage taken from them is reduced by 25%; applies to NP as well.  
(Passive 2\) When Baobhan is outside her Home Base, she becomes Fairy Knight (Tristan), gaining the ‘Round Table’ and ‘Levitating’ Attributes. When she is inside her Home Base (or an ‘Indoors’ area?), she becomes Rain Witch (Baobhan). These changes are applied when she finishes her Move.  
(Active) By spending one Command Spell, her Master can command Mikocer to change to any of her forms for 3 full Rounds, Mikocer cannot change form due to her Passive effects while the form change caused by the Command Spell is Active. This effect cannot be used again while a form change caused by the Command Spell is Active. 

(Mikocer) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) All MAG damage taken is negated including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Mikocer) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 6 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Mikocer) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d6 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+5) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).
