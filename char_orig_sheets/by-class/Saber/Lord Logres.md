## (Logres) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A

(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

## (Logres) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A

(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

## (Logres) Knighthood Conferment — Rank: A

(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit directly next to herself. Applies the ‘Knighthood’ effect on that Unit (Unremovable), gaining the following effects-  
1\. Lord Logres & her Master are treated as having a Master-Servant Relationship with allied ‘Knighthood’ Units in Combat situations (see **Rules – Relationships** in the Rulebook). (Servant=Knighthood, Master=Logres/her Master).  
2\. A Unit with the ‘Knighthood’ effect can perform the ‘Cover’ action for Lord Logres and her Master, Total Damage taken while performing this ‘Cover’ action is only increased by 50% instead of 100%. (Refer to ‘**Rules \- Relationships**’ for information on the ‘Cover’ action.)  
Then, Lord Logres gains 1 Logres Token.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever another allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself is revived through Guts and any other revival effect, that revived Unit gains the following effects-  
1\. The amount of Health restored from the revival effect is increased by 100%. If the revived Unit had ‘Knighthood’, also restore its Agility and Luck by 25% of their maximum values   
2\. Apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. If the revived Unit had ‘Knighthood’, apply it for 2◈ Turns instead.  
3\. If the affected Unit had ‘Knighthood’, remove ‘Knighthood’ from that Unit after applying Effect 1 & 2\.  
4\. Lord Logres gains 1 Logres Token.

## (Logres) The Faces of King Arthur

(Passive) At the start of a Day Round, Logres transforms into Dracon Mode. At the start of a Night Round, Logres transforms into Miroir Mode. Then, Logres gains the corresponding effects as follows-  
1\. Dracon Mode: At the start of a Round where she is in Dracon Mode, gain the Unremovable ‘Dracon’ effect- Range is increased by 1, Normal Attacks at a Range of 2 or higher are Ranged, use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Fire damage with an 80% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns; this ‘Dracon’ effect is removed from Logres when she exits Dracon Mode or performs a non-Mana Burst Normal Attack at a Range of 2 or higher.  
2\. Miroir Mode: Range is increased by 2\. At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and deal Light damage (half). At a Range of 2, Normal Attacks are Ranged, use Base Attack (MAG) and deal Light damage. At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks are Ranged, use 60% of Base Attack (MAG) and deal Light damage.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Round where Logres is about to change to Dracon/Miroir Mode due to time of day. Logres maintains her current Mode instead. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. If playing without Day/Night cycle, the effect becomes ‘Used during your Turn. Swaps from Dracon Mode to Miroir Mode and vice-versa’. Gain 1 Logres Token when this Active effect is used.

1\. Deals Light damage (half).  
2\. Base Attack (MAG) is used, hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself {M}. Light damage. Can only activate once per Round.

8\. Your choice.  
If an unusable number is rolled, roll again (or apply Effect 1 instead?). All non-NP Attacks which deal Light damage deal 50% extra Light damage to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute.
