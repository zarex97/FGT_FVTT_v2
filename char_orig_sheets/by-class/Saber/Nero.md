(Nero) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Nero) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Nero) Imperial Privilege (Applause) — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores her Health by 30% of its maximum value.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 45%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 45%; if NP, 25%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Applause’ buff for 1◈ Turns, successful Normal Attacks deal 3 extra damage for every point of Luck on Nero, and restore 1 Luck at the end of the Damage Step.  
5\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nero) Migraine — Rank: B  
(Passive) Reduces the chance of all buffs/debuffs/effects being applied/inflicted on Nero by 20% excluding Nero’s own. Note: Roll separately for each effect applied.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies nvDebuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by non-volatile debuffs is reduced by 100%, and restores Nero’s Health by 15% of its maximum value. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nero) Thrice, Though I Welcome the Setting Sun — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Guts (Invictus) to Nero for 2◈ Turns, if Nero is defeated she is revived with 5% of her Max Health. Can only be used 3 times throughout the entire game. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Nero) Invictus Spiritus — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Nero is revived through the effects of Guts (Invictus), apply the following effects to herself-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp (Invictus) for 2◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
2\. Remove all debuffs from herself.  
3\. Applies Buff ChUp (Invictus) for 2◈ Turns, chance of applying buffs is increased by 40%.  
4\. Applies NP Regen (Invictus) for 2◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
Note: ‘Invictus’ buffs do not stack, if they are applied to her while she still has them, their durations are reset instead. 

(Nero) Aestus Domus Aurea: Golden Theater of the Flamboyant — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Team\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects a 7x7 panel area around Nero. All enemy Units within the NP area upon activation are trapped and are unable to leave until either Nero is defeated or the NP duration ends. If an enemy Unit tries to enter Aestus Domus Aurea, roll an eight-sided die. The enemy Unit can only enter if an 8 is rolled. Enemy Units outside Aestus Domus Aurea are unable to Attack Units inside it and vice versa. Nero cannot leave the NP area. This NP has the following effects-  
1\. All enemy Units within Aestus Domus Aurea are inflicted with permanent Curse. This Curse debuff is removed once the NP ends.  
2\. Nero’s Normal Attacks have the Ignore Def effect and have a 60% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Within the NP area, all allied Units deal 20% extra damage, while all damage dealt by enemy Units is reduced by 20%. Applies to NP as well.  
4\. Lasts for 2◈ Turns. However, after the initial NP period, Nero can extend the NP duration by 1◈ more Turns by reducing her Health by 100 (cannot be used if Nero’s Health is less than 100).  
5\. The NP is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Nero is defeated.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after the NP ends.
