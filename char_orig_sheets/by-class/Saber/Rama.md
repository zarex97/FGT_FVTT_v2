(Rama) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Rama) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Rama) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Rama) Martial Blessing — Rank: A  
(Passive) Allows the use of Vishnu Bhuja.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%; and applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage is increased by 100%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Rama) Charisma — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 20 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Rama) Curse of Separation — Rank: A  
(Passive) When Rama would be defeated and is not revived by any effect, for every Reunion Counter on himself, he has a 3% chance of having that Attack be canceled, then Sita is summoned on a panel directly next to himself (will be contracted to Rama’s Master). Can only activate once per game.

(Rama) Dream of Reunion — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Whenever Rama is defeated and revived with an effect, gain 1 Reunion Counter.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Rama is defeated, he is revived with 100 Health.  
2\. Restores Rama’s Health by 25% of its maximum value.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
5\. Gain 1 Reunion Counter.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.
