(Medusa) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Medusa) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Medusa) Divinity — Rank: D-  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 15 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Medusa) Breaker Gorgon: Self Seal \- Dark Temple — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit within Range. Cannot be used on a Unit if there is an obstacle/obstruction between Medusa and the target Unit.  
(Example: Unit \[Cannot be targeted\]-Unit \[Can be targeted\]-Medusa).  
Has a 100% chance of inflicting Petrify on the DU; this chance is reduced by 10% for every MAG Rank the DU has (e.g. MAG Rank C=30%), and is reduced by 50% if the DU and Medusa are not facing each other. If Petrify was not inflicted, the DU is instead inflicted with Stun for 1◈ Turns. Petrify from this Skill is not affected by debuff resistance from Magic Resistance. Then, apply the following effects to Medusa-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp (STR) for 1◈ Turns, STR Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies ~~Curse Atk (STR)~~ the ‘Breaker’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Normal Attacks which deal STR damage inflicts Curse at the end of the Damage Step.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as a ‘Mystic Eye’ Skill. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Medusa) Demonic Blood Vessels — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Medusa) Factor Predation (War Goddess) — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 3 times.  
2\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 3 times.  
3\. Applies Crit Guard for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of a Unit’s Attack is reduced by 30% against Medusa.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) After Medusa uses Chrysaor: The Golden Sword of Monsters, she enters Athena Mode for 3◈ Turns at the end of the Combat Process. Medusa gains the following effects while in Athena Mode-  
1\. All damage taken is reduced by 60%; if NP, 40%.  
2\. When Medusa’s Normal Attacks are Evaded, the Evade roll of the DU is doubled.  
3\. Chance of inflicting Petrify is increased by 20%.  
4\. Chrysaor: Echidna cannot be used.  
After the 3◈ Turns have passed, Medusa can extend the duration of Athena Mode by 1◈ more Turns by reducing her Health by 100 and her Master’s Health by 30 (cannot be extended if either of their Health is equal or below the required value) and can be repeatedly extended in this way. If Medusa uses her NP again while in Athena Mode, its duration is refreshed to its initial values.
