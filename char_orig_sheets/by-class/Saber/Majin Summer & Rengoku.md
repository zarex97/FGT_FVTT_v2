(SummerMajin) Walking Talking Sword: Rengoku  
(Passive) The materialization of Okita Alter’s sword. Has the following effects-  
1\. Whenever Okita performs a Normal Attack, she can perform an additional Attack at the end of the Combat Process using Rengoku. Rengoku has a Base Attack (STR) of 50\. Attacks by Rengoku cannot be Countered. Units who are damaged by Rengoku’s Attacks do not perform an Injury Roll.  
2\. When Okita+Rengoku receives a buff that affects Females only, the buff is only applied to Okita; while when they receive a buff that affects ‘Gender Unknown’ or “Genderless’ only, the buff is only applied to Rengoku. Aside from that, all other buffs that are applied on Okita affect both of them unless stated.  
3\. For every 3 successful Attacks performed by Rengoku, Okita gains 1 Rengoku Token.

(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, first Okita’s Master’s Health is reduced by 30 (cannot be used if her Master’s Health is 30 or less). Then, Okita enters Rengoku Mode for 3◈ Turns. While in Rengoku Mode, she gains the following effects-  
1\. ‘Walking Talking Sword: Rengoku’ cannot be used.  
2\. Base Attack (STR) is increased by 50\.  
3\. Agility Check Rolls are reduced by 3\. MOV is increased by 1\.  
4\. All damage taken is reduced by 20%; if NP, 30%.  
5\. Range is increased by 1\. At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks are Ranged & use Base Attack (MAG).  
6\. All buffs that would affect Rengoku only will now affect Okita.  
If Okita takes damage equal to more than half of her current Health while in Rengoku Mode, it is forcibly ended at the end of the Combat Phase. When Rengoku Mode ends, Okita’s Master’s Health is reduced by 30 (cannot drop below 1 in this way). Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after Rengoku Mode ends.
