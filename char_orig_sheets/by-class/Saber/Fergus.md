(Fergus) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Fergus) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Fergus) Bravery — Rank: A  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, STR damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Fergus) Nature of a Rebellious Spirit — Rank: B  
(Passive) Negates the effect of Charisma — Rank: B on Units in Combat with Fergus. If Charisma — Rank: B is used within 2 panels of Fergus, its effects are negated. Fergus is not affected by Charisma — Rank: B. Also affects Atk Up (Charisma) buffs from Charisma — Rank: B.

(Fergus) Brave Warrior’s Pride — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%; the magnitude(s) of this buff are increased by 10% when Attacking a Unit with the ‘Charisma’ Skill.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%; the magnitude of this buff is increased by 20% when Attacking a Unit with the ‘Charisma’ Skill.  
3\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Fergus) Eye of the Mind (True) — Rank: A  
(Passive) Evade rolls are reduced by 3, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Def Up for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Fergus) Caladbolg: The Rainbow Sword — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
{S} Base Attack (STR) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area around Fergus for 4x damage plus 100\. Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%; and inflicts Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is increased by 50%. If (any variation of) Cú Chulainn is hit by Caladbolg, he is inflicted with Death.   
Note: If this NP is used when on a ‘Flying Fortress’ NP, it also deals damage to said NP, and Total Damage dealt to it is increased by 100%. (Examples of ‘Flying Fortress’ NP: Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Carolus Patricius)  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
