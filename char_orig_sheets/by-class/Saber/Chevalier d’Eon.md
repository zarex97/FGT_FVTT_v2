(d’Eon) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(d’Eon) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(d’Eon) Self-Suggestion — Rank: A  
(Passive) The source of d’Eon’s Strength. Chance of being inflicted with non-volatile debuffs is reduced by 50%.

(d’Eon) Let the White Lily Shine — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Removes all debuffs from d’Eon.  
2\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
3\. Restore 2 Luck to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of d’Eon and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to those Units, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
4\. Apply Buff Removal ResUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of d’Eon, chance of buffs being removed from this Unit is reduced by 90%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(d’Eon) Eye of the Mind (True) — Rank: C  
(Passive) Evade rolls are reduced by 2, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge to d’Eon for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received by 15%; if NP, 5%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(d’Eon) Looks of Loveliness — Rank: C  
(Passive) Gender-specific effects ignore the gender specification when used on d’Eon (i.e. takes effect regardless of targeted gender); includes both positive and negative effects.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Restores d’Eon’s Health by 20% of its maximum value, then applies Decoy to d’Eon for 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(d’Eon) Fleur de Lys: Sword Dance of Falling Lilies — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3x damage, then has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 1◈+½◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈+½◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
3\. Has a 30% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. For each of the above debuffs that are successfully inflicted, this NP has a 10% chance of inflicting Instakill (e.g. all three debuffs inflicted, Instakill chance=30%).  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(d’Eon) Fleur de Lys: Gorgeous Splendor of Blooming Lilies — Rank: C+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of d’Eon. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn for 2◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%.  
3\. Has a 60% chance of inflicting Charm for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 5◈+⅓◈ Turns.

Note: Sword Dance of Falling Lilies cannot be used if Gorgeous Splendor of Blooming Lilies is on Cooldown and vice versa.

(d’Eon) Fleur de Lys: Profusion of Hundred Flowers, Dance of Lilies — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) In order to use this NP, d’Eon’s Health has to be at least 80% of its maximum value; then d’Eon’s Health is reduced by half of its maximum value when it is used.. When this NP is used, it affects all enemy Units within a 4 panel area of d’Eon. This NP has a 300% chance of inflicting Charm on those Units for 3◈ Turns, then applies the ‘Lys’ effect on on those Units-  
1\. All damage dealt is reduced by 30% including Noble Phantasm.  
2\. All damage received is increased by 30%.  
3\. Chance of being inflicted with Charm is increased by 30%.  
After that, apply the ‘Profusion’ effect to d’Eon. Whenever an enemy Unit enters a 2 panel area of d’Eon, it has a 60% chance of being inflicted with Charm for 1◈ Turns, and has a 75% chance of being inflicted with the ‘Lys’ effect.  
This NP can only be used once during the entire game. Both the ‘Profusion’ and ‘Lys’ effects are neither buffs or debuffs and are Unremovable.

Note: Profusion of Hundred Flowers, Dance of Lilies cannot be used if either Sword Dance of Falling Lilies or Gorgeous Splendor of Blooming Lilies is on Cooldown
