(Asstolfo) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.  
If Astolfo is inflicted with NP Seal, his Magic Resistance is reduced to Rank D.  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.

(Asstolfo) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Asstolfo) Destruction Declaration — Rank: C\~B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, it has a 40% chance of activating its ‘Strong’ effect; otherwise its ‘Normal’ effect is activated instead.  
Normal: Applies NP Regen for 3 times, every 2 Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of the Turn; then removes all debuffs from Astolfo. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Strong: Has the following effects-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 3 times, every 2 Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of the Turn.  
3\. Removes all debuffs from Astolfo.  
4\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Asstolfo) Magic Flute That Calls Panic — Rank: C

1\. Inflicts Terror (Flute) for 1◈ Turns on all affected enemy Units, chance of being inflicted with Stun (Flute) from Terror (Flute) is 35%.  
2\. Applies Dodge to Astolfo for 1◈+½◈ Turns; 3 times.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Asstolfo) Majestic Triumphal Return — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the following effects to Astolfo-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.

(Asstolfo) Vulcano Caligorante: Fortuitous Netting — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. First, target an enemy Unit. The effects of this NP depend on whether the DU is affected by a ‘Flute’ debuff  
1\. If the DU is not affected by Terror (Flute) or Stun (Flute)  
First restore Astolfo’s Agility by 2 and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%. Then, deals 3.5x damage and inflicts NP Seal for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. If the DU is affected by Terror (Flute)-  
First restore Astolfo’s Agility by 3 and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Then, deals 3.5x damage and inflicts NP Seal for ⅔◈ Turns.  
3\. If the DU is affected by Stun (Flute)-  
First restore Astolfo’s Agility by 4 and apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%. Then, deals 3.5x damage and inflicts NP Seal for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Asstolfo) Crazy Trip Drive Idol: Idol Rampage Lacking Good Sense — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, roll an eight-sided die. This NP summons 3+X clones of Astolfo within a 4 panel area of himself. The clones of Astolfo are indistinguishable from the real Astolfo. While this NP is Active, Astolfo cannot use Skills and Vulcano Caligorante.  
The clones have the same Stats as Astolfo, but do not Evade or perform Luck Checks. While this NP is Active, all Total Damage dealt by any of the Astolfos is reduced by 50%. When an Astolfo is Attacked, it has a (100**÷**X)% chance that it is the real Astolfo, where X=number of Astolfo copies on the board. If the Attacked Astolfo was not the real Astolfo, it immediately disappears and reappears on a random panel somewhere else (max distance=6 panels). If the Attacked Astolfo was the real Astolfo, the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
If the real Astolfo takes damage from an Attack while this NP is Active, this NP ends at the end of that Combat Phase and all clones disappear; if he did not take damage from that Combat Phase, he will disappear and reappear on a random panel somewhere else (max distance=6 panels) at the end of that Combat Phase.  
While this NP is Active, Astolfo’s Master’s Servant Range is increased by 2\.  
2◈ Turns after this NP is activated, at the end of each Round while this NP is still Active, Astolfo’s Master loses 5 Health for each additional Astolfo on the board.  
Every 4◈ Turns after this NP is activated, if this NP is still Active, double the number of Astolfos on the board at the end of the Turn.  
If Astolfo’s Master’s Health is equal to or less than the amount needed to be deducted due to this NP, this NP is forcefully deactivated, leaving only the Astolfo nearest to its Master on the board; Astolfo’s Master does not suffer Health loss if this happens.  
Astolfo clones do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn (Moving all Astolfos count as Moving only one Unit). The same Astolfo can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Astolfo’s Master if any Astolfos are directly next to him/her.  
Can be deactivated at the end of any Turn, leaving only the Astolfo nearest to its Master on the board.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns after this NP is deactivated.
