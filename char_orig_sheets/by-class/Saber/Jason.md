(Jason) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Jason) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Jason) In The Face of Adversity  
(Passive) This Skill is only in effect when Jason’s Health is less than or equal to 20% of its maximum value, otherwise it has no effect. The effects of this Skill are as follows-  
1\. Base Attack (STR) is increased by 50  
2\. Evade rolls are reduced by 2\. Does not affect non-Evade Agility Check rolls.  
3\. All damage taken is reduced by 25% including NP.

(Jason) The Requested Golden Fleece — Rank: \-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Jason. Restores the Unit’s Health by 20% of its maximum value, then removes one random debuff. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Jason) Flashing at the Tiger’s Den — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Jason. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 100%.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn on himself for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jason) Conquering the Distant Sea Routes with Friends — Rank B++  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Jason. First, applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%. Then, applies the following effects to all affected Units with the ‘Argo-related’ Attribute except himself-  
1\. Reduces NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Jason) Astrapste Argo: The Dazzling Ship that Splits the Heavens — Rank B++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Can be used in 2 different ways-  
1\. Used during your Turn. Jason randomly summons a Servant with the ‘Argo-related’ Attribute on a panel next to him, the Servant can immediately Act after being summoned. The summoned Servant disappears after 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, and cannot use its Noble Phantasm (passive Noble Phantasms do not count). Cooldown: 3◈+⅔◈ Turns.   
The Servant summoned in this way does not count towards the number of Units who Move and/or Attack during your Turn; and can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Jason+his Master and the summoned Argo-related Servant are treated as having a ‘Servant-Master relationship’ in Combat situations (see **Rules – Relationships** in the Rulebook). (Servant=Argo-related Servant, Master=Jason+his Master).  
2\. {S} Range=3. Base Attack (STR) is used. First restores 3 Luck to Jason and applies Atk Up to Jason for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%. Then, hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage plus 200\.  
After that, Jason performs 3 Luck Checks; Jason receives 50 damage for each failure (do not perform an Injury Roll for Jason if damage is received in this way; and Luck is only reduced by 1 for each Failure (minimum loss=1)); this damage is not affected by damaging-modifying effects on himself.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.
