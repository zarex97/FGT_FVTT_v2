(Eli Brave) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Eli Brave) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: C  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is further increased by 5d8 including NP.  
2\. While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+10) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Eli Brave) Class Skill: Double Class — Rank: E  
(Passive) Literally does nothing.

(Eli Brave) Heroine’s Great Principles — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns; and applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns at the end of each Turn. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Eli Brave) Mana Burst (Courage) — Rank: D   
(Active)  This Skill is used when Eli performs a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, Base Attack used is Eli’s Base Attack (STR) and X0% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined, where X=the number rolled on a six-sided die. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Then, applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns if X is 1 to 3; 4◈ Turns if X is 4 to 6\.

(Eli Brave) Legend of the Crimson Heroine the Movie — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, increase Eli’s NP Cooldown by 3◈ Turns (2◈ Turns if used while standing on ‘Halloween’ Terrain). Then, roll a six-sided die. The effect corresponding to the number rolled is applied to Eli-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%.  
2\. Applies Invuln to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Eli for ⅔◈ Turns.  
3\. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Eli, restore their Health by 25% of their maximum value.  
4\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
5\. Applies Atk Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Eli for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
6\. Applies Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Eli for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.  
If Eli is standing on ‘Halloween’ Terrain, roll two dice instead (reroll duplicate numbers).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Active 1\) Used during your Turn. Roll a six-sided die, then summons a Green Slime on a panel directly next to herself. If a 1 or 6 is rolled, this Skill has an additional effect-  
6: Summons two Green Slimes instead of one.  
1: The Green Slime is summoned as an enemy Unit (controlled by the GM), when it is summoned, it immediately performs a Normal Attack on Elizabeth that has a 100% chance of inflicting Slow for 1◈ Turns (instead of the normal 20%); Elizabeth’s Evade roll is increased by 3 if she attempts to Evade this Attack.  
The same Green Slime can only Move and Attack once per Turn.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns

(Active 2\) Can only be used if there is a ‘Green Slime’ directly next to herself. Range=3. Used during your Turn or as a Counter. {R} Elizabeth throws a Green Slime directly next to herself at a Unit within Range, dealing 50% Water damage (not affected by damage-modifying effects on herself EXCEPT effects which modify STR damage dealt) and inflicting Slow for 1◈ Turns; the Green Slime then dies (cannot be revived). Counts as Elizabeth’s Attack for the Turn.
