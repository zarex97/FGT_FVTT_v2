(Jacques) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Jacques) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 3 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Jacques) The Last Templar  
(Passive) While Jacques has a ‘Knightly’ or ‘Pilgrim’ buff, Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and 25% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 175+50=225); effect of Magic Resistance is reduced by 75%. While Jacques has both a ‘Knightly’ and ‘Pilgrim’ buff, Normal Attack Range is increased by 1 and uses Base Attack (STR) & 50% of Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 175+100=275); effect of Magic Resistance is reduced by 50%. This Skill is negated while Jacques has the ‘Pèlerinage’ buff (?)

(Jacques) Vow of the Knightly Order — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Def Up (Knightly) for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage taken is reduced by 20% (30%); if NP, 10% (15%).   
2\. Applies Atk Up (Knightly) for 1◈ Turns to to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, all damage taken is reduced by 20% (30%); if NP, 10% (15%).  
2\. Applies Regen (Knightly) for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Health is restored by 10% (15%) of its maximum value at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of any Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune (Knightly) for 1◈ Turns, 1 times to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) When an allied Unit is within a 2 panel area of Jacques, the magnitude of all ‘Knightly’ buffs on that Unit is increased to the values in this colour. As for Regen (Knightly) and Debuff Immune (Knightly), if it would be consumed when an affected Unit is within a 2 panel area of Jacques, it has a 50% chance of not being consumed.

(Jacques) Recommended Almsgiving — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. Has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts NP Degen (Alms) for 1◈ Turns to all enemy Units within the Skill area, NP Cooldown is increased by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn, and natural NP Cooldown reduction is cancelled.  
2\. Applies NP Regen (Alms) for 1◈ Turns to himself, whenever a Unit’s NP Cooldown is increased due to the effect of ‘NP Degen (Alms)’, Jacques’ NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn; this Cooldown reduction effect can activate more than once if more than one Unit had its NP Cooldown increased by ‘NP Degen (Alms)’ in the same Turn.  
3\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to other random allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. The number of Units NP Regen is applied to is equal to the number of enemy Units inflicted with NP Degen (Alms) with this Skill usage.  
4\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jacques) Guardian of the Pilgrimage — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself, applies the ‘Pilgrim’ effect to that Unit for 2◈ Turns (this Unit will be referred to as the ‘Pilgrim’). This effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Decoy (Pilgrim) for 1◈ (2◈?) Turns to Jacques, as long as Jacques is within a 2 panel area of the Pilgrim, Jacques and the Pilgrim are treated as having a ‘Servant-Master relationship’ in Combat situations (see **Rules – Relationships** in the Rulebook). (Servant=Jacques, Master=Pilgrim).  
1\. Applies Guts (Pilgrim) for 1◈ Turns to Jacques and the Pilgrim, when the Pilgrim is defeated it is revived with 100 Health, while if Jacques is defeated he is revived with 200 Health.  
2\. Applies Dmg Cut (Pilgrim) for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage taken by the Pilgrim is reduced by 100, while all damage taken by Jacques is reduced by 200\.  
3\. Applies Buff Removal ResUp (Pilgrim) for 1◈ Turns, chance of having buffs removed from the Pilgrim is reduced by 80%, while chance of having buffs removed from Jacques is reduced by 100%.  
4\. Jacques restores 3 Luck.  
5\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to Jacques, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: If used on himself, has the following effects instead-  
1\. Does not apply the ‘Pilgrim’ effect.  
2\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when this Unit is defeated it is revived with 150 Health.  
3\. Applies Dmg Cut for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage taken is reduced by 150\.  
4\. Applies Buff Removal ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of having buffs removed from this Unit is reduced by 90%.  
5\. Restores 3 Luck.  
6\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Jacques) Pèlerinage du Temple: Such a Long Pilgrimage's Journey — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=MOV, Range+1 for the Combat Process. First, applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 10% (+10% if he has a ‘Knightly’ buff, \+10% if he has a ‘Pilgrim’ buff) and applies the ‘Pèlerinage’ buff to himself for 2◈ Turns, 6 times, Jacques’ Attacks deal Fire damage (half), deal 50 extra Fire damage, and inflict Burn (Pèlerinage) for 1◈ Turns at the start of the Damage Step. Burn (Pèlerinage) is separate from normal Burn. Burn (Pèlerinage) can stack; a Unit with multiple stacks of Burn (Pèlerinage) or with both normal Burn and Burn (Pèlerinage) only has its Base Attack reduced once.  
Jacques targets an enemy Unit within the NP’s Range; Jacques Moves towards the DU and stops on a panel directly next to it, then performs a Normal Attack on the DU. Next, this NP hits a 3x3 panel area around the DU for 3x damage {S} using Jacques’ Base Attack (MAG) and has a 60% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns at the start of the Damage Step, damage dealt is increased by 20% for every Burn debuff on the DU. Fire damage. Then, has a 500% chance of inflicting the Burn (Pilgrim) debuff to himself for 2◈ Turns (demerit), this debuff cannot be prevented by Debuff ResUp or Immune effects (but can be removed through removal effects). Its effects are as follows-  
a. Has the same effects as a regular Burn debuff but does not stack with it. Burn (Pilgrim) will replace regular Burn if he had it when applied.  
b. At the start of every Turn, his Base Attack (both) has a 60% chance of not being reduced for that Turn due to the effects of Burn (Pilgrim).  
c. While Jacques has the ‘Pèlerinage’ buff while he is inflicted with Burn (Pilgrim), the effects of the ‘Pèlerinage’ buff are boosted as follows- Attacks deal 100 extra Fire damage instead of 50, and inflict Burn (Pèlerinage) for 2◈ Turns instead of 1◈ Turns.  
d. While Jacques has the ‘Pèlerinage’ buff while he is inflicted with Burn (Pilgrim), when the ‘Pèlerinage’ buff is removed from himself, the Burn (Pilgrim) debuff has a 60% chance of being removed from him as well.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.
