(Gilles) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Gilles) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Gilles) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: EX  
Cannot be used/Activated unless specific conditions are met (see ‘Prelati’s Encouragement’). When Mad Enhancement is Activated, Gilles’ Alignment changes from Lawful Good to Neutral Evil.  
While Mad Enhancement is Active, Gilles gains the following effects-  
1\. Gilles’ Master loses 30 Health at the end of every Turn he Acts; his Master’s Health cannot drop below 30 in this way.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 75%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 100% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Gilles’ Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. If this Servant’s Master is defeated while ME is active, ME cannot be deactivated and its STN is reduced by 1 additional Turn at the end of every Turn.  
6\. While ME is Active, Gilles can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Gilles’ Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, are Unremovable and cannot be negated/deactivated once it becomes Activated.

(Gilles) Military Tactics — Rank: C  
(Passive) Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Gilles. All damage received from \[Anti-Army\] Noble Phantasms is reduced by 25%; all damage dealt by \[Anti-Army\] Noble Phantasms is increased by 25%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies ‘NP DmUp (nonAA)’ for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Gilles, NP damage dealt by Noble Phantasms **other** than \[Anti-Army\] is increased by 15%. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Triumphal Return of the Holy Maiden.

(Gilles) Triumphal Return of the Holy Maiden — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Gilles, NP damage dealt is increased by 15%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units with the ‘Lawful’ Alignment within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Overcharge for 1◈ Turns, 1 time to all allied Units with the ‘Good’ Alignment within a 2 panel area of himself, the magnitude of all non-damage effects of NP are increased by 20%.  
4\. If there is an allied Unit with the \[Star\] Attribute within a 2 panel area of himself, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
5\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Military Tactics.  
(Passive) If an allied non-Alter Jeanne d’Arc is present on the field, Effects 1 to 4 affect all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself instead. However, if Mad Enhancement has been activated due to Prelati’s Encouragement, then this effect only activates if there is an allied Jeanne d’Arc Alter on the field.

(Gilles) Golden Rule — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever this Unit performs a Crit, it has an 80% chance of reducing its NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.

(Gilles) Prelati’s Encouragement — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) When this Skill is used, Gilles has a 15% chance of permanently activating his Mad Enhancement Skill. Every time this Skill is used after the first usage, activation chance is increased by 15% (max. 90%).

(Gilles) Saint War Order: Rally Upon the Holy Banner and Roar — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]

1\. Applies Atk Up to himself for 2◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself for ⅔◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
3\. Inflicts Def Dwn on himself for 2◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.

(Gilles) Prelati's Spellbook: Textbook of the Sunken Spiral Castle — Rank: A+ (NP)  
Can only be used when Mad Enhancement is Activated. Allows the use of the Spells ‘Summoning: Horror’ and ‘Summoning: Elder Horror’.

(Gilles) Summoning: Horrors  
Spell. Can be used as a Counter. Roll a four-sided die. The number rolled is the number of Horrors summoned by Gilles (they will appear within a 5x5 panel area around Gilles). Counts as Gilles Attack for the Turn.  
Horrors have 100 Health, 10 Agility, 5 Luck, Range=1, MOV=3 and Base Attack (STR) is 100, Detect=2 panels. Horrors have the ‘Dark (Outsider)’ and ‘Mob’ Attribute.  
Horrors do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Horror can only Move/Attack once per Turn.  
When a Horror is defeated, Gilles Flips a Coin. If Heads, the defeated Horror turns into two new Horrors. This effect does not activate if the Horror was defeated with Light damage or Attack that deals extra damage to ‘Dark’ or ‘Outsider’ Attribute Units.  
Enemy Units cannot Attack Gilles or his Master if there are any Horrors directly next to them.  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Summoning: Elder Horror. Cannot be used if Prelati's Spellbook is on Cooldown.  
Cooldown: (Number of Horrors x ⅔◈) Turns

(Gilles) Summoning: Elder Horror  
Spell. Can be used as a Counter. Summons an Elder Horror on a panel next to Gilles. Counts as Gilles’ Attack for the Turn.  
The Elder Horror has 300 Health, 13 Agility, 7 Luck, Range=2, MOV=4 and Base Attack (STR) is 125, Detect=3 panels. Elder Horrors have the ‘Dark (Outsider)’ and ‘Mob’ Attribute.  
Elder Horrors do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Elder Horror can only Move/Attack once per Turn.  
When an Elder Horror is defeated, Gilles rolls a four-sided die. If a 3 is rolled, the defeated Elder Horror turns into three new Horrors. This effect does not activate if the Elder Horror was defeated with Light damage or Attack that deals extra damage to ‘Dark’ or ‘Outsider’ Attribute Units.  
Enemy Units cannot Attack Gilles or his Master if there are any Elder Horrors directly next to them.  
Cannot be used if Prelati's Spellbook is on Cooldown. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Summoning: Horror.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
