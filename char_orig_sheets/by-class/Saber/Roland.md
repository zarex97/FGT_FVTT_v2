(Roland) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Roland) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Roland) Adamantine Body — Rank: A  
(Passive) All STR damage taken is reduced by 300; if NP, 500\. These values are halved if the received Attack has the Pierce effect. This effect is ignored by the AU if its STR Rank is A+ or higher.  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of the Combat Phase when Attacked. Applies Invuln for ⅓◈ Turns and reduces NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Roland) Soft Soles  
(Passive) Whenever Roland participates in a Combat Phase, the enemy Unit has a chance to Attack Roland’s Soles during its Attack (the Player states whether the AU performs a Sole Attack or not). Roland cannot Block Sole Attacks. If Roland Failed to Evade the Sole Attack, the percentage chance of succeeding are as follows-  
1\. Attack from the Front: 10%  
2\. Attack from the Sides: 25%  
3\. Attack from the Back: 40%  
a) Increase the success chance by 10% if the AU’s Agility is equal to or higher than Roland’s.  
b) Reduce the success chance by 10% if the Attack was an AoE Attack (i.e. hits an area instead of a single target).  
c) Increase the success chance by 10% if Roland was Attacked from within the Fog of War.  
f) Luck Check can be used to increase the success chance of a Sole Attack, a successful Luck Check increases the success chance by 25%.  
A successful Sole Attack ignores the Passive effect of ‘Adamantine Body’ and inflicts the Unremovable debuff ‘Barefooted’ for 2◈ Turns- the Passive effect of ‘Adamantine Body’ is negated.

(Roland) Bitter Tears from a Love of Love — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%.  
2\. Removes all debuffs from himself.  
3\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Charm ResDwn for 1◈ Turns on himself, chance of being inflicted with Charm is reduced by 50%.  
4\. Affects all enemy Units within a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to himself, removes all buffs from the affected Units.  
5\. Has a 50% chance of inflicting Berserk (Bitter) for 1◈ Turns on himself (ignores Debuff Resist and Debuff Immune effects). Berserk (Bitter) has the same effect as regular Berserk, except that Roland can still be inflicted with Charm while he has the Berserk (Bitter) debuff. If he is inflicted with Charm while inflicted with Berserk (Bitter), the Charm debuff takes priority. Also, while he has the Berserk (Bitter) debuff, his Alignment changes from ‘Lawful’ to ‘Chaotic’.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Roland) Intentionally Tardy Oliphant — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to himself, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
3\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn for every other allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself (2 Turns if ◈ is greater than 4).  
4\. Reduce Roland’s Health by 5% of its maximum value (cannot drop below 1 in this way).  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Roland) Roncevaux Oliphant: Severely Delayed Horn — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. Range=X. In order to use this NP, first select a number of your choice, starting from 1; the Range of this NP is the number selected.  
When this NP is used, all allied Units within the NP’s Range all immediately Move towards Roland until they are within at least a 3 panel area around Roland; this Move Action ignores the MOV of all affected allied Units. Then, apply the following effects to those allied Units who were Moved in this way-  
1\. Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. AGL Up for 1◈ Turns, the value of Agility Check rolls are reduced by 3\.  
4\. Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 30%.  
Then, Roland’s Health is reduced by X \* 100 (Note: Roland will be defeated if his Health drops below 0 in this way\!). If Roland is still alive, the ‘Intentionally Tardy Oliphant’ Skill enters Cooldown; if it is already in Cooldown then its Cooldown is reset.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

The effects of the ‘Wish’ are completely up to the Player, but the cost of the ‘Wish’ will be determined by the GM, i.e. how many Hallowed Tokens it will cost, or maybe an even greater cost is needed.
