(Charlemagne) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Charlemagne) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Charlemagne) Holy Knight Emperor — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Charlemagne. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Demonic) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Ward (Divine) for 1◈ Turns to himself, all damage received from Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is reduced by 30%.  
4\. Applies the ‘Paladin’ buff to all affected Units EXCEPT himself for 1◈ Turns, Normal Attacks deal 50 extra Light damage, and all Light damage dealt OTHER than bonus Light damage from the ‘Paladin’ buff itself is increased by 100\.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Charlemagne) Mana Burst (Light) — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used when performing a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, Charlemagne’s Base Attack used is his BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=400). Not affected by Magic Resistance. Light damage (half). Damage dealt to Units with the ‘Evil’ Alignment or ‘Dark’ Attribute is further increased by 50% (does not stack if DU has both). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Charlemagne) Over the Royal Road — Rank: C  
(Passive) Whenever Charlemagne initiates an Attack on a Unit (i.e. an Attack that isn’t a Counter), apply the ‘Royal Road’ buff to himself as long he is within that enemy Unit’s Range or that enemy Unit is within Charlemagne’s Range (this condition shall be termed as ‘Battling’). The effects of the ‘Royal Road’ buff are as follows-  
1\. Normal Attack damage dealt is increased by 15%. All damage received is reduced by 15% including NP.  
2\. The ‘Royal Road’ buff ~~is categorized as both an Atk Up and Def Up buff. It~~ cannot be prevented by buff prevention effects and is Unremovable except through its own effect.  
When the enemy Unit Moves enough panels away from Charlemagne that they are no longer Battling, and both Units do not engage in Combat with each other ~~for the next 1◈ Turns~~ by the end of the next Round, remove ‘Royal Road’ from Charlemagne, unless Charlemagne is still Battling with a different Unit.  
However, if Charlemagne is the one who Moves enough panels away from the enemy Unit so that that they are no longer Battling, and both Units do not engage in Combat with each other ~~for the next 1◈ Turns~~ by the end of the next Round, apply the following effects to Charlemagne; unless Charlemagne is still Battling with a different Unit-  
Remove the ‘Royal Road’ buff from Charlemagne. Then, inflict the ‘Deflated’ debuff on Charlemagne for 2◈ Turns. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. All damage dealt is reduced by 15%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. All damage received is increased by 15%.  
3\. As long as Charlemagne is inflicted with ‘Deflated’, ‘Royal Road’ cannot be applied to him.  
4\. The ‘Deflated’ debuff ~~is categorized as both an Atk Dwn and Def Dwn debuff. It~~ is Unremovable and ignores debuff resist & debuff immune effects.  
(Summary: Charlemagne gets the ‘Royal Road’ buff when he initiates an Attack on an enemy Unit. If the enemy Unit retreats, Charlemagne loses that buff unless he is still Battling a different Unit. However, if it is Charlemagne who retreats, not only does he lose that buff, he is also inflicted with ‘Deflated’ unless he is still Battling a different Unit.)

(Charlemagne) Traveling the Path of the King — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 2 times. Also applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target, Crit Chance is increased 5%. Then, applies the following effects to himself depending on whether Charlemagne has the Invuln buff or not-  
1\. If Charlemagne has the Invuln buff, inflict NP Seal (Deflated) for ⅓◈ Turns. Ignores debuff resist and debuff immune effects and is Unremovable.  
2\. If Charlemagne does not have the Invuln buff, apply NP Regen for 2◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. Then, apply the ‘Heroic King’ buff for 2◈ Turns, its effects are as follows-  
Whenever Charlemagne is successfully Attacked or performs a successful Crit Attack, he gains a Joyeuse Token for 2◈ Turns (max. 12\) at the end of the Combat Process.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns if he had Invuln/4◈-⅓◈ Turns if he did not have Invuln.

(Charlemagne) Joyeuse Ordre: Exemplify the King's Heroics, O' Twelve Radiant Traveling Swords — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-King (Self)\]  
(Passive) Whenever Charlemagne successfully defeats a non-Mob Unit, he gains 1 Joyeuse Token (Note: Joyeuse Tokens gained from this effect have no duration).  
(Active) Can only be used when Holy Knight Emperor is not on Cooldown, or there is a Unit on the board affected by a buff from the ‘Holy Knight Emperor’ Skill. Cannot be used on the same Turn as ‘Holy Knight Emperor’ or if Charlemagne is affected with Skill Seal.  
{R} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=5. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range (7x7 if Charlemagne had more than 6 Joyeuse Tokens) for 5x damage, damage dealt is further increased by 15% for every Joyeuse Token on Charlemagne. At the end of the Combat Process, Charlemagne loses all buffs applied by Holy Knight Emperor, and Holy Knight Emperor enters Cooldown if it is not. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Charlemagne) Charles Patricius: My Fleeting Glory — Rank: A (NP) \[Fortress\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, place the ‘Charles Patricius’ token on the board, with the middle panel being the panel Charlemagne was standing on. The token has an area of 7x7 panels. All Units who were within a 7x7 panel area of Charlemagne are moved onto the ‘Patricius’ token. Has the following effects-  
1\. The Patricius functions as a second Home Base for Charlemagne’s Faction.  
2\. Charlemagne’s Master’s ZON is ignored when he Attacks (no damage reduction) while within the Patricius.  
3\. In order to Escape the Patricius, enemy Servants have to Succeed a Luck Check against Charlemagne (can be performed anywhere within the Patricius). If Successful, the Unit is Moved to the nearest panel immediately outside the Patricius.  
4\. Enemy Units can attempt to enter the Patricius. Either the Unit’s STR or AGI Parameter has to be Rank A, and the nearest panel of the Patricius must be within the Unit’s MOV minus 2 (when attempting to enter the Patricius, the Unit’s MOV is reduced by 2\).  
5\. All damage received by allied Units within the Patricius is reduced by 15% including Noble Phantasm.  
6\. Units within the Patricius cannot be targeted by Attacks from outside the Patricius and vice versa. However, the Patricius can be Attacked with an AoE Noble Phantasm. In this case, all Servants within the Patricius receive damage from the NP, but its Total Damage is reduced by 50%; while Masters are undamaged and unaffected by other effects.  
Note: If the NP used on the Patricius was an \[Anti-Fortress\] or higher Noble Phantasm, Charles Patricius is forcefully ended at the end of the Combat Process.  
7\. All damage dealt by allied Units within the Patricius is increased by 15% except Noble Phantasms.  
8\. Units can Move under the Patricius (i.e. onto panels occupied by the Patricius, place the Patricius token on top of those Units).  
9\. Charles Patricius initially lasts for 2◈ Turns. After the initial NP period, Charlemagne can extend the duration of Charles Patricius by 1◈ more Turns by reducing his Health by 200 (cannot be used if Charlemagne’s Health is less than 200), and can be repeatedly extended.  
10\. The Patricius is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Charlemagne or his Master is defeated.  
*Categorized as ‘Flying Fortress’*. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns after the NP ends
