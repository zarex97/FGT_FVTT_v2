(Muramasa) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 25% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 25% including NP.

(Muramasa) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Muramasa) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is further increased by 5d12 including NP.  
2\. While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with Territory Creation, only Effects 1 and 2 of Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Muramasa) Sword Aesthetic Appreciation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Crit Damage dealt by all allied Units within a 3 panel area of himself is increased by 5%.  
(Passive 2\) Normal Attacks of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself have a 10% chance of having the Ignore Def effect.

(Muramasa) Era of Misfortune — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘King’ Attribute is increased by 20% including NP.

(Muramasa) Tameshimono — Rank: B+  
(Active) Sword Testing. Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Roll a six-sided die. Restore X Agility, X=3 if number rolled is 1 or 2, X=4 if number rolled is 3 or 4, X=5 if number rolled is 5 or 6\.  
2\. Roll a six-sided die. Restore X Luck, X=3 if number rolled is 1 or 2, X=4 if number rolled is 3 or 4, X=5 if number rolled is 5 or 6\.  
3\. Roll a six-sided die. Apply Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X%; if NP, (X-10)%. X=40 if number rolled is 1 or 2, X=50 if number rolled is 3 or 4, X=60 if number rolled is 5 or 6\.  
4\. Roll a six-sided die. Apply Crit Up to self for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by X%. X=30 if number rolled is 1 or 2, X=50 if number rolled is 3 or 4, X=70 if number rolled is 5 or 6\.  
5\. Roll a six-side die. Apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by X%. X=5 if number rolled is 1 or 2, X=10 if number rolled is 3 or 4, X=15 if number rolled is 5 or 6\.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns

(Muramasa) Karmic Vision — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Muramasa performs an Attack against a Unit with the Dodge or Invuln buffs, he gains one Karmic Vision Strike (KVS) if his Attack was Evaded, while he gains 2 if it was not Evaded. (Maximum number of KVS \= 6.)  
If Muramasa is affected by a buff removal effect, he loses half of his KVSs, rounded down.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Pierce for ⅓◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies the ‘Gyou’ buff for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the Dodge or Invuln buffs is increased by 100% including NP.   
3\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 20% plus 10% for every KVS on Muramasa.  
4\. Applies the Crit DmUp buff for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 20% plus 10% for every KVS on Muramasa.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Muramasa) Homura — Rank: EX  
(Active) Blaze. Used during your Turn. Reduce Muramasa’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns, then apply the ‘Homura’ buff to himself for 1◈ Turns. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. Whenever Muramasa performs a successful Normal Attack, his NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns, even if no damage was dealt.  
2\. Normal Attacks deal 100 (50?) extra Fire damage with a 10% chance of inflicting Burn for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. At the end of every Turn, all Offensive Debuffs are removed from Muramasa.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Muramasa) Tsumukari Muramasa: Baseless Blade Works — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Base Attack (STR) is used. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 1 Luck plus 1 more Luck for every 2 KVSs Muramasa has.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X%; if NP, (X-10)%; where X=(10 plus 5 for every KVS Muramasa has).  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 10%.  
4\. Hits a 1x3 or 3x1 panel area in any direction next to Muramasa. First removes all Offensive Buffs from the affected Units, then deals 5x damage plus 100 that has the Ignore Def effect. Has a 50% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns. Fire damage (half).  
5\. If used diagonally, Total Damage received by the DU 3 panels away from Muramasa is reduced by 20%.  
6\. Apply the ‘Karmic’ buff for 1◈ Turns and the ‘Tsumukari’ effect for 1 time to Muramasa  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

‘Tsumukari’ effect: Neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable. The next time Muramasa uses the ‘Tameshimono’ Skill, all its effects activate at the maximum value/roll.

‘Karmic’ buff: Whenever Muramasa performs an Attack against a Unit while he has any KVS, consume one KVS to ignore Dodge and Invuln on the DU as well as the DU’s Evade and Block Actions. Muramasa does not gain any KVS from this Attack. Note: KVS is not consumed if the DU does not have Dodge or Invuln and if the DU does not Evade or Block the Attack.
