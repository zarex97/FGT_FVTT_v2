(Lip) Alter Ego

(Lip) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Lip) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Lip) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 40% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Lip) Princess Order — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has different effects depending on whether used on another Unit or herself as follows-  
Used on another Unit  
1\. Applies the ‘Prince’ effect for 2◈ Turns, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff. If applied on her Master, her Master cannot be Overpowered or Underpowered. Additionally, Passionlip and the Prince are treated as having a ‘Servant-Master relationship’ in Combat situations (see **Rules – Relationships** in the Rulebook). (Servant=Prince, Master=Passionlip).  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Prince) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100% when directly next to Passionlip; if this Unit is 2 panels away from Passionlip, 85%; if this Unit is 3 panels away or further, 70%.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp (Prince) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit is increased by 10%.  
4\. Applies Crit Up (Prince) for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
5\. Applies Ward (Prince) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken from Standard Class Servants is reduced by 50%.  
6\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the target Unit, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Note: ‘Prince’ buffs have no effect on a Unit without the ‘Prince’ effect.  
Used on herself  
1\. Applies the ‘Princess’ effect for 2◈ Turns, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff. If her Master is within a 2 panel area of herself while she has the ‘Princess’ effect, then her Master cannot be targeted for any Attacks, even by Units who can normally ignore Relationship Rules (e.g. Units with Presence Concealment).  
2\. Applies NP DmUp (Princess) for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 40% while her Master is directly next to her, 30% while her Master is 2 panels away from her, 20% if her Master is 3 panels away or further.  
3\. Applies NP Regen (Princess) for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn; if her Master is directly next to her, 2 Turns instead of 1 Turn.  
4\. Applies Guts (Princess) for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, when Passionlip is defeated she is revived with 1 Health; if her Master is directly next to her when she is defeated and revived through Guts (Princess), then the buff is not consumed.  
5\. Applies Debuff Immune (Princess) for 1◈ Turns, 1 time; does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase. If her Master is directly next to her when Debuff Immune is triggered, then the buff is not consumed.  
6\. Applies Instakill Resist for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Instakill is reduced by 100% (does not affect Death & Erase); if her Master is directly next to her, then it also affects Death.  
Note: ‘Princess’ buffs have no effect on a Unit without the ‘Princess’ effect.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive 1\) As long as there is a Unit on the field with the ‘Prince’ effect or Passionlip has the ‘Princess’ effect, Passionlip enters Princess Mode and gains the following effect- Range is increased by 2 panels. At a Range of 2, Normal Attacks are {Ranged} and use Base Attack (MAG). At a Range of 3 or higher, Normal Attacks are {Melee}, use Base Attack (STR) and deal Water damage (half) with a 10% chance of inflicting Slow for 1◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) Only one of the ‘Prince’ or ‘Princess’ effect can exist on the field at any time. If the ‘Prince’ effect is applied to a Unit while another Unit has it or the ‘Princess’ effect, then it is removed from the previous Unit and vice-versa. If the ‘Prince’ or ‘Princess’ effect is applied to a Unit who already has it, then its duration is extended.
