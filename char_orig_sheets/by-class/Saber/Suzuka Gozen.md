(Suzuka) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Suzuka) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Suzuka) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Suzuka) Jintsūriki (JK) — Rank: B  
(Active) Supernatural Power (Joshikousei). Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 25%.   
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Suzuka) Mystic Eyes — Rank: B+  
(Active) Range=2. Used during your Turn. Used on an enemy unit. Cannot be used on a Unit if there is an obstacle/obstruction between Suzuka and the target Unit.  
(Example: Unit \[Cannot be targeted\]-Unit \[Can be targeted\]-Suzuka) Has two effects-  
1\. Inflicts Atk Dwn on the DU for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. Has a 100% chance of inflicting Charm on the DU if it is a Male for 1◈ Turns. For every Rank in Magic Resistance the DU, chance of inflicting Charm is reduced by 10% (e.g. Rank E=90%, Rank D=80%, and so on).  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Suzuka) Blessing of Wisdom — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen (Wisdom) for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Applies Aim (Wisdom) for 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp (Wisdom) for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
4\. Has a 300% chance of inflicting Disorder for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Skill Seal from Disorder is 30%.  
5\. Has a 300% chance of inflicting Mental Debuff ResDwn for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is increased by 30%.  
Note: Effects 4 and 5 ignore the debuff resist effect of her own Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Cannot be used while she has the ‘Shoutoren’ effect.

(Suzuka) Kenmyouren \- Monju Chi-ken Daishintō Ren'ai Happa Tenkiame — Rank: B++ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Sword of Transcendent Wisdom and Daishintou, Love Blast, Heavenly Demon Rain. {R} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=3 panels. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 3.5x damage plus 200, then applies Crit DmUp to herself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%; and also apply NP Regen to herself for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
If Suzuka has the ‘Trichiliocosm’ effect, this NP hits a 5x5 panel area instead; Total Damage dealt by Tenkiame is further increased by 30%; and the Crit DmUp increases her Crit Damage by 80% instead of 50%.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns. If used while she has the ‘Trichiliocosm’ effect, Cooldown is doubled (2\*(6◈+⅔◈) Turns).

(Suzuka) Shoutoren \- Saichi no Shukufuku — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Shoutoren \- Blessings of Wisdom. Can only be used when Suzuka’s Health is less than 50% of its maximum value. When used, gain the ‘Shoutoren’ effect for 2◈ Turns, this effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after the ‘Shoutoren’ effect ends. Cannot be used while she has a ‘Wisdom’ buff.  
(Passive 1\) These effects are only active while Suzuka has the ‘Shoutoren’ effect-  
1\. Agility Check rolls are reduced by 3\. Enemy Units who Evade Suzuka’s Attacks have their Evade roll increased by 3\.  
2\. MOV increases by 1\.  
3\. Daitsuuren Skills no longer enter Cooldown when used, but each one can only be used once per Round (Turn?). The chance of Daitsuuren Skills inflicting their respective debuffs is increased by 50%.  
4\. Tenkiame now only hits enemy Units within the NP area.  
5\. Kenmyouren \- Trichiliocosm is usable even if Suzuka does not meet the required condition.  
(Passive 2\) When the ‘Shoutoren’ effect ends, apply the following effects to herself at a 300% chance-  
1\. NP Lock for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Dmg Loss for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 30 including NP.  
3\. Delay+3.  
Ignores the debuff resist effect of her own Magic Resistance.
