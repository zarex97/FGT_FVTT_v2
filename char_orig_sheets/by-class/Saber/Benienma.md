(Benienma) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Benienma) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 4\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt ~~to Servants~~ is increased by 100% (doubled); if NP, 50%. Does not apply against Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When this Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 10% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Damage Spells.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable; if this Unit is inflicted with Skill Seal while PC is Active, it is immediately deactivated.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Benienma) Class Skill: Inn Creation — Rank: B  
(Passive) Has the following effects-  
1\. When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d10 including NP.  
2\. While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+15) including NP.  
3\. When an allied Unit’s Health, Agility and Luck are restored from being in its Home Base, Health is further restored by 50, and either Agility or Luck is further restored by 1 (your choice).  
4\. If a Unit afflicted with debuffs remains in the Home Base for 2 full Rounds (instead of the normal 3 full Rounds), it is cured of all debuffs at the end of the Round.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Territory Creation’. If there are multiple allied Units with Territory Creation, only effects 1 and 2 of Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).
