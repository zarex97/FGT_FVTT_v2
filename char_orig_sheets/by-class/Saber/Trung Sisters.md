**Trung Sisters**

Trưng Trắc and Trưng Nhị are summoned as two separate Servants as one. Trưng Trắc and Trưng Nhị have their individual Stats; however, the maximum distance between the two is 3 panels between them (2 if diagonal). If either one is defeated, the other one is also defeated as well regardless of remaining Health. Both Trưng Trắc and Trưng Nhị are respectively allowed to Move and Attack once during their Turn; each one counts as 0.5 Units.

When calculating whether the damage dealt by either one of them needs to be reduced if she is too far from their Master, as long as the other counterpart is within their Master’s ZON, damage dealt is not reduced.  
When either Trưng Trắc or Trưng Nhị uses a Skill, the Skill enters Cooldown for both of them.

(Trưng Trắc) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Trưng Trắc) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

## (Trưng Trắc) Divinity — Rank: C

(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Trưng Trắc) Charisma of the Rebellion — Rank: B-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
2\. Restores 1 Luck plus 1 more if Trưng Nhị is within the Skill area.  
3\. If Trưng Nhị is within the Skill area, apply Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
Cooldown: 4◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Trưng Trắc) Sisterly Bond — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects herself and Trưng Nhị. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restore 2 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.  
Next, apply either effect 3a or 3b to herself, then apply the other one to Trưng Nhị.  
3a. Applies the ‘Trắc’ buff for 1◈ Turns, when a successful Normal Attack which deals MAG damage is performed, apply Atk Up (STR) for ⅓◈ Turns, all STR damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
3b. Applies the ‘Nhị’ buff for 1◈ Turns, when a successful Normal Attack which deals STR damage is performed, apply Atk Up (MAG) for ⅓◈ Turns, all MAG damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Trưng Trắc) Exalted Queen Trung — Rank: EX  
(Passive) If the Region of the Grail War is Vietnam or Southeast Asia, the Trung Sisters are summoned in their Exalted Form. They gain the following effects when in Exalted Form-  
1\. The Trung Sisters are combined into a single Unit, use the single ‘Trung Sisters’ figurine instead.  
2\. The magnitude of Divinity is doubled.  
3\. Normal Attacks deal Water damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Slow for 1◈ Turns.  
4\. For ‘Charisma of Rebellion’, the other sister is always treated as being in the Skill area.  
5\. For Effect 3 of ‘Sisterly Bond’, apply both the ‘Trắc’ and ‘Nhị’ buffs to herself.  
6\. Punji Trap and Assault Copter cannot be used.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. First, if the Trung Sisters are not already in Exalted Form, they change into Exalted Form for 2◈ Turns. (Remove both sisters from the field and place the Trung Sisters figurine on the panel occupied by the sister who used this Skill. Health, Agility, and Luck values after changing will be the average of the two sisters combined.) Next, apply the following effects (if the sisters were already in Exalted Form, use the values in this colour)-  
1\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns (2◈ Turns).  
2\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
3\. Applies Crit Guard for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself, Crit Chance of a Unit’s Attack is reduced by 15% (25%) against a Unit with this buff.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Trưng Trắc) Punji Trap  
{M} Attack Skill. Range=3. Deals Nature damage with a 50% chance of inflicting Bleed for 1◈ Turns and a 50% chance of inflicting Poison. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns plus ½◈ more Turns for every debuff inflicted.
