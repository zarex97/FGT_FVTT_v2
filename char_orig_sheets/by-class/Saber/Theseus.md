(Theseus) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Theseus) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Theseus) Ariadne’s Prayer — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Randomly activate one of the following effects. If Theseus had the ‘Aporitos’ effect when this Skill is used, remove it from itself and activate all three effects instead-  
1\. Restores 3 Luck and applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Theseus) Instinct (Labyrinth) — Rank: B++  
(Passive 1\) When Theseus is Attacked, he has a 60% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If Instinct Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is 30% instead. If Theseus is within a ‘Labyrinth’, chance of automatically Evading is 80% (if NP, 40%).   
(Passive 2\) Whenever Theseus automatically Evades an Attack through Passive 1 of this Skill, gain the ‘Aporitos’ effect (neither buff or debuff and Unremovable, does not stack).   
(Passive 3\) Crit Chance is increased by 10%; if Theseus is within a Labyrinth, 20%.  
All Passive effects of this Skill are negated if Theseus is inflicted with Skill Seal; the ‘Aporitos’ effect is immediately removed if Theseus is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up (Counter) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance of Counters are increased by 30%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp (Counter) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt by Counters are increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅔◈ Turns.

(Theseus) Martial Retribution — Rank: B  
(Active) Used on an enemy Unit within Range, can only be used on Units who wield a ‘Weapon’. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase when Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (Retribution \- insert DU name here) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 10% for every Rank in STR the target Unit had (i.e. STR Rank E \= Atk Up 10%, STR Rank D \= Atk Up 20%, and so on); if NP, the magnitude is halved. Atk Up (Retribution) does not stack, cannot be negated by effects that prevent buffs from being applied and is Unremovable.  
2\. Inflicts Atk Dwn (Retribution) on the target Unit, all damage dealt is reduced by 10% for every Rank in STR that Unit had; if NP, the magnitude is halved.  
3\. For every Rank in STR the target Unit had, has a 10% chance of inflicting Disable (Retribution) for ⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: ‘Retribution’ debuffs ignore debuff resist and debuff immune effects. They are also Unremovable and do not stack, but are automatically removed when Atk Up (Retribution \- insert DU name here) is removed from Theseus.  
4\. This Skill counts as ‘Stealing’ the target Unit’s ‘Weapon’, but does not count as a Normal Attack for the Turn. If the DU wields more than one ‘Weapon’, the Stolen ‘Weapon’ is random. If the Stolen ‘Weapon’ had any Passive effects (the easiest way would be to check Passive effects under NP), Theseus also gains those Passive effects as long as he has the Atk Up (Retribution) buff.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note 2: This Skill cannot be used on Units who wield ‘Holy’, ‘Demonic’, or ‘Divine’ ‘Weapons’.

(Theseus) Ariadne Apórrito Ádis: Thus, the Thread Leads to the Terminus — Rank: C++ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Theseus’ MOV cannot be reduced while within a ‘Labyrinth’ area. When Theseus attempts to escape from a ‘Labyrinth’ area, the success chance will always be 100%. This also affects any allied Unit who are directly next to Theseus and are within the same ‘Labyrinth’ area. This Passive effect is negated if Theseus is affected by NP Seal.  
(Active) Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+2 for the Combat Process; if Theseus is within a ‘Labyrinth’ area, Range=his MOV. First, has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%. If this NP is used when Theseus is within a ‘Labyrinth’ area, instead applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 75%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (D.Beast) for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic Beast’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
Then, Theseus is Moved to the nearest panel which would place him directly next to the DU and deals 3x damage plus 200, damage dealt is further increased by 150% against Units with the ‘Giant’ Attribute. Then, applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 5◈+⅔◈ Turns.

(Theseus) Dyskolia Speira: Greatly Precipitous Kismetic Spiral — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Range+1 for the Combat Process. Can only be used at the start of a Damage Step when Attacked by a Unit within Range. The AU’s Attack is negated, then Theseus deals STR damage equal to the amount of damage he would have taken from the enemy Unit’s Attack to the AU (not affected by any damage-modifying effects on himself), this is treated as a Counter.  
If the AU’s Attack was an NP, Flip a Coin. If Heads, the AU’s Attack is negated; if Tails, Total Damage taken is reduced by 50%. Then, proceed with ‘Theseus deals STR damage equal to the amount of damage he would have taken from the enemy Unit’s Attack to the AU (not affected by any damage-modifying effects on himself), this is treated as a Counter.’ Note: If the coin flipped Tails, Dyskolia Speira would still deal STR damage equal to the damage Theseus would have taken before the 50% Total Damage reduction.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
