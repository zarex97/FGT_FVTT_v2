(Mordred) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Mordred) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Mordred) Instinct — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is Attacked, it has a 60% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If Instinct Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is 30% instead.  
(Passive 2\) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
This Skill is negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Mordred) Cigarette Lion — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Mordred, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp to Mordred for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Mordred) Mana Burst — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used when performing a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, Mordred’s Base Attack used is her BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=360); and for that Combat Phase, Mordred’s Evade Rolls are reduced by 1\. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Cannot be used while Mordred has the ‘Crimson’ buff.

(Mordred) Knight of Crimson Thunder — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies the ‘Crimson’ buff for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, Normal Attacks use her Base Attack (STR) and 40% of her Base Attack (MAG) combined (e.g. 185+70=255). Not affected by Magic Resistance.  
2\. Applies the ‘Thunder’ buff for 1◈ Turns, Range is increased by 1 and Normal Attacks deal 20 bonus Lightning damage.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp (Crimson) for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt at a Range of 1 is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Mana Burst.

(Mordred) Helm of Hidden Infidelity — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the following effects-  
1\. Removes all debuffs from Mordred.  
2\. Applies Def Up for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. Reduces NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Mordred) Secret of Pedigree: Helm of Hidden Infidelity — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive) Mordred’s Health, Agility and Luck are not revealed/included in her description/figurine drop down menu. It only exists in her Notebook Page (May the GM observe it whenever there are any changes to the values of the Stats). Additionally, all of her Notebook information excluding Parameters and Class Skills are hidden to all other Players. These effects are disabled when Clarent Blood Arthur is used, and can only be reactivated 3◈ Turns after Clarent Blood Arthur was used. Mordred can also willingly deactivate this NP, and can only be reactivated 3◈ Turns after it was deactivated.

(Mordred) Clarent Blood Arthur: Rebellion Against My Beautiful Father — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Cannot be used on the same Turn as Mana Burst, or if Mana Burst is in Cooldown. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x5 or 5x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Mordred for 4x damage plus 100\. Deals extra damage to Units named Arturia or Arthur, damage dealt is further increased by 200%. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
