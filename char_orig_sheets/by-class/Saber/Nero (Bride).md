(NeroBride) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(NeroBride) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(NeroBride) Nuptiae Domus Aurea: Wedding Hall of the Flamboyant — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Team\]  
(Non-damaging) Affects a 7x7 panel area around Nero. Can only be used if Nero’s Master is standing next to her. All Units within the NP area upon activation are trapped. This NP has the following effects-  
1\. If an allied or enemy Unit within Nuptiae Domus Aurea attempts to Escape, roll a six-sided die. Escape is successful if a 6 is rolled. The Unit cannot Move on the same Turn it attempts to Escape, unless it is Successful. If Successful, the Unit is moved to a panel outside the NP area, and cannot Act for the rest of its Turn. Units outside the NP area are able to enter it.  
2\. All allied Units within the NP area recover 100 Health, 1 Agility and 1 Luck at the end of its Turn, at the end of a Turn it Acts, and at the end of the Round. This effect is doubled for Nero’s master.  
3\. Within the NP area, all damage dealt by allied Units is increased by 10%, and all damage received is reduced by 10%. This effect is doubled for Nero. Applies to Noble Phantasm as well. Exception to Nero’s Master, see Effect 4\.  
4\. Within the NP area, all Total Damage dealt by Nero’s Master is increased by 100%, and all Total Damage taken is reduced by 50%. Applies to Noble Phantasm/Magic Crest as well. Additionally, Nero’s Master cannot be Overpowered or Underpowered.  
5\. Lasts for 2◈ Turns. The NP is forcefully ended at the end of the Turn if Nero is defeated.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns after the NP ends.

(Nero) Fax Caelestis: Closing Rose That Fames Stars — Rank: B++ \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Attack Skill. Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=2. Can be used in two ways-  
1\. When used within Nuptiae Domus Aurea- Deals 3.5x damage plus 200 and inflicts Burn for 4◈ Turns. Then, inflicts Def Down on the DU for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, damage received is increased by 40%; and inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 40%. Cooldown: 6◈+⅔◈ Turns.  
2\. When used outside of Nuptiae Domus Aurea- Deals 2.5x damage plus 200 and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Then, inflicts Def Down on the DU for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, damage received is increased by 20%; and inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈+⅔◈ Turns.  
Fax Caelestis is *Categorized as Noble Phantasm.*
