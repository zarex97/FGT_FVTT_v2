(Sigurd) Fragments of Gram  
(Passive) For Normal Attacks at a Range of 2 or higher, Sigurd’s Base Attack (STR) is reduced by 30\.

(Sigurd) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Sigurd) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Sigurd) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Sigurd) Dragonkind Modification — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Has the same effects as ‘Independent Action — Rank: EX’  
1\. Sustainability does not apply.  
2\. Master’s Servant Range is increased by 3 panels.  
3\. Cannot be contracted by enemy Casters and Masters.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage dealt is increased by 60%; if NP, 40%; and applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when Sigurd is defeated he is revived with 100 Health. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Sigurd) Primordial Rune (Warrior) — Rank: B  
Enables the use of Primordial Rune (Warrior) Spells. Only one Primordial Rune (Warrior) Spell can be used per Turn.

(Sigurd) Óss  
Primordial Rune (Warrior) Spell. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Applies Crit DmUp to Sigurd for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Sigurd) Fé  
Primordial Rune (Warrior) Spell. Used during your Turn. Roll a die depending on the number of Turns there are in a Round in this game, as follows-  
3 Turns or less: Four-sided die  
4 to 6 Turns: Eight-sided die  
7 to 8 Turns: Ten-sided die  
9 to 10 Turns: Twelve-sided die  
More than 10 Turns: Twenty-sided die  
Reduce Sigurd’s Skill and NP Cooldown except for his Primordial Rune (Warrior) Spells by X Turns, X being the number rolled on the die. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Sigurd) Nauðr  
Primordial Rune (Warrior) Spell. Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies AGL Up for 1◈ Turns, Agility Check rolls are reduced by 2\.  
2\. Applies LUC Up for 1◈ Turns, Luck Check rolls are reduced by 2\.  
3\. Applies MOV Up for 1◈ Turn, MOV is increased by 1\.  
4\. Applies the ‘Nauðr’ buff to himself for 1◈ Turns- Negate the effect of ‘Fragments of Gram’.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Sigurd) Crystallized Wisdom — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Crit Chance is increased by 15%. This effect is negated as long as a Unit other than Sigurd has the Crit Up (Glasses) buff.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of Sigurd. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up (Glasses) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 100%. This buff is Unremovable.  
2\. Applies Debuff Immune for ⅓◈ Turns, does not affect to Instakill, Death and Erase.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
4\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Sigurd) Gram: Dawn of Destruction — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) At a Range of 1, Normal Attacks have the ‘Demonic’ Attribute and damage dealt to Units with the ‘Dragon’ Attribute is increased by 25%. This effect applies to Sigurd’s Normal Attacks at all Ranges as long as the ‘Nauðr’ buff is applied to him.
