(Iori) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Iori) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Iori) Crimson Codex — Rank: B  
(Passive) Enables the use of Jewel Magecraft. Iori starts the game with 12 Jewels. When there is an allied Unit with the ‘Item Construction’ on the field, that Unit can stand directly next to Iori and use that Skill to craft more Jewels for Iori. The number of Jewels that can be created by the Unit with ‘Item Construction’ depends on its Rank, as follows-  
Rank EX: 1d20  
Rank A: 1d12  
Rank B: 1d10  
Rank C: 1d8  
Rank D: 1d6  
Rank E: 1d4  
For every \+ in Rank, one extra Jewel is crafted. For every \- in Rank, one less Jewel is crafted. There is a Cooldown of 3◈ Turns before that Unit can use ‘Item Construction’ to craft Jewels again.

(Iori) Jewel Magecraft: Ember

(Iori) Book of Five Rings — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. When this Skill is used, Iori can change to a different Stance. Iori can only change Stances once per Turn.  
(Passive 1\) Iori gains various effects depending on what Stance he is in. He starts the game in either Earth Stance or Water Stance.  
(Passive 2\) After Iori performs 3 successful Normal Attacks while remaining in the same Stance, he gains the Afterglow effect of that Stance (e.g. Afterglow: Earth). When Iori changes Stances while he has an Afterglow effect, remove the Afterglow effect and apply the corresponding Afterglow buff to himself. Iori can only have one Afterglow buff at any time. If a new Afterglow buff is applied to him, the previous one is removed.  
(Passive 3\) Whenever Iori performs a successful Evade where the value of his Evade roll is less than three points lower than the AU’s current Agility (e.g. AU current Agility is 18 and he rolls 16\~18), he immediately gains the Afterglow effect of his current Stance.  
(Passive 4\) Normal Attack Crits reduce NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: Afterglow effects are Unremovable, but Afterglow buffs are not.
