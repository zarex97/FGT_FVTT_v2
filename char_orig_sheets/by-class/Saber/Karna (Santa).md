(SUNta) Holy Night  
(Passive) All damage dealt during a Night Round as dictated by the game state is increased by 25%; if NP, 10%.

(SUNta) Boxing Day  
(Passive) Karna’s AGI Rank is normally treated as Rank: A (use AGI: Rank A to calculate his Max Agility); however when Karna enters a Combat Phase at the Range of 1, his AGI Rank is treated as EX and gains the following effects for that Turn-  
1\. Karna’s Evade rolls are reduced by 1d4.  
2\. The Evade rolls of Units in Combat against Karna are increased by 1d4.  
3\. If the Combat Phase takes place during Karna’s Turn and he has not Moved during that Turn, Karna’s MOV is increased by 1 for the rest of the Turn.  
4\. If the Combat Phase does not take place during Karna’s Turn or it takes place during his Turn and he has already moved; at the end of the Combat Phase, Karna can Move 1 panel.

(SUNta) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(SUNta) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(SUNta) Hard Puncher — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Crit Damage dealt is increased by 10%.  
(Passive 2\) The Range of Karna’s Counter Attacks are increased by 1 panel.

(SUNta) Footed-Santa — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, 3 times, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(SUNta) Flashing Fist — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or when Attacked. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Dodge for 1◈ Turns, 2 times.  
2\. Applies Crit Up (Counter) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 50% when Countering, otherwise Crit Chance is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp (Counter) for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt when Countering is increased by 100%, otherwise Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

Note: Footed-Santa cannot be used in the same Combat Process as Flashing Fist, and vice-versa (but can be used during the same Turn, outside of Combat).

(SUNta) Hero of Charity (Holy Night) — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself. Reduce the affected Unit’s NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns and applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 3 times; Instakill, Death, and Erase are not affected. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

If this NP successfully hits the DU, first remove all Guts buffs from the DU, then deal 4x damage. Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

(SUNta) Guraharaja Santa Cross: Saint’s Cross Punch — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
{M} Can only be used on an enemy Unit that has engaged Karna in at least 3 Combat Processes throughout this game. Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=2. This NP has the Aim buff. Deals 4x damage with a chance of inflicting Instakill, as follows-  
Base chance: 30%  
If used at a Range of 1: \+20%  
If used as a Counter: \+40%  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.

Note: Winning Arkaputra cannot be used on the same Turn as Guraharaja Santa Cross and vice-versa.
