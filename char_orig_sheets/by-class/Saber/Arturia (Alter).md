(Arturia) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.  
(Passive 3\) When Arturia receives MAG damage from an Attack with the ‘Anti-Dragon’ Attribute, Magic Resistance only negates damage from a MAG Rank of up to D, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.

(Arturia) Vivian’s Blessing  
(Passive) Arturia receives no negative effects from being within a Waterside area.

(Arturia) Charisma — Rank: E  
(Passive) All damage dealt by other allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5 including NP. This effect is negated if this Unit has Atk Up (Charisma) or is inflicted with Skill Seal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (Charisma) for 1◈ Turns to herself, all damage dealt by allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself is increased by 5%; has no effect on NP. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Arturia) Instinct — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is Attacked, it has a 60% chance of automatically Evading the Attack. If Instinct Fails, the Combat Process proceeds as normal. If the Attack is a Noble Phantasm, the chance of automatically Evading it is 30% instead.  
(Passive 2\) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
This Skill is negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.

(Arturia) Twilight Star — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduce Arturia’s NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns. Then, apply S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Arturia for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.   
After using this Skill, Arturia gains the ‘Twilight Star’ effect for 1◈ Turns (2◈ Turns?), which grants her one free usage Burst Air even if Burst Air is on Cooldown, and damage dealt is not halved even if Mana Burst is on Cooldown.   
Burst Air does not enter Cooldown when it is used while Arturia has the ‘Twilight Star’ effect; if used in this way, remove the ‘Twilight Star’ effect from herself after using Burst Air.  
Additionally, while Arturia has the ‘Twilight Star’ effect, the Range of her Normal Attacks and Burst Air are increased by 1\. The ‘Twilight Star’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Arturia) Mana Burst (Reverse Scale) — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used when performing a Normal Attack. First, apply effects (1) and (2). For that Combat Process, Arturia’s Base Attack used is her BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (e.g. BA=420). Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
1\. Applies Pierce for 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Applies NP Regen (Gekirin) for 1◈ Turns, 3 times; whenever Arturia performs a successful Normal Attack using Mana Burst, Attack Skill, or NP, reduce her NP Cooldown by ⅓◈Turns for each DU hit by the Attack. 

(Arturia) Mana Burst: Burst Air  
{R} Attack Skill. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals normal damage to 1 target. Dark damage. If used while Mana Burst is on Cooldown, Total Damage dealt is reduced by 50%. Cooldown: 1◈+½◈ Turns.

(Arturia) Tyrant Clap  
{M} Attack Skill. Range=1. First, deals 50% damage using Base Attack (MAG). Then, if the DU did not successfully Evade the Attack, Arturia can throw (Move) the DU to any panel within a 3 panel area of herself; and if she does, the DU receives 50% damage using Arturia’s Base Attack (STR) after being thrown. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Arturia) Vortigern: Hammer of the Vile King — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
{M} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x4 or 4x1 panel area in any direction (if diagonal, 1x3 or 3x1 panel area instead) next to Arturia for 3.5x damage. Dark damage. Then, all affected Units are knocked back by 1 panel. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns. 

(Arturia) Excalibur Morgan: Sword of Promised Victory — Rank: A++ (NP) \[Anti-Fortress\]  
{R} Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x6 or 6x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Arturia for 4x damage plus 200\. Dark damage. Has a 40% chance of inflicting Curse. Then, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn (2 Turns if ◈ is 8 or greater) for every 2 Units affected by this NP. Cooldown: 7◈+⅔◈ Turns.

Note: Vortigern cannot be used if Excalibur Morgan is on Cooldown and vice-versa.
