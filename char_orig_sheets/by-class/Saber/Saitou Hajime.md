(Saitou) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D

2\. All damage received is decreased by 30%; if NP, 15%.

4\. Range of Mugyō is increased by 1\.

Saitou can end the effect of Chikai no Haori at the end of any Turn. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after Chikai no Haori ends.
