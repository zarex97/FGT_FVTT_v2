(Okita) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: E  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to E is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 10%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 5%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Okita) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: E  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 1 panel for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Okita) Eye of the Mind (False) — Rank: A  
(Passive) Evade Rolls are reduced by 3, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns, and applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 40%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Okita) Weak Constitution — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever Okita performs an Action (Move, Attack, Block, Evade, Counter, Skill, NP, etc.), roll a ten-sided die. If a 1 is rolled, the action is cancelled, Okita loses 10 Health and she is unable to act for the rest of the Turn (10% chance). Deactivates Chikai no Haori if it is Active. Note: If a Skill or NP is cancelled as a result of this effect, it is not used and therefore does not enter Cooldown.

(Okita) Peerless Blade — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Process when performing an Attack. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 100%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%.  
3\. Reduce NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
4\. Apply the ‘Peerless’ effect to herself for 1 time. When the effect of Weak Constitution would activate, the ‘Peerless’ effect is removed from Okita, and Weak Constitution does not activate. This effect is neither a buff or a debuff, does not stack, and is Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Okita) Shukuchi — Rank: B+  
(Active) Reduced Earth. Can be used in two different methods.  
1\. Used when Okita performs a Normal Attack or Sandanzuki (see below). Cannot be used if Okita has already Moved during the Turn. When this Skill is used, Okita can Move up to 6 panels, Attack, then Move again up to 6 panels. The DU cannot Counter the Attack. If the DU Evades, increase the value rolled by 3\. Damage dealt from this Attack is increased by 50%; if NP, 30%.  
2\. Used when Okita is Attacked. Instead of deciding between Evade, Block and do nothing, Okita can Move up to 6 panels before the AU performs its Attack; if Okita Moves out of Range of the Attack, the Attack fails.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever Shukuchi is used, Okita’s Agility is restored by 3 before any of its other effects activate.

(Okita) Mumyō Sandandzuki — Rank: \- (NP) \[Anti-Unit Mystic Sword\]  
Base Attack (STR) is used. Range+1 for the Combat Process. Deals 3x damage that has the Ignore Def effect. Cannot be Blocked. Then, has the following effects-  
1\. Inflicts Def Down for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30%; if Chikai no Haori is Active, apply as ‘all damage taken is increased by 50%’ instead.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 30% and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever this Unit is successfully Attacked.  
3\. Inflicts Crit Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage taken is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Okita) Chikai no Haori — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Non-damaging) Coat of Oaths. When this NP is used, it applies the following effects on Okita-  
1\. All damage dealt is increased by 30% including NP.  
2\. All damage received is decreased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. MOV is increased by 1\. Number of panels that can be Moved during Shukuchi is also increased by 1\.  
4\. Range is increased by 1\.  
These effects last until the Passive Effect of Weak Constitution successfully triggers, or the Player cancels it. For every Round Chikai no Haori is active, the trigger chance of the Passive effect of Weak Constitution is increased by 5% (the Round it is activated=10%, next Round=15% and so on). If Chikai no Haori is deactivated through the passive effect of Weak Constitution, Okita loses 50 Health, then reset the occurrence chance of Weak Constitution back to 10%. Okita can end the effect of Chikai no Haori at the end of her Turn or end of any Turn she Acts. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after Chikai no Haori ends.
