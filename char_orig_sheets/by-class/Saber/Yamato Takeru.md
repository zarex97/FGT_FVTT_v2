(Yamato) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Yamato) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Yamato) Divinity — Rank: D  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 20 including NP. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Yamato) Bloodstained Prince — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts for 1◈ Turns, when this Unit is defeated they are revived with 20% of their Max Health.  
2\. NP Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
3\. Applies the ‘Takeru’ buff for 1◈ Turns, whenever an allied Servant within a 3 panel area of Yamato is defeated, his NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
(Passive) Whenever an allied Servant is defeated within a 3 panel area of himself, Yamato has a chance of changing into Takeru Mode, this chance increases by 15% every time this event occurs. However, if his Master is defeated within a 3 panel area of himself, Yamato immediately changes into Takeru Mode.

(Yamato) Shinma Jinsatsu — Rank: A

1\. Applies Dmg Up (Divine) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Divine’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
2\. Applies Dmg Up (Demonic) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Demonic’ Attribute is increased by 50%.  
3\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Yamato) Mana Burst (Water) — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used when performing an Attack. If it was a Normal Attack, for that Combat Process, Yamato’s Base Attack used is his BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=410), deals Water damage (half). Otherwise, apply the following effects-  
1\. Restore 3 Luck.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Yamato) Divine Water: Minagami — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Can be activated or deactivated at any time, once per Turn. When Yamato is summoned, he starts the game with Minagami active. While Minagami is Active, whenever Yamato performs a Normal Attack and the DU Evades, the DU’s Evade roll is increased by 1 if its AGI Rank is higher than Yamato’s, and is increased by 2 if its AGI Rank is equal or lower than Yamato’s.  
(Active/Non-damaging) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Can only be used if Mana Burst (Water) is not on Cooldown. Applies the ‘Minakage’ buff for 2◈ Turns, its effects are as follows-  
1\. Minagami is treated as Active as long as Yamato has this buff, and it cannot be deactivated.  
2\. Range is increased by 1\.   
3\. Normal Attacks deal Water damage (half).  
4\. Normal Attacks at a Range of 2 or higher deal 30% extra Water damage using Base Attack (MAG).  
5\. Mana Burst (Water) cannot be used.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
While Yamato is inflicted with NP Seal, the effects of Minagami are negated and any ‘Minakage’ buffs are removed from Yamato.

(Yamato) Minagami・Tagitsuse  
{R} Attack Skill. Can only be used while Yamato has the ‘Minakage’ buff. Range=3. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Deals 60% extra damage and inflicts Slow for 1◈ Turns. Water damage. Then, remove the ‘Minakage’ buff at the end of the Combat Process.

(Yamato) Sword of Heaven: Ame-no-Murakumo-no-Tsurugi — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World\]

While Yamato is inflicted with NP Seal, these effects are negated.

(Yamato) Murakumo・Meadow Extinguisher  
{M} Attack Skill. Base Attack (STR) is used. Range=1. First, the 5x5 panel area around Yamato becomes a ‘Meadow’ area. Deals 50% extra damage and inflicts Burn for 3◈ Turns. Wind damage (half) and Fire damage (half). Then, all other Meadow panels created by this Skill are hit with 50 Fire damage and returned to normal, which will also be received by any Units standing on those panels, who will also be inflicted with Burn for 2◈ Turns. Yamato is not affected by this Skill. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Note: Ignores Effect 2 of ‘Meadow’ Terrain, already accounted for in the Attack Skill’s effect.
