(Diarmuid.S) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A+ is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Diarmuid.S) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Diarmuid.S) Moralltach & Beagalltach  
(Passive) When Diarmuid enters Combat, if the enemy Unit has at least 3 Parameters of the same or higher Rank than himself, he can use either Moralltach or Beagalltach for his Normal Attacks. Otherwise, he can only use Beagalltach. If the enemy Unit has no Parameters, Diarmuid can choose to Attack with either Moralltach or Beagalltach.  
If Diarmuid’s Health is less than 15% of its maximum value, he performs all Attacks with both Moralltach & Beagalltach simultaneously, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and the Passive effects of both Swords are in effect.

(Diarmuid.S) Mana Burst (Leap) — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used when performing a Normal Attack. First, apply Evade to Diarmuid for ⅓◈ Turns and restore 2 Agility. Then for that Combat Process, Base Attack used is his BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=310). Not affected by Magic Resistance. Then, apply MOV Up for 1◈ Turns to himself, MOV is increased by 1\. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Diarmuid.S) Honor of the Knights of Fianna — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Crit Up to himself for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%.  
2\. Applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all other allied Units within a 2 panel area of Diarmuid, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Diarmuid.S) The Dazzlingly Handsome — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 2 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 30%; if NP, 15%.

If Diarmuid already meets the conditions for Attacking with both Moralltach & Beagalltach simultaneously, then this buff is not consumed when performing said Normal Attack.  
6\. Applies Invuln for 1◈ Turns, 3 times to all allied Female Units within a 2 panel area of himself.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Diarmuid.S) Beagalltach: Ripples of Passion — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive 1\) When Beagalltach is used in Combat, all damage taken by Diarmuid is reduced by 10% including NP. Whenever Diarmuid successfully deals damage with Beagalltach, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn.  
(Passive 2\) This NP increases Diarmuid’s Magic Resistance from Rank B to Rank A+. While Diarmuid is inflicted with NP Seal, his Magic Resistance is reverted back to Rank B.

(Diarmuid.S) Moralltach: Surging Seas of Fury — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) Normal Attacks performed with Moralltach have a 10% chance of inflicting Instakill. When Moralltach is used in Combat, the chance of Diarmuid being inflicted with Instakill in that Combat Phase is reduced by 25%.  
(Active) {M} Range=3 plus any MOV increases due to Status Effects. Base Attack (STR) is used. Deals 3.5x damage and removes all Defensive Buffs from the DU; then has a 60% chance of inflicting Instakill. If Diarmuid uses both Moralltach and Beagalltach, the chance of inflicting Instakill is 80%. Cooldown: 6◈ Turns.
