(Chiron) Great Sage of Centaurs  
(Active) Used during your Turn, Chiron switches between Human Form and Centaur Form. Can only be used once per Round.  
(Passive) In Centaur Form, MOV is increased by 2, but value of Evade Rolls are increased by 2\. Additionally, Chiron gains the ‘Passenger Seat’ ability and loses the ‘Humanoid’ Attribute while in Centaur Form.  
Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.

(Chiron) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (8◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 3 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 4 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Chiron) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Chiron) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30\. This extra damage is dealt as Fixed Divine Damage and does not count towards the amount required for an Injury Roll.

(Chiron) Clairvoyance — Rank: B+  
(Passive 1\) When Chiron uses a Normal Attack at a Range of 3 or higher, the DU rolls a ten-sided die. The DU has a 90% chance of using Evade- when Evading. Otherwise, the Combat Process proceeds as normal.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 35%. Also applies Range Up (Clairvoyance) for 1◈ Turns, Range is increased by 1 (does not stack). Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Passive 1 of this Skill is triggered, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.

(Chiron) Eye of the Mind (True) — Rank: A  
(Passive) Evade rolls are reduced by 3, does not affect non-Evade Agility Checks. Upon a successful Evade, reduce the Cooldown of this Skill by ⅓◈ Turns.  
(Active) Used at the start of a Combat Process when Attacked. Applies Dodge for ⅓◈ Turns and applies Def Up for ⅔◈ Turns, all damage taken is reduced by 25%; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Chiron) Consecration of Eternal Life — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Chiron. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 50%; and applies S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%. Then, Chiron’s Health is reduced by 5% of its maximum value (cannot drop below 1 in this way). Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Chiron) Wisdom of Divine Gift — Rank: A+  
When Chiron is Summoned, he is given a list of Skills (preferably Rank B to Rank A) that must have an Active effect, of all other Servants on the field. He can then select three of them, and use their effects as effects of this Skill. Some notable exceptions of Skills that cannot be copied are listed at the end of this page. Rank EX Skills cannot be copied.  
Additionally, Chiron is able to teach one of the copied Skills to another Servant. Both Masters must agree to this teaching, and both Chiron and the learning Servant must be directly next to each other. The teaching process lasts for 1◈ Turns; Chiron and the learning Servant cannot Act until the teaching process is complete, and the teaching process is cancelled if interrupted (e.g. enemy Servant Attack).

(Chiron) Antares Snipe: Heavenly Scorpion Single-Shot — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Active) Can only be used at Night; and can only be used once per Night Round. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=5. First, removes all Defensive Buffs from the DU, then deals 2x damage plus 100; then inflicts Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 40%. Damage dealt to Units with the \[Earth\] Attribute is further increased by 150%. When this NP is used, Chiron’s Master loses Health equivalent to if a Rank E Noble Phantasm was used instead.  
If possible, this NP can be used to target a specific ‘body part’ or ‘weak point’ on the DU (i.e. Unit with the ‘Vital Spot’ Attribute); if used in this way, the chance of hitting that specific target is increased by 75%.  
Note: Can only be used during ‘Night’ Rounds as dictated by the game state (not from ‘Night’ created by effects such as ‘Darkness’ Terrain).  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
(Trigger) Automatically activates when Chiron is defeated. ⅓◈ Turns after Chiron was defeated, the Active effect of Antares Snipe is automatically used on the Unit who defeated him at the start of the Turn. Chiron’s ex-Master does not lose Health from this effect. If ⅓◈ Turns after Chiron was defeated is a Day Round, then this effect automatically activates at the start of the next Night Round.

Note: Units with the ‘Vital Spot’ Attribute- Siegfried, Achilles, Gilles, Prelati (with the Spellbook), any other Unit who have a specific effect tied to a specific weak spot

Items held:

Skills that cannot be copied by Wisdom of Divine Gift-  
Natural Body, ‘Mystic Eye’ Skills, ‘Mana Burst’ Skills, Kishu no Ma

Basically, Skills that a Servant is physically born with (e.g. Natural Body) or are part of their physical bodies (e.g. Mystic Eyes); and Skills that are extremely unique or characteristic to the Hero (e.g. Affections of the Holy Grail) cannot be copied.

Note: When the GM gives the list of Skills to Chiron, the GM could just give the list of Skill names, or with summarized effects, or with full effects.
