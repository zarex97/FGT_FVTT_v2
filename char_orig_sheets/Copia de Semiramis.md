Note: The ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill is different from the ‘Double Summon’ Active Skill.

(Semiramis) Double Summon: Caster — Rank: B  
(Trigger) When Semiramis is summoned, Flip a Coin. If Heads, she is summoned with the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill (i.e. this Skill). If Tails, she does not have the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill.  
(Passive 1\) If Semiramis has Double Summon: Caster, her Range is 3 panels, 1 target. At a Range of 1 to 2, her Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR). At a Range of 3 or higher, her Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG).  
(Passive 2\) If Semiramis does not have Double Summon: Caster, her Range is 2 panels, 1 target. Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR).  
(Passive) Semiramis is both a ‘Caster’ and ‘Assassin’ Class Servant.

(Semiramis) Class Skill: Presence Concealment — Rank: C+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. The effects of this Skill lasts for 2◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. This Unit cannot be targeted for an Attack or an enemy Unit's Skill. If it is caught in an AoE Attack and fails to Evade, Flip a Coin. If Heads, no damage and effects are received; if Tails, Total Damage taken from that Attack is reduced by 50% & PC is deactivated.  
2\. This Unit’s Attacks cannot be Blocked or Countered unless the DU's current AGI Rank is equal to or higher than it. If the DU Evades, the Evade Roll is increased by 3\.  
3\. This Unit is able to Attack Masters and Move to any panel regardless of enemy Master-Servant positions.  
4\. All damage dealt is increased by 100%; if NP, 50%. Does not apply to Masters.  
5\. After performing an Attack while PC is Active, PC is deactivated at the end of the Combat Process.  
6\. When This Unit Moves into an enemy Servant’s Range (or Detect, if in use), it has a 35% chance of being discovered.  
7\. Active Skills targeting/affecting an enemy Unit(s) cannot be used unless stated. Note: Does not include Attack Skills and Spells that deal damage.  
8\. The effects of Presence Concealment are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable.  
Cooldown: 1◈ Turns after PC is deactivated.

(Semiramis) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, roll a four-sided die; Semiramis creates that number of \[Semiramis’ Poison\] Items. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
Item- \[Semiramis’ Poison\]  
Whenever Semiramis is standing directly next to an allied Unit, any number of \[Semiramis’ Poison\] can be passed from Semiramis to that Unit (once per Turn). The usage of \[Semiramis’ Poison\] is as follows-  
Consume 1 \[Semiramis’ Poison\], the Unit gains the ‘Queen’s Poison’ effect for 3◈ Turns; the ‘Queen’s Poison’ effect is neither a buff or a debuff and is Unremovable. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. The chance of inflicting volatile debuffs is increased by 30%.  
2\. The chance of being inflicted by volatile debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
3\. When a Unit with the ‘Queen’s Poison’ effect performs a Normal Attack which uses Base Attack (STR), Poison is inflicted on the DU at the end of the Damage Step, with a 50% chance of inflicting an additional Stage of Poison (Note: This 50% extra chance of inflicting an extra Stage of Poison is not affected by debuff chance increasing/reducing effects, it is a flat 50% chance); then remove the ‘Queen’s Poison’ effect from the AU.

(Semiramis) Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
Has different effects depending on whether Semiramis has the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill.  
1\. If Semiramis has it, the effects of Territory Creation are reduced to Rank C for her Faction’s Home Base. Territory Creation — Rank: EX is only applied to the whole area of Semiramis’ ‘Hanging Gardens of Babylon’.  
(Territory Creation — Rank: EX)  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 6d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
2\. If Semiramis does not have it, when she uses the ‘Double Summon’ Skill, she gains the effects of Territory Creation — Rank C while she has the ‘DSC’ buff.  
(Territory Creation — Rank: C)  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d8 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+10) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Semiramis) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP.

(Semiramis) Double Summon — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Applies the ‘Construction’ effect for 1◈ Turns- HGoB Construction is increased by 1d6 at the end of every Turn. The ‘Construction’ effect is neither a buff or debuff and is Unremovable.  
3\. If Semiramis does not have the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill, she gains the ‘DSC’ buff for 1◈ Turns, this buff grants her the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Semiramis) Familiar: Doves — Rank: D  
(Passive) Whenever Semiramis sees a Unit for the first time, the ‘Dove’ effect is applied to it (neither a buff nor a debuff, is Unremovable). Semiramis can see the position of all Units with the ‘Dove’ effect on the field, regardless of Fog of War (but the effect does not remove Fog of War for her).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=4. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. Inflicts Debuff ResDwn for ⅓◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is increased by 30%; then reduce Semiramis’ NP Cooldown by X Turns, where X=number of enemy Units on the board with the ‘Dove’ effect (max Cooldown reduction=1◈ Turns). Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Semiramis) Arrogant King’s Poison — Rank: B+  
(Active) Requires 3 \[Semiramis’ Poison\] to use. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range. Inflicts Poison on all affected Units, and also inflicts Def Dwn (B) for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Semiramis) Scales of the Sacred Fish  
Spell. Used at the start of a Combat Phase when Semiramis or an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself is Attacked; the Unit gains the Shield (200) buff for 2◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Semiramis) Summoning: Bašmu  
Spell. Can only be used when Semiramis has the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill. Has different effects depending on whether Semiramis is within her ‘Hanging Gardens of Babylon’ or not.  
1\. If used when she is not within her HGoB: Damage Spell. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 25% extra damage and inflicts Poison. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
2\. If used when within her HGoB: Semiramis summons a Bašmu on a panel directly next to her. Counts as her Attack for the Turn. Its Stats are as follows-  
Bašmu  
Health: 1250  
Agility: 14  
Luck: 7  
MOV: 5  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR/MAG): 150  
Attributes: \[Earth\], Large, Dragon  
Normal Attacks use Base Attack (STR) and have a 50% chance of inflicting Poison.  
(Bašmu) Cursed Poison Dragonfire  
(Active) Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to itself for 3x and inflicts Poison, then inflicts Crit DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is reduced by 30%. *Categorized as Noble Phantasm.* Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.  
Bašmu can Move to any panel, when it Moves to any occupied panels, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel until the space is free for Bašmu to stand on.  
Bašmu do not count towards the number of Units who Move/Attack in a Turn. A Bašmu can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Semiramis or her allied Units if a Bašmu is next to them.  
Only one Bašmu summoned by this Spell can exist on the field. Bašmu cannot leave the HGoB. If HGoB is removed from the field while Bašmu is summoned, it disappears.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Semiramis) Sikera Ušum: Arrogant King's Alcohol — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Has different effects depending on whether Semiramis was summoned with the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill. Its effects are as follows-  
1\. If Semiramis was not summoned with it: When this NP is used, it affects the 5x5 panel area around her (which Moves with her) for 2◈ Turns.   
2\. If Semiramis was summoned with it: Can only be used within the ‘Throne Room’ of ‘Hanging Gardens of Babylon’. Affects the Throne Room for 3◈ Turns, and all Units within the Throne Room when the NP was activated cannot leave it while it is Active.  
The effects of this NP area are as follows-  
a. Semiramis’ Normal Attacks which use Base Attack (STR) inflict Poison.  
b. When a Unit other than Semiramis or her Master Acts then ends its Turn within the NP area, it is inflicted with Poison.  
c. Units inflicted with Poison while within this NP area receive Poison damage at the end of its Turn and at the end of any Turn it Acts, in addition to at the end of the Round.  
d. When a Unit with Poison Immune effects is in this NP area, the Poison Immune effect is reduced to a Poison Resist effect, the chance of being inflicted with Poison is reduced by 75%. Units with Poison Resist effects that are not Poison Immune in this area have the magnitude of those Poison Resist effects halved. Only applies to effects that specify Poison.  
e. Units in the NP area who are weak to Poison (i.e. either takes extra damage from Poison or have an increased chance of being inflicted with Poison) receive double Poison Damage (in addition to any other effects they might have which increase Poison Damage received). Does not apply to external debuffs or effects affecting said Units (i.e. has to be an effect the Unit already has).  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns after the NP ends. 

(Semiramis) Hanging Gardens of Babylon: Aerial Garden of Vanity — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-World/Bounded Field\]  
(Activation) Only in effect and usable if Semiramis was summoned with the ‘Double Summon: Caster’ Skill. Hanging Gardens of Babylon (HGoB) can only be used when the HGoB Construction counter reaches 100\. There are several ways to increase the HGoB’s Construction-  
1\. If the Grail War’s region is in a Middle East region, the HGoB Construction counter starts at 25 instead of 0\. If the Grail War region is directly next to a Middle East region, the HGoB Construction counter starts at 10 instead of 0\.  
2\. When Semiramis is summoned, roll 2 six-sided dice. The HGoB Construction is increased by X, where X=the number of both six-sided die multiplied together.  
3\. At the end of every Round, roll a four-sided die and increase HGoB Construction by 2 plus the number rolled.  
4\. Whenever Item Construction is used, HGoB Construction is increased by the number of \[Semiramis’ Poison\] produced.  
5\. Whenever a non-Spell Skill is used excluding Item Construction, increase HGoB Construction by 2\.  
6\. Semiramis or any allied Unit can perform ‘Gather’ during its Turn, which increases Construction by 3\. However, if Semiramis uses it, Construction is increased by 5, while it is increased by 4 if her Master uses it. Using ‘Gather’ counts as a Unit’s ‘Move’ for that Turn. A Unit cannot Attack on the same Turn it uses ‘Gather’.

If the Grail War’s Region is in a Middle East region, all Construction increases are doubled excluding effects 1 and 2; if the Grail War’s Region is directly next to a Middle East region, all Construction increases are increased by 2 excluding effects 1 and 2\.

When HGoB’s Construction reaches 100, it can be activated. In order to activate the Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Semiramis has to be within her Home Base, and cannot Act for 3◈ Turns. If Semiramis is Attacked during this period, the period of 3◈ Turns is interrupted and she has to restart the activation process. If Semiramis is not interrupted, the Hanging Gardens of Babylon is activated at the end of the last Turn in that period. Semiramis’ Master only loses Health as per NP usage rules only when HGoB successfully activates, not at the start of the NP activation process. However, Semiramis can perform the activation without being in her Master’s ZON.

When HGoB is activated, place the HGoB token on the panel where Semiramis was standing; and she is Moved to the middle panel of HGoB, and all allied Units of your choice are transported to any panel within the HGoB. The HGoB’s token size is 9x9 panels (11x11 panels on Large Board). The 5x5 panel area in the middle of the HGoB is the ‘Throne Room’.

While Semiramis is on her HGoB, all of her Parameters are increased by one Rank (e.g. from D to C), ZON does not apply to her, and her Sustainability is increased by 2◈ Turns.  
STR: E to D, Base Attack (STR) increased by 25\.  
END: D to C, Max & current Health increased by 500\.  
AGI: D to C, Move increased by 1, Max & current Agility increased by 2\.  
MAG: A to EX, Base Attack (MAG) increased by 50\.  
LUC: A to EX, Max & current Luck increased by 4\.

The Stats of the HGoB are as follows-  
Health: 6000  
Agility: 0  
Luck: 0  
MOV: 2 (3 on the Large Board)  
Range: Does not Normal Attack, see below  
Base Attack (MAG): Uses Semiramis’  
The HGoB can move onto occupied panels (when it does, place it on top of the figurines). The HGoB counts as a second Home Base for Semiramis’ Faction.  
During Semiramis’ Turn. the HGoB can Move/Attack once per Turn. The HGoB cannot be affected by buffs and/or debuffs. It has the following Attacks (HGoB cannot Evade, Block, and Counter; and cannot be Countered; and counts as a separate Unit from Semiramis, but does not count towards number of Units who Move or Act in a Turn)-

(HGoB) Dragon Wing Warriors  
Range=4 plus the area under the HGoB and the area of the HGoB. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range. All affected Units receive 50 Fixed STR damage X times, where X=Number rolled on a six-sided die plus 4\. Each instance of damage can be separately Evaded or Blocked. Damaged Units only perform an Injury Roll once regardless of number of hits taken. Cooldown: 1◈ Turns.

(HGoB) Aerial Garden of Vanity  
Range=7. Cannot hit under or above the HGoB. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area within Range for 2x damage. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

Enemy Units on the ground cannot target Units onboard the HGoB for Attacks/Skills/Noble Phantasms. Enemy Units on the ground can only Attack the HGoB with ranged Attacks, and allied Units on the HGoB can only Attack enemy Units on the ground with ranged Attacks. Units onboard the HGoB cannot Attack Units directly below the HGoB (with ranged Attacks) and vice versa.

An enemy Unit can attempt to board the HGoB. Roll a twelve-sided die. If a 12 is rolled, the Unit successfully boards the HGoB. However, if the Unit meets certain the conditions, the roll value required to board the HGoB can be reduced as follows-  
1\. If the Unit’s AGI Rank is C to B, the roll value required is reduced by 1\. If the Unit’s AGI Rank is A or higher, the roll value required is reduced by 2\.  
2\. If the Unit’s LUC Rank is C to B, the roll value required is reduced by 1\. If the Unit’s LUC Rank is A or higher, the roll value required is reduced by 2\.  
3\. If the Unit attempts to board the HGoB on the same Turn it was Attacked by the HGoB’s Dragon Wing Warriors, the roll value required is reduced by 2\.  
4\. If the Unit has the ‘Levitating’ Attribute, roll an eight-sided die instead, with the base roll value required being 8\.  
When an enemy Servant successfully boards the HGoB, it can bring its Master with it if its Master was within a 2 panel area of it.

If a Unit standing on the edge of the HGoB is knocked back which would knock it off the HGoB, it performs an Agility Check. If successful, it has a choice of Moving to the nearest unoccupied HGoB panel other than the panel it was previously occupying, or it can land on the Game Board panel directly under it. If failed, it lands on the Game Board panel directly under it and takes (10\*2d6) STR damage.  
If the Unit who was knocked off the HGoB was a Master, it has to perform an Overpower roll if it lands on the Game Board (i.e. like if it was Attacked by a Servant); even if it had already performed one from the initial Attack.  
If a Master who is directly next to its Servant fails its Agility Check, its Servant can perform an Agility Check too, if successful, its Master is not knocked off the HGoB.

A non-Civilian or non-Master Unit standing on an edge panel of a HGoB can Jump off the HGoB and land on a Game Board panel within its MOV; in this case, the Unit’s MOV is reduced by 1\.  
If a Servant would Jump off the HGoB with its Master directly next to it, the Servant can choose to bring its Master with it, the Master will land next to its Servant in the same orientation. This does not count as Moving the Master.

The HGoB is destroyed when Semiramis is defeated or its Health drops to 0\. When the HGoB is destroyed, all Units on it perform either an Agility Check or a Luck Check roll. If the roll fails, the Unit takes 100 Fixed STR damage. A Master who was within a 2 panel area of its Servant does not need to roll if its Servant succeeded its roll. Then, all Units onboard the HGoB are randomly scattered below it. After the Hanging Gardens of Babylon is destroyed, Semiramis can attempt to rebuild it from scratch (its Construction is reduced to 0).
