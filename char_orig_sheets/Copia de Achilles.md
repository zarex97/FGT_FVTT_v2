**Achilles**

True Name: Achilles  
Region: Greece  
Alignment: Lawful Neutral  
STR: B+  
END: A  
AGI: A+  
MAG: C  
LUC: D

Attributes: Male, Servant, \[Earth\], Humanoid

Base Health: 1500  
MOV: 7 (Dismounted); 8 (Mounted)  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 135  
Base Attack (MAG): 150  
Sustainability: 2◈ Turns  
   
Health: XXX/XXX  
Agility: XX/XX  
Luck: XX/XX

When Achilles Acts, the player must state whether he is Mounted, or Dismounted. Achilles is always Dismounted when it is not his Turn.  
If Mounted at the start of a Combat Phase, Achilles can Dismount at the start of the Combat Phase; but he cannot Mount his chariot when he initiates Combat while Dismounted.

(Achilles) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
Mount: Troias Tragōidia. All effects/abilities related to Riding can only be used while Mounted EXCEPT Passive 1 (see Dromeus Komētēs).  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The Move Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Achilles) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Achilles) Divinity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 30 including NP.

(Achilles) Battle Continuation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage received is reduced by 2d10+20; if NP, the number of dice rolled is doubled.  
(Passive 2\) When this Unit is defeated, it is automatically revived with 5d20 Health. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns, and the Unit’s Health must have been restored back to above half its maximum value at least once since the last activation of this effect.

(Achilles) Bravery — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted with Mental Debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, STR damage dealt is increased by; if NP, 15%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Achilles) Affections of the Goddess — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the following effects on Achilles-  
1\. Applies Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 50%; if NP, 25%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
3\. Applies Debuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Achilles) Andreias Amarantos: Amaranth of the Brave — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive/Non-damaging) Heroism’s Eternal Man. All damage received is reduced as follows-  
1\. Unit without Divinity: Receives 0 damage  
2\. Unit with E-Rank Divinity: All Total Damage received is reduced by 50% (halved).  
3\. Unit with D-Rank Divinity: All Total Damage received is reduced by 25%.  
4\. Unit with C-Rank Divinity and above: Receives normal damage.  
The effect of Andreias Amarantos is lost if Achilles’ Heel is damaged (see below).

(Achilles) Troias Tragōidia: Tempestuous Immortal Chariot — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Troy’s Tragedy.  
(Passive) Riding Attack damage is increased by 25%, does not affect NP. At the end of any Turn where Achilles Acts while Mounted, his Master loses 25 Health.   
(Active) Base Attack (STR) is used. This NP is used in the form of a Riding Attack, with a distance of 13 panels. First restores X Agility and applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X0%; if NP (X-1)0%; X=(the amount of remaining MOV Achilles has divided by 2). Deals 4x damage. Then, applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by Y0%, Y=number of Units successfully hit by this NP. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: Hits in both directions (Front and back) on the normal board; but only hits in one direction (Front) if playing on the Large Board. 

(Achilles) Dromeus Komētēs: Comet Form — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive) Runner Comet. Is only in effect when Unmounted. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has the effects of Passive 1 of Riding (Double Move).  
2\. The value of Evade rolls are reduced by 4\.

(Achilles) Runner Comet — Rank: A+  
(Active) Can only be used when Unmounted and at the start of a Combat Phase. Restore 3 Agility, then applies N.Atk Up for that Turn, Normal Attack damage dealt is increased by 30%; and Crit DmUp for that Turn, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%. Cannot be used if Achilles is affected by Skill Seal or NP Seal. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Achilles) Achilles’ Heel  
Whenever Achilles participates in a Combat Phase while Unmounted, the enemy Unit has a chance to Attack Achilles’ Heel during its Attack (the player states whether the AU performs a Heel Attack or not). Achilles cannot Block Heel Attacks. If Achilles Failed to Evade the Heel Attack, the percentage chance of succeeding are as follows-  
1\. Attack from the Front: 0%  
2\. Attack from the Sides: 5%  
3\. Attack from the Back: 10%  
a) Increase the chance of successfully Attacking Achilles’ Heel by 5% if the AU’s Agility is equal to or higher than Achilles’.  
b) Increase the chance of successfully Attacking Achilles’ Heel by 5% if the Attack was performed at a Range of 3 or higher.  
c) Increase the chance of successfully Attacking Achilles’ Heel by 5% if Combat was initiated by the AU (i.e. not a Counter Attack after being Attacked by Achilles’ himself)  
d) Reduce the chance of successfully Attacking Achilles’ Heel by 10% if the Attack was an AoE Attack (i.e. hits an area instead of a single target).  
e) Increase the chance of successfully Attacking Achilles’ Heel by 10% if Achilles was Attacked from within the Fog of War (meaning from a place where he had no vision of).  
f) Luck Check can be used to increase the success chance of a Heel Attack, a successful Luck Check for a Heel Attack increases the success chance by 25%(?).  
If the AU’s Heel Attack fails, Achilles successfully Evades the Attack. If the AU’s Heel Attack succeeded, Achilles receives damage that ignores all Defensive Buffs and damage reducing effects. Then, the following effects are applied to Achilles after his Achilles’ Heel has been damaged-  
1\. The effect of Andreias Amarantos is lost.  
2\. The Passive effects of Dromeus Komētēs are lost and it is penalized, as follows-  
a) MOV is now reduced by 1 when Unmounted.  
b) Now when performing Evade rolls when Unmounted, increase the value rolled by 1\.  
c) The magnitude of Runner Comet is reduced to 10% for both buffs.

(Achilles) Diatrekhōn Astēr Lonkhē: Spear-tip of the Star Traversing the Skies — Rank: B+ (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Meteor Spear (Non-damaging) Duel Field. Can only be used when Unmounted. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Achilles challenges the targeted Unit to a duel. The Noble Phantasm is only activated if the opposing Unit (player) agrees to the duel, and all conditions of the duel (including defeat) must be decided and agreed on by the two involved parties.  
When this Noble Phantasm is activated, Achilles and the opposing Unit are enclosed within a 5x5 panel area where they are standing on the game board. Then, both Units will continuously duel (Combat) until one Unit is ‘defeated’ as per the terms of the duel; and when the ‘defeat’ occurs, the NP is deactivated.  
No Units can enter the NP area or interfere with the duel, even with Command Spells; the involved Units are not allowed to leave the NP area until the defeat occurs as well. Also, Luck Check cannot be used by the involved Units; and all buffs and debuffs that were caused by Units not involved in the duel are negated for the duration of the duel.  
This Noble Phantasm cannot be used on Female Units and/or Units with at least 3 Parameters one Rank lower than Achilles’. Additionally, this Noble Phantasm cannot be used on the following 3 Servants: Hector, Chiron, Penthesilea.  
Cooldown: 6◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Achilles) Akhilleus Kosmos: The Miniature World Enclosed by the Azure Sky — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Barrier\]  
(Passive) Used when Achilles Moves when Unmounted. If Achilles intends to Move onto an occupied Panel, the Unit occupying said panel is forced to Move backward until Achilles stops Moving in that direction. If the Unit does not or cannot vacate those panels, that Unit is forcefully Moved to one of the panels to its sides, and receives damage equivalent to a Normal Attack from Achilles (Base Attack (STR) is used).  
(Non-damaging) Once, when Achilles or any allied Unit within a 2 panel area of himself is targeted/is within Range of an AoE Noble Phantasm of Rank A and above, Akhilleus Kosmos can be used. Negate all damage and effects of that Noble Phantasm in a 5x5 panel area around Achilles. After that, Akhilleus Kosmos is broken; all its effects are lost and cannot be used for the rest of the game.
