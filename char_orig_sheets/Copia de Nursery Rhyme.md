(Alice) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 5d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+20) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

Agility: 10  
Luck: 6  
MOV: 4  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 75  
Trump Soldiers do not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The same Trump Soldier can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Nursery or her Master if any Trump Soldiers are directly next to them. Counts as Nursery’s Attack for the Turn. Cooldown: ⅓◈ Turns for each Trump Soldier summoned.

(Alice) Nursery Rhyme: Nameless Forest — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Passive) At the end of every Round, this NP affects all enemy Units within a 2 panel area of Nursery. Each time a Unit is affected by this NP, it gains one Nameless Forest Token; this effect does not occur if Nursery is inflicted with NP Seal. For every Nameless Forest Counter on a Unit, reduce its Max Health by ~~50~~ 25, Base Attack (both) by ~~20~~ 10, and Max Luck by 1\. During an affected Unit’s Turn, it can attempt a Luck Check once per Turn to remove the effects of this NP (i.e. all Nameless Forest Tokens) from itself, the effects of the NP are removed on a successful Luck Check (Health and Luck that are lost from the effects of this NP are not restored); the value of the dice rolled for the affected Unit’s Luck Check is modified as follows-  
If the Unit is within its Home Base: \-3 (stacks with the MAG Rank modifiers as seen below)  
MAG Rank EX: \-3  
MAG Rank A: \-2  
MAG Rank B: \-1  
MAG Rank C: No change  
MAG Rank D: \+1  
MAG Rank E: \+2  
At the end of the Unit’s Turn, a Unit with at least 3 Nameless Forest Tokens rolls a twelve-sided die. If the number rolled is equal to or lower than the number of Nameless Forest Tokens on the Unit, the Unit disappears (i.e. is defeated). However, a Unit cannot disappear due to the effects of this NP if it is within its Home Base.  
Every time a Unit successfully removes Nameless Forest Tokens from itself, the chance of it gaining Nameless Forest Tokens again is reduced by 10% (base chance=100%); this effect can stack.

(Alice) Nursery Rhyme: A Tale for Somebody’s Sake — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=4. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 3x damage. Then, inflicts Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%, and increases the NP Cooldown of all affected Units by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns.

(Alice) Nursery Rhyme: Jabberwock — Rank: C (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) When this NP is used, Nursery summons the Jabberwock on a panel next to her. Its Stats are as follows-  
Health: 1500  
Agility: 6  
Luck: 6  
MOV: 3  
Range: 2 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (STR): 200  
Attributes: Demonic, Giant  
When the Jabberwock is summoned, it disappears after 3◈ Turns. When the Jabberwock is summoned for the first time, the \[Vorpal Blade\] Item appears on a random panel on the game board, this Item can be picked up by a Unit walking onto its panel.  
The Jabberwock can Move to any panel, if it Moves onto an occupied panel, all Units occupying said panels are knocked back by 1 panel. Whenever the Jabberwock receives damage from Servants, its Health is restored by 75% of the damage received.

(Jabberwock) Alice Eater  
Used during your Turn. Applies Atk Up to itself for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 50%; if NP, 25%; and extends its period of existing on the board for 3◈ more Turns. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

The Jabberwock does not count towards the number of Units that Move and/or Attack during your Turn. The Jabberwock can only Move/Attack once per Turn. Enemy Units cannot Attack Nursery or her Master if the Jabberwock are directly next to them. Summoning it counts as Nursery’s Attack for the Turn. When the Jabberwock is summoned again after disappearing, its Stats will be the same as when it disappeared. Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after the Jabberwock disappears.

Item- \[Vorpal Blade\]  
*One, two\! One, two\! And through and through, the Vorpal Blade went snicker-snack\!*

The above Stat boosts do not affect NP & Attacks *Categorized as NP*.  
Normal Attacks with this Item Equipped use Base Attack (STR)  
If the Master with this Item Equipped cannot be Underpowered by Servants.  
When the Jabberwock is Attacked by this Equipped Item, it receives 3x damage instead of 50% extra damage due to having the ‘Demonic’ Attribute, and the ‘Whenever the Jabberwock receives damage from Servants, its Health is restored by 75% of the damage received’ effect is permanently removed from the Jabberwock; then the Vorpal Blade breaks and can no longer be used. Cannot be obtained by Nursery or her Master.

Does not affect Nameless Forest Tokens. Can only be used once during the entire game.
