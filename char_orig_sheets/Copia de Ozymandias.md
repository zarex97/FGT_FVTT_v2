(Ozymandias) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
Solar Ship: Mesektet.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The Move Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Ozymandias) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Ozymandias) Divinity — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 40 including NP.

(Ozymandias) Pharaoh of the Hot Sands — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.  
2\. If used during a Day Round, applies Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
3\. If used during a Day Round, applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Ozymandias) Imperial Privilege — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores his Health by 30% of its maximum value.  
2\. 60% chance of applying Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. 60% chance of applying Def Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is reduced by 40%; if NP, 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Ozymandias) Protection from Ra — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Ozymandias. Reduce their NP Cooldown by ⅔◈ Turns; then apply Buff ChUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of applying buffs is increased by 40%. Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Ozymandias) Mesektet: The Solar Ship of the Dark Night — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive) The source of Ozymandias’ Normal Attacks. All Normal Attacks use Base Attack (MAG) and deal extra damage to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute, damage dealt is increased by 100%. Light damage.  
(Active) Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 4x damage plus 100; deals extra damage to Units with the ‘Dark’ Attribute, damage dealt is increased by 100% (doubled). Light damage.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.

(Ozymandias) Ramesseum Tentyris: The Shining Great Temple Complex — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Fortress/Fortress/Anti-Unit\]  
Used during your Turn. Can only be used after 7 full Rounds have passed. First, the Master’s Health is reduced by 50% of its maximum value (cannot be used if his Master’s Health is less than that), then the NP affects an 11x11 panel area around Ozymandias. This area will now be termed as the ‘Complex’. The Complex cannot intersect the Home Base of enemy Players. Has the following effects-  
1\. The following additional Units are also present within the Complex: Sphinx, Sphinx Queen, and Sphinx Wehem-Mesut. Their information is further detailed below.  
2\. Divine Protection: All damage received by Ozymandias and his allied Units while within the Complex is reduced by 50% including NP. Whenever Ozymandias is defeated while within the Complex, he is revived with 20% of his Max Health. When any of the Sphinxes are defeated within the Complex, it is revived with 10% of its Max Health.  
3\. Divine Curse: A curse that affects all enemy Units within the Complex. Has different effects depending on the Unit within-  
Normal Human: Dies at the end of the Turn after entering the Complex.  
Non-normal Human, Servant, other Units: Have the following effects applied to them-  
a) All damage dealt is reduced by 20% including Noble Phantasm.  
b) All damage received is increased by 20%.  
c) When performing Evade and Luck Check Rolls, the number rolled is increased by 2\.  
d) All Units are inflicted with permanent Stage 1 Curse as long as they are within the Complex. It is automatically removed after leaving the Complex.  
4\. God’s Curse: Servants cannot use their Noble Phantasms. Units with Divinity equal to Ozymandias or higher ignores this effect. Does not affect Attacks/Skills/Spells that are only *Categorized as Noble Phantasms*.  
5\. The Complex functions as a second Home Base for Ozymandias and his Master only.  
6\. Ozymandias’ Sustainability does not decrease while he is within the Complex. His Master’s ZON is ignored when he Attacks (no damage reduction).  
Once activated, Ramesseum Tentyris remains constantly Active. There are a few ways that it can be ended-  
1\. It is willingly deactivated by the Ozymandias himself during his Turn, or at the start or end of any Turn or Round. In this case, the Cooldown for it is 8◈ Turns after it ends. When reactivated, the Health of the Sphinxes remain the same.  
2\. It is Attacked with 2 \[Anti-Fortress\] or higher Noble Phantasms in the same Round from outside, or they are used by enemy Units within the Complex, or would receive more than 3000 damage on the same round. In this case, Ramesseum Tentyris cannot be used again for the rest of the game.  
3\. When Ozymandias’ Master is defeated, Ramesseum Tentyris will be forcefully ended after 2◈ Turns, at the end of the Turn. If Ozymandias is defeated, Ramesseum Tentyris is forcibly ended at the end of the Turn.  
4\. Ramesseum Tentyris: Pyramid Drop is used; in this case Ramesseum Tentyris ends first, then the Pyramid Drop occurs. After this, Ramesseum Tentyris cannot be used again for the rest of the game.

(Ozymandias) Ramesseum Tentyris: Dendera Electric Bulb  
Can be used by Ozymandias as his Normal Attack while within Ramesseum Tentyris. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range is any panel within Ramesseum Tentyris, and also has a Range of 4 panels away from the border of Ramesseum Tentyris (if diagonal, 3 panels). Can be used in 2 methods-  
1\. Hits 1 Unit within Range for 2x damage.  
2\. Hits a 2x2 panel area within Range for 1.5x damage.  
Counts as Ozymandias’ Attack for the Turn. Damage dealt is not affected by Atk Up or other damage increasing effects on Ozymandias. Every time the Dendera Electric Bulb is used to Attack, his Master’s Health is reduced by 10\. Cannot be used if his Master’s Health is less than 10\.

(Ozymandias) Ramesseum Tentyris: Pyramid Drop — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Fortress\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Range=5. Hits a 5x5 panel area within Range for 5x damage; then inflicts NP Seal for 3◈ Turns; and inflicts Def Dwn for 3◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 50%. After that, the NP area becomes ‘Day’ for 2◈ Turns and Ozymandias’ Skill Cooldown is reduced by 1◈ Turns.  
Can only be used once during the entire game. After this NP is used, Ramesseum Tentyris: The Shining Great Temple Complex can no longer be used for the rest of the game.

The Sphinxes are able to leave Ramesseum Tentyris. However, when Ramesseum Tentyris ends or is deactivated, all Sphinxes disappear regardless of position. Then if Ramesseum Tentyris is reactivated, the Sphinxes will respawn within the Complex, but with the same Stats as when they disappeared.
