(Karna) Fated Rivals of the Mahabharata  
(Passive) If Arjuna is on the opposing Faction and within Range of Karna, they will only Attack each other. This effect can be negated for 1◈ Turns by spending a Command Spell, but it will only affect Karna.

(Karna) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to C is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 30%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Karna) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Karna) Divinity — Rank: A  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 50 including NP.

(Karna) Discernment of the Poor — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Range=2. Used on an enemy Unit. Inflicts NP Seal for ⅓◈ Turns and inflicts Debuff ResDwn for ⅓◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted with debuffs is increased by 50%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Active) Used during your Turn or at the start of a Combat Phase. Switch the effect of this Skill from 1 to 2, or 2 to 1\. Can only be used once per Round.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Karna) Mana Burst (Flames) — Rank: A  
(Passive) Karna’s Normal Attacks have a 25% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns on the DU. Karna is Immune to Burn. All Total Fire Damage taken is reduced by 50% including NP.  
(Active) This Skill is used when performing a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, Karna’s Base Attack used is his BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (e.g. BA=300). If the Attack is not Evaded, Burn is inflicted on the DU for 2◈ Turns. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Fire Damage (half). Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Flash of the Sun God.

(Karna) Flash of the Sun God — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Restores 3 Agility.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 30%.  
3\. Applies NP DmUp for 1◈ Turns, NP Damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Mana Burst (Flames).

(Karna) Kavacha and Kundala: O' Sun, Become Armor — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit (Self)\]  
(Passive) All damage received by Karna is reduced by 90% including NP; this effect is ignored by Attacks which have the Pierce effect. While Kavacha and Kundala is in effect, Karna’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn that Karna is involved in a Combat Phase, his Master’s Health cannot drop below 1 in this way. The effect of Kavacha and Kundala is lost when Vasavi Shakti is used/Activated.

(Karna) Vasavi Shakti: O' Sun, Abide to Death — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Divine\]  
(Passive/Activation) When Vasavi Shakti is Activated (used), the following effects are permanently applied to Karna-  
1\. The effect of Kavacha and Kundala is permanently lost.  
2\. Karna’s Base Attack (STR) is increased by 25, STR Rank is increased from B to A.  
3\. Karna’s Normal Attacks deal extra damage to Units with ‘Divinity’. For every Rank in Divinity the DU has, damage dealt is increased by 30 (e.g. Divinity Rank C, \+90 damage). If the DU has the ‘Divine’ Attribute but no ‘Divinity’ Skill, then damage dealt is increased by 100\.  
4\. Normal Attacks have a 50% chance of inflicting Burn for 2◈ Turns (instead of 25%).  
5\. Karna’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Combat Process Karna is involved in, his Master’s Health cannot drop below 1 in this way.   
The above 5 are Passive effects of Vasavi Shakti after it has been Activated. Note: ‘Activating’ Vasavi Shakti is different from using the Active effect below this sentence; also it does not cost Master Health to ‘Activate’ it.  
(Active) Can only be used if Vasavi Shakti has been Activated. Cannot be used if Brahmastra Kundala is on Cooldown. Range=3 to 4\. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 3x3 panel area within Range for 5x damage and inflicts Burn for 4◈ Turns to all affected Units. Fire damage. This NP deals extra damage to DUs with ‘Divinity’ as follows-   
Divinity — Rank B to EX: Damage dealt is further increased by 200% (tripled).  
Divinity — Rank E to C: Damage dealt is further increased by 100% (doubled).  
‘Divine’ Attribute, no Divinity: Damage dealt is further increased by 150%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

(Karna) Brahmastra Kundala: O' Brahma, Curse Me — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Country\]  
Cannot be used if Mana Burst (Flames) or Vasavi Shakti is on Cooldown. Range=5. Base Attack (STR) and Base Attack (MAG) combined is used. Hits a 7x7 panel area within Range for 4x damage plus 100, and inflicts Burn for 3◈ Turns to all affected Units. Then, inflicts Def Dwn (B) on all affected Units for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 30%; if NP, 40%. Fire damage. Affected by Magic Resistance. Karna is not affected by this NP if he is within the NP area.   
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns, and Mana Burst (Flames) also enters Cooldown when this NP is used.

Note 2: When Karna uses a NP that deals damage, his Master’s Health loss from him using the NP overwrites the 20 Health loss from when Karna would normally Act/Attack.
