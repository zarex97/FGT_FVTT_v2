(Nemo) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
Storm Border.  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.  
The Move Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Nemo) Storm Border: Zero Sail  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When this Skill is used, Nemo and any number of allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself enters the Storm Border and warps into the Imaginary Numbers Space. Enemy Units within a 3 panel area of Nemo when Zero Sail is activated can attempt to enter the Storm Border as well, roll a twenty-sided die; the enemy Unit only successfully enters if it rolls 18 or higher.  
While within the Storm Border, all Units still take their Turn normally. At the end of any Turn, Nemo can choose to resurface the Storm Border, which places all Units within the Storm Border onto a 5x5 panel area on the board excluding enemy Home Bases, in any orientation. The maximum distance at which Nemo can travel depends on how much time was spent in Imaginary Numbers Space. This distance is 2+X panels, where X=1 for every ⅓◈ Turns spent within Imaginary Numbers Space (e.g. 1◈ Turns spent in Imaginary Numbers Space, Nemo can travel 2+3=5 panels).   
The maximum time the Storm Border can spend within Imaginary Numbers Space is 2◈ Turns, Nemo is forced to resurface after 2◈ Turns have passed since using Zero Sail.   
If Nemo is defeated while Zero Sail is Active, he performs a Luck Check before dying. If successful, the Storm Border immediately resurfaces (but he is still defeated); if failed, all Units within the Storm Border are inflicted with Erase.  
Units within the Storm Border cannot use Skills, NP, or any other ability that creates a Unit/Item/object that has the ‘Large’ or ‘Giant’ Attribute.   
Cooldown: 5◈ Turns after Nemo resurfaces.

(Nemo) Quickfire  
Attack Skill. Range=3. Roll 6 six-sided die, this Attack Skill deals 25 STR damage for each die that rolls X or higher, where X=5. The value of X is modified as follows.  
Plus 1 if-  
1\. The enemy Unit Evades (instead of performing an Evade roll)  
Minus 1 if-  
1\. This Attack Skill is used at a Range of 2 or lower.  
2\. The enemy Unit is inflicted with Slow, Immobilize or Stun.  
3\. The enemy Unit’s Agility is equal or lower than Nemo’s.  
After performing this Attack Skill, if the enemy Unit does not perform a Counter on Nemo, reduce the Cooldown of Quickfire by 1◈ Turns.  
A DU damaged by this Attack Skill only performs an Injury Roll once, regardless of number of hits received. Damage of this Attack Skill is not affected by damaging modifying effects on Nemo.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Nemo) Triton’s Conch  
Attack Skill. Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 5x5 panel area around Nemo. Deals 1.5x damage to Units directly next to Nemo, while Units at a 2 panel distance receive 0.5x damage. Then, inflicts the ‘Deafen’ debuff to all affected Units for 1◈ Turn, all Evade rolls are increased by 2, MOV is reduced by 1, and Detect is reduced by 1 panel.  
If the Unit was 2 panels away from Nemo, the chance of being inflicted with Deafen is 50% instead.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.

(Nemo) Barrel Bombing  
Attack Skill. Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Nemo for 150 Fire damage and inflicts Burn for 2◈ Turns. Damage of this Attack Skill is not affected by damaging modifying effects on Nemo. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Nemo) Great Ram Nautilus: I Conquer, with Nautilus' Great Naval Ram — Rank: A (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
Range=7. Base Attack (STR) is used. When this NP is used, first apply the following effects to Nemo-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for this Turn to Nemo, NP damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
2\. If Nemo is within a ‘Waterside’ or ‘Imaginary Numbers Space’ area, apply Aim to himself for this Turn.  
3\. If Nemo is within a ‘Waterside’ or ‘Imaginary Numbers Space’ area, apply NP DmUp to himself for this Turn, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%.  
Then, deals 4x damage. If the DU has the ‘Large’ Attribute, damage dealt is further increased by 150%.  
Cooldown: 7◈ Turns.
