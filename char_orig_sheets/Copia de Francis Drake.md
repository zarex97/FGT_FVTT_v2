(Drake) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: D  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to D is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 20%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Drake) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The Move Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Drake) Voyager of the Storm — Rank: A+  
(Passive) Sails on the ‘Golden Hind’ (see below).  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel range of Drake; or if the Golden Hind is boarded, affects all allied Units upon the Golden Hind. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%; and applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 20%. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Drake) Pioneer of the Stars — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Reduces Drake’s NP Cooldown by 1◈+⅔◈ Turns; then applies Pierce for 1◈+½◈ Turns; and applies S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 10%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Drake) Blazing Golden Rule — Rank: A  
(Passive) Whenever this Unit performs a Crit, reduce its NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 20%.  
3\. Applies Ignore Def for 1◈ Turns.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Drake) Golden Hind: Wild Hunt — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Non-damaging/Passive/Activation) When the Golden Hind is activated, place the ‘Golden Hind’ token on the field, then place Drake upon it. The Golden Hind’s token size is 12 panels (4x3 panels). The Golden Hind’s Stats are as follows-  
Health: 2500  
Agility: 10 (Only performs Injury Roll when damaged by NP)  
Luck: Shared with Drake  
MOV: 6  
Range: 5 panels, 1 target  
Base Attack (MAG): 200  
Attributes: Large, Mechanical  
Detect: 4 panels  
The Golden Hind can Move onto occupied panels (when it does, place it on top of the figurines). Other allied Units can board the Golden Hind. Including Drake, it can hold a maximum of nine Units.   
At the end of every ~~Round/1◈ Turns~~ full Round Golden Hind is Active, Drake’s Master loses 50 Health. The Golden Hind is forcefully deactivated at the end of the Round if Drake’s Master has 50 Health or less; her Master does not lose Health at the end of the Round in this situation. This effect overwrites the normal Master Health loss when a Servant uses its NP.  
Enemy Units cannot target Units onboard the Golden Hind for Attacks/Skills/Spells/Noble Phantasms. AoE Attacks and Noble Phantasms deal full damage to the Golden Hind, and 50% Total Damage to all Units upon it; while Masters onboard the QAR take no damage and effects from those.  
Enemy Units on the ground can only Attack the Golden Hind with ranged Attacks, and allied Units on the Golden Hind can only Attack enemy Units on the ground with ranged Attacks. Units onboard the Golden Hind cannot Attack Units directly below the Golden Hind with ranged Attacks and vice versa.  
Drake’s Normal Attacks are replaced with Attacks from the Golden Hind.  
If an enemy Unit attempts to board the Golden Hind, it rolls a ten-sided die. The enemy unit successfully boards the Golden Hind if a 10 is rolled. When the Golden Hind is destroyed/deactivated, all Units onboard it are randomly scattered below it. The Golden Hind cannot be affected by buffs and/or debuffs.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns after the Golden Hind is destroyed/deactivated. Drake can deactivate the Golden Hind during her Turn, or at the start or end of any Turn or Round.

(Drake) Golden Wild Hunt: The Night of the Golden Hind and the Storm — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
The Golden Hind’s Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 7x3 or 3x7 panel area in the direction the Golden Hind is facing (i.e. where the ship’s bow is facing) for 4x damage plus 100\. Additionally, damage dealt by the Golden Wild Hunt is affected by the state of Drake’s Golden Rule Skill, as follows-  
1\. Not activated and not on Cooldown: No change  
2\. Activated, before ⅓◈ Turns have passed: Damage dealt increased by 30%.  
3\. Activated, after ⅓◈ Turns have passed: Damage dealt increased by 20%.  
4\. Activated, after ⅔◈ Turns have passed: Damage dealt increased by 10%.  
5\. On Cooldown: Total Damage dealt is reduced by 15%.  
Note: Can be used even if the Golden Hind isn't present/activated; in this case the Range is still the same, just applied to Drake. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.
