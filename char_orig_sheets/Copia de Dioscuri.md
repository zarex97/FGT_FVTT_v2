**Dioscuri**

Castor and Pollux are summoned as two separate Servants as one. Castor and Pollux have their individual stats; however, the maximum distance between the two is 2 panels between them. If either one is defeated, the other one is also defeated as well regardless of remaining Health. Both Castor and Pollux are respectively allowed to Move and Attack once during their Turn; each one counts as 0.5 Units.

When calculating whether the damage dealt by either one of them needs to be reduced if he/she is too far from their Master, as long as the other counterpart is within their Master’s ZON, damage dealt is not reduced.

When either Castor or Pollux uses a Skill, the Skill enters Cooldown for both of them.

(Castor) Class Skill: Avenger — Rank: B  
(Passive) All damage taken by Castor is increased by 80 including NP. If Castor Counters after receiving an Attack, increase the damage dealt when Countering by 80 including NP.

(Castor) Class Skill: Oblivion Correction — Rank: C  
(Passive) Crit Chance is increased by 15%.

(Castor) Class Skill: Self-Replenishment (Mana) — Rank: D  
(Passive) At the end of Castor’s Turn and at the end of any Turn he Acts, his Health is restored by 40 and NP Cooldown is reduced by 2 Turns.

(Castor) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: B-  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. Castor’s Master loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn he Acts; when its Health is 20 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.   
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 35%; if NP, 15%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 55%; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and Castor’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. Castor’s Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns if his Master is defeated while ME is Active.  
6\. While ME is Active, Castor can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and cannot be removed except by its own effect/duration.  
Cooldown: 2◈ Turns.  
 If Castor is directly next to Pollux while Mad Enhancement is Active, his Master’s Health lost from the effects of active Mad Enhancement are halved.

(Castor) Twin God’s Divine Core — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 80 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Castor performs a successful Normal Attack, he has a 5% chance of reducing his NP Cooldown by 1 Turn at the end of the Damage Step.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Castor) Stars of the Chief God — Rank: A  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Castor’ and ‘Pollux’ buffs on himself and Pollux for 1◈ Turns. Has the following effects-  
1\. ‘Pollux’ buff: When the affected Unit performs a Normal Attack that does not Crit, apply S.Crit Up for ⅓◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself (and Pollux if she is out of the Skill’s Range), Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
2\.  ‘Castor’ buff: When the affected Unit performs a Normal Attack that is a Crit, reduce the Unit’s NP Cooldown by 1 Turn.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Castor) Guardians of Navigation — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of himself (and Pollux if she is out of the Skill’s Range). Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies NP DmUp for ⅓◈ Turns, NP damage dealt is increased by 15%.  
2\. Applies Atk Up for ⅓◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 15%; if NP, 5%.  
3\. Applies Debuff Immune for 1◈ Turns, 1 time, the next debuff inflicted on the affected Unit is negated, does not affect Instakill, Death, and Erase.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.

(Castor) Mana Burst (Light/Ancient) — Rank: A+  
(Active) This Skill is used when performing a Normal Attack. First, either restore 2 Agility and 2 Luck to Castor; or restore 1 Agility and 1 Luck to both Castor and Pollux. Then, for that Combat Process, the Base Attack used is Castor’s BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=300). Not affected by Magic Resistance. Light damage (half). This Attack has a 50% chance of inflicting Blind on the DU for 1◈ Turns. Then, reduce Castor’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Pollux) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
When Castor is directly next to Pollux, Castor also receives the effect of this Skill.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Pollux) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.
