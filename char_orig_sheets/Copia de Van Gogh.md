(Gogh) Class Skill: Existence Outside The Domain — Rank: A  
(Passive) Has the following effects on Van Gogh-  
1\. Chance of being inflicted by debuffs including Instakill is reduced by 25%, this effect is halved for Death (10%) and further halved for Erase (5%).  
2\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is reduced by 40% including NP; all damage dealt to Units with the ‘Mad Enhancement’ Skill is increased by 40% including NP.  
3\. All damage received from Units with the ‘Existence Outside The Domain’ Skill or the ‘Outsider’ Attribute is increased by 40% including NP.  
4\. Crit Chance is increased by 15%.  
5\. When a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement Attacks this Unit, the damage boosting effect of Mad Enhancement is negated. When this Unit Attacks a Unit with Active Mad Enhancement, the damage reducing effect of Mad Enhancement is negated.

(Gogh) Class Skill: Item Construction — Rank: B-  
This Skill affects all allied Units within a 2 panel area of this Unit.  
(Passive 1\) The chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 35%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
(Passive 2\) The chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 35%; this effect is halved for Instakill (15%), and further halved for Death (5%).  
Note: If a Unit is affected by multiple instances of this Skill, only the Item Construction with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Item Construction does not stack).

(Gogh) Divinity — Rank: B+  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 45 including NP.

(Gogh) Insanity — Rank: C  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 6% including NP.

(Gogh) Sunflower’s Curse — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) Gogh cannot be ordered to commit suicide/kill herself, even with a Command Spell.  
(Passive 2\) Gogh’s Health cannot drop below 1 due to the effects of Curse (Gogh cannot be defeated due to the effects of Curse).

(Gogh) Imaginary Numbers Arts — Rank: B+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Guts to self for 1◈+⅔◈ Turns, when Gogh is defeated she is revived with 20% of her Max Health.  
2\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Curse on herself, 3 times.  
3\. Reduce Gogh’s NP Cooldown by X Turns, where X=(⅓◈\*the stage of the Curse debuff on Gogh; e.g. Gogh has Stage 7 Curse, so NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈+⅓◈ Turns).  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns.

(Gogh) Het Gele Huis — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Affects all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Gogh. Has the following effects-  
1\. Has a 150% chance of inflicting Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 10%.  
2\. Inflicts Def Dwn (C) for 1◈ Turns, all damage taken is increased by 20%, and Agility is reduced by 1 whenever this Unit is successfully Attacked.  
Then, applies the following effects to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself-  
1\. Applies Evade for 1◈ Turns, 1 time.  
2\. Applies Regen for 1◈+½◈ Turns, Health is restored by 5% of its maximum value at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of a Turn the Unit Acts, and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Curse on all affected allied Units.  
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns. Cannot be used if ‘The Yellow House’ is on Cooldown (see below).

(Gogh) Channel Marker Soul — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) Curse Damage taken is halved.  
(Passive 2\) Whenever Gogh is inflicted with Curse or has Curse removed from herself to the effects of the ‘Gogh’ buff, reduce her NP Cooldown by 1 Turn for every Stage of Curse inflicted/removed in this way. 

(Gogh) Shadow of Longing Falls Upon the Water in the Urn — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Used on an allied Unit within a 2 panel area of herself. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 60%.  
Then, applies the following effects to Gogh-  
1\. Apply the ‘Gogh’ buff to herself for 1◈ Turns, has the following effect- whenever Gogh performs a successful Attack, remove one stage of Curse from Gogh and apply Atk Up to herself for 1◈ Turns if a stage of Curse was removed, all damage dealt is increased by 10%; if NP, 5%. If the Attack was a Crit, remove 2 stages of Curse and apply the Atk Up buff twice. Does not stack, but reset its duration if used while she already has a ‘Gogh’ buff.  
2\. Remove all Curse debuffs from **all** Units within a 3 panel area of Gogh, then apply them to herself (apply all stages of Curse accordingly, if any affected Unit has more than one stage of Curse).  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Gogh) De Sterrennacht: The Starry Night — Rank: EX (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]  
(Non-damaging) Used during your Turn. First, inflicts Terror for 1◈ Turns to all enemy Units within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to Gogh, chance of being inflicted with Stun from Terror is 60% (60% Terror). Then, apply the following effects to all allied Units within a 3 panel area of Gogh-  
1\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns again to all affected allied Units with the ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 100%.  
3\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by X0%; if NP, the value is halved. X=(3+the number of affected allied Units with the ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill excluding herself).  
4\. Applies Area CritUp for 1◈ Turns to Gogh, Crit Chance of all allied Units within a 2 panel area of the Unit with this buff is increased by 10%.  
Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.

2\. Applies Regen for 3◈ Turns, Health is restored by 5% of its maximum value at the end of the Unit’s Turn, at the end of a Turn the Unit Acts. and at the end of the Round.  
3\. Has a 500% chance of inflicting Curse twice on all affected allied Units.  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns. Cannot be used if ‘Het Gele Huis’ is on Cooldown (see above).
