(Medusa) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: A+  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 5 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 3◈-⅓◈ Turns  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied. 

(Medusa) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to B is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 40%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Medusa) Divinity — Rank: E-  
(Passive) All damage dealt is increased by 5 including NP.

(Medusa) Independent Action — Rank: C  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (6◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 2 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.  
(Passive 4\) Every time Medusa kills a Civilian when she is a Free Servant, increase her Sustainability by 1◈ Turns.

(Medusa) Monstrous Strength — Rank: B  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 80%; if NP, 40%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Monstrous Snake Metamorphosis.

(Medusa) Monstrous Snake Metamorphosis — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-  
1\. Applies Atk Up (STR) for 1◈ Turns, STR Damage dealt is increased by 30%; if NP, 15%.  
2\. Applies Crit DmUp for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage dealt is increased by 30%.  
3\. Applies Dmg Up (Bind) for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt to Units inflicted with Bind effects is increased by 50%.  
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Monstrous Strength.

(Medusa) Mystic Eyes — Rank: A+  
(Active) Mystic Eyes of Petrification: Cybele. Used during your Turn. Range=2 panels. Used on an enemy Unit. Cannot be used on a Unit if there is an obstacle/obstruction between Medusa and the target Unit, and can only be used if Medusa is facing the targeted Unit.  
(Example: Unit \[Cannot be targeted\]-Unit \[Can be targeted\]-Medusa) Has different effects depending on who it is used on.  
Humans (including Masters): Perform an Agility Check. If Successful, the DU is Stunned for 2◈ Turns. If Failed, the DU is Petrified.  
Non-humans, Servants with MAG of Rank C or lower: Perform an Agility Check. If Successful, reduce the DU’s Agility by 2\. If Failed, roll again. On the second time, if Successful, the DU is Stunned for 1◈ Turns. If Failed, the DU is Petrified.  
Servants with MAG of Rank B or higher: Perform an Agility Check. If Successful, reduce the DU’s Agility by 2\. If Failed, the DU is Stunned for 1◈ Turns.  
Debuffs inflicted by this Skill ignore the DU’s debuff resistance due to Magic Resistance.   
Cooldown: 4◈-⅓◈ Turns

(Medusa) Blood Temple — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅔◈ Turns and apply NP Regen for 1◈ Turns, NP Cooldown is reduced by 1 Turn at the end of every Turn. If Blood Fort Andromeda is Active, NP Cooldown is reduced by 2◈ Turns instead. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Medusa) Blood Fort Andromeda: Outer-Seal, Blood Temple — Rank: B (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
(Passive) To use this Noble Phantasm, Medusa has to first Mark the four corner panels of a 5x5, 7x7, or 9x9 panel area, these Marks will be known as Bloodmarks. Using the ‘Mark’ Action places a Bloodmark on the panel Medusa is standing on, and counts as her Attack for the Turn. Bloodmarks can be placed on any panel, even within enemy Home Bases.  
If all four Bloodmarks are complete, Bloodfort Andromeda is activated. Only Masters can destroy a Bloodmark, and it is done by simply Attacking it, Bloodmarks can only be seen from a distance of 3 cells Maximum.  
When Bloodfort Andromeda is activated, it is continuously Active until Medusa is defeated. This NP constantly affects all Units within its area excluding Medusa and her Master, and has different effects depending on the Unit within it-  
Normal Human: Immediately dies. Either Medusa or her Master heals 100 Health and 1 Agility.  
Master, non-normal Human: Loses 40 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts within Bloodfort Andromeda. The total Health lost from all affected victims is used to heal either or both Medusa and her Master (total amount healed between the two cannot exceed the amount of Health drained from victims).  
Servant, non-Human: Loses 20 Health at the end of every Turn it Acts within Bloodfort Andromeda. The total Health lost from all affected victims is used to heal either or both Medusa and her Master (total amount healed between the two cannot exceed the amount of Health drained from victims).  
The effects of this NP is halved against Units with the ‘Mechanical’ Attribute.  
There can only be one activated Bloodfort Andromeda area at any time. Whenever Bloodfort Andromeda is complete (Activated), all other Bloodmarks will vanish. Medusa cannot place new Bloodmarks while Bloodfort Andromeda is Active.

(Medusa) Bellerophon: Bridle of Chivalry \- Pegasus — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Base Attack (MAG) is used. Hits a 1x13 or 13x1 panel area in a straight line in any direction (including diagonal) next to Medusa for 4x damage plus 100\. Then, applies Crit Up for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 30%. Not affected by Magic Resistance. Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns.  
Note: Hits in both directions (Front and back); but only hits in one direction (Front) if playing on the Large Board.
