(Arturia) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.  
(Passive 3\) When Arturia receives MAG damage from an Attack with the ‘Anti-Dragon’ Attribute, Magic Resistance only negates damage from a MAG Rank of up to C, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 25%. Also affects NP.

(Arturia) Class Skill: Riding — Rank: B  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Increases MOV by 4 panels for this Turn. Additionally, ‘Double Move’, ‘Riding Attack’, and ‘Passenger Seat’ can be used on this Turn. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns  
(Passive 1\) Double Move: Can Move twice during its Turn, once before and once after Attacking, total distance Moved cannot exceed the Unit's MOV.  
(Passive 2\) Riding Attack: Can Attack all Units in its path while Moving in a straight line as its Normal Attack during its Turn. Cannot Attack or Move after it has stopped. Can be combined with Passenger Seat.  
If the Unit has already Moved during its Turn and intends to use Riding Attack, the number of panels it can Move for its Riding Attack is equal to its MOV minus the number of panels it has already Moved.  
(Passive 3\) Passenger Seat: The Servant's Master can Move together with its Servant; after Moving, both Servant and Master must be in the same orientation/position prior to the Move. Counts as only Moving one Unit. Can be combined with Riding Attack.  
The MOV Up effect from Riding’s Active usage is not a buff and cannot be removed by buff removal effects, or prevented by an effect that prevents buffs from being applied.

(Arturia) Knight of Utopia — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Has the following effects-

2\. Applies Atk Up for 1◈ Turns to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of herself with the ‘Round Table’ Attribute, all damage dealt is increased by 20%; if NP, 10%.

4\. Applies the ‘Utopia’ buff for 1◈ Turns to herself, whenever Arturia performs a successful Attack, NP Cooldown is reduced by ⅓◈ Turns for every Unit successfully hit by that Attack.   
Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.  
Note: This Skill is categorized as ‘Charisma’.

(Arturia) Instinct — Rank: A

(Passive 2\) Crit Chance is increased by 10%.  
This Skill is negated while inflicted with Skill Seal.

1\. Reduce Arturia’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns.  
2\. Apply S.Crit Up to all allied Units within a 2 panel area of Arturia for ⅓◈ Turns, Crit Chance is increased by 15%.

Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Arturia) Mana Burst — Rank: A  
(Active) This Skill is used when Arturia performs a Normal Attack. For that Combat Process, Arturia’s Base Attack used is her BA(STR) and BA(MAG) combined (BA=375); and for that Combat Phase, Arturia’s Evade Rolls are reduced by 2\. Not affected by Magic Resistance.  
Can be used while on Cooldown, but she has to be within her Master’s Servant Range and her Master loses 20 Health; using it in this way does not reset Cooldown.  
Cooldown: 3◈ Turns. Cannot be used on the same Turn as Dragon’s Reactor Core.

(Arturia) Avalon: The Everdistant Utopia — Rank: EX (NP) \[Barrier\]  
(Non-damaging) When Arturia is summoned, roll an eight-sided die. If an 8 is rolled, she is summoned with the \[Avalon\] Item. Can be used in two different methods-  
1\. Can only be used 8 full Rounds after the start of the game (i.e. from the 9th Round onwards). Used when Arturia or an allied Unit directly next to Arturia is Attacked. Negate all damage and effects of that Attack including NP, on the Unit who was Attacked. If it was an AoE Attack, this NP affects all Units within a 1 panel area of Arturia. Using this NP does not reduce her Master’s Health as a cost. Cooldown: 8◈ Turns.  
2\. Used on her Master, when Arturia is directly next to her Master and they are both in their Home Base. Arturia places \[Avalon\] inside her Master (this counts as Arturia giving the \[Avalon\] Item to her Master); both Arturia and her Master cannot Attack on the same Turn, and cannot Move after Avalon is used in this way. The same process is used for Arturia to reobtain \[Avalon\] from inside her Master. While Avalon is inside Arturia’s Master, it has the following effects-  
a. Max Health is increased by 100; Max Luck is increased by 2, and Base Attack (STR & MAG) is increased by 50\.  
b. If Arturia is at most 3 panels away from her Master- At the end of her Master’s Turn, at the end of any Turn her Master Acts, and at the end of a Damage Step whenever her Master takes damage, its Health is restored by 20% of its maximum value and Agility is restored by 2\. If Arturia is more than 3 panels away- At the end of her Master’s Turn, at the end of any Turn her Master Acts, and and at the end of a Damage Step whenever her Master takes damage, its Health is restored by 10% of its maximum value and Agility is restored by 1\.  
c. If Arturia’s Master would be defeated while its Health is at 10% of its maximum value or higher, it is not defeated and its Health drops to 1 instead.  
d. Arturia’s Master is immune to Death and Erase.  
e. Arturia’s Master cannot be Overpowered.  
\[Avalon\] cannot be Stolen or dropped. If Arturia is defeated while \[Avalon\] is inside her Master, her Master keeps it and still gains its effects; treat the effects as if Arturia was more than 3 panels away from her Master, but reduce the condition of effect (c) to ‘...defeated while its Health is at 5% of its maximum value or higher…’.  
If Arturia’s Master has \[Avalon\] inside him/her, he can give \[Avalon\] to another allied Master (uses the same process as Arturia placing Avalon inside her Master). If \[Avalon\] is inside any Master other than Arturia’s Master, Health Regen from effect (b) is reduced to 5%, and condition of effect (c) is reduced to 5% (see above).

While \[Avalon\] is inside a Master, it can use it in the following manner-
