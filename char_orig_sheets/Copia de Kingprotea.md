(Kingprotea) Alter Ego  
(Passive) All damage dealt to Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is increased by 50% including NP. All damage received from Units with the ‘Outsider’ Attribute and/or ‘Existence Outside the Domain’ Skill is reduced by 50% including NP.

(Kingprotea) Class Skill: Mad Enhancement — Rank: A+  
(Active) Used during your Turn. When Mad Enhancement is Activated, it can only be deactivated 2◈ Turns after it was activated and vice versa. Has the following effects while Active-  
1\. This Servant’s Master loses 25 Health at the end of every Turn its Acts; when its Master’s Health is 25 or less, ME is forcibly deactivated.  
2\. All damage taken is reduced by 55%; if NP, 25%.  
3\. All damage dealt is increased by 85% including NP; this effect is halved for Attacks which use Base Attack (MAG) (40%).  
4\. MOV is increased by 2, Range is increased by 1, and this Servant’s Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
5\. This Servant’s Sustainability is reduced by 2◈ Turns if its Master is defeated while ME is Active.  
6\. While ME is Active, this Servant can only Evade with Evade-.  
7\. The effects of Mad Enhancement are neither a buff or a debuff, and are Unremovable. 

(Kingprotea) Class Skill: Territory Creation — Rank: EX  
(Passive 1\) When this Unit is in its Home Base, all damage dealt by it is increased by 6d20 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) While this Unit is on the field, all damage taken by allied Units who are in their Home Base is further reduced by (3d10+30) including NP.  
Note: If there are multiple allied Units with this Skill, only the Territory Creation with the highest Rank takes effect (i.e. Territory Creation does not stack).

(Kingprotea) Class Skill: Independent Action — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) Has a high Sustainability (7◈ Turns).  
(Passive 2\) Master’s ZON is increased by 2 panels.  
(Passive 3\) Requires 3 successful ‘Contract Servant’ rolls to be contracted by enemy Masters and Casters.

(Kingprotea) Goddess’s Divine Core — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) All damage dealt is increased by 100 including NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is decreased by 30%.  
This Skill counts as ‘Divinity’.

(Kingprotea) Self-Suggestion — Rank: EX  
(Passive) Chance of being inflicted with nvDebuffs is reduced by 60%.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Removes all debuffs from Kingprotea, then applies nvDebuff ResUp for 1◈ Turns, chance of being inflicted by non-volatile debuffs is further reduced by 60%. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kingprotea) Huge Scale — Rank: B  
(Passive 1\) With the ‘Giant’ Attribute, Kingprotea can Move to any panel regardless of whether it is occupied or not; if the panel(s) is(are) occupied, all Units occupying said panels will be knocked back by 1 panel until Kingprotea has space to stand on.  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Applies the ‘Endless Proliferation’ buff to herself for 3◈+⅓◈ Turns and reduce her NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. The effect of Endless Proliferation is as follows-  
At the end of the Turn after every ⅓◈ Turns, Kingprotea gains 1 Proliferation stock. The effect of Proliferation is as follows-  
For each Proliferation stock Kingprotea gains, Max and current Health is increased by 20% of Kingprotea’s original Max Health.  
For every 3 Proliferation stocks Kingprotea has, Kingprotea’s size will grow by 1 panel (scaled up from 1x1 panels to 2x2 panels, from 2x2 panels to 3x3 panels and so on). Each time Kingprotea grows, her Range is increased by 1 panel and her MOV is decreased by 1\.   
Kingprotea can only have a maximum of 10 Proliferation stocks. Proliferation stocks are not lost when Endless Proliferation ends. Each Proliferation stock counts as a separate buff. When Kingprotea loses Proliferation stocks, their effects are immediately lost.  
(Passive 2\) For every Endless Proliferation stock Kingprotea has, NP damage received is reduced by 10%. (maximum cap of this effect is 80%).  
(Passive 3\) If Kingprotea has 1 Proliferation stock, the chance of buffs being removed from herself is reduced by 35%. For every additional Endless Proliferation stock Kingprotea has, the magnitude is increased by 5%.  
Cooldown: 6◈ Turns

(Kingprotea) Infantile Regression — Rank: C  
(Active) Used during your Turn. Removes the ‘Endless Proliferation’ buff and all Proliferation stocks from Kingprotea. Then, reduce Kingprotea’s NP Cooldown by ⅓◈ Turns x number of Proliferation stocks removed in this way. Ignores effects that prevent buffs from being removed. Then reduce the Cooldown of Kingprotea’s Skills by 1◈ excluding this Skill. Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Kingprotea) Monstrous Strength — Rank: EX  
(Active) Used at the start of a Damage Step when performing an Attack. STR Damage dealt by that Attack is increased by 150%; if NP, 75%.  Cooldown: 4◈ Turns.

(Kingprotea) Giant Monster of the Great River — Rank: B  
(Passive) Whenever Kingprotea uses the ‘Monstrous Strength’ Skill, apply NP DmUp (GAO) to herself X times, where X=the number of Proliferation Stocks she has. The effect of NP DmUp (GAO) is as follows- NP Damage dealt is increased by 10%; at the end of this Unit’s Turn except the Turn this Skill was activated and at the end of any Turn she Acts, remove one ‘NP DmUp (GAO)’ buff from herself.

(Kingprotea) Earth Mother’s Wail  
Attack Skill. Range+2 for the Combat Process. Deals normal damage which uses Base Attack (MAG). Cooldown: 2◈ Turns

(Kingprotea) Airavata King Size: Colossal Silhouette Emerging From The Ocean of Life — Rank: E (NP) \[Anti-Unit\]

Hits a 3x3 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to herself for 2x damage. Then, apply Atk Up to Kingprotea for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 40%; if NP, 20%. Cooldown: 3◈ Turns.
