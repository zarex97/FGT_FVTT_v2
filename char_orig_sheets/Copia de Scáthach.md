(Scáthach) Class Skill: Magic Resistance — Rank: A  
(Passive 1\) MAG damage from a MAG Rank of up to A is negated, otherwise MAG damage taken is reduced by 50%. Also affects NP.  
(Passive 2\) Chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 25%.  
Also affects Instakill and Death **unless** the Instakill or Death debuffs are from an Attack/Attack Skill/Spell/NP that deals STR damage or that is not affected by Magic Resistance. Erase is completely unaffected.  
Note: Other debuffs from Attacks/Attack Skills/Spells/NPs that deal STR damage or are ‘not affected by Magic Resistance’ will still have their inflict chance reduced from Magic Resistance.

(Active) Used during your Turn. Gain 2 PRS Tokens. Then, the Skill can be used in two different methods, as follows-

1\. Atk Up for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is increased by 25%; if NP, 15%.

1\. Atk Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage dealt is reduced by 25%, if NP; 15%.  
2\. Def Dwn for 1◈ Turns, all damage received is increased by 25%.  
3\. Crit Dwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Chance is reduced by 25%.  
4\. Crit DmDwn for 1◈ Turns, Crit Damage is reduced by 25%.

8\. Your choice of any of the above effect(s).  
If a duplicate number is rolled, apply the effect twice. Cooldown: 4◈ Turns

(Passive 2\) When one of Scáthach’s Primordial Rune Spells are used, the other two cannot be used until Cooldown has ended for the used Spell.

(Scáthach) Gate of Skye: Gate to the Magical Realm Brimming with Death — Rank: A+ (NP) \[Anti-Army\]  
Affects all of Scáthach’s targets of choice within a 5x5 panel area in any non-diagonal direction next to her. All targeted Units perform a Luck Check; if their MAG is Rank B, reduce the value rolled by 2; if their MAG is Rank A, reduce the value rolled by 4\.  
If the targeted Unit’s Luck Check fails, it is inflicted with Death. If the Unit’s Luck Check Succeeds, it receives 4x damage plus 100 (uses Scáthach’s Base Attack (MAG)).  
Note: Masters can be targeted regardless of their distance from their Servant. If a Servant fails to shove their targeted Master out of the NP area, the effect of the NP is applied to the Master (Servant unable to interfere).  
Cooldown: 7◈+⅓◈ Turns. Cannot be used if Primordial Rune, Wisdom of Dún Scáith, and/or Gáe Bolg Alternative are on Cooldown; when this NP is used, Primordial Rune and Wisdom of Dún Scáith enters Cooldown.

Items held/extra notes:

Skills that cannot be copied by Wisdom of Dún Scáith-  
Natural Body, ‘Mystic Eye’ Skills, ‘Mana Burst’ Skills, Kishu no Ma

Basically, Skills that a Servant is physically born with (e.g. Natural Body) or are part of their physical bodies (e.g. Mystic Eyes); and Skills that are extremely unique to a Servant (e.g. Affections of the Holy Grail) cannot be copied.

Note: When the GM gives the list of Skills to Scáthach, the GM could just give the list of Skill names, or with summarized effects, or with full effects.
