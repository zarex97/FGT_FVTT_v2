Before starting, first, each Player/Faction selects one Master Essences out of the available choices. The Master Essences impart different enhancements on their respective Servants. Masters are divided into four Ranks, and the limitations on selections are as follows-

Rank A Master (Master’s Base Attack (MAG)=125.) (ZON+1 panel, Servant Sustainability+1◈ Turns.) Only 1 allowed.   
Formalcraft: Servant Max Luck \+4.  
Imaginary Around: Servant Max Agility+4.  
Limited/Zero Over: Servant Max Health+200.  
Kaleidoscope: Servant NP is usable 4 Rounds earlier (Round 2 onwards).  
Heaven’s Feel: Servant NP deals 200% extra damage. 1 use only, Player decides when it’s used.  
   
Rank B Master (Master’s Base Attack (MAG)=125.) (ZON+1 panel, Servant Sustainability+1◈ Turns.) Only 2 allowed.  
Steel Training: All damage taken by Servant is reduced by 20%; if NP, 15%.  
Primeval Magic: Servant’s chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 20%. Does not affect Instakill, Death, & Erase.  
Projection: Servant Max Luck \+3.  
Gandr: Servant Max Agility \+3.  
Verdant Destruction: Servant Max Health+150.  
Antumbra: Servant’s Normal Attacks/Skills/Spells deal 20% extra damage.  
Elegance: Servant Crit Chance is increased by 20% (Base Crit Chance=70%). Does not affect NP.  
Imaginary Number: Servant NP is usable 3 Rounds earlier (Round 3 onwards).  
Divine Banquet: Reduces Servant’s NP Cooldown by 3◈ Turns. 1 use only, player decides when it’s used.  
Angel’s Song: Servant NP deals 150% extra damage. 1 use only, Player decides when it’s used.

Rank A and Rank B Masters are allowed to add a \+ to one of their respective Servant’s Parameters.  
   
Rank C Master (Master’s Base Attack (MAG)=100.) Only 2 allowed.  
Azoth Blade: All damage taken by Servant is reduced by 15%; if NP, 10%  
False Attendant: Servant’s chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 15%. Does not affect Instakill, Death, & Erase.  
Black Keys (B): Servant Max Luck \+2.  
Black Keys (G): Servant Max Agility \+2.  
Black Keys (R): Servant Max Health+100.  
Pendant: Servant’s Normal Attacks/Skills/Spells deal 15% extra damage.  
Grimoire: Servant Crit Chance is increased by 10% (Base Crit Chance=65%). Does not affect NP.  
Leyline: Servant NP usable 2 Rounds earlier (Round 4 onwards).  
Crystal: Reduces Servant’s NP Cooldown by 2◈ Turns. 1 use only, Player decides when it’s used.  
Dragonkind: Servant NP deals 100% extra damage. 1 use only, Player decides when it’s used.

Rank D Master (Master’s Base Attack (MAG)=100.) Only 2 allowed.  
Tenacity: All damage taken by Servant is reduced by 10%; if NP, 5%.  
Meditation: Servant’s chance of being inflicted by debuffs is reduced by 10%. Does not affect Instakill, Death, & Erase.  
Technique: Servant Max Luck \+1.  
Preemption: Servant Max Agility \+1.  
Destruction: Servant Max Health+50.  
Flash: Servant’s Normal Attacks/Skills/Spells deal 10% extra damage.  
Chance: Servant Crit Chance is increased by 10% (Base Crit Chance=60%). Does not affect NP.  
Harvest: Servant NP usable 1 Round earlier (Round 5 onwards).  
Concentration: Reduces Servant’s NP Cooldown by 1◈ Turns. 1 use only, Player decides when it’s used.  
Oracle: Servant NP deals 50% extra damage. 1 use only, Player decides when it’s used.

The Players may substitute a higher Ranking Master with a lower Ranking Master instead. For example, instead of one Rank A Master and two Rank B Masters, the Player chooses 3 Rank B Masters instead. After selecting their Masters, the Players have to assign their Masters to their respective Servants before making their/any choice of Servant/being randomly given Servants.

Rank A and Rank B Masters are ‘High Rank Masters’, while Rank C and Rank D Masters are ‘Low Rank Masters’.

High Rank Masters: Base Attack (MAG)=125, ZON+1 panel, Servant’s Sustainability+1◈ Turns, and is allowed to add a \+ to one of their respective Servant’s Parameters.  
Low Rank Masters: Base Attack (MAG)=100.

In case of electing to playing without master essences, all masters are set as rankless and they use the Low Rank Master Base Attack (MAG). This is a configurable setting.
