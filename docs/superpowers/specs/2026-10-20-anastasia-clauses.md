# Anastasia (Summer) — the Clause list

*Anastasia & Viy* on her sheet; renamed by reading 9.

**Audit opened 2026-10-20 (#258).** Built by hand, line by line, from
`char_orig_sheets/Copia de Anastasia & Viy.md`. No generated draft was used.

One Clause per rule that can fail on its own. A rider stays with the hit it rides on unless it has a
die or a condition of its own. A Clause stating *"if NP"* needs a Noble Phantasm reading.

**90 Clauses: all `Pressed` or `Observed`, none left to tests** (70 by interface, 14 Observed, 6 in the engine; live, PC
world `fgt2026`, War *Anastasia Audit*, Player1 her side, Player2 the other; the log in Ch. 46 §46.43). Ten defects
the presses and their tests found, all fixed: §46.4-SB to SL.

**She is built, all ten abilities and Soaked** (`packs/_source/servants/anastasia.yml`, Appendix D
§D.27, design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-16-anastasia-design.md`, built 2026-09-16). This is an
audit of what is there: each Clause is held against its reader, then pressed live.

**Built to be reused.** Every piece is on the generic path: her Note is EMIYA's `rangeBanded` Normal
Attack, Independent Action with Viy is a variant of the shared class template, Watermelon reuses
Blind's `Suppress` and EMIYA's `imposed` Evade modifier, Ice Block Launcher's `chancePerPanel` is a
generic per-distance chance, Soaked is an ordinary `status` effect. A fix found here goes into the
shared piece, never into an Anastasia-only branch.

### Already ruled

agree (§46.6) · durations count from the Turn after the Turn of use (#187 reading 5) · *"All Total X
Damage taken is reduced by 50%"* is a Total Damage cut on that element's share at stage 15 (#206
reading 6): Swimsuit! and Soaked (b) · Soaked is consumed when Fire breaks Freeze, no damage dealt
(design R2, user ruling 2026-09-16) · *"Day Round"* is the phase of the panel the Unit stands on
(#185 readings 12–14) · *"NP Cooldown"* naming no NP reaches every NP (#197 reading 6) · Crit Up and
its kin do not reach an NP unless they state a figure for one, Crit Up (Viy) states 20, and Crit
DmUp does not reach an NP (#131) · S.Crit Up is an aura its holder carries, reaching allies within 2
and the holder (#132) · Def Dwn (A) drains 1 Luck once per Attack that successfully Attacks its
bearer, a Block and a hit for 0 included (Van Gogh reading 3, §46.4-OZ) · a phase stating no `when`
runs after the damage; Snegleta's Def Dwn (A) is `beforeDamage` (#191) · an NP's *"Range+N"* is her
Range plus N (§46.4-F) · riders land on a hit, not on hurt, and a debuff an NP inflicts after its
damage lands only when the hit deals damage (#190 reading 9) · an Aim Attack cannot be Evaded or
Dodged (Appendix A §A.3) · Magic Resistance's Instakill clause exempts an Attack that deals STR

### Readings to settle

Asked by grilling, 2026-10-20. Rulings are written here as they land.

**Round 1, 2026-10-20:** readings 2 to 8 ruled as recommended; reading 1 ruled Archer, which opened readings 9 to 11.
**Round 2, 2026-10-20:** readings 9 to 11 ruled as recommended. Every reading is ruled.

1. **Her class** (`SB.class`). The sheet names none, and Appendix D prints a dash. The content gives
   her `servantClasses: [caster]`, its comment calling it *"what her opponents would call her"*. Four
   readers treat that as the Caster class: she may contract enemy Servants and takes a Contract when
   she kills a Master (`rules/contract.mjs:233`, `:278`), she is `Magus` (`domain/attributes.mjs:68`),
   and her Master's ZON starts from the Caster base (`rules/zon.mjs`). **Ruled 2026-10-20, a reading not offered:** she is an **Archer**. The Caster is the other sheet, *Copia de Anastasia.md*; this one is her summer version, to be renamed (reading 9). No Caster powers: no contract, not `Magus`, the Archer ZON base.
2. **Her Region** (`SB.region`). The sheet: *"Region: Russia"*. The content: `[russia, europe]`, so a
   War set in Europe ranks her up as if it were home. The Region graph already makes Russia border
   Europe. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** Russia only.
3. **Crit Up (Viy) and her ranged Normal Attack** (`SE.2.mix`). Crit Up (Viy) reaches *"Attacks which
   use Base Attack (MAG)"*. Her Note: at Range 3 or more she uses *"Base Attack (STR) and 10% of Base
   Attack (MAG) combined"*. The content reads `attack:component:mag`, and the ranged band's component
   is `str`, so it is not reached. The comments in `anastasia-spirit-eyes.yml` and `crit-up-viy.yml`
   claim it is. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** reached. An attack uses BA(MAG) when any of its base sources is MAG, read as a generic option `attack:usesBase:mag`.
4. **Soaked (a) when the Attack carries no Freeze** (`SK.a`, `SK.a.add`). *"When this Unit receives Ice
   damage, it has a 25% chance of being inflicted with Freeze, this is additive to any Freeze chance
   the Attack might have."* The content only raises a Freeze chance the Attack already carries
   (an incoming `ApplicationChance` of −25 on `freeze`). None of her Ice Attacks inflicts Freeze, so
   with her own kit Soaked never Freezes anyone. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** an Attack dealing more than 0 Ice damage to a Soaked Unit rolls Freeze at 25%; one carrying its own Freeze chance rolls once at that chance plus 25. The attacker inflicts it, so its bonuses and the target's resistances apply. Freeze's Round-end Ice damage is not an Attack and rolls nothing.
5. **Snegleta's own hit and Def Dwn (A)'s Luck** (`SN.luck`). *"First, inflict Def Dwn (A) … and Luck is
   reduced by 1 whenever damage is received from an Attack. Then, deals 3.5x damage."* #209 and #239
   made a Def Dwn not hear the Attack that inflicted it, but those Def Dwns came after their hit.
   Here it comes first. The engine raises `attackReceived` after the `beforeDamage` phases, so it
   should drain 1 for the NP's own hit. To be pressed. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** the NP's own hit drains 1 Luck: Def Dwn (A) is on the DU when that damage lands.
6. **Watermelon's Blind and her own resistance** (`WM.a.blind`). *"Anastasia gains Blind."* The applier
   rolls it like any debuff. *Corrected 2026-10-20:* I said it failed 5% of the time; it never
   did, because her Fae Contract is +5 outgoing too and the two cancel, 100 + 5 − 5
   (`skill-path-resistance.test.mjs`). The ruling makes that stated rather than an accident. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** it lands with no roll, as Van Gogh's *"apply"* stages do (reading 6b there).
7. **Which Attacks offer Watermelon's Active** (`WM.a.when`). *"Used at the start of a Combat Phase
   when initiating an Attack."* The content offers it at `combatPhaseStart` on every Attack she
   declares: a Normal Attack, Ice Bucket Challenge, either NP. Whether a Counter opens that window is
   to be pressed. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** offered on any Attack she initiates, never on a Counter.
8. **"For each panel between Anastasia and the DU"** (`IL.ik`). Design R3 read it as the distance (5%
   adjacent, 30% at her reach of 6), from the Dioscuri's *"2 panels between them"*. It was never put to
   you. The other reading is the gap: 0% adjacent, 25% at 6. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** the distance, as built: 5% adjacent, 30% at 6.

9. **Her name and ids** (`SB`). Reading 1 made her the summer form of the Caster on *Copia de
   Anastasia.md*, still unbuilt. You suggested *"Anastasia (Summer)"*. Her ids are `anastasia` and
   `anastasia-*`, and the Caster's sheet has its own *Fae Contract* and *Shvibzik*, so the Caster's
   ids would collide with hers. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** renamed *Anastasia (Summer)*, True Name
   unchanged; the Servant and her ten abilities move to `anastasia-summer` and `anastasia-summer-*`,
   leaving the plain ids to the Caster. Her actor in `fgt2026` is re-imported from the pack.
10. **Fae Contract, one skill at two Ranks** (`FC.res`, `FC.inf`). Hers, B+: *"Debuff Resist is
   increased by 5%. Chance of inflicting debuffs is increased by 5%."* The Caster's, A: *"…reduced
   by 10% … increased by 10%. Also affects Instakill, Death, and Erase."* Built as hers alone.
   **Ruled 2026-10-20:** one shared, Rank-keyed `fae-contract`, both rows authored from the two
   sheets; only B+ is pressed now.
11. **The tracker and the audit issue.** #112 lists one *"Anastasia"*. Two sheets now stand behind
   that name, and this audit needs its issue, a sub-issue of #112. **Ruled 2026-10-20:** her line
   becomes *Anastasia (Summer)*, the Caster *Anastasia* joins the sheets with no build, and the audit
   issue is opened with this list as its body.
