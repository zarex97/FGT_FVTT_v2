# Groups: proposed

A group gives every pair of its members the group's preset (spec §2). An explicit pair overrides the group
for that pair, so the Jason–Medea grudge stays 20/75 inside the Argonauts. Members are wiki figures; each
expands to every roster sheet of that figure. Nothing here is in the game until approved.
