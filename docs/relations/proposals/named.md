# Named on a sheet: proposed

A sheet whose own mechanics single out another Servant: the `named` preset (100/100), the strongest tie (spec §2). Found by searching every roster sheet for every other figure's names, then reading each hit: 729 hits, of which lists of affected Servants, skill and NP names (*Excalibur Morgan*, *Alluring Nightingale*, *Vivian's Blessing*), and variants of one figure were set aside. ✱ marks a proposed enemy lean (50/150) where the sheet makes them fight.

**21 to rule · 51 proposed.** “Any version of Cú Chulainn” is expanded to all four Cú figures.
