**Mechanism:** A special rule rides a generic mechanism, and the generic one offers exits the sheet forbids. Quickfire's no-roll Evade advanced as a failed Evade, which leads to Lucky Evasion. Zero Sail is a mode, and any mode's owner may toggle it off. An engine-only counter became a bar button through the generic `classifyUse`, and Possession's `maxUses: 1` bounded the button while its generic revival fired again. Heracles showed the mirror case: a Command Spell's generic suspension let Mad Enhancement out, but the way back only re-armed compulsions and ForceMode, so a never-off mode stayed off. Gilles (Caster) (#323): a form's generic drop of every unnamed ability took the Spellbook out of the gate its Spells read, which 11i keeps.
**Lesson:** When a sheet bends a generic mechanic, list every exit the generic path offers (edges, toggles, prompts, buttons, revivals) and close each one the sheet rules out. Close it in the engine too, not only on the bar: a socket or a macro reaches the engine without the bar's offer (#206). Best of all, put the rule at the one door every path passes: the Dioscuri's linked death rode the damage path alone while six other doors wrote `defeat`, and it now lives in `io.defeat` (#209). The same holds for a knockback's damage heard as "from an Attack", a `consumesUse` spending a whole effect, a self-only short-cut skipping the twin, and a window-only ability pressable on the bar. Kiritsugu's one shot per Attack rode the generic reaction rung, whose attacking path left the used offer standing, pressable again (#243): a used offer leaves at the one door every resolved reaction passes. For every exit, also find its way back: a suspension, a seal or a toggle that lapses must re-arm the special mode it let out.
