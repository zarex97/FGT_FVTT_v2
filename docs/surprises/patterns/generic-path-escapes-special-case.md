**Mechanism:** A special rule rides a generic mechanism, and the generic one offers exits the sheet forbids. Quickfire's no-roll Evade advanced as a failed Evade, which leads to Lucky Evasion. Zero Sail is a mode, and any mode's owner may toggle it off.
**Lesson:** When a sheet overrides a generic mechanic, list every exit the generic path offers (edges, toggles, prompts) and close each one the sheet rules out. Promote to `CLAUDE.md` at a third member.
