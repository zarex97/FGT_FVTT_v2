**Mechanism:** Unrelated traps met while driving the live world. On the VPS, CDP drags reach the canvas but move no token, the full suite outruns a tool call and `pkill -f` matches its own shell. On the PC, a claude-in-chrome drag moves a token only part of the way. On both, the first boundary after a reload is not a typical one to time or trust. Driving two tabs on the PC: a background GM tab never finishes token animations, a canvas that failed to draw has no placeables, Foundry drags every controlled token, and both tabs share `localhost`'s cookie so a reload rejoins as the last user. Builder subagents inherited plan mode and stopped at their plans. A Cloudflare quick tunnel polled the moment it printed its name was cached as "no such name" by every resolver asked, so retries read the cache, not the tunnel.
**Lesson:** Read `docs/agents/vps.md` *Driving the board headless* before driving, and use its recipes. Wait 60 s after a page reload, and confirm each `nextTurn` landed before the next. Drags stay open as a known limit: walk the rest with `TokenDocument#move` from the owner's client. Time several boundaries, never the first after a reload. For two tabs, follow the `two-tab-drive-kit` memory. Confirm plan mode is off before sending builders out.
