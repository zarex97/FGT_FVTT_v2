lesson: For every "first", "then", "if defeated" or "when X" clause, find the key that sets its timing and press the order live; a boundary reads the cycle that ended, not the live clock; for each event ask who hears it and what already exists when it fires; an irreversible step waits for the last gate, and a "while X" reads X at declaration. A control for a setting exists while the setting can still change; anything that waits on a person writes what it shows first, then waits. A clock or a mark is keyed as finely as what it times: a prompt, not its card; an order within a Turn, not the Turn. A predicate on a board annotation is asked after the board is built, never at collection. A span is read at every Turn of it, not at its end; a line is said before what it brings runs. A cost is paid before what it powers. A charge spent by "the next" use is spent only by a use begun while it was held.

**Mechanism:** When something runs is set by a rule written elsewhere, not by where it is written. A phase listed first runs after damage without `when: beforeDamage`. A death check ordered literally runs ahead of the revival chain. A `revival:` predicate is answered at collection unless `DEFERRED_PREFIXES` holds it. "If her Master has 25 or less and would lose it" is a moment, not a state; `turnEnd` is only her own side's Turn; a defeated body stays a Turn. An exception keyed to when charges were stamped rather than when the ability was used; a Range read after the ride had already moved her. A Round's end runs after `combat.round` has moved on, so units projected at the live Round read their ended Round's records as stale. Heracles added marks coarser than what they timed: the prompt clock was kept once per card, so a Counter inherited the reaction's spent time and timed out unseen, and Battle Continuation's "since" compared Turns, so a half-Health crossing in the Turn it fired reopened it. Anastasia (#258) added Soaked's *"at the end of a Day Round"*: `self:phase:` is written by the board pass after the rules are collected, and asked at collection it read false and dropped the handler. Gilles de Rais (Saber) (#260) added a Counter that priced a `perUnit` Cooldown before its phases had summoned the units it counts, so the clock was 0. Gilles (Caster) (#323) added two: a log batch's summons posted before the die line that sent them, and *"for 1 full Round"* read at the Round's last moment, after the Unit in reach had left.
**Lesson:** For each ordering word in a clause, find the key that decides its timing (`when`, `DEFERRED_PREFIXES`, the revival chain, the ladder's `TRANSITIONS`) and press the order on a live board. At a boundary, read every stamped record at the cycle that ended, not at the live clock, which has already moved. For each event, ask who hears it and what already exists when it fires: the Dioscuri's Def Dwn riders were applied before `damageTaken` fired, so they heard the hit that inflicted them; a killing blow's own Health drop forced Mad Enhancement off before the defeat asked; `turnEnd` is heard only by the active side; "the rest of that Turn" authored as ⅓◈ ran through the next tick. An irreversible step (a reveal, a card, a write) waits for the last gate that can still refuse, the late ones included: Range is refused by targeting, after the preflight (#249). A clause "while X" reads X when the act is declared, not when it ends: PC.5 ended a concealment the Attack itself had landed. A control for a setting exists while the setting can still change: the War rules button lived in a panel drawn only after the War started, when its locked rules no longer could. Anything that waits on a person writes what it shows first, then waits: a held rung wrote its card only when the whole run ended, so the offer it held for never appeared. A step that runs before its owner exists reads the previous one: the wizard rolled a Normal war on the last war's ruleset (2026-10-18). Key a clock or a mark as finely as the thing it times: a prompt's clock by the prompt (kind, state, history length, Unit), never by its card; a "since" by an order within the Turn, or by wiping the mark when the ability fires. A predicate on a board annotation is asked after the board is built, never at collection. A cost is paid before what it powers.
