**Mechanism:** An attack that catches several Units opens one Combat Process per defender. An effect that names the whole attack ("Servant NP deals 200% extra damage", "First, apply NP DmUp") placed on one Process reaches one target, or, placed on every Process, lands once per target. Single-target tests cannot tell the two apart.
**Lesson:** Read whom the clause names: the attack, its user or the Noble Phantasm is the declaration's, paid once and carried to every Process; the defender, the hit or the damage taken is the Process's. Put a declaration's effect where the declaration lives (`engine/declaration-window.mjs`, the group-once payments of `attack.mjs`), and press it on an area attack with two targets.
