lesson: A sheet governs its bearer: another Unit's side is its own sheet, and a condition one sheet states lives on that sheet's element, never in the reader others share.

**Mechanism:** A clause about two Units (*"they will only Attack each other"*) was read as authority over the other Unit's behaviour. The user ruled that Arjuna's side is whatever Arjuna's sheet says. Van Gogh (#239) found its mirror: Sikera Ušum's "Units ... who are weak to Poison" was written into the one reader every `VulnerabilityAmplifier` shares, so Channel Marker Soul's halving of Curse never applied.
**Lesson:** Build a sheet's clause for its bearer only. When a clause names another Unit's behaviour, note it as that Unit's to rule and leave it for that audit. When a clause carries a condition ("weak to", "while inside"), put it on the element that clause authors (`ifWeak`), and leave the shared reader unconditional.
