lesson: When code asks about a role (Master, summoner, owner, guard), ask about the relationship that confers it, the contract, never the actor's type or its side.
landed: CLAUDE.md#Lessons
---
**Mechanism:** A role is asked as a document type. "Is this a Master?" was `type === "master"`, so a Servant holding a Contract — Medea after Rule Breaker — was nobody's Master to the Contract, the multi-Servant tax and the freeing on defeat. The type check is right for every Unit the code was written against and silently wrong for the first that fills the role another way.
**Lesson:** Ask the relationship that confers the role: a Master is whoever holds a Contract (`masterId` points at it), a summoner is whoever `summonerId` names. When a new Unit fills an old role, grep every reader of that role for a type test. A side or match membership is the same mistake: a Master's guards and its Command Spells are its contracted Servants, not its faction's (#204). A Servant is what its contract names, not an actor: a legion of a hundred bodies is one Servant, so a grant per Servant is one per legion (#226).
