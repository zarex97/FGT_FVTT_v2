**Mechanism:** One question is answered in two places, and the answers drift. The bar's gate and the declaration's gate, two effect paths each reading a chance, a die rolled at dispatch and again at application, an entry gate reading a bare snapshot while its neighbours read the board, a click handler that never asks whether its slot is greyed, `isSpell` and `category: spell` disagreeing about what a Spell is, and a regen counter and `tickPeriodics` disagreeing about the Turn an effect stops. Raikou added the preview missing an element the resolution granted, an active mode's slot asking the activation gate, an instance's block read from the ability, and a damage-type resistance read at the stage for all damage.
**Lesson:** Before adding or fixing a rule, grep for every other site that answers the same question. The preview, the bar and the card are readers too. Make them all call one reader, and fix the reader, not one site.
