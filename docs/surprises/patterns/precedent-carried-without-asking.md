**Mechanism:** A reading ruled for one Servant was carried into another's grill as a house rule. Penthesilea's #190 reading 6 (Evade and Block are not Acting) was recommended for Raikou's upkeep as settled, and the user overruled it, reopening Penthesilea's in #194.
**Lesson:** A reading from another audit is a precedent to ask about, not a rule: cite it and ask. Promote to `CLAUDE.md` at a second case.
