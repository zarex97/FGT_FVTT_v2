**Expected:** the Linked Summon spec said a TYPE-MOON wiki `Relationships` entry creates a relationship,
written after reading one entry (Jason on Heracles).
**Actual:** mining 344 roster pages gave 3051 entries naming another roster Servant, 2247 pairs. Most
are FGO My Room lines spoken in Chaldea: Dobrynya on Taigong Wang, *"aren't you going to wear a
swimsuit?"*; Lilith on Gilgamesh and Enkidu, *"I don't got any connections to like the goldie king and
his handsome clay puppet"*, an entry that denies the tie it is filed as.
**Cause:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-linked-summon-design.md:50 @ 19944df` — the evidence rule
was set from one entry's shape, not from a sample of the corpus. An entry is evidence of a line, not of
a tie.
**Fix:** open. Each entry is read and classed by its context (life or legend, a Grail War, a story
chapter, Chaldea chatter, a denial) before any preset is proposed; how each class maps to a preset is
put to the user (#376).
