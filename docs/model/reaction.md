**What it is:** what a Unit does when something happens to it or near it, outside its own Turn: Evade, Block,
Counter, a Skill used when Attacked, cancelling the Attack.

**Fields:**
- the reaction ladder, `whenAttacked`, `whenAllyAttacked`, `whenTargetedByNP`, `whenFatallyHit`, cancelling the
  Attack. Known.
- attack a Unit that enters Range (**Variant**, Archer 2026-10-21): Okki (Summer)'s Yadokari Turrets attack
  *"Whenever an enemy Unit enters the Range"*, and the Attack cannot be Countered. Orion, on a summon-time roll,
  attacks any Demonic or Beast Unit that enters his Range. No window opens on another Unit's move today. Not run,
  #302.

**Kin:** Archer: R17, R83, 2 sheets.
