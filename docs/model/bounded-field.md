**What it is:** an area an Ability opens, with its own rules for who enters, who leaves and what happens inside.

**Fields:**
- isolation, entry and exit checks, paid extension, Home Base, ZON ignored inside, forced end. Known.
  keys: `phase.createField`, `field.field.isolation`, `field.field.extension`, `field.field.countsAsHomeBase`
- `negatedBy` (**Variant** `an enemy effect`, Caster 2026-10-21): Paracelsus's Azoth *"Negate all effects of an
  activated/constantly active NP area within a 3 panel area of himself"*. Scheherazade's Open Sesame lets her
  allies *"freely enter and exit"* an enemy Fortress NP. Nothing switches off another side's field today. Not
  run, #295.
  keys: none (#295)

**Kin:** Caster: R18, R99, 2 sheets.
