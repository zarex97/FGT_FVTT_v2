**What it is:** what a Unit does with its Turn: Move, Attack, a Riding Attack, a Mark.

**Fields:**
- `kind`: `attack` `move` `ridingAttack` `mark`. Known, each written in `rules/actions.mjs`.
- authored by content (**Variant**, Caster 2026-10-21): an Action a sheet defines that spends the Turn's Move or
  Attack. Avicebron's *Gather* counts as his Move and adds 2 Construction. Andersen's *Write* adds 1d8 Märchen
  counters, and he cannot Move or Attack that Turn. David's *Search* finds the Ark the GM hid on a secret panel
  (Archer). Mary Anning's Search succeeds 30% more often and her Searchable Areas are a panel larger (Lancer). Every
  kind today is hand-written. Not run, #298.

**Kin:** Caster: R199, R40, 2 sheets.
