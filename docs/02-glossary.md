# 02 — Glossary

Every term of art in F/GT, with a formal definition and the class or subsystem that owns it.
Terms are grouped by subsystem; within a group they are alphabetical. Where the rulebook and
common usage differ, the rulebook wins and the difference is noted.

Notation used in definitions:
- **Owner** — the class/module in this design that is authoritative for the concept.
- **Ch.** — the chapter that specifies it.

---

## 2.1 Core game structure

### Act
Any of: Move, Attack, Evade, Block, Counter, Luck Check, use Spell, use Skill, use NP, or
anything else the rules call an "Action". Critically, "Acts" is the trigger condition for a
large number of end-of-turn effects (`at the end of every Turn the Unit Acts`), so the engine
must track *whether a unit acted this turn* as a distinct flag from *whether it moved* or
*whether it attacked*.
**Owner:** `UnitTurnState`. **Ch.** 18.

### Board / Field
The playing grid. 13×13 (regular) or 25×25 (large). Synonymous terms in the rulebook.
Modelled as a Foundry `Scene` with a square grid.
**Owner:** `BoardModel`. **Ch.** 08.

### Combat Phase
The complete exchange initiated by one attack: the initiating Combat Process **plus** any
Combat Processes generated by Counters. A Combat Phase contains 1 or 2 Combat Processes
(counters cannot themselves be countered).
**Owner:** `CombatPhase`. **Ch.** 12.

### Combat Process
A single instance of battle between an Attacking Unit and its Defending Unit(s), running
Steps 1 through 5 (the rulebook says "1 to 4" in one place and describes Step 5 separately;
we treat facing-update as Step 5 and Counter as Step 6).
**Owner:** `CombatProcess`. **Ch.** 12.

### Faction
A side in the war. In the 3-player format there are two player Factions plus the GM. In the
7-player format, seven players cooperate as one Faction. A Faction owns a Home Base and a
turn slot.
**Owner:** `Faction`. **Ch.** 04.

### Game
One complete match, from setup to Grail acquisition or elimination. Maps to a single Foundry
`Combat` document — the whole match is one encounter, like a chess game.
**Owner:** `FGTCombat`. **Ch.** 25.

### GM / Overseer
Synonymous. The Game Master. Takes a Turn in the Round, introduces Random Events, adjudicates.
**Owner:** n/a (a Foundry user with the GM role). **Ch.** 26.

### Home Base
The coloured area at each end of the field belonging to a Faction. Confers: +100 Health and
+1 Agility at end of Round (to units not involved in Combat there that Round); debuff cure
after 3 full Rounds; −10% damage taken including NP; +20% damage dealt (10% if NP) when both
combatants are inside it.
**Owner:** `HomeBaseZone`. **Ch.** 19.

### Panel
One grid square. The rulebook's unit of distance. One panel = one grid cell = `gridDistance 1`.
**Owner:** `GridOffset` (Foundry). **Ch.** 08.

### Round
One full cycle of Turns — every player's Turn plus the GM's Turn. Round boundaries drive the
Day/Night cycle, Home Base regeneration, Poison staging, Burn damage, and Grail progress.
**Owner:** `RoundScheduler`. **Ch.** 07.

### Turn
One player's or the GM's period of action. The number of Turns per Round equals the number of
players plus one, and is the value behind ◈.
**Owner:** `TurnScheduler`. **Ch.** 07.

### ◈ (Tick operator)
The symbol representing **the number of Turns in a Round**. `3◈` means 3 × turns-per-round.
Fractional forms (`⅓◈`, `⅔◈`, `½◈`) are common and **always round down**, including cases
where standard rounding would round up (`4.6666… → 4`, not 5).
**Owner:** `TickMath`. **Ch.** 07.

## 2.2 Units

### AU — Attacking Unit
The unit that declared the current Attack. A role, not a type; the same unit is the DU when
countered.
**Owner:** `CombatProcess.attacker`. **Ch.** 12.

### Civilian
A neutral non-combatant that may appear on the field. Killed instantly by any Servant attack;
the killer restores 100 Health and 1 Agility. Servants with the `Good` alignment refuse to
kill them without a Command Spell.
**Owner:** `CivilianUnit`. **Ch.** 19.

### DU — Defending Unit
The unit being attacked in the current Combat Process. For AoE attacks there are multiple DUs
and several rules degrade accordingly (e.g. no facing update, per-unit resistance rolls).
**Owner:** `CombatProcess.defenders`. **Ch.** 12.

### Free Servant
A Servant whose Master has been **killed**. Still player-controlled, but contractible by both
allied and enemy Masters/Casters, and subject to Sustainability countdown.
**Owner:** `ContractState.FREE`. **Ch.** 16.

### Master
A human magus commanding one or more Servants. Has 3 Command Spells, `Base Attack (MAG)` of
100 or 125, a ZON, and the `Human`/`Living Human`/`Humanoid` attributes. Vulnerable to
Overpower.
**Owner:** `MasterUnit`. **Ch.** 04.

### Platform
Our term (not the rulebook's) for a large, boardable, movable entity that carries units:
Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Golden Hind, Storm Border. Modelled with Scene Levels.
**Owner:** `PlatformUnit`. **Ch.** 20.

### Servant
A summoned Heroic Spirit. Has 5 Parameters, class skills, personal skills, and 1+ Noble
Phantasms. Has the `Spirit` attribute unless Demi-/Pseudo-Servant.
**Owner:** `ServantUnit`. **Ch.** 04.

### Summon
A unit created by an ability (Bašmu). Generally does not count toward the controller's
move/attack budget.
**Owner:** `SummonUnit`. **Ch.** 04.

### Unbound Servant
A Servant whose Master has **used up or lost** their Command Spells (but is alive). Still
player-controlled; contractible by *enemy* Masters/Casters only.
**Owner:** `ContractState.UNBOUND`. **Ch.** 16.

### Unit
Any token on the board that can be targeted, damaged, or moved. Base of the hierarchy.
**Owner:** `Unit`. **Ch.** 04.

## 2.3 Statistics

### Agility
A depleting resource (`current/max`), not a modifier. Used for Evade rolls (roll under
current Agility) and reduced by Injury Rolls. Derived from the AGI Parameter at setup.
**Owner:** `Stats.agility`. **Ch.** 06.

### Base Attack (STR) / Base Attack (MAG)
The two attack values. Which one is used is determined by unit class, ability text, or
explicit override. Some abilities use both combined. Masters' normal attacks use BA(MAG).
**Owner:** `Stats.baseAttack`. **Ch.** 06.

### Base Health
The Health value derived purely from the END Rank (A=1500, B=1250, C=1000, D=750, E=500),
before the setup coin-flip roll and ±100 per rank modifier.
**Owner:** `Stats.baseHealth`. **Ch.** 06.

### Health
Current/max hit points. The unit is defeated when Health drops below 0 (the rulebook says
"below 0"; several effects say "reduced to 0" — treated as identical, see Ch. 41).
**Owner:** `Stats.health`. **Ch.** 06.

### Luck
A depleting resource. Used for Luck Checks (roll under current Luck) and **decremented by 1
after every Luck Check**, win or lose. This makes Luck a genuine budget, not a stat.
**Owner:** `Stats.luck`. **Ch.** 06.

### MOV
Panels movable per Turn. Derived from AGI Rank. No diagonal movement is permitted.
**Owner:** `Stats.mov`. **Ch.** 06, 08.

### Parameters
The five Ranks: STR, END, AGI, MAG, LUC. Ranked E through A with EX above, and `+`/`-`
modifiers. Parameters *derive* stats at setup; some effects compare Parameters at runtime
(Magic Resistance, Karna's Brahmastra, HGoB boarding rolls).
**Owner:** `Parameters`. **Ch.** 05.

### Range
Attack reach, expressed as "N panels, M targets". Diagonals count, **except** at Range ≥ 3
where diagonal reach is reduced by 1. See Ch. 08 for the exact shape.
**Owner:** `Stats.range`. **Ch.** 06, 08.

### Stats
The rulebook's narrow definition: Health, Agility, Luck. (Contrast Parameters.) We use the
term more broadly for all numeric unit state and call the rulebook's triple the
"contested stats" where the distinction matters.
**Owner:** `Stats`. **Ch.** 06.

### Sustainability
The number of Turns a Free Servant survives after its Master dies. `N/A` means indefinite.
Zero means immediate disappearance. Reduced by NP usage while Free.
**Owner:** `Stats.sustainability`. **Ch.** 16.

### ZON — Effective Servant Zone
The radius, in panels, around a Master within which its Servant operates at full strength.
Defaults by class (Saber/Lancer/Rider/Berserker 2, Archer/Assassin 4, Caster 5) and is
increased by Independent Action, Mad Enhancement, and High Rank Masters. Outside ZON, a
Servant's attack damage is reduced by 5d10. NPs require the Servant to be inside ZON.
**Owner:** `ZonZone`. **Ch.** 16.

## 2.4 Ranks

### Rank
An ordinal grade: `E, D, C, B, A, EX`, each optionally suffixed with one or more `+` or a
single `-`. Examples in the source: `A+`, `B-`, `A++`, `C++`, `EX`.
**Owner:** `Rank` value object. **Ch.** 05.

### Rank comparison
Two distinct operations that must not be conflated:
- **Ordinal comparison** (`compare`) — is A+ ≥ A? Used by Magic Resistance negation, boarding
  rolls, Presence Concealment's counter clause.
- **Modifier arithmetic** (`step`) — "for every + in Rank, increase X by N". Counts suffixes.
**Owner:** `RankMath`. **Ch.** 05.

## 2.5 Abilities

### Active Skill
A skill the unit must use, with a Cooldown. Usable during your Turn unless stated, and usable
after moving/attacking unless stated. Using one counts the unit toward the turn's
move-or-attack budget.
**Owner:** `SkillItem` with `activation: "active"`. **Ch.** 15.

### Attack Skill
A subtype of Active Skill that deals damage, normally with BA(STR). Usually counts as the
unit's Attack for the Turn.
**Owner:** `SkillItem` with `kind: "attackSkill"`. **Ch.** 15.

### Categorized as Noble Phantasm
A property that a Skill or Spell may carry. Such abilities are affected by NP-scoped effects
(NP DmUp, NP Seal, NP cooldown reduction) and *not* by skill-scoped ones. Bašmu's
"Cursed Poison Dragonfire" is an example.
**Owner:** `AbilityFlags.categorizedAsNP`. **Ch.** 15.

### Class Skill
A skill granted by the Servant's class (Magic Resistance, Riding, Independent Action,
Territory Creation, Item Construction, Presence Concealment, Mad Enhancement, plus Avenger's
Avenger/Oblivion Correction/Self-Replenishment). Mostly passive, rank-scaled.
**Owner:** `SkillItem` with `isClassSkill: true`. **Ch.** 15, Appendix B.

### Cooldown
Number of Turns before a Skill or NP can be used again. **Begins counting the Turn *after*
use**, not the turn of use. Reducing to zero makes it available.
**Owner:** `CooldownTracker`. **Ch.** 07.

### Damage Spell
A subtype of Spell that deals damage, always with BA(MAG) unless stated. Usually counts as
the unit's Attack for the Turn.
**Owner:** `SkillItem` with `kind: "damageSpell"`. **Ch.** 15.

### Noble Phantasm (NP)
A Servant's ultimate ability. Usable from Round 6 onward (Round 4 for Assassin). Costs the
Servant's Master Health based on NP Rank and Master Rank. Requires the Servant to be within
its Master's ZON. Hits all units in the stated area including allies unless stated.
**Owner:** `NoblePhantasmItem`. **Ch.** 15.

### Magic Crest (MC)
A Master ability, usable from Round 3 onward. Resolves like an NP at Step 3 of Combat.
**Owner:** `MagicCrestItem`. **Ch.** 15.

### Passive Skill
Always in effect. Contributes rule elements continuously. A skill may have both Passive and
Active components; Cooldown applies only to the Active part.
**Owner:** `SkillItem` with `activation: "passive"`. **Ch.** 15.

### Spell
A subset of Skill, normally available to Casters. `Thaumaturgy` and `Primordial Rune Spell`
are named sub-families with their own constraints.
**Owner:** `SkillItem` with `kind: "spell"`. **Ch.** 15.

## 2.6 Combat vocabulary

### Attack
An umbrella term. The rulebook is explicit: *Attack, Noble Phantasm, Magic Crest,
Counter: Attack, Counter: Noble Phantasm and Counter: Magic Crest are all treated as
'Attack' unless stated.* This matters enormously for effect triggers.
**Owner:** `AttackContext`. **Ch.** 12.

### Attack+ / Attack−
The two attack rolls. `Attack+` is a **Crit**. Selected by coin flip, so base crit chance is
50%; crit-chance modifiers shift this. `Attack−` is a normal (weak) hit.
**Owner:** `CritResolver`. **Ch.** 14.

### Block
A defensive Reaction. Reduces incoming damage by the Block roll. Doubled against NP. A unit
with Invuln cannot Block. Pierce ignores Block.
**Owner:** `ReactionKind.BLOCK`. **Ch.** 12.

### Counter
A Reaction available to a DU that survived or successfully evaded, if the AU is within the
DU's Range. Repeats Steps 1 and 4 with roles reversed. **Counters cannot be countered.**
**Owner:** `ReactionKind.COUNTER`. **Ch.** 12.

### Crit
`Attack+`. Base chance 50%. Crit Chance modifiers do not affect NP unless stated.
**Owner:** `CritResolver`. **Ch.** 14.

### Damage Step
Step 3 of the Combat Process — the moment damage is computed and applied. A large number of
effects trigger "at the start of" or "at the end of" the Damage Step, so it is an explicit
event boundary, not just a computation.
**Owner:** `DamageStep`. **Ch.** 12, 13.

### Evade
A defensive Reaction. Roll `Evade` (or `Evade−` if the DU's Agility is lower than the AU's);
success if the rolled value is **lower** than the DU's current Agility. +3 to the roll value
against NP, +2 against AoE, +1 from the sides, +2 from behind.
**Owner:** `ReactionKind.EVADE`. **Ch.** 12, 14.

### Fixed damage
Damage unaffected by any damage-modifying effect on either side, including Block — but
**affected by Invuln**.
**Owner:** `DamageKind.FIXED`. **Ch.** 13.

### Injury Roll
Performed by a DU that survives and takes more than 100 damage. Reduces the DU's Agility by
the rolled value. Multi-hit attacks trigger at most one Injury Roll.
**Owner:** `InjuryResolver`. **Ch.** 14.

### Luck Check
Roll a d20; success if the value rolled is **lower** than the unit's current Luck. Uses the
`Luck Check−` variant if the roller's Luck is lower than the opponent's. Costs 1 Luck per
check regardless of outcome. Eight named Luck Check actions exist (Ch. 14).
**Owner:** `CheckResolver`. **Ch.** 14.

### Overpower
When a Servant successfully attacks a Master: coin flip; on Heads the Master is instantly
defeated. Prevented by Invuln, Shield, and the `Master's Luck` Luck Check; reduced 10% by
Def Up/Dmg Cut.
**Owner:** `OverpowerResolver`. **Ch.** 16.

### Reaction
Any Action performed in response to an enemy Action — most commonly Block/Evade/nothing.
The `Accel` buff prevents opponents from Reacting.
**Owner:** `Reaction`. **Ch.** 12, 27.

### Total damage
Damage after **all** damage-modifying effects are calculated. Many effects specify
"Total Damage" explicitly, and they must be applied at the correct stage.
**Owner:** `DamageResult.total`. **Ch.** 13.

### Underpower
When a Master successfully attacks a Servant: coin flip; on Tails, Total Damage is halved
(including NP). Reduced 10% by the Master having Atk Up or NP DmUp.
**Owner:** `UnderpowerResolver`. **Ch.** 16.

## 2.7 Effects

### Buff / Debuff
Positive and negative statuses. Both are further classified as **Offensive** or **Defensive**
(which matters for `Off.Debuff Immune` etc.), and debuffs additionally as **Non-volatile**,
**Mental**, **Volatile**, or **Other**.
**Owner:** `EffectCategory`. **Ch.** 10.

### Cure
Debuff removal by an effect. Distinct from Expire.
**Owner:** `EffectRemoval.CURE`. **Ch.** 11.

### Dispel
Buff removal by an effect. Distinct from Expire.
**Owner:** `EffectRemoval.DISPEL`. **Ch.** 11.

### Expire
Natural removal at the end of a duration. Buff-removal-resistance does **not** protect
against Expire.
**Owner:** `EffectRemoval.EXPIRE`. **Ch.** 11.

### Magnitude
The strength value of an effect: in `Atk Up (20%)` the magnitude is 20%.
**Owner:** `EffectInstance.magnitude`. **Ch.** 11.

### Stage
A stacking counter used by Curse and Poison. Curse Stage N deals 25×N damage; Poison Stage N
deals 20×2^(N−1).
**Owner:** `EffectInstance.stage`. **Ch.** 10.

### Unremovable
An effect that removal effects cannot touch. It can still Expire.
**Owner:** `EffectFlags.unremovable`. **Ch.** 11.

### Neither-buff-nor-debuff
A third category, used by Presence Concealment, Mad Enhancement, Riding's MOV Up,
Queen's Poison, the Dove effect, the Fragarach effect, and Construction. These are immune to
both dispel and cure, and are not counted by "remove 1 buff" effects.
**Owner:** `EffectCategory.STATUS`. **Ch.** 10, 11.

## 2.8 Key named effects
(Full catalogue in Appendix A; these are the ones whose names are used loosely in prose.)

| Term | One-line semantics |
|---|---|
| **Aim** | Ignores Dodge and the Evade action. |
| **Anti-Purge** | Takes no damage from anything, even Pierce. Beats Invuln. |
| **Bind** | Umbrella keyword for Stun, Disable, Immobilize, Slow, Petrify, Shock, Webbed, Seal, Freeze, Crystalfreeze. |
| **Break** | Ignores Block (chance-based) and deals extra damage if Blocked. |
| **Decoy** | Forces enemies within range to move toward and attack only this unit. |
| **Delay** | Pushes the affected player down the turn order by X. Unremovable. |
| **Dodge** | Automatic successful Evade for a duration/count. Cannot use the Evade action. |
| **Guts** | Revive on defeat with X health. Priority: Special Guts > Guts > passive revival. |
| **Invuln** | Takes no damage; 50% reduction vs NP; cannot Block; Pierce ignores it. |
| **Multihit** | One Attack that hits N times; separate Evades, single Block, single Injury Roll. |
| **Pierce** | Ignores Invuln and the Block action. Does **not** ignore Def Up or Dmg Cut. |
| **Repel** | AU takes X fixed damage at end of Combat Process, regardless of Range. |
| **Substitution** | Cannot be hit by anything, even Aim. Beats Dodge. |

## 2.9 Targeting vocabulary
(Formalized in Ch. 09.)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AoE** | Any attack affecting more than one panel. Grants +2 to defenders' Evade rolls. |
| **Detect** | The radius at which a unit may Discover a Presence-Concealed unit. Minimum 2 panels. |
| **Non-diagonal direction** | One of the four cardinal directions. "A 5×5 area in any non-diagonal direction next to X" means the 5×5 block is placed flush against X on one of four sides. |
| **Party / Party Area** | All allied units within a 2-panel area of the effect source. |
| **Range** | The set of panels a unit can attack, per its Range stat and the diagonal-reduction rule. |
| **Within Range** | Targeting anchored anywhere inside the caster's Range, as opposed to anchored *at* the caster. |

## 2.10 Attributes

Attributes are tags on units that other effects key off. The source defines implication rules
which the engine must apply transitively:

```
Human          ⟹ Humanoid, Living Human
Demi-Servant   ⟹ Human
Giant          ⟹ Large
Divine         ⟺ Divinity skill
Demonic Beast  ⟹ Non-Hominidae
Demon          ⟹ Demonic
Servant        ⟹ Spirit          (unless Demi-Servant or Pseudo-Servant)
Servant        ⟹ Hominidae       (unless Non-Hominidae or Demonic Beast)
Swimsuit skill ⟹ Summer
```

Attribute categories seen in the reference set: gender (`Male`, `Female`, `Genderless`),
unit type (`Servant`, `Master`, `Civilian`), origin (`[Man]`, `[Sky]`, `[Earth]`, `[Star]`,
`[Beast]`), body (`Humanoid`, `Non-Hominidae`, `Large`, `Giant`, `Mechanical`, `Animalistic`,
`Dragon`), social (`King`, `Anti-Hero`, `Child`, `Threat to Humanity`), and metaphysical
(`Divine`, `Outsider`, `Undead`, `Magus`, `Living Human`, `Levitating`).

Note `[Earth]` the origin attribute is unrelated to the Earth *element*.
**Owner:** `AttributeSet`. **Ch.** 04.

## 2.11 Damage types and elements

Elemental tags appear on damage: `Fire`, `Water`, `Lightning`, `Ice`, `Light`, `Curse`,
`Poison`, `Burn`. They interact with resistances (`FlamHeal`, `PoisHeal`, `CursHeal`,
`Dragonblight` which zeroes elemental damage) and with status interactions (Fire damage
removes Freeze).

Separately, damage has a **source component**: STR, MAG, or a combination. Magic Resistance
only reduces the MAG portion. Silence removes the MAG portion from normal attacks.
**Owner:** `DamagePacket`. **Ch.** 13.

## 2.12 Terms we introduce (not in the rulebook)

These are ours. They are flagged so nobody searches the rulebook for them.

| Our term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Snapshot** | A plain-data projection of documents for the pure rules layer. |
| **Intent** | A described mutation returned by the rules layer, applied by orchestration. |
| **Rule element** | A declarative unit of effect behaviour attached to an item or effect. |
| **Predicate** | A boolean expression over roll options / unit state, gating a rule element. |
| **Roll option** | A string tag in a set describing the current context, e.g. `target:attribute:divine`. |
| **Tick** | One Turn, as a unit of duration. `◈` converts rounds-worth-of-ticks to ticks. |
| **Platform** | A boardable large entity (HGoB, Golden Hind, Storm Border). |
| **Reaction ladder** | The Step 2 → 2.5 contest sequence. |
| **Anchor** | The reference point a targeting shape is positioned against. |
